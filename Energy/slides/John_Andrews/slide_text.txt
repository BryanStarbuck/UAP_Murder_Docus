* DISAPPEARED after demonstrating a water-to-fuel additive to the U.S. NAVY — they offered $2 MILLION for the formula.
* Poured drops of green liquid into plain water, and the engine ran on it. Navy officers witnessed it at Brooklyn Navy Yard, 1917.
* One account says he was MURDERED in his ransacked home. His sister fled to Scotland with copies of his notes — then she was MURDERED too.
* The U.S. Naval Institute published articles about him in 1936 AND 1979, treating the demonstration as genuine.
* A reporter found him alive on a Pennsylvania farm in 1942, where he claimed he'd "forgotten the formula."
