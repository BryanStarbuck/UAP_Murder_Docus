* Invented electronic television at 21. Then achieved NUCLEAR FUSION in his lab -- and ITT KILLED the funding at peak results.
* The Farnsworth Fusor was the first device to demonstrably produce nuclear fusion reactions. It worked. Then Wall Street pressured ITT's board to cut the budget.
* His most advanced fusor was built in 1965, just before the funding was cut. The technology was abandoned for financial reasons, not scientific ones.
* Spent years fighting RCA's David Sarnoff for credit on television. Won -- but the legal battles drained him financially and emotionally.
* DIED of pneumonia at 64 in Utah after alcoholism and a nervous breakdown. His fusion work was never continued. The fusor concept survives only as a neutron source.
