* VANISHED from the SS Dresden while crossing the English Channel. His bed was unslept in. Hat and overcoat folded neatly under the deck railing. Age 55.
* He was en route to meet the British Royal Navy about powering their submarines with his engine -- a deal that would have given Britain a decisive advantage over Germany, less than a year before WWI.
* His engine could run on vegetable oil, meaning farmers could produce their own fuel -- bypassing Rockefeller's petroleum empire entirely.
* His wife found 20,000 marks in cash he left her. His bank accounts were nearly empty. He knew he might not make it.
* Body found 10 days later in the North Sea, too decomposed to identify. Only personal effects confirmed by his son. 1913.
