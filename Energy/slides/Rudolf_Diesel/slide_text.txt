* VANISHED from the SS Dresden crossing the English Channel at age 55. Body found days later in the North Sea.
* He was heading to meet the British Royal Navy about powering submarines with his diesel engine.
* His cabin bed was unslept in. His hat and coat were folded neatly under the deck railing.
* Bank accounts nearly empty. He gave his wife 20,000 marks in cash before departing — as if he knew.
