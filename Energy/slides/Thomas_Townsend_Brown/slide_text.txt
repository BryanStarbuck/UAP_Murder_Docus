* Discovered electrogravitics in the 1920s -- high-voltage capacitors producing thrust toward their positive plate. The U.S. military CLASSIFIED his research after he submitted Project Winterhaven in 1952.
* By 1957, electrogravitics was a classified subject. A 1956 report by a British defense group was itself classified. His public research effectively ended.
* Allegedly received a "hush-money pension" from Northrop Corporation starting in 1967 in exchange for silence about electrogravitic propulsion.
* Some claim the B-2 stealth bomber uses his electrogravitic technology. He spent his final years in poverty on Catalina Island.
* Died at 80 of natural causes. His death was not suspicious -- but his life's work was taken behind classification walls and never came out.
