* DIED penniless despite holding patents on a water-derived fuel that could cut through metals and neutralize radioactive waste. Age 75, heart and kidney failure.
* Born Ilya Velbov in Bulgaria, declared an "enemy of the state," fled to Australia. Spent decades demonstrating Brown's Gas -- produced by splitting water.
* His gas could reduce radioactivity in nuclear waste, a claim witnessed by scientists and journalists. If verified, it would be worth billions.
* Despite public demonstrations, held patents, and decades of effort, no major company ever commercialized the technology.
* He died the day before his 76th birthday in an Australian hospital. Multiple other water fuel inventors -- Stanley Meyer, Daniel Dingel -- also died or were prosecuted.
