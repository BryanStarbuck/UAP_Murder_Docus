## Their Claims

Buhler claims to have discovered and experimentally validated a **new electrostatic force mechanism** — not ionic wind, not electromagnetic — capable of generating measurable thrust in hard vacuum ($10^{-6}$ Torr) without expelling any reaction mass.

His core experimental result: **approximately 2 millinewtons of thrust at 6,000 volts**, demonstrated in a horizontal gravity pendulum apparatus using grounded ITO (indium tin oxide) shielded test articles in a vacuum chamber. This result has been replicated by third parties and by Buhler's own team across more than 2,000 experimental trials over approximately 10 years.

The mechanism Buhler identifies has two complementary formulations:

**Classical formulation:** Starting from the field energy density $U = \int \frac{\varepsilon_0}{2} E^2 \, dV$ rather than the conventional $U = qV$, the force equation yields two terms:
1. A **surface pressure term**: net force proportional to the difference in electrostatic pressure $\frac{\varepsilon_0}{2}(E_1^2 - E_2^2)$ between the two asymmetric electrode faces
2. A **volumetric divergence term**: force proportional to $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{E}$ within the electrode volume

The asymmetric geometry ensures $E_1^2 \neq E_2^2$ between the high-curvature anode and the low-curvature cathode, producing a non-zero net pressure difference and thus a net thrust in the direction from cathode to anode.

**Quantum formulation:** Buhler proposes a third-order QED perturbation theory explanation, extending beyond the standard second-order treatment (which fully accounts for particle attraction/repulsion via virtual photon exchange). At third order, 12 terms appear. The relevant new term involves a virtual photon emitted from the field of one electrode that interacts with the external field rather than being absorbed by the second charge — the **vacuum recoils** upon this virtual photon emission, and the asymmetric geometry biases the direction of this recoil, producing net thrust. In his notation:

$$F_{\text{quantum}} \propto Q^2 \cdot Q_{\text{opposing}} = (\varepsilon_0 E^2) \cdot Q_{\text{opposing}}$$

which is **pressure times the charge of the opposing plate** — consistent with the classical two-term expression but derived from QED rather than Maxwell stress tensor integration.

**Important constraint:** All of Buhler's vacuum tests use a **grounded ITO-shielded enclosure** surrounding the test article. Buhler explicitly states: *"In all cases we have to contain the thruster in a ground."* This means the force is measured relative to a grounded structure, not in a fully isolated floating configuration. Whether the thrust persists in a completely isolated (floating potential) vacuum geometry has not been demonstrated. The ground plane is the classical reaction partner that permits $F_{\text{net}} \neq 0$ for the test article — consistent with [Track Attempt 2](../Physics_Math/Track_33/overview.mdx) Day 3 findings.

## The Counterargument

**Mainstream physics position:** Asymmetric electrostatic thrust in vacuum should be zero for an isolated system by Newton's Third Law applied to the Maxwell stress tensor. For a conductor system in vacuum with no free charges, the integral of $T_{ij}\hat{n}_j$ over any closed surface enclosing the system sums to zero — there is no net external force. The ARL-TR-3005 study (Bahder & Fazi, 2003) found that asymmetric capacitor thrust in vacuum was negligible compared to in-air measurements, consistent with the ionic wind explanation.

**Alternative explanations for Buhler's non-zero vacuum result:**
1. **Ground plane reaction**: Buhler explicitly uses grounded ITO shields — the system is not isolated. The thrust is measured relative to the grounded structure, which is the classical reaction partner. This is not "reactionless" thrust in the strictest sense; it is thrust from an electrostatic interaction between the asymmetric test article and the surrounding grounded chamber. Demonstrating the same force with a completely floating, isolated test article would be far more extraordinary.
2. **Stray capacitance and electromagnetic coupling**: At 6,000 V, even tiny capacitive coupling to the chamber walls or support structure could produce forces consistent with the observed 2 mN.
3. **Measurement artifacts on scales**: Buhler himself acknowledges that electrostatic coupling to scale electronics can corrupt scale-based measurements ("scales can lie to you"), which is why he prefers the tethered pendulum geometry. But the pendulum itself has a grounded fiber or cable that could serve as a reaction path.

**Credibility assessment:** Buhler is a credentialed NASA physicist with legitimate non-controversial work (Electrodynamic Dust Shield). His 2,000-trial dataset and third-party verification are unusually rigorous for alternative propulsion claims. The ground-plane dependency does not invalidate the experimental result — it constrains the interpretation. Whether a ground-plane-free version can produce the same thrust is the critical unanswered question.
