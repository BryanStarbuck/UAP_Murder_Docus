
# Karl Wolfe
Former US Air Force sergeant with a top secret crypto clearance who worked as a precision electronics photographic repairman at Langley Air Force Base, and who testified at the 2001 National Press Club Disclosure Project that he was shown NASA Lunar Orbiter photographs depicting artificial structures on the far side of the Moon. Wolfe was killed on October 10, 2018, at age 74, when he was struck by a tractor-trailer while riding his bicycle in Lansing, New York.

## Biography

Karl Robert Wolfe was born on April 19, 1944, in Lansing, New York, the youngest of seven children born to Hazel and Mattis Wolfe. He grew up in the Finger Lakes region of upstate New York and graduated from Lansing Central School in 1963. Wolfe went on to attend the State University of New York at Buffalo (SUNY Buffalo), where he earned dual degrees in Electrical Engineering and Social and Cultural Anthropology.

Wolfe enlisted in the United States Air Force, where he was trained in precision electronics and assigned to the Tactical Air Command (TAC) headquarters at Langley Air Force Base in Hampton, Virginia. He held a top secret crypto security clearance, which granted him access to sensitive photographic intelligence facilities on the base. His primary role was repairing and maintaining precision electronics equipment used in photographic processing.

After his military service, Wolfe pursued interests in consciousness research and spirituality, conducting seminars and retreats in upstate New York focused on self-awareness and higher consciousness. He remained in the Lansing, New York, area for most of his life.

Wolfe was predeceased by his parents, his sister Emma, and his brothers Corky, Wally, and Gus. He is survived by his brother Morris, several sisters-in-law, and his niece Chrisie.

## Their Claims

Karl Wolfe's central claim concerns a single incident in the autumn of 1965 at Langley Air Force Base. According to his sworn testimony, the events unfolded as follows:

### The Assignment at Langley AFB (1965)

Wolfe was sent to a facility at Langley AFB where NASA's Lunar Orbiter photographs were being processed. The Lunar Orbiter program (1966-1967) was a series of five unmanned spacecraft missions designed to photograph the Moon's surface in preparation for the Apollo landings. Photographic data from the orbiters was received and processed at multiple facilities, including those at Langley.

Wolfe was called to the facility to repair a piece of photographic processing equipment that was creating a bottleneck in the production of lunar photographs. The process involved developing 35mm strips of film from successive passes of the Lunar Orbiter around the Moon, which were then assembled into large composite photographs called "mosaics" measuring approximately 18.5 by 11 inches.

### What He Claims to Have Seen

While Wolfe was working on the equipment, another airman — described by Wolfe as a fellow technician working in the darkroom — led him to one side of the lab and, according to Wolfe's testimony, told him: "By the way, we've discovered a base on the backside of the moon."

The airman then showed Wolfe a mosaic photograph of the lunar far side. Wolfe described seeing geometric structures that he said were clearly artificial in nature: spherical buildings casting distinct shadows, vertical towers of exceptional height, and mushroom-shaped structures distributed across the terrain. Wolfe stated that the structures were unmistakable and could not be attributed to natural geological formations.

Wolfe emphasized that the photographs he was shown were from the far side of the Moon — the hemisphere that permanently faces away from Earth and was not visible from ground-based telescopes at that time. This detail is significant because the far side of the Moon was largely unphotographed before the Lunar Orbiter missions.

### The Significance of His Clearance Level

Wolfe noted that although he held a top secret crypto clearance, the information he was shown exceeded his need-to-know authorization. He described the moment as a spontaneous disclosure by a fellow airman rather than an authorized briefing. Wolfe stated that he understood the gravity of what he had been shown and kept the information to himself for decades before coming forward publicly.

## Disclosure Project Testimony (2001)

On May 9, 2001, Wolfe delivered his account as one of over 20 military, intelligence, and government witnesses at Dr. Steven Greer's Disclosure Project press conference at the National Press Club in Washington, D.C. The event called on the United States Congress to hold open hearings on UFOs, extraterrestrial contact, and classified energy and propulsion technologies.

Wolfe's testimony was notable for several reasons:

- He provided it under oath, stating his willingness to testify before Congress
- He identified himself by full name, rank, and duty station
- He described specific technical details of the photographic processing operation that were consistent with the known Lunar Orbiter program procedures
- His account was corroborated in general terms by the fact that Lunar Orbiter imagery was indeed processed at facilities associated with Langley AFB

Wolfe appeared alongside other Disclosure Project witnesses including [Mark McCandlish](Mark_McCandlish.md), Donna Hare (a former NASA contractor who made related claims about airbrushing anomalies from NASA photographs), and numerous military officers and intelligence personnel.

## Death

On October 10, 2018, Karl Wolfe, age 74, was riding his bicycle on a southbound lane in Lansing, New York, when he was struck by a tractor-trailer. He was transported to a nearby hospital but died from injuries sustained in the collision.

The New York State Police investigated the incident. No charges were filed against the driver of the tractor-trailer, and law enforcement did not publicly release the driver's name.

### Circumstances Noted by Researchers

Wolfe's death occurred during a period when several prominent UFO disclosure witnesses died under circumstances that some researchers have described as suspicious. Among those who died within a few years of Wolfe's death were:

- **Mark McCandlish** (April 13, 2021) — Fellow Disclosure Project witness, died of a gunshot wound in Redding, California, reportedly after offering to testify before the Senate Intelligence Committee
- **Stanton Friedman** (May 13, 2019) — Nuclear physicist and prominent UFO researcher
- **Karl Wolfe** himself died just months before the U.S. Navy confirmed the authenticity of UAP encounter videos in 2019 and Congress began holding public hearings on UAPs

There is no public evidence that Wolfe's death was anything other than a traffic accident. The investigation by New York State Police did not produce findings suggesting foul play. However, researchers within the UAP disclosure community have noted the pattern of disclosure witnesses dying before Congressional hearings intensified.

## Key Arguments & Evidence They Cite

- Wolfe held a verified top secret crypto clearance and served at Langley AFB during the period of the Lunar Orbiter program, lending credibility to his claim of being present in the photographic processing facility
- The technical details Wolfe provided about the photographic processing methodology (35mm film strips assembled into mosaics) are consistent with the known Lunar Orbiter imaging system
- Wolfe's testimony was delivered under oath at a nationally covered press event, with full identification, increasing the personal and legal risk of fabrication
- The far side of the Moon was largely unknown to the public in 1965, meaning Wolfe's claim could not have been easily constructed from publicly available imagery at the time
- Multiple other witnesses, including Donna Hare, have independently described NASA practices of altering or suppressing anomalous features in space photography

## The Counterargument

- Wolfe's account is based on a single incident in 1965, recalled and publicly disclosed 36 years later in 2001, raising questions about the reliability of decades-old memory
- The identity of the airman who allegedly showed Wolfe the photographs has never been publicly confirmed, and no corroborating witness has come forward
- Wolfe acknowledged that the disclosure exceeded his need-to-know authorization, meaning the unnamed airman would have been committing a security violation — making the spontaneous nature of the disclosure unusual
- NASA's Lunar Orbiter imagery has been digitized and made publicly available; independent analysis of this imagery has not confirmed the presence of artificial structures on the far side of the Moon
- The Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (launched 2009) has photographed the entire lunar surface at high resolution, and its publicly available imagery does not show the structures Wolfe described
- Wolfe's death was investigated by New York State Police and no evidence of foul play was reported; bicycle-versus-truck accidents, while tragic, are not uncommon
- Wolfe did not claim to have retained or copied any photographs, leaving his testimony without physical evidence
