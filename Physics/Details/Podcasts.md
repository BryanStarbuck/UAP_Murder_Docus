
# Podcasts on UAP Physics & Classified Breakthroughs

Podcasts have become one of the most significant channels for UAP physics information to reach the public. Congressional whistleblowers, classified program insiders, physicists, and investigative journalists have used long-form podcast interviews to present detailed claims that would never fit in a news segment. Several of the most consequential UAP disclosures in history -- Bob Lazar's gravity amplification claims, David Grusch's congressional whistleblower testimony, Luis Elizondo's AATIP revelations -- reached their largest audiences through podcast appearances. This file catalogs both dedicated UAP podcasts and landmark episodes on mainstream shows.

These hosts apply higher evidentiary standards than entertainment-focused shows.

**Military and intelligence hosts as force multipliers.** The emergence of hosts with military and intelligence backgrounds -- [Shawn Ryan](#16-the-shawn-ryan-show) (Navy SEAL, CIA contractor), [Ryan Graves](#5-merged-with-ryan-graves) (Navy pilot, DARPA), and [John Burroughs](#19-phenomenon-radio) (Rendlesham Forest witness) -- has created interview environments where insiders share more detailed and specific information than they do with civilian journalists.
