
# William Cooper

American conspiracy theorist, radio broadcaster, and author of *Behold a Pale Horse* (1991), one of the most widely read and influential books in UFO and conspiracy literature. Cooper claimed to have seen classified Navy documents describing governmental dealings with extraterrestrials, recovered craft, and antigravity propulsion technology. He was shot and killed by Apache County sheriff's deputies on November 5, 2001, at age 58, during an attempted arrest at his home in Eagar, Arizona.

## Biography

Milton William Cooper was born on May 6, 1943, in Long Beach, California, into a military family — his father was a U.S. Air Force officer. Cooper enlisted in the United States Navy and served from the mid-1960s through 1975, achieving the rank of First Class Petty Officer (QM1, E-6) after eight years of service.

Cooper's naval career included multiple postings. He served aboard the USS Tiru (SS-416), a submarine, and later aboard the USS Tombigbee (AOG-11). He was stationed at Naval Support Activity Danang in the Republic of Vietnam, where he served as a Harbor and River Patrol Boat Captain with the Dong Ha River Security Group at Cua Viet. He later served aboard the USS Charles Berry (DE-1035), at Headquarters Commander in Chief Pacific Fleet (CINCPACFLT) at Makalapa, Hawaii — where he claimed to have been the Petty Officer of the Watch and designated KL-47 SPECAT operator in the Command Center — and aboard the USS Oriskany (CVA-34). Cooper reportedly received several decorations for his service, including two with "V" for Valor. He received an Honorable Discharge on December 11, 1975.

After leaving the Navy, Cooper attended a junior college in California and worked for several technical and vocational schools. In 1988, he began making his claims public, first on the ParaNet Bulletin Board System, an early UFO-focused online forum. In 1991, he published *Behold a Pale Horse*, which became one of the best-selling conspiracy and UFO books of all time and has remained in print continuously since publication.

From 1992 until his death, Cooper broadcast *The Hour of the Time*, a shortwave radio program transmitted from a studio in his home in Eagar, Arizona. The show was relayed via audio cassette, satellite, or telephone link to WWCR in Nashville, Tennessee, which broadcast it on a 100,000-watt shortwave transmitter reaching listeners worldwide. Over the course of nearly a decade, Cooper produced approximately 1,900 episodes covering UFOs, government secrecy, constitutional law, and what he described as a long-running global conspiracy.

## Their Claims

Cooper's claims evolved significantly over his career. His initial focus was on UFO phenomena and alleged government contact with extraterrestrial beings, but he later shifted toward broader political conspiracy theories and, notably, reversed some of his earlier UFO positions.

### Classified Naval Intelligence Documents

Cooper claimed that during his service at CINCPACFLT in Hawaii, he had access to classified documents that described the U.S. government's secret relationship with extraterrestrial beings. According to Cooper, these documents detailed:

- The recovery of crashed alien spacecraft and biological entities beginning in the late 1940s
- A secret committee known as "Majestic 12" (MJ-12) that managed all information related to extraterrestrial contact
- Formal agreements between the U.S. government and alien species, in which the government received advanced technology in exchange for allowing limited experimentation on human subjects
- The existence of underground facilities where recovered technology was studied and reverse-engineered

### UFO Sighting Aboard USS Tiru

Cooper claimed that in 1966, while serving aboard the submarine USS Tiru, he and fellow Navy personnel witnessed a massive metallic craft "larger than a football field" that repeatedly entered and exited the ocean. This account describes what would now be termed "transmedium" travel — a craft operating seamlessly in both air and water — a characteristic that has been independently reported in numerous military encounters and is one of the "Five Observables" identified by the Pentagon's UAP investigation programs.

### Antigravity and Advanced Propulsion Technology

In *Behold a Pale Horse*, Cooper made specific claims about classified propulsion technology:

- He stated that the United States possessed and actively flew "atomic-powered antigravity-type craft" at facilities in Nevada
- He described the Alien Reproduction Vehicle (ARV) as having been "built by Northrop, McDonnell Douglas, and General Electric" — a claim that predates and influenced [Mark McCandlish](../Details/Mark_McCandlish.md)'s later, more technically detailed description of the same alleged craft
- He described propulsion systems involving rotating plasma contained in resonant cavities, counter-opposing magnetic fields, and quartz capacitor resonators connected to coils — descriptions that overlap with concepts in electrogravitics and electromagnetic propulsion research
- He claimed that recovered alien technology had been successfully reverse-engineered and that operational craft existed within classified programs

These claims parallel those made independently by [Bob Lazar](Bob_Lazar.md), who described gravity amplification propulsion systems at a facility near Area 51, and by [Phil Schneider](Phil_Schneider.md), who claimed involvement in underground facilities where alien technology was studied.

### Energy Systems and Black Budget Programs

Cooper described massive black-budget programs funded through hidden channels, claiming hundreds of billions of dollars were directed toward programs involving alien technology reverse-engineering. He described classified energy systems that he claimed were derived from recovered craft, including power generation technology far exceeding publicly known capabilities. These descriptions are thematically consistent with zero-point energy and advanced electromagnetic systems described by other figures in UAP research.

### Later Reversal on Extraterrestrial Claims

Significantly, Cooper later revised his position on extraterrestrial involvement. By 1999, he publicly stated: "There are no aliens, no alien abductions and no alien cattle mutilations." He came to believe that the UFO phenomenon was a government deception operation — that the technology was real but entirely human-made, and that the alien narrative was a psychological operation designed to conceal terrestrial black programs and to serve as a tool of social control. This reversal added complexity to his legacy: he maintained that the technology existed but reframed its origin.

### June 28, 2001 Broadcast

On June 28, 2001, Cooper broadcast an episode of *The Hour of the Time* in which he warned listeners to expect "a major attack" on the United States that would be blamed on Osama bin Laden. He stated that bin Laden was being set up as a scapegoat. When the September 11 attacks occurred approximately eleven weeks later, this broadcast was widely cited by supporters as evidence that Cooper had access to insider knowledge. Skeptics note that by mid-2001, multiple mainstream outlets including CNN had already published articles warning of potential bin Laden-directed attacks on U.S. targets.

## Death

In July 1998, Cooper was charged with tax evasion, and an arrest warrant was subsequently issued. Cooper evaded repeated attempts to serve the warrant over a period of more than three years, during which time he reportedly declared that he would not be taken alive.

On the night of November 5, 2001 — exactly two months after the September 11 attacks — Apache County sheriff's deputies arrived at Cooper's residence at 96 North Clearview Circle in Eagar, Arizona, to serve a warrant for aggravated assault and two counts of endangerment (related to confrontations with neighbors, separate from the tax charges). According to the official account, when deputies attempted to make contact, Cooper emerged from his home and opened fire, striking Deputy Robert Martinez in the head. Deputies returned fire, killing Cooper. Deputy Martinez survived his injuries.

The Arizona Department of Public Safety investigated the shooting. The official report concluded that Cooper initiated the gunfire and that the deputies' use of lethal force was justified.

### Circumstances Raising Questions

Supporters and researchers have raised several points about the circumstances:

- The timing of the raid — two months after September 11 and five months after Cooper's broadcast warning of an imminent attack blamed on bin Laden — struck many as suspicious
- Cooper had publicly stated for years that he expected to be killed by government agents, and some supporters interpret the confrontation as a provoked encounter
- The decision to serve the warrant at night, at the home of a known armed individual who had declared he would resist arrest, has been questioned as an unnecessary escalation
- Cooper's associate Doyel Shamley, who was present at the property during the raid, has given accounts that conflict with the official report in certain details
- The warrant was for relatively minor charges (assault and endangerment stemming from a neighbor dispute), yet the operation involved multiple armed deputies

### Official Position

Law enforcement maintains that Cooper was a fugitive who had evaded arrest for years, that he initiated the shooting, and that the deputies acted in lawful self-defense. No criminal charges were brought against any of the deputies involved.

## Legacy

*Behold a Pale Horse* has sold hundreds of thousands of copies and remains one of the most widely circulated books in conspiracy and UFO literature. It has been particularly influential in hip-hop culture and among communities skeptical of government transparency. The complete archive of Cooper's *Hour of the Time* broadcasts — approximately 1,900 episodes — is preserved on the Internet Archive and continues to attract new listeners.

Cooper's influence on the UAP disclosure movement is significant despite the controversies surrounding his claims. His early descriptions of recovered craft, government secrecy programs, and antigravity technology anticipated themes that would later emerge in mainstream UAP discourse through figures such as [Bob Lazar](Bob_Lazar.md), [Phil Schneider](Phil_Schneider.md), and eventually [David Grusch](David_Grusch.md), who testified under oath before Congress about classified crash-retrieval programs.

His later reversal — maintaining that the technology was real but human-made — represents a position that has gained traction among some UAP researchers who argue that at least some UAP sightings involve classified terrestrial craft rather than extraterrestrial vehicles.

## The Counterargument

- Cooper served in the Navy at a relatively junior level; critics argue that as a petty officer and quartermaster, he would not have had the security clearance required to access the type of classified intelligence documents he described
- Ufologists have accused Cooper of plagiarizing material from their research, including items that had been deliberately fabricated as hoaxes or pranks, undermining the credibility of his claimed firsthand access
- Cooper himself acknowledged that many UFO-related documents, including the original MJ-12 documents, were confirmed hoaxes — yet he originally presented some of this material as authentic
- His claims became increasingly broad over time, eventually encompassing nearly every major conspiracy theory, which critics argue suggests a pattern of accumulation rather than genuine insider knowledge
- No independent verification of his claimed access to classified Naval Intelligence documents has been produced
- The circumstances of his death, while tragic, are consistent with the official account of a fugitive who had declared he would resist arrest and who fired on law enforcement officers
- His reversal on extraterrestrial claims in 1999, while intellectually notable, also suggests that his earlier and most famous claims about alien contact may have been unreliable
