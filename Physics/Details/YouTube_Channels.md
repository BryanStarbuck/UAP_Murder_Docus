
# YouTube Channels on UAP Physics & Classified Breakthroughs

YouTube has become a primary platform for serious discussion of UAP physics, classified breakthroughs, exotic propulsion, and the suppression of public science. The channels and videos documented here range from dedicated UAP research channels run by former military pilots and independent journalists to specific episodes on major interview platforms where physicists, whistleblowers, and military insiders have made significant disclosures. This resource is organized into two sections: channels with sustained UAP physics coverage, and landmark individual videos on larger platforms.
