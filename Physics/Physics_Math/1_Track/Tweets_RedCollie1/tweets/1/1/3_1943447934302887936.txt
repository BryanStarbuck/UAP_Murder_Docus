OVERALL SUMMARY: Telescope photograph of Comet 17P/Holmes during its famous October/November 2007 outburst, showing the large spherical coma surrounding the nucleus. Taken by observer T. Scarmato in Calabria, Italy on November 19, 2007, using a Canon 10D camera with 8cm refractor at f/5 (15 exposures of 30 seconds each). The 2007 Holmes outburst was notable for producing one of the largest cometary comas ever observed, briefly making the comet visible to the naked eye.
