Newton's Third Law states: For every force exerted by body A on body B, there is an equal and opposite force exerted by body B on body A. In translational form:

**F**_AB = -**F**_BA

The claimed exception: when a spinning magnet applies an upward force to a counter-spinning flywheel through a shared magnetic field (spin/counter-spin configuration), the equal and opposite downward reaction is not returned to the spinning magnet or to the vehicle body, but instead is absorbed by the electromagnetic field. Source: 1929005668222042437.yaml, 1946698758093443281.yaml (note_tweet)
