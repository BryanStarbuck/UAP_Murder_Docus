## 7. Energy of 3I/ATLAS Propulsion System (Ion Thrusters Claim)

Source: 1989821797349691738.yaml, referenced_tweet text

A referenced tweet states: "3I/ATLAS seems to use a ring of six powerful ion thrusters."

Ion thrusters use electromagnetic acceleration of ionized propellant to produce thrust. Specific impulse (I_sp) for ion thrusters: 1,500 – 10,000 seconds (compared to ~450 s for liquid chemical rockets). The power requirement scales as:

P = (1/2) · F · v_exhaust = F · g₀ · I_sp / 2

For high specific impulse propulsion, high electrical power is required but mass flow is minimal. A "ring of six powerful ion thrusters" would require a substantial onboard power source — likely nuclear or some other compact high-energy-density source.

Drew's observation that 3I/ATLAS glows green due to nickel (tweet 1989821797349691738) is consistent with nickel as a propellant or structural material in an ion thruster system: nickel has a characteristic emission spectrum in the green (~341 nm, ~352 nm, ~376 nm excited state lines in the near-UV, with some green emission under high-energy excitation).
