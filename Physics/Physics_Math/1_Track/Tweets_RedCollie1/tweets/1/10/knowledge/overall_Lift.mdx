### Spatial Configuration for Lift

Critical design change: outer ring must be placed ABOVE the 6 coils, not below.

For m_z > 0 (upward dipole) and upward field increasing with z (∂B_z/∂z > 0), the force is upward. The counter-spinning outer ring above the central magnet position produces a stronger field above the center than below, giving the required positive gradient.

Alternatively stated: the central magnet is attracted upward toward the stronger field region created by the counter-spinning outer ring when the outer ring is positioned above.
