## Section: 2034175444287951081.yaml — Non-Magnetic ZrO2 Ball-Bearing: Materials Choice

"Now we are using a non-magnetic ZrO2 ball-bearing, and everything works fine!"

Zirconium dioxide (ZrO2, zirconia):
- Crystal structure: monoclinic (room temp), tetragonal (>1170°C), cubic (>2370°C)
- Density: 5.68 g/cm³
- Magnetic susceptibility: weakly diamagnetic (χ ≈ -10^-5, negligible)
- Electrical resistivity: ~10^10 Ω·m (electrical insulator)
- Thermal conductivity: ~2.5 W/(m·K) (low)
- Compressive strength: ~2000 MPa
- Mohs hardness: ~8.5

Selection rationale in context of spin/counter-spin device:
1. Non-magnetic: no perturbation of the designed magnetic field geometry
2. Non-conducting: no eddy current losses that would create braking torque
3. Hard/wear-resistant: suitable for the high-speed rotation of the spinning central magnet
4. Low friction (as a rolling element or journal bearing material)
