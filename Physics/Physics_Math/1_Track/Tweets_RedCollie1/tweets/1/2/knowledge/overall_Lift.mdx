This implies a downward body force on the crop with very short range (< 15 feet) — consistent with a strong local downward magnetic or electric force field (not pressure wave).

**Counter-rotation observed:** "The grass was going one way and the machine was going the other way" — ground effects rotated in one direction; craft body rotated counter to that. This is the Drew counter-rotation signature: the base field/vortex drives the grass clockwise while the craft body counter-rotates counterclockwise (or vice versa).

**Step-formation ascent:** "The big one, like the three big ones in the center took off first and the little ones followed. And they were just right over top of my head." — Sequenced ascent from a linked formation, with the central (larger) units lifting first. This is consistent with a coupled formation where the larger central units provide the primary lift field and the outer (smaller) auxiliary units provide supplementary field or stabilization.

**Instantaneous departure:** "All of a sudden they started to go slowly. And they just took off as a flat. They were gone." — Departure velocity far exceeding any conventional aircraft from a standing hover. No acceleration ramp-up was perceived; the craft went from hover to disappearance in a duration too short to observe. This is the canonical UAP "instant acceleration" behavior.
