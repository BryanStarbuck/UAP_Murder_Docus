## Section: 2018934125730857447.yaml — Three-Phase Rotating Field Mathematics

**Three phase voltages:**
  V_U(t) = V_0 cos(ωt)
  V_V(t) = V_0 cos(ωt - 2π/3)

**Magnetic field from 6-coil winding (U-V-W-U-V-W):**
With coils at angular positions 0°, 60°, 120°, 180°, 240°, 300° carrying phases U, V, W, U, V, W:

  B(φ, t) = B_0 [cos(ωt)cos(φ) + cos(ωt-2π/3)cos(φ-2π/3) + cos(ωt-4π/3)cos(φ-4π/3)
             + cos(ωt)cos(φ-π) + cos(ωt-2π/3)cos(φ-π-2π/3) + cos(ωt-4π/3)cos(φ-π-4π/3)]

Using product-to-sum identity: cos(A)cos(B) = ½[cos(A-B) + cos(A+B)]

  B(φ, t) = (3 B_0 / 2) cos(ωt - φ)   [dominant rotating component]

This is a pure rotating magnetic field in the azimuthal direction, rotating at angular frequency ω — the standard result for a balanced 3-phase winding with 2 coils per phase at 180° mechanical spacing.
