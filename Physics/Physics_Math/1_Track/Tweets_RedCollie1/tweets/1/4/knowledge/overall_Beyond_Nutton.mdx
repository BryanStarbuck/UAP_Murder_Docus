### Central Thesis: Specific Physics Classes That Appear to Violate Newton's Laws But Do Not

**Three demonstrations of apparent Newton's-Law violation:**

**1. Gyroscopic precession (spinning top + lateral air jet)**

Newton's Second Law for a non-spinning object: F = ma → a in the direction of F.
For a spinning gyroscope: dL/dt = τ → precessional motion perpendicular to τ.

The deviation from Newtonian prediction:
- Expected (non-spinning): Lateral force F_x → lateral acceleration a_x → motion in x-direction.
- Observed (spinning): Lateral torque τ_y → precession Ω_z (about Z-axis) → effective upward motion (for a top with downward angular momentum L = -Iω ẑ).

The critical non-Newtonian feature: no downward counter-force appears in the air jet. The source of the air jet experiences no measurable downward reaction. The angular momentum change is internal to the rotating body.

**Formal statement:** For a gyroscope, Newton's Third Law appears violated because the "reaction" to the applied torque is the precession itself — stored as a rate of change of angular momentum direction, not as a force on the torque source. This is not a violation of the full Newtonian/Lagrangian framework but is beyond the naive F = −F' pair intuition.

**2. Permanent-magnet brushed homopolar motor — Lorentz force with no mechanical back-reaction**

Newton's Third Law (naive): Torque on disc → equal and opposite torque on stator.
Observation: Disc spins; no observable reaction torque on stator in the expected direction.

**Formal resolution:** The Lorentz force on the current-carrying disc is F = J × B. By Maxwell's stress tensor, the equal and opposite force is distributed through the electromagnetic field, not through a localized mechanical contact. The Maxwell stress tensor T_ij = ε₀(E_iE_j − ½δ_ij E²) + (1/μ₀)(B_iB_j − ½δ_ij B²) carries the momentum transferred between the disc and the field source. In an ideal homopolar motor with perfect rotational symmetry, the net torque on the stator from the Maxwell stress tensor is zero.

**The beyond-Newton content:** Newton's Laws, formulated for mass points with contact forces, do not capture the momentum stored in electromagnetic fields. When field momentum dominates, the mechanical action-reaction pair is not the complete picture. Maxwell's equations contain the full correct description, but this is beyond Newton.

**3. Liquid-metal homopolar motor — same conclusion**

Identical physics to (2) but the liquid-metal brushes eliminate mechanical friction as a complicating factor. The "no back-reaction" result is cleaner and more directly attributable to the electromagnetic field momentum distribution rather than to mechanical friction artifacts.
