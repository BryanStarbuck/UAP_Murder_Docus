    **S** = (1/μ₀)(**E** × **B**)  [Poynting vector — energy flux]
    **g** = **S**/c² = ε₀(**E** × **B**)  [electromagnetic momentum density]
    **l** = **r** × **g**  [electromagnetic angular momentum density]

The Z-component of lift from electromagnetic angular momentum:

    F_z = -(d/dt) ∫ l_z dV + (surface flux of l_z)

If the device can sustain a continuous downward angular momentum flux, it maintains a continuous upward force.

Counter-spin: stator field rotates at ω, rotor spins at Ω in the *opposite* direction. The relative speed is ω + Ω (sums rather than differences). This creates:

When the device geometry breaks the up-down symmetry (disc above base magnet array, not symmetric), the Z-direction components of the oscillating Lorentz forces do not cancel, resulting in a net upward force.

The counter-spin configuration is proposed to maximize this Z-asymmetric force component. The specific series-wiring of coils is the electrical implementation of the counter-spin field topology that maximizes the Z-force at minimum input power.
