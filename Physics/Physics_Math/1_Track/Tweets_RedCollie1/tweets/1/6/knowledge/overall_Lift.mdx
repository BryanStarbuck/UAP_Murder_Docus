Ponderomotive force: F_pond = −(α/4) ∇(|E|²) or the magnetic equivalent F_pond = (μ₀/4) α_m ∇(|H|²). If the gradient of the field amplitude is non-zero along Z (as would be the case for a finite-radius coil stack), a net Z-directed ponderomotive force could result.
