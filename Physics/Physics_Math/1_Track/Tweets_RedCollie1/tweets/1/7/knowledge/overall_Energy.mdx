### Electromagnetic Energy Input Driving Against Gravity — Is Energy Conserved?

Drew's device uses electrical energy (three-phase AC from a variable-frequency drive) to produce mechanical rotation of the magnetic top and, claimed, a net upward force. This is NOT a violation of energy conservation in the direct sense: the three-phase power supply continuously inputs electrical energy, which is transduced into mechanical kinetic energy (rotation) and, during the rising phase, gravitational potential energy.

The energy balance:
  P_electrical_in = d/dt(E_kinetic_spin) + d/dt(E_gravitational_potential) + P_losses

where P_losses = Ohmic heating in coils + mechanical friction + electromagnetic radiation losses.

No over-unity (free energy) claim is made by Drew for the basic device. The anomaly is not energy generation but rather the direction of the energy transduction: electromagnetic energy → gravitational potential energy via the precessing magnetic top mechanism, bypassing the aerodynamic or rocket-reaction mechanism normally used for anti-gravity.

**What is anomalous energetically:**

In a standard electric motor, electrical energy converts to rotational kinetic energy and torque. No gravitational potential energy change occurs for the motor itself (the rotor spins in place). In Drew's device, the spinning magnetic top not only rotates but RISES — the electromagnetic drive is converting electrical input into gravitational potential energy of the entire assembly. This requires a vertical force component derived from the horizontal rotational input, which is the anomalous coupling.

The formation described: 300 meters in length, approximately 30 tonnes of flattened wheat, no heat signatures at ground level, made apparently in seconds (formation appeared between last observation and dawn — a time window of perhaps 1-2 hours at most).

Energy to flatten 30 tonnes of wheat mechanically:
  Work = F * d = M * g * h_reduction ~ 30,000 kg * 9.81 m/s^2 * 0.5 m (height reduction of stems) ≈ 147,000 J = 147 kJ

Time to deposit (assuming 10 seconds for instantaneous formation): Power ~ 147 kJ / 10 s = 14.7 kW

This is a modest power level. However, the absence of thermal signatures constrains the mechanism: if the energy were deposited as heat, the wheat temperature would need to increase by at most ΔT before reaching the dehydration temperature (~60°C). If the energy mechanism is purely mechanical (momentum transfer without heat), the power required is the same but the coupling mechanism must be electromagnetic (radiation pressure, Lorentz force on plant biopolymers, acoustic shock).

The discharge current flowing through the air above the field could induce a transient magnetic field pulse (B_pulse ~ mu_0 * I / (2*pi*r)) that, if large enough and properly directed, could exert Lorentz forces on the conducting sap in plant stems, bending them without thermal damage.

This is consistent with a field-induced (inductive) rather than thermal energy deposition mechanism.
