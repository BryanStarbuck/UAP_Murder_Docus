## XY-Plane Dynamics Causing Z-Axis Lift: The Physical Argument

The core paradox Drew is trying to resolve: how does horizontal rotation (XY-plane spin) produce vertical (Z-axis) lift?

**Candidate mechanisms from the physics:**

1. **Gyroscopic precession coupling**: A spinning disc precesses under an applied torque. If the magnetic force on the disc has both horizontal and vertical components, the gyroscopic response to the horizontal component can produce a net vertical force. Specifically, if the outer N-ring exerts a radially inward magnetic force on the spinning S disc (horizontal, centripetal), the gyroscopic reaction to this force is a vertical torque, which translates into a vertical displacement force. The magnitude scales as F_z ∝ F_radial · ω_spin / ω_precession.

2. **Magnetohydrodynamic pressure**: The rotating magnetic field drives currents in the conducting parts of the device. These currents, interacting with the field, produce a J×B body force. The geometry of the nested spin system (inner CW + outer CCW) creates a divergent J×B pattern that has a net upward component if the field geometry breaks the up-down symmetry (which it does, since the drive coils are below or to the side, not above the lifting magnet).

3. **Non-conservative spin-orbit coupling**: In the spin + counter-spin configuration, the effective potential seen by the central disc magnet is time-varying (due to the counter-rotation of the outer ring). A time-varying potential can extract energy from the rotation and convert it to translational kinetic energy (Z-direction) if the frequency of the potential variation is resonant with the natural oscillation frequency of the disc magnet on its Z-axis equilibrium. This is a parametric resonance mechanism.

Drew does not explicitly select among these mechanisms; the experimental program is intended to determine which is operative. The most recent stated conclusion (page_008.json, tweet 2016310121002107303): "For UFO physics, I can make the upward spinning force with certainty."

## Scaling Arguments and UFO Size

From page_008.json (note_tweet of 2016050711683891370): "Anti-gravity would be much better than rockets."

This is Drew's motivation for the entire research program. Classical rocket propulsion requires expulsion of mass at high velocity (Newton's third law). For a rocket with initial mass M₀ and exhaust velocity v_e, the final velocity is v_f = v_e · ln(M₀/M_f) (Tsiolkovsky rocket equation). For orbital velocity (~7.9 km/s) with v_e ≈ 4.5 km/s (chemical rockets), the mass ratio M₀/M_f = e^(7900/4500) ≈ e^1.76 ≈ 5.8 — meaning >80% of launch mass must be propellant.

A magnetic lift mechanism (if it works) requires no propellant, eliminating the mass ratio penalty. The power requirement scales as F_z · v_z (force times vertical velocity), which for a given power plant is fundamentally more efficient for altitude gain than rocket propulsion.
