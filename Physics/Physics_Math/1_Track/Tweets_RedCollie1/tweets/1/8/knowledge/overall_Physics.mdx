## Right-Hand Rule and Rotary Induction Motor Application

**Rotary induction motor principle:** The rotating magnetic field from N stator coils (via 3-phase current) induces eddy currents in the rotor. By Lenz's law, these induced currents create a magnetic field opposing the change that caused them. The interaction between the stator's rotating field and the rotor's induced field creates a torque. The slip speed (ω_s - ω_r) determines the induced EMF and hence the current and torque.
