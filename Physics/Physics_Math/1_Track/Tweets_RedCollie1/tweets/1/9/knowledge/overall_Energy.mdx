### Vacuum-Pushing — Interaction with the Quantum Vacuum as Energy Source

"They fly by 'pushing against the vacuum'."

The quantum vacuum is not empty. Quantum field theory requires that every field mode
(k, polarization) possesses a zero-point energy ½ℏω_k even when no photons are
present. The total vacuum energy density (with a UV cutoff at the Planck momentum
k_max = 1/l_Planck):

    ρ_vac = (ℏ / (2(2π)³)) ∫₀^{k_max} ω(k) 4πk² dk

For electromagnetic field with ω(k) = ck:
    ρ_vac ~ ℏ c k_max⁴ / (16π²) ~ 10¹¹³ J/m³

This enormous energy density is ordinarily undetectable because it is uniform and
its gradient is zero. If an oscillating electromagnetic system can couple to vacuum
field modes — creating local gradients in the vacuum energy density through
coherent amplitudes — it could exchange momentum with the vacuum field. This is the
physical basis of "pushing against the vacuum."

The Casimir force provides laboratory evidence that the vacuum energy is physically
real and can produce measurable forces:
    F_Casimir = -(π² ℏ c A) / (240 d⁴)

where A is the plate area and d is the separation. This demonstrates that
macroscopic geometry can modify the local vacuum energy density and produce
mechanical forces from the vacuum without any reaction mass.

If a spinning magnetic system creates a spatial asymmetry in the effective vacuum
mode density (through interaction of the rotating EM fields with vacuum
fluctuations), a sustained directional force could result — the vacuum absorbing
the reaction impulse rather than ejecting mass. This is the proposed physical basis
for propellantless propulsion and lift.
