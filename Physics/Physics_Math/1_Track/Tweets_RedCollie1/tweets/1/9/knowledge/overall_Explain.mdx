### Jumping Ring Experiment as Precursor

The "jumping ring" experiment (AC electromagnetic induction) is cited as the
nearest classical analog to UFO levitation. In the standard Lenz's Law
demonstration, an AC-excited solenoid induces eddy currents in an aluminum ring
placed over it; the ring's induced magnetic moment opposes the solenoid's changing
flux, producing a repulsive (upward) force.

Drew's proposed extension: add fast spin to the rising magnet in the AC jumping
ring configuration. The hypothesis is that combining the translational upward force
from the induced-eddy-current repulsion with a gyroscopic/precessional effect from
fast spin of the inner magnet produces greater upward force than either effect
alone.

Experimental result reported: three-wire coils with 3-phase power, and a spinning
magnet, do not give enough upward force by themselves. The spin-plus-levitation
combination is the target.
