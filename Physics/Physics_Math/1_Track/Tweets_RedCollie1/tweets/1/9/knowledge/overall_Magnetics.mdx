### Magnetic Spinning Top — Precession Reversal

Standard top: precesses in the same rotational direction as its spin angular
momentum vector. For a top spinning clockwise (viewed from above), the precession
is clockwise.

Magnetic spinning top (claimed new phenomenon): "A magnetic top being spun up
rather than falling down, precesses in the opposite direction. Never done before on
Earth."

This is a claimed novel result. In classical gyroscope mechanics, the precession
rate is Ω_p = τ/L where τ = r × F_gravity (the gravitational torque), and
L = Iω (the spin angular momentum). The direction of precession is determined by
the right-hand rule applied to Ω_p = (r × F)/L. For a magnetic top in a magnetic
field, an additional torque τ_mag = m × B (where m is the magnetic dipole moment
and B is the external field) supplements the gravitational torque. Drew claims that
under the magnetic influence the precession direction reverses — i.e., the magnetic
torque τ_mag dominates and its cross-product orientation with L is opposite to that
of the gravitational torque.

The significance: if the precession direction can be controlled magnetically, the
gyroscopic coupling between the precession plane and the spin axis can be
redirected to produce force in a chosen direction, including Z-axis (vertical) lift.

### Faraday Disk — Induced Magnetic Field Under Rapid Spin

The Faraday disk (homopolar generator/motor): a conducting disc rotating in an
axial magnetic field. When spinning, the conducting disc generates a radial EMF
(the homopolar EMF):

V = ½ ω B r²

where ω is the angular velocity of the disc, B is the axial magnetic flux density,
and r is the disc radius. In the motor configuration, applying a radial DC current
(via liquid-metal brushes at center and rim) in an axial magnetic field produces a
torque T = B I A_eff driving spin.

The critical additional claim from the crop-picture interpretation: "We may have to
apply 3-phase power to a Faraday disk to achieve UFO levitation. It has an induced
magnetic field when in rapid spin." The induced field is the self-field of the
spinning disc currents. For a disc spinning with surface current density J_surface,
the induced axial magnetic field at the disc center is of order B_induced ~ μ₀ J r.
The 3-phase field superimposed on this self-field creates a rotating field
interaction.

Video transcript: "I'm gonna add juice to this circuit and the wires will rotate
one direction and the disc will rotate the other direction. But the wires are not
enough mass to start the disc rotating in the opposite direction. Yet the disc
starts rotating in the opposite direction. Where are the torques interacting? This
is not equal and opposite torques."

The torque asymmetry: the wires experience a Lorentz force F = I L × B (force on a
current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field). By Newton's Third Law, the disc
should experience an equal and opposite torque. The observation that the disc
rotates opposite to the wire rotation, despite the wires having insufficient mass
to account for it by mechanical reaction, is the anomalous result. Drew interprets
this as the homopolar motor exhibiting an apparent violation of Newton's Third Law
of Motion.

### Spin and Counter-Spin — Two Implementation Strategies

Strategy 1: Combination of clockwise and anti-clockwise fast-spinning 3-phase
magnetic fields. In 3-phase AC systems, a rotating magnetic field is produced by
three coils spaced 120° apart, driven by currents phase-shifted by 120°:

B_x(t) = B₀ cos(ωt)
B_y(t) = B₀ cos(ωt - 2π/3)
B_z-component from third coil = B₀ cos(ωt - 4π/3)

The superposition of these three fields produces a rotating field vector in the
coil plane with angular frequency ω. Reversing the phase sequence (UVW → UWV)
reverses the rotation direction. A clockwise and an anti-clockwise 3-phase field
simultaneously applied — using two independent sets of 3-phase coils with opposite
phase sequences — creates an interference pattern with nodes and antinodes in the
combined field that is not achievable with a single rotating field.

Strategy 2: A special combination of 6 coils (implied from the video text referencing
crop circle crop pictures with a six-coil ring).

### N/S Magnet Configuration on Counter-Rotating Rings

Crop picture evidence: two big rings each covered in N/S magnet. The proposed
physical architecture: inner flywheel and outer flywheel with permanent magnet poles
arranged alternately (N-S-N-S) around each ring. Counter-rotation of the two rings
creates a time-varying magnetic field at any fixed point in space between them.

If the inner ring has poles of angular spacing Δθ and spins at ω₁ and the outer
ring spins at ω₂ in the opposite direction, then the effective pole-spacing
frequency seen at a fixed point is:

f_eff = (ω₁ + ω₂) × (N_poles / 2π)

where N_poles is the number of pole-pairs per ring. This generates an AC magnetic
field even from permanent magnets, with frequency determined by both spin rates.
The superposition of the two counter-rotating pole patterns creates a complex
spatially non-uniform field with radial and azimuthal components.
