## I. Energy Input to Proposed Propulsion Device: 3-Phase AC Electrical

Source tweet (1928420628841041943, note_tweet):
"If we arrange six wire coils into the shape of a hexagon, and give 3-phase
power, we can create a helical magnetic field."

Source tweet (1971332491660476417):
"Three-phase power like for the fans in your kitchen or bathroom. Three wire
coils. Makes things SPIN."

### Energy input mechanism:
* The proposed device is powered by 3-phase alternating current — a standard
  industrial electrical supply.
* Energy is delivered to 6 wire coils (or 3 coil pairs), creating a rotating
  magnetic field.
* This is not an over-unity (free energy) claim: the device requires external
  electrical power input.
* The energy efficiency claim is propulsion efficiency: electrical energy is
  converted to kinetic energy (lift) without the thermodynamic losses of
  combustion and without the mass penalty of reaction propellant.

### Energy conversion pathway:
Electrical energy (3-phase AC)
-> Rotating magnetic field (electromagnetic energy in coil array)
-> Torque on central permanent magnet (mechanical energy, rotational)
-> [Anomalous coupling] -> Vertical translation of central magnet (mechanical
   energy, gravitational PE increase)
-> [Missing step] -> Whole device lift (kinetic energy of craft)

The missing step (coupling from spinning magnet lift to whole-craft lift) is the
key unsolved energy pathway.

## II. Gravitational Potential Energy Increase: Anomalous Energy State

Source tweet (1958069037126361427, note_tweet, 2025-08-20):
"That 'spinning vortex' increases the gravitational potential energy of a
central spinning magnet, yet it does not make the whole device 'rise off the
ground' like for a UFO."

### Energy analysis:
* Gravitational PE of central magnet: E_grav = m * g * h
* If h increases (magnet rises), E_grav increases.
* Energy source for this PE increase: the rotating magnetic field (3-phase AC).
* Power delivered to magnet (gravitational PE rate): P_grav = dE_grav/dt = m*g*(dh/dt)
* Power input from coil system must exceed P_grav (some energy goes to rotational KE
  and ohmic losses in coils).

The anomalous aspect is not energy creation (energy is conserved from the
electrical input) but the FORM of energy transfer: electrical energy -> gravitational
PE via a magnetic intermediary, with no mechanical contact.

* Whether the mechanism is "anomalous" depends on whether it is explainable by
  the helical gradient-B force (standard EM, Section III of overall_Physics.mdx)
  or requires a new coupling term.

### Possible physical candidates (not stated by Drew, inferred from context):
* Biophoton emission: weak coherent light emission (~1-1000 photons/cm^2/sec)
  from biological tissue, in the visible to near-UV range.
* Thermal infrared: all objects at body temperature (~310 K) emit ~10 micron IR

* Bioelectric field: skin surface potentials of a few millivolts to hundreds of
  millivolts, associated with ion transport across membranes. These could drive
  a capacitively-coupled LED detector.
* Unknown emission: if none of the above account for the LED response, a novel
  energy type would be implied.

### Experimental status: claimed experimental result; device described but
not characterized quantitatively in available tweets.

"Why are you still using jets, 80 years after they were first invented?"

## VI. 3I/ATLAS Propulsion Energy: Rocket Burn + Gravity (Classical)

Source tweet (1984911816301170747, note_tweet):
"If some small spacecraft detaches from 3I/ATLAS, and does a hot-blue rocket-burn,
that reduces its speed from 130,000 to 67,000 mph."

* "Hot-blue rocket-burn": consistent with hydrogen-oxygen or fluorine-based
  chemical rockets (blue-white exhaust from high-temperature combustion).
* This is conventional chemical propulsion — Drew uses it as the hypothetical
  mechanism for a sub-craft detaching from 3I/ATLAS, contrasting with the
  magnetic propulsion he is developing for terrestrial UAPs.
* The delta-v of 63,000 mph (28.2 km/s) requires enormous propellant mass per
  Tsiolkovsky's rocket equation:
  m_prop/m_total = 1 - exp(-Delta_v / v_exhaust)
  For v_exhaust ~ 4.5 km/s (H2/O2 rocket):
  m_prop/m_total = 1 - exp(-28.2/4.5) = 1 - exp(-6.27) ~ 1 - 0.0019 = 0.9981
  => ~99.8% of the spacecraft mass must be propellant. Impractical for a large craft.
  For the alien drone with magnetic propulsion: no propellant fraction required.

## VII. Energy Not Stored: No Battery or Zero-Point Energy Claim

Drew's posts do not claim zero-point energy (ZPE) or vacuum energy extraction
as the energy source for the proposed propulsion device. The energy source is
explicitly identified as 3-phase AC electrical power (an external supply).

This distinguishes Drew's thesis from ZPE-based propulsion claims (e.g., the
Haisch-Rueda inertia hypothesis, the Casimir-force drives proposed in the
1990s-2000s). Drew's mechanism is:
* Not over-unity.
* Not zero-point energy extraction.
* Anomalous in the CONVERSION of electrical energy to gravitational PE (via
  magnetic coupling) rather than in the energy SOURCE.

## VIII. Energy of Crop Formation: Physical Constraint Analysis

From image description 3_2025112019356282880.txt and tweet 2018623329180307491:

Physical energy requirements of the formation:
* Mass of wheat flattened: ~37 tons (from tweet 2018623329180307491 context)
* Area: 500 x 300 meters = 150,000 m^2 (from tweet 2045649639228186680:
  "500 x 300 meters of private unpaid land, in just a few hours of darkness")
* Duration: < 4 hours (English midsummer darkness)
* Average power required to flatten a wheat stalk: if bending energy ~ 0.1 J
  per stalk, and average stalk density ~ 200 stalks/m^2, energy per m^2 =
  20 J/m^2. Total energy = 20 * 150,000 = 3 megajoules.
* Power = 3 MJ / (4 * 3600 s) ~ 208 W. This is within the range of a 200-watt
  device, suggesting energy is not the limiting constraint.
* However, the directed precision required (complex geometric patterns with no
  visible surveying equipment or personnel) points to a directed-beam or field
  technology rather than bulk energy delivery.

Note: 37 tons of wheat over 150,000 m^2 gives 0.247 kg/m^2, which is reasonable
for dense wheat. The energy calculation above assumes simple bending; actual
requirements may include thermal effects on node tissue (Levengood's
magnetite/microwave node-exploding hypothesis), which would require higher power.
