* Powered by 3-phase alternating current (same principle as 3-phase induction

* 3-phase power creates a rotating magnetic field vector in the plane of the coil

* A central permanent magnet placed within this rotating field experiences a
  helical torque — it spins about the vertical (Z) axis AND rises vertically.
* The rising motion (Z-axis lift) is the anomalous output: standard induction
  motor theory predicts rotational torque, not net vertical translation.
