Source tweet (2040006468402069701, note_tweet):
"Those 6 wire coils make another ring of N-S magnets spin in the opposite
direction, possibly to bypass Newton's 3rd Law."

### Lift in the context of Newton's 3rd Law:
* If the 3-coil sub-system exerts an upward force on the central magnet, the
  coil assembly experiences an equal downward force (Newton's 3rd).
* This would press the craft into the ground, not lift it.
* Drew's proposed solution: the 6-coil inner sub-system simultaneously drives
  the outer magnetic ring DOWNWARD (reaction to its upward force on the outer
  ring), such that the downward force on the outer ring is transferred to the
  environment (air/ground) rather than to the craft frame.
* Alternatively: the counter-rotating ring creates an equal and opposite angular
  momentum internally, eliminating the need to exchange linear or angular
  momentum with the environment.

### Mechanism analogy:
* Similar in concept to a helicopter: main rotor lifts the body, tail rotor
  counters the reaction torque. But in this system, the counter-rotation is
  magnetic and internal, with no tail rotor equivalent.
* The key difference from a helicopter: the proposed system does not expel
  air downward as reaction mass (no downwash). Lift source is claimed to be
  purely from field-matter coupling.

    the Stern-Gerlach force in the atomic physics context.
  - If the helical field gradient force (Section III of overall_Physics.mdx) is
    the lift mechanism, polarity independence is automatically satisfied.
