## Experimental Configuration: Hexagonal Coil Array + 3-Phase Power

### Device geometry:
* 6 wire coils arranged at the vertices of a regular hexagon.
* Coil plane is horizontal (XY plane).
* Central permanent magnet placed at the geometric center of the hexagon,
  with its magnetic dipole axis vertical (Z axis) or possibly unconstrained.

### 3-Phase Power Input:
* 3-phase alternating current (phases separated by 120 degrees) is supplied
  to the 6 coils, likely in pairs (each phase drives two opposing coils).
* This generates a rotating magnetic field vector that sweeps through 360
  degrees in the XY plane with angular frequency omega = 2*pi*f, where f is
  the AC supply frequency.
* The rotating field vector is identical in principle to the rotating stator
  field of a 3-phase induction motor.

### Observed outcome:
* The central permanent magnet experiences the rotating XY field and responds
  by:
  (a) SPINNING: rotating about the Z-axis (standard induction motor response).
  (b) RISING: translating upward along the Z-axis (anomalous, non-standard).
* The Z-axis rising is the claimed non-textbook phenomenon.
