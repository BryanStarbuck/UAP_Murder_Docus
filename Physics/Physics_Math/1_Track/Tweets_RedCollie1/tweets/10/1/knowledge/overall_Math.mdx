## I. 3-Phase Rotating Magnetic Field (Foundational Math)

### Phase currents (balanced 3-phase system):
I_A(t) = I_0 * cos(omega_e * t)
I_B(t) = I_0 * cos(omega_e * t - 2*pi/3)
I_C(t) = I_0 * cos(omega_e * t - 4*pi/3)

where:
* I_0 = peak current amplitude [Amperes]
* omega_e = 2*pi*f = electrical angular frequency [rad/s]
* f = supply frequency (e.g., 50 Hz or 60 Hz)
* Phase offsets: 0, 2*pi/3, 4*pi/3 (120 degree separation)

### Superposition of magnetic fields from 3 coil pairs:
B_net(t) = (3/2) * B_individual * [cos(omega_e * t) x_hat + sin(omega_e * t) y_hat]

where:
* B_individual = peak field per phase at the center
* B_net is a constant-magnitude vector rotating at omega_e in the XY plane
* |B_net| = (3/2) * B_individual = constant (no pulsation, purely rotating)

## II. Magnetic Dipole Torque in Rotating Field

For a permanent magnet with dipole moment:
m = m_z * z_hat (initially aligned with Z-axis)

In field B_net:
tau = m x B_net
    = m_z * z_hat x (B_0 * cos(omega_e * t) * x_hat + B_0 * sin(omega_e * t) * y_hat)
    = m_z * B_0 * cos(omega_e * t) * (z_hat x x_hat) + m_z * B_0 * sin(omega_e * t) * (z_hat x y_hat)
    = m_z * B_0 * cos(omega_e * t) * y_hat + m_z * B_0 * sin(omega_e * t) * (-x_hat)
    = m_z * B_0 * [-sin(omega_e * t) * x_hat + cos(omega_e * t) * y_hat]

* tau lies entirely in the XY plane — drives azimuthal (phi) precession of m.
* No Z-component of torque: tau_z = 0.
* Standard EM predicts SPIN only; no Z-force.

## III. Helical Magnetic Field Parameterization

B_helix(r, phi, z, t) = B_0 * [sin(k*z - omega*t) * phi_hat + cos(k*z - omega*t) * z_hat]

where:
* k = 2*pi / lambda_pitch [m^-1] = wavenumber along Z-axis
* lambda_pitch = pitch length of helix
* omega = angular rotation frequency [rad/s]
* phi_hat, z_hat = cylindrical unit vectors

### Magnetic dipole potential energy in helical field:
U = -m . B_helix = -m_0 * B_0 * cos(k*z - omega*t - phi_m)

where phi_m is the azimuthal phase of the dipole moment m.

### Force on dipole (gradient force):
F = -nabla(U) = -m_0 * B_0 * k * sin(k*z - omega*t - phi_m) * z_hat
                -m_0 * B_0 * (1/r) * d/dphi[cos(k*z - omega*t - phi_m)] * phi_hat

At the synchronous (phase-locked) condition where phi_m tracks omega*t:
phi_m = omega*t + phi_0

F_z = -m_0 * B_0 * k * sin(k*z - phi_0)

For appropriate z and phi_0 such that sin(k*z - phi_0) < 0:
F_z > 0 (upward force)

This is the magnetic mirror / gradient-B force that could produce the observed
Z-axis rise without violating standard EM — provided the hexagonal coil geometry
produces a helical (rather than purely planar rotating) field.

## IV. Angular Momentum in Counter-Rotating System

### Sub-system angular momenta:
L_1 = I_1 * omega_1 (central magnet, positive direction)
L_2 = I_2 * omega_2 (outer ring, omega_2 < 0, opposite direction)

### Zero-net-angular-momentum condition:
L_total = L_1 + L_2 = I_1 * omega_1 + I_2 * omega_2 = 0
=> omega_2 = -(I_1/I_2) * omega_1

### Implications:
* If L_total = 0, the system exchanges zero angular momentum with the
  environment.
* By conservation of angular momentum, the craft housing does not rotate
  (or rotates only due to bearing friction).
* Newton's 3rd Law for rotational systems: if the coil system drives the
  central magnet (reaction torque on coils), and simultaneously drives the
  outer ring in the opposite direction (reaction torque equal and opposite),
  the net torque on the housing frame = 0.
* This is not actually a violation of Newton's 3rd Law but a redistribution
  of angular momentum WITHIN the system.

## V. Gravitational Potential Energy Anomaly

Standard gravitational PE:
E_grav = m * g * h

where m = inertial mass, g = 9.81 m/s^2, h = height above reference.

Drew's claim: the spinning vortex increases E_grav of the central spinning
magnet beyond what standard kinematics predicts. If we define h(t) as the
height of the magnet's center of mass:

dh/dt = F_z(t) / m (where F_z is the net vertical force)

If F_z =/= 0 from the helical gradient force (Section III), then h increases,
and E_grav = m*g*h increases accordingly. This is NOT anomalous — it is
precisely the expected result of an upward gradient-B force.

The true claim that would be anomalous: E_grav increases WITHOUT h increasing
(i.e., the inertial mass effectively decreases while gravitational mass stays
constant, violating the equivalence principle). Drew's tweets do not fully
clarify which interpretation is meant; the anomalous statement ("none of this
is in physics textbooks") suggests the latter.
