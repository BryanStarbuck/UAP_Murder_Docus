# Energy Claims: Power Sources, Anomalous Observations, and Conservation Considerations

## 1. Three-Phase AC Power as the Input Energy Source

The primary energy input to Drew's prototype UFO drive is three-phase alternating current (AC)
delivered to 6 (or 3) wire coils. This is conventional electrical energy — no exotic or
zero-point energy source is claimed or required for the benchtop prototype.

Three-phase power provides:
* A rotating magnetic field (the fundamental feature of three-phase induction motor technology).
* Phase relationships: V_1 = V_0 sin(omega*t), V_2 = V_0 sin(omega*t + 2pi/3),
  V_3 = V_0 sin(omega*t + 4pi/3), where omega = 2*pi*f (supply frequency).
* The rotating field rotates at angular velocity omega (or at omega/P for P pole pairs in the
  stator geometry), providing continuous torque to the central spinning magnet.

No claim of over-unity or energy creation is made for the prototype. The energy source is
the three-phase electrical grid.

## 3. The Non-Newton's Third Law Claim: Implications for Energy Conservation

The core claim in tweet 1992723258538696892:
"This Lorentz force does NOT follow Newton's 3rd Law. So theoretically it should be capable
of allowing upward force, without any downward force, if it cancels overall spin angular momentum."

Analysis of energy conservation implications:
If a force F_z (upward) acts on the craft without an equal and opposite downward force on the
environment (truly reactionless), then to lift a mass m by height h, the work done is:
  W = m * g * h

If this work is supplied entirely by the electromagnetic input power P_input, then:
  P_input * dt = m * g * v_z * dt  =>  P_input = m * g * v_z

This is energetically consistent with energy conservation — the input electrical power is
converted to gravitational potential energy. No violation of energy conservation is required
or claimed. The claim is about momentum conservation (Newton's Third Law), not energy
conservation.

Whether the electromagnetic field carries the momentum (resolving the apparent Newton's
Third Law violation via field momentum) or whether there is a genuine violation remains
unresolved in the source material.

## 4. Energy Flow in the Liquid Metal Vortex

The liquid metal vortex receives energy from:
* DC current injection (Ohmic heating: P = J^2/sigma_conductivity per unit volume).
* J x B body force doing work on the fluid: P_EM = f_body * v_fluid = (J x B) * v

For a conducting fluid with conductivity sigma [S/m], the power balance per unit volume:
  d/dt (rho * v^2 / 2) = (J x B) * v - viscous dissipation - thermal conduction losses

At steady state, the kinetic energy of rotation is balanced by the input electromagnetic power
minus losses (viscosity, thermal). The spinning liquid metal acts as an energy-storing flywheel
as well as the active medium for the lift mechanism.

Liquid metals appropriate for this application (based on conductivity and low melting point):
* Mercury (Hg): sigma = 1.04 * 10^6 S/m, melting point = -38.8°C. Available as liquid at room
  temperature but toxic.
* Gallium (Ga): sigma = 7.1 * 10^6 S/m, melting point = 29.8°C. Non-toxic, liquid near room temp.
* Galinstan (Ga-In-Sn alloy): melting point ~ -19°C, sigma ~ 3.46 * 10^6 S/m. Non-toxic.
* Lead-bismuth eutectic (LBE): melting point = 123.5°C, used in nuclear reactor coolant loops.

The source material does not specify which liquid metal is used; gallium or galinstan would be
appropriate for a benchtop prototype due to low melting point and non-toxicity.

  pressure) is also anomalous for a standard comet — requiring a non-radiation-pressure
  explanation for the emission direction of large particles.

Drew's interpretation (tweet 1950805613400986073, note_tweet): the most logical explanation
is intelligent (alien) control of the object, hence the atypical flow characteristics.

## 9. Power Scaling for UFO Craft

From tweet 1939298175808053455 (referenced tweet — user comment):
"Lift = more power / Down = less power"

This describes a linear relationship between input power P_input and lift force F_z:
  F_z ~ P_input

If the lift mechanism is the Lorentz force (F = qv x B = I*L * B for a conductor), and I
(current) scales linearly with input power at fixed voltage, then:
  F_z = I * L * B ~ P_input   (at fixed B and geometry)

For a large-scale craft with mass M_craft, the minimum power for hover is:
  P_hover = M_craft * g * v_exhaust_equivalent / eta_EM

where eta_EM is the electromagnetic efficiency. Unlike a conventional rocket (where v_exhaust
is a fixed property of the propellant chemistry), the electromagnetic coupling efficiency
depends on the magnet/coil geometry and the conductivity of the liquid metal or plasma.
