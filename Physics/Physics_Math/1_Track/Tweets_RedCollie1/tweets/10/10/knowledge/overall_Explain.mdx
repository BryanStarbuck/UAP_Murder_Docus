# Bridging Claim: Novel Spin-Rise Effect as UFO Levitation Precursor

## Core Thesis (Experimental + Interpretive)

Dr. Horace Drew (RedCollie1) claims to have reproduced in a private laboratory a novel electromagnetic effect
first described by a crop picture that appeared near Barbury Castle, England, in July 1991. The effect is:

* A permanent magnet, driven to spin horizontally by three-phase AC power delivered through six wire coils
  arranged symmetrically around it, spontaneously rises along a vertical axis (Z-axis ascent) as a
  consequence of that spin, without any direct vertical thrust mechanism.

The Barbury Castle 1991 crop picture is interpreted by Drew as encoding the "horizontal-spin, then
rise-precession of a spinning top" using 3-phase power. The apparatus reproduces this: six wire coils
providing 3-phase magnetism to a central spinning magnet cause the magnet to spin and then rise. Video
evidence from the lab (13_1928965887698284544_video.mp4) shows the magnet descending when power is off,
spinning when power is applied, and rising — and resisting being pushed back down while spinning.

The advancement beyond Newtonian mechanics claimed is:

* Conventional electromagnetic motors produce torque in a plane (XY), not net force along Z.
* The Lorentz force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field (F = qv x B) is invoked
  to explain a net upward force that does not produce a Newtonian reaction downward force, at least
  in the claimed experimental geometry.
* Drew states explicitly: "This Lorentz force does NOT follow Newton's 3rd Law. So theoretically
  it should be capable of allowing upward force, without any downward force, if it cancels overall
  spin angular momentum." (tweet 1992723258538696892)

## Proposed UFO Drive Architecture (Three-Component Model)

Derived from interpretation of 50-100 crop pictures and laboratory prototyping:

Component 1: A spinning, liquid-metal vortex — the primary rotor medium. The Lorentz force (F = J x B,
where J is current density and B is magnetic flux density) acts on current-carrying liquid metal in an
external magnetic field to produce rotational motion (spin) in the XY plane.

Component 2: Six wire coils arranged around the outside of the vortex, energized with DC current,
providing a static confining magnetic field (analogous to a solenoid ring or Helmholtz coil geometry)
to hold the spinning vortex in place and define the geometry of the field.

Component 3: Three wire coils positioned just below the spinning vortex, driven with three-phase AC
current. These provide the rotating magnetic field (analogous to the stator of an induction motor) that
drives the liquid-metal vortex to spin, and also provide the underlying Lorentz force coupling that
produces the claimed Z-axis rise.

The full benefit claimed: anti-gravity or reactionless lift — vertical ascent of the spinning assembly
without ejecting mass downward, thereby circumventing the rocket equation and enabling inertia-free
flight if angular momentum is cancelled by contra-rotation.

## Newton's Third Law and the Contra-Rotation Solution

Drew recognizes the standard problem: if the rotor rises (Z-axis force), Newton's Third Law demands
a reaction force pushing the frame downward, equivalent to torque imparted to the 3-phase wire coils.
His proposed solution is:

* Two contra-rotating discs (tweet 1939298175808053455): "I will try TWO contra-rotating discs next,
  to eliminate (cancel) net torque on the 3-phase wire coils. We already have lots of lift, but also
  lots of downward torque by Newton's 3rd Law."
* Cancel net angular momentum L_total = L_rotor + L_counter-rotor = 0, such that no net torque
  is imparted to the frame.
* In the large-scale (real UFO) version, Drew proposes that high-voltage DC gaseous plasma provides
  the counter-spin, because constructing a mechanical ball bearing at meter-scale is impractical.
  The plasma ionization also explains the blue corona observed around some UFOs.

## Spin Angular Momentum and the Einstein-de Haas Effect (Electron Spin)

A separate but related experiment (video 13_1935610801450070016_video.mp4) demonstrates the
Einstein-de Haas effect: if electron spin angular momentum is real and not merely a mathematical
construct, flipping electron spin states simultaneously in a ferromagnetic bolt by reversing the
current through surrounding coils should produce a macroscopic mechanical rotation of the bolt.

The bolt is suspended from a string. An electric current through coils flips electron spins
simultaneously. The experiment observes a macroscopic rotation, confirming that electron spin angular
momentum is a real mechanical quantity — angular momentum is transferred from the quantum subsystem
(electrons) to the classical macroscopic body (bolt).

Quantitative context from the transcript: electron mass is approximately 0.03% of total atom mass.
Therefore the ratio of electron angular momentum to total atom angular momentum is small, and the
macroscopic rotation is very small but detectable by the string-suspension torsion method.

The relevance to propulsion: if spin angular momentum at the quantum scale is a real, physically
manipulable quantity, then controlling the collective spin state of a macroscopic spinning medium
(such as a liquid-metal vortex) may allow coupling of quantum-scale angular momentum to macroscopic
mechanical motion — potentially enabling propulsion effects not accessible via classical mechanics alone.

## Lorentz Force in Liquid Metal Vortex: Theoretical Mechanism

From tweet 1992580530165125291 (referenced tweet to 1992723258538696892):
"These two crop pictures, drawn at almost the same time in May 1999, show how UFO levitation works
in part due to the Lorentz spin of liquid metal in a magnetic field.
Slide 1 shows the flow of liquid metal with DC current alone, and NO magnetic field.
Slide 2 shows [flow with magnetic field applied]."

The mechanism: J x B force (Lorentz body force in magnetohydrodynamics, or MHD) drives a conducting
liquid metal into circular flow (spin) when an external magnetic field B is applied perpendicular to
the current density J. This is the MHD pump or electromagnetic stirring effect. In this architecture,
the spinning liquid metal becomes the primary energy-coupling medium between the electromagnetic field
and the mechanical spin state, and the claim is that the resulting counter-spin of the inner magnet
relative to the liquid metal pool creates an upward force not balanced by a downward reaction.

## Summary of Advancement Claimed

The central claim is that a three-phase electromagnetic drive acting on a spinning magnet (or
liquid-metal vortex) can produce net Z-axis force (lift) through a Lorentz force mechanism that
bypasses Newton's Third Law when overall angular momentum is cancelled. This would constitute:

* A reactionless drive: no exhaust, no propellant, no downward thrust required.
* Scalable from benchtop magnet (demonstrated experimentally, qualitatively) to large UFO craft
  (with plasma replacing mechanical ball bearing for counter-spin).
* Powered by three-phase AC electricity — a technology already available on Earth.

No quantitative force measurements (Newtons of lift vs. input power in watts) are provided in
the source material. The experimental evidence is qualitative (visual observation of rise) rather
than quantitative (force balance measurements). This is an important limitation noted for completeness.
