# Lift, Propulsion, and Z-Axis Rise Mechanisms

## 1. The Spin-Rise Effect: Core Experimental Observation

The primary lift mechanism documented in this batch is the "spin-rise" or "spin-and-rise" effect
observed in Drew's laboratory prototype. The sequence of events (from video 13_1928965887698284544):

1. Power off: central spinning magnet sits at rest (no vertical position change).
2. Power applied to 6 three-phase wire coils: magnet begins rotating in the XY plane.
3. Consequence: magnet spontaneously rises along the Z axis (vertical ascent).
4. Rise continues and is sustained: "Spin and rise. Spin and rise."
5. Downward pressure applied manually: "Can't do it." The magnet resists being pushed back down
   while spinning.
6. Implication: the lifting force is sustained and significant relative to the magnet's weight,
   sufficient to resist manual downward pressure.

This is a tabletop experimental result (qualitative, not quantitative — no force measurements
in Newtons are reported in the source material).

## 2. Mechanism: Lorentz Force as the Source of Z-Axis Lift

Drew explicitly attributes the lift to the Lorentz force acting on the spinning magnet in the
magnetic field created by the six outer wire coils. From tweet 1967348741389910024 (note_tweet):

"Those E.T. crop artists tell us that the basic, 3-part design of a 'UFO drive' consists of

The Lorentz force on a current-carrying element in a magnetic field is F = I(dl x B). When the
spinning magnet passes through the non-uniform field of the outer DC coils, the moving magnetic
poles of the spinning magnet constitute a current-equivalent source. The interaction between the
moving magnet's field and the DC field of the outer coils produces a force with a vertical
component.

The critical non-classical claim (tweet 1992723258538696892):
"This Lorentz force does NOT follow Newton's 3rd Law. So theoretically it should be capable of
allowing upward force, without any downward force, if it cancels overall spin angular momentum."

The practical lift result (referenced in conversation 1939298175808053455):
"We already have lots of lift" — indicating the Z-axis force is substantial and observed.

## 3. Contra-Rotation as the Path to Reactionless Lift

The problem identified experimentally (tweet 1939298175808053455):
"We already have lots of lift, but also lots of downward torque by Newton's 3rd Law."

The proposed solution: two contra-rotating discs.
"I will try TWO contra-rotating discs next, to eliminate (cancel) net torque on the 3-phase
wire coils."

Physical reasoning:
* One spinning disc (CCW) imparts +L angular momentum, and the 3-phase coil frame absorbs -L.
* This -L manifests as downward torque on the apparatus frame.
* Adding a second identical disc spinning CW (+L absorbed by it) cancels the -L on the frame.
* Result: zero net torque on the frame, zero net angular momentum in the mechanical system.
* If the Z-axis force is maintained (because it arises from the Lorentz force mechanism, not
  from torque reaction), then the system achieves lift without downward reaction torque.

This is the reactionless lift hypothesis.

## 4. Counter-Spin Geometry: Inner vs. Outer Components

From tweet 1971706241295831207 (note_tweet):
"The spin of that magnet, counter to the spin of liquid-metal by which it is surrounded,
apparently creates an upward force, while not pushing the spinning, liquid metal downward by
Newton's 3rd Law."

This describes a specific geometry for the lift mechanism:
* Inner component: permanent magnet spinning in direction A.
* Outer component: liquid metal pool spinning in direction -A (counter-direction).
* The relative counter-rotation of inner vs. outer components is claimed to generate the
  upward force without a downward reaction on either component.

The analogy offered: "Think of a 'magnetic propeller' which turns as if space were made of water?"
This implies the spinning magnet "pushes against" the magnetic field structure of the surrounding
liquid metal (and its associated current distributions) in the same way a propeller pushes against
water, but in the electromagnetic domain. The "reaction" (which in a water propeller pushes the
propeller backward) is absorbed by the magnetic field configuration and manifests as counter-spin
of the liquid metal rather than downward force.

## 10. Prototype Status and Engineering Constraints

From tweet 2028696506321207728 (referenced tweet):
"I pretty much have it solved now, except a need for less friction by clever engineering."

The primary engineering barrier identified: bearing friction. At the rotational speeds required
to produce observable lift, bearing losses (friction) dissipate the input power and limit the
achievable spin rate. The solution requires:
* High-hardness, low-friction bearings (ZrO2 or Si3N4 ceramic, as specified in tweet 2033853862105518134).
* At large scale, replace mechanical bearings with plasma counter-spin to eliminate friction
  entirely.

The prototype has demonstrated "lots of lift" and is described as nearly solved from a physics
standpoint; the remaining barrier is mechanical engineering of the bearing interface.

## 11. Relationship to Gravity

Drew does not explicitly propose a mechanism for gravitational shielding or modification in
the source material of this batch. The lift effect is presented as electromagnetic in origin
(Lorentz force) rather than as gravitational nullification. The anti-gravity framing appears
in the broader hypothesis:

From tweet 1971706241295831207 (note_tweet):
"The suggestion is that 'spinning a magnet' with 3-phase power, up and out of a pool of
spinning liquid metal (turning by the Lorentz force), may create 'anti-gravity'."

But this is qualified as "the suggestion" and "quite an interesting hypothesis" — not an
established result. The claimed mechanism is electromagnetic, not gravitational.

The term "anti-gravity" is used colloquially to mean "reactionless vertical force opposed to
gravity" (i.e., lift without propellant), not a reduction in gravitational coupling constant G
or local spacetime curvature modification.
