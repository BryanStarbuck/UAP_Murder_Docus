# Physics: Equations, Chains of Reasoning, and Dynamics

## 1. Spin-Rise Effect: Phenomenological Description

### Experimental Observation (Lab Prototype)

* Input: Three-phase AC current applied to 6 wire coils arranged around a central permanent magnet.
* Intermediate result: The magnet spins in the XY plane (horizontal rotation).
* Final result: The magnet rises along the Z axis (vertical ascent).
* Constraint resistance: The rising magnet resists being pushed back down while spinning.
* This constitutes an experimentally observed but incompletely theorized coupling between
  XY-plane rotational motion and Z-axis translational motion.

### Precession Analogy

Drew's primary interpretive frame is the spinning top / gyroscope:
A spinning top with angular momentum L = I*omega (where I is the moment of inertia and omega is
the angular velocity vector along the spin axis) undergoes precession in the presence of a
gravitational torque tau = r x mg (where r is the displacement from the support point).

The precession angular velocity is:
  Omega_precession = tau / L = (r x mg) / (I * omega)
  = M * g * r / (I * omega)

For a top that is also rising (rise-precession), the nutation and precession combine to produce
a helical trajectory of the top's axis. Drew claims the crop picture at Barbury Castle (1991)
encodes exactly this: the "horizontal-spin, then rise-precession of a spinning top" using
3-phase power.

The chain of reasoning:
  3-phase power -> rotating magnetic field in XY plane
  -> torque on central magnet -> XY spin (omega in Z direction)
  -> (unknown mechanism) -> net force F_z upward (rise)

The "unknown mechanism" connecting XY spin to Z force is the central claimed discovery.
Drew invokes the Lorentz force as the most probable candidate (see below).

## 2. Lorentz Force and Newton's Third Law: The Core Anomaly

### Standard Lorentz Force

For a charge q moving with velocity v in fields E and B:
  F = q(E + v x B)

For a current-carrying conductor of length element dl carrying current I in field B:
  dF = I (dl x B)

For a conducting fluid (liquid metal) with current density J [A/m^2] in field B [T]:
  f = J x B  [N/m^3]   (Lorentz body force density)

### The Claimed Violation

From tweet 1992723258538696892:
"This Lorentz force does NOT follow Newton's 3rd Law. So theoretically it should be capable
of allowing upward force, without any downward force, if it cancels overall spin angular
momentum."

Drew's claim: in certain geometries, the Lorentz force on the spinning magnet/liquid metal
produces a net upward force on the rotor without producing an equal-and-opposite downward force
on the stator/frame.

The physical rationale given (implicitly): Newton's Third Law applies to forces between pairs of
particles interacting directly. The Lorentz force is mediated by the electromagnetic field; if
the field itself carries momentum (electromagnetic momentum density g = (1/c^2) * (E x H) =
epsilon_0 * (E x B)), then the reaction to the Lorentz force on matter is absorbed by the
electromagnetic field momentum rather than appearing immediately as a mechanical reaction on the
apparatus frame.

This is a known but rarely invoked subtlety of classical electrodynamics: Newton's Third Law
holds for the total system (matter + field), but not necessarily for the matter subsystem alone
when the field stores significant momentum. Whether the specific geometry of Drew's experiment
actually achieves this remains unquantified in the source material.

### The Experimental Evidence (Video 13_1992722894875688960)

Video transcript:
"Okay, I'm gonna add juice to this circuit and the wires will rotate one direction and the disc
will rotate the other direction. But the wires are not enough mass to start the disc rotating in
the opposite direction. Yet the disc starts rotating in the opposite direction. Where are the torques
interacting? This is not equal in opposite torques."

Interpretation: When current flows, the wires (low mass) rotate in one direction and the disc
(high mass) rotates in the opposite direction. A naive Newtonian analysis predicts that the
torque on the wires equals and opposes the torque on the disc (Newton's Third Law for torques).
But because the wire mass is much smaller than the disc mass, the wire angular acceleration is
much larger. Drew observes that the disc rotates in the unexpected direction and identifies this
as an anomaly in the torque balance. The question posed is: "Where are the torques interacting?"
— implying the Lorentz force is redistributing angular momentum through the field, not directly
between wire and disc as mechanical bodies.

## 3. Gyroscopic Dynamics: XY-Plane Coupling to Z-Axis Motion

### Standard Gyroscope Equations (Euler)

The Euler equations for a symmetric rigid body with principal moments I_1 = I_2 ≠ I_3:
  I_1 * (d omega_1/dt) - (I_2 - I_3) * omega_2 * omega_3 = tau_1
  I_2 * (d omega_2/dt) - (I_3 - I_1) * omega_3 * omega_1 = tau_2
  I_3 * (d omega_3/dt) - (I_1 - I_2) * omega_1 * omega_2 = tau_3

For the spinning magnet in the 3-phase coil field, the applied torque tau (from the Lorentz force
on the magnet poles in the rotating B field) drives omega_3 (spin about the Z axis, the symmetry
axis of the 6-coil arrangement). The nonlinear coupling term (I_1 - I_3) * omega_1 * omega_2
could in principle drive oscillation of omega_1 and omega_2 (nutation), but the claimed behavior
is a steady rise rather than nutation — suggesting a sustained upward force rather than a
precessional oscillation.

### Rise-Precession Interpretation

If the spin axis of the magnet is tilted from the Z axis (as in a precessing gyroscope), the
Lorentz force from the stator field exerts a torque perpendicular to the tilt, causing precession.
For a "rise-precession" (the axis spiraling upward), an additional upward component of force is
required. Drew's claim is that the 3-phase Lorentz force provides this upward component inherently
when angular momentum is managed correctly.

## 4. Angular Momentum Conservation and Contra-Rotation Strategy

Total angular momentum of the system:
  L_total = L_rotor + L_frame + L_field

Newton's Third Law for rotation: d L_total / dt = tau_external
If tau_external = 0 (no external torque), L_total = constant.

If L_rotor = +L (rotor spinning CCW), the frame must absorb -L (CW torque) to conserve total
angular momentum. This is the "lots of downward torque by Newton's 3rd Law" that Drew observes
experimentally and describes in tweet 1939298175808053455.

Contra-rotation solution:
  L_rotor_1 = +L (CCW, disc 1)
  L_rotor_2 = -L (CW, disc 2)
  L_total_mechanical = 0
  => no net torque on frame

With L_total_mechanical = 0, if an upward Lorentz force still acts (because the force depends
on the absolute spin rate, not the net angular momentum), then the system can in principle rise
without imparting net torque to the environment.

## 6. Rayleigh Scattering and Wavelength-Dependent Scattering (3I/ATLAS Dust Tail)

From tweet 1961929968369414305 (note_tweet):
"Just as we see 'red' sunrises or sunsets on Earth, after the 'blue' part of normal sunlight gets
scattered away, by its long distance of travel through DUST in our atmosphere.
In a similar fashion, if we study the bright, round 3I/ATLAS at 'blue' wavelengths, we cannot
see any 'tail'. That is because any sunlight reflected from its long 'tail' of DUST gets scattered
away from us, if it is 'blue'.
Yet if study 3I/ATLAS at 'green' or 'red' wavelengths, then we can see a 'tail', because 'green'
or 'red' sunlight can be reflected from the dust in that long 'tail' and still be seen on Earth,
with not as much 'scattering'."

This is an application of Rayleigh scattering, where the scattering cross section scales as:
  sigma_scatter ~ (1/lambda^4)  (Rayleigh regime, particle << lambda)
or Mie scattering for larger particles:
  sigma_Mie ~ f(x)  where x = 2*pi*r/lambda

For short wavelengths (blue, lambda ~ 450 nm), scattering is stronger, preferentially removing
blue light from long paths through dust. Red light (lambda ~ 700 nm) scatters less and propagates
further, explaining why the dust tail of 3I/ATLAS is visible in red/green but not blue.

The observation that the anti-tail of 3I/ATLAS (pointing toward the Sun, rather than away) is
present supports the hypothesis that the object is losing mass through sublimation or ablation,
with the anti-tail composed of larger dust grains that are not pushed by radiation pressure.

## 9. Quantum Computational Advancement: Beyond Binary to Quaternary

From tweet 2021886996587983073 (note_tweet):
"Evolution is only stepwise if you remain 'binary'.
A quantum leap to 4 symbols will produce spectacular advances.
Reduced size, requiring less electrical power, and much more intelligence.
Can you imagine DNA with just 2 bases instead of 4?
Of course not. Carbon-based life with just 2 DNA bases would remain very primitive."

The physics argument: information density in computing scales as log2(N) bits per symbol, where
N is the number of distinguishable states per element. Binary uses N=2 (1 bit per element);
quaternary uses N=4 (2 bits per element), doubling information density per physical element.

DNA uses N=4 bases (A, T, G, C), encoding 2 bits per base pair. A hypothetical binary DNA would
encode 1 bit per base pair, requiring twice as many bases for the same information — consistent
with the claim that 4-symbol systems are fundamentally more efficient.

The proposed application: a quaternary (base-4) computing substrate would require fewer physical
elements for equivalent computational power, reducing energy consumption, which scales (by
Landauer's principle) as k_B * T * ln(2) per bit erased — reduced element count directly reduces
minimum thermodynamic energy cost.
