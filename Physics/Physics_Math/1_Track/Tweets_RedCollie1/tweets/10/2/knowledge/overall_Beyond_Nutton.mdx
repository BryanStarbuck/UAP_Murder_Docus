1. PRIMARY BEYOND-NEWTONIAN CLAIM: HELICAL MAGNETIC LIFT BYPASSES N3L
====================================================================

Source: tweet 1964253437702656144 (text + note_tweet), video 13_1964248354038435843

Newton's Third Law (N3L) in the linear-force context:
    F_action = -F_reaction

The claimed experimental result that violates this in the linear domain:
A spinning magnet in a helical three-phase magnetic field rises upward (Z-axis)
with a measurable upward force (F_up = at least M_magnet * g, as inferred from
the stable rise against gravity) while the scale below shows no increase in
weight (delta_W = 0 on the scale).

If this result is taken at face value, it implies:
    F_up (on magnet) exists, AND
    F_down (reaction on apparatus/scale) = 0

This is a violation of N3L as applied to linear forces in the Z-direction.

Drew's proposed explanation (not a violation, but a redirection):
The counter-reaction is angular (a torque), not translational. The system
obeys N3L in the angular momentum domain (sideways counter-torque), but the
linear reaction force is zero. This would be new physics if confirmed: a
mechanism by which linear upward force is generated through a process whose
Newton's reaction is entirely rotational.

Quote: "the quick rise of a spinning magnet by a 'helical' magnetic field
creates no 'downward' counter-reaction by Newton's 3rd Law. If the upward
reaction force is clockwise, then the downward counter-reaction force will be
anti-clockwise, or vice versa."
