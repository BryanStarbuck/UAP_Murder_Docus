4. ANOMALOUS ENERGY IN 3I/ATLAS BRIGHTENING AT PERIHELION
====================================================================

Source: tweet 1984912237547696489 (text), tweet 1976399302173061403 (note_tweet)

Drew flags the brightening and "hot-blue" color change of 3I/ATLAS at
perihelion as anomalous:
"IMPORTANT FACT ABOUT 3I/ATLAS: why did it turn bright and hot-blue at
perihelion?"

For a natural comet, brightening at perihelion is explained by:
(a) Increased solar irradiance (I ~ 1/r^2): solar flux increases as ~(1/r)^2
    as the object approaches the Sun.
(b) Enhanced sublimation of ices -> outgassing -> coma formation -> increased
    scattering cross-section.

For natural comets, the color tends toward neutral or dust-scattered reddening,
not anomalous blue emission. "Hot-blue" could in principle indicate:
* High-temperature blackbody emission (T_eff > 10,000 K for peak in blue)
* Ionized gas emission lines (e.g., C+ 133.5 nm or other UV/blue transitions)
* Anomalous energy deposition mechanism (e.g., artificial power source)

Drew implies the color/brightness change is evidence of artificial energy
generation within 3I/ATLAS (i.e., the object has an onboard energy source
that activates at perihelion). No energy budget calculation is provided in
this source batch.

state. Drew does not elaborate on the thermodynamic implications. No energy
