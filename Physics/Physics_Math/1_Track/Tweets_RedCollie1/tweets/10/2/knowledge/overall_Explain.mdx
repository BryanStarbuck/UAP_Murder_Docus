CENTRAL THESIS: UFO/UAP Helical Magnetic Field Propulsion
====================================================================

Author: Dr. Horace Drew (RedCollie1), Caltech / MRC LMB Cambridge
Research program: hundreds of self-described laboratory experiments
Primary claim source: tweet 1964253437702656144 (note_tweet field) and
                      associated video 13_1964248354038435843_video.mdx

The propulsion advancement claimed is the following:

A "helical" magnetic field -- generated by three-phase AC applied to three
wire coils arranged around a central spinning permanent magnet -- produces
upward lift (Z-axis force) on the spinning magnet. This force:

(1) Acts independently of whether the magnet's north or south pole faces up.
    Experimental result (video 13_1964248354038435843): magnet flipped from
    north-up to south-up configuration at identical 3-phase frequencies
    (10 Hz, 15 Hz, 20 Hz, 23 Hz, 25 Hz, 30 Hz) still rises with equivalent
    upward force and zero net change in scale reading.

(2) Creates almost no downward counter-reaction by Newton's 3rd Law.
    The claimed mechanism: when the upward reaction force is clockwise, the
    downward counter-reaction force is anti-clockwise (or vice versa), so
    the counter-reaction manifests as a sideways torque rather than downward
    weight. This is the central beyond-Newtonian claim.

(3) Is analogous to a spinning top or gyroscope that rises. The gyroscopic
    precession and the helical field topology together are asserted to redirect
    what would be a downward Newton's 3rd Law reaction into a lateral
    counter-torque.

Direct quote (note_tweet, tweet 1964253437702656144):
"the quick rise of a spinning magnet by a 'helical' magnetic field creates
no 'downward' counter-reaction by Newton's 3rd Law. If the upward reaction
force is clockwise, then the downward counter-reaction force will be
anti-clockwise, or vice versa. In order words, the quick rise of a spinning
magnet creates almost no increase of downward force-weight on a scale placed
below, but instead creates a sideways counter-torque, as it leads to an
increase of gravitational 'potential' energy."

Advancement benefit asserted: if the downward Newton's 3rd Law reaction can
be fully cancelled (rather than merely redirected into lateral torque), then
net upward thrust with zero ground-reaction thrust enables UAP-style
levitation. The author identifies this as the singular unresolved obstacle:
"We've definitely got an anti-gravity motion with the big upward force taking
the magnet upward, but we have to find a way to cancel the downward force in
order to use it for UFO levitation." (video 13_1964248354038435843)

             (galinstan: gallium-indium-tin eutectic alloy).

             3-phase drive.
Below:       A stationary permanent magnet (m1) that (a) induces Lorentz-
             force-driven counter-rotation of galinstan via DC current (12V),
             and (b) magnetically levitates m2 slightly above the liquid metal
             surface so m2 can spin freely without the high resistive drag of

Counter-rotation relationship: disc magnet m2 spins clockwise under 3-phase
drive; liquid metal in the Corbino disc spins anti-clockwise under Lorentz
force from m1 with DC current. This means:

    Net angular momentum L_total = L_m2 (clockwise) + L_galinstan (anti-clockwise)
    Target: L_total ≈ 0

Author's claim (tweet 1992514814242099289, note_tweet):
"A disc magnet 'm2' spins with 3-phase magnetism, slightly above a pool of
liquid metal galinstan, which spins in the opposite sense by a Lorentz force.
Thus total spin angular momentum remains close to zero!"

Proposed advancement benefit: a system with near-zero net angular momentum
but substantial upward magnetic thrust force. If Newton's 3rd Law reaction
is rotational (torque) rather than linear (force), cancelling the net angular
momentum through counter-rotation may suppress the downward back-reaction
entirely. This is the hypothesized mechanism for bypassing Newton's 3rd Law.

Direct quote (tweet 2008156787409822198, note_tweet):
"Such 'counter-rotation' of 3-phase and Lorentz forces is supposed to create
