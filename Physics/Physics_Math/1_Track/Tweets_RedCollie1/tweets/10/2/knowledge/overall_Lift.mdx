3. THE "DOWNWARD FORCE" PROBLEM: WHY COMPLETE LEVITATION IS NOT YET ACHIEVED
====================================================================

Source: video 13_1964248354038435843, tweet 1964253437702656144 (note_tweet)

The current experimental result shows:
* Large upward force (magnet rises)
* BUT the system reports zero net change in scale weight, meaning:
  upward force ≈ downward counter-force (Newton's 3rd Law manifests somehow)

The result "no change of weight on the scale" admits two interpretations:

Interpretation A (Drew's preferred): the counter-reaction to the upward lift
is a lateral torque (angular, not translational). The scale measures only
translational downward force. Since the reaction is angular, the scale reads
zero net change. Net upward linear force EXISTS but has no downward linear
reaction.

Interpretation B (conventional): the system is internally braced; the upward
force on the magnet is balanced by an equal downward force on the coil assembly,
which is not measured by the scale in this configuration.

Drew acknowledges that canceling the downward force is the unsolved problem:
"We've definitely got an anti-gravity motion with the big upward force taking
the magnet upward, but we have to find a way to cancel the downward force in
order to use it for UFO levitation."

The proposed solution is the Corbino disc + counter-rotation design (see below).
