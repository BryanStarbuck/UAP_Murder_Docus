1. HELICAL MAGNETIC FIELD: THE CENTRAL MAGNETIC PHENOMENON
====================================================================

Source: tweet 1964253437702656144 (text + note_tweet), video 13_1964248354038435843

Claim: UFOs fly by means of a "helical" magnetic field. This is asserted to
be not described in physics textbooks or journals. Dr. Drew states he has done
hundreds of experiments to demonstrate the general principle.

Physical description:
Three wire coils (labeled one, two, three) are wound around the outside of a
flexible mounting rod. The coils are driven with three-phase AC power from a
precision frequency source. The three-phase drive creates a rotating magnetic
field in the interior space. This rotating field is described as "helical"
because the combination of rotation in the XY-plane and the axial extent of
the coils along the Z-axis produces a helically-structured field topology.

Experimental apparatus (video 13_1964248354038435843):
* Rod diameter: 4 mm (flexible)
* Permanent magnet: 70 mm diameter disc, 14 mm axial hole
* Coil configuration: 3 coils, driven in three-phase
* Frequency range tested: 10 Hz, 15 Hz, 20 Hz, 23 Hz, 25 Hz, 30 Hz
* Below: electronic scale to measure weight change

Observed behavior:
* At low frequencies (~10 Hz): erratic spin
* At ~20-25 Hz: magnet rises "like a top" -- stable gyroscopic rise
* Scale reading: no change when magnet rises (claimed zero net downward force)

Pole-independence of lift:
The experiment was repeated with the magnet flipped (south pole up instead of
north pole up). Result: identical behavior -- clockwise spin, rise at ~20 Hz,
no change in scale weight. Drew interprets this as evidence that the helical
field topology, not the pole orientation, is the operative mechanism.

Direct quote (note_tweet, tweet 1964253437702656144):
"That 'helical' magnetic field works the same with either North or South poles
'up' for a central spinning magnet, which RISES like a spinning top or
gyroscope. It works equally well for clockwise or anti-clockwise 3-phase
magnetic fields, but will collapse, if we switch the field quickly from
clockwise to anti-clockwise, or vice-versa."

Collapse condition: rapid reversal of 3-phase field rotation direction
(clockwise to anti-clockwise or vice versa) collapses the lift effect. This
implies a phase-coherent helical field topology is required; sudden polarity
switching destroys the topology.

3. LORENTZ FORCE ON LIQUID METAL (GALINSTAN): MAGNETIC FIELD + CURRENT
====================================================================

Source: tweet 1992514814242099289 (note_tweet), video 13_1971360840071933952,
        tweet 1971364431973245335 (note_tweet), tweet 2008156787409822198 (note_tweet)

Galinstan (gallium-indium-tin low-melting-point liquid metal alloy) in a
stainless steel container with a permanent magnet immersed or placed below.

Mechanism: DC current passing radially through the liquid metal (Corbino disc
geometry) interacts with the axial magnetic field of the embedded/below magnet
via the Lorentz force:

    F = J × B

where J is the current density vector (radial in Corbino disc geometry) and
B is the axial magnetic flux density of the permanent magnet. The resulting
force F is azimuthal, driving rotation of the conductive liquid metal.

Direction reversal: reversing the DC leads reverses the current direction J,
which reverses J × B, reversing the rotation direction of the vortex.
Demonstrated experimentally in video 13_1971360840071933952.

Direct quote (video 13_1971360840071933952):
"If we have liquid galinstan, which is sort of like mercury, a conductive liquid
metal, and we put it in a stainless steel container, and we put a magnet inside
in the liquid. There's another magnet like this sitting underneath there that we
turn on the DC power. The vortex will flow very strongly to the embedded magnet.
This is the other direction with the DC lead reversed."

Corbino disc geometry significance (tweet 2008156787409822198, note_tweet):
The Corbino disc is a radial-current geometry in which current flows from a
central electrode outward to a circular perimeter electrode (or vice versa).
In an axial magnetic field B_z, the radial current J_r produces an azimuthal
Lorentz force F_theta = J_r × B_z, driving rotational flow without the
brushes required by conventional motor geometries. The 15 round DC electrodes
(outer) and central DC electrode (inner) described in the crop picture schematic
correspond to a multi-electrode Corbino disc implementation.

Direct quote (tweet 1971364431973245335, note_tweet):
"Each of those little line-circle shapes represents a 'brush' used to make
electrical connections, between 15 round DC electrodes outside, and some
central DC electrode inside (not shown in this schematic, but seen in the
original crop drawing). As a second step, we somehow have to add 3-phase
power (the 'trident' shape) to get 'UFO flight'! If that large central circle
is a disc or ball-magnet, then it will spin easily when 3-phase power is
applied."

5. STATIC MAGNET m1: DUAL FUNCTION
====================================================================

Source: tweet 1992514814242099289 (note_tweet)

Stationary magnet m1, placed below the galinstan pool, serves two independent
magnetic functions simultaneously:

Function 1 -- Lorentz vortex drive:
m1's axial field B_z combined with DC current (12V) through the Corbino disc
creates the azimuthal Lorentz force that spins the galinstan counter to m2.

Function 2 -- Magnetic levitation of m2:
m1's field provides repulsive levitation force on m2, holding m2 slightly above
the liquid metal surface. This is essential because if m2 were immersed in the
galinstan, the viscous and resistive drag of the liquid metal would prevent m2
from spinning at all.

Direct quote (tweet 1992514814242099289, note_tweet):
"Magnet 'm1' provides two functions. Firstly, it makes the galinstan spin with
DC current (12 volts) by a Lorentz force. Secondly, it lifts magnet 'm2'
slightly above the pool of liquid galinstan, so that it can spin and rise
freely by 3-phase magnetism. If we were to place magnet 'm2' directly into
the liquid metal, it would not spin at all (due to high counter-resistance)."
