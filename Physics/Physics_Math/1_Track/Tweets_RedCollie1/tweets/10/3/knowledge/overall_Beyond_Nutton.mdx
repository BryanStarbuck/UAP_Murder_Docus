Drew's proposed exception: at sufficiently high spin (omega) and magnetic field (B), the rotational configuration of the electromagnetic system produces a net force on the craft that is not balanced by an equal and opposite reaction on a discernible external body. (b) A coupling of the electromagnetic system to a background field (vacuum fluctuations, spacetime geometry) that absorbs the reaction momentum — analogous to the Unruh effect or radiation pressure recoil.
(c) A genuinely new physics effect — a spin-velocity-dependent coupling that is absent from both classical and quantum field theory — representing physics beyond the Standard Model.

* A new physics domain where the gravitational force on a body can be neutralized or reversed by an engineered electromagnetic field configuration.

In mainstream physics, no mechanism is known to screen or reverse gravity via EM fields. The equivalence principle (gravity couples universally to all energy-momentum) prevents EM shielding of gravity. Beyond-Newtonian proposals include:

* Woodward's Mach effect: oscillating mass fluctuations from time-varying EM fields produce a net thrust — not gravity reversal, but reactionless thrust.

Drew's approach (RMF + Corbino disc + N/S magnet) is positioned as a physically realizable version of anti-gravity using macroscopic conducting media rather than superconductors.
