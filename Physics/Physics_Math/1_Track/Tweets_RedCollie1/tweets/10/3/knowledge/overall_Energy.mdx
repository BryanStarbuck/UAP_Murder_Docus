(a) The energy is not mechanical (rope and boards would flatten both brown and green plants without selectivity).
(b) The energy is not uniform thermal (uniform heating would affect all plants).
(c) The energy exhibits a selectivity analogous to spectroscopic absorption: different molecular chromophore content in brown vs. green plants absorbs the energy differentially.

Candidate physics (Drew's claim: "some kind of physics we do not know yet"):
* Microwave/millimeter-wave radiation: brown/dried wheat has different dielectric constant epsilon_r and loss tangent tan(delta) than green wet wheat. Power absorbed per unit volume P = sigma*|E|^2 / 2 = omega*epsilon_0*epsilon_r*tan(delta)*|E|^2 / 2 — if the radiation frequency is chosen to maximize absorption in brown wheat while minimizing it in green, selective flattening results. This is physically possible via dielectric heating and is analogous to microwave cooking selectivity.
* Infrared laser: brown plants absorb differently in IR wavelengths than green (chlorophyll has different IR spectrum than dry cellulose/lignin). A tuned IR frequency could selectively heat and flatten brown crop.
* The "unknown physics" claim: Drew implies the selectivity exceeds what standard EM mechanisms can achieve, suggesting a novel interaction between the energy source and plant biochemistry — possibly involving biological coherence, biophotonic fields, or an energy form that couples to the oxidation state of plant matter.

## 2. Energy for UFO Propulsion — Three-Phase AC Power

Source tweet: 1939107002552393811 (text):
"The Buga orb-sphere and three related crop pictures showing the same UFO-style physics (whether 5-phase or 3-phase AC power)."

The UFO propulsion system operates on AC power — specifically polyphase AC, either 3-phase or 5-phase. This is an important energy specification:

* 3-phase AC power: total instantaneous power P = V_L * I_L * sqrt(3) * power_factor (for balanced 3-phase load). For an ideal balanced resistive load: P = 3 * V_phase * I_phase. The power is constant (no ripple) in balanced 3-phase — unlike single-phase which has sinusoidal power ripple at 2*f.
* 5-phase AC power: similarly provides constant instantaneous power for a balanced 5-phase system: P = (5/2) * V_phase * I_phase * power_factor.

The constant power delivery of polyphase systems is significant for propulsion: it means the electromagnetic force on the rotating components is constant (no oscillating thrust from power ripple), enabling smooth, vibration-free flight. Drew's model requires the interior Corbino disc liquid metal to receive constant power for stable levitation.

Energy source within the craft: Not explicitly specified in source material for this batch. However, the reference to "UFO physics" as described in crop circles, combined with Drew's reference to the Corbino disc configuration, implies an onboard power generator — possibly a homopolar generator or a nuclear power source (given UAP proximity to nuclear sites, see Rendlesham/Wadley references).

* Final velocity: v_f = 67,000 mph = 29,960 m/s
* Delta-v: Delta_v = 28,140 m/s

Kinetic energy change per kg of spacecraft:
Delta_KE = (1/2)(v_i^2 - v_f^2) = (1/2)(58100^2 - 29960^2) = (1/2)(3.375e9 - 8.98e8) = 1.24e9 J/kg

For v_e = 30,000 m/s (ion/nuclear): m_prop/m_0 = 1 - exp(-0.94) = 0.61 — more practical.

The "hot-bright-blue" signature implies exhaust at T ~ 6000-7000 K (Wien's law: lambda_max = b/T = 2.898e-3/6500 = 446 nm — blue light). ## 6. Energy That Flattens Crop: Frequency-Selective Heating Model (Extended)

The anomalous energy selectivity (brown vs. green wheat) can be modeled as a dielectric heating phenomenon:

Power absorbed per unit volume in a dielectric:
P = (1/2) * omega * epsilon_0 * epsilon_r * tan(delta) * |E|^2

For brown dried wheat: tan(delta)_brown at microwave frequencies >> tan(delta)_green (green wheat has high water content with high dielectric loss; brown wheat has low water content — WAIT: standard microwave cooking would heat wet/green more than dry/brown). 

The reversed selectivity (brown heated, green unaffected) is anomalous for standard microwave heating. This implies either:
(a) The energy operates in a frequency range where dry cellulose/lignin absorbs more than water — possible in specific THz or far-IR windows.
(b) The energy is not electromagnetic in nature but couples to the vibrational modes of dry organic polymers (cellulose crystallinity) while water's hydrogen-bond network disperses the energy without structural damage.
(c) A coherent energy source that destructively interferes in green plant water and constructively interferes in brown plant material — requiring a phase-coherent energy field with spatial coherence length >> plant spacing.

Drew classifies this as "some kind of physics we do not know yet" — a genuine anomaly in the energy domain beyond current physical models.
