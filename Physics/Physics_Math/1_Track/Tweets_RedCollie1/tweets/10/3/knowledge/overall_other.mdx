### 4a. Alzheimer's Disease: Lithium Deficiency Model
Source tweet: 1953973451691323491 (note_tweet):
"Here is a brief summary of the new 'Nature' paper on Alzheimer's Disease as caused by lithium deficiency in the brain. Think 'scurvy'."

Slide 1: Analysis of 27 elements in brains of normal vs. mentally-impaired older people. Finding: huge loss of lithium (Li) in MCI (Mild Cognitive Impairment) or AD (Alzheimer's Disease) patients.

Slide 2: Finding: lithium carbonate (Li2CO3, inorganic salt) is NOT effectively delivered to increase brain lithium in a mouse model. Needed: organic lithium salt such as lithium orotate (lithium salt of orotic acid) — already sold widely as a "Mood Regulator" at 5 to 20 mg daily.

Slide 3: Finding: lithium deficiency induces high levels of a protein kinase called GSK-3-beta (Glycogen Synthase Kinase 3 beta), which produces plaques or other pathological changes in the brain. Lithium orotate added to drinking water of mice represses GSK-3-beta activity — proposed mechanism.

Drew's analogy: "Think 'scurvy'" — implying Alzheimer's could be a simple micronutrient deficiency disease with a straightforward nutritional remedy, analogous to how vitamin C deficiency causes scurvy and is cured by citrus/vitamin C supplementation.

Scientific context (not editorialized by Drew): GSK-3-beta inhibition by lithium is a known pharmacological effect in the published literature (Klein & Melton, PNAS 1996). The novel claim is that lithium deficiency (rather than amyloid overproduction) is the primary driver of AD pathology, suggesting supplementation with bioavailable organic lithium as prevention/treatment.
