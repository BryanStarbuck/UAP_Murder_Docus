## Claimed Anomalous Energy Phenomenon: Crop Circle Plant Selectivity

Source: tweet 2021692098093752745 and referenced tweet 2021691874340442115:

"He [Paul Jacobs] has shown that, in many cases, only the brown wheat gets flattened by an
unseen energy, rather than green plants. The energy thus seems 'tuned' to a certain kind of
plant, by some kind of physics we do [not understand]."

Drew comments: "THE GREEN PLANTS ARE STILL STANDING!"

Physical implication:
* An energy delivery mechanism that selectively deposits energy into brown (dried, dead) plant
  stems but not into green (living, water-saturated) plant stems.
* This selectivity is NOT explained by simple thermal (blackbody radiation absorption) or
  microwave heating effects in a naive model, because:
  - Green plants contain more water and would absorb microwave energy MORE strongly
    (water has high microwave absorption cross-section at 2.45 GHz, etc.).
  - A thermal beam heating dried vs. living stems selectively would require very precise
    spectral tuning.
* The selectivity could potentially be explained by:
  - A narrowband electromagnetic frequency specifically absorbed by brown dried cellulose /
    lignin but not by chlorophyll-containing tissue.
  - An interaction with the different dielectric constant of dried vs. water-saturated tissue.
  - A plasma-based or acoustic mechanism that couples differently to the two plant states.

No mechanism is proposed in the source material beyond the statement that "some kind of physics"
is involved.

## Crop Circle Formation Energy Estimate (Scale Constraint)

From tweet 2045733823170982184:

A crop circle formation in the Netherlands (2009) measured 500 × 300 meters.
This formation:
* Appeared overnight.
* Was not seen being created.
* No humans were observed on the land.
* A human-replication attempt using 25–30 people on a 100 × 60 m scale (1/5 scale) yielded
  "only poor results."

Energy implications of 500 × 300 m crop formation created in a single night:
* Area: ~150,000 m² of crop physically altered (stems bent/twisted/laid flat).
* Energy required to bend/heat grain stems to allow bending without breaking:
  - Each stem requires warming to ~70–100°C to plastically deform the cellulose node.
  - Mass of stems per m²: approximately 0.3–1.0 kg/m²
  - Specific heat of grain stems: ~2000 J/(kg·K)
  - ΔT ≈ 60 K
  - Energy per m²: ~0.3 kg × 2000 J/(kg·K) × 60 K = 36,000 J/m²
  - Total: 36,000 × 150,000 = 5.4 × 10⁹ J ≈ 5.4 GJ

5.4 GJ delivered silently overnight without visible light, sound, or human presence —
if the claim is accurate — requires an energy delivery mechanism inconsistent with any
known directed-energy technology deployable covertly in a rural setting.

Drew describes the process: "They heat each plant stem to stretch and bend it, because that
makes it easier to weave." This is consistent with the localized microwave/plasma node-heating
mechanism proposed by other crop circle researchers (e.g., BLT Research Team).

## Energy Context of 3I/ATLAS

Source: tweets 1971420455899951286, 1984927500540264467, 1989649174359638439:

* 3I/ATLAS mass: >33 billion tons = >3.3 × 10¹³ kg.
* Claimed to use ion propulsion ("Does 3I/ATLAS use 'ion propulsion'?").
* Avi Loeb cited 9 anomalies including "unprecedented recent brightening while being bluer
  than the Sun."

Energy considerations for ion propulsion at interstellar scale:
* The object's kinetic energy at its observed velocity (interstellar velocities, order
  10–100 km/s relative to solar system) represents:
  E_kinetic = (1/2) M v²
  For M = 3.3 × 10¹³ kg, v = 30 km/s:
  E_kinetic = 0.5 × 3.3 × 10¹³ × (3 × 10⁴)² = 1.485 × 10²² J ≈ 1.5 × 10²² J

  This is approximately 360 terawatt-years of energy — far beyond any conventional power
  source for such a mass at interstellar scale.

* "Unprecedented brightening" of 3I/ATLAS could imply active energy emission (power source
  on board), not passive sublimation. The spectral bluing (bluer than Sun) is anomalous for
  a comet but consistent with a hot power source or active electromagnetic emission.
