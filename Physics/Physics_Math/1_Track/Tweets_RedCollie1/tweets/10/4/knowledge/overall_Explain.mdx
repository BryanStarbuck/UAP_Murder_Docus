## What Propulsion or Energy Benefit is Claimed

1. CONTACTLESS MAGNETIC LEVITATION VIA THREE-PHASE ROTATING FIELD
   - A permanent magnet (40 mm diameter, single-pole orientation) placed inside a three-phase
     wire coil arrangement (3 coils below + 6 coils above, or 3 coils only) is caused to spin
     and simultaneously rise along the z-axis (vertical lift) when three-phase AC power is
     applied.
   - The rotational frequency of the applied field is experimentally tuned: at 10 Hz minimal
     lift is observed; at 20-25 Hz "considerable force" lift is achieved.
   - This is described as producing "UFO-style levitation": the craft rises and spins about
     the vertical axis.

2. COUNTER-ROTATING BODY DYNAMICS AS A KEY ENGINEERING CHALLENGE
   - Per Newton's Third Law (action-reaction), a central magnet spinning clockwise induces
     a counter-clockwise reactive torque on the surrounding wire coil structure.
   - This counter-clockwise reactive spin opposes the intended levitation mode by pushing the
     outer structure downward (opposite to the desired upward force on the central magnet).
   - The research specifically addresses how to decouple the reaction torque from the outer body
     so net upward force on the craft is achieved.

3. FIELD CANCELLATION MANAGEMENT (UVW / U'V'W' PHASE ARRANGEMENT)
   - When six counter-clockwise outer coils are wound as a single set (U,U,V,V,W,W labeling),
     their fields cancel the clockwise inner coils in the center where the magnet resides,
     destroying lift.
   - Rewiring as two independent three-phase sets (UVW + U'V'W', anti-clockwise but split)
     produces field cancellation only at the very center, leaving a residual rotating field
     further out. This allows the central magnet to spin and rise without being counteracted.
   - Result: net clockwise spin and z-axis lift of the magnet are preserved even when
     the outer counter-clockwise coil set is energized.

4. PROPOSED NEXT STEP: LORENTZ FORCE FROM LIQUID METAL (GALINSTAN)
   - To replace the downward counter-spin force (Newton's Third Law reaction) with a
     non-downward-pushing spin, the researcher proposes using counter-spinning liquid metal
     (galinstan) driven by a 6-volt DC battery placed over another magnet below,
     exploiting the Lorentz force: F = q(v × B).
   - Galinstan (gallium-indium-tin eutectic, liquid at room temperature) is proposed as
     the working fluid because it is electrically conductive and can sustain Lorentz-force-driven
     azimuthal flow without mechanical friction.

5. VORTEX MAGNETISM (NON-TEXTBOOK CLAIM)
   - A phenomenon described as "vortex magnetism" is claimed: a spinning magnetic vortex around
     a central rotating magnet is stated to increase the gravitational potential energy of the
     central magnet, yet this effect does not appear in standard physics textbooks.
   - The outer three-phase wire coils may need to rotate (spin) physically along with the
     central magnet to enter the "upward vortex," suggesting a mechanical + electromagnetic
     coupling that differs from static-coil induction motor theory.
   - This effect is claimed to be independent of the polarity orientation of the central magnet
     (North-up or South-up yields the same vortex lift behavior).

6. CLAIMED EXTRATERRESTRIAL TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER VIA CROP CIRCLES
   - The engineering diagrams (3 coils below, 6 coils above, central magnet) are stated to
     originate from crop circle formations (specifically a 1998 crop picture described as
     showing "how it flies").
   - Alien-made crop pictures are claimed to repeatedly encode these coil configurations,
     phase connections, and magnet arrangements across multiple independent formations.

## Specific Physics Advances Enabling the Propulsion Benefit

* Three-phase rotating magnetic field → angular momentum imparted to permanent magnet → simultaneous
  spin and z-axis translation (lift). This is the classical induction principle but applied in an
  open-air frictionless configuration with the magnet levitating freely.

* Coil phase topology (UVW + U'V'W') → spatial field cancellation engineered to be confined to
  the geometric center → outer field remains active → magnet in center decoupled from outer
  counter-rotating field → net lift without cancellation.

* Angular momentum conservation (Newton's Third Law) explicitly identified as the primary
  engineering barrier: L_total = L_magnet + L_craft_body = const. If L_magnet increases
  (clockwise), L_craft_body must decrease (counter-clockwise) unless an independent angular
  momentum source is introduced.

* Proposed resolution: Lorentz-force-driven liquid metal azimuthal flow provides an independent
  angular momentum channel, decoupling craft-body reaction torque from the central magnet's spin,
  enabling net upward thrust without downward reactive force on the body.
