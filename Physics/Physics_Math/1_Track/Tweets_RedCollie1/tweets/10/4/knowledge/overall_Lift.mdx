Based on the 1998 crop picture schematic and laboratory reproduction:

* 3 wire coils, three-phase power, clockwise rotation.
* These coils drive the central magnet to spin clockwise and rise upward.

UPPER ASSEMBLY (designed to NOT cancel the lower drive):
* 6 wire coils, three-phase power, anti-clockwise rotation.
* Critical wiring topology: UVW + U'V'W' (two independent three-phase sets, not UUVVWW).
* Effect of UVW + U'V'W' topology at center: anti-clockwise field cancels to zero at center.
* Effect away from center: anti-clockwise field persists (confirmed by compass).
* Result: upper coils do NOT oppose the lift-driving clockwise field at the magnet location.

Observational result from scaling up (70 mm magnet):

* Fields cancel only at very center.
* Magnet still acquires some anti-clockwise spin as it grows larger (field null no longer
  perfectly contains the entire magnet volume).
* Net: majority of clockwise spin preserved → lift preserved.

4. Laboratory reproduction (video 2 in batch): the spin / counter-spin is confirmed in the model.

Drew's statement: "We are on the right track" — confirming the model matches the observed UAP
behavior even if the net lift problem is not yet solved.

The key engineering problem:
* If the outer body counter-rotates and is pressed downward, the net upward translation of
  the WHOLE CRAFT (body + magnet system) is reduced or eliminated.
* Only the central magnet element is rising; the outer body is being pushed down.
* For UFO-style levitation of the whole craft, the counter-spin force on the outer body must
  be replaced with something that does not push the craft down.
