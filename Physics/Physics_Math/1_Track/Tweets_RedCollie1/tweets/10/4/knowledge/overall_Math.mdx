## 1. Angular Momentum Conservation

  L_total = L_magnet + L_body = constant  [kg·m²/s]

Components:
  L_magnet = I_magnet · ω_magnet   [kg·m²/s]
  L_body   = I_body   · ω_body     [kg·m²/s]

Where:
  I_magnet = (1/2) m_magnet r_magnet²   [kg·m²]  (solid cylinder approximation)
  I_body   = moment of inertia of outer housing and coils
  ω_magnet = angular velocity of central magnet about z-axis [rad/s]
  ω_body   = angular velocity of outer body about z-axis [rad/s]

For initially quiescent system: L_total = 0, therefore:
  I_magnet · ω_magnet = −I_body · ω_body

Sign convention: positive = clockwise (viewed from above), negative = counter-clockwise.

If ω_magnet > 0 (clockwise), then ω_body < 0 (counter-clockwise).

Magnitude relationship:
  |ω_body| / |ω_magnet| = I_magnet / I_body

For small magnets (I_magnet << I_body): ω_body is small but non-zero.
For large magnets (I_magnet approaches I_body): ω_body becomes significant.

## 2. Magnetic Torque on Dipole in Rotating Field

  τ = m × B

Components:
  m = magnetic dipole moment vector of permanent magnet [A·m²]
  B = rotating field vector [T]
  τ = torque vector [N·m]

Rotating field components:
  B_x(t) = B_0 cos(ωt)
  B_y(t) = B_0 sin(ωt)
  B_z    = B_z0 (static component from coil geometry)

Where:
  B_0 = peak rotating field amplitude [T]
  ω = 2π f   [rad/s]
  f = applied three-phase frequency [Hz]: experimentally 10 Hz to 25 Hz

For m = m_z ẑ (magnet aligned with z-axis):
  τ = m_z ẑ × (B_x x̂ + B_y ŷ) = m_z (B_x (ẑ × x̂) + B_y (ẑ × ŷ))
    = m_z (B_x ŷ − B_y x̂)

The azimuthal torque component drives spin. A z-axis force requires either a field gradient
∂B_z/∂z or the non-classical "vortex magnetism" mechanism:
  F_z = ∇(m · B) = m_z · (∂B_z/∂z)   [classical gradient force]

## 3. Three-Phase Field Superposition for Opposing Coil Sets

For coil set A (clockwise, lower three coils):
  B_A(r, t) = B_A0 f_A(r) [cos(ωt) x̂ + sin(ωt) ŷ]

For coil set B (anti-clockwise, outer six coils in UVW + U'V'W' topology):
  B_B(r, t) = B_B0 f_B(r) [cos(ωt) x̂ − sin(ωt) ŷ]

Note: anti-clockwise set has opposite sign on the y-component.

Total field at center (r = 0), for the anti-clockwise set B:
  B_B(0, t) = 0   (by symmetry of paired opposing coils)

Total field at intermediate r > 0:
  B_B(r, t) = B_B0 f_B(r) [cos(ωt) x̂ − sin(ωt) ŷ]   (non-zero)

Where f_A(r) and f_B(r) are spatial envelope functions of each coil geometry.

At r = 0: The central magnet experiences only B_A (clockwise), not B_B.
At r > 0: Both fields present; compass needle at outer radius confirms anti-clockwise B_B.

## 4. Lorentz Body Force in Conducting Fluid

Volume force density on galinstan:
  f = J × B   [N/m³]

Where:
  J = σ E   (Ohm's law in fluid frame, σ = electrical conductivity of galinstan)
  E = applied electric field from DC source (6-volt battery)
  B = static field of lower permanent magnet

Azimuthal component (φ-direction in cylindrical coordinates):
  f_φ = J_r B_z − J_z B_r

For radial current J_r in vertical field B_z:
  f_φ = J_r B_z   [drives azimuthal rotation of fluid]

Angular momentum acquired by fluid element per unit time:
  dL_fluid/dt = ∫∫∫ r · f_φ dV = τ_fluid

When τ_fluid (counter-clockwise) = −τ_magnet (counter to magnet's clockwise spin):
  L_fluid satisfies angular momentum conservation without L_body changing.

Galinstan properties (relevant):
  Composition: Ga₆₈.₅In₂₁.₅Sn₁₀ (approximately, by mass fraction)
  Melting point: −19°C (liquid at room temperature)
  Electrical conductivity: ~3.46 × 10⁶ S/m (comparable to mercury)
  Density: ~6440 kg/m³

  F = ṁ v_e   [N]   (exhaust momentum flux)

Where:
  ṁ = propellant mass flow rate [kg/s]
  v_e = exhaust velocity [m/s]

Specific impulse:
  I_sp = v_e / g_0   [s]

Where g_0 = 9.81 m/s² (standard gravity).

For noble gas ion thrusters (xenon or argon noted in source as "argon gas or xenon gas"):
  v_e ≈ 20,000–80,000 m/s   (I_sp ≈ 2000–8000 s)
  vs. chemical: v_e ≈ 3000–4500 m/s

Mass of 3I/ATLAS: >33 billion tons = >3.3 × 10¹³ kg

For meaningful acceleration:
  a = F / M = ṁ v_e / M

Even at v_e = 80 km/s and ṁ = 10⁶ kg/s:
  a = 10⁶ × 8×10⁴ / 3.3×10¹³ ≈ 2.4 × 10⁻³ m/s²   (tiny but non-zero over cosmic timescales)
