## Physical Principles Explicitly Invoked

### 1. Newton's Third Law of Motion (Action-Reaction)

Statement as used in the experimental context:
  For every internal torque applied to the central magnet (clockwise, +z),
  an equal and opposite torque is applied to the outer system (counter-clockwise, −z).

Mathematical form for angular momentum in a closed system with no external torques:
  dL_total/dt = 0
  ⟹ L_magnet + L_body = constant

If initially L_total = 0 (system at rest):
  L_magnet(t) = −L_body(t)

If L_magnet(t) = I_magnet · ω_magnet(t) (clockwise, positive), then:
  L_body(t) = −I_magnet · ω_magnet(t) (counter-clockwise, negative)

Where I_magnet = moment of inertia of central magnet about spin axis (z-axis).

Experimental confirmation: spinning a blade clockwise on a floating boat causes the boat to
rotate counter-clockwise when the blade direction is reversed — demonstrating that total angular
momentum of an isolated system is conserved.

### 2. Lorentz Force (proposed next experimental step)

  F = q(E + v × B)

For a bulk conducting fluid (galinstan) carrying current density J in external magnetic field B:
  f_volume = J × B  [force per unit volume, N/m³]

In the proposed configuration:
* Galinstan (liquid at room temperature: Ga-In-Sn eutectic) serves as the working conductor.
* A DC current (6-volt battery) drives current density J through the galinstan.
* The field B is provided by a permanent magnet placed below the galinstan reservoir.
* J × B produces an azimuthal body force on the liquid, driving counter-clockwise circulation.
* This circulation carries angular momentum (L_galinstan) counter-clockwise, satisfying
  angular momentum conservation without requiring the solid outer body to counter-rotate.

### 3. Rotating Magnetic Field (Three-Phase Induction Principle)

Three-phase current system (phases U, V, W at 120° phase separation) in three symmetric coils
produces a spatially uniform rotating magnetic field:

  B_x(t) = B_0 cos(ωt)
  B_y(t) = B_0 sin(ωt)

Where ω = 2π f_applied (f in Hz, experimentally tuned 10–25 Hz).

The total field vector B_rot(t) rotates at angular velocity ω in the xy-plane (horizontal plane).

Torque on a permanent magnetic dipole m in this rotating field:
  τ = m × B_rot(t)

For m aligned with the field rotation axis (z), the torque is primarily azimuthal, driving
the magnet to spin. A secondary vertical force component (attributed by Drew to the "vortex
magnetism" mechanism) produces z-axis lift.

### 4. Electromagnetic Field Cancellation (Superposition)

When two independent three-phase coil sets (A: UVW and B: U'V'W') are geometrically opposed
at equal radii from center:

At the geometric center (r = 0):
  B_A(0, t) + B_B(0, t) = 0  (fields exactly cancel by symmetry)

At intermediate radius (r > 0):
  B_A(r, t) + B_B(r, t) ≠ 0  (fields do not cancel away from center)

This superposition property is exploited to create a field-free zone at the magnet location
(center) for the anti-clockwise field, while the clockwise field from the lower three coils
acts unimpeded on the central magnet.

Consequence: the clockwise field drives the magnet; the anti-clockwise field does not interfere
with the magnet (it is cancelled at center), but it creates a residual field at outer radii
(experimentally confirmed with compass needle measurement).

## Ion Propulsion Physics (3I/ATLAS Claim)

The interstellar object 3I/ATLAS is proposed to use ion propulsion:

Ion propulsion principle: charged particles (ions) are accelerated through an electrostatic
or electromagnetic field and ejected at high exhaust velocity, producing thrust by momentum
conservation:
  F_thrust = ṁ_exhaust · v_exhaust

Where:
* ṁ_exhaust = mass flow rate of expelled ions (kg/s)
* v_exhaust = exhaust velocity of ions (m/s)

Ion drives achieve v_exhaust >> chemical propellant exhaust velocities (tens of km/s vs. ~4 km/s
for chemical), at the cost of low thrust magnitudes (millinewtons typical for laboratory systems).

For a >33-billion-ton object to be accelerated by ion propulsion to interstellar velocities, the
exhaust velocity and power requirements would be extraordinary — far beyond any known human
technology.

dust tails) is cited as the most anomalous signature: natural comet anti-tails are formed by
large dust grains and always point somewhat toward the Sun, but they are not directed entirely
into the solar wind at all observed angles. The proposed "star sheet" or deflector is described
as an icebreaker-type shield projecting forward to clear the interstellar medium ahead of the
craft.
