## The Advancement Beyond Conventional Physics

Conventional understanding: Magnetic levitation (e.g., Maglev trains) uses diamagnetic repulsion or active feedback in superconducting or electromagnetic systems operating in fixed horizontal planes. Lift against gravity requires either buoyancy, aerodynamic lift, or thrust-reaction forces (rockets, jets). None of these operate by a gyroscopic "pressing against the vacuum."

Drew's claimed advance: A spinning gyroscopic permanent magnet, driven by a rotating three-phase field, generates a reactionless or reduced-reaction upward thrust by interacting with the vacuum of space in a manner unrecognized by mainstream electrodynamics. The counter-spin (Lorentz/Corbino mechanism) neutralizes the angular-momentum reaction force, leaving net vertical propulsion.

Practical implication stated explicitly (2028381856648110270): "this device is intended to produce a new kind of space propulsion, replacing 'rockets'."
