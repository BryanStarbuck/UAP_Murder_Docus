## XY-Plane vs. Z-Axis Dynamics

* The three-phase rotating field primarily acts in the XY-plane (horizontal rotation of field vector).
* This horizontal-plane rotation of the magnetic field induces spin about the Z-axis in the permanent magnet.
* The gyroscopic spin about the Z-axis then produces a force along the Z-axis (upward, i.e., anti-gravity/lift).
* The Lorentz/Corbino counter-spin is also in the XY-plane (horizontal rotation of liquid metal), with the B-field aligned along Z (axial magnet below or in center).
* The net result: XY-plane spin mechanisms produce Z-axis force, without conventional rocket thrust or aerodynamic lift.
