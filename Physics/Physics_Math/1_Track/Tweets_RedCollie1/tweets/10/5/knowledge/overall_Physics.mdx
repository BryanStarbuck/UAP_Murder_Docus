## Drew's Quantum Mechanics / Special Relativity Unification Claim

Source: Note_tweet 1935493845317452116 (tweet with animated GIF of physics diagram).

Drew presents a personal synthesis of quantum mechanics and special relativity, arguing there is no fundamental incompatibility. The chain of reasoning presented:

### Equation Set (Drew's formulation):

1. hf (light, spin-1 photon) = mc²
   * A photon of frequency f has energy hf, and this equals mc² by Einstein mass-energy equivalence. Since the photon has spin 1, this is the spin-1 case.

2. hf (particle at rest, spin-1/2) = 2 × mc²
   * A spin-1/2 particle at rest has an energy given by hf = 2mc². Drew claims this differs from the photon case by a factor of 2 due to the half-integer spin topology (Möbius strip model, see below).

3. hf (particle in motion) = mv² by de Broglie
   * de Broglie relation: λ = h/p = h/(mv), so p = h/λ, and the kinetic energy expression mv² (not ½mv²) appears in Drew's formulation, suggesting he is using E = p*v = (h/λ)*v = hv/λ = hf, i.e., the de Broglie wave energy (group vs. phase velocity distinction relevant here). This is a non-standard kinematic expression and may conflate phase and group velocities.

4. hf (particle at rest plus in motion) = 2mc² + mv² = 2mc²(1 + v²/(2c²)) by special relativity
   * Drew adds the rest-energy and kinetic terms: 2mc² + mv².
   * He rewrites: 2mc²(1 + v²/(2c²)).
   * He labels this "a low-speed approximation for simplicity." This is analogous to Taylor-expanding the Lorentz factor: E = γmc² ≈ mc²(1 + v²/(2c²)) at low v/c, but with the prefactor 2mc² instead of mc², due to his spin-1/2 rest energy claim.

### Physical Interpretation Drew Provides:

* All particles at rest have an "unseen" periodicity of motion through time — he analogizes this to a Möbius strip traversal — which gives them "great energy at rest." This is his reinterpretation of the Zitterbewegung (trembling motion) of the Dirac electron, or more precisely a topological model of the spinor double-cover structure in SU(2).

* When a particle is in relative motion with respect to an observer, "we 'see' some of that internal periodicity by the de Broglie waves." This maps to the standard quantum mechanical result that the de Broglie wavelength λ = h/(mv) encodes the internal phase periodicity as seen from a moving frame.

* The right-hand rule for magnetism "derives from a right-handed twist in its Möbius-strip subatomic topology." This is a non-standard claim connecting spinor topology to classical electromagnetic handedness.

* "There is also a subtle reason why the speed of light is finite, that comes from its spin-1/2 periodicity." This is a highly non-standard claim; in standard QFT, the photon is spin-1. Drew is suggesting a deeper topological reason for c being finite, related to spinor periodicities.

* Drew argues quantum probabilism arises from projecting higher-dimensional deterministic objects into 3D space. Analogizes to seeing a 4-dimensional cube (tesseract / hypercube) from 3D: its 3D cross-sections appear as different shapes depending on orientation.

## Newton's Third Law Challenge: Counter-Spin Strategy

Source: Note_tweet 1992566706888495443; tweet 1979720343540682959.

* Standard Newtonian mechanics: any upward force on an internal gyroscopic element produces an equal and opposite downward reaction on the vessel containing it. Net external force = 0. No net levitation.
* Drew's proposed circumvention: Place a Lorentz-force counter-spin element (liquid metal galinstan in Corbino-disc geometry, driven by DC current + axial permanent magnet) just below the three-phase-driven magnet.
* Argument: "This will leave total spin angular momentum unchanged. Hopefully Newton's 3rd Law will no longer apply, if the counter-spin force is of a Lorentz kind with liquid metal."
* The reasoning: if the upward-pushing torque from the three-phase magnetic drive equals the downward Lorentz-force torque from the counter-spin element, the angular momenta cancel. If the mechanism of force transfer differs (one electromagnetic gyroscopic, one Lorentz), then in Drew's view the third-law pair may not close within the mechanical system, leaving a net external force.
* This is a non-standard physics claim. Standard analysis would say the third law must hold for all internal forces regardless of mechanism. However, Drew is testing this hypothesis experimentally.
