   (a) Still fall with acceleration g downward (center of mass still obeys F=mg)

explanations:
* The observer is supporting the gyroscope at a point other than directly below
  center of mass (the horizontal precession creates a different force distribution)

  effective apparent weight
* A genuine non-classical effect: the gyroscope's spin reduces its effective
  gravitational mass (distinct from inertial mass -- a test of the equivalence principle)

modification of gravitational mass, contradicting the weak equivalence principle.
