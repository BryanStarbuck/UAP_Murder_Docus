Energy considerations for a helical magnetic field propulsion system:

   U_B = integral (B^2 / (2*mu_0)) dV  (total field energy stored)

For a propagating helical wave in a conductor/plasma:
   The Poynting vector S = (E x B) / mu_0 carries energy in the propagation direction

as axial electromagnetic momentum flux rather than heat or XY-plane field energy.
Whether this momentum flux provides net thrust depends on whether the helical field
interacts with an external medium (vacuum quantum field, or structured space medium).
