  copper wire

* Driven by three-phase power supply with UVW phase labeling
* A second set of 3 wire coils (triangular arrangement, 120 deg spacing) also receives
  three-phase UVW power
* The two sets (3-coil triangle + 6-coil hexagon) are connected in series: UVW UVW
  "like a magnetic track"

The rotating magnetic field B_rot(t) produced by a balanced 3-phase set of coils is:
   B_x(t) = B_0 * cos(omega * t)
   B_y(t) = B_0 * cos(omega * t - 2*pi/3)  [phase-shifted by 120 deg]
   Third coil: cos(omega * t - 4*pi/3)       [phase-shifted by 240 deg]
The superposition of these three sinusoidal fields from symmetrically placed coils
produces a spatially rotating field of constant magnitude B_0 rotating at angular
frequency omega in the XY plane (perpendicular to the coil axis).

LORENTZ FORCE ON IONIZED ARC: EXPERIMENTAL SETUP
============================================================

Source (1954098493859270934.yaml note_tweet / 13_1954092358477524994_video.mdx):

Experimental setup:
* Two parallel wires carrying DC current (6.6 kV DC voltage applied)
* Air between wires ionized, producing a plasma arc (ionized nitrogen/oxygen)
* Ring magnet placed below the arc, between the two wires

Observation with North pole up:
"The flame is very strong due to the rise" -- arc deflects in one direction (right)
   F_L = q(v x B) where v is ion drift velocity, B is magnet field direction

Observation with South pole up (180-degree flip):
"It's a strong view to the left" -- arc deflects in opposite horizontal direction

Physics: the Lorentz force on a moving charge q with velocity v in magnetic field B:
   F = q(v x B)
The direction of F is perpendicular to both v (along electric field, +red to -black)
and B (along magnet pole axis). Reversing B direction (N->S, S->N) reverses the sign
of (v x B) and therefore reverses the deflection direction.

Analogy to gyroscopic precession:
"A linear force of electricity, between plus-red and minus-black wires, gets turned
by 90 degrees, when it is applied to tiny atoms or particles that are spinning rapidly
(like for air blown onto a spinning top)."

In the Lorentz force framework: the "spin" of the electrons/ions in the magnetic field
context is their orbital/spin alignment with B. When spin is aligned parallel to B, the
velocity cross B gives a lateral force rather than an axial one.

CONSERVATION OF ANGULAR MOMENTUM: SPIN/COUNTER-SPIN REQUIREMENT
============================================================

Source (1979722853189841331.yaml note_tweet):

"In terms of what Edwin Fuhr saw (video 2), we can be SURE that some (hidden) magnet
inside of that hovering saucer was spinning clockwise, to make its upper parts spin the
other way (anti-clockwise), for conservation of total spin angular momentum."

Conservation of angular momentum (L_total = const):
   L_inner + L_outer = const = 0 (if initially at rest)
Therefore:
   L_inner = -L_outer
   I_inner * omega_inner = -I_outer * omega_outer

The external hull (upper parts) spinning anti-clockwise while the internal magnet spins
clockwise is the mandatory consequence of angular momentum conservation for an isolated
system. The external counter-spin is not a feature but a physical necessity.

HOMOPOLAR MOTOR (CORBINO DISC) WITH LIQUID MERCURY
============================================================

Source (2008295445022601460.yaml, 1985115001921863786.yaml):

The Corbino disc is a radially-symmetric conducting disk with current flowing radially
outward from a central electrode in an axial magnetic field. The J x B Lorentz body
force acts azimuthally, causing the disc to rotate.

For liquid mercury in a Corbino geometry:
   f_phi = J_r * B_z
   where J_r = I / (2*pi*r*t) (radial current density at radius r, thickness t)

This generates a homopolar motor torque without commutation. Combined with a counter-
spinning central magnet (driven by 3-phase coils), Phase 1 (liquid metal) and Phase 2
(counter-spinning magnet) provide spin/counter-spin.
