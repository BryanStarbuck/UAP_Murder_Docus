EQUATION 3: GYROSCOPIC PRECESSION TORQUE
============================================================

Source (1954098493859270934.yaml note_tweet, 13_1950755144062373888_video.mdx):

   tau = dL/dt = r x F

For a spinning gyroscope with:
* L = angular momentum vector = I * omega (I = moment of inertia, omega = spin angular
  velocity vector, pointing along spin axis)
* r = position vector of center of mass from pivot
* F = external force (gravity: F = -m*g*z_hat)

   tau = r x F = r x (-m*g*z_hat)

If r is in the horizontal (x) direction, r = r_0 * x_hat:
   tau = r_0 * x_hat x (-m*g*z_hat) = r_0 * m * g * (x_hat x (-z_hat))
   x_hat x (-z_hat) = x_hat x (-z_hat) = y_hat
   tau = r_0 * m * g * y_hat   (horizontal torque, perpendicular to both r and gravity)

Precession rate:
   Omega_prec = tau / L = (r_0 * m * g) / (I * omega)

The gravitational force does not accelerate the gyroscope downward; instead it drives
horizontal precession. This is the mathematical basis for the claim that gravity's
downward pull becomes a horizontal (sideways) force for a spinning system.
