LORENTZ FORCE: CLASSICAL BASELINE AND ITS GYROSCOPIC ANALOG
============================================================

Source (1954098493859270934.yaml note_tweet):

Classical Lorentz force on a moving charge:
   F = q * (v x B)

Components:
* q: electric charge of ion/electron
* v: drift velocity of charge carrier (direction: from + to - terminal, along E-field)
* B: magnetic field direction (along pole axis, N or S)
* F: force perpendicular to both v and B

Experimental verification (ionized air arc at 6.6 kV DC between two wires, ring
magnet below):
* North pole up: arc deflects right (F perpendicular to B_z and v_x gives F_y)
* South pole up: arc deflects left (B_z reversal reverses F_y)

Gyroscopic analog:
"A downward force of gravity is applied similarly to a fast-spinning gyroscope. That
downward force likewise gets turned sideways by 90 degrees, due to rapid spin of the
gyroscope."

For a spinning gyroscope with angular momentum L = I*omega (moment of inertia I,
angular velocity vector omega):
   Torque tau = dL/dt = r x F_grav = r x (mg * (-z_hat))
For r = horizontal arm: tau is horizontal (perpendicular to both r and F_grav)
This produces horizontal precession, not vertical fall:
   tau = r x F = |r| * mg * sin(theta) * (horizontal direction)

The 90-degree deflection of both the Lorentz force and the gravitational force on a
spinning body is presented as the foundational physics of UAP propulsion: gravity's
downward pull becomes horizontal precession, reducing apparent weight.

============================================================

effective mechanical medium. The claim: this resistance IS the mechanism that UAPs
use to push against the vacuum, analogous to a propeller's blade pushing against air.

The chain of reasoning:
   spin -> angular momentum L -> anisotropic resistance to rotation
   -> effective medium with directionality
   -> propeller/screw action possible in vacuum

INVERTED CONE TOPOLOGY: LEVITATION PHASE TRANSITION
============================================================

Source (1946435673562751159.yaml note_tweet):

Claimed equation from "a page from a science textbook of the future":

   w = h / a

where:
* w = angular velocity (omega) of the spinning magnetic gyroscope
* h = height above the surface (altitude of the magnet on the Z-axis)
* a = area (cross-sectional area of the precession cone, in the XY-plane)

Geometric interpretation -- the "inverted cone" topology:

At low rpm (w small):
   h is small (magnet stays near bottom)
   a is large (wide precession base -- magnet precesses in wide horizontal circles)

At high rpm (w large):
   h is large (magnet rises high on Z-axis)
   a is small (narrow precession -- magnet spins tightly around pole, nearly vertical)

Phase transition: "A 'transition' from low to high rpm causes precession at near-zero
height to become spin at high height."

The geometry of states forms an inverted cone in (h, a) space. The transition from
precessional orbit (large a, small h) to axial spin (small a, large h) is a topological
change in the motion's character, not merely a continuous smooth change.

Physical relationship: from w = h/a, the upward force (F_up ~ w) relates to altitude
and area:
   F_up ~ w = h/a
   h = F_up * a

So altitude is proportional to precession area for a given w, or proportional to w
for a given area. As w increases beyond the transition threshold, the system
reorganizes: precession collapses (a -> 0) and altitude rises (h -> max).

NEWTON'S THIRD LAW BYPASS: CLAIMED EXPERIMENTAL EVIDENCE
============================================================

Source (2045812737897558122.yaml note_tweet):

"AC Lenz levitation shows non-Newtonian forces, when its disc conductor is given
rapid spin."

Lenz levitation (AC Lenz effect):
* A time-varying magnetic field (dB/dt != 0) from an AC electromagnet above a
  conducting disc induces eddy currents in the disc (Faraday's law):
   EMF = -dPhi/dt  (Phi = magnetic flux through disc)
   J = EMF / R_eff (eddy current density, R_eff = effective resistance)
* These eddy currents interact with the applied B field (Lenz's law):
   F_Lenz = J x B (upward, opposing the change in flux)
* Classically: F_up on disc = F_down on electromagnet source (Newton's Third Law)
* Claimed experimental result: when the disc conductor is simultaneously spinning
  rapidly (spin axis coincident with B axis), the downward reaction force on the source
  does not equal the upward force on the disc. This constitutes a claimed bypass of
  Newton's Third Law.

The mechanism proposed: the spin of the conducting disc introduces a Coriolis-like
coupling between the eddy currents and the applied field. In a rotating frame:
   F_eff = F_Lenz + F_Coriolis = F_Lenz + 2*m*(v x omega)
The additional Coriolis term changes the force balance asymmetrically -- upward on
disc vs. downward on source -- because the rotating disc frame is non-inertial.

============================================================

Source (2002133263150961012.yaml):

"They drew a 'closed time-like curve' in crops, used for time travel. The CIA have known
about such time travelers since 1991."

A closed timelike curve (CTC) is a worldline in spacetime that loops back to its own
past. In General Relativity, CTCs arise in:
* Godel metric (rotating universe)
* Kerr metric (rotating black hole interior)
* Traversable wormholes with specific topology
* Van Stockum dust cylinder
* Tipler cylinder

For a CTC to exist, the spacetime must allow a path along which ds^2 < 0 (timelike)
that forms a closed loop. The condition in general metric g_mu_nu:
   g_mu_nu * dx^mu * dx^nu < 0 for the full closed path
