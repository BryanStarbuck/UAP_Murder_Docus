## 1. Relativistic Origin of Magnetism: Lorentz Boost of Electrostatics

Source: YAML 1917826496196141183 (note_tweet)

This is the first and most precisely stated non-Newtonian physics claim in the dataset.

Classical Newtonian mechanics: forces act instantaneously at a distance; no concept
of electromagnetic fields, no speed-of-light limitation.

The author's claim:
"Certain right-handed features of the electron, not seen for v = 0, rotate from
time 4-D into space 3-D to create right-handed magnetism."

This is the special relativity (SR) derivation of the magnetic force as a relativistic
consequence of electrostatics. In Newtonian mechanics, there is no magnetic force on a
stationary observer in a purely electrostatic field. SR shows:

For a stationary test charge q at rest relative to a neutral wire (equal densities of
positive and negative charges at rest: net E = 0, B = 0):
  In the lab frame: the wire is neutral; no force on q.

For a test charge q moving at velocity v parallel to wire carrying current I:
  Newtonian prediction: no force (q not accelerating, no E field from neutral wire).
  SR prediction: in the test charge's rest frame, the positive charge density is
    rho_+ = rho_0 / gamma   (less dense; positive charges at rest in lab frame, Lorentz-contracted)
    rho_- = rho_0 * gamma   (more dense; negative charges moving at -v in test frame)
  Net charge density in test-charge frame: rho_net = rho_- - rho_+ > 0
  -> Electric field E' in test-charge frame -> electric force on q.
  -> This electric force, when transformed back to lab frame, is the magnetic force.

The transformation: F_lab = F'_test / gamma  [for transverse force]
  F_lab = q * v x B  [the magnetic Lorentz force]
  where B = mu_0 * I / (2*pi*r) [standard Biot-Savart result for long straight wire]

Conclusion: magnetism is a relativistic correction to electrostatics of order v^2/c^2.
In Newtonian mechanics (c -> infinity), magnetism vanishes. This IS beyond Newtonian
mechanics and is the formal result of Special Relativity applied to electrostatics.

## 2. General Relativistic Frame Dragging / Gravitomagnetic Effect

Source: YAML 1917826496196141183 (note_tweet)

"So what about gravity for two objects in fast relative motion? We know that Mercury
precesses forward, and that light bends around the Sun. But what is this extra feature

like for magnetism? If not, what is it?"

This directly poses the GR frame-dragging / gravitomagnetism question. In Newtonian
gravity:
  F_grav = -G * m1 * m2 / r^2 * r_hat   [instantaneous, no velocity dependence]

In GR (linearized / weak-field):
  The geodesic equation for a test particle:
    d^2 x^mu / d tau^2 + Gamma^mu_alpha_beta * (dx^alpha/dtau) * (dx^beta/dtau) = 0

  The Christoffel symbols Gamma^mu_alpha_beta encode both gravitoelectric (Newtonian)
  and gravitomagnetic (frame-dragging) contributions.

  Gravitomagnetic force on moving mass m at velocity v:
    F_gm = m * v x (2 * B_g)
  where B_g = curl(A_g) = (G/c^2) * curl(J/r^3) [for distributed source J = rho*v_source]

  This is beyond Newtonian mechanics: a new velocity-dependent gravitational force
  proportional to 1/c^2, analogous to the magnetic force in EM but for gravity.

  Observed consequences:
  - Mercury perihelion precession: 43.0 arcsec/century (GR) vs. 0 (Newtonian).
    Full GR calculation: delta_phi_per_orbit = 6*pi*G*M / (a*(1-e^2)*c^2)
    For Mercury: a = 5.79e10 m, e = 0.206, M_sun = 1.989e30 kg
    -> delta_phi = 5.02e-7 rad/orbit = 43.0 arcsec/century. Confirmed.
  - Light deflection: delta_phi = 4*G*M / (b*c^2) [b = impact parameter]
    Twice the Newtonian prediction (Newtonian gives 2*G*M/(b*c^2) if light has
    effective mass from E=mc^2, but GR gives 4*G*M/(b*c^2) due to spatial curvature).
    Confirmed by Eddington 1919 eclipse observations.
  - Lense-Thirring precession of gyroscopes near rotating mass.
  - Geodetic precession (de Sitter precession) of orbit plane.
