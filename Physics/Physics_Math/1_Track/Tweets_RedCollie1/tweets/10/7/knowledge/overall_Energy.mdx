## 4. Hall Effect Accelerator: Energy Budget

Source: MDX 13_2018894777127936000_video, YAML 2018894910129352739

Device: 6-stage coilgun, 24V DC supply, ball magnet projectile.

Energy stored per coil stage:
  E_coil = (1/2) * L * I^2
where L = inductance of the coil (H), I = current (A).

Energy transferred to projectile per stage:
  Delta_KE = (1/2) * m * (v_f^2 - v_i^2) = integral(F * dx)
           = (1/2) * (L_1 - L_2) * I^2   [change in inductance energy]
where L_1 = inductance with projectile at entry, L_2 = inductance with projectile at exit.

Total efficiency of a coilgun:
  eta = Delta_KE_total / E_electrical_input
For simple single-stage: eta ~ 1-25% depending on switching timing.
For 6-stage at 24V: demonstration-level power, no anomalous energy noted.

## 5. Ion Thruster Energy: 3I/ATLAS Propulsion

Source: YAML 1989818106110513487

"3I/ATLAS seems to use a ring of six powerful ion thrusters."

Ion thruster energy budget:
- Specific impulse: I_sp = v_e / g_0 (for xenon HET: I_sp ~ 1600-3000 s; I_sp*g_0 ~ 16-30 km/s exhaust velocity)
- Power-to-thrust ratio: P/F = v_e / 2 = I_sp * g_0 / 2
  For I_sp = 3000 s: P/F = 3000 * 9.81 / 2 ~ 14,700 W/N
- Six thrusters at "powerful" level: e.g., 6 x 100 kW = 600 kW total power
  -> Total thrust ~ 600,000 / 14,700 ~ 40 N (very small for an asteroid-class body)
  
The claim that a natural-looking cometary body uses ion thrusters implies a concealed
energy source providing hundreds of kilowatts to megawatts of electrical power.
The author does not specify the energy source for this propulsion system.

## 7. Energy Implications of Spin/Counter-Spin Levitation (If Confirmed)

If the counter-rotating liquid metal system does produce an upward force F_lev:
  Power input: P_in = P_DC + P_3phase (both conventional electrical power)
  Gravitational potential energy gain rate: dU/dt = F_lev * v_z (where v_z = upward velocity)
  Work efficiency: eta_lev = F_lev * v_z / P_in

For the device to be "useful" (net energy positive for levitation):
  F_lev * v_z > P_in
  This would imply a gravitational energy gain exceeding electrical input — which would
  require an external energy source (Earth's gravitational field acting as a reservoir
  in a non-Newtonian coupling, or vacuum energy coupling).

The author does not make this explicit claim in the source material; the stated goal is
simply to measure whether F_lev > 0, not to claim over-unity behavior.
