## Core Propulsion/Levitation Claim

Dr. Horace Drew (RedCollie1) asserts that crop circle formations encode step-by-step
engineering instructions for constructing a UAP propulsion/levitation system. The
central engineering thesis is that "UFO levitation" is achievable by humans through
reverse-engineering of principles demonstrated in both the crop circle record and in
direct laboratory experiments.

The propulsion benefit claimed: generation of a net upward (Z-axis) force — levitation
off the ground — via the interaction of two distinct electromagnetic torque mechanisms
acting in opposing rotational directions simultaneously in a liquid-metal medium.

## Physics Advances That Enable the Claimed Propulsion Benefit

### 1. Liquid-Metal Homopolar Motor Operation

The liquid-metal homopolar motor (or homopolar generator/pump) operates via crossed
DC electric current and static magnetic field producing a Lorentz body force on a
conducting fluid. In the terrestrial context:

- Liquid gallium (Ga, melting point 29.76 degC, electrical conductivity ~ 3.7 x 10^6 S/m)
  is used as the conducting fluid medium.
- Liquid galinstan (Ga-In-Sn eutectic alloy, melting point ~ -19 degC, similarly
  high electrical conductivity) is also used.
- DC current is applied radially or axially through the liquid metal.
- A static permanent magnet (disc magnet, 60 mm diameter as cited in experiment) is
  placed below or inside the liquid pool, providing the B-field.
- The Lorentz force F = J x B (where J is the current density vector and B is the
  applied magnetic flux density) acts as a volumetric body force throughout the fluid,
  inducing bulk azimuthal rotation (homopolar pumping / spin).
- Experimental result (stated): 12V DC applied to liquid gallium in a stainless steel
  bowl causes the fluid to spin anti-clockwise.

### 2. Three-Phase Rotating Magnetic Field (RMF) Drive

A three-phase alternating current supply applied to a set of wire coils produces a
spatially rotating magnetic field (RMF). This is the operating principle of the
induction motor:
- Phase windings spaced 120 degrees apart in azimuth, energized with AC voltages
  mutually phase-shifted by 120 degrees (U, V, W phases).
- The resulting superposition of the three sinusoidal magnetic field contributions
  produces a field vector of constant magnitude rotating in the XY-plane at the
  supply angular frequency omega = 2*pi*f.
- Applied to liquid metal (gallium or galinstan), the rotating field drives the fluid
  by electromagnetic induction — the same mechanism as a linear induction pump in
  nuclear engineering (LMFBR coolant pumping) but here applied as an azimuthal motor.
- Experimental result (stated): a 60 mm permanent magnet immersed in the liquid metal
  and the liquid spinning anti-clockwise (from DC current) is forced to reverse to
  clockwise rotation by three-phase power applied in the opposing rotational sense.

### 3. Spin / Counter-Spin Configuration

The claimed propulsion advance beyond these two known mechanisms lies in their
simultaneous superposition in opposing rotational senses:
- Inner element (disc magnet / inner bowl) spins clockwise.
- Outer element (upper bowl / liquid metal exterior shell) spins anti-clockwise.
- The two counter-rotating mass/field systems are proposed to create a net vertical
  (Z-axis) force component — levitation — by a mechanism not yet fully specified in
  the source tweets but described as distinct from classical rotational dynamics.
- Analogy cited: Edwin Fuhr (Langenburg, Saskatchewan, Canada, 1974) witnessed a
  UFO with an outer shell spinning one direction and an inner section spinning the
  opposite direction. The author treats this as corroborating evidence that spin/
  counter-spin is the operative mechanism.
- The device is described as having 3 wire coils below and 6 wire coils above, the
  6 upper coils wired U-V-W-U-V-W (doubled three-phase), required to spin in the
  direction opposite to the 3 lower coils. The assembly operates like an "annular
  induction motor that pumps liquid metal."

### 4. Hall Effect Accelerator (Supplemental Device)

A separate experimental device is demonstrated:
- Six wire coils arranged around a central bore.
- 24V DC applied.
- A small permanent ball magnet is accelerated through the coil bore by sequential
  energization of coils (coilgun / Gauss gun principle).
- Dr. Drew terms this a "Hall Effect accelerator," although the mechanism demonstrated
  is more precisely a multi-stage coilgun (electromagnetic induction accelerator).
  The Hall effect proper describes a transverse voltage developed across a conductor
  carrying current in a perpendicular magnetic field; Dr. Drew may be using the term
  loosely or to indicate a related electromagnetic acceleration principle.

### 5. Claimed Advancement Beyond Classical EM

The author claims:
- That the specific combination of Lorentz-driven homopolar spin + counter-directional
  three-phase RMF spin produces an effect not predicted by standard electromagnetic
  theory alone: specifically, a net upward force (reduction in apparent weight / levitation).
- Laboratory testing of this gravity-weight effect ("I will test gravity-weight soon
  to find out") is stated as planned, indicating this remains in the experimental
  hypothesis stage at time of writing.
- The crop circle diagrams are interpreted as encoding exact engineering parameters
  (coil counts, wiring topology, current directions) that make this work.

## Summary of Propulsion Chain

Liquid metal (Ga or galinstan)
  + DC current (12V demonstrated; higher voltages for scale)
  + Static disc magnet below (homopolar motor principle)
  -> azimuthal fluid rotation (direction set by current polarity and magnet orientation)

Simultaneously:
Three-phase AC (U-V-W winding, 120-degree phase separation, 6 coils above)
  -> rotating magnetic field in XY-plane
  -> electromagnetic torque on liquid metal in opposite rotational direction

Superposition of opposite-sense torques on liquid metal and disc magnet system
  -> claimed emergence of net Z-axis (upward) force = "UFO levitation"

The specific physics mechanism linking counter-rotation to vertical force is not yet
derived in the source material; it is stated as the research hypothesis under test.
