## 1. Right-Handed Magnetic Field of a Current-Carrying Coil

Source: YAML 1917826496196141183 (note_tweet)

Claim (theoretical): When a charged electron moves through a wire coil relative to
stationary protons and neutrons, it generates a right-handed magnetic field that wraps
around the long axis of the wire, parallel to the direction of electron motion.

Formal statement: This is the Biot-Savart result integrated over a solenoid geometry.
For an infinitely long solenoid of n turns per unit length carrying current I, the
interior field is:
  B = mu_0 * n * I  (uniform, axial, direction given by right-hand rule)

The author frames this in a relativistic / 4-dimensional context:
"Certain right-handed features of the electron, not seen for v = 0, rotate from
time 4-D into space 3-D to create right-handed magnetism. It all depends on our
point of view."

This is a reference to the Lorentz transformation origin of magnetism: in the frame
of a stationary observer, a current-carrying wire with moving electrons creates a
magnetic field that can be understood as arising from the Lorentz contraction of the
charge distribution — i.e., magnetism as a relativistic consequence of electrostatics.

## 2. Gravity Analogous Magnetic Component for Masses in Relative Motion

Source: YAML 1917826496196141183 (note_tweet)

The author poses an open research question: for two masses in fast relative motion,
general relativity predicts frame-dragging / gravitomagnetic effects analogous to the
magnetic field of moving charges. The known effects cited:
- Mercury's perihelion precession (26.6 arcsec/century beyond Newtonian) — Schwarzschild
  geodetic precession + Lense-Thirring.
- Light bending around the Sun: delta_phi = 4GM / (c^2 * b) where b is the impact
  parameter (confirmed to be twice the Newtonian prediction).

The question posed: "Could it [the extra gravity component for masses in fast relative
motion] be right-handed like for magnetism?"

This refers to gravitoelectromagnetism (GEM), the linearized weak-field approximation
of GR in which the Einstein field equations take a Maxwell-like form:
  div(g) = -4*pi*G*rho
  div(B_g) = 0
  curl(g) = -dB_g/dt
  curl(B_g) = (16*pi*G/c^2)*J + (1/c^2)*dg/dt
where g is the gravitoelectric field and B_g is the gravitomagnetic field.
The gravitomagnetic field B_g is the GR analog of the magnetic field, sourced by
mass-energy currents J. For a rotating mass (Lense-Thirring effect), B_g induces
precession of nearby gyroscopes: Omega_LT = G*J / (r^3 * c^2) [schematic form].

The author notes no reliable experimental data on the exact tensorial structure of
the gravitomagnetic analog for two masses in fast relative motion. This remains
an open question in the source material.

## 3. Lorentz Force on Liquid Metal (Homopolar Motor / MHD Pump)

Source: YAMLs 1967126894186557513, 1992580530165125291, 1976971268139135206
Source: MDX 13_1976969574974472194_video (lab transcription), 13_1976969778192687104_video

The Lorentz force on a volume element of conducting fluid:
  dF = J x B  (body force per unit volume, N/m^3)
where:
  J = sigma * (E + v x B) — Ohm's law in MHD, sigma = electrical conductivity (S/m)
  B = applied external magnetic flux density (T)
  E = applied electric field (V/m)
  v = fluid velocity field

For the homopolar motor geometry (disc magnet below, DC current radially injected):
- B is axial (along Z).
- J is radial (in XY-plane).
- F = J x B is azimuthal -> produces azimuthal rotation (spin) of the liquid metal.
- The direction of spin depends on the polarity of the DC current relative to the
  orientation of B: reversing either reverses the spin direction.

Experimental results stated:
- Liquid gallium in a stainless steel bowl with a disc magnet below + 12V DC current
  applied via red (positive) and black (negative) leads: liquid gallium spins
  anti-clockwise.
- The same setup with NS magnets (i.e., permanent magnet with north-south axis
  identified): spin observed and confirmed as consistent with Lorentz force prediction.
- A 60 mm permanent magnet as the field source: confirmed homopolar spin of the gallium.

Stated correspondence principle: "We know that liquid gallium will behave just like
aluminium [in liquid state, i.e., any liquid metal with high conductivity] putting in
liquid state."

This draws a direct analogy between liquid gallium MHD behavior and that of liquid
aluminum — both are non-ferromagnetic metals with high electrical conductivity,
expected to behave as classical MHD fluids under the J x B body force.

## 4. Three-Phase Rotating Magnetic Field (RMF) and Its Interaction with Liquid Metal

Source: YAMLs 1976971268139135206, 2008295626753360204, 2015732010086785296
Source: MDX 13_1976969778192687104_video, 13_1976970101728690177_video, 13_1992579295949258752_video

A three-phase AC power supply drives three sets of wire coils arranged at 120-degree
azimuthal intervals. The resulting magnetic field in the interior of the coil assembly:
  B_x(t) = B_0 * cos(omega*t)
  B_y(t) = B_0 * cos(omega*t - 2*pi/3)
  B_z(t) = B_0 * cos(omega*t - 4*pi/3)   [for a three-coil arrangement in 3D]
or for a planar arrangement in XY:
  B_x(t) = B_0 * cos(omega*t)
  B_y(t) = B_0 * sin(omega*t)    [pi/2 phase shift for 2-phase; 2*pi/3 for 3-phase]

The magnitude |B| = B_0 = constant; the field vector rotates at omega = 2*pi*f rad/s.

Interaction with liquid metal:
- The rotating B-field induces eddy currents in the conducting fluid by Faraday's law:
  curl(E_induced) = -dB/dt
- These induced currents J_induced interact with B via the Lorentz body force
  J_induced x B, driving the fluid in the direction of field rotation.
- This is identical in principle to an induction motor: the liquid metal plays the
  role of the squirrel-cage rotor.

Key experimental result stated:
(a) 60 mm permanent magnet inside gallium/galinstan spinning anti-clockwise (DC-driven),
    three-phase field applied: magnet turns clockwise. Directions are opposite.
(b) Same magnet spinning clockwise (DC current reversed), three-phase power applied:
    magnet turns clockwise. Both effects add.
(c) A crop circle from May 1999 encoded both configurations:
    - "Slide 1: flow of liquid metal with DC current alone, and NO magnetic field"
    - "Slide 2: liquid metal SPINS when given both DC current (plus/minus), and also a
      magnet m1 placed just below"

Wiring topology specified for the UFO levitation device:
- 3 wire coils below (lower assembly): one set of three-phase windings.
- 6 wire coils above (upper assembly): U-V-W-U-V-W wiring — doubled three-phase
  configuration with coil pairs at each azimuthal position. Required to spin in
  the direction OPPOSITE to the 3 lower coils.
- The assembly is described as functioning like an "annular induction motor that pumps
  liquid metal."

## 8. Ion Thruster Ring as Alternative Magnetic / Electromagnetic Propulsion Claim

Source: YAML 1989818106110513487

Regarding 3I/ATLAS (astronomical object):
"3I/ATLAS seems to use a ring of six powerful ion thrusters."

Ion thruster operation: xenon (or other propellant) ionized by electron bombardment
or RF ionization; ions accelerated through a magnetic/electric grid system.
The electric field E and magnetic field B in the ion optics region:
- Hall-effect thrusters: E is axial, B is radial -> E x B drift provides ion Hall
  current; thrust from ion beam momentum flux.
- Gridded ion thrusters: ions accelerated by electrostatic potential across biased grids,
  with magnetic field used for electron confinement only.

Six-thruster ring arrangement: azimuthal symmetry preserves momentum balance in the
plane perpendicular to thrust axis; net thrust is axial.
