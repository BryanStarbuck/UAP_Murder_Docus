## 2. Proposed Governing Equation

w = h / a

* w = angular velocity of the spinning disc or magnetic flywheel (rad/s)
* h = height above ground (m)
* a = horizontal cross-sectional area (m^2)

Physical interpretation: For a spinning disc of fixed cross-sectional area a, increasing angular velocity w requires increasing height h (the disc rises). The equation is stated as "the simplest" of several equations from a "future science textbook." It is not derived from any existing physical framework and is presented as phenomenological.

Equivalent form: h = w * a. The height is proportional to the product of angular velocity and disc area.

Implication for design: A disc with low height and large area (flat, wide pancake shape) is the optimal geometry for demonstrating the effect. This shape maximizes the ratio a/h, requiring less angular velocity to achieve a given height.

## 3. Mechanism: Gyroscopic Instability Pushing Against the Vacuum

### Classical Gyroscope Background
A spinning body with large angular momentum L = I*omega is gyroscopically stable (resists precession). Precession rate Omega_p = tau / (I*omega). As omega increases, the precession rate decreases, and the gyroscope becomes increasingly rigid in space.

### Drew's Proposed Extension
When the spinning magnetic disc spins rapidly enough, it develops a "gyroscopic instability" — a dynamic instability where the system is no longer stable against small perturbations in the vertical direction. This instability is claimed to produce a net upward force.

The mechanism is described as the disc "pushing against the vacuum," analogous to:
* A propeller pushing against air: thrust F = rho * A * v * delta_v (Rankine-Froude momentum theory, where rho is air density, A is propeller disk area, v is induced velocity, delta_v is velocity increment)
* A boat propeller pushing against water

The claim is that the vacuum of space has some property analogous to the mechanical impedance of a fluid, against which the spinning disc can push. This property is not identified with any known vacuum quantum effect in the source material.
