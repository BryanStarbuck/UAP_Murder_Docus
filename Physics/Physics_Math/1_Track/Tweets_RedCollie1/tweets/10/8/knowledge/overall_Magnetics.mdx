## 1. Spinning Magnetic Levitation — Core Phenomenon

### Discovery Origin
* Hamid Ucar (2021): First documented spinning magnetic levitation experiment. Used two small permanent magnets attracted by both (a) magnetic dipole-dipole interaction and (b) helical magnetic field coupling. ### Rotating Magnetic Field (r.m.f.) Generation
* Device: 3 copper wire coils arranged to generate a 3-phase rotating magnetic field.
* Expanded configuration: 12 coils arranged in a ring, energized as 3-phase AC power in the sequence U-V-W-U-V-W-U-V-W-U-V-W (4 full cycles of the 3-phase pattern around the ring).
* Wiring topology: All "U" coils wired in series (not parallel). Same for all "V" coils and all "W" coils. This series topology is identified as the key enabling condition for the observed counter-spin phenomenon.
* Frequency range tested: 20 Hz, 25 Hz, 30 Hz (tweet 13_2018897166006403072_video.mdx: "20 Hertz spinning beautifully... 25, 30 Hertz. Look at it spin.")
* Coil resistance specification: "We need about 10 ohms in each coil for good power." (13_2018897183253348352_video.mdx)

### Rotor Polarity Control and Counter-Rotation
* With 8 rotor magnets arranged N-S-N-S-N-S-N-S (alternating polarity): rotor spins WITH the rotating magnetic field (synchronous induction, consistent with classical motor operation).
* With 8 rotor magnets arranged N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N (all same polarity, all north facing outward): rotor spins AGAINST the rotating magnetic field (counter-rotation relative to the r.m.f.).
* Result: By changing only the polarity of the magnets on the rotor, the direction of rotation can be reversed without changing the coil wiring or current direction.
* Thermal behavior: "The coils don't get hot." (13_2018897183253348352_video.mdx)
