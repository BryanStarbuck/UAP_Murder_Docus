## Section: 1964864724551745953.yaml — Energy Input/Output in Homopolar Motor with Proposed Newton's 3rd Law Anomaly

### Energy Balance Implication
If the homopolar motor generates angular momentum L_disc in the disc with no equal counter-angular-momentum in the source magnet, and if this L_disc is subsequently converted to useful work (upward force F_z), then the energy accounting becomes:

    Energy input: W_battery = I²R·t + work done against any back-EMF
    Energy output: Kinetic energy of disc (L²/2I_disc) + work done by F_z (lifting craft against gravity)

Standard physics requires W_battery ≥ KE_disc + W_lift (energy conservation). Drew's claim does NOT violate energy conservation — he is claiming a violation of momentum conservation (Newton's 3rd Law for angular momentum), not energy conservation.

However, the absence of a counter-force (no reaction on the source magnet) means no work is done against that reaction. In a standard rocket or propeller, energy is consumed both in accelerating the propellant/air downward AND in overcoming the reaction. In Drew's proposed mechanism, if there is no reaction to overcome, the energy efficiency of the lift could in principle approach:

    η = W_lift / W_input → higher than for reaction-based propulsion

This is not over-unity (still W_input > W_lift), but it represents anomalously high efficiency from the perspective of reaction-propulsion standards.

### No Zero-Point Energy Claim
Drew does not invoke zero-point energy (ZPE) or vacuum fluctuations as the energy source in these tweets. The energy source is explicitly conventional: electrical energy from a battery or 3-phase power supply.

## Section: 1919152565394325698.yaml — Element 115 as Alleged Energy Source (Lazar Model, Rejected by Drew)

### The Lazar Element 115 Energy Claim
Bob Lazar claimed that element 115 (moscovium, Mc, Z=115) generates gravity waves by an undisclosed mechanism involving its strong nuclear interaction when bombulated with protons, and that these gravity waves are then amplified and directed to produce propulsion. The energy source in Lazar's model is the nuclear binding energy of element 115 — a super-stable superheavy element not found naturally in our solar system.

The physics claim: superheavy elements near the "island of stability" (predicted centered near Z=114, N=184) may have significantly extended half-lives compared to other transactinides. The island of stability arises from shell-model nuclear structure:
    - Magic proton numbers: 2, 8, 20, 28, 50, 82, (114?)
    - Magic neutron numbers: 2, 8, 20, 28, 50, 82, 126, (184?)

Moscovium (Z=115, A≈291 for the most stable known isotope) has t½ ~ 220ms for ¹¹⁵Mc-291. If a neutron-rich isotope of Z=115 near N=184 could be produced or found, it might have t½ on geological or stellar timescales — hence "super-stable."

Lazar's gravity wave amplifier claim has no basis in known nuclear or gravitational physics. The strong nuclear force does not couple to gravitational waves in the Standard Model.

### Drew's Assessment
Drew rejects the element 115 narrative as likely disinformation, designed to mislead civilian researchers and prevent independent replication. His own energy source is prosaic: 3-phase electrical power.
