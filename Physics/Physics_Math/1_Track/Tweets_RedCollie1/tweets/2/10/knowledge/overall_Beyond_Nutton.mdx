**Claim 1 — Spinning top under lateral air jet:**
Standard prediction (Newtonian): lateral force → lateral momentum transfer, balanced by equal and opposite reaction.
Observed behavior: lateral force → vertical (upward) motion of top.
Reaction force (downward on air source): absent, not observed.

Chain of reasoning: The angular momentum L of the spinning top couples with the applied torque τ = r × F to produce precession in a direction perpendicular to both L and τ. The upward motion is not a momentum transfer from the air to the top in the direction of the applied force. Instead, it is a gyroscopic response that converts the lateral impulse into vertical angular momentum change. The reaction is "absorbed" into the change in the direction of L rather than transmitted as a reactive force back to the air source.

Newtonian mechanics predicts this behavior correctly if the full angular momentum equation dL/dt = τ is used. The apparent violation of Newton's Third Law is that no equal and opposite downward force appears on the air source. This is because the "reaction" is internal to the gyroscope — the angular momentum of the spin has changed direction, and this change is the reaction, not a force on the external agent. The external agent (air jet) sees no reaction force because the gyroscope's angular momentum acted as the "medium" of force absorption.

**Claim 2 — Solid homopolar motor with brushes:**
Standard prediction (Newtonian for rotating machines): torque on rotor produces equal and opposite torque on stator/housing.
Observed behavior: the brushed homopolar motor does not produce a reaction torque on the external circuit in the conventional sense.

But the Lorentz force does no work (F · v = 0), so no energy is transferred from the mechanical spin back to the magnetic field source. Without energy transfer, there is no torque reaction on the external magnet.
