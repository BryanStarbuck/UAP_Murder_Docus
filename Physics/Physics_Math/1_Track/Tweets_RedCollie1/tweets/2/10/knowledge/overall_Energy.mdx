### Tesla Bifilar Wire Coil — Energy Storage and Field Geometry

Drew references a crop picture from June 10 showing a "bifilar wire coil, originally patented by Nikola Tesla."

A bifilar coil consists of two wires wound in parallel (side by side), with opposite current directions in adjacent turns. This configuration cancels self-inductance (L ≈ 0) while maintaining large capacitance C between the parallel wires. The energy storage in such a coil is predominantly electrostatic (E = CV²/2) rather than magnetic (E = LI²/2).

Physical implication for UAP systems: A bifilar coil would not resist rapid changes in current (no inductive back-EMF), allowing extremely fast current switching. This could be relevant to pulsed power systems in UAP propulsion, where rapid switching of current direction in the 3-phase drive coils is required.
