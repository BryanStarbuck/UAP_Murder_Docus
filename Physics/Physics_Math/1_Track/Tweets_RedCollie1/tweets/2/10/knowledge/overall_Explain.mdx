### Drew's Central Thesis on UFO Propulsion as Teachable Physics

Dr. Drew's core claim is that UAP/UFO propulsion can be understood as a specific, currently untaught combination of known electromagnetic forces operating beyond the classical Newtonian action-reaction paradigm. The output of the system is net upward thrust (Z-axis translation, i.e., lift) achieved without a conventional reaction mass expelled downward. The bridge between mechanism and output operates as follows:

**Stage 1 — XY-plane spin via Lorentz force (no Newtonian back-reaction):**
A conducting liquid metal (galinstan: Ga-In-Sn eutectic alloy; or historically mercury, as in Indian vimana accounts) is placed in a strong static magnetic field oriented along the Z-axis. A DC current is driven radially or axially through the liquid. The resulting Lorentz force F = q(v × B) is azimuthal — perpendicular to both the radial current density J and the axial field B. This drives a circulating (toroidal, vortex) flow of the liquid metal in the XY-plane. The critical feature: the Lorentz force does no work on individual charge carriers (F · v = 0 since F ⊥ v), and therefore does not induce an equal and opposite reaction force by Newton's Third Law. The spin of the homopolar liquid-metal motor therefore carries inertia but no apparent back-reaction.

**Stage 2 — Transition from XY rotation to Z-axis translation via 3-phase rotating magnetic field:**
Three wire coils spaced 120° apart in the azimuthal plane (3-phase configuration) are energized with currents phase-shifted by 120°. This creates a rotating magnetic field that couples to the spinning liquid-metal vortex. The claim, derived from crop circle diagrams interpreted by Drew as instructional, is that this rotating field converts the circular (XY) spin of the vortex into helical motion — i.e., the vortex begins to travel upward (along the Z-axis) while continuing to spin. Because the initial XY spin was established via a force that generates no Newtonian back-reaction, the transition to Z-axis motion likewise produces upward thrust without a corresponding downward reaction force. The device therefore rises.

**Why this exceeds Newtonian mechanics (the bridge):**
In standard Newtonian rocket propulsion, F_up = -dp_exhaust/dt — upward momentum is balanced by downward momentum of expelled mass. In the Drew model, the propulsive force source is a Lorentz-force-driven rotation that is, by the geometry of the cross product, always perpendicular to the velocity of charge carriers and performs no work in the classical sense. The rotating magnetic field then couples this angular momentum into linear momentum along Z. Since the originating force (Lorentz) is orthogonal to velocity, there is no reactive impulse to be balanced — the system interacts with the electromagnetic field geometry rather than with a reaction mass. The output is lift against gravity.

2. Six outer wire coils providing static DC magnetic field — holds the vortex in place and supplies the B-field for the Lorentz force

**Gyroscopic analogy for the XY-to-Z conversion:**
Eric Laithwaite's 1974 Royal Institution lectures (cited by Drew, tweet 1955255144645530015) demonstrated that a fast-spinning gyroscope subjected to a downward gravitational force responds with a 90° deflection — precession — rather than downward acceleration. Drew draws an explicit analogy: just as the Lorentz force on an electromagnetic charge deflects the particle 90° from its velocity direction, so too does gyroscopic precession deflect the gravitational force 90° from the vertical. In Laithwaite's most dramatic experiment, a spinning gyroscope balanced on a pivot appeared to transfer its center of mass to the pivot point, challenging naive application of torque mechanics. This is not claimed as direct UFO levitation but as background physics illustrating the same principle: fast rotation converts a downward force into a sideways or upward motion.
