### Spinning Top Under Air Jet: Experimental Analog of Lift Without Back-Reaction

**Non-spinning top:**
Air jet applies force F_jet from right → top moves left (momentum conservation, Newton's Third Law)

**Spinning top:**
Air jet applies force F_jet from right → top moves UP, not left
Counter-force (air moving down): absent

Physical mechanism: The angular momentum L of the spinning top couples with the torque τ = r × F_jet to produce a gyroscopic response. The precession direction is perpendicular to both L and τ. For L vertical and F_jet horizontal, the precession is vertical — the top rises.

The absence of a downward reaction on the air jet is the key anomaly from a Newtonian standpoint. In Newtonian mechanics, the upward momentum of the top should be balanced by downward momentum of the air. But the top's spin redistributes the impulse — the angular momentum of the spinning top absorbs and converts the lateral momentum into vertical precession without generating a corresponding downward impulse. This is the physics of "interacting with the field" rather than "reacting against a mass."
