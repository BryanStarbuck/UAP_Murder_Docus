### Lorentz Force as UFO Propulsion Basis — The Core Magnetic Mechanism

**Standard Lorentz force:**
F = q(E + v × B)

For the magnetic component alone (relevant to homopolar/liquid-metal case):
F_mag = q(v × B)

Key property: F_mag · v = q(v × B) · v = 0 (always zero, since a × b is perpendicular to a)

This means the magnetic Lorentz force does zero work on a charged particle. It changes the direction of motion but not the speed. This property is critical to Drew's argument: a force that does no work cannot, by the standard Newtonian framework, generate a back-reaction that transfers momentum back to the source. Hence a motor driven purely by the Lorentz force (homopolar motor) does not obey Newton's Third Law in the conventional sense of action-reaction propulsion.

### Lorentz vs. Lenz Force — Critical Distinction

**Lenz force (standard electromagnetic induction):**
When a conductor moves through a magnetic field, induced currents produce a field opposing the inducing field (Lenz's law). This is the operating principle of conventional electric generators and regenerative braking. The induced field weakens the applied field. Back-reaction is present and strong.

**Lorentz spin force (homopolar motor):**
When current is driven through a conductor in a perpendicular magnetic field, the conductor experiences azimuthal acceleration (spin). The spin-induced motion creates an additional magnetic field that reinforces, rather than opposes, the applied field.

From Tesla's 1901 essay on current paths within a homopolar motor (cited by Drew, page_020.json line 4522): "Lorentz spin creates an induced magnetic field which strengthens the applied magnetic field." Drew identifies this as the fundamental distinction: the Lorentz spin is a self-reinforcing process, opposite in character to the self-weakening Lenz process in conventional generators. This self-reinforcement may be relevant to why the homopolar vortex can maintain spin without the strong back-reaction seen in Lenz-based devices.

### Lorentz Spin and Self-Reinforcing Magnetic Field (Tesla 1901 Analysis)

From Drew's analysis of Tesla's 1901 essay on homopolar motor current paths:

In a standard Lenz-law induction generator:
B_induced opposes B_applied → net field = B_applied − B_induced (weakening)

In a Lorentz-force homopolar motor:

This is because in a homopolar motor, the current flows radially (or axially) while B is axial (or radial), so the resulting Lorentz spin current circulates azimuthally.
