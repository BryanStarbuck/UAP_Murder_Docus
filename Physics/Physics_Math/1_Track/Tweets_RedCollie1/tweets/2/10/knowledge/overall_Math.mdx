### Lorentz Force — Vector Form and Key Scalar Properties

**Full Lorentz force:**
F = q(E + v × B)

**Magnetic component only (relevant to homopolar motor):**
F_mag = q(v × B)

**Work done by magnetic force:**
W = ∫ F_mag · dr = ∫ q(v × B) · v dt = ∫ q[(v × B) · v] dt = 0

Since (v × B) is always perpendicular to v, the dot product (v × B) · v = 0 identically.
Corollary: The magnetic Lorentz force does not change the kinetic energy of a charged particle; it only changes the direction of motion.

**For a current-carrying conductor segment of length dL:**
dF = I(dL × B)

where I is the current and dL is the directed length element. For a radial current segment in a perpendicular axial field B = Bẑ, with dL = dr r̂:
dF = I(dr r̂ × B ẑ) = IB dr (r̂ × ẑ) = IB dr (-φ̂)

This is purely azimuthal — no axial or radial component. The Lorentz force on a radial current in an axial field is purely rotational.

**Torque on a current-carrying disc (Corbino disc geometry):**
For a disc of inner radius r₁ and outer radius r₂, with total current I flowing radially, in axial field B:
τ = ∫[r₁ to r₂] r · dF_azimuthal = ∫[r₁ to r₂] r · I · B · dr / (2πr) = IB(r₂ - r₁)/(2π)
(for uniform current density J = I/(2πr·d) where d is disc thickness)

More precisely, for a current sheet:
τ = IB(r₂² - r₁²)/(4π)

This torque drives the disc to spin, and the key property is that the reaction torque on the external circuit is zero (not transmitted through a rigid shaft, so the disc spins "freely").

### Gyroscopic Precession — Angular Momentum and Force Relations

**Precession equation:**
dL/dt = τ = r_g × F_grav

where:
- L = Iω (angular momentum of spinning gyroscope)
- τ = torque from gravity
- r_g = vector from pivot to center of mass of gyroscope
- F_grav = -Mg ẑ

**Precession angular velocity:**
Ω_prec = τ / (L sin θ) = Mg·r_g / (I·ω·sin θ)

where θ is the angle between L and the vertical. For horizontal spin axis (θ = π/2):
Ω_prec = Mg·r_g / (I·ω)

Note the inverse relationship: faster spin (larger ω) → slower precession. This is the key to why heavy gyroscopes spin slowly enough to be demonstrable with Laithwaite-scale equipment.

**Energy exchange between precession and potential energy (video 13_1955254508520607744):**
Laithwaite demonstrates that accelerating the precession (by pushing on the inner ring) causes a hanging weight to rise. The energy balance:
ΔPE = Mgh = ΔKE_prec = ΔKE_external_push

The system acts as a gyroscopic lever: external rotational kinetic energy (push) converts to gravitational potential energy (height gained by weight) via the gyroscope's angular momentum coupling.
