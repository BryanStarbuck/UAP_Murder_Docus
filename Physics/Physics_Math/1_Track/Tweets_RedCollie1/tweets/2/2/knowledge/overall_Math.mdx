The image description notes: "The trefoil knot topology is mathematically related to torus knot theory (T(2,3) torus knot)."

The trefoil knot T(2,3) is the simplest non-trivial knot. Its parametric equations on a torus with major radius R and minor radius r:

x(t) = (R + r cos(3t)) cos(2t)
y(t) = (R + r cos(3t)) sin(2t)
z(t) = r sin(3t)

- Alexander polynomial: Δ(t) = t − 1 + t⁻¹
- Jones polynomial: V(t) = −t⁻⁴ + t⁻³ + t⁻¹
- Crossing number: 3
- Knot group: π₁(S³ \ T(2,3)) = ⟨a, b | a² = b³⟩ (braid group B₃ quotient)
