### The UFO Physics Problem: Newton's Third Law and Directed Spin Force

The core physics problem stated explicitly: the 3-phase spin of a wobbling magnet against the surrounding vacuum produces a net upward force (Z-axis), but by Newton's Third Law (N3L), an equal and opposite downward force is exerted on the surrounding coil frame. The device as a whole has zero net force and cannot accelerate.

Standard Newton's Third Law formulation:
**F**_on_coils = −**F**_on_magnet

For the device to achieve net Z-axis acceleration, this equality must be broken in the Z-direction. The proposed mechanism is that the counter-spin of an outer ring redirects the reaction force into the XY-plane (torque) rather than the Z-axis, effectively breaking the Z-component of N3L while preserving it in the XY-plane.

Whether this is physically achievable within standard electrodynamics is the central contested question. Standard classical electrodynamics (Maxwell's equations + Lorentz force) conserves momentum rigorously: the electromagnetic field itself carries momentum, and any force on a current distribution is accompanied by an equal force on the source of the field, accounting for field momentum. No configuration of currents and magnets can produce a net force on a closed system without ejecting mass or radiation.

The claim therefore implicitly invokes one of:
(a) Coupling to the quantum vacuum (virtual particle pressure imbalance)
(b) Violation of standard conservation laws via exotic spacetime effects
(c) A misidentification of the reaction medium (the vacuum is the reaction medium, not the coil frame)

Option (c) is the most physically interesting: if the vacuum is the reaction medium, then the device exerts a force on the vacuum (creating a vacuum momentum flux) while the vacuum exerts a force on the device. The center of mass of the system (device + vacuum field) is preserved, but the device alone accelerates while the vacuum field carries the opposing momentum. This is exactly how a rocket works (propellant is the reaction medium), and it would imply that the vacuum has sufficient momentum-carrying capacity to serve as a propellant substitute.

### Space Vortex Theory

The claim "spin creates a space vortex" is a compressed reference to a class of theories proposing that spacetime has fluid-dynamic properties (analogous to a superfluid or quantum vacuum condensate), and that a rapidly spinning body creates a persistent vortex structure in this medium.

In standard general relativity, a rotating massive body does create a spacetime effect — frame dragging, described by the Lense-Thirring metric:

ds² = −(1 − 2GM/rc²)c²dt² + (1 − 2GM/rc²)⁻¹ dr² + r² dθ² + r² sin²θ dφ² − (4GJ sin²θ)/(rc) c dt dφ

where J is the angular momentum of the rotating body. The off-diagonal term −(4GJ sin²θ)/(rc) c dt dφ represents frame dragging: the rotation of spacetime around a spinning body.

For astrophysical objects (Kerr black holes, neutron stars), this effect is measurable (Gravity Probe B confirmed it for Earth). For small laboratory objects, the effect is unmeasurably small under GR.

The "space vortex" claim extends this to the electromagnetic domain: a rapidly spinning magnet creates a measurable vortex in the electromagnetic vacuum (not just in the gravitational sector), large enough to produce macroscopic propulsive forces. This would require a quantum vacuum coupling coefficient many orders of magnitude larger than predicted by standard electroweak theory.
