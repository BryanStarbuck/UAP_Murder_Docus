### Lorentz Force Without Requiring Precisely-Opposite Counter-Spin

**Tweet text:** "The Lorentz force allows 'spin' without requiring precisely-opposite 'counter spin'."

**Physical significance:** This resolves a constraint in the levitation model. The Lorentz force:
```
F = q (v × B)
```
produces a force perpendicular to both the velocity and the magnetic field. This perpendicular force can drive spin without requiring the standard Newton's 3rd Law counter-reaction in the opposite direction.

Specifically, in the homopolar/Corbino geometry, the Lorentz force drives azimuthal spin of the conductor, but the reaction force is distributed into the magnetic field (as field momentum) and the current source — not as a mechanically observable equal-and-opposite rotation. This is the physical basis for the claimed Newton's 3rd Law asymmetry: in electromagnetic systems, the action-reaction is mediated through the field, and the mechanical manifestation may appear asymmetric.
