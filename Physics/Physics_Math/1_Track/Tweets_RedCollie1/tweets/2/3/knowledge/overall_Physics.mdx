**Observation:** A levitating spinning system exhibits a downward counter-spin. If only conservation of spin angular momentum were operative, there would be no downward counter-spin at equilibrium (conservation would simply track total L without requiring reaction torques).

**Physical reasoning chain:**
1. Forward spin momentum is continuously added to the system (the drive mechanism injects angular momentum dL/dt > 0)
2. Newton's 3rd Law requires an equal and opposite reaction
3. The reaction manifests as downward counter-spin
4. Therefore the upward levitation force F_z must be mechanically or electromagnetically coupled to the forward spin rate dL/dt

**Implication for the physics:** This is NOT classical gyroscopic precession. Classical precession conserves total L; it doesn't generate a net upward force. The levitation requires a non-conservative interaction between the spinning system and the medium (vacuum, electromagnetic field, or spacetime metric) such that angular momentum addition produces vertical force.
