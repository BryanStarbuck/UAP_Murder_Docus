### Homopolar Motor Newton's Third Law Violation — Forward Force Without Backward Reaction

If the system is quasi-static (negligible EM field momentum), then:

This is the standard result — the conductor's forward force is exactly canceled by a backward force on the magnet.

**If the claim is genuine (that there is literally no reaction force on the magnet):**

This would require ∂g_EM/∂t ≠ 0 even in the quasi-static limit — electromagnetic field momentum building up without radiation. This cannot occur in classical Maxwell electrodynamics but might occur in non-linear electrodynamics (Born-Infeld theory, etc.) or in theories with modified vacuum permeability.

**Beyond-Newtonian significance:** If a homopolar motor in the correct configuration produces asymmetric force (forward but not backward), and this can be redirected along z, the result is a reactionless thruster — directly violating Newton's 3rd Law and enabling unlimited free propulsion.
