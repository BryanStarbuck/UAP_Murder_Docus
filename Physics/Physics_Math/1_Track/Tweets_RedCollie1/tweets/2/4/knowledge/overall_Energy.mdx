**Claimed anomaly:** "The sideways-spinning motion of a homopolar motor does not seem to follow Newton's 3rd Law. There is a forward reaction, without any backward counter-reaction."

Input electrical energy: P_in = V · I (Watts)

Heat dissipation: P_heat = I² · R_resistance (Watts)

If Newton's 3rd Law is violated in the force sense but not in the energy sense, this would be a subtle distinction: force asymmetry could exist without energy non-conservation if the "missing" reaction force is absorbed by the electromagnetic field (field momentum storage). In this case:

**Implications for energy extraction:** If the forward Lorentz force can be redirected upward and the reaction is absorbed by the EM field (which radiates away), then the device could produce continuous upward force at the cost of continuous electrical power input — a thruster without reaction mass. This is analogous to a photon drive (which also "cancels" Newton's 3rd Law by emitting photons that carry the reaction momentum away) but operating via Lorentz force rather than photon emission.

### Counter-Rotating Vortex Energy Storage and Release

**Analogy:** "Twisting a bedsheet" — twisting both ends in opposite directions stores elastic energy in the twist (torsion). When released, the stored torsional energy can be released as kinetic energy.

**Magnetic field energy in the counter-spinning vortex:**

The magnetic field energy density is:

u_B = B² / (2μ_0)

In the shear zone between counter-rotating rings, the magnetic field gradients are large. The field energy stored in the shear zone:

U_shear = ∫_shear (B² / 2μ_0) dV

This exceeds the energy in either ring alone (because of the strong field gradients from counter-rotation). The extra energy ("torsion energy" in Drew's language) is available to do work on the central magnet — driving it up or down.

**Magnetic pressure difference:**

Above vs. below the shear zone:

ΔP = (B_above² - B_below²) / (2μ_0)

If B_above > B_below (stronger field above the central magnet), the magnetic pressure pushes the central magnet downward (from high to low field region, diamagnetic effect).

If B_below > B_above (stronger field below), the central magnet is pushed upward.

Drew observes both up and down behavior depending on configuration — consistent with this magnetic pressure asymmetry.

**Energy required to maintain counter-rotation:**

Both rings are driven against mutual magnetic braking forces. The energy input to maintain the counter-rotation at constant ω:

P_input = τ_1 · ω_1 + τ_2 · ω_2

where τ_1, τ_2 are the braking torques from the magnetic interaction between the counter-rotating rings. This energy ultimately goes into:
1. Heat (Ohmic losses in any conductors)
2. The upward/downward force on the central magnet (mechanical work)
3. Radiation (if operating at resonant frequencies)

The efficiency of conversion from P_input to mechanical work (lift) is the key engineering figure of merit.
