### Helical Magnetic Fields from Spinning Permanent Magnets

**Configuration:** A permanent N-S bar/disc magnet spinning about its own N-S axis (spin axis collinear with magnetic dipole axis) at angular velocity ω.

**Classical dipole field (short range):** The standard magnetic dipole field from a stationary magnet of moment m = m_z ẑ:

B_r = (μ_0 / 4π) · (2m cos θ / r³)
B_θ = (μ_0 / 4π) · (m sin θ / r³)

This produces the familiar r^{-3} spatial falloff for B and r^{-4} falloff for force between two dipoles. Drew confirms this empirically: at short distances, the spinning magnet is attracted by standard "r-squared dependence for dipole magnetism" (the force law between dipole and pole scales as r^{-4} for axial geometry).

**Helical field regime (long range):** When the N-S magnet spins about its own axis, the rotation of the magnetic field vector sweeps out a helical pattern in space, analogous to the electromagnetic field of a rotating dipole. The rotating dipole produces time-varying B fields with a characteristic wavelength λ = 2π c / ω in the radiation zone. However, the near-field helical field structure is distinct from radiation: it corresponds to a magnetic field that winds helically around the spin axis, with pitch determined by ω and the rotation radius.

In cylindrical coordinates (ρ, φ, z) with z as the spin axis:

B_ρ(ρ, φ, z, t) = f(ρ, z) · cos(φ - ωt)
B_φ(ρ, φ, z, t) = g(ρ, z) · sin(φ - ωt)
B_z(ρ, φ, z, t) = h(ρ, z)

The azimuthal dependence φ - ωt represents the helical sweep of the dipole field. The spatial functions f, g, h describe the near-field envelope, which decays more slowly than r^{-3} along the helical direction, potentially as r^{-1} to r^{-2} depending on the mode structure.

**Astrophysical analogy:** Drew explicitly invokes helical magnetic fields in astrophysical jets (e.g., AGN jets, pulsar jets). In those contexts, the magnetic field winding in a jet is described by the pitch angle: tan(ψ) = B_φ / B_z, where B_φ is the toroidal and B_z the poloidal component. The same structure applies here at laboratory scale.

**Experimental result:** At distances where the r^{-4} dipole force has decayed to negligible values, the spinning magnet continues to exhibit levitation via this helical field mechanism. The levitation is stable and weightless — the magnet holds its position without mechanical contact. This demonstrates a long-range magnetic interaction with effective range beyond the classical dipole falloff.

**Material note (from video):** The experiment uses a 30-gram permanent magnet. The "drum wheel" is moved progressively further from the magnet to eliminate the short-range dipole attraction, revealing the residual long-range levitating force.

### Magnetohydrodynamic Azimuthal Current in Conducting Liquid (Homopolar Motor Analogy)

**Experiment:** DC current (50V applied voltage) passing through a conducting liquid (described as "dion" — likely a conducting electrolyte or mercury substitute) placed above an 80 mm neodymium (Neo) permanent magnet.

**Observation:** With N pole up, the liquid swirls counterclockwise (viewed from above). With S pole up (magnet flipped), the liquid swirls clockwise. The rotation direction reverses exactly with the magnet polarity reversal.

**Physical mechanism — Lorentz force on current-carrying fluid:**

The current density J flows radially (in/out) through the liquid. The magnetic field B is axial (along z, perpendicular to the disc). The Lorentz force per unit volume is:

f = J × B

For radial J = J_r r̂ and axial B = B_z ẑ:

f = J_r r̂ × B_z ẑ = J_r B_z (r̂ × ẑ) = -J_r B_z φ̂

This azimuthal body force is exactly the mechanism of a homopolar (Faraday disc) motor. The azimuthal force on the conducting fluid drives rotation in the φ direction.

**Reversal with polarity:** When B_z → -B_z (magnet flipped), f_φ → -f_φ, reversing the rotation direction, consistent with the observation.

**Context — Newton's 3rd Law anomaly:** The parent tweet (1964875578043203907, referenced in this tweet) states: "we know that the sideways-spinning motion of a homopolar motor does not seem to follow Newton's 3rd Law. There is a forward reaction, without any backward counter-reaction." This is the central experimental claim: the azimuthal Lorentz force on the conductor produces a forward (azimuthal) motion without an equal-and-opposite reaction torque on the magnet. If the magnet is free to rotate, this would be detectable as differential torque.

**Implication:** If the goal is to "change the forward direction" (as stated in the parent tweet), i.e., redirect the azimuthal Lorentz force into an axial (Z-axis) force, then one could potentially achieve propulsion without reaction — the core of the UFO lift hypothesis.

### DC vs. 3-Phase Spin of Galinstan over a 60 mm Magnet

**DC phase:** DC electricity applied drives galinstan spin anti-clockwise (counterclockwise viewed from above). This is a homopolar motor effect: J_radial × B_axial = F_azimuthal (counterclockwise).

**3-phase phase:** 3-phase AC power (phases U, V, W) applied to wire coils creates a rotating magnetic field (RMF). The RMF drives the permanent disc magnet to spin clockwise — the opposite direction to the galinstan's anti-clockwise DC spin.

**Physical mechanism of RMF-driven rotation:**

A balanced 3-phase system with coils at 120° spatial intervals:

B(t) = B_0[cos(ωt) x̂ + cos(ωt - 2π/3) ŷ + cos(ωt - 4π/3) ẑ'] (simplified 2D version)

The resulting field rotates uniformly at angular frequency ω. An induction principle (like an induction motor) drives the permanent magnet to rotate in the direction of the rotating field. The permanent magnet effectively locks onto the RMF rotation.

**The counter-spin configuration achieved:**

Galinstan: anti-clockwise (CCW) rotation via DC
Disc magnet: clockwise (CW) rotation via 3-phase RMF

This is the target counter-spin configuration identified from the Fuhr UFO observation and the crop circle series. The experimental setup, as of October 2026 (tweet date), achieves this configuration in the lab, though further refinement is needed (Drew notes "probably need to get that magnet to spin anti-clockwise at the start along with galinstan" before introducing the 3-phase counter-rotation).
