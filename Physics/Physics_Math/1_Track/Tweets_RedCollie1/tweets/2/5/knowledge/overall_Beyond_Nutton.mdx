**Claim 1: Homopolar Motor Violates Newton's 3rd Law**

Stated directly: "we know that the sideways-spinning motion of a homopolar motor does not
seem to follow Newton's 3 Law. There is a forward reaction, without any backward
counter-reaction."

Newton's 3rd Law: For every action force F_AB (object A exerts on object B), there is an
equal and opposite reaction force F_BA (object B exerts on object A):
F_AB = −F_BA

Applied to the homopolar motor: the azimuthal (tangential) force on the disc is
F_disc_φ = I · dr · B_z. Newton's 3rd Law requires a back-reaction: the current-carrying
circuit (the wire, the magnet) must experience −F_disc_φ. In a standard homopolar motor,
this back-torque IS observed when the magnet is free to rotate — the disc and magnet
counter-rotate. Drew's claim is that the back-reaction is absent or reduced in his
configuration.

Standard electromagnetic analysis: the Lorentz force on the disc J_r × B_z → azimuthal
force. The source of B_z (the permanent magnet) experiences a reaction torque by Lenz's
law (the current in the disc creates a magnetic field that exerts a torque on the magnet).
This is the standard back-reaction. If the magnet is mechanically fixed (held), the
reaction appears as stress in the bearing/support, not as magnet rotation.

If Drew's experiment has the magnet mechanically fixed, the "no backward counter-reaction"
may simply mean the magnet doesn't visibly rotate (because it's held), not that Newton's
3rd Law is violated. The alternative interpretation — a genuine violation — would require
careful closed-system momentum measurements showing net angular momentum change in a
closed system.

Status: Experimental claim (alleged violation). Not confirmed by independent measurement.
Would be extraordinary if confirmed.

**Claim 2: Non-Newtonian Forces from AC Coil + Spinning Disc**

From the video transcription (13_2041414520774836224): "Like I've seen in other systems for
magnetic AC coil pushing up on a spinning disc, you get a lot of non-Newtonian forces which

Drew distinguishes between:

    distribution and field — these are "Newtonian" in Drew's terminology (even though Lorentz

(b) Non-Newtonian forces: observed forces exceeding or differing in direction from what
    standard theory predicts

The class of systems: AC coil (producing alternating/rotating magnetic field) pushing on a
spinning disc (a rotor with angular momentum). The interaction involves:
- Induction of eddy currents in the disc by the AC coil's changing flux
- Lorentz force on those eddy currents
- Combined force on the spinning disc

In standard electromagnetic theory, the AC coil can levitate a spinning disc (as in
induction-based magnetic levitation) but the forces are fully predicted by Faraday's law
and Lorentz force. The prediction: F_z = -(Φ_induced²·σ_eff·ω_disc)/(some geometry factor),
i.e., a calculable repulsive force.

Drew's claim: the observed force exceeds the prediction. This is an experimental claim
requiring quantitative measurement (measured force vs. predicted force). No quantitative
data is given in the tweets.

Beyond-Newton context: if confirmed, anomalous forces from a spinning disc in an AC magnetic
field would challenge Maxwell's equations as applied to this system. Possible conventional
explanations not yet ruled out:
- Non-linear B-H response (ferromagnetic saturation creates harmonics)
- Electromechanical resonance (disc mechanical resonance coinciding with AC frequency)
- Temperature-dependent conductivity changing during spin-up
- Unaccounted geometric factors in the force prediction

Drew's specific claim: "Just like we perceive electrons as either 'particles' or 'waves'.

**Claim 4: Counter-Rotating Magnetic System Produces Thrust Without Identified Reaction Mass**

"Still it flies! But what is the large, central magnetic flywheel pushing against?"

Newton's 2nd Law for the center of mass of a system: F_ext = d(p_total)/dt. For a closed
system (no external forces), the center of mass cannot accelerate. A craft that produces
thrust from purely internal interactions (counter-rotating magnetic discs) would violate
Newton's 2nd/3rd laws if no external reaction mass is identified.

Possible reaction media (all speculative):
1. Earth's magnetic field: if the craft's spinning magnetic field exerts a force on
   Earth's geomagnetic field, then by Newton's 3rd Law, Earth's field exerts an equal
   and opposite force on the craft. Earth's field is ~25-65 μT at surface — very weak
   but real. The craft's interaction would need to be engineered to couple strongly to this
   weak field.

2. Quantum vacuum: spinning magnets couple to vacuum electromagnetic fluctuations (ZPF).
   If the interaction is asymmetric (more momentum transferred from vacuum to craft than
   back), the craft accelerates at the expense of ZPF field momentum. The QED vacuum has
   momentum density g = (1/c²)(E × H), and changes in this field momentum would
   constitute a force.

3. Higher-dimensional medium: if space has additional dimensions, the reaction force is
   carried through those dimensions and does not appear in our 3D space — making the
   drive appear reactionless from a 3D perspective while being fully Newton-compliant in
   the higher-dimensional space.

The operational claim: "Still it flies!" — the New Jersey drone (December 2024, described
as having this counter-rotating coil architecture) was observed to fly. This is the
empirical data point. The theory for why it flies (what it pushes against) is what Drew
is attempting to determine.

**Claim 5: Gyroscope at r = 0 Decouples from Gravitational Field**

"If we can make a model where r begins large, then decreases to zero with increased angular
velocity ω, then we might see UFO-like anti-gravity effects."

In Newtonian mechanics: the gravitational force F = mg is independent of rotation. A
gyroscope spinning at any ω still has weight mg. The gyroscope only affects the rotational
dynamics (precession) not the translational gravitational force. So a Newtonian gyroscope
cannot produce anti-gravity.

Drew's beyond-Newtonian claim: by contracting the geometry (r → 0) with increasing spin,
the device achieves "anti-gravity effects" — the effective interaction with gravity is
reduced or eliminated. In strict Newtonian mechanics, this is impossible (rotation does
not affect gravitational mass or the gravitational force). The claim therefore requires
either:
(a) A general relativistic effect (frame-dragging / Lense-Thirring effect): a spinning
    mass creates a gravitomagnetic field that can, in principle, partially shield
    gravitational effects. The Lense-Thirring precession: Ω_LT = (G/c²r³)(3(L·r̂)r̂ − L)
    where L is the angular momentum of the central body. For lab-scale spins, this is
    negligibly small (~10⁻²⁸ rad/s for a 1 kg·m² gyroscope at 1000 rpm near Earth's
    surface).
(b) A non-standard coupling between spin (angular momentum) and translational motion
    (linear momentum) — sometimes called "spin-spin coupling" or "torsion" in
    Einstein-Cartan gravity
(c) A coupling of the magnetic moment (precessing in the vacuum field) to the vacuum
    field's momentum, as in the quantum vacuum model above

Status: Highly speculative. No confirmed experimental evidence that gyroscopic effects
reduce gravitational weight. The Podkletnov spinning superconductor experiments (1990s)
claimed ~0.5-2% gravity shielding but were not reproducibly confirmed.
