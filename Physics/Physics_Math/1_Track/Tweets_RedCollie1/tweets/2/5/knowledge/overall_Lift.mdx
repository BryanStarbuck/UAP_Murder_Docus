**Gyroscopic Anti-Gravity via r → 0**

"If we can make a model where r begins large, then decreases to zero with increased angular
velocity ω, then we might see UFO-like anti-gravity effects."

The gravitational coupling of a gyroscope to gravity scales as:
Gravitational torque τ_g = mgr (where r is horizontal offset of CM from support)

For a sphere (r = 0 by symmetry): τ_g = 0
→ No precession, no gravitational coupling

This is not "defeating gravity" in the sense of canceling the gravitational force F = mg,
but rather decoupling the rotational dynamics from gravity. The object still has weight mg
but does not precess or tip — it maintains its orientation. In the UAP context, this would
mean the craft doesn't tumble or tip despite acceleration, which is one observed UAP
characteristic (UAPs maintain stable orientation during extreme maneuvers).

For the full anti-gravity interpretation (actual reduction of effective weight), the
gyroscopic model alone is insufficient. Drew acknowledges this: "We might see UFO-like
anti-gravity effects" — conditional, not definitive.
