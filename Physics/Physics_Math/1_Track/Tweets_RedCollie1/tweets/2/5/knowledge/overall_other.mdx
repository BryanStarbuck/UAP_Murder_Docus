**DNA Structure in ET Crop Picture — Two DNA Helical Forms**

The 2001 Chilbolton crop circle (next to a large radio telescope in Chilbolton, UK):
"Slide 1 shows two different types of DNA. One with 10 nucleotides per turn, and the other
with 6 nucleotides per turn."

Standard B-form DNA: 10 base pairs per turn (approximately 10.4 for B-DNA in physiological
conditions)
Non-standard form (A-form or 2',5' linked): A-form DNA has ~11 bp per turn; single-stranded
DNA can form structures with varied helical pitch

Drew's biochemical interpretation: "Those are the two major forms of DNA by chemical
evolution as 3',5' or 2',5'. Work by Leslie Orgel on the origin of life."

Leslie Orgel (Salk Institute): worked on the origin of life and RNA world hypothesis.
2',5' RNA linkages vs. 3',5' linkages: in prebiotic chemistry, both linkages can form
abiotically. 2',5'-linked RNA is less stable and does not form helical structures efficiently,
while 3',5' links produce the canonical double helix. The "6 nucleotides per turn" may
refer to 2',5'-linked DNA with an altered helical geometry.

Drew: "Frank Drake tried to dismiss these images, because ... Drake did not know any
molecular biology!" — Frank Drake (SETI Institute, Drake Equation) apparently claimed the
crop picture was a human fake. Drew argues Drake's dismissal was based on ignorance of
