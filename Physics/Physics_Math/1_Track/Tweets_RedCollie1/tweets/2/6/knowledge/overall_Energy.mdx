## Section: 1958799775534653808.yaml — Gravitational Potential Energy vs. Scale Weight Paradox

**Anomalous energy observation:**
When a disc-magnet is driven upward by a 3-phase rotating magnetic field at ~10 Hz:
- The magnet gains gravitational potential energy: ΔE_grav = M_magnet · g · Δh > 0
- Work is done against gravity
- Yet the scale reading (total system weight) does not change

This is not a violation of energy conservation — the electrical energy input from the 3-phase power supply does work on the magnet, increasing its gravitational PE. The anomaly is in the force distribution: the upward force on the magnet does not appear as a reduced downward force on the scale, because the Newton's 3rd Law partner force (downward on the coil assembly via the pole) exactly cancels the "missing" weight.

**Energy flow in the proposed piton mechanism:**

- Output: (a) ΔE_grav = M_system · g · Δz_cm (if system center of mass rises) + (b) Q_heat (resistive losses) + (c) ΔE_kinetic_spin (rotational KE of spinning magnet)

For the mechanism to produce sustained lift:
  P_electrical > (M_system · g · v_lift) + P_heat

where v_lift is the vertical velocity of the system. This is not zero-point energy — it is standard electromagnetic energy conversion. There is no claim of over-unity here; the claim is that the GEOMETRY of the force coupling allows the electrical energy to do useful work on the system center of mass, which the direct 3-phase experiment alone cannot achieve.

## Section: 1964917258494599207.yaml — Anomalous Energy in Lorentz Force Systems

**Mercury vortex energy anomaly:**
In the mercury bowl experiment (DC current + external B field → mercury rotates), there is no observable counter-rotation reaction. In a standard system, the electrical energy input would be split between: (a) kinetic energy of mercury rotation + (b) kinetic energy of counter-rotating reaction (in the external circuit/magnet). If there is no counter-rotation, all electrical energy goes into mercury rotation — apparently 100% efficient conversion of electrical energy to one-directional rotational kinetic energy.

In a standard motor with Newton's 3rd Law reactions, the reaction torque reduces the net torque available for useful work. The absence of a reaction torque (if Drew's interpretation is correct) means:
  P_input = P_useful (100% conversion, no reaction losses)

This is not zero-point energy but would represent an anomalous efficiency > standard motor efficiency (which is always < 100% due to reaction forces and associated mechanical losses).

**Helical extension energy claim:**

without any downward thrust on the propellant/medium (no reaction mass expenditure). This would be equivalent to a reactionless drive — a direct conversion of electromagnetic energy to gravitational potential energy of the vehicle, without momentum exchange with any exhaust. This would be anomalous from the standpoint of standard physics, but Drew explicitly frames it as an "exception to Newton's 3rd Law" wide enough to "drive a truck through."
