**Force 3 (Under study): Downward Reaction Producing Full Craft Lift**

The physical interpretation is that the spinning magnet's upward force is not simply Newton's 3rd Law pair with a downward force on the coil assembly; instead, the reaction is mediated through the geometry of the electromagnetic field and couples to the vehicle structure in a non-obvious way.

- Intermediate: spinning flywheel generates upward Z-axis force (gyroscopic + magnetic vortex effect)
- Reaction: coils counter-spin (XY-plane angular momentum conservation)

The engineering challenge is that Force 1 alone (spinning magnet rising) does not lift the coil assembly that is driving it. Drew's proposed solution (from tweet 1958799775534653808) is a multi-step "piton climbing" mechanism:
- A large wire coil placed slightly below the 3-phase drive coils is attracted to the spinning magnet by N/S dipole coupling.

- Since the large coil is below the 3-phase drive coils, it pulls the entire drive assembly upward.
- Analogous to a climber hammering pitons above their current position and ascending to each one in sequence.
