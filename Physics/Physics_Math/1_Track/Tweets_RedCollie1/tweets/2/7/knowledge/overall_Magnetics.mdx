> "So that is at least some food for you to think about... I find no difficulty in saying this thing in a black box had a mass, an angular velocity, and no angular momentum, because we know that in a reactive circuit you have volts, amps, and no watts."

**The analogy mapping:**
| Electromagnetism | Mechanics |

| Inductance L (AC) | Gyroscopic property (rotational) |
| Voltage V | Force |
| Current I | Velocity |
| Power P = VI cos φ | Work rate |
