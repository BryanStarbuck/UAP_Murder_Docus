  on the precession. For a top released from rest (not spinning in the full precession mode):
  The nutation frequency: ω_n = L/I_θ where I_θ is the moment of inertia about the tilt axis

  averaged effect can cause the tip to trace an upward spiral

top does not rise. The replacement mechanism must provide a torque that plays the role of τ_g,

has 2ⁿ vertices, n×2^(n-1) edges, and (n choose k)×2^(n-k) k-dimensional faces.
