### Rotational Kinetic Energy as Anomalous Lift Energy Source

In gyroscopic rise (tippe-top inversion), the mechanism involves conversion of
rotational kinetic energy to gravitational potential energy:

KE_rot = ½ I₃ ω₃²

During rise: ΔPE_grav = Mg·Δz_cm > 0
Energy conservation: ΔPE_grav = -ΔKE_rot + W_friction

This is classically energy-conserving. The anomalous claim is not that energy
conservation is violated but that MOMENTUM conservation is violated — that the
upward momentum of the rising gyroscope has no equal and opposite downward
reaction momentum.

However, if true propellantless propulsion (momentum without reaction) exists:
this would violate conservation of linear momentum, requiring a new source of
momentum — either from:
1. The gravitational field (momentum stored in the field)
2. The zero-point vacuum fluctuations (ZPF momentum exchange)
3. A gravitomagnetic field carrying away the reaction momentum

**Zero-point field energy density**:
ρ_ZPF = ℏ/2 · ∫₀^{ω_cutoff} (ω³/π²c³) dω

For cutoff at Planck frequency (ω_P = √(c⁵/Gℏ) ≈ 1.9×10⁴³ rad/s):
ρ_ZPF ≈ c⁷/(16π²ℏG²) ≈ 10¹¹³ J/m³

If the spinning gyroscope couples to ZPF, even an infinitesimal coupling constant
α_zp could supply the anomalous momentum at the expense of ZPF energy density.
This is the theoretical underpinning of Haisch-Rueda-Puthoff stochastic
electrodynamics (SED) model of inertia.

actually be drawing from a topologically connected extra-dimensional energy reservoir.

- A Möbius strip has ONE surface — not two separate surfaces
