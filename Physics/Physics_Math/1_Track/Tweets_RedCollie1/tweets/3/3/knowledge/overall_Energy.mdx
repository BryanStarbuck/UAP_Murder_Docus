The claim is that a spinning magnetic dipole can couple to these vacuum modes and extract
reaction force from the vacuum. This would be a form of vacuum momentum exchange:

and by Newton's 3rd law, the source (spinning magnet) experiences a reaction.

The claim is that this radiation reaction, normally small and symmetric (no net directional thrust
for a symmetric radiator), can produce a net upward (Z-axis) force when the spinning magnetic
dipole is in a gravitational field and/or has the off-axis geometry described.

must come from somewhere. The three-phase AC power input is the energy source for:
(a) maintaining the spin against bearing friction
(b) producing the electromagnetic field interactions

In standard physics, converting electrical input → rotational kinetic energy → gravitational

against gravity). The anomalous claim is that the mechanism is MORE efficient than expected —
that the vacuum interaction somehow augments the energy available for lift beyond what the
electrical input provides. This would violate energy conservation unless the vacuum zero-point

### "Transitional Magnetism" — Energy Regime Between Magnetic and Gravitational

The term "transitional magnetism" describes a proposed physical regime where:
- Angular frequency ω (rotational energy input) is high enough to couple to gravity
- The coupling converts rotational energy into gravitational potential energy (height h)
- The governing relation h = ω · a implies energy conversion: E_rotational → E_gravitational

Standard energy of rotation: E_rot = ½ I ω²
Gravitational potential energy of lifted mass m at height h: E_grav = mgh

For the formula h = ω · a to represent energy balance:
  mgh = mgh = mg(ω · a)

Energy input required: E_input = mg · ω · a
Angular frequency required for hovering: ω_hover = mg/(mg · a) = 1/a (schematically)
But this doesn't balance dimensionally — indicating the formula contains an implicit coupling
constant that carries the energy exchange rate between rotational and gravitational modes.

The "transitional" aspect: below some threshold ω, the magnetic/rotational system behaves
normally (no gravity coupling). Above the threshold, the system enters "transitional magnetism"
where rotational energy directly converts to gravitational potential energy (lift). This would
represent a phase transition in the coupling between spin and gravity.
