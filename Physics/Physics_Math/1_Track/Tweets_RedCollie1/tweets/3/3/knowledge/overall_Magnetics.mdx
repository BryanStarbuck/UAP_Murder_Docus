### Three-Phase Magnetic Gyroscope for UFO Levitation — Device Description

Device architecture (described as basic principle of UFO levitation, claimed to be encoded in
alien-made crop pictures):

- Central permanent magnet positioned slightly off-axis relative to the rotation axis
- Three wire coils arranged azimuthally around the central spinning magnet
- Three-phase AC power applied to the coils drives the magnet spin
- As angular velocity increases with increased power, the device rises (Z-axis levitation)

Key magnetic physics claim: a stationary magnet cannot push against the vacuum of space. A spinning
magnet behaves as a gyroscope and can push/press against the vacuum, producing upward thrust.

The off-axis positioning of the central magnet is specifically noted. This implies that the net
magnetic moment M is not aligned with the rotation axis ẑ, so that as the magnet spins with angular
velocity ω, the magnetic moment vector M(t) rotates in the XY plane (if the tilt is from ẑ),
sweeping out a cone. This rotating magnetic dipole radiates electromagnetic energy and produces a
time-varying magnetic field in the surrounding space, analogous to a rotating electric dipole
radiating photons.

The three-coil (three-phase) geometry produces a rotating magnetic field in the stator (outer coils),
which drives the rotor (central magnet) spin. Three-phase field: if the coils are separated by 120°
and powered with phases 0°, 120°, 240°, the superposition of their fields produces a uniformly
rotating field vector B_total(t) = B_0[cos(ωt) x̂ + sin(ωt) ŷ], which is a standard rotating
magnetic field used in induction motors.

The claim that this combination produces vertical thrust (upward rise) goes beyond standard
electromagnetic induction theory, which would produce only torque (rotation) not translational lift.

conductor, producing rotation.

### "Transitional Magnetism" — Formula for UFO Physics

with the governing formula:

  ω = h / a

where:
- ω = angular frequency (fast rotation)
- h = height (vertical displacement achieved)
- a = area (cross-sectional or disk area of the flying object)

The formula states: height h of a flying disc is proportional to angular frequency ω times area a:

  h ∝ ω · a

or rearranged: ω = h / a

Drew's interpretation: fast angular rotation ω may create an upward force that gives height h.
The height achieved is proportional to both the angular frequency and the cross-sectional area.

This implies the upward force scales as:
  F_z ∝ ω · a

which, for a spinning magnetic disc of area a = πR², would give F_z ∝ ω · πR².

The term "transitional magnetism" is Drew's label for the regime of magnetic behavior where
rotation (angular frequency ω) produces spatial displacement (h) — i.e., the regime where
magnetic + rotational dynamics transition into gravitational/levitative effects.

The proposed experimental implementation requires building a "3-phase magnetic gyroscope, which
precesses like a rising top." This exactly maps to the three-phase spinning magnet device described
in the video transcription: three-phase AC drives rotation, the off-axis magnet geometry causes
precession, and the precession (like a rising top) produces the upward displacement h.
