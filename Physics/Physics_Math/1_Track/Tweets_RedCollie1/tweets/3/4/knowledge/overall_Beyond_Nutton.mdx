## Section: 1936549026281017413.yaml — Challenge to Newton's 3rd Law

**Direct claim**: "Lenz levitation without Newton's 3rd Law???"

**Newton's 3rd Law**: For every action force **F**_AB there is an equal and opposite reaction force **F**_BA = -**F**_AB.

**Application to Lenz levitation**: In standard electromagnetic Lenz levitation, a conducting ring floating above an AC electromagnet experiences:
- Upward eddy-current repulsion force F_up on the ring.
- Equal and opposite downward force F_down = -F_up on the electromagnet/source coil.

Net momentum of system: dP_total/dt = F_up + F_down = 0. Newton's 3rd Law is satisfied; the source coil is pushed down as the ring is pushed up.

**Drew's proposed violation**: In the beaded magnetic flywheel + 3-phase coil experiment, the claim is that the object achieves Z-axis levitation without an equal and opposite Z-axis downward force on the source coils. If confirmed, this would violate Newton's 3rd Law as a dynamical principle, implying that linear momentum is not conserved in the standard sense — consistent with a coupling to spacetime torsion that routes the reaction through the underlying spacetime geometry rather than back to the source device.
