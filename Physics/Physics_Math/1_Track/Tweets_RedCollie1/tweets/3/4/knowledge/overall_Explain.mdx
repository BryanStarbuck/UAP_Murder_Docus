**Mechanistic analogy**:
- Lorentz force: a charged particle (ion or electron) moving with velocity **v** through a magnetic field **B** experiences force **F** = q(**v** × **B**), directed 90 degrees to both **v** and **B**. The particle curves rather than accelerating toward the opposite electrode.
- Gyroscopic precession: angular momentum **L** of the spinning disc interacts with the torque **τ** = **r** × **F**_gravity such that the precession angular velocity **Ω**_p = **τ**/|**L**| = mgr/(Iω), where ω is spin angular velocity, I is moment of inertia, m is mass, g gravitational acceleration, r lever arm. As ω → ∞, **Ω**_p → 0 and the gyroscope appears to levitate.

**Advancement claim**: The 90-degree force re-direction principle, already established in electromagnetic physics (Lorentz force), may operate analogously for gravitational force on rapidly spinning masses, providing a pathway to controlled gravitational force re-direction without requiring an exotic energy source — only sufficiently rapid spin.
