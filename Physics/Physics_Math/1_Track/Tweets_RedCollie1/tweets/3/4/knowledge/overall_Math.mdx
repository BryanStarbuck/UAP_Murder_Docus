## Section: 1940698415371018587.yaml / note_tweet — Precession Angular Velocity

**Gyroscopic precession rate**:

   Ω_p = τ / |L| = mgr / (Iω)

where:
- Ω_p = precession angular velocity [rad/s] (rotation about Z-axis in XY-plane)
- τ = torque magnitude = mgr [N·m]
- m = mass of gyroscope [kg]
- g = gravitational acceleration = 9.81 m/s² [m/s²]
- r = distance from pivot to center of mass [m]
- I = moment of inertia of spinning disc about spin axis [kg·m²]
- ω = spin angular velocity of disc [rad/s]

**Limiting behavior**:
- As ω → ∞ : Ω_p → 0 (gyroscope does not precess; spin axis is fixed)
- As ω → 0 : Ω_p → ∞ (gyroscope falls — not physically meaningful for large τ)
- Intermediate range: finite precession, with apparent weight reduction ∝ Ω_p × Ω_p

**Relationship**: the angular momentum of the spinning disc is:
   L = Iω [kg·m²/s]

The precession rate Ω_p satisfies:
   Ω_p × L = τ
   Ω_p × Iω = mgr

**Vector form**:
   **F** = q(**E** + **v** × **B**)

- **F** = force on charge [N]
- q = electric charge [C]
- **E** = electric field [V/m]
- **v** = particle velocity [m/s]
- **B** = magnetic flux density [T]

**Cross product expansion** (for **v** = v_x x̂ and **B** = B_z ẑ):
   **v** × **B** = (v_x x̂) × (B_z ẑ) = v_x B_z (x̂ × ẑ) = -v_x B_z ŷ

Force is in the -Y direction: 90-degree deflection from both **v** (X) and **B** (Z).

With **B** reversed (B_z → -B_z):
   **v** × **B** = v_x(-B_z)(x̂ × ẑ) = +v_x B_z ŷ

Force reverses to +Y: explains the arc deflection experiment (13_1940693448664784896_video.mdx), where flipping the ring magnet (N→S) reverses the arc deflection direction.

## Section: page_024.json — tweet 1956522809716535664 — Scale-Weight Conservation Paradox

**Paradox formulation**:
Let:
- M = total mass of system (gyroscope + support)
- W_scale = force registered by floor scale = Mg (measured)
- W_hand = force felt by operator holding gyroscope = W_reduced << Mg

Conservation of total downward force requires:
   W_scale = Mg = W_hand + N_support

where N_support is normal force transmitted through the support structure. If W_hand is reduced (operator feels less force), then N_support must increase correspondingly, conserving total downward force on scale.

**Twist**: When the gyroscope is elevated and precessing, its center of mass is above the pivot at radius r from the vertical axis. For a circular orbit, the precession provides centripetal force toward the Z-axis. This centripetal force has a downward component that partially substitutes for normal weight, routing the force through horizontal tension in the precessing motion rather than through vertical compression.

**Mathematical statement**: Total vertical force balance:
   F_up(support) + F_up(vortex, local) = Mg
   F_up(vortex, local) + F_up(support) = Mg

The vortex effect is local — the body feels lighter — but globally (on the scale) the total vertical force is conserved because the precessing orbit contributes a centrifugal force component that is vertically projected back to the support.

**Still unresolved** (per Drew, August 2025): The exact mechanism by which the local weight is redistributed to the support through the precession geometry, maintaining exact scale conservation, remains unexplained within standard physics.
