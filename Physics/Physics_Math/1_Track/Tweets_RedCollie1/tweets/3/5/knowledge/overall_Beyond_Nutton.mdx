**Lorentz Force Propulsion Without Newton's 3rd Law Back-Reaction:**

Standard Newtonian mechanics (and its electromagnetic extension): any force on a system that does work produces an equal and opposite reaction. By Newton's 3rd law, propulsion requires ejecting mass or energy in the reverse direction.

**Logical chain:**

Premise 1: F_B · v = 0 (Lorentz magnetic force does zero work — this is mathematically correct in standard EM)

Premise 2: Newton's 3rd law back-reaction requires energy transfer (implied by Drew)

Premise 3: Therefore, Lorentz-force propulsion has no 3rd law back-reaction (Drew's conclusion)

**Assessment from standard physics:** Premise 2 is incorrect in general. Newton's 3rd law (F_AB = -F_BA) is independent of work or energy transfer; it holds for contact forces, gravitational forces, and electromagnetic forces including the magnetic Lorentz force. A current-carrying wire in a magnetic field experiences a force (F = IL×B) and by Newton's 3rd law, the source of the magnetic field experiences an equal and opposite force. Drew's claim that the zero-work property of the Lorentz force eliminates back-reaction goes beyond standard physics.

However, the claim has a partial justification in the context of electromagnetic propulsion in vacuum: the Lorentz force can redirect charged particles without transferring energy to them, and the reaction is carried by the electromagnetic field itself (momentum in the Poynting vector E×B/c²). In the far field (radiation zone), some momentum can be radiated away without returning to the source, creating a net reaction force in the near field — a known effect in antenna physics (radiation pressure).
