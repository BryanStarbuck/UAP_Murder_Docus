### Ionized Air / Electrohydrodynamic (EHD) Physics — DC High Voltage

**Experimental context:** Linear rail-gun geometry using high-voltage DC electrodes separated by varying gaps, with ionized air as the working medium. Drew comments the effects are "entertaining but not directly relevant to UFO propulsion."

**Regime 1 — Closely Spaced Electrodes (plasma arc / ablation regime):**
- Applied voltage: 6,000 V DC
- Regime: arc discharge, sufficient to melt copper electrodes
- Physical mechanism: ionization of atmospheric N₂ and O₂ producing heavy cations N⁺ and O⁺ that are accelerated toward the negative (black/cathode) electrode
- Result: cathode ablation and destruction; anode (red) remains intact
- Ion species directionality: heavy cations → cathode; electrons → anode
- Electrode damage asymmetry: cathodic destruction arises because electron impact on the anode is energetically less damaging per unit area than massive-ion bombardment on the cathode. The momentum transfer per collision scales as m_ion >> m_e, so kinetic energy deposited per ion far exceeds that per electron at equivalent drift velocity

**Regime 2 — Widely Spaced Electrodes (ionic wind / EHD thrust regime):**
- Applied voltage: 40,000 V DC
- Regime: corona discharge — below arc threshold
- Physical mechanism: partial ionization creates an ion wind driven by Coulomb force on space-charge carriers drifting toward the positive (red/anode) electrode
- Macroscopic effect: strong directional wind from black (cathode) electrode toward red (anode) electrode, detectable as a tactile gust
- Electrode survival: both electrodes remain intact at this gap
- Key observational contrast: at large gap, bulk neutral air entrained by drift motion of ions produces a directed flow aligned with E-field; at small gap, plasma arc localizes energy deposition at the cathode

**Unified governing physics:**
- F_Coulomb = qE on each charge carrier, where E = V/d (approximately, for uniform field) with V = applied potential, d = electrode separation
- At small d: V/d large enough to sustain glow-to-arc transition; N⁺, O⁺ thermal velocity sufficient to reach cathode and deposit large momentum
- At large d: V/d below arc threshold; sustained corona; net EHD body force on neutral fluid = ρ_q · E (space-charge density × field)
- The EHD body force per unit volume: f = ρ_q E − ε₀/2 · E² ∇ε, where ρ_q is free charge density. For air at STP the second term is negligible, leaving f ≈ ρ_q E driving ionic wind
