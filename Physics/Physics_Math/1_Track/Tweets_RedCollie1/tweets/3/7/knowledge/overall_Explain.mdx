### Overview of Drew's UAP Propulsion Model: From Physics to Flight Output

Drew's model progresses through several integrated physical mechanisms, each producing a specific output that bridges to the next stage. The full chain from bench physics to flight-capable propulsion:

---

### Stage 1 — Magnetic Focal Point: Output = Static Levitation (Hover)

**Input physics:** Large ring coil/magnet with axial field B_z(z). Spinning disc magnet with dipole moment m, spin angular momentum L = I·ω.

**Mechanism:** The axial gradient ∂B_z/∂z of the ring magnet produces a position-dependent force F_z = m·∂B/∂z on the spinning disc. At the focal point z_f, F_z = Mg (weight of spinning disc). Gyroscopic rigidity (from L = I·ω) stabilizes the disc against Earnshaw instability — the spin provides the "stiffness" that static systems lack.

**Output:** The spinning disc magnet hovers at height z_f above the ring coil. This is stable, controlled levitation — hover mode. The craft sits in a magnetic equilibrium analogous to the Levitron toy but at potentially large scale.

**Advancement beyond Newtonian:** Classical mechanics predicts the magnet would fall or require mechanical support. The gyroscopic + magnetic combination produces true contactless levitation of the spinning element.

### Stage 2 — 3-Phase Rotating Field: Output = Controlled Spin Rate and Sustained Levitation

**Input physics:** Three-phase AC current in three coils arranged 120° apart (or 9, 12, 15, 18 coils in multiples of 3). The phased currents produce a smoothly rotating magnetic field vector in the XY-plane at the electrical drive frequency ω_drive.

**Mechanism:** The rotating field exerts a continuous torque on the spinning disc magnet, maintaining its angular velocity ω against dissipation. Without this drive, ω would decay (friction, drag, eddy currents), reducing L and destabilizing the levitation. The 3-phase drive is both:
1. A spin-up mechanism (accelerates the disc to operating ω)
2. A spin-maintenance mechanism (keeps ω constant against losses)

**Output:** Sustained, controlled spin rate. Because levitation stability requires ω > ω_critical (Braunbek condition), maintaining ω via 3-phase drive directly maintains the levitation condition. The craft hovers stably as long as the 3-phase drive operates.

**Advancement beyond Newtonian:** Newtonian mechanics predicts spin-down under friction. 3-phase electromagnetic coupling continuously replenishes angular momentum, defeating dissipation. The rotating electromagnetic field is the energy source converting AC electrical power to mechanical angular momentum.

### Stage 3 — Gyroscopic Precession Redirecting Gravity: Output = Gravity Nullification in Horizontal Plane

**Input physics:** Spinning disc magnet (gyroscope) with angular momentum L. Gravitational field g. Magnetic field from ring coil provides axial restoring force.

**Mechanism:** Any gravitational torque (or equivalently, any perturbation that would tilt the spin axis) is converted by gyroscopic precession into horizontal (XY-plane) rotational motion rather than downward fall:

  τ_grav = dL/dt   →   Ω_p = τ_grav / |L|   (horizontal precession)

Gravity does not accelerate the center of mass downward because the gravitational torque is entirely absorbed into precession.

**Output:** Effective gravity cancellation for the spinning element and, by extension, any craft structure rigidly coupled to it. The craft does not fall — it precesses. If precession is controlled (e.g., stopped by counter-rotation), the craft simply hovers.

**Advancement beyond Newtonian:** In Newtonian mechanics, F = ma → a = g for any unsupported mass. The gyroscopic system redirects the effect of gravitational force from translational acceleration (falling) to rotational motion (precession), effectively decoupling translation from gravity within the operating regime.

### Stage 4 — Z-Axis Thrust: Output = Vertical Ascent / Propulsion

**Input physics:** 3-phase driven spinning magnet at focal point z_f. Ring coil field gradient. Newton's Third Law reaction forces.

**Mechanism (Drew's proposal):**
When the spinning magnet rises by dz from z_f, the new focal point z_f' = z_f + δ is above the current position, so the magnet continues to be pushed upward (it is now in the region below the new equilibrium, where F_z_magnetic > Mg). This creates iterated upward displacement — thrust.

Additionally, the 3-phase coils exert clockwise torque on the spinning magnet; by Newton's Third Law, the anti-clockwise reaction torque on the coils (outer shell) drives counter-rotation of the outer ring — this can be converted to translational thrust via asymmetric field geometry or differential magnetic coupling.

**Output:** Net upward force exceeding gravity → vertical ascent. The craft rises. By controlling the current in the ring coil (changing the focal point height) and the 3-phase drive frequency (changing spin rate and thus precession dynamics), the craft can be steered in the Z-axis (altitude) and potentially the XY-plane (by tilting the spin axis via differential phase control of the 3-phase coils).

**Advancement beyond Newtonian:** Classical reaction-mass propulsion requires expelling mass (rocket equation: Δv = v_e · ln(m_i/m_f)). Drew's system produces thrust with no exhaust, no propellant mass expulsion — force is generated by interaction between the internal spinning magnetic system and the external vacuum/field, not by expelling matter. This violates the classical requirement for reaction mass.

### Stage 5 — Liquid Metal MHD: Output = Lorentz Force Vortex as Propulsion Foundation

**Input physics:** Conducting liquid metal (galinstan: Ga-In-Sn) in DC current + permanent magnet field. Lorentz body force f = J × B.

**Mechanism:** Azimuthal Lorentz force drives toroidal vortex rotation. The rotating conducting fluid self-generates an axial magnetic dipole moment (via Ampere's law). This self-generated axial field interacts with the external magnet to produce a net vertical force. The geometry is essentially a liquid-metal homopolar motor (Faraday's disk motor) operating in MHD regime.

**Output:** First step toward levitation — demonstrated at bench scale (observed spinning of galinstan at ~50 A, 6 V). Provides proof-of-concept that electromagnetic body forces on liquid conductors can produce continuous rotation without moving parts (except the fluid itself).

**Path to full propulsion:** Optimize external B field strength → increase toroidal current → increase self-generated axial field → increase vertical Lorentz force → lift off. Replace liquid metal with spinning solid metal disc (Stages 1–4 above) for higher-frequency, higher-power operation.

### Stage 6 — Counter-Rotating Discs: Output = Torque-Balanced, Angular-Momentum-Neutral Craft

**Input physics:** Two magnetic discs rotating in opposite directions (Step 3 and 4 in Drew's sequence).

**Mechanism:** Counter-rotation: L_1 = +I_1·ω_1, L_2 = -I_2·ω_2. If |L_1| = |L_2|: L_total = 0. No net gyroscopic coupling to inertial space — the craft can reorient freely without gyroscopic resistance. Yet each disc individually produces its Lorentz force and magnetic gradient lift.

**Output:** Gyroscopically neutral craft that can maneuver in all directions (including the extreme direction changes observed in UAP encounters) without experiencing the gyroscopic torque reactions that would afflict a single-disc system. The absence of net angular momentum prevents the craft from being "locked" into a fixed orientation by angular momentum conservation.

**Advancement beyond Newtonian:** A conventional aerospace vehicle with spinning rotors has a fixed angular momentum vector that resists reorientation. The counter-rotating system eliminates this constraint while preserving the levitation force of both discs — decoupling the levitation mechanism from orientation control.
