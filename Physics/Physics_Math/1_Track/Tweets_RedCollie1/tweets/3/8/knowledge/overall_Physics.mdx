**Comet physics:** The anti-tail (or counter-tail) of a comet is a dust tail that appears to point toward the Sun rather than away from it, due to projection effects when Earth passes through the comet's orbital plane. The anti-tail consists of larger dust particles with lower radiation pressure coupling (β = F_rad/F_grav < 1). This is consistent with standard cometary plasma physics (analogous to Venus's interaction with the solar wind — absence of intrinsic magnetic field, induced ionospheric magnetopause).
