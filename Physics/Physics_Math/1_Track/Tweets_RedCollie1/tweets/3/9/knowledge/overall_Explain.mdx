### Experimental System Description

Dr. Drew describes a laboratory apparatus consisting of:
- One permanent ring magnet (70 mm outer diameter, 14 mm central hole, 3 mm thickness)
- A ring of six 3-phase wire coils, wired in the pattern U-U-V-V-W-W
- The magnet spins either above or below the coil ring under 3-phase AC drive

### Key Experimental Results (Claimed First-Time on Earth)

**Result 1 — Z-axis anti-gravity analog of spinning top:**
The spinning permanent magnet always rises upward against the downward force of gravity, analogous to a spinning gyroscope precessing against gravitational torque. The observed direction-independence across both N-up and S-up orientations eliminates classical magnetic monopole repulsion as the mechanism (see overall_Magnetics.mdx).

**Result 2 — Newton's Third Law Reaction:**
The 3-phase wire coil assembly moves downward with a force precisely equal in magnitude to the upward force generated by the spinning magnet:
> F_up (magnet) = F_down (coils)

**Result 3 — Net Gravitational Weight Unchanged (System-Level):**
Because the reaction force is equal and opposite within the system, the total weight of the combined magnet + coil assembly as measured by a scale beneath the device remains unchanged:
> W_total = W_magnet + W_coils = const
> ΔW_scale = 0

This is consistent with conservation of momentum in a closed system: the internal forces cancel and no net external force is generated on the ground-reference frame by the spinning-magnetic interaction alone.

**Result 4 — Geometric Configuration Independence:**
Magnet placed above OR below the coil ring yields qualitatively similar levitation and reaction dynamics. This implies the coupling is not dependent on the axial sign of the relative position vector between magnet and coil centroid.

**Result 5 — Pole Orientation Independence:**
N-up and S-up orientations of the permanent magnet produce identical upward force behavior. This is a critical deviation from classical dipole-field theory, which predicts opposing signs of axial force depending on whether a dipole is oriented parallel or antiparallel to an external field gradient. The observed symmetry implies the force-producing mechanism is NOT classical magnetic-pole attraction/repulsion but is instead related to the spin angular momentum of the magnetic domains.

**Result 6 — 3-Phase Rotation Direction Independence:**
Both clockwise and counterclockwise rotation directions of the 3-phase rotating magnetic field produce identical upward force on the spinning magnet. Combined with Result 5, this double-directional independence severely constrains candidate force mechanisms.

**Result 7 — Helical Field Threading:**
The 3-phase coil system (U-U-V-V-W-W wiring) creates a helical magnetic field geometry. The physical interpretation offered is that a spinning permanent magnet can thread ("rise through") this helical field upward against gravity.

### Conclusion and Forward Path

Drew explicitly states: "a spinning magnet plus 3-phase wire cannot create a 'UFO drive' by themselves, even when the upward force of a spinning magnet reaches 1–2 kg." The system demonstrates anti-gravitational-analog force on the magnet, but the equal and opposite reaction on the coils means no net lift is transferred to a vehicle frame. He proposes adding a "third component" to break this symmetry and convert the internal upward force into net external propulsion.

This is the central engineering problem: the spinning-magnet/helical-field interaction produces a real internal force (1–2 kg range demonstrated), but coupling that force to produce net Z-axis displacement of the entire device requires a mechanism that does not generate an equal downward reaction on the coil platform. The "third component" is the current open research question.

**Bridging relevance:** The Lorentz force (F = q**v** × **B**, or in macroscopic form **J** × **B** for current-carrying conductors) provides the mechanism by which an applied DC current in a liquid or conducting medium, crossed with an ambient magnetic field, produces a force at 90° to both. This is the same mechanism proposed for the dowel/liquid-metal stage of the UFO levitation model (above). If the rotating field from the 3-phase coils provides **B**, and the current through the central dowel provides **J**, then **F** = **J** × **B** acts radially (if **J** is axial and **B** is toroidal) or azimuthally (if **J** is radial and **B** is axial), potentially providing an asymmetric force component not captured in the simple spinning-magnet system.
