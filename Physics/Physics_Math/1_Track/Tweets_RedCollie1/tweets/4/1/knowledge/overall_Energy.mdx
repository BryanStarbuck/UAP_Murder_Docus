### Nuclear Fission and Fusion as Power Sources for Interstellar Vessel

Drew's note on interstellar propulsion energy requirements: "They could all live safely inside, and get their power from nuclear fission or fusion reactions."

This is a statement about the energy scale required for interstellar travel. For a ship of mass M traveling at velocity v (non-relativistic approximation):

  Kinetic energy: KE = (1/2)*M*v^2

For M = 10^9 kg (large asteroid-mined ship) and v = 0.01c (1% of light speed):
  KE = (1/2)*(10^9)*(3×10^6)^2 = 4.5 × 10^{21} J

For nuclear fusion (D-T), energy density ≈ 3.4 × 10^{14} J/kg:
  Required fuel mass = KE / (energy density) = 4.5×10^{21} / 3.4×10^{14} ≈ 1.3 × 10^7 kg

This is approximately 1.3% of the ship mass for acceleration phase alone (not including deceleration). Drew frames this as feasible for a civilization with access to asteroid-scale resources — the ship mines its own fuel from the asteroid body from which it is constructed.

The physics of fission vs. fusion:
* Fission (U-235 or Pu-239): ~0.1% mass-energy conversion, produces high-energy neutrons (2 MeV average), requires shielding
* Fusion (D-T or D-He3): ~0.3-0.5% mass-energy conversion, D-He3 produces mostly protons (not neutrons), more spacecraft-compatible

For a spacetime wormhole device mentioned separately in this tweet ("place some device for a spacetime wormhole"), the energy requirements would be orders of magnitude higher — Thorne-Morris traversable wormhole estimates require exotic matter with negative energy density of order:

  E_wormhole ~ -c^4 * R^2 / (G * r_0)

where R is the throat radius and r_0 is the length of the wormhole, typically requiring negative energy densities far exceeding what any fusion process produces.

### Jets Around 3I/ATLAS as Potential Thruster Evidence

Drew's observation of 3I/ATLAS images (November 17, 2025): anti-tail visible on left, "series of fan-like jets (thrusters?)" visible on right.

The physics of cometary jets vs. artificial thrusters:

Natural cometary jets: Produced by sublimation of volatile ices (H2O, CO2, CO) when sunlit. The gas velocities are:
  v_jet ~ sqrt(2*k_B*T/m) ≈ 300-1000 m/s (thermal velocity)

For an artificial thruster (ion drive or plasma jet), exhaust velocities can reach:
  v_exhaust (ion drive) ~ 30,000-300,000 m/s (specific impulse I_sp = 3000-30,000 s)

The "fan-like" multiple jet structure (multiple distinct jets rather than a single coma) is cited as inconsistent with natural sublimation (which produces a symmetric or single-direction coma). Multiple distinct jets at specific angular positions would suggest structured exhaust ports — i.e., deliberate propulsion rather than random outgassing.

Drew also inverts light-for-dark in the image to reveal an anti-tail. Anti-tails (pointing toward the Sun rather than away) in natural comets are explained by large dust grains with low beta (radiation pressure to gravity ratio). An artificial anti-tail or inward-directed exhaust would indicate active inward deceleration — braking thrust to slow the object as it approaches perihelion.

### Spinning Magnet as Gyroscope — Energy Considerations

"A fast-spinning magnet acts like a gyroscope."

Gyroscopic energy:
  E_gyro = (1/2) * I * Omega^2

For a disc magnet of mass m = 0.1 kg, radius r = 30mm:
  I = (1/2)*m*r^2 = (1/2)*(0.1)*(0.03)^2 = 4.5 × 10^{-5} kg*m^2

At Omega = 2*pi*30 Hz = 188 rad/s:
  E_gyro = (1/2)*(4.5×10^{-5})*(188)^2 = 0.795 J

At Omega = 2*pi*200 Hz = 1257 rad/s (higher speed):
  E_gyro = (1/2)*(4.5×10^{-5})*(1257)^2 = 35.6 J

The energy stored in the gyroscopic rotation is substantial at high speeds. The claim of anomalous lift from this spinning magnet would, if real, involve extraction of energy from this rotational kinetic energy store, or coupling to an external AC field that continuously replenishes the rotational energy.

Energy input from 3-phase power: The 3-phase field does work on the spinning magnet at rate:
  P_input = tau * Omega = (magnetic torque) × (angular velocity)

For torque tau ~ m × B (magnetic moment cross field):
  P_input = m * B_0 * Omega * sin(delta)

where delta is the angle between m and B. At steady-state spin (Omega constant), P_input goes to zero (no net work) in standard electromagnetism. The anomalous effect requires that P_input ≠ 0 at steady state — implying a continuous energy flow from the AC source into the kinetic/potential energy of the rising magnet.
