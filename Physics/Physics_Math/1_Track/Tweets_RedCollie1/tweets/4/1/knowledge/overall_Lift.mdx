Observation: "A N-S magnet will also rise up, when we spin a horizontal N-S permanent magnet below."

  B_z(z, t) = (mu_0 * mu_h / 4*pi) * (2*cos^2(theta)*... )  [time-varying]

where mu_h = horizontal moment magnitude, theta = polar angle. The time-averaged axial force on the vertical magnet m_v above is not zero if there is a nonlinear coupling — for example through the induced oscillation of the vertical magnet's own position or through coupling to its eddy currents.

This observation establishes that z-axis levitation can be produced without any electrical coils — with only permanent magnets and mechanical rotation. This is significant for understanding the underlying physics: the effect cannot be attributed purely to AC electromagnetic induction, since no AC source is present.

  Omega_precession = gamma * B_eff

For a macroscopic spinning magnet in an external field B_0 along z:

where m is the magnetic moment, I is the moment of inertia, Omega_spin is the spin angular velocity.
