### Counter-Spin Without Counter-Fall — The Central Non-Newtonian Claim

**Observation:** The outer counter-spinning section of a UFO does not descend despite having no apparent upward force in the Newtonian sense.

**Why this is non-Newtonian:**
Newton's second law: F = ma. For the outer shell spinning counter-clockwise in the horizontal plane, with no net torque in the vertical plane, there is no Newtonian reason for it to either ascend or descend. It should remain stationary in the Z direction.

But the claim is stronger: it is not merely that it remains stationary, but that it actively levitates (remains aloft while the entire craft rises). The rising of the entire assembly — inner rotating magnet AND outer counter-spinning flywheel — under only the inner magnet's drive force requires the outer flywheel to contribute to or not oppose the ascent.

**Possible non-Newtonian mechanism — the Laithwaite gyroscope argument:**
Eric Laithwaite's 1974 Royal Institution lecture claimed that a spinning gyroscope of mass m, when caused to precess horizontally (at the end of a rope), appeared to lose weight. The effective vertical force equation becomes:

    F_vertical = m g − (I ω_spin ω_precession / r_pendulum)

where r_pendulum is the pendulum arm length, ω_spin is the gyroscope spin rate, and ω_precession is the precession rate. If:
    I ω_spin ω_precession / r_pendulum → m g

the effective weight → 0 (weightlessness). If the quantity exceeds mg, the gyroscope "rises."

Laithwaite's observation was disputed by classical mechanics practitioners because the full vector analysis (including the precessional kinetic energy) must include the work done in maintaining the precession. In a free system without constraint, the gyroscope does not reduce its weight. However, in a magnetically constrained system (outer flywheel held in counter-spin by the rotating field), the precession may be maintained at no cost in classical energy — which is the non-Newtonian aspect.

**Direct non-Newtonian implication:** If confirmed experimentally (sustained levitation of a spin/counter-spin assembly with less energy input than m_total × g × h_rise), this would falsify the standard Newtonian + Maxwellian framework for electromagnetic-mechanical interaction. The result would constitute evidence for a missing force in classical mechanics.

For a spin/counter-spin levitation device, reduced torque ripple means a more steady vertical force (F_z), potentially the difference between oscillating partial lift (as observed with 3-phase) and steady levitation.

For N=3: ripple at 6f, amplitude ∝ 1/3
For N=5: ripple at 10f, amplitude ∝ 1/5

The 5-phase case provides smoother vertical force — potentially crossing the threshold from oscillating lift (3-phase, insufficient for steady levitation) to steady lift (5-phase, sufficient).
