- Produces high-voltage oscillations at f_0, which can be very high (kHz–MHz range)
- Output: high-frequency, high-voltage RF emissions

    S = E × H; |S| = |E||H|cosφ

In the context of the interstellar object 3I/ATLAS, Drew responds to a question about whether nickel is emitted due to a propulsion system: "It is more probably being shed in small amounts from the outer shiny surface of 3I/ATLAS, something like when a space shuttle loses its heat tiles."

This implies Drew does not attribute the nickel emission to propulsion exhaust (which would require an energy source driving the emission), but rather to surface ablation (thermal or friction-based). If 3I/ATLAS were propulsion-driven, the energy budget would need to account for the mass loss rate and velocity of the shed material:

    F_reaction = (dm/dt) · v_exhaust

For surface shedding (ablation), the energy source would be solar heating or atmospheric friction, not an exotic internal energy source.
