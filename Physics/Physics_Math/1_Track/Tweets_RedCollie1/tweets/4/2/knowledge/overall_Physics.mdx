### Newton's Third Law Reaction Torque — The Core Engineering Problem

The 40 mm magnet, spinning clockwise under 3-phase field excitation, exerts an anti-clockwise torque on the wire-coil frame by Newton's third law (conservation of angular momentum).

Formal statement: if L_magnet is the angular momentum of the spinning magnet,

    dL_magnet/dt = τ_applied

by Newton's third law, the equal and opposite torque τ_reaction = -τ_applied acts on the frame. This reaction torque causes the frame (and thus the craft) to rotate anti-clockwise, which is kinematically undesirable.

Required cancellation: Drew hypothesizes a "counter-spinning Lorentz force" cancels this. The Lorentz force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field is F = IL × B. If a second set of conductors carries current in the opposite rotational direction in the same (or a counter-oriented) magnetic field, the resulting Lorentz torque is opposite in sign to the reaction torque, providing cancellation.

This is the central unresolved physics problem in the experimental program as of the available tweets.

### Gyroscopic Spin Versus Rotating Magnetic Field — Compass Test Discriminant

Measurement protocol: place compasses in the field of a suspected UAP or of a laboratory model. If compasses spin continuously, the field is a rotating (3-phase or multi-phase) magnetic field. If compasses deflect to a fixed bearing, the field is static.

Physics: for a static magnetic dipole field B = B_0 (constant direction), a compass needle aligns to a fixed angle θ = arctan(B_y/B_x). No continuous rotation is possible.

For a rotating field B(t) = B_0 [cos(ωt) x̂ + sin(ωt) ŷ], the compass needle tracks the rotating field vector, producing continuous rotation at angular frequency ω.

Drew's discriminant statement: "Gyroscopic spin alone will not produce this measured effect." A gyroscope (spinning mass) produces a static magnetic-moment-like field if it has charge, but not a rotating field vector. Therefore, the compass rotation observed under UFOs is evidence of a rotating magnetic field (powered mechanism) rather than a passively spinning magnetic mass.
