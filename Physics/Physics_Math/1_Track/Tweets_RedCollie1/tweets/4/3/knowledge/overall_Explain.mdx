Source claim (video transcript): "I'm gonna add juice to this circuit and the wires will
rotate one direction and the disc will rotate the other direction. But the wires are not

Newton's Third Law to rotational systems. In a conventional closed system, equal and

(a) An external torque source (electromagnetic field coupling to an external mass, e.g.,
    Earth's magnetic field),
(b) A non-trivial Lorentz-force geometry wherein the current-carrying wire's interaction
    with the magnetic field transfers torque to the disc through the shared field rather
    than through direct mechanical coupling.

The observer's framing — "Where are the torques interacting? This is not equal and
opposite torques" — identifies the anomaly as a candidate for "beyond-Newtonian"
torque transfer physics.
