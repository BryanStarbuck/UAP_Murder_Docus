### The Core Engineering Claim: UFO Levitation is an Engineering Problem, Not a Theoretical One

Dr. Drew's central thesis bridges a series of physical mechanisms to the output of anomalous vertical lift ("RISE LIKE A UFO") against gravity. The chain of causation he proposes is:

**Mechanism chain (XY-plane dynamics producing Z-axis output):**

1. A permanent disc magnet is spun in the XY-plane by a homopolar motor or 3-phase wire coil assembly.
2. The spinning magnet acts as a gyroscope; its angular momentum vector L points along the spin axis (Z-direction when spin axis is vertical).
3. A 3-phase helical magnetic field (the "trident" shape from crop circle diagrams) is generated by a ring of 3 or 6 or 9 wire coils surrounding the spinning magnet. This field rotates in the XY-plane.
4. The interaction of the rotating external field with the spinning permanent magnet produces a RISE force directed along the +Z axis (upward, against gravity g).
5. Both North and South poles of the permanent magnet participate equally in the RISE force — the force is symmetric under pole inversion, which distinguishes this from conventional magnetic levitation (which is pole-asymmetric).
6. The magnitude of the demonstrated rise force in Drew's benchtop prototypes is claimed to reach 1–2 kg-force in a small model system.

**The force-compensation paradox (key physical puzzle):**

Drew explicitly states: "Such a RISE against the downward force of gravity, however, produces NO change of weight on a scale!" He frames this as an analog to the spinning top: a spinning top presses down onto its support surface with a force equal in magnitude to the precession-driven rise force. The complete system (spinning magnet + 3-phase wire coils) appears to induce an equal-magnitude DOWNWARD reaction force on the supporting structure, leaving the total system weight measured on a scale unchanged.

This is the central engineering bottleneck he identifies: to achieve free-flight (UFO-like lift-off), the downward reaction force must be decoupled from the ground, i.e., the 3-phase coils must be carried onboard the craft rather than grounded. The question he poses: "How can we make the complete system 'lift up into the air' like a UFO?"

**The spin/counter-spin upgrade (later prototypes):**

In tweet 2035959113520460153 and 2042518945157238885, Drew introduces a refinement: a spin/counter-spin configuration. The inner spinning disc magnet rotates in one direction (e.g., clockwise, +ω_inner), and an outer flywheel of 4 magnets rotates in the opposite direction (counter-clockwise, −ω_outer), driven by 6 outer wire coils with U-V-W-U-V-W 3-phase phasing.

He states: "The angular momentum due to counter-spin will always be greater than that due to spin. Likewise the angular reaction by Newton's Third Law to counter-spin will always be greater than that due to spin."

This asymmetry in angular momentum magnitudes (|L_outer| > |L_inner|) is claimed to produce a net uncompensated angular reaction, which Drew suggests may be the mechanism by which the ground reaction force is overcome or asymmetrically redirected.

**Practical force observations (video transcription 13_2042517002548580352_video.mdx):**

At ~30 Hz for the outer 4-magnet ring (counter-clockwise) and ~30 Hz for the inner disc magnet (clockwise), the device exhibits the following: the inner magnet "tries to go up, but the magnets outside won't let it go up." This suggests a vertical force is being generated on the inner magnet, restrained by the magnetic coupling to the outer ring. The released upward force when decoupled from the outer ring is the proposed propulsion output.

**Galinstan liquid metal vortex (video 13_1968862743961980928_video.mdx):**

The second experimental iteration replaces the solid spinning disc magnet with a spinning vortex of galinstan (a eutectic liquid metal alloy: gallium-indium-tin). The galinstan vortex is spin-driven by a Lorentz force from 11 permanent magnets arranged in a ring, powered by a 12V DC supply. Reversing DC polarity reverses vortex spin direction. The intended step 3 is to place 3-phase wire coils beneath the spinning galinstan vortex ("trident force") to attempt vertical rise.

**Relationship to classical gyroscope physics (tweet 1980900551522152688, 1920981572414488861):**

Drew explicitly ties his device to the classical observation that a spinning top can "go airborne" — a spinning top/gyroscope undergoes precession under gravity, and precession converts the gravitational torque into a horizontal angular motion of the spin axis. The vertical persistence of the spin axis during precession is the analog mechanism Drew proposes underlies the anomalous rise force in his spinning magnet system. The key extension beyond classical mechanics is that in his system, the precession force is not simply redirecting the gravitational torque into lateral spin-axis rotation, but is being converted into a net vertical force output by the interaction with the external rotating magnetic field.
