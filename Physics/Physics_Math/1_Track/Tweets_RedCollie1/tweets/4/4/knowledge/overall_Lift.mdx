### The Force Compensation Problem: Why the Scale Reads Zero

Critical experimental observation: "Such a RISE against the downward force of gravity, however, produces NO change of weight on a scale!"

Physical interpretation: the total system (spinning magnet + 3-phase coil assembly + support structure) does not lose weight as measured on a balance. This means:
- The upward force on the spinning magnet (+F_rise in +Z) is exactly counterbalanced by a downward force (−F_rise in −Z) on the coil assembly/support.
- Newton's Third Law is obeyed within the complete system.
- The "anti-gravity" effect is an internal force redistribution, not a true reduction in gravitational coupling.

**Spinning-top analog:**
"The rising force of a spinning top presses down onto a tabletop with precisely the same force as by which it rises."

In the precessing top: the normal force N from the tabletop equals the weight Mg (no net vertical acceleration of the center of mass). The "rise" of the spinning top (its tendency to stay upright and appear to defy gravity) is not a violation of Newton's Third Law but a redistribution of forces enabled by the gyroscopic effect.

**The engineering challenge:**
"How can we make the complete system 'lift up into the air' like a UFO?"

To achieve free flight, the downward reaction force must not be grounded. If the coil assembly is on the ground and the spinning magnet is above it, the magnet rises but the ground absorbs the reaction. If the coil assembly is onboard (attached to the craft), the reaction pushes the craft down — net zero external force.

Drew's proposed solution (implicit in later tweets): the spin/counter-spin configuration's angular momentum asymmetry changes the direction or magnitude of the reaction force, allowing net external force to emerge.
