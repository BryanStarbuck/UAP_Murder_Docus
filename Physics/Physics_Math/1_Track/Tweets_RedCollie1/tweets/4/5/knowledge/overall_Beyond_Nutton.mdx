### The Core Beyond-Newton Claim: "Escaping" Newton's Third Law

Drew states explicitly: "It has to do with fast-spinning magnets and 'escaping' Newton's 3rd Law, by combining spin with counter-spin."

**Newton's Third Law (classical statement):**
  F₁₂ = −F₂₁
  For every force exerted by body A on body B, body B exerts an equal and opposite force on body A.

**Implication for propulsion:** In a closed system with only internal forces, the center of mass cannot accelerate:
  ΣF_internal = 0 → d²(r_cm)/dt² = 0

**The proposed escape mechanism:**
Drew's claim is that the spin + counter-spin configuration of two counter-rotating magnetic discs modifies or circumvents this law, allowing a net external force (lift/thrust) to emerge from purely internal electromagnetic dynamics.

**Physical basis for the claim (beyond Newton, within electrodynamics):**
Electromagnetic systems are not required to satisfy Newton's Third Law in its mechanical form when the electromagnetic field itself carries momentum. The complete momentum conservation law is:
  d/dt (p_mech + p_EM) = F_external
  where p_EM = ε₀ ∫ (**E** × **B**) dV = (1/c²) ∫ **S** dV

If the counter-rotating disc geometry creates a time-varying electromagnetic momentum flux (Poynting vector with a net directional component), then the mechanical momentum of the disc assembly can change without a corresponding reaction on any identifiable mechanical body — the reaction appears in the electromagnetic field, which propagates away. This is the electromagnetic radiation-pressure / recoil mechanism, and is a known effect (e.g., laser sails in space). However, in Drew's case the claimed effect operates at very low frequencies (Hz range), where radiation efficiency is negligibly small. The claim therefore requires a mechanism beyond classical radiation recoil.

**Possible beyond-Newton mechanisms under investigation:**
1. **Woodward Mach Effect:** Transient fluctuations in inertial mass of an accelerating object (derivable from the Wheeler-Feynman absorber theory or from Sciama's field-theoretic formulation of Mach's principle). For an object of mass m undergoing acceleration a in a varying potential energy field Φ:
   δm = (4/c²)(dE/dt)/ω²  (Woodward's formula)
   If the effective inertial mass oscillates in sync with the applied force (via an electromagnetic mechanism), a net thrust can emerge. Drew does not cite Woodward explicitly in this batch but the experimental setup is structurally similar to Mach-effect thruster experiments.

2. **Electromagnetic angular momentum coupling to linear momentum:** When two magnetic systems counter-rotate, the electromagnetic field in the gap between them carries angular momentum:
   L_EM = ε₀ ∫ r × (**E** × **B**) dV
   Changes in L_EM do not directly produce linear momentum, but through coupling to non-symmetric boundary conditions (the axial geometry of the coil system), angular-to-linear momentum transfer may occur via the Abraham–Minkowski stress tensor in dielectric/magnetic media.

3. **Broken time-reversal symmetry:** Counter-rotating magnetic discs create a composite magnetic field that is not time-reversal invariant (a single rotating disc under time reversal would spin in the opposite direction, equivalent to a different physical state; the counter-rotating pair under time reversal produces the mirror state with inner and outer swapped). Broken time-reversal symmetry in the field is a necessary condition for non-reciprocal electromagnetic effects, some of which may produce net directional forces.

### Spontaneous Counter-Rotation from a Single Clockwise Field

"What is truly remarkable here, is that we can get fast, powerful, clockwise spin, as well as fast, powerful, anti-clockwise counter-spin, from the SAME 3-phase, clockwise rotating magnetic field!"

**Beyond-Newton significance:**
The single rotating field input (which carries angular momentum in one direction only, +L_z) simultaneously imparts +L_z to the inner magnet and −L_z to the outer ring. The total angular momentum of the mechanical system:
  L_total_mech = L_inner + L_outer = (+L_inner) + (−L_outer)

If |L_inner| ≠ |L_outer|, the total mechanical angular momentum ≠ 0, and the difference must be accounted for by angular momentum exchange with the driving field (supplied by the power supply). This is expected and not paradoxical.

However, if L_inner + L_outer = 0 (equal and opposite), the mechanical angular momentum is zero while the field drives both. This is a near-zero net angular momentum configuration. In this configuration, the system does not carry net spin angular momentum, but it DOES carry orbital angular momentum in the rotating field topology. The combination of zero net mechanical spin with strong internal counter-rotation is the proposed prerequisite for Newton's Third Law modification.

**Connection to gyroscopic precession and propulsion:**
A gyroscope with zero net spin would not precess. The counter-rotating disc system, if L_inner + L_outer → 0, effectively reduces the net gyroscopic resistance to precession of the entire device. Simultaneously, each individual subsystem (inner and outer) maintains high individual gyroscopic stiffness. This may create a configuration where the device as a whole has unusual inertial properties that differ from those predicted by standard Newtonian mechanics for a rotating rigid body.

### Gyroscopic Dynamics at the Classical-Quantum Boundary

"A fast-spinning magnet acts like a gyroscope, and thus is [stable]."

The gyroscope at macroscopic scales operates entirely within classical Newtonian mechanics (Euler's equations for rigid body rotation). However, a spinning permanent magnet is simultaneously a macroscopic gyroscope AND a quantum magnetic system (its magnetization arises from quantum electron spin). The coupling between the classical angular momentum L = Iω and the quantum magnetic moment **m** = γ**L** (where γ is the gyromagnetic ratio) means the gyroscope is also a magnetic gyroscope: it precesses in an external B field at the Larmor frequency:
  ω_L = γ B = (e/2m_e) × g_factor × B

For a macroscopic permanent magnet, γ is the effective gyromagnetic ratio of the collective magnetization (different from the electron value). The precession of the macroscopic magnetic dipole in the rotating B field of the three-phase coils is what produces the coupling between the field and the rising motion. As spin rate increases and gyroscopic stiffness increases, the precession is suppressed and the magnet locks to the field — at which point the axial force is maximized. This transition from precessing (wobbly) to locked (stable rising) behavior is the threshold at which the beyond-Newton propulsive effect is hypothesized to emerge.
