### The "Upward Pole" Force as a Propulsive Output

The central propulsive claim in this batch is the emergence of a net axial (Z-axis) thrust force on a spinning permanent magnet placed inside a three-phase rotating magnetic field. Drew designates this the "upward pole" force. The mechanism bridges XY-plane rotational dynamics to Z-axis translational output via a non-trivial coupling that is not accounted for in classical magnetostatics.

**Experimental configuration:**
- Permanent ring magnet: 100 mm diameter, 13 mm central hole, mounted on an 8 mm stainless steel rod
- Placed inside a three-phase rotating magnetic field generated by three coil assemblies at 120-degree intervals
- Two polarity orientations tested: blue "South" pole facing up vs. red "North" pole facing up

**Observed output:**
- With blue South pole up: the magnet rises axially along the rod to the center / top of the three-phase field region. This occurs for both clockwise and anti-clockwise spin of the driving field — the polarity of rotation does not determine whether the upward motion occurs.
- With red North pole up: the magnet spins and rises slightly but does not propagate to the top. The upward pole force is polarity-dependent.

**Critical kinematic observation distinguishing thrust from mechanical screw action:**
The video (13_1912105612931932160) establishes explicitly that the upward motion is NOT due to a gyroscopic screw mechanism. The rod carrying the magnet was pre-drilled in both directions with a 13 mm drill through the plastic insert, eliminating any thread structure. Furthermore, at the moment of ascent, the rod and cork are observed to be rotating slowly or not at all, while the magnet nonetheless rises. Drew's explicit conclusion: "It's a thrust motion. Up with thrust and not spin." This is the key bridge: XY-plane magnetic spin couples to Z-axis linear thrust via a field topology effect, not via mechanical torque-to-translation conversion.

**Inversion symmetry observation:**
"Also in every case, the upward pole spin-motion for blue-up permanent magnets is always opposite to spin-motion in the magnet below." This counter-rotation relationship between the rising pole and the base magnetic subsystem is identified as consistent with the witness account of Edwin Fuhr (Canada), who reported observing the top of each UFO rotating opposite to a spinning force below.
