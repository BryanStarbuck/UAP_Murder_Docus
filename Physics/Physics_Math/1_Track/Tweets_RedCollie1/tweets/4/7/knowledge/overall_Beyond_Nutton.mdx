The reaction to the Lorentz force is not a force on another body but a change in electromagnetic field momentum.

P_field = ε₀ ∫(E × B) dV = (1/c²) ∫ S dV    [kg·m/s]

dP_mechanical/dt = -dP_field/dt (conservation of total momentum)

Implication: the mechanical system CAN appear to violate Newton's Third Law when only the mechanical momenta are tracked — the "reaction" has been transferred to P_field. A craft using Lorentz forces could therefore accelerate mechanically while the "reaction" appears nowhere in the mechanical domain (it is absorbed into the field).

This is the standard explanation for electromagnetic recoil — but in conventional engineering, the field momentum effects are negligible. Drew's claim is that in a specifically engineered high-field, high-current rotating magnet system, the field momentum effects become dominant and can be harnessed for macroscopic propulsion.

### Chain of Reasoning for Beyond-Newtonian Propulsion:

Step 1: Fast-spinning magnet develops angular momentum L = Iω (Z-axis, upward).
Step 2: The gyroscopic stiffness of L produces an effective upward force F_up in response to external perturbations (field gradients, gravity interaction).

Step 4: The proposed intervention: engineer the system so F_down is expressed as a Lorentz force (field-mediated) rather than a Newtonian contact force (body-mediated).
Step 5: Lorentz force reaction goes into electromagnetic field momentum P_field = (1/c²)∫S dV, not into mechanical reaction on the craft.
Step 6: Net mechanical force on craft = F_up (upward) + 0 (no Newtonian downward reaction) = F_up ≠ 0.
Step 7: The craft accelerates upward without a mechanical reaction thrust — propellantless propulsion.

This is beyond Newtonian mechanics because it requires: (a) electromagnetic field momentum to be a significant fraction of the mechanical momentum change, and (b) the field momentum to be "expelled" into the environment rather than looping back into a reaction on the craft.
