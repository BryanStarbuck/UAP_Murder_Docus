### Step 1: Spinning Magnet Produces Upward Force

Drew's claim: "We can get a strong 'upward' force from a fast-spinning magnet (or magnetic flywheel), because it 'pushes against space' like a spinning gyroscope."

Physics of gyroscopic reaction force:
- A spinning body with angular momentum L = Iω (pointing along Z-axis for a horizontally-spinning disc) resists changes to its spin axis.
- When an external torque τ is applied, the gyroscope precesses: Ω_prec = τ/(|L| sin θ) where θ is the angle between τ and L.
- Crucially: the gyroscope's resistance to precession can manifest as a force directed along the precession reaction axis.
- For a vertically-spinning magnet (spin axis = Z-axis, L pointing up): an upward force corresponds to the gyroscope "pushing against" the spatial medium in the direction of its spin axis.

Drew's "pushes against space" language is informal but corresponds to the physical concept of gyroscopic rigidity: the spinning body's angular momentum vector L acts like a stiff vector that resists reorientation. Any attempt to tilt or deflect L generates a reaction force perpendicular to both L and the deflecting force — in the geometry considered, with L vertical and the deflecting influence (gravity / field gradient) in some horizontal direction, the reaction force can have a vertical upward component.

### Step 2: The Fundamental Problem (Newton's Third Law)

From 1972971982804373872 note_tweet verbatim:
"The problem is that such an 'upward' force is accompanied by an equal and opposite 'downward' force by Newton's 3rd Law. So we see an increase of gravitational potential, but no loss of total weight ('up' plus 'down'), unless that spinning magnet goes into 'free fall'."

where F_up is the upward gyroscopic force and F_down = -F_up is the equal and opposite reaction force applied to the craft/support structure below the spinning magnet.

Implication: unless the reaction force is redirected or absorbed, no net lift is possible. The system as a whole (magnet + craft) does not gain net vertical momentum.

From note_tweet verbatim: "How to solve this great problem? The E.T. crop artists have suggested (many times) that we should try to change that 'downward' force into another kind of force, which does NOT follow Newton's 3rd Law: namely the Lorentz force of electromagnetism."

When the Lorentz force accelerates a charged body, the reaction is absorbed into the electromagnetic field momentum rather than into a discrete mechanical body. Therefore, from the perspective of the mechanical system (the craft), there is no direct mechanical downward reaction force — it has been transferred to the field.

If F_Lorentz_down_component → 0 (Lorentz force entirely redirected into electromagnetic field momentum), then:

### Step 4: "Free Fall" as Alternative Path to Lift

Drew notes: "no loss of total weight ... unless that spinning magnet goes into 'free fall'."
In free fall, the spinning magnet experiences no reaction from a support structure — it is in a geodesic. In free fall, there is no "downward" reaction force from a mechanical support, because the support is not present. This is equivalent to the Einstein equivalence principle: in a freely falling frame, gravity is locally absent. A spinning magnet in free fall with gyroscopic upward force could therefore potentially escape its trajectory upward.
This is a secondary mechanism Drew mentions as a precondition for the primary Lorentz conversion mechanism.
