$$\frac{d\mathbf{L}}{dt} = \boldsymbol{\tau}$$

This is a classic constrained dynamics problem where energy redistribution produces anomalous force amplification at the constraint.

### Classical AC Lenz Levitation Physics
**Faraday's Law (induction):**
$$\mathcal{E} = -N\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt}$$

For an aluminum ring of resistance R, the induced current is:
$$I_{ring} = \frac{\mathcal{E}}{R} = -\frac{N}{R}\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt}$$

**Force on the ring:**
$$F_{Lenz} = I_{ring} \cdot \oint (d\mathbf{l} \times \mathbf{B}_{ext})$$

This force is directed upward (repulsive, opposing the increasing flux from the coil). By Newton's 3rd law, the force on the coil from the ring's field is equal and opposite — directed downward into the coil assembly.

Net force on the {ring + coil + iron core} system = 0. No gravitational decoupling.

When the aluminum disc conductor is given rapid rotation (angular velocity Ω about Z-axis), the effective current distribution changes. In the non-rotating disc, eddy currents form closed loops in the XY plane driven by the time-varying normal B-field. In the rotating disc, each current element has an additional velocity component v_Ω = Ω × r (tangential).

The Lorentz force on a current element is:
$$d\mathbf{F} = I(d\mathbf{l} \times \mathbf{B}) + \rho(\mathbf{v}_\Omega \times \mathbf{B})dV$$

The second term (rotation-induced Lorentz force) is absent in the non-rotating disc. It is directed radially (inward or outward depending on B-direction and rotation sense) and modifies the current path within the disc. The modified current path changes the magnetic dipole moment of the disc, which changes the interaction energy with the external AC field.

The claim is that this modification **breaks the up-down force symmetry**: the upward Lenz force and the downward Newton's-3rd-law reaction are no longer equal in magnitude. ### The Steam Engine as Transduction Model
The Watt steam engine converts thermodynamic energy (steam pressure, linear force on piston) to rotational mechanical output (crankshaft). This established the principle that **energy in one form and direction can be transduced to energy in another form and direction** through a mechanical coupling mechanism.

Physical formulation of the analogy:
- Steam pressure P acting on piston area A → linear force F = PA
- Connecting rod converts linear displacement dx to angular displacement dθ via crank geometry
- Work: dW = F·dx = τ·dθ (where τ is the torque on the crankshaft)
- Power: P_mechanical = τ·ω (shaft power)

The efficiency of transduction depends on the mechanical design (crank angle, connecting rod length ratio, etc.).

### Tesla's AC/3-Phase: Field Transduction
Tesla's invention of 3-phase AC power introduced:
- Rotating magnetic fields (motor action) via 3-phase winding geometry

where ρ is fluid density, **u** is velocity field, p is pressure, μ_d is dynamic viscosity, **J** is current density, **B** is magnetic field.

**Induction equation:**
$$\frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t} = \nabla \times (\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{B}) + \frac{1}{\mu_0 \sigma}\nabla^2 \mathbf{B}$$
