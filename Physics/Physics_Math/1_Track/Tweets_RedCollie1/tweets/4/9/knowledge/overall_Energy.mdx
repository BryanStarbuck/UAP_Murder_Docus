### Homopolar Generator: DC Electrical Energy from Rotational Kinetic Energy

The homopolar generator converts rotational mechanical energy to DC electrical energy
with no commutator, no alternating phases, and in principle no friction losses beyond
bearing and brush resistance. The energy conversion efficiency in an ideal homopolar
generator:

  η = P_electrical / P_mechanical = (EMF · I) / (τ_load · ω)   ... (2)

For an ideal device (no resistive losses in disk):
  EMF = (1/2) · ω · B_z · R²     (equation from overall_Math.mdx)
  τ_load = (1/2) · I · B_z · R²  (back-torque from current in field)
  P_electrical = EMF · I = (1/2) · ω · B_z · R² · I
  P_mechanical = τ_load · ω = (1/2) · I · B_z · R² · ω
  → η = 1 (ideal)                                       ... (3)

In practice, η < 1 due to:
  - Brush resistance R_brush (I²·R_brush losses)
  - Disk resistance R_disk
  - Eddy current losses in the surrounding structure

The galinstan fluid demonstration Drew is preparing uses the liquid metal as a
resistance-minimized rotating conductor (no solid brushes needed if the current is
collected in a fluid channel), potentially allowing higher efficiency than conventional
metal-disk homopolar generators.

**Energy-from-spin insight**: If the "spinning magnet on a pole" experiment (tweet
1912464896169767391) is indeed showing an anomalous upward force beyond ∇(m·B), then
the energy budget of that system would also be anomalous: the work done against gravity
as the magnet rises (F_z · Δz) would exceed the work input by whatever is spinning the
magnet. Such a device would not conserve energy in the standard sense. Drew does not
explicitly claim over-unity energy generation in this batch of tweets, but the "NEW
PHYSICS" framing of the spinning pole experiment implies that the energy accounting is
not understood with current physical models.

### Spin-Waves in Gaseous Coma: Physical Phenomenon Identification

Drew defends the interpretation of "spin-waves seen within a large gaseous coma" as
genuine rotational dynamics, not atmospheric distortion. Spin waves in a gaseous medium
are propagating disturbances in the angular momentum density field of the gas:

  ρ · ∂v_φ/∂t + ρ · (v · ∇)v_φ = -∂P/∂φ / r + F_φ_external   ... (5)

where v_φ is the azimuthal velocity component, ρ is gas density, P is pressure, and
F_φ_external is the external force (gravity, radiation pressure from the Sun, etc.).

Genuine spin waves (as opposed to atmospheric refraction artifacts) would manifest as
coherent azimuthal velocity patterns propagating outward from the nucleus. The
distinguishing feature is spatial coherence: genuine spin waves would show consistent
phase relationships across the coma, while atmospheric seeing distortion would produce
incoherent random smearing.

Drew argues this is a real spin wave, not atmospheric artifact — relevant to establishing
the energy mechanism of the object.
