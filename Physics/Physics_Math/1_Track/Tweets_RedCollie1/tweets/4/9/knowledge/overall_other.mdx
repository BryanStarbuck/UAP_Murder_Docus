### 4-Symbol Alien Computing vs. Human 2-Symbol Computing

Drew references "Rodney" who has "reverse-engineered 4-symbol alien computing." The
claimed analog: "Their computing is as far ahead of ours, as AC electrical power is
ahead of DC." He references Tesla's electrification of Niagara Falls as the parallel
technological leap.

**Technical content**: Standard computing is binary (2-symbol: 0, 1). A 4-symbol base
(base-4 or quaternary) system uses symbols {0, 1, 2, 3}. A quaternary digit (quad)
carries log₂(4) = 2 bits of information, vs. 1 bit per binary digit. This doubles
information density per symbol, but does not inherently change computational complexity
classes (any finite alphabet Turing machine is computationally equivalent to a binary
one). The claimed advantage over binary (labeled "as far ahead as AC over DC") must
therefore refer to something other than information-theoretic capacity — perhaps to
physical implementation efficiency (fewer logic gates for the same operation count) or
to a novel computing paradigm (e.g., base-4 quantum computing using qudit systems with
d=4, which has real advantages in certain quantum error correction schemes).

The "Encryption Chain Reaction" referenced is not explained in the tweet text.
