For a precessing dipole, the time-averaged ⟨m⟩ = m ẑ (only the component along the precession axis survives time-averaging, because the transverse components average to zero over a precession period). Thus:

    ⟨F⟩ = ∂/∂z [m_z B_z] ẑ = m_z (∂B_z/∂z) ẑ
