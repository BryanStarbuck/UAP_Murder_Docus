## Section: 1912530735082402161.yaml — 3-Phase Rotating Field Mathematics

**Three-phase current system:**

    I_a(t) = I₀ cos(ωt)
    I_b(t) = I₀ cos(ωt − 2π/3)
    I_c(t) = I₀ cos(ωt − 4π/3)

    I_a + I_b + I_c = 0  (for all t)

This identity is fundamental to balanced 3-phase systems.

**Rotating field vector resultant:**

    B(t) = B₀[cos(ωt) x̂ + cos(ωt − 2π/3)(−1/2 x̂ + √3/2 ŷ) + cos(ωt − 4π/3)(−1/2 x̂ − √3/2 ŷ)]

Expanding and collecting x̂ and ŷ components using the identity cos(α + β) = cos α cos β − sin α sin β:

    B_x(t) = B₀[cos(ωt) − (1/2)cos(ωt − 2π/3) − (1/2)cos(ωt − 4π/3)]
           = B₀[cos(ωt) − (1/2)(cos ωt cos 2π/3 + sin ωt sin 2π/3) − (1/2)(cos ωt cos 4π/3 + sin ωt sin 4π/3)]
           = B₀[(3/2) cos(ωt)]

    B_y(t) = B₀[(√3/2)cos(ωt − 2π/3) − (√3/2)cos(ωt − 4π/3)]
           = B₀[(3/2) sin(ωt)]

Therefore:

    B(t) = (3B₀/2)[cos(ωt) x̂ + sin(ωt) ŷ]

**Key result:** |B(t)| = 3B₀/2 = constant. The field rotates at angular frequency ω with constant amplitude. This is the standard result for a balanced 3-phase rotating magnetic field.

**Implication for compass deflection at distance r:**
