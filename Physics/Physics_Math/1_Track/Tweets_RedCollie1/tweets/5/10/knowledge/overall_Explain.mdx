## Section: 2026678149858341019.yaml
### Source: AC Lenz Levitation — Identification of Known Physical Mechanism

Drew explicitly identifies the mechanism in a referenced image as "simple AC Lenz levitation." This is a known electromagnetic levitation effect:
- An alternating current (AC) creates a time-varying magnetic field.
- This time-varying field induces eddy currents in a nearby conductor (e.g., aluminum plate).
- The induced currents create their own magnetic field opposing the applied field (Lenz's law: ε = -dΦ_B/dt).
- The opposing fields produce a repulsive force, lifting the conductor.

**Bridging function:** Drew distinguishes "simple AC Lenz levitation" (known physics, no exotic claims) from his UFO levitation target — implying the UFO levitation involves additional physics beyond simple Lenz induction.
