### Source: Homopolar (Unipolar) Motor — Lorentz Force and Electron Spin

**Teaching exercise framing:** Drew poses the question: "What is a Lorentz force? How does that relate to a subatomic force of electron SPIN?"

**Homopolar motor physics:**
- Device: two batteries, two permanent magnets, arranged on conducting arms.
- Current from batteries flows between the two magnets through a wire.
- The Lorentz force F = q(v × B) acts on the current-carrying conductor in the magnetic field, producing torque → rotation.

**Two-magnet configurations and rotation outcomes (experimental results):**

Configuration A — Magnets in OPPOSITE orientations (N-S, S-N):
- Current path: between the two magnets through the wire.
- Result: both magnets spin in the SAME direction.
- Physical reason: The opposing magnet polarity arrangement reverses the sense of B at the second magnet location, compensating for the reversed current geometry, so the Lorentz force torque direction is the same at both magnets.

Configuration B — Magnets in SAME orientation (both N-S or both S-N):
- Result: the two magnets spin in OPPOSITE directions.
- Physical reason: Identical B-field polarity at both locations, same current direction → same force sense → counter-torque between the two.

**Momentum persistence:** When power is removed, the magnets continue spinning by mechanical momentum (angular momentum conservation), decaying due to friction.

**Historical reference:** Drew explicitly attributes the first demonstration of this two-magnet configuration to Nikola Tesla, approximately 120 years prior (c. 1905).

**Relation to UFO physics — electron SPIN coupling:**
- Drew's question "How does that relate to a subatomic force of electron SPIN?" implies that the Lorentz force at the macroscopic level (F = qv × B) is physically connected to the electron magnetic moment μ_s = -g_s μ_B S/ℏ, where g_s ≈ 2.002, μ_B is the Bohr magneton, and S is the electron spin operator.
- The macroscopic rotation of the homopolar motor is, in Drew's framework, a scaled-up manifestation of spin-magnetic coupling at the quantum level.
- This bridges classical electrodynamics (Maxwell / Lorentz) to quantum spin physics and, in Drew's hypothesis, to the propulsion mechanism of UAPs.

**Device geometry (from 13_1926609637857538048_preview.txt):**
