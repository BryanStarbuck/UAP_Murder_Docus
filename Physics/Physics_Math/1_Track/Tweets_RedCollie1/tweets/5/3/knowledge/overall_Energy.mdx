**Energy accounting anomaly:**

Where does the 240 W go? In a standard motor stalled against a stop, it goes entirely into resistive heating (I²R losses). If the 1 kg of force is genuinely a net useful output beyond the joule heating, the source of that mechanical potential energy is anomalous from a standard first-law standpoint unless the field interaction is extracting energy from the precessional spin of the magnet (which was imparted from the input power) or from some other source.
