**P_output = η · P_input** where η ≤ 1

**Maximum power transfer theorem**: Maximum power is transferred from source (impedance Z_s) to load (impedance Z_L) when Z_L = Z_s* (complex conjugate of source impedance).

In Drew's context: the small input signal (possibly a reference frequency oscillator) is impedance-matched to the large 3-phase drive circuit (power amplifier stage). The power stage delivers large current/voltage that **matches the waveform** of the small input — same frequency, same phase, much larger amplitude.

**Thermodynamic framing**: The lift energy comes from the electrical power supply (the 3-phase coils). There is no claimed over-unity (energy gain > input). The device's efficiency is:

**η = (M_device · g · v_z) / P_electrical**

where v_z is the upward velocity of the lifted mass. Drew's claim is that this efficiency can be high because the reaction partner (the vacuum) provides effectively zero resistance to the downward force, making the "lifting" process energetically efficient.
