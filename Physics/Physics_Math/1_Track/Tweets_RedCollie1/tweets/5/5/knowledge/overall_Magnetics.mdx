## Section: 1962688582201839721.yaml — Lorentz Force in Spinning Conducting Medium

**Lorentz force** applied to a spinning conducting or magnetized element:

**F = q(v × B)**

For a spinning conducting disc or liquid-metal vortex rotating at angular velocity Ω in an applied magnetic field B:
- Each volume element dV of the conductor carries charge dq moving at velocity v = Ω × r
- The Lorentz force on each element: dF = dq(v × B) = dq(Ω × r) × B

This produces a torque distribution within the conducting medium that sustains its spin and, under the right geometric conditions, generates a net body force.

**Wobble-spin superposition**: When a 3-phase AC field is applied to the already-spinning element, the driving field oscillates at frequency ω while the element spins at Ω. The interaction produces a precessional torque:
- If ω ≈ Ω (resonant condition), the element enters a gyroscopic precession mode
- The spin axis itself rotates, mimicking a "pitched propellor" geometry

**The flat-spin vs. wobble-spin magnetic distinction** (from Drew's note_tweet):
- Flat spin (disc in XY-plane): The rotating magnetic field configuration is symmetric about the Z-axis → field topology is stationary in the lab frame → zero net Lenz opposition from vacuum.
- Wobble spin (precessing axis): The magnetic field topology at the spinning element changes continuously with respect to the surrounding space → induced EMF in the vacuum (or vacuum-equivalent medium) → opposing force per Faraday's Law:

**∇ × E = -∂B/∂t**

The time-varying B at the spinning-precessing element generates a back-reaction field E, which in turn generates a restoring/opposing force. Drew's claim is that this opposition is the medium against which the device generates thrust.

## Section: 2036366795591000267.yaml — Counter-Spin Magnetic Configuration and Newton's Third Law Cancellation

**Six coil ring wired as U-V-W-U-V-W**:

This is a three-phase winding with the winding sequence repeated twice around the ring. The U-V-W-U-V-W winding, depending on the reversal of the second U-V-W set relative to the first, can produce:
- **Clockwise rotation at center** (inner pole-pair interaction)
- **Anti-clockwise rotation at outside** (outer pole-pair interaction)

This is analogous to a dual-rotor induction machine where inner and outer rotors counter-rotate.

**Magnetic forces and Newton's Third Law**:

Let F_inner be the torque exerted on the coil ring by the central spinning magnet (reaction is anti-clockwise on the ring).
Let F_outer be the torque exerted on the coil ring by the outer spinning flywheel (reaction is clockwise on the ring).

**If |F_inner| = |F_outer|, then the net torque on the coil ring = 0.**

This cancellation requires careful tuning of the magnetic moment magnitudes:
- Inner magnet: magnetic moment μ_inner
- Outer flywheel: net magnetic moment μ_outer

Condition for zero net reactive torque: **μ_inner × B_coil_inner = μ_outer × B_coil_outer**

When this condition holds, the 6-coil ring experiences zero net angular impulse from either spinning element. All spin kinetic energy is delivered to the spinning elements, and their only remaining reaction partners are external to the device.

**Z-axis force generation**: The spinning magnets (central CW and outer CCW) generate time-varying magnetic fields in the vacuum surrounding them. The vacuum's response (per Lenz/Faraday) opposes this time-varying field, producing a restoring force. In the configuration Drew describes, the geometry of counter-rotation concentrates this vacuum reaction force **downward** (below the device), which by Newton's Third Law pushes the device **upward**.
