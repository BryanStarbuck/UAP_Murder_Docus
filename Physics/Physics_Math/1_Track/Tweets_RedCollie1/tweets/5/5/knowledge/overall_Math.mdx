## Section: 1956260439614218473.yaml — Gyroscope Dynamics Equations

**Standard gyroscope precession**:

Angular momentum of spinning gyroscope: **L = I_spin · ω_s ê_s** (where ê_s is the unit vector along spin axis)

Gravitational torque: **τ = r_pivot × (Mg ẑ) = Mg·r·(ê_r × ẑ)**

where r_pivot is the distance from pivot to CM, ê_r is the horizontal unit vector from pivot to CM.

Precession equation: **dL/dt = τ**

Precession angular velocity: **Ω_p = τ/L_⊥ = Mgr / (I_spin · ω_s)**

Note: Ω_p decreases as ω_s increases (faster spin → slower precession at fixed gravity).

**Drew's Q2 quantification**:

The experimentally observed relation (from Drew's description) is:
- At low Ω_p: no lifting capacity → effective weight W_eff ≈ W_static = Mg
- At high Ω_p: can lift "quite large weights" → W_eff < W_static

**Proposed functional form** (consistent with Drew's description):
**W_eff(Ω_p) = Mg · g(Ω_p)**

where g(0) = 1 (no lift at zero precession) and g(Ω_p) → 0 as Ω_p → ∞ (full weight cancellation at infinite precession).

Drew does not provide the explicit functional form; determining g(Ω_p) is the experimental program. The simplest hypothesis consistent with dimensional analysis:
**W_eff = Mg / (1 + α·Ω_p²)**

where α is a parameter with dimensions [s²] depending on the moment of inertia, spin speed, and magnetic moment of the gyroscope.

**At high Ω_p**, the device can lift weights M_lift where:
**M_lift ≤ (M_gyro · α · Ω_p²) · g**

This implies **lift capacity scales as Ω_p²** — consistent with the observed strong frequency dependence (28 Hz >> 25 Hz in terms of felt lift force).

## Section: 2036366795591000267.yaml — Counter-Spin Newton's Third Law Cancellation: Formal

**Coil ring dynamics** with two counter-rotating magnetic loads:

Let angular momenta of inner (CW) and outer (CCW) flywheels be:
- **L_inner = +I_inner · ω_inner ẑ** (positive = clockwise about +Z)
- **L_outer = -I_outer · ω_outer ẑ** (negative = anti-clockwise about +Z)

The 6-coil ring (rotor) must supply the electromagnetic torques:
- **τ_on_inner = +dL_inner/dt** (accelerating the inner flywheel CW)
- **τ_on_outer = -dL_outer/dt** (accelerating the outer flywheel CCW)

By Newton's Third Law, the flywheels exert reaction torques on the coil ring:
- **τ_reaction_from_inner = -dL_inner/dt** (CCW torque on ring)
- **τ_reaction_from_outer = +dL_outer/dt** (CW torque on ring)

**Net torque on coil ring**:
**τ_net_ring = -dL_inner/dt + dL_outer/dt = d(L_outer - L_inner)/dt**

**Cancellation condition** (zero net torque on ring):
**d(L_outer - L_inner)/dt = 0**

i.e., **L_outer = L_inner + constant**

**At steady state** (both flywheels at terminal velocity, no acceleration):
**dL_inner/dt = 0, dL_outer/dt = 0 → τ_net_ring = 0**

In steady-state operation, the coil ring experiences zero net reactive torque. This is the mathematically exact condition under which the Newton's Third Law cancellation holds.

## Section: 2030520627535528156.yaml — 16-Magnet Ring Configuration: Torque Spectrum

**Configuration**: 1 central magnet + ring of 16 equally spaced outer magnets.

**Azimuthal spacing of outer magnets**: Δφ = 2π/16 = π/8 = 22.5°

**Magnetic interaction energy** between central dipole (moment μ_c, at origin) and each outer dipole (moment μ_k, at radius R, angle φ_k):

**U_k = (μ_0/4πR³)[μ_c · μ_k - 3(μ_c · R̂_k)(μ_k · R̂_k)]**

For identically oriented dipoles (both pointing along Z):
**U_k = (μ_0/4πR³)[μ_c · μ_k · (1 - 3cos²θ_k)]**

where θ_k is the polar angle between Z and R̂_k (= 90° for ring in horizontal plane → cos θ_k = 0):
**U_k = (μ_0/4πR³) · μ_c · μ_k** (for horizontal ring)

Total interaction energy (16 magnets):
**U_total = 16 · (μ_0/4πR³) · μ_c · μ_k**

This is purely repulsive or attractive (fixed sign) for aligned dipoles — no torque.

**For counter-rotating counter-aligned dipoles**: As central magnet spins, its moment direction rotates in the XY-plane. The instantaneous torque on each outer magnet varies sinusoidally as:
**τ_k(t) = (3μ_0/4πR³) · μ_c · μ_k · sin(Ω_c t - φ_k)**

The sum of torques from all 16 outer magnets:
**τ_total(t) = Σ_k (3μ_0/4πR³) · μ_c · μ_k · sin(Ω_c t - k·π/8)**

This is a Fourier series; the dominant term is at frequency Ω_c (fundamental). The 16-fold symmetry means that all harmonics below 16th order cancel except the fundamental — giving a nearly perfect sinusoidal drive torque on the outer ring at the spinning frequency Ω_c. This provides the cleanest possible force coupling between the central and outer spinning elements.
