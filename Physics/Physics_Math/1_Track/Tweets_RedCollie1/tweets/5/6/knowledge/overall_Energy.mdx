### Anomalous Power Balance in 3-Phase Rising Force Device

**Input:** 250 W at 17 Hz
**Output force:** 9.81 N upward

**Energy analysis:**
If the 9.81 N upward force lifts a 1 kg mass at velocity v, the output power is:
P_out = F × v = 9.81 × v [W]

For the device to be at unity energy balance: v = P_in / F = 250 / 9.81 = 25.5 m/s

This means the 250 W input is sufficient to accelerate a 1 kg object upward at 25.5 m/s indefinitely — only if all input power is converted to work against gravity. If the force is static (holding a mass against gravity without moving), the output work is zero and the 250 W is entirely dissipated as heat/losses. In that case, the energy balance is normal.

**The energy anomaly question:**
Drew does not explicitly claim overunity (more output energy than input). The claim is that the **existence** of the rising force at all is "not in textbooks" — the anomaly is the phenomenon's existence, not its efficiency.

However, if this device could be coupled into a self-sustaining loop (Drew's proposed next step: "couple the rising force to the 3-phase wire coils that generate a rotating magnetic field, causing it to SPIN"), then:
- The rising force could drive the device upward
- The upward motion relative to the external field would change the coupling
- No claim is made about energy generation — the device consumes 250 W from an external source

**Assessment:** This is a propulsion energy system, not a free-energy device. Energy input is 250 W; output is mechanical force. The anomaly is the directionality (XY input → Z output) not the energy magnitude.

### Mercury Vimana Energy Source — Historical Claims

Ancient Indian texts (Vaimānika Shāstra and Mahābhārata) describe vimanas powered by liquid mercury. Drew's interest is in whether a physically plausible mechanism exists by which mercury serves as both an energy-transducing medium and a propulsion element.

**AC jumping ring energy analysis:**
Input: AC power to coil (P = I² R_coil for resistive heating + stored magnetic energy)
Output: Upward kinetic energy of mercury / force on mercury

The AC levitation mechanism is energy-consuming: the coil must continuously supply reactive power to maintain the alternating field. For electromagnetic levitation of a 1 kg mercury sample:
- Weight: F = mg = 9.81 N
- Required field gradient: F = m (∂B/∂z) × χ_induced ≈ (eddy current force)
- Power required is not anomalous — this is standard induction furnace / levitation melting physics

**DC spinning mercury energy analysis:**
Input: DC power + field from permanent magnet (magnet requires no ongoing power)
Output: Spinning mercury (kinetic energy of rotation)

In the homopolar motor regime: the permanent magnet supplies no energy (a static field does no work on charges; the power comes entirely from the DC source). Therefore:
P_input = I × V (DC electrical input)
P_output = τ × ω (rotational kinetic energy rate + viscous losses)

No anomalous energy behavior in the homopolar motor itself. The anomaly, if real, is in the CONVERSION of spinning mercury kinetic energy to vertical lift — not in the motor's energy balance.

### Zero-Energy Geodesic — Gravity Cancellation Without Energy Input

The most energy-relevant concept in Drew's framework:

**"The outer part of that gyroscope follows a zero-energy geodesic near its fast-spinning disc, where no 'force' of any kind is needed to make it precess in a broad circle."**

**Standard physics context:**
In GR, objects in free fall (no external force) follow geodesics. Following a geodesic costs zero energy — orbital motion is "free" in this sense. The Earth in orbit around the Sun is on a geodesic; no energy is expended to maintain the orbit.

**Drew's extension:**
If a fast-spinning disc creates a region of modified spacetime where the geodesics are altered, then an object following these modified geodesics requires no energy input. The craft would hover "for free" — only the spinning disc's rotation must be maintained, and that only against bearing friction and air drag (arbitrarily small in principle).

**Energy implications:**
- Maintaining orbit (following geodesic): 0 energy
- Maintaining spin of the geodesic-modifying disc: P = I_disc × ω² × (friction coefficient) / (Q-factor of bearing)
- In principle, for a perfectly frictionless bearing in vacuum: energy cost → 0
- In practice: energy cost is far less than the gravitational potential energy that would otherwise be needed to maintain altitude

**This represents a qualitatively different class of energy requirement:** not "how much energy to lift?" but "how much energy to maintain the field that modifies the geodesic?" The latter could be arbitrarily small if the mechanism is real and the geometry is optimized.

### Liquid Metal as Reactive Mass — Angular Momentum as Energy Sink

Drew's proposed solution to Newton's 3rd Law involves the liquid metal spinning system absorbing momentum. This is an energy redistribution, not energy generation:

**Energy flow in the complete system:**
1. External electrical energy → 3-phase coils → rotating magnetic field
2. Rotating magnetic field → spin of ring magnet + upward force on magnet
3. Upward force on magnet → mechanical potential energy gain (if magnet rises)
4. External electrical energy → DC electrode circuit → Lorentz force on liquid metal → liquid metal angular momentum
5. Liquid metal angular momentum → absorbs Newton 3rd downward impulse → no downward force on frame

**Conservation accounting:**
Total energy input = 3-phase coil input + DC electrode input
Total energy output = magnet potential energy gain + heat (losses in coils, liquid metal, friction)

No overunity claim is explicit. The claim is that the DIRECTION of energy transfer (upward levitation) is achieved without the conventional reaction (downward thrust on the surrounding medium), which appears to violate Newton's 3rd Law unless the liquid metal's angular momentum serves as the reaction system.

**Whether this actually works as a momentum sink:**
Angular momentum L = I_liq ω_liq is an axial vector (points along Z). Linear momentum p = m v is a polar vector. Converting between these requires a coupling mechanism. In standard classical mechanics, angular momentum and linear momentum are independently conserved — you cannot transfer linear momentum (Z direction) to angular momentum (θ direction) without an external torque or force coupling them.

This is the fundamental unresolved physics in Drew's model.

### Non-Classical Motor — Possible Self-Organized Energy State

**Counter-rotation in the 9-coil / 12-magnet configuration:**
The rotor turns opposite to the rotating field. In standard motor theory, this represents operation in the braking/plugging regime — the motor would be absorbing energy at double the normal rate (from both supply and from whatever mechanical energy was pre-existing).

For the rotor to sustain counter-rotation without decelerating, the motor must reach a steady-state where the electromagnetic torque in the counter-rotation direction balances the resistive (friction) torque. This would require the fractional winding harmonics to produce a stronger backward torque than the forward fundamental torque.

**Energy implications:**
In standard plugging mode: P_electrical = P_mechanical + P_heat (energy consumed from supply is large)
In Drew's device (counter-rotation sustained with low input): energy balance is unclear.

If the device is efficiently counter-rotating at low power input, the energy source could be:
(a) The permanent magnets' stored magnetostatic energy (magnets gradually demagnetizing)
(b) The asymmetric coupling between the fractional winding sub-harmonics converting some field energy more efficiently
(c) An anomalous energy input from an unidentified source

No explicit energy measurement is provided by Drew for this device beyond the qualitative observation of counter-rotation.
