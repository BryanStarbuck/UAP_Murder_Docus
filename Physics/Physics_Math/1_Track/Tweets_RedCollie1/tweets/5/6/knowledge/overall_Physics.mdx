**Hypothesis 2 — Faraday Homopolar Spinning Mercury (DC excitation):**

The Lorentz force on current-carrying mercury is F = J × B, where J is the radial current density and B is the axial magnetic field. Drew's claim: the spinning mercury then "lifts up like a UFO," though the mechanism for converting rotational motion to Z-axis lift is not specified by Drew and represents the unsolved part of this hypothesis.

**Relation to Faraday's original motor:**

- Mounted on central axis/bearing inside a stainless steel bowl

**Operating principle (standard homopolar motor):**

**Copper bus bars:** Provide low-resistance current paths to the disc rim and center shaft. Blue cable supplies DC power.

**Relevance to Drew's work:** The homopolar motor is Faraday's original 1821 design.
