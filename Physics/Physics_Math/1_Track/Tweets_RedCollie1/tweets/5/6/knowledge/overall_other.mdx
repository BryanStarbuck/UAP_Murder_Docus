The sustained metallic nickel → nickel-water complex signal in 3I/ATLAS implies an artificial composition or active mechanism releasing nickel-containing materials.

**Relevance:** This is observational chemistry evidence for the non-cometary nature of 3I/ATLAS, consistent with Drew's thesis that it is an artificial (non-natural) object.
