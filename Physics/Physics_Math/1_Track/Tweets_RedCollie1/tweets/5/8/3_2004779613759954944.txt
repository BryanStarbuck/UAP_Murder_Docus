PHYSICS CONCEPT:
This diagram illustrates the Faraday homopolar disk generator (also called a unipolar generator or acyclic generator):
- A conducting disk rotates in a uniform axial magnetic field (B into the page)
- Each radial element cuts magnetic flux lines, generating an EMF by the motional EMF equation: dε = (v × B)·dl
- The total EMF is ε = ωBR²/2 where ω is angular velocity and R is disk radius
- Current flows radially (center to rim or vice versa), producing DC without commutator
- The homopolar generator is the simplest electromagnetic induction device
