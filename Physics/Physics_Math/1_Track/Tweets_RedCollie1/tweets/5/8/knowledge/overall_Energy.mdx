## Section: 1952498229557530678.yaml — 3I/ATLAS Anomalous Behavior: No Tail

Drew: "3I/2025 has no 'tail' like for an ordinary 'comet'."

**Physical significance for energy assessment**:

A conventional comet produces a tail through sublimation of ices (H₂O, CO₂, CO) as it approaches the Sun — the solar radiation pressure pushes sublimated gas and entrained dust into the anti-solar direction, producing the characteristic tail.

The absence of a tail from 3I/ATLAS implies:
1. Either the material is not sublimating in the conventional manner
2. Or the gas/dust is being directed (e.g., by internal propulsion) rather than pushed by solar radiation pressure

**Avi Loeb's data**: "No visible tail and no gas fingerprints" — consistent with an object that is either not outgassing or is controlling its outgassing direction. Drew interprets this as evidence the object is propulsion-active and managing its own energy balance.
