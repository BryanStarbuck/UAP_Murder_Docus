PHYSICS/SCIENCE RELEVANCE: This photograph is related to the "Nazca mummies" or "Nazca alien hands" controversy that emerged around 2017-2019. Various Peruvian specimens were presented by researchers including Jaime Maussan claiming they represent non-human biological entities. The three-fingered tridactyl structure is the key anomalous feature. Critics have noted these specimens show characteristics consistent with human bones modified or arranged artificially.
