PHYSICS/SCIENCE RELEVANCE: This crop circle formation is famous for its resemblance to a DNA double helix structure when the central crossing arms are interpreted as two intertwined strands with hydrogen-bond "rungs" at the crossing points. This specific formation type has appeared multiple times in English fields (notably at East Field, Alton Barnes, Wiltshire area). Researchers have interpreted the small circles as nucleotide base pairs or hydrogen bonds. The formation has been cited in UAP literature as evidence that non-human intelligences are communicating information about genetic biology, potentially indicating awareness of human DNA structure or suggesting modification of human genetics. The DNA helix motif in crop circles is one of the most frequently discussed formation types in UAP genetic engineering hypotheses.
