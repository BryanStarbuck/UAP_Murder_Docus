### Anomalous Weight Reduction — Possible Free Energy Connection

The Laithwaite anomaly (weight reduction felt locally but not measured at scale) raises an energy conservation question:

If the person holds the spinning device and it feels lighter by delta_W, the person performs less work lifting it through height h:
W_felt = (M*g - delta_W) * h < M*g*h = W_classical

Yet the device arrives at height h with the same potential energy (since M is unchanged):
PE = M * g * h (unchanged, as mass M has not changed)

The deficit: W_felt < PE_gained.

Where does the extra energy come from? In the Laithwaite framework:
* Rotational kinetic energy of the spinning gyroscope could be partially converted to gravitational potential energy.
* In steady-state operation (constant spin rate maintained by a motor), the motor supplies this additional energy.
* But if the weight reduction exceeds what could be accounted for by rotational KE conversion, it implies an external energy input — potentially from the ZPE field via the rotating magnetic/gyroscopic coupling.
