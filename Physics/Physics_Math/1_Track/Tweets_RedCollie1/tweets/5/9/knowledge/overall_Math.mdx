### Corbino Disc / Homopolar Motor — Exact Torque Formula

Homopolar motor torque for a conducting disc of radius R in axial field B_z with total radial current I:

tau = (1/2) * I * B_z * R^2

This follows from integrating the Lorentz force over the disc:
d_tau = r * (J_r * B_z) * dA = r * (I / (pi * R^2)) * B_z * (2 * pi * r * dr)

Integrating from r=0 to r=R:
tau = integral_0^R r * (I / (pi * R^2)) * B_z * 2 * pi * r * dr
    = (2 * I * B_z / R^2) * integral_0^R r^2 dr
    = (2 * I * B_z / R^2) * (R^3 / 3)
    = (2/3) * I * B_z * R

[Note: the (1/2) * I * B_z * R^2 formula assumes uniform field; exact result depends on current distribution. The above derivation gives (2/3)*I*B_z*R for uniform J_r. The factor depends on geometry.]

Angular velocity of the Corbino disc:
omega_Corbino = tau / (I_moment * Gamma)

where I_moment is moment of inertia of the disc and Gamma is a damping coefficient. At steady state (d_omega/dt = 0):
tau_drive = tau_drag => steady-state RPM is achieved.

For liquid metal implementation, the drag is viscous:

where eta_l is the dynamic viscosity of the liquid metal.
