TYPE: Black and white diagram — crop circle schematic or molecular/atomic structure diagram

CONTENT:
- Black line art on white background
- A geometric pattern with 4-fold (quasi-rhombal/diamond) symmetry
- Overall shape: a diamond/rhombus outline filled with internal elements

STRUCTURAL ELEMENTS:

Vertical central axis:
- A straight vertical line running through the exact center of the pattern from top to bottom
- Multiple solid black circles arranged along this vertical axis (approximately 9 circles, varying in size)
- The circles are largest near the center and smallest at the very top and bottom

Horizontal elements:
- Multiple horizontal bars (solid lines) radiating outward from the vertical axis
- Each bar has circles at its endpoints
- The bars decrease in length from center toward top and bottom (creating the diamond outline)
- Approximately 9-11 horizontal bars per side (stacked vertically)

Central element:
- A special feature at approximately the center — two semicircle outlines forming an eye or vesica shape, bisected by the vertical axis (horizontal striped lines through this oval shape)

Circle sizes:
- Sizes vary: the circle at the very center (intersection point) is largest of the junction circles
- Endpoint circles on bars are approximately 1.5-2x the size of junction circles

SHAPE ANALOGY:
The overall form strongly resembles a DNA double helix viewed in 2D schematic (rungs of the ladder as horizontal bars, backbone phosphate groups as circles on the axis and ends), or alternatively a crystallographic structure, or a simplified atomic orbital diagram. The central oval/eye could represent an atomic nucleus or a cross-section of a molecular structure.

COLORS:
- Black and white only

IMAGE DIMENSIONS: Approximately 960 x 960 pixels, square

CONTEXT:
This diagram closely resembles the classic "DNA strand" crop circle formation that appeared at East Field, Wiltshire, UK in 1996. That formation was widely interpreted as depicting a double helix DNA structure from above. It is frequently cited in crop circle / extraterrestrial contact research.
