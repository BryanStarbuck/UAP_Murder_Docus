### Homopolar Generator EMF Equation

For a conducting disc of radius r rotating at angular velocity ω in an axial magnetic flux density B:

  V_emf = (1/2) × ω × B × r²

**Derivation:** A radial conducting element at radial position ρ (0 ≤ ρ ≤ r) moves with tangential velocity v(ρ) = ω × ρ. The motional EMF across an infinitesimal radial element dρ is:
  dV = (v × B) · dr = ω × ρ × B × dρ

Integrating from hub (ρ = 0) to rim (ρ = r):
  V = ∫₀ʳ ω B ρ dρ = ω B [ρ²/2]₀ʳ = (1/2) ω B r²

**Variable identification:**
- V: terminal EMF (Volts)
- ω: angular velocity of disc (rad/s)
- B: axial magnetic flux density (Tesla)
- r: disc radius (meters)

**For two counter-rotating discs (Norman Paulsen drive):**
If disc 1 rotates at +ω₁ and disc 2 at -ω₂ (opposite sense), and brushes are placed between the outer rims of both discs, the relative angular velocity governing the inter-disc EMF is:
  ω_rel = ω₁ + ω₂

Giving enhanced inter-disc EMF:
  V_total = (1/2) × (ω₁ + ω₂) × B_eff × r²

where B_eff accounts for the combined magnetic flux from both rotating magnet systems.

### Angular Momentum Balance for Counter-Rotating Dual-Ring System

**Angular momentum of each ring:**
  L_k = I_k × ω_k

where I_k is the moment of inertia of ring k and ω_k is its angular velocity (scalar for rotation about common axis).

**For inner ring (co-rotating with field, positive ω):**
  I_inner = M_inner × r_inner² / 2    (thin ring approximation)
  L_inner = I_inner × ω_inner > 0

**For outer ring (counter-rotating against field, negative ω):**
  I_outer = M_outer × r_outer² / 2
  L_outer = -I_outer × ω_outer < 0   (negative sign indicates opposite direction)

**Zero net angular momentum condition:**
  L_total = L_inner - I_outer × ω_outer = 0
  ⟹ I_inner × ω_inner = I_outer × ω_outer
  ⟹ (M_inner × r_inner²) × ω_inner = (M_outer × r_outer²) × ω_outer

**Design constraint:** To achieve L_total = 0 with both rings spinning at the same angular velocity (ω_inner = ω_outer = ω), requires:
  M_inner × r_inner² = M_outer × r_outer²

i.e., the mass and radius of each ring must be chosen to match their moments of inertia.

**If ω_inner ≠ ω_outer (different pole counts select different synchronous speeds):**
The ratio of angular velocities is determined by the pole pair count p:
  ω_sync = 2πf / p
  
For stator frequency f, inner ring with pole pairs p_inner, outer ring with pole pairs p_outer:
  ω_inner = 2πf / p_inner
  ω_outer = 2πf / p_outer

Zero net angular momentum then requires:
  I_inner / p_inner = I_outer / p_outer
  ⟹ M_inner × r_inner² × p_outer = M_outer × r_outer² × p_inner
