**Lenz levitation baseline (classical):**

These eddy currents produce their own magnetic field opposing the change in flux (Lenz's Law). The interaction between the applied field and the eddy current field produces a repulsive force:
  F_Lenz = -d/dt [flux through disc] × (eddy current coupling factor)

For a disc of conductivity σ, thickness δ, radius r, in an AC field of frequency f and amplitude B₀:
  J_eddy ~ σ × dB/dt ~ σ × 2πf × B₀ × cos(2πft)

The time-averaged vertical force (levitation force) is proportional to f, σ, δ, and B₀².

- Modification of the effective conductivity tensor by rotation (magneto-rotational coupling)
- Interaction between the orbital magnetic moment of the eddy current loops and the spin angular momentum of the disc
