This is the key experimental setup frame for a Lenz's law or eddy current demonstration:
- A spinning copper or aluminum disc rotating above a stationary permanent magnet
- When a conducting non-magnetic disc spins above a permanent magnet, eddy currents are induced in the disc, creating a magnetic braking force (Lenz's law)
- Conversely, a rotating permanent magnet above a stationary conductor induces eddy currents in the conductor
- Some experimenters claim that specific geometries or rotation rates produce net upward thrust via asymmetric eddy current distribution
