## 1. Counter-Rotation from Single Rotating Field — Claimed Non-Classical Result

### Source
Tweet (id 2026879995961028857, 2026-02-26):
"It is truly amazing that a single, 6-coil, right-handed, rotating magnetic field can make an outer flywheel spin anticlockwise, and an inner magnet spin clockwise, at the very same time! This violates EVERYTHING they teach in school and peer-reviewed journals."

### Status: Claimed Experimental Result
This is presented as a laboratory observation, not just a theoretical proposal. The observation is:
- Input: single 6-coil stator driven by (implied) 3-phase AC at 40-50 Hz
- Output: outer flywheel rotates CCW, inner magnet rotates CW simultaneously
- Speed: both at 40-50 Hz (inferred from "spin's immediately, 40, 50 hertz" in transcription)

### Classical Physics Prediction vs. Claimed Result
Standard induction motor theory predicts:
- A rotor inside a stator rotates in the direction of the rotating field (synchronous or near-synchronous with positive slip)
- A conducting ring outside the stator is exposed to fringe flux — it may rotate in any direction depending on field harmonic content

The claim that this "violates everything they teach" implies the experimenter does not accept the harmonic analysis explanation (that backward-rotating 5th harmonic drives the outer ring CW while fundamental drives inner CCW).

### Physical Test to Distinguish
If counter-rotation is driven by the 5th harmonic:
- Outer ring speed should be approximately f/5 relative to the field fundamental, or 8-10 Hz for a 40-50 Hz field
- Inner magnet speed should be near f = 40-50 Hz

If counter-rotation is a genuinely new phenomenon:
- Both elements should spin at the same rate (40-50 Hz) without the 1:5 ratio
- The torque would not be explicable by standard induction equations

The transcription states both reach 40-50 Hz, which is inconsistent with 5th harmonic drive of the outer element (which would predict ~8-10 Hz). This, if accurate, is a non-trivial result that would require explanation beyond standard harmonic analysis.

## 2. Spinning Magnetic Vortex — Inward Pull on Magnet and Ionized Air

### Source
Tweet (id 1913947949807378671, 2025-04-20):
"Both ionized air and a small cube magnet get 'pulled in' by a spinning magnetic 'vortex'. New physics, now PROVEN and CLEARLY VISIBLE."

### Status: Claimed Experimental Result (videos presented, transcription minimal)
Transcription of video (13_1913946853445619712_video.mdx): "4x7 speed" (speed indicator only, no verbal content)
Preview description: spinning copper disc above permanent magnet; rotary tool with disc element in wooden rail apparatus

### Classical Physics Analysis
For a spinning permanent magnet above a conducting ring or nearby magnet:
- Rotating dipole field: B(r, theta, t) = (mu_0/4*pi) * [3(m*r_hat)r_hat - m] / r^3, where m = m_0 * (cos(omega*t) x_hat + sin(omega*t) y_hat) for rotation about z-axis
- Force on a test permanent magnet at position r: F = grad(m_test * B)
- Time-averaged force from rotating dipole on nearby dipole: generally zero in far field for symmetric configurations

For the magnet to be "pulled in" (net centripetal force toward the rotation axis), there must be either:
(a) A gradient of B^2 directed inward (toward the axis) at the test magnet's position — possible near the fringe of the rotating field where B increases toward the pole tip
(b) A phase-locked rotation of the test magnet that creates a static coupling (synchronous attraction), requiring the test magnet to spin in phase with the driving field
(c) An eddy-current-driven magneto-hydrodynamic flow in the ionized air that creates an aerodynamic inflow

The observation of ionized air being pulled in could be explained by (c): if the rotating magnetic field ionizes air locally (unlikely at laboratory field strengths) or if a pre-ionized gas is used, MHD body force (J x B with azimuthal J and axial B) would drive flow inward in the radial direction for the correct sign combination.

### Claimed Beyond-Newton Aspect
The experimenter presents this as "new physics" because the inward pull of a small solid permanent magnet on the spinning vortex cannot be explained by the above classical mechanisms alone (particularly (c) does not apply to a solid magnet). If the solid cube magnet genuinely moves toward the rotating field axis in free space, this would be a non-classical force.

## 3. No Back-Reaction — Newton's Third Law Violation Claim

### Source
Tweet (id 1966040356119797872, note_tweet):
"It seemingly shows no back-reaction by Newton's Third Law."

### Status: Claimed Observation
The context is the galinstan vortex: the liquid metal spins in a container but the container does not spin in the opposite direction (no visible recoil).

### Classical Analysis
In the galinstan experiment with fixed magnet and fixed electrodes:
- The Lorentz force J x B acts on the fluid
- The reaction force acts on the current source (between the electrodes): equal and opposite force on the electrode structure
- The reaction force acts on the magnet: equal and opposite torque on the magnet

Both reaction forces (on electrodes and magnet) are transmitted to the rigid apparatus, which is fixed to the table. The table/floor absorbs the reaction. There is no macroscopic visible recoil because:
(a) The apparatus is physically constrained
(b) The reaction forces cancel within the rigid structure

The claim of "no back-reaction" is therefore misleading in the classical analysis — the back-reaction is present but absorbed by the mechanical constraints of the apparatus. For a free-floating device (not fixed to anything), classical EM theory predicts equal and opposite reactions that would prevent net thrust.

The experimenter's implicit claim: that the proposed 3-phase + vortex system, when operated as a free-floating craft, will continue to produce net upward thrust without back-reaction — i.e., that some interaction with the surrounding space (or field) provides the reaction momentum. This would be beyond classical EM.

Has no gyroscopic coupling to external frame (no precession), but also no net angular momentum to exchange with the environment. By itself, this produces no propulsive force.

The "new physics" claim is that the interaction between the counter-rotating fields (not just the mechanical rotation, but the electromagnetic fields of the counter-rotating elements) produces a net axial force through a mechanism not in the standard EM Lagrangian.     delta_m = (P_dot * E) / (4*pi*G*rho_0*c^2)

    Applied to rotating systems: combined rotation + field oscillation may produce sustained mass fluctuation → net force

The proposed mechanism involves pairing of Cooper pairs in the superconductor creating a gravitomagnetic field opposing Earth's gravity. Counter-rotating discs would produce a doubled effect. No verified replication.

(c) Tajmar Effect: measured anomalous gravitomagnetic field from rotating superconductors (Tajmar et al., 2006, Physica C), with claimed measured magnitude ~1000x GR prediction. Not replicated by other groups to the same magnitude.
