## 1. No-Back-Reaction Claim — Apparent Violation of Newton's Third Law / Conservation of Momentum

### Source
Tweet (id 1966040356119797872, note_tweet):
"The E.T. crop artists have drawn a 'liquid metal' spinning vortex in many places over the years. It seemingly shows no back-reaction by Newton's Third Law."

### Physical Significance
If a device produces a propulsive force without any back-reaction (no ejected mass, no field carrying momentum away from the system), this violates conservation of linear momentum — a foundational consequence of Noether's theorem applied to spatial translation symmetry. In a closed system:

d(p_total)/dt = 0
p_craft + p_exhaust = constant

Without exhaust (no propellant expelled) and without field momentum (no electromagnetic radiation or photon thrust), d(p_craft)/dt would require d(p_environment)/dt = -d(p_craft)/dt, meaning the craft pushes against the surrounding medium. The claim of "no back-reaction" is thus either:
(a) That the surrounding ionized medium absorbs the reaction momentum invisibly (MHD interaction with ambient plasma or ionized air)
(b) That the system genuinely violates conservation of momentum (extraordinary claim requiring extraordinary evidence)
(c) That the experimenter does not observe macroscopic mechanical recoil of the fixed experimental apparatus, which is trivially explained by the apparatus being fixed to the table

The energy input is well-defined: P_input ~ 200-500 W from the DC power supply. If this energy produces a net upward force F, the power required is P = F * v (force times velocity of ascent). For F = 1 N (a small levitation force) and v = 0.01 m/s: P_required = 0.01 W, which is well within the input budget. So no violation of energy conservation is required for the levitation claim — only momentum conservation is challenged.

- Phase A: V_A = V_0 * cos(2*pi*f*t)
- Phase B: V_B = V_0 * cos(2*pi*f*t - 2*pi/3)

where phi = power factor angle (phi = 0 for pure resistive coils).

For the 6-coil (3-phase, 2-pole) stator described:
- 6 coils, 3 phases, 2 coils per phase (one per pole per phase)

This is the conventional electrical input energy. The claim is not that the energy is generated anomalously, but that the energy-to-force conversion is non-classical — the mechanism (counter-spin → Z-force) is proposed as a new transduction path from electrical rotational energy to axial mechanical force.

V_fluid = pi * (0.0025) * 0.01 = 7.85 x 10^-5 m^3
m_fluid = 6440 * 7.85e-5 = 0.505 kg
KE_bulk = (1/2) * 0.505 * 25 = 6.3 J

This is the stored angular kinetic energy in the vortex. When the 3-phase field is applied additionally, the question is whether this stored angular momentum can be redirected into axial momentum — energy transduction from rotational to linear (Z-axis) kinetic energy.
