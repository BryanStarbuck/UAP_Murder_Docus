- Nickel atomic emission lines are in the visible green wavelength range (~520–550 nm)

- If the 3I/ATLAS coma is emitting nickel lines rather than C₂ lines, this indicates a nickel-rich surface — consistent with a nickel-coated artificial object

**Anomaly 3: Object size (5 km) and behavior**
3I/ATLAS is described as 5 km in diameter and nickel-coated — an unusual combination for a natural cometary body. Natural cometary nuclei are typically silicate-rich with some metallic content, but a thick nickel coating is atypical.
