conservation of momentum is maintained.

A "fuel-free" upward force that requires no reaction mass represents a genuine violation
of classical mechanics if:
a) No electromagnetic field momentum is being imparted to the external environment
b) No acoustic/fluid momentum is being transferred to the surrounding medium
c) The device is not drawing momentum from Earth's magnetic field (interaction with
   a large external field is technically "fuel-free" from the device perspective but
   requires an external field to react against)

a profound departure from Newtonian mechanics.
