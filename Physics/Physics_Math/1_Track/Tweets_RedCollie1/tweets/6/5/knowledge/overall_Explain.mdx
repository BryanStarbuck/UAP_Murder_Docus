## Section: Overall Framework — Dr. Drew's Research Program

### The Bridging Claim: From XY-Plane Rotation to Z-Axis Lift

The core experimental and theoretical bridge in Dr. Drew's work is the claim that
rotation in the XY-plane (horizontal spinning of a magnet or mechanical rotor) produces
a net force in the Z-axis (vertical direction). This coupling of XY dynamics to Z-axis
output is the essential "bridge" that makes the proposed propulsion mechanism work.

Standard physics does not predict this coupling in the geometry Dr. Drew uses:
* In standard induction motor physics, a rotating magnetic field in the XY-plane exerts
  torque on a rotor in the θ-direction (rotational), not in the Z-direction (axial).
* In standard Lenz repulsion, a conductor repels away from the time-varying field source,
  which in an axially symmetric geometry would be radially outward, not axially upward.
* In standard permanent magnet dynamics, a disc magnet above a rotating field would
  spin synchronously with the field and experience Z-axis torque oscillations that average
  to zero, not a net upward displacement.

The bridge Dr. Drew observes empirically is:
ELECTRICAL THREE-PHASE DRIVE → XY-PLANE ROTATION OF DISC MAGNET → NET Z-AXIS UPWARD FORCE

The mechanism connecting XY rotation to Z-axis force is the central unresolved physics
question in his work. He refers to it as the "vortex field" mechanism.

### Bridge 3: Dual Counter-Rotating System → Controlled Altitude

The most complete propulsion bridge described in this dataset:

Input 1: Three-phase power driving the outer coil ring clockwise → this drives the inner
disc magnet anti-clockwise (through magnetic coupling) → generates F_up (upward Z-axis).

Input 2: The thin outer ring of 16 NN-SS magnets spinning clockwise at elevated frequency
(counter to inner magnet) → generates F_down (downward Z-axis on inner magnet).

Bridge mechanism: F_net = F_up − F_down (vector difference of two magnetic-spin-induced forces).

Outputs:
* F_net > 0 → craft ascends (net upward acceleration)
* F_net = 0 → craft hovers (neutral buoyancy in gravitational field)
* F_net < 0 → craft descends (net downward acceleration)

Control parameter: The ratio f_outer/f_inner determines which force dominates. By varying
the drive frequency of the outer counter-spinning ring relative to the inner spin, altitude
is continuously controlled.

This bridges the full path from input power → mechanical spin configuration → net Z-axis
force → altitude control — the complete propulsion chain for a UAP craft operating on
this principle.

2012 are interpreted as engineering diagrams of this exact dual-force propulsion system.

### Bridge 5: Single Rotating Electromagnetic Device → Weight Increase

This experiment shows the inverse bridge — a device that generates DOWNWARD force increase:

Input: Increasing rotational frequency (0 → 15 → 17 Hz)
Bridge: At threshold rotation speed (when pole is at top), downward force initiates

The directionality (downward rather than upward) suggests that either:
a) This device geometry produces the opposite sign of Z-axis force compared to the
   disc-magnet-on-coil experiment
b) The same mechanism that produces upward force in one geometry produces downward force
   in another — direction depends on relative orientation of spinning components

This is important for understanding the bridge mechanism: if the same fundamental physics
produces both upward and downward force depending on geometry, then orientation control
of the spinning components is the key design parameter for the propulsion system.

Relevance: A craft equipped with this mechanism could control both ascent (F_z > 0)
and descent (F_z < 0) by switching or rotating the geometry of the spinning magnetic
components — without any additional physical mechanism.
