### Tesla Unipolar Motor as Energy Conversion Device

"Please Tesla 1901 'Unipolar Motor' to see the complete physics. This system works as the opposite of what they teach in schools."

The Faraday/Tesla homopolar (unipolar) generator was the first electrical generator, demonstrated by Faraday in 1831. It produces continuous DC without commutation:

**Generator mode:**
    EMF = (1/2) · B_z · ω · r²   [continuous DC]
    P_out = EMF · I = (B_z·ω·r²)²/(4R)   [electrical power output]

**Motor mode (inverse):**
    Torque = B_z · I · r   [for radial current I flowing through disk of radius r in axial field B_z]
    Angular velocity: ω = EMF_applied/(B_z·r²/2)

The "opposite of textbook" statement refers to the polarity of energy conversion. In standard AC motors:
- Rotating B-field (produced by AC coils) → torque on stationary rotor
- Input: AC electrical energy; Output: mechanical rotation

In the unipolar motor/generator:
- Static B-field (permanent magnet or DC coil) + rotating conductor
- Can operate as motor (electrical → mechanical) or generator (mechanical → electrical) depending on external circuit
- The same physical device inverts its function by reversing the energy flow direction

**Drew's UAP application:**
He implies the UAP's spinning magnet system operates in a mode analogous to unipolar generator — the magnets spin (driven by unknown energy source) and the coils (static, surrounding) generate electricity for ship systems. The physics is "inverted" relative to the textbook induction motor where electricity drives the spinning.
