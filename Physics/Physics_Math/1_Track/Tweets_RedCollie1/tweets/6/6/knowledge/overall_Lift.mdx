### Primary Lift Mechanism: Gyroscopic Super-Precession

**Physical setting:**
A spinning disk (or magnetic flywheel) on a pivot, subject to gravitational force Mg downward (−ẑ). The disk spins at high angular velocity ω, giving it angular momentum L = Iω.

**Gravity-Cancellation Phase (Lift Partial):**
The pivot point supports a force in the +ẑ direction equal to Mg - F_cancel, where F_cancel arises from the gyroscopic reaction to precession. When the disk precesses at the natural rate Ω_p = Mgr/(Iω):
    Net weight at pivot = 0    (complete gravitational cancellation)

This is not lift (not net upward force), but complete cancellation — effective weightlessness at the pivot while the disk slowly precesses.

**Super-Precession Phase (True Lift):**
When precession is driven above the natural rate:
    Ω_applied > Mgr/(Iω)

The gyroscopic reaction force at the pivot exceeds the gravitational force:
    F_reaction = I·ω·Ω_applied / r > Mg

Net lift force:
    F_lift = I·ω·Ω_applied / r - Mg  [in +ẑ direction]

For vertical takeoff: F_lift ≥ Mg (object weight) → minimum condition:
    Ω_applied ≥ 2Mgr/(Iω)

This defines the minimum applied precession rate for net upward acceleration.

**Decay of lift with spin:**
As ω decreases (spin slows due to friction), F_lift decreases for fixed Ω_applied:
    F_lift = I·ω·Ω_applied/r - Mg

At ω_critical = Mg·r/(I·Ω_applied): F_lift → 0, lift ceases. Below ω_critical: negative (downward force dominates). This explains observations where the gyroscope slowly descends as it loses spin.

**UAP Analogue:**
For real UAPs, Drew states: 3-phase or 5-phase magnetism creates rapid spin (high ω) of an internal magnetic flywheel. The "pivot" is the craft structure. The precession is driven electromagnetically (not by hand/string). The super-precession condition (Ω_applied > Ω_natural) is achieved by controlling the electromagnetic driving frequency. Once the flywheel spins fast enough and precession is slightly over-driven, net lift emerges.

**Barnett Effect route to Z-lift:**

This axial magnetization M_z then interacts with the axial field gradient ∂B_z/∂z to produce an axial force:

This provides a pathway from XY-plane rotation to Z-axis lift without violating standard EM.
