## Self-Excited Dynamo vs. Lenz's Law — The Claimed Physical Distinction

Source: YAML 1988208405702705389 (note_tweet)

Within classical electrodynamics, Lenz's law is a consequence of Faraday's law:
  EMF = −dΦ_B/dt

This means any induced current always opposes the change in flux — the induced field always opposes the primary field change (Lenz opposition).

Drew explicitly distinguishes a second regime — the self-excited dynamo — where the induced field ADDS to the primary field:
* This is not formally "beyond" classical electrodynamics — self-excited dynamos exist in engineering (homopolar generators, Earth's geodynamo)
* However, Drew presents the constructive addition as being qualitatively different from Lenz opposition, implying it represents a separate physical mechanism not taught in standard electromagnetism curricula
* The claim is that this constructive-addition regime is the key to UAP levitation, and that the mainstream physics community has ignored or suppressed this avenue

Whether this is truly non-classical:
* The self-excited homopolar generator (Bullard disk dynamo) is a well-established classical MHD result
* The novelty in Drew's claim, if any, lies not in the self-excited dynamo itself but in the specific geometry (liquid metal annular channel + disc magnet center + 3-phase stator) and the claim that this combined configuration produces a Z-axis lift force

The Z-axis force from the combined system — if real as observed — would be the non-classical result. Classical MHD in this geometry produces azimuthal body forces (Lorentz rotation) and torques, not net axial (Z) forces.

Source: MDX 13_2043457882012917760_video.mdx, YAML 2043457990888718568

Observation from the video: "Grass is moving one way and the machine was going the other way."

This describes counter-rotation: the craft spins counterclockwise while the grass below rotates in the opposite direction.

Classical aerodynamic prediction: A spinning disc hovering above a surface (ground effect) would produce:
* Downwash vortex below the disc — co-rotating with the disc's bound vortex system
* The boundary layer at the ground would experience flow in the same rotational direction as the induced downwash

Counter-rotation of the grass relative to the disc is anomalous for aerodynamic mechanisms.

A magnetic explanation: If the craft's rotating magnetic field induces eddy currents in the conducting ground (moist soil has electrical conductivity), the Lorentz force on these eddy currents would be in the SAME direction as the field rotation. By Newton's 3rd law, the reaction on the field source (the craft) would be in the OPPOSITE direction — i.e., the craft experiences a torque opposing the induced currents' torque. This would manifest as the craft creating counter-rotation in the ground/grass while itself spinning counterclockwise.

This is an eddy current braking/induction interaction — the ground acts as a secondary circuit. The observation is consistent with this electromagnetic ground coupling, which is not a purely Newtonian (mechanical) effect but involves electromagnetic induction in the medium below the craft.
