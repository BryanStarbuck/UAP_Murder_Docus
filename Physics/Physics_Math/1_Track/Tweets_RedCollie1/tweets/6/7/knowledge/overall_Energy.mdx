Energy implications:

* In Drew's framing, the DC power input drives the Lorentz rotation of the liquid metal, which generates an induced field that reinforces the central disc magnet
* The energy source is the DC power supply — the "addition" to the field is not free energy but represents conversion of electrical power input to magnetic field energy

Contrast stated:
* Moving magnet + wire coil = induced current REDUCES the moving magnet's field (Lenz's law) — energy extracted from the moving magnet's kinetic energy
* Spinning liquid metal + disc magnet = induced field ADDS to disc magnet's field — a different energy conversion pathway

The thermodynamic constraint: both cases must satisfy energy conservation. In the Lenz case, the opposing force does work against the moving magnet, extracting kinetic energy. In the self-excited dynamo case, the input DC power is the energy source for both the Lorentz rotation AND the enhanced field. There is no claimed violation of energy conservation in the explicit text — but Drew presents the constructive addition as a unique and non-obvious feature absent from textbook treatments.

## Cometary C₂/CN Emission Energy — Green Coma

Source: TXT 3_2005082745282985984.txt

The green color of a cometary coma arises from:
* C₂ (dicarbon) Swan band emission: principal bands at λ = 516.5 nm, 473.7 nm, 438.2 nm (d³Πg → a³Πu transitions)
* CN (cyanogen radical) violet system: principal band at λ = 388.3 nm (B²Σ⁺ → X²Σ⁺)

Energy of the C₂ Swan band photons:
  E = hν = hc/λ = (6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s × 2.998 × 10⁸ m/s) / (516.5 × 10⁻⁹ m)
  = 3.844 × 10⁻¹⁹ J = 2.40 eV

The energy source for this emission is solar UV photodissociation of parent molecules (HCN → CN + H; C₂H₂ → C₂ + H₂, etc.) followed by fluorescence/resonance scattering of sunlight. This is a solar energy harvesting mechanism — the comet re-emits solar UV as optical emission, generating the visible green coma.

This is classical cometary physics — included because the TXT describes cometary physics in the context of UAP comparison imagery.

## UFO Energy Budget — No Visible Exhaust, No Reaction Mass

Source: TXT 13_1913570616609026048_preview.txt, YAML 2043457990888718568

The contrast between propeller propulsion and claimed UAP propulsion has energy implications:

Classical propeller:
* Energy input (motor shaft power) → kinetic energy of accelerated fluid → thrust
* Power required for vertical hover:
    P_hover = T × v_induced = W × v_induced
  where W = craft weight, v_induced = induced velocity through the disk
* Efficiency limited by actuator disk theory; large disk area → low v_induced → high efficiency

UAP propulsion (as claimed):
* No fluid momentum exchange → no v_induced → in principle, no mass flow power term
* Energy coupling directly to gravitational/inertial field
* In the extreme limit (perfectly efficient field coupling), the energy cost of hovering approaches zero (no work done against a conservative field in steady state)
* Drew does not state this explicitly but the implication of "no wake, no exhaust, no reaction mass" is that the energy expenditure per unit time for UAP hovering is not governed by the actuator disk momentum equation

This is the key claimed advantage over conventional propulsion: potentially orders-of-magnitude lower power consumption for levitation and propulsion if the coupling mechanism is to the gravitational/inertial field rather than to a fluid medium.
