## Contrast with Aerodynamic Lift — Propeller Wake vs. UAP Wake

Source: TXT 13_1913570616609026048_preview.txt (propeller vortex CGI visualization)

Classical aerodynamic/hydrodynamic lift via propeller:
* Requires acceleration of a fluid mass rearward (or downward for vertical lift)
* The Rankine-Froude momentum disk theory:
    T = ṁ · Δv = ρ A v_disk · 2v_induced
  where T = thrust, ṁ = mass flow rate, ρ = fluid density, A = disk area, v_disk = velocity through disk, v_induced = induced velocity in far wake
* Produces a helical tip vortex wake — physically observable as a corkscrew pattern of vortex rings extending behind the propeller
* The CGI visualization shows 8-10 discrete toroidal vortex rings in the wake

UAP contrast:
* "UAP observations where no visible vortex wake or disturbed fluid is detected"
* No momentum exchange with fluid medium required
* Lift (Z-axis force) is produced by the rotating magnetic field interacting with field/inertial/gravitational coupling — no reaction mass expelled
* This is the key distinction: classical lift = Newton's 3rd law applied to fluid; claimed UAP lift = direct field coupling, no fluid momentum exchange
