   Source: tweet 1941823558147342518 (2025-07-06), note_tweet

   Drew's claim: "If this horizontal-to-vertical motion [of the gyroscope] is powerful enough, and does not create any downward back-reaction by Newton's 3rd Law, then we will have a purely 'upward' force, which acts in opposition to the downward force of gravity. In other words, 'anti-gravity'."

   Experimental test proposed: "We can look for any loss of weight on a scale?"
   If a closed system loses weight during gyroscope axis transition without any expelled mass, this would constitute:
   -- A violation of Newton's 3rd Law (no reaction partner for the upward impulse)
   -- A violation of conservation of linear momentum (momentum of the system changes without external force)
   -- Or: a coupling to the gravitational field of Earth such that Earth experiences the reaction (mediated by some unknown gravitational coupling mechanism, not through material contact or radiation pressure) — in which case total system momentum is conserved but the local reaction is absent
   Status: Hypothetical. No experimental result reported in these sources.

2. LORENTZ-INDUCED FIELD REINFORCEMENT (VIOLATES LENZ'S LAW PRINCIPLE)
   Source: tweet 1965941848457003483 (2025-09-11)
   Classical prediction: Lenz's law, derived from Faraday's law and the principle of energy conservation, requires that the induced EMF always opposes the change causing it. In any conductor (solid or plasma), the induced currents create a field opposing the driving flux change.
   Drew's claim: "Lorentz spin creates an induced magnetic field which strengthens the applied magnetic field! Completely unlike the Lenz force used in our electric generators."
   If correct, implications:
   -- The induced field adds to the applied field → the total field grows → more Lorentz force → more induced current → more induced field: positive feedback loop
   -- This loop has no classical saturation mechanism (classical Lenz saturation occurs precisely because the induced field weakens, not strengthens, the driving field)
   -- A physically realizable positive-feedback B-amplification loop would represent a new electromagnetic phenomenon with no analog in Maxwell's equations as classically formulated
   -- It would require either: modification of Maxwell's equations in plasma media, or a non-linear plasma response that generates a co-aligned field through mechanisms not captured in linear MHD
   Distinction from classical instabilities: Classical MHD does exhibit instabilities (e.g., tearing mode, kink mode, Rayleigh-Taylor in magnetized plasma) that involve field amplification, but these are transient and lead to restructuring, not steady-state co-aligned field amplification.
   Status: Experimental claim (observed in lab with ionized argon gas); no theoretical framework provided.

3. 3-PHASE AC MOTOR SPONTANEOUS COUNTER-ROTATION
   Source: tweet 2017865458909680022 (2026-02-01)
   Classical prediction: A 3-phase induction motor rotor rotates in the same direction as the stator's rotating magnetic field. Counter-rotation requires an applied external torque opposing the motor's natural rotation. Spontaneous counter-rotation without external torque is forbidden by classical induction motor theory.
   Drew's claim: "This 3-phase AC motor spins either with or against, the same clockwise rotating magnetic field. Their so-called 'theory' for a 3-phase magnetic motor says this is 'impossible'. When I found this out, just before Covid in 2019, at first I could not believe how stupid the 'experts' were."
   If correct, implications:
   -- The motor can spontaneously choose its rotational direction (bistable behavior)
   -- This would require a torque mechanism not dependent on slip direction — contradicting the standard torque-slip curve T(s) which is an odd function of slip s and always produces CW torque for CW rotating field
   -- Possible explanations within known physics: (a) the rotor geometry breaks the CW/CCW symmetry (intentional or accidental design feature), (b) hysteresis effects in ferromagnetic rotor materials produce asymmetric torque, (c) the motor is actually a synchronous reluctance machine with position-dependent torque, (d) the rotating field is not perfectly balanced (single-phasing creates a pulsating field with both CW and CCW components)
   -- Drew's interpretation: this is novel physics proving that standard textbook 3-phase motor theory is wrong
   Status: Experimental observation by Drew (2019); no third-party verification; no theoretical explanation provided beyond the observation.
