## Claim 1: Spinning Gyroscope Creates Anti-Gravitational Force During Spin-Up

Source tweet (1941998706066387266, text + note_tweet):
"My current hypothesis of UFO levitation is that the upward, anti-gravity force comes
from the rise of a spinning gyroscope, as we go from low to high spin?"

Status: Experimental hypothesis, not yet tested (author states intent to test).

Beyond Newtonian mechanics because:
In Newtonian/Lagrangian mechanics, a gyroscope precesses (phi_dot = M*g*l / (I_3*omega_3))
but does not translate vertically. The net center-of-mass force on a torque-free gyroscope
in a uniform gravitational field is: F = -Mg z_hat (gravitational weight only, no upward
correction from spin). No coupling between d(omega_3)/dt and F_z exists in standard mechanics.

This claim asserts F_z = F_z(d omega_3/dt) — a new term not present in the Lagrangian
for the symmetric top. To accommodate this, the action functional would need an additional
term:
  Delta_S = integral k * (d|L|/dt) * z dt

This term violates the symmetry of the Lagrangian under spatial translations in z when spin
is changing — a symmetry that is normally guaranteed by homogeneity of space (Noether's theorem:
spatial translation symmetry → conservation of momentum). If this term is real, it implies
a preferred direction in space (the gravity direction) that actively couples to angular
momentum change — beyond standard GR or Newtonian gravity.

## Claim 2: Fast-Rotating Magnetic Fields Create Spinning Vortices in 4-D Spacetime

Source tweet (1956985159343026197, text + note_tweet):
"fast-spinning gyroscopes, as well as fast-rotating magnetic fields, seem to create 'spinning
vortices' in 4-D spacetime, which we do not yet fully understand, but which can clearly (by
experiment) cancel or reverse the 'downward' force of gravity, in small regions of 'vortex'
spacetime or nearby."

Status: Experimental claim (reported as established from "many recently-posted videos").

Beyond Newtonian mechanics because:
Rotating magnetic fields in classical electromagnetism (Maxwell equations in flat Minkowski
spacetime) do not couple to the gravitational field at all. The Maxwell stress tensor:
  T^mu_nu_EM = (1/mu_0) [F^mu_alpha F_nu^alpha - (1/4) delta^mu_nu F_alpha_beta F^alpha_beta]

contributes to the stress-energy tensor and thus, via Einstein's equations:
  G^mu_nu = (8*pi*G/c^4) T^mu_nu

does produce spacetime curvature. However, for laboratory-scale EM fields:
  B ~ 0.1 T → U_EM ~ B^2 / (2*mu_0) ~ 4000 J/m^3
  Equivalent mass density: rho_eff = U_EM / c^2 ~ 4.4 × 10^-14 kg/m^3

This is 14 orders of magnitude below rock density and produces utterly negligible metric
perturbation (Schwarzschild radius of the EM energy in 1 cm^3: r_s ~ 10^-42 m).

Therefore, the claimed "spinning vortices in 4-D spacetime" from laboratory rotating
magnetic fields requires either:
(a) A coupling constant between the rotating EM field and spacetime torsion/curvature
    that is ~10^24 larger than predicted by standard GR (an extraordinary enhancement)
(b) A new physical field mediating between the EM rotation and spacetime geometry —
    possibly a scalar-tensor coupling, a torsion field (Einstein-Cartan), or a non-Abelian
    extension of the EM gauge field with gravitational coupling
(c) The effect is classical but was not predicted due to an incomplete analysis of the
    rotating magnet / surrounding magnet boundary conditions (i.e., the experiment is
    accurately described but the "4-D spacetime" interpretation is an overreach, while
    the force itself could have a mundane EM/mechanical origin)

## Claim 3: Permanent Magnet Rotor Reversal (N-N-N-N Configuration Spins Against Field)

Source tweet (2017866159291568301), referenced_tweets[0].text:
"A ring of 4 permanent magnets will spin either WITH a rotating magnetic field (made by
6 wire coils wired as U-V-W-U-V-W), or AGAINST a rotating magnetic field, depending on
whether those 4 rotor magnets are arranged as N-S-N-S or N-N-N-N."

Status: Experimental claim reported as verified.

Beyond classical EM prediction because:
For a ring of permanent magnets (treated as magnetic dipoles) in a rotating external field,
the standard torque on each dipole:
  tau = m × B_ext

where m = magnetic dipole moment, B_ext = rotating external field.

For N-S-N-S arrangement (alternating poles):
  Dipole moments alternate direction: +m, -m, +m, -m
  Net torque on the ring aligned with field rotation → ring follows field (standard PM motor)

For N-N-N-N arrangement (all same poles):
  All dipole moments point in same direction: +m, +m, +m, +m
  Net torque analysis for a 4-pole, all-north ring in a 2-pole rotating field:

  The interaction energy: U = -m . B_ext = -m * B_0 * cos(theta - omega_e*t)
  Torque: tau = -dU/dtheta = -m * B_0 * sin(theta - omega_e*t)

  For a free-spinning ring (moment of inertia I_ring), the equation of motion:
    I_ring * d^2(theta)/dt^2 = -m * B_0 * sin(theta - omega_e*t)

  This is the equation of a forced pendulum. Standard synchronous capture occurs when
  |tau| is large enough to maintain theta = omega_e*t + const. (synchronous following).

  For the N-N-N-N case, the effective magnetic moment of the ring with respect to the
  external field has a different angular periodicity than the N-S-N-S case. A ring of
  4 same-pole magnets has a 4-fold angular periodicity in the lab frame, while the
  rotating 2-pole field has a 2-fold periodicity. The interaction produces torques at
  the beat frequencies — potentially driving rotation at omega_beat ≠ omega_e.

  Specifically, the N-N-N-N ring interacts with the 2-pole field such that the torque
  is periodic at 2*(omega_ring - omega_e) (for the 4-pole ring in the 2-pole field).
  This can result in the ring rotating at omega_ring = -omega_e (counter-field rotation)
  at a stable equilibrium, which is non-obvious from the dipole torque formula alone
  and requires the full Fourier analysis of the spatial field harmonics.

  This is within classical electromagnetism (not truly "beyond") but is non-obvious and
  reportedly counterintuitive enough that Drew describes mainstream engineers as failing
  to predict or understand it ("The 'experts' are so dumb, that it takes your breath away").

## Claim 4: Novel Up-and-Down Spin-Motion Never Seen Before

Source tweet (2043608507887587705):
"We may almost be there! Can a novel up-and-down spin-motion. discovered in my lab today
(never seen before on Earth), release gravitational energy in pulses?"

Status: Experimental observation (discovery reported as of 2026-04-13).

Beyond classical mechanics because:
A stable coupled oscillation mode involving simultaneous spin (phi_dot ≠ 0) and vertical
translation (z_dot ≠ 0) is not a standard mode of a gyroscope or spinning magnet in a
magnetic trap. Standard modes of a magnetically trapped spinning dipole:
* Axial oscillation (z-bounce at frequency omega_z = sqrt(d^2 U_B / dz^2 / m))
* Transverse oscillation (radial oscillation)
* Precession (angular oscillation of spin axis)
* Spin (constant or decaying omega_3)

These modes are orthogonal in the harmonic approximation. A coupling between the spin mode
and the axial oscillation mode requires a non-harmonic term in the magnetic potential —
specifically, a term of the form U ~ omega_3^2 * z or higher-order coupling.

to 6 mm) and when 3-phase power is applied suggests the coupling is field-strength and
field-rotation dependent — consistent with the proposed torsional spacetime mechanism
if the non-linear term is: U ~ (B_rot * omega_3)^2 * z / c^2 or similar.

The match to 2012 crop pictures (slides 2, 3, 4) is presented as independent confirmation:
if crop formations from 2012 schematically illustrate this specific oscillation mode, and

## Claim 5: Spontaneous 15 Hz Rotation of Magnetically Attracted Flywheel

Source: 13_2043606527630786560_video.mdx transcription:
"This thing will start spinning at about 15 hertz, even though it's attracted to all four
magnets on our side."

Status: Experimental observation (witnessed in video).

Potentially anomalous because:
A system of permanent magnets in a static configuration with a physically symmetric
attractive potential should exhibit metastability or neutrally stable rotation (no preferred
direction), not spontaneous onset of a specific direction rotation at 15 Hz. The onset of
rotation at a specific frequency (not just any speed) implies a resonant mechanism.

Classical explanation (most likely): slight asymmetry in the 4-magnet arrangement creates
a sinusoidal potential U_B(phi) with period pi/2 (4 magnets → 4-fold symmetry → 4 potential
wells per revolution). The system rolls over a potential barrier and, once rotating, reaches
a terminal velocity set by the balance of driving magnetic torque and damping (bearing
friction, air resistance). Terminal velocity:

  T_net = T_magnetic(omega) - T_friction(omega) = 0

For 15 Hz = 94.2 rad/s to be the terminal velocity, the magnetic driving torque and
friction torque must balance at this speed. This is entirely classical.

However, Drew presents it as part of the anomalous sequence: spontaneous 15 Hz rotation
→ 3-phase power → 17 mm lift, suggesting the 15 Hz is the critical operating condition
that enables the lift effect. Whether the 15 Hz onset is itself anomalous or classical
is not distinguished in the source.

Source tweet (1994946476280299874, note_tweet):
"Its core spins on a time-scale of minutes, not hours. While its overall intensity remains
approximately the same. There is NO WAY that a natural comet could spin like this."

Status: Contested experimental claim (observational data interpretation).

Beyond Newtonian gravitational mechanics because:

for rotational breakup:
  omega_crit = sqrt(4*pi*G*rho/3)

For rho = 500 kg/m^3: omega_crit ~ 3.7 × 10^-4 rad/s (T ~ 4.7 hours)

Observed (claimed): T ~ 1-10 minutes → omega ~ 0.01-0.1 rad/s → 28 to 280 × omega_crit

This implies either:
(a) The object has extraordinary tensile strength far exceeding cometary material, requiring
    exotic solid-state physics (metamaterial, compressed exotic matter, or crystalline structure
    with tensile strength comparable to industrial steel rather than icy rubble)
(b) The core is not self-gravitating (too small) and surface tension or internal bonding
    provides the structural integrity — possible if the "core" is a compact dense object
    with radius << 1 km and rho >> 500 kg/m^3
(c) The rotation is of a non-solid fluid or plasma component (coma jet activity creating
    apparent rotation), not the solid nucleus
(d) It is an artificial object whose structure is engineered to withstand rapid rotation

Drew's interpretation: option (d), "possibly a large spaceship or a mini-black hole"
(note_tweet: "There is NO WAY that a natural comet could spin like this. Possibly a large
