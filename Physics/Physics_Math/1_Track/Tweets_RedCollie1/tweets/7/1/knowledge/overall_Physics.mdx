## Gyroscopic Physics: Standard and Anomalous

### Standard (Newtonian) Gyroscope Mechanics

For a symmetric top with angular momentum L = I_3 * omega_3 (along body symmetry axis),
subject to gravitational torque tau = r × (M * g):

Precession: Omega_prec = tau / |L| = M*g*l / (I_3 * omega_3)

Direction: Omega_prec is perpendicular to both L and g — i.e., horizontal, not vertical.
Standard precession produces NO net vertical translation.

Nutation: When angular momentum is not perfectly aligned with the precession axis,
the gyroscope nutates — oscillating in polar angle theta about the precession orbit.
This is governed by:

  (d^2 theta)/(dt^2) = (Omega_z - Omega_prec cos(theta)) * (Omega_prec - Omega_z cos(theta)) / sin(theta)
                       - Omega_prec^2 sin(theta) cos(theta)
                       + (I_3 * omega_3 / I_1) * Omega_z * sin(theta)

Nutation frequency: omega_nut ~ I_3 * omega_3 / I_1

from low to high spin"

The phrase "from low to high spin" identifies the transient spin-up regime as the critical

In the standard Euler equations for a torque-free symmetric top:

  I_1 * (dOmega_2/dt) + (I_1 - I_3) * omega_3 * Omega_1 = 0
  I_3 * (d(omega_3)/dt) = tau_3

No vertical (Z) force term appears. The anomalous claim requires an additional term:

"Its core spins on a time-scale of minutes, not hours. While its overall intensity
remains approximately the same. NASA claimed that it spins once every 16 hours, based

* NASA claim: rotation period = 16 hours, derived from photometric light curve analysis
  (variation in reflected/emitted light as surface features rotate in and out of view)

If the core rotation period is minutes rather than hours:
  omega_core = 2*pi / (few minutes) ~ 0.01-0.1 rad/s

  omega_NASA = 2*pi / (16 * 3600 s) ~ 10^-4 rad/s

in their underlying assumptions" — specifically that the photometric variation measures
something other than rotation (e.g., jet activity, surface composition changes, or
deliberate obfuscation).

(centrifugal disruption) unless the object has extraordinary tensile strength or its
mass distribution is highly non-spherical. Drew claims "there is NO WAY that a natural
comet could spin like this" — using the rapid rotation as evidence for artificial origin.

The reported mass: Avi Loeb estimates 3I/ATLAS mass at 33 billion tons = 3.3 × 10^13 kg.
For comparison: Earth's Moon has mass ~ 7.3 × 10^22 kg. So 3I/ATLAS mass ~ 10^-9 lunar masses.

For structural integrity under rapid rotation, the critical spin rate (breakup):
  omega_crit = sqrt(4*pi*G*rho/3)

For cometary density rho ~ 500 kg/m^3:
  omega_crit ~ sqrt(4*pi * 6.67e-11 * 500 / 3) ~ 2 × 10^-4 rad/s

This corresponds to a rotation period of ~8.6 hours — so a "few minutes" rotation
would indeed exceed the gravitational breakup threshold for a cometary-density body,
supporting the claim that the rapid spin is anomalous for a natural body.

## Hubble/SPHERE-X/James Webb Resolution Comparison (Observational Physics)

Source tweet (1960653588021305375), referenced_tweets[0].text:
"Hubble resolution 0.04 arcseconds per pixel (slide 1).
SPHERE-X resolution 6.2 arcseconds per pixel (slide 2).
James Webb 'expected' resolution 0.07 arcseconds per pixel (slide 3)."

The angular resolution comparison:
* Hubble Space Telescope (HST): 0.04"/pixel — diffraction limit at ~500 nm:
  theta_min = 1.22 * lambda/D = 1.22 * 500e-9 / 2.4 = 2.54 × 10^-7 rad = 0.052"
  (consistent with reported 0.04"/pixel at highest sampling)
* SPHEREx: 6.2"/pixel — a wide-field infrared survey telescope, designed for survey
  speed at the cost of angular resolution; not designed for resolved imaging
* James Webb Space Telescope (JWST): 0.07"/pixel reported — diffraction limit:
  theta_min = 1.22 * 2e-6 / 6.5 = 3.75 × 10^-7 rad = 0.077"
  (consistent for near-infrared at 6.5 m primary)

3I/ATLAS or other anomalous objects). The physics basis for the concern: if JWST actually
achieves 0.07"/pixel resolution but released images show effective resolution of ~6"/pixel
(matching SPHEREx), this would represent deliberate downsampling by a factor of ~88x,
implying concealment of 88x finer structure.
