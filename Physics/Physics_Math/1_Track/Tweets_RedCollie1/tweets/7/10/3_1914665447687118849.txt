KEY PHYSICS TERMS AND CLAIMS:
1. "Outside perimeter of each disc carries twelve magnets" — 12-pole arrangement on the disc periphery. This is a specific, constructible design: a 12-pole permanent magnet arrangement on a spinning disc. 12 poles = 6 north + 6 south, equally spaced at 30° intervals.
2. "Draw off electrical current from the central hubs" — homopolar generator/Faraday disc principle. In a homopolar generator, a conducting disc rotating in a magnetic field generates a radial EMF from center to rim (or vice versa), and current can be extracted between the central axle and the rim.
3. "Perpetual flow of electrical energy as long as the discs rotate" — this is claimed to be overunity or zero-point energy extraction, since in conventional physics a generator requires a mechanical energy input equal to or greater than the electrical output.
4. "Magnetic and gyroscopic effect creates its own gravity" — gyromagnetic effects (Einstein-de Haas, Barnett) plus gyroscopic stability are claimed to generate artificial gravitational fields.
