   interaction) produces upward force. This is explicitly framed as beyond Newtonian mechanics: "it
   is like a gyroscope that's spinning against an invisible force of the vacuum."
   Source: 13_2032260628157714433_video.mdx, YAML 2032263041216365012.

2. **Counter-rotating outer flywheel providing stabilization and amplified lift.** A second rotating
   element (outer flywheel with eight magnets arranged NS, NS, NS, NS alternating) rotates
   anti-clockwise while the inner magnet spins clockwise. Two crop-circle diagrams interpreted as
   blueprints (2007, 2014) are cited as showing this dual spin/counter-spin geometry. The Barbary
   Castle 1991 crop picture (the first large formation) is interpreted as showing spinning/precession
   of a "top" with three-phase power feeding a large outer flywheel and a narrow central rising
   magnet — described as resembling the "Carr anti-gravity device" studied at Stanford in the 1980s.
   Source: YAML 2007946359039726071, YAML 2032263041216365012, MDX 13_2032260628157714433.

3. **Corbino disc geometry as central element.** The Corbino disc (a disc of conductive material
   in a magnetic field through which radial current flows, i.e., a homopolar geometry) is explicitly
   identified as the central element of the claimed UFO drive. Six wire coils arranged in two
   triangular groups (3 lower + 3 upper for the six-coil configuration, or 3-phase UVW × 2) generate
   a rotating magnetic field; the Corbino disc / central permanent magnet resides at the geometric
   center. This produces spin at the center and counter-spin in the outer annular region.
   Source: YAML 2007946359039726071 ("Spin and counter-spin near its central Corbino disc").

4. **Liquid-metal homopolar drive as DC variant.** Norman Paulsen's account of Indian "vimanas"
   using liquid mercury plus DC power applied at the outer perimeter and at the central hub is
   connected to Tesla's US Patent 406,968 (dynamo-electric machine with two counter-rotating discs).
   The proposed modern implementation uses gallium instead of mercury (requires temperature > 50°C
   to remain liquid). This is explicitly framed as a "liquid homopolar motor/generator": DC power
   drives a conducting liquid rotating in a magnetic field; the counter-rotating disc geometry is
   Paulsen's description; the homopolar generator principle (Faraday disc) would allow extraction
   of electrical current from the central hubs.
   Source: YAML 1914669302005027316, note_tweet field; TXT 3_1914665381630988289,
   TXT 3_1914665447687118849.

5. **Six-wire-coil geometry producing hole in center for field study.** Three-phase power
   applied to six wire coils (two sets of UVW = UUVVWW) creates a rotating magnetic field with a
   null region at the geometric center. A test magnet placed at center spins in one direction;
   the field in the spaces between coils on the outside spins in the opposite direction. This
   inner-clockwise / outer-anti-clockwise field structure is argued to replicate the UFO spin
   topology.
   Source: MDX 13_2014594201829220352_video.

6. **Specific device construction (experimental, not theoretical only).** The author reports
   actually building the device:
   * Lower stage: 3 wire coils in tight triangular arrangement, wired UVW, three-phase power,
     field goes clockwise. Mounted on stainless steel rod, 8 mm diameter, 150 mm tall.
   * Upper stage: 6 wire coils (also three-phase, wired UVW × 2), field also clockwise.
   * Central permanent magnet: disc magnet, diameter 70 mm, 14 mm central hole, slides onto rod.
   * When three-phase power applied to lower coils, magnet spins clockwise and rises.
   * Outer flywheel: plastic body with 8 permanent magnets (30 mm each, alternating NS), mounted
     on 89 mm outer-diameter stainless steel tube on Teflon rings (to be replaced by 90 mm ID

   * Six upper coils at low speed drive flywheel anti-clockwise.
   * Both the central magnet (clockwise) and outer flywheel (anti-clockwise) operate simultaneously.
   Source: MDX 13_2032260628157714433_video.

7. **Helical magnetic field, not dipole or induction.** A key physics claim: the lift mechanism
   does not operate by electromagnetic induction. The author explicitly states: "It works by a
   'helical' magnetic field. Not a 'dipole' magnetic field." The North or South orientation of the
   pole facing the horizontal rotating field is stated to be irrelevant. This claim is described as

   Source: YAML 1963247821026738254, note_tweet.

8. **Vortex physics as unified framework.** The author claims UFOs fly by "vortex" physics, and
   cites a specific mathematical formula allegedly received in a 2021 dream:
   ω = h / a
   where ω is angular frequency, h is the height of the spinning vortex, and a = π × R²

   * Higher angular rotation ω → larger vortex height h
   * Larger area a (or radius R) → smaller vortex height h
   * The formula describes "transitional magnetism" as the underlying UFO physics mechanism.
   Source: YAML 1957348224119017742, note_tweet.
