PHYSICS/UAP RELEVANCE:
This close-up is the definitive documentation image of the Buga sphere's engraved surface. The physical embedded pins (not merely engraved) are the most anomalous feature — they appear to be functional or decorative elements integrated into the surface, requiring precision manufacturing. The circuit-board aesthetics of the central design combined with unknown script characters is the primary claim made by researchers: that the artifact represents a schematic of a technology encoded in a non-human writing system. The triangular corner elements with internal line patterns bear resemblance to delta-wing or pyramidal field concentrators discussed in some UAP propulsion theories.
