## The Proposed Propulsion Mechanism and Its Physics Advances

### Step 1 — Counter-Rotating Magnetic Flywheel Pair

The system consists of two discrete rotating magnetic assemblies (flywheels), each driven by or interacting with rotating magnetic fields generated by multi-phase wire coil sets.

* Flywheel A: driven to spin clockwise (CW) in the XY plane.
* Flywheel B: driven to spin counter-clockwise (CCW) in the XY plane.
* The counter-rotation of the two flywheels is the necessary and sufficient condition claimed for Z-axis (vertical) propulsion.

### Step 3 — Rotor Magnet Polarity Controls Direction

A key undocumented (claimed to be absent from all textbooks) finding:

* A ring of permanent magnets wired as N-S-N-S (alternating polarity) will spin AGAINST a rotating magnetic field produced by 6 wire coils wired as U-V-W-U-V-W.
* A ring of permanent magnets wired as N-N-N-N (same polarity) will spin WITH the rotating magnetic field.
* This polarity-determined direction inversion is claimed to be completely absent from academic literature.

Source (referenced_tweet in 2017867745082462217): "A ring of 4 permanent magnets will spin either WITH a rotating magnetic field (made by 6 wire coils wired as U-V-W-U-V-W), or AGAINST a rotating magnetic field, depending on whether those 4 rotor magnets are arranged as N-S-N-S or N-N-N-N."

### Step 4 — Specific Coil-and-Magnet Geometry Demonstrated

Demonstrated configuration:
* Stator: 6 wire coils, wired U-V-W-U-V-W in three-phase configuration, producing an anti-clockwise rotating magnetic field.
* Rotor: 8 magnets arranged N-S-N-S-N-S-N-S (alternating polarity).
* Result: Rotor spins CLOCKWISE — opposite to the field rotation.

This is the non-textbook result. Classical induction motor theory predicts the rotor follows the field direction. The claim here is that with the alternating N-S-N-S arrangement the rotor contra-rotates relative to the stator field.
