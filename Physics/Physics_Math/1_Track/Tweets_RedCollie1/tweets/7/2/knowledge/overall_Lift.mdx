### Mechanism of Stepped Lift

The crop picture geometry (series of overlapping circles ascending from a base ring) is interpreted as encoding a discrete oscillatory lift cycle:
1. Counter-spinning flywheel pair reaches a critical relative phase angle φ where the vertical magnetic force between them is maximally repulsive — producing an upward impulse.
2. The system advances to the next phase position (advancing by one pole pair equivalent) — producing a second impulse.
3. Each half-cycle of the relative rotation produces one upward step.

Frequency of stepping: For flywheels spinning at +ω and −ω, relative rotation rate = 2ω. With p pole pairs, impulse frequency = 2ωp/2π steps per second.

Physical basis: As a gyroscope's spin rate ω increases:
* Angular momentum L = Iω increases linearly.
* Precession rate Ω_p = Mgr/(Iω) decreases.
* At high ω, the spin axis becomes increasingly rigid in space (gyrostatic rigidity).
* If the gyroscope is mounted in a configuration where reduced precession rate → reduced effective weight transfer to the support structure, apparent weight reduction (anti-gravity) results.

The Buga Sphere artifact is proposed as a physical implementation:
* Metallic sphere with embedded brass/copper pins (~16-18 pins in a circuit-board pattern).
* Sphere on a vertical pole — the "ball on top of a pole" structure suggests a spin axis along the pole axis (vertical).
* At fast spin (high ω), the sphere allegedly rises along the pole axis.

This behavior would require:
* The ball-on-pole geometry to allow free vertical translation while spinning.
* A mechanism coupling spin angular velocity to vertical displacement (e.g., magnetic levitation interaction between the spinning magnetized sphere and magnets embedded in the pole).

## 5. Jumping Ring / Lenz's Law Repulsion → Applied to Flywheels

Crop picture reference (video transcription 13_2017866917693034499_video.mdx):
"Here's another one, spins one way, spins the other way and it shoots up like a jumping ring."

In the standard jumping ring experiment:
* An AC electromagnet rapidly creates a changing magnetic flux.
* A conducting ring placed coaxially experiences induced EMF ε = −dΦ/dt.
* Induced current I_ring = ε/R_ring flows in a direction to oppose the changing flux (Lenz's law).
* Interaction between I_ring and the electromagnet field produces F = I L × B (upward force on the ring).

For two counter-spinning magnetic flywheels:
* Each flywheel generates a rotating magnetic field.
* The counter-rotating fields produce a rapidly changing mutual flux (dΦ_mutual/dt at rate 2ω).
* Lenz's law dictates each flywheel's field opposes the change in flux from the other.
* The opposition force has a component along the mutual axis (Z-axis if flywheels are coaxial) — this is the upward lift force.

This is a directly testable mechanism within classical electrodynamics.

* Air ionized at 6.6 kV becomes a plasma with free charges (ions and electrons).
