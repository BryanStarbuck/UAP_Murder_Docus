## 1. The Non-Textbook Motor Physics: Contra-Rotation of Alternating-Polarity Rotor

### Experimental Setup

* Stator: 6 wire coils wired U-V-W-U-V-W (three-phase, 60° angular spacing between coils).
* Stator field: rotates anti-clockwise (ACW) in the horizontal plane.
* Rotor type A: 8 permanent magnets, alternating N-S-N-S-N-S-N-S around circumference.
* Observed rotor motion: CLOCKWISE — contra-rotation relative to stator field.

Source: video transcription 13_2017866917693034499_video.mdx.

### Standard Motor Physics Context

In a conventional three-phase induction motor, the rotating magnetic flux density B_s(θ, t) produced by the stator can be written:

B_s(θ, t) = B_0 cos(ωt − pθ)

where ω = 2πf (supply angular frequency), p = pole pair number, θ = azimuthal position angle.

For a permanent magnet synchronous motor (PMSM), a p-pole-pair rotor magnet configuration locks to the p-th harmonic of the rotating stator field and co-rotates at synchronous speed n_s = f/p (in rev/s).

### The Anomalous Finding

With 6 stator coils and 8 rotor magnets (alternating N-S):
* Stator pole pairs produced by 6 coils: p_stator = 3 (for U-V-W-U-V-W wiring producing a 3-pole-pair field).
* Rotor: 8 magnets alternating → 4 pole pairs (p_rotor = 4).

The mismatch p_stator = 3 ≠ p_rotor = 4 means the rotor cannot synchronize to the fundamental rotating field in the conventional sense. Instead, interaction occurs via harmonic coupling.

The sub-harmonic / super-harmonic interaction: the stator's U-V-W-U-V-W wiring with 6 coils produces a fundamental at p=3 but also produces a backward-rotating harmonic at p = 3 (or higher harmonics depending on winding distribution). The 4-pole-pair rotor may lock to a backward-traveling harmonic component of the stator field.

In conventional motor analysis, the rotating field has forward and backward components:
B_total = B_forward + B_backward where:
B_forward = (B_0/2) cos(ωt − pθ)
B_backward = (B_0/2) cos(ωt + pθ)

If the rotor locks to the backward component (speed n_backward = −f/p), it contra-rotates. Drew's observation is consistent with this mechanism if the winding geometry preferentially excites a backward-rotating harmonic that the 8-magnet N-S-N-S rotor couples to more strongly than to the forward wave.

### The N-N-N-N Case (Co-rotation)

With all rotor magnets N-N-N-N (all same polarity → effectively a 1-pole-pair or 0-pole configuration, approximating a monopole or single-pole rotor):
* The rotor couples to the fundamental forward-traveling wave.
* Result: co-rotation with the stator field.

This polarity-controlled direction switching is presented as entirely absent from motor textbooks.

### Physical Chain of Reasoning

Step 2 — Magnetic force on ions:
In the field B of the spinning magnet, free charge carriers experience: F = q(v × B)
* Electrons (q = −e): gyrate with cyclotron frequency ω_ce = eB/m_e (high frequency, small radius).
* Positive ions (q = +Ze): gyrate more slowly at ω_ci = ZeB/m_i (lower frequency, larger radius).

Step 3 — Vortex formation:
A spinning magnet produces a rotating B-field. The J × B body force on the plasma (where J = σ(E + v × B) by Ohm's law for a conductor) drives bulk rotation of the ionized gas. This is the magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) body force:

f = J × B [N/m³]

Step 4 — Downward spiral:
The observed downward spinning of ionized air toward the magnet suggests the rotating field couples to the ionized gas angular momentum and the gas also responds to the magnetic pressure gradient: ∇(B²/2μ₀), which pulls paramagnetic plasma toward regions of higher field intensity (near the magnet poles).

Step 5 — Reaction force (claimed upward Lorentz force on magnet/apparatus):
By Newton's third law, if the ionized gas is accelerated downward and inward (toward the magnet), the magnet experiences a net upward and outward reaction. Drew describes this as "a standard, upward Lorentz force" on the stationary magnet.

This is the claimed propulsion mechanism: the spinning magnet ionizes and vortexes surrounding air downward, producing an upward thrust on the magnetic/craft system — analogous to a magnetohydrodynamic thruster operating in the surrounding air medium.

## 3. Gyroscopic Physics — Anti-Gravity Hypothesis

Source: 1942000099472834853.yaml referenced_tweet (parent tweet 1941998706066387266): "My current hypothesis of UFO levitation is that the upward, anti-gravity force comes from the rise of a spinning gyroscope, as we go from low to high spin?"

### Standard Gyroscope Mechanics

For a symmetric rigid body (moment of inertia I about spin axis) spinning at angular velocity ω in a gravitational field g:

Angular momentum vector: L = Iω (along spin axis)

Precession rate about vertical axis:
Ω_p = τ/|L| = Mgr/(Iω)

where:
* M = mass of gyroscope
* g = gravitational acceleration (9.81 m/s²)
* r = distance from pivot to center of mass
* I = moment of inertia about spin axis
* ω = spin angular velocity

At low ω: Ω_p is large → gyroscope nutation is fast and unstable.
At high ω: Ω_p → 0 → gyroscope approaches vertical stability.

### Drew's Anti-Gravity Claim

The claim is that as ω increases from low to high, the gyroscope physically rises (Z-axis displacement increases). This would require a net upward force component:

F_z = d(L_z)/dt ≠ 0

In standard mechanics, a gyroscope in a gravitational field precesses but does not rise — the center of mass remains at constant height (neglecting friction). The claim of rising at high spin implies a non-Newtonian force term or an additional physical mechanism:

Possible mechanisms:
1. Gyroscopic Magnus effect: a spinning sphere or disk moving through fluid (air) experiences lift (Magnus force F = ρ v × (ω × r) per unit volume).
2. Dynamic nutation: at the transition from high nutation to stable precession, the gyroscope may exhibit transient vertical oscillations that sum to a net upward displacement.
3. Non-Newtonian gyroscopic effect: as proposed by Laithwaite (1974 Royal Institution lecture), certain gyroscope configurations appear to exhibit reduced effective weight — later disputed experimentally but not fully resolved theoretically for all geometries.

Drew connects this to the Buga Sphere artifact (a metallic sphere with embedded pins and circuit-board engravings) as a possible physical implementation.
