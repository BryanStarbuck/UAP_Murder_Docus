## 9. 3I/ATLAS and Comet Behavior (Visual / Astrophysical)

Video transcription (13_1994946719365435394_video.mdx) — partial (Whisper base model, possible errors):
"I never saw any comment doing this."
"I can see the sublimation."
"I can see dust tail growing."
"A comet spinning. This way with all the gas dust everything around."

Standard cometary behavior for comparison:
* Cometary sublimation: volatile ices (H₂O, CO₂, CO) sublimate when sunlight heats the nucleus (T > sublimation point), producing a gas coma and driving dust particles outward.
* Dust tail: fine particles (< 1 μm to 100 μm) driven by radiation pressure, pointing roughly away from the Sun (β-meteoroids / type I tail).
* Ion tail (type II): ionized gas driven by solar wind, points exactly antisolar.
* Standard cometary nucleus spin: hours to days.

The "never saw any comment doing this" (likely "comet doing this" — transcription artifact) refers to the anomalous core spin at minute timescales, not standard cometary behavior.
