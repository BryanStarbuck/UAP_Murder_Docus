PHYSICS/UAP RELEVANCE:
This CT cross-section is the primary evidentiary image for claims about the Buga sphere's anomalous internal structure. A mushroom-shaped internal inclusion of distinct material within a dense metallic matrix is not consistent with natural mineral formation processes, which typically produce either homogeneous matrix structures or irregular/random inclusions. The precise mushroom geometry suggests an intentional or engineered internal structure. In UAP materials research, this type of multi-layer composite construction — with an outer heterogeneous shell, dense matrix interior, and geometrically shaped internal element — is described as consistent with metamaterial construction techniques claimed to exist in recovered non-human artifacts.
