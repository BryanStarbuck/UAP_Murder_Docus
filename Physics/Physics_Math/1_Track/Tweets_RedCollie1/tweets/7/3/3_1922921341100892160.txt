PHYSICS/UAP RELEVANCE:
Wardenclyffe Tower was Nikola Tesla's experimental facility in Shoreham, New York (construction began 1901), intended to be a worldwide wireless power transmission system. The tower was designed to transmit electrical energy through the Earth's ionosphere to any point on the globe without wires. Tesla claimed it could also serve as a global communications system. The project was shut down and the tower demolished in 1917. In UAP physics research, Tesla's work on wireless energy transmission, scalar waves, and resonant Earth-ionosphere cavity effects is frequently cited as a precursor to claimed classified physics applications. The Wardenclyffe tower represents the publicly visible limit of Tesla's work — researchers claim the classified extension of these principles was subsequently developed in black programs.
