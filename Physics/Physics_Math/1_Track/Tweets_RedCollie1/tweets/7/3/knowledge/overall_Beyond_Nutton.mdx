pushes back on vehicle.

Note: Drew does not claim Newton's 3rd Law is violated — he claims the reaction force is
taken by the vacuum rather than by a material structure. This is consistent with Newton's 3rd
Law if the vacuum is considered a medium that can receive and absorb reaction forces (analogous
to how a boat's oar pushes on water and the water pushes back). The non-classical aspect is
the identification of the quantum vacuum as a physical medium that can bear reaction forces.

## 2. Orientation-Independent Magnetic Levitation — Non-Classical Control Result

Source: YAML 2017877032907247916 (note_tweet), MDX 13_2007699282548404229_video.mdx

### Why Orientation-Independence Is Non-Classical

Classical magnetic gradient force on a dipole:
    F = ∇(m · B)

For an axially magnetized magnet with moment m = m ẑ:
    F_z = m_z × ∂B_z/∂z + m_x × ∂B_x/∂z + m_y × ∂B_y/∂z

If we flip the magnet (N-up → S-up): m_z → -m_z, so F_z → -F_z. The force reverses.
Therefore, if N-up rises, S-up should fall in any classical gradient-force levitation scheme.

### The Experimental Result

With 8 mm bore / 5 mm rod / 3-phase r.m.f.:
* N-up: magnet rises.
* S-up: magnet also rises.
* ANY orientation: magnet rises.

This orientation-independence is the key non-classical experimental result. Drew cites it as
ruling out ALL dipole magnetic gradient mechanisms. The effect cannot be explained by:
* Magnetic gradient force (∇(m·B)) — orientation-dependent, ruled out.
* Eddy current repulsion (depends on relative velocity and field, not pole orientation for
  a non-conducting magnet) — possibly orientation-independent but the rod must be conducting
  for eddy currents, not specified.
* Bernoulli/aerodynamic lift from spin (Magnus effect) — orientation-independent BUT
  produces horizontal, not vertical, force for a vertically spinning disc.

The stated explanation — "pushing against the vacuum of space" — is the non-classical claim
that goes beyond Newtonian + standard electromagnetic mechanics.

Source: YAML 1978591957686661487 (note_tweet), txt file 3_1978591003490848768.txt

### Beyond Simple Magnetic Dipole Interactions

Simple Newtonian/Maxwellian magnetic interactions between magnets are:
* Dipole-dipole attraction/repulsion: F ∝ 1/r⁴
* Gradient force: F = ∇(m·B)
* Torque: τ = m × B

The Lorentz homopolar force identified by Drew as the "counter-spin" mechanism is distinct:
* It involves the coupling of a rotating conductor (or rotating magnetic body) to an axial
  magnetic field via EMF induction.
* The resulting force has both tangential (braking torque) and axial (Z-direction) components.
* The Z-component of the homopolar Lorentz force is the downward reaction that Drew identifies
  as the mechanism requiring cancellation.

This is the "beyond simple magnet" physics: conventional magnets near each other have
well-understood F ∝ 1/r⁴ interactions. The homopolar inductive coupling of a SPINNING magnet
in a ROTATING FIELD is a second-order (rotational dynamics × electromagnetic induction)
interaction that goes beyond simple static or quasi-static Newtonian magnetic physics.
