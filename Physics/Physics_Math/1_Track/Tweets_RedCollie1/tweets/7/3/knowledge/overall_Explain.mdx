   * Three copper wire coils arranged in a 3-phase AC configuration create a rotating
     magnetic field (r.m.f.) of axial flux geometry.
   * This r.m.f. causes a ring magnet placed on a central rod to spin and simultaneously rise.
   * The 3-phase geometry is stated to differ critically from DC or single-phase fields;
     only 3-phase produces the combined spin+rise behavior.

2. COUNTER-SPIN MECHANISM — Lorentz Homopolar Force:
   * The downward reaction to the spinning magnet's upward force is identified as the
     "Lorentz homopolar force."
   * A second magnetic flywheel, spinning in the OPPOSITE direction and positioned in the
     same approximate XY plane (same height, further from center), is proposed to counteract
     this downward Lorentz reaction.
   * The reasoning: if the reaction acts on a fast-spinning object rather than a stationary
     structure, the fast-spinning object may redirect the applied force sideways by 90 degrees
     (Lorentz force analogy), effectively nullifying the downward component.

3. GYROSCOPIC FORCE REDIRECTION:
   * Analogy invoked: a fast-spinning top redirects any applied force perpendicular to the
     spin axis (a well-established result in classical mechanics: the gyroscopic precession
     torque τ = dL/dt = Ω × L, where Ω is the applied angular velocity perturbation and L
     is angular momentum). The claim is that this same principle, applied to a spinning
     magnetic flywheel, redirects the downward Newton's 3rd Law reaction into a lateral
     (horizontal) direction, leaving a net upward force.

4. ROD-TO-BORE DIAMETER RATIO CRITICAL PARAMETER:
   * The experiment (MDX 13_2007699282548404229_video.mdx) establishes that the ratio of
     rod outer diameter to magnet bore diameter is critical.
   * 5 mm rod / 5 mm bore: magnet pushes against the rod, does not rise.
   * 5 mm rod / 6 mm bore: partial effect, rises in one orientation only.
   * 5 mm rod / 8 mm bore: magnet rises in BOTH pole orientations — cited as evidence that
     the force is against the vacuum rather than against the magnetic pole of the rod end.
   * This ratio criterion is analogous to the aerodynamic criterion for lift (wing geometry
     must be "arranged correctly, just like for adjusting the shape of an airplane wing, to

5. SOLID-STATE TESLA COIL ANALOG (speculative connection):

mass) is achievable via a specific configuration of 3-phase rotating magnetic fields acting
on spinning ring magnets, where the bore-to-rod diameter ratio is tuned so that the magnet's

validated, this would constitute propellantless propulsion — eliminating the need for chemical
rockets (explicitly mentioned in note_tweet 2017877032907247916: "Elon Musk will no longer
need to use chemical rockets").
