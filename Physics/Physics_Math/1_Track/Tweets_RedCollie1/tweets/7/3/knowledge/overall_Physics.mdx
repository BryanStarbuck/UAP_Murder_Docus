## 1. Rotating Magnetic Field and Induced Torque

Source: YAML 2017877032907247916 (note_tweet), YAML 2021005921309385050 (note_tweet),
        MDX 13_2007699282548404229_video.mdx

### 3-Phase Rotating Field

For three coils with axes at 0°, 120°, 240° (in the XY-plane), carrying currents:

    I_A(t) = I₀ sin(ωt)
    I_B(t) = I₀ sin(ωt + 2π/3)
    I_C(t) = I₀ sin(ωt + 4π/3)

Each coil generates a magnetic field component along its axis. Superposition yields a
resultant rotating field of constant magnitude rotating at angular frequency ω:

    B_rot(t) = B₀ [cos(ωt) x̂ + sin(ωt) ŷ]

where B₀ = (3/2) μ₀ n I₀ for a solenoid-type geometry (n = turns per unit length).

This rotating field exerts a torque on a magnetic dipole moment m placed at the center:

    τ = m × B_rot(t)

For m = m ẑ (axially magnetized ring magnet), the torque is:

    τ = m ẑ × B₀ [cos(ωt) x̂ + sin(ωt) ŷ]
      = m B₀ [-sin(ωt) x̂ + cos(ωt) ŷ]

This torque vector rotates in the XY-plane, dragging the magnet into synchronous rotation
at ω (synchronous motor principle — the magnet "locks" to the rotating field if the load
torque is below the pull-out torque).

## 3. Newton's 3rd Law and the Counter-Spin Strategy

Source: YAML 2017877032907247916 (note_tweet), YAML 2007700816019562994 (note_tweet)

### The Problem

If the spinning magnet exerts an upward force F_up on itself, Newton's 3rd Law requires
an equal and opposite downward force F_down = -F_up on whatever the magnet is "pushing
against." If pushing against the rod → F_down is on the rod → on the apparatus → no net
upward motion. If pushing against the vacuum → F_down is on the vacuum (distributed into
the field), potentially allowing net lift.

Even in the vacuum-push interpretation:
* The 3-phase coil stator that drives the magnet also receives a reaction force.
* Drew states: "its strong, spinning 'upward' force is cancelled by an equal 'downward' force
  (spinning in the opposite direction) due to Newton's 3rd Law. So total weight on a gravity
  scale does not change."

### The Proposed Counter-Spin Solution

* Add an outer magnetic flywheel spinning in the OPPOSITE direction at the same XY-plane
  height, at a larger radius from the center.
* Physical reasoning (Drew's chain of logic):

  A. A fast-spinning gyroscope redirects any applied force perpendicular to the spin axis
     by 90°. This is the gyroscopic precession equation:
     τ = dL/dt,  where L = I Ω (angular momentum of flywheel, I = moment of inertia, Ω = spin rate)
     An applied torque τ_applied = r × F_applied (where F_applied is the downward Newton
     reaction force, r is the moment arm) causes precession:
     dL/dt = τ_applied  →  L precesses about the torque axis.
     For a horizontal spin axis (flywheel in XY-plane), an applied downward force (Z-axis)
     produces a torque that causes precession in the XY-plane (horizontal), effectively
     redirecting the Z-force into a horizontal direction.

  B. If the counter-spinning flywheel receives the downward Newton reaction force and
     redirects it into the XY-plane (horizontal), then no net downward force remains to
     cancel the upward force of the inner spinning magnet.

  C. The outer flywheel spins in the opposite direction to the inner magnet. This means:
     L_inner = I_inner Ω_inner ẑ (upward angular momentum vector)
     L_outer = -I_outer Ω_outer ẑ (downward angular momentum vector, opposite spin)
     Total angular momentum: L_total = L_inner - L_outer
     If I_inner Ω_inner = I_outer Ω_outer, then L_total = 0.
     A system with zero total angular momentum does not precess as a whole, but each
     component separately still experiences gyroscopic effects.

  D. The net result claimed: the downward Newton reaction is absorbed by the outer flywheel's
     gyroscopic precession (redirected to XY-plane horizontal motion), leaving the inner
     magnet's upward force uncancelled → net upward thrust.

## 4. The Lorentz Homopolar Force

### Homopolar (Faraday Disk) Generator Physics

A conducting disk of radius R rotating at angular velocity ω in an axial magnetic field B_z:
* Induced radial EMF: V = (1/2) ω B_z R²
* Radial current (if circuit closed): I_r = V / R_circuit
* Axial Lorentz force on the current-carrying disk: F_z = I_r × (2πR) × B_z (integrated over disk)
  More precisely: F_z = ∫₀ᴿ (I_r / R) × B_z × 2πr dr, integrated appropriately.
* This force opposes the rotation (Lenz's law), and has a component in the Z-direction.

In Drew's model, the spinning ring magnet in the axial r.m.f. acts as a homopolar generator:

* The resulting Lorentz force has a downward Z-component = the "counter-spin" force that

* The crop circle series (2012) is interpreted as encoding: slide 3 = 3-phase upward spin;
  slide 4 = Lorentz homopolar force as counter-spin / downward reaction.

## 6. Solid-State Tesla Coil — High-Frequency Discharge Physics

Source: YAML 1922922039230201873, txt files 3_1922921376442351616.txt, 3_1922921417860853760.txt

### Tesla Coil Operating Principles

* Primary circuit: capacitor C charged to voltage V₀; discharged into primary coil L_p
  via spark gap or solid-state switch (transistor in small models).
  Resonant frequency: f = 1/(2π√(L_p C))
* Secondary coil L_s: magnetically coupled to L_p; voltage step-up ratio = √(L_s/L_p)
  (for loosely coupled resonant transformer at matched frequencies).
* At resonance, energy oscillates between L and C in each circuit; peak secondary voltage:
  V_s = V₀ √(C_p/C_s) (ideal, lossless case).
* Visible discharge effects (from 3_1922921376442351616.txt):
  - Corona discharge: ionized air streamers from high field-gradient points (E > 3 MV/m).
  - Brush discharge (St. Elmo's fire type): diffuse glow at lower field strengths.
  - Transient arc discharge: spark channel with E >> 3 MV/m, current up to kA for μs.
* Drew's hypothesis (YAML 1922922039230201873): the Buga sphere's CT scan interior
  (mushroom-shaped internal inclusion in a dense metallic matrix) might be a solid-state
  Tesla coil. High-frequency "lightning emissions" from such a device could propel the sphere
  through space via directed plasma thrust.

### Physical Mechanism of Plasma Thrust

If the solid-state Tesla coil emits directed plasma via corona or arc discharge:
* Thrust estimate (reaction thrust): F = ṁ v_e where ṁ is mass ejection rate of ionized
  air/plasma and v_e is exhaust velocity.
* For typical corona discharge: ion density ~10¹⁴–10¹⁶ m⁻³, ion drift velocity ~100 m/s.
  Resulting thrust is very small (μN range for small devices).
* For high-energy arc pulses: F ≈ I² L / d (magnetic pressure on the arc channel of length d),
  reaching N-scale for kA currents.
