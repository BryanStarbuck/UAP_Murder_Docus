- Ball weight = m_ball × g = 1 kg × 9.81 m/s² = 9.81 N
- If the magnetic force lifts the ball, this force must originate from and be borne by the man/magnet system
- Therefore the man's effective downward force = 100 kg × g + 1 kg × g = 101 kg × g

The slide is making the point that electromagnetic coupling changes the distribution of gravitational weight without changing the total. This is used to introduce the concept that UAP craft may use electromagnetic fields to effectively redistribute or redirect gravitational forces — achieving apparent "weight reduction" of the craft by transferring the force to the surrounding field/medium.
