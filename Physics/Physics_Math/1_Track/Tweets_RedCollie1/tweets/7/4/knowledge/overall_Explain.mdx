## Core Claimed Advance: Rotating Magnetic Levitation as UAP Propulsion Principle

The central thesis developed across these source files is that UAP (UFO) flight is achieved through
a combination of rotating magnetic fields generated by multi-phase AC wire coil arrays, causing a
free-rotor magnet to simultaneously spin and rise along the Z-axis. The claimed advance beyond
Newtonian and classical electromagnetic mechanics is as follows:

### What Propulsion/Energy Benefit Is Achieved

* A permanent or electromagnet rotor placed at the center of a 3-phase or 5-phase wire coil array,
  when those coils are energized with three-phase (UVW) or five-phase AC power, will spontaneously
  spin and rise upward against gravity — achieving vertical lift (Z-axis displacement) from purely
  electromagnetic means.
* This is claimed to be a propulsive force distinct from classical magnetic levitation (Meissner
  effect, diamagnetic levitation, or passive Halbach array levitation), because it arises from a
  rotating field interacting with a free-rotor, not from static field opposition.
* The craft remains magnetically inert to the external environment: in the 6-coil N-S-N-S-N-S
  (alternating north/south) configuration, the net external magnetic dipole moment of the coil
  array cancels to zero. A UFO using this mechanism would appear non-magnetic at a distance —
  it would not attract ferromagnetic objects, towers, or metallic structures nearby.
  This is explicitly identified as a practically beneficial feature for a flying craft.

### The Specific Physics Advances Claimed to Enable This

1. Alternating N-S-N-S-N-S wire coil configuration with 3-phase power (UVW):
   Classical electromagnetic textbooks specify that a rotating magnetic field for induction motor
   action requires N-N-N-N-N-N pole orientation (all poles same direction, sequenced by phase).
   The experiment demonstrated that alternating N-S-N-S-N-S coil orientation ALSO produces
   rotation and levitation of a central rotor magnet. The author states: "All textbooks would
   suggest that those wire coils have to be N-N-N-N-N-N. The new result thus breaks all known
   'laws of physics'." (YAML 1927341040354169205, note_tweet)
   This is an experimental result, not a theoretical prediction.

2. Spin-induced Z-axis rise (levitation) without measurable anti-gravity weight change:
   The spinning magnet rotor rises vertically and presses against a stop — exerting measurable
   upward force — but the total weight of the device on a laboratory scale shows no change.
   This is interpreted as: the upward force does not reduce gravitational mass of the system;
   it is an electromagnetic thrust transferred through the rotating field, not a reduction of
   the gravitational interaction. The author explicitly notes: "There's no change of anti-gravity
   weight of the whole device on the scale." (video 13_1933725484875198470)
   This distinguishes the effect from claimed anti-gravity and situates it as electromagnetic
   thrust coupling.

3. Inversion test confirming magnetic (not mechanical) origin of the lift:
   To confirm the rise is magnetically driven rather than a mechanical artifact of bearing friction
   or rotation asymmetry, the magnet is flipped (north-for-south). A purely mechanical effect
   would cause the magnet to go down when flipped; instead, it still rises. This is taken as
   experimental confirmation that the levitation force is of magnetic origin, scaling with
   rotational speed: "The faster it goes, the more it goes up." (video 13_1933725484875198470)

4. 6-coil configuration achieving magnetic neutrality at distance:
   Six wire coils arranged with alternating N-S-N-S-N-S polarity, powered by 3-phase AC (UVW),
   produce a rotating field that causes a central disc magnet (70mm diameter, 20mm central hole)
   to spin and rise. The three north-pole and three south-pole coils are symmetrically arranged
   so that their external fields cancel. Claimed benefit: the craft is electromagnetically
   invisible to ferromagnetic materials at a distance. (video 13_1927339091730817024)

5. 5-phase coil configuration (Buga sphere hypothesis):
   The Buga sphere (claimed UAP artifact, crash-landed in Colombia) shows "5 lunar crescents"
   interpreted as 5-phase AC power connections to 5 wire coils arranged in a ring around a
   central rotor. The hypothesis is that 5-phase power drives a rapidly rotating magnetic field
   which spins a magnetic gyroscope rotor on a central pole. The gyroscopic angular momentum
   then generates an upward precession force opposing gravity. (YAML 1942032833985560854, note_tweet)
   This is explicitly labeled a working hypothesis to be tested in the lab.

6. Spin/counter-spin mechanism (jumping ring analogy):
   Crop circle formations from July/August 2012 are interpreted as encoding a two-step mechanism:
   "spin" and "counter-spin", analogous to a jumping ring experiment, produce the upward thrust.
   The 3-phase magnetic rotation provides the spin; a counter-rotating element provides the
   counter-spin. (YAML 1978592179171004542, referenced_tweets text)

7. Magnetic flywheel (Leedskalnin connection):
   A small-scale replication of the magnetic flywheel attributed to Ed Leedskalnin (Coral Castle,
   1930s) is referenced as an independently discovered instance of the same physics — electromagnetic
   rotation generating both lift and electricity generation. (YAML 2014101401836597500, referenced text)

8. Gyroscopic rotor on a pole as anti-gravity mechanism:
   The current working hypothesis for the Buga sphere specifically: 5-phase AC creates a
   fast-rotating magnetic field which spins a magnetic gyroscope rotor. Gyroscopes "DO sit on a
   stable pole." The upward force arises from gyroscopic stability interacting with the pole axis
   in a gravitational field. Whether this produces a net upward force against gravity is explicitly
   stated as requiring laboratory testing. (YAML 1942032833985560854)
