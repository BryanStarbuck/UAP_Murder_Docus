## Primary Lift Mechanism: Spinning Magnet in Rotating Field Rises Along Z-Axis

### Core Experimental Observation

A disc magnet (permanent magnet) placed at the center of a wire coil array, when the coils
are energized with 3-phase AC power (UVW), simultaneously:
  (a) Spins in the XY plane (rotates around the Z-axis), driven by the rotating magnetic field
  (b) Rises along the Z-axis (levitates upward against gravity)

This combined spin-rise behavior is the central lift mechanism proposed for UAP propulsion.
The XY-plane spin is the prerequisite for the Z-axis rise. No spin = no rise.

Source: Video 13_1933725484875198470 — laboratory demonstration with measurement.

### Experimental Parameters for Demonstrated Lift

Configuration 1 (3 coils, 3-phase, with stop):
  Magnet: 70mm diameter, 14mm hole, mounted on 6.3mm aluminum rod
  Coil arrangement: 3 wire coils, UVW 3-phase, pointing inward
  Result: magnet spins -> rises -> presses against silicon bead stops
  Force measured: "quite a lot of force there, rising and holding up"
  Scale weight: NO CHANGE — total device weight unchanged during levitation

Configuration 2 (3 coils, 3-phase, without stop):
  Same magnet, same coils, stop removed
  Result: magnet rises periodically to a critical height h_c, loses field coupling, falls,
  then rises again — periodic oscillation in Z
  Observation: "without any stops on the pole, a millimeter magnet with a spinning will
  just rise up in the air periodically over those three phase coils. When it gets to a
  certain height it loses the magnetic field and falls down again."
  Physical interpretation: F_z(h) > m*g for h < h_c; F_z(h) < m*g for h > h_c.
  The coil field falls off with height faster than gravity, creating an unstable equilibrium.

Configuration 3 (6 coils, 3-phase, N-S-N-S-N-S):
  Magnet: 70mm diameter, 20mm hole
  Larger version: 80mm diameter magnet, 6mm rod, 10mm hole
  Coil arrangement: 6 wire coils, alternating N-S-N-S-N-S polarity, UVW 3-phase
  Result: same spin-and-rise behavior as 3-coil configuration
  Added property: near-zero external magnetic field (coils cancel at distance)
  Application: UAP would appear magnetically inert to environment while levitating

Configuration 4 (3 coils on top of sphere, Buga sphere simulation):
  Coils tilted inward as they would sit on a sphere surface
  Silicon bead stops at top
  Magnet below the coils (rotor hangs below)
  Result: "the magnet will start spinning rapidly, go up to the balls. And it'll start
  pushing the pole up with periodically. It's certainly getting some rise there."
  Assessment: erratic, partial results; "an interesting effect to be studied further"

### Speed Scaling of Lift Force

"The faster it goes, the more it goes up." (Video 13_1933725484875198470)

The Z-axis lift force F_z increases monotonically with rotor angular velocity omega_z.
This is stated as an empirical observation. No analytical expression is given in the source
materials for F_z(omega). However, dimensional analysis and the jumping-ring analogy suggest:

  For a jumping ring: <F_z> proportional to omega^2 * R^2 / (R^2 + omega^2 * L^2)
  At high frequencies (omega*L >> R): <F_z> approaches a saturation value ~ I_0^2 / (omega*L)

For a spinning permanent magnet rotor, the XY-plane rotation frequency of the rotor
omega_rotor determines how rapidly its dipole field rotates. A faster-spinning rotor
interacts more vigorously with the spatial gradient of the coil field, potentially
producing larger F_z. Exact functional form: undetermined from source materials.

### Lift Verification Protocol

The inversion test (flip magnet north-for-south) is used to distinguish magnetic from
mechanical lift origin:
  Mechanical artifact (bearing friction, gyroscopic precession of mechanically spinning disc):
    Flipping magnet polarity: no effect on direction of motion
  Magnetic lift:
    Flipping magnet changes coupling to field. For some configurations, lift might reverse.
    But observed result: "again we see it goes up" — lift persists after flip.
  Conclusion: the force is magnetically driven and robust to polarity reversal.
  Implication: the force mechanism depends on field interaction, not the sign of the dipole.
  This is unexpected — further theoretical work needed.

### Connection Between XY-Plane Rotation and Z-Axis Force

The connection between in-plane rotation (omega_z) and axial lift (F_z) is the central
unexplained physics question in these source materials. Several analogies are invoked:

1. Jumping ring (Lenz's law): AC field induces currents in a conducting ring, producing F_z.
   Here: permanent magnet rotor replaces conducting ring; rotating field replaces AC solenoid.
   The coupling mechanism: the spinning rotor creates a time-varying flux linkage with the
   coil array equivalent to a rotating EMF, inducing reactive forces.

2. Spin/counter-spin (cited from 2012 crop circles):
   "This series of four crop pictures from July or August of 2012 told us EXACTLY how UFOs fly:
   by 'spin' and 'counter-spin', like for a 'jumping ring'."
   The counter-rotating element (secondary rotor, flywheel, or field) creates the opposing force
   needed for net upward thrust.

3. Periodic low-spin/high-spin transition:
   Lab observation (YAML 2043717838167535986): spinning magnet (blue) oscillates up-down relative
   to stationary flywheel (red, with 4 attractive magnets above it). This is interpreted as a
   periodic transition between "low-spin geometry" (rotor at lower Z, stronger coupling) and
   "high-spin geometry" (rotor at higher Z, weaker coupling). The hypothesis: this captures and
   releases gravitational potential energy each cycle, like a two-stroke engine.

### Gravity-Related Claims

Claim: "no change of anti-gravity weight of the whole device on the scale"
  This is a NEGATIVE result for true anti-gravity (gravitational mass reduction).
  The upward force on the rotor is an internal electromagnetic force, not a reduction of
  gravitational coupling. The device does not become lighter; it generates internal thrust.

Claim: "see if we can understand the basic principles of anti-gravity" (Video 13_1933725484875198470)
  This is aspirational language. The experimental result itself does not demonstrate anti-gravity
  in the sense of gravitational mass reduction. The author uses "anti-gravity" loosely to mean
  "upward force opposing gravity" rather than "modification of gravitational coupling constant."

Hypothesis for Buga sphere: "Upward force to oppose downward gravity?" (YAML 1942032833985560854)
  A gyroscope on a pole, spun at high angular velocity by 5-phase rotating field, might generate
  upward force. This is explicitly framed as "nobody knows, we have to test this in the lab."
  Standard physics: gyroscope does NOT generate net upward force in uniform gravity field.
  Non-standard claim: requires experimental test.

### Five-Coil (5-Phase) Buga Sphere Lift Hypothesis

The Buga sphere shows 5 "lunar crescents" at top. These are interpreted as 5-phase AC coil
connections. The 5 coils produce a rotating field with amplitude (5/2)*B_0. This field spins
a magnetic gyroscope rotor on a central pole at very high angular velocity.

Proposed mechanism: gyroscopic rotor on pole + gravitational torque produces precession.
If precession somehow produces net axial (Z-axis) force — not yet established.

Alternative mechanism: the same spin-rise effect as demonstrated with 3/6-coil configurations,
but enhanced by 5 coils and 5-phase power. First approximation tested: 3 coils arranged as
they would be on a sphere surface. Result: partial, erratic rise — promising but inconclusive.
