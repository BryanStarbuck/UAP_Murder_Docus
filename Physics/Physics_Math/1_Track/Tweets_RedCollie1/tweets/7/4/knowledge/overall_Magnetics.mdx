The rotating magnetic field is the central magnetic phenomenon across all source files.
A rotating magnetic field is produced when multi-phase AC current is supplied to spatially
distributed wire coils. In a 3-phase system (phases U, V, W), each coil is energized with
a sinusoidal current displaced 120 degrees in time phase. The superposition of the three
spatial fields, each oriented at 120 degrees to the others and time-shifted by 120 degrees,
produces a resultant field vector that rotates continuously in the XY plane at the AC supply
frequency.

The author refers to these rotating field patterns in crop circles as "wavy symbols" or

### XY-Plane Dynamics (Spin / Rotation in the Horizontal Plane)

The rotating magnetic field vector rotates continuously in the XY plane at the supply
frequency. For 3-phase 50 Hz AC, the field completes 50 full rotations per second (or 60
rotations/second at 60 Hz). The central rotor magnet aligns with this rotating field and
is dragged into synchronous or near-synchronous rotation — standard reluctance motor
behavior for the rotation component.

The XY-plane rotation is the prerequisite condition for the Z-axis rise. The rise is
observed to scale with rotational speed: "The faster it goes, the more it goes up." This
implies the Z-axis thrust force F_z is a monotonically increasing function of angular
velocity omega_z of the rotor.

### Z-Axis Dynamics (Levitation / Rise Component)

The Z-axis force is the novel experimental finding. In standard induction motor theory,
the rotor experiences torque in the XY plane but not a net axial (Z-axis) force, because
the coil geometry is assumed symmetric about the Z axis and the rotor is constrained
within the stator bore. In this configuration, the rotor is a free disc magnet above
the coil plane, and the axial component of the force is observed to be upward.

The mechanism hypothesized (not fully derived in source materials):
* As the disc magnet spins in the rotating field, its own dipole field rotates

* This is analogous to the "jumping ring" experiment: a conducting ring above an AC-energized
  solenoid is repelled upward by the induced eddy currents. Here, a permanent magnet rotor
  replaces the conducting ring, with the rotating field replacing the AC solenoid.

### Magnetic Neutrality Property (6-Coil N-S-N-S-N-S)

The 6-coil alternating N-S configuration achieves external field cancellation by symmetry.
In a ring of 6 coils at 60-degree spacing, with alternating north-south orientation:
* Each N-coil dipole moment: m_N = n * I * A (pointing inward, +z)
* Each S-coil dipole moment: m_S = n * I * A (pointing outward, -z)
* Scalar sum: 3 * m_N + 3 * (-m_N) = 0

At distances large compared to the ring radius R, the external field of the array is dominated
by the dipole term, which is zero. Higher-order multipole terms (quadrupole, octupole) decay
as R^-3, R^-5, etc. The practical result is near-zero external field at moderate distances.

non-magnetic from the distance." (Video 13_1927339091730817024)
