## I. Attraction-to-Repulsion Transition Under Counter-Rotation ("Delta" Effect)

Source: video transcript 13_2043978897302401026_video.mdx; YAML 2043979750209925538 (note_tweet)

Experimental description (video transcript):
"What is essentially happening here, new to physics on Earth, is that we can have a magnet
here, attracted to this center of a magnetic flywheel here when there's no spin motion.
So there's no UFO lift there, but when we get spin motions of the magnet and the flywheel
in opposite directions, then we get a repulsion between the magnet and the flywheel, which
I call delta, which starts spinning and they have to move apart, and that seems to be quite

From the note_tweet (2043979750209925538):
"The careful study of alien crop pictures has led me to discover a new kind of physics, where
a non-spinning magnetic attraction turns into a spinning magnetic repulsion."

Classical electromagnetic prediction: Maxwell electromagnetism does not predict that the sign
of the force between two permanent magnets changes when those magnets are set into counter-rotation
about their mutual axis. The magnetostatic force F = −∇U between two dipole moments m_1 and m_2
depends on their spatial configuration and orientations, not on their angular velocities (in the
non-relativistic regime). The Lorentz force on moving charges does add velocity-dependent terms
(F = q(E + v×B)), but for macroscopic bulk spin of a magnet at sub-relativistic peripheral
velocities, the net force correction is small.

What the source claims: the force sign change occurs at a macroscopic, readily observable level,
not at a tiny correction level. This is presented as experimentally observed, not predicted by
any existing classical or quantum theory.

Possible extensions of known physics that could accommodate this:
(a) Eddy-current (Lenz-law) repulsion dominating over static attraction at high angular velocity:
    This IS a classical effect. A conducting spinning flywheel in the field of a spinning magnet
    experiences Lenz-law braking. If the flywheel is also magnetic (permanent magnet material),
    the induced currents oppose the changing flux from the counter-rotating magnet. At high enough
    relative angular velocity, the Lenz-law repulsive force magnitude could exceed the static
    dipole attractive force. This would be classical (Faraday/Lenz), but the claim that it
    produces net lift force (axial thrust) would be non-classical.
(b) Macroscopic spin-spin interaction analogous to quantum exchange:
    In quantum mechanics, the exchange interaction between two spin-1/2 particles is
    E_exchange = -2J S_1 · S_2, where J > 0 → ferromagnetic (parallel spins attract / align),
    J < 0 → antiferromagnetic (antiparallel spins repel). Drew's framework treats macroscopic
    counter-rotating magnets as macroscopic analogs of antiparallel quantum spins → repulsion.
    This is beyond Newtonian mechanics and beyond standard macroscopic EM.
(c) Velocity-dependent force law at macroscopic scale:
    A term of the form F_delta = α × ω_1 × ω_2 × r̂ (where ω_1, ω_2 are the angular velocities
    of the two magnets and r̂ is the displacement unit vector between them) would produce
    attraction when spins are parallel (same sign, product positive) and repulsion when
    counter-rotating (opposite signs, product negative). This would be a new macroscopic
    force law not derivable from Maxwell equations or Newton's law of gravitation.

Drew labels the effect "new to physics on Earth," distinguishing it from all three classical
explanations above.

## II. Vortex Magnetism: Weight Non-Transfer Under Rotational Field Interaction

Source: TXT files 3_1914620064239902722.txt, 3_1914620258562007040.txt, 3_1914620299041234945.txt

The "vortex magnetism" concept:
* Static dipole levitation: iron ball (1 kg) levitated → apparent weight 0 kg; man's weight
  → 101 kg. Newton's 3rd law is preserved — the weight transfer is through the magnetic force
  path. This is classical and well-understood.
* Vortex (rotating) magnetism: iron ball rises to height h → apparent weight REMAINS 1 kg;
  man's weight REMAINS 100 kg. The ball has acquired E_p = mgh > 0 without any change in the
  weight distribution of the system.

Beyond Newtonian: Newton's laws in the static field case require that the lifting force on the
ball be balanced by an equal and opposite force somewhere in the system (ultimately grounded to
Earth through the man). The vortex magnetism claim is that this reaction-force path is broken —
the reaction is borne by the rotating field rather than the man. This is consistent with EM
momentum conservation (the rotating field carries momentum density g = ε₀(E×B)) but is NOT
consistent with naive mechanical Newton's third law if one treats the rotating field as external
to the system.

Beyond classical EM: classical rotating magnetic fields (produced by AC induction motors) do
produce torques on ferromagnetic materials, but the reaction torques are transmitted through the
stator mounting — they are mechanically grounded. The claim that the reaction is "absorbed by
the field" without mechanical grounding is non-standard.

## V. 3-Phase Field Cancellation Problem and the Proposed Solution

Source: YAML 1963193369116528702 (text and referenced_tweet)

Text: "If we simply place three coils with clockwise 3-phase power near three coils with
anti-clockwise 3-phase power, everything cancels. But there might be another way to do this!"

Referenced tweet: "I am beginning some new experiments on UFO levitation soon. It is a 'magnetic
cube-puzzle' which friendly ET's want us to solve! Lorentz spin anti-clockwise (think 'Corbino
disc'), then 3-phase spin clockwise."

The cancellation problem is a classical electromagnetic result: two sets of identically configured
coils with opposite phase sequences produce a zero net rotating field at their common center.
This is a well-known null result in AC motor design.

The proposed beyond-Newtonian approach: use TWO DIFFERENT PHYSICAL MECHANISMS for the two
counter-rotations:
1. Corbino disc geometry → Lorentz force → anti-clockwise spin of inner disc/rotor
2. 3-phase AC coil arrangement → rotating magnetic field → clockwise spin of outer flywheel

Because these are two different interaction pathways (Lorentz force on current-carrying conductor
vs. induction on ferromagnetic rotor), they do NOT cancel each other. Each mechanism acts on a
different physical object:
* Corbino Lorentz torque acts on the inner conducting disc (spins it anti-clockwise)
* 3-phase induction torque acts on the outer magnetic flywheel (spins it clockwise)

The result: two independently-spinning components, rotating in opposite directions, without the
mutual cancellation that plagues two sets of counter-wound coils acting at the same location.

This is not beyond classical EM — both the Corbino torque and the 3-phase induction are
classical. The non-classical claim is that combining them to achieve counter-rotation produces
the "delta" repulsion and net axial lift force.

## VII. Magnetic Gyroscope as UAP Propulsion Core

Source: Referenced tweet of 1942033499692945417 (Buga sphere hypothesis)

"They may create a fast-rotating magnetic field to spin up a 'magnetic' gyroscope rotor.
Gyroscopes [provide lift / prevent angular deflection]."

A "magnetic gyroscope" is a gyroscope rotor made of magnetized material (permanent magnet or
ferromagnet magnetized along its spin axis). When it spins at high ω, its angular momentum
L = I × ω is aligned with its spin axis (vertical). The rotating external field (from the
5-phase coils) applies a torque τ = m × B to the magnetic moment m of the gyroscope.

Classical gyroscope physics: τ = dL/dt = Ω_p × L → precession, no axial translation.

Beyond-classical claim: the counter-rotation of the gyroscope's own field (as it spins with
moment m) against the applied external rotating field B produces the "delta" repulsion directed
along the gyroscope's spin axis (Z). This is the axial force claim — gyroscopes in classical
mechanics do NOT self-levitate; the claim of axial force from magnetic gyroscope counter-rotation
is non-classical.

The Buga sphere is presented as a physical example of this device in operational use.
