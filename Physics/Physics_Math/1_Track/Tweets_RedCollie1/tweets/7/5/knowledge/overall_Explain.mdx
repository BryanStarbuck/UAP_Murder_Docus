* Conservation of momentum and Newton's third law are preserved: force on ball upward equals force on man downward.
* No gravitational potential energy is gained "for free" — the energy cost is borne by whatever holds the magnet, via the man's weight increase.

### Stage 2 — Vortex Magnetism (Rotating Field)
* The same man now uses "vortex magnetism" — a rotating or helical magnetic field configuration — to make the iron ball rise "like a top."

* Yet the iron ball has risen — it now has greater gravitational potential energy E_p = mgh = 1 kg × g × h, where h > 0.
* Implication drawn: gravitational potential energy was gained without a corresponding decrease in the mechanical/potential energy of the man or the magnetic field source.

The source of the anomalous potential energy gain is claimed to be the rotating magnetic field itself — specifically, that a rotating (vortex) magnetic field can couple to an external energy reservoir (zero-point field, quantum vacuum) and transfer energy into the mechanical system, enabling levitation without conventional work input.

## Advancement Beyond Classical Electromagnetism

The claimed advance: standard Maxwell electromagnetism predicts that a rotating magnetic field (produced by 3-phase or 5-phase AC coil arrangements) will exert torque on ferromagnetic or conducting rotors (standard induction motor principle), but predicts zero net axial (vertical) force in free space absent a gradient in the field.

The experimental claim reported in the video: counter-rotation produces a macroscopic repulsion ("delta") not predicted by classical linear superposition of magnetic dipole fields. If confirmed, this would imply either:
(a) A velocity-dependent correction to the magnetic force law at macroscopic scale — analogous to the Abraham-Lorentz force but for macroscopic spin states; or
(b) A spin-spin interaction term (akin to the Barnett effect or the Einstein-de Haas effect at macroscopic scale) that becomes dominant under counter-rotation geometry.

The stated benefit: this "new physics" — if reproducible and scalable — would enable propulsion by pure electromagnetic means, with no propellant, no reaction mass, and power potentially drawn from the rotating magnetic field itself (which in turn could couple to the quantum vacuum).

## 5-Phase vs. 3-Phase: Efficiency Claim

Tweet 1969904219017900157:
"rotating AC magnetic field — 5-phase is more powerful than 3-phase"

The physical basis for this claim: a rotating magnetic field produced by n-phase AC is more spatially uniform (approaches a true rotating vector field) as n increases. For n=3, the field magnitude oscillates with a residual ripple of 1/3 the peak amplitude. For n=5, the ripple is smaller (~1/5). A more perfectly circular rotating field reduces torque ripple on the rotor and could improve coupling efficiency between the rotating field and the spinning flywheel components.

This is a known engineering result in AC motor design; Drew's claim is that a more perfect rotating field is also more effective at producing the "vortex magnetism" lift effect.
