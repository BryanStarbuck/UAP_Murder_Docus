## 1. Rotating Magnetic Field (RMF) — 3-Phase and 5-Phase

### 3-Phase RMF (U-V-W)
Three coils displaced by 120 degrees in space, driven by three AC currents displaced by 120 degrees in phase (U, V, W). The resulting superposition of three sinusoidal magnetic field contributions produces a composite field vector of constant magnitude that rotates at the AC supply frequency:

    B_total(t) = B_U(t) + B_V(t) + B_W(t)

where B_U(t) = B0 * cos(omega*t) * e_U, etc., with e_U, e_V, e_W the unit vectors along each coil axis. The result is a rotating field vector in the plane defined by the three coil axes.

Setup: A permanent bar magnet (N-S oriented horizontally) is attached to a Dremel rotary tool and spun at high RPM. A second permanent magnet (N-S oriented, stationary or loosely suspended above) responds by rising vertically ("way up").

### Physics Analysis of the XY-Plane vs. Z-Axis Dynamics

XY-plane dynamics: The spinning permanent magnet creates a time-varying magnetic field. At any fixed point above the spinning magnet, the field oscillates as the magnet rotates. For a magnet rotating at angular velocity omega_r in the XY plane, the field at a point on the Z-axis (axial point) from a dipole is:

    B_z(t) = (mu_0 / 4pi) * (3(m . hat_r)hat_r - m) / r^3

where m = m_0 * (cos(omega_r * t) * e_x + sin(omega_r * t) * e_y) for a horizontal dipole rotating in the XY plane.

On the symmetry axis (Z-axis), the field from a horizontal rotating dipole averages to zero in the XY components but generates an oscillating z-component:

    B_z(t) = -(mu_0 * m_0 / 4pi * r^3) * [angular terms dependent on theta]

The time average of B_z from a horizontally rotating dipole at a point on the rotation axis is zero by symmetry. Therefore the standard time-averaged Kelvin force on a second magnet placed on the Z-axis above is also zero to first order. The observation of sustained lift ("the magnet goes way up") is therefore anomalous under classical magnetostatics alone.

Z-axis rise mechanism (author's claim): The author attributes this to "vortex magnetism" — the rotating field creates a vortical (helical) field geometry that couples to the Earth's gravitational field or quantum vacuum, producing a net Z-axis force. This is the non-classical element of the claim.

### Barnett Effect and Gyromagnetic Analogies
Text slides (3_1914620624045268995.txt and 3_1914620679762407424.txt) invoke the spinning magnet analogy:

    "A fast-spinning UFO will act almost like a 'spinning magnet' as it rises, with no change of weight."
    "If we could make a rotating-magnetic field rise up WITH that spinning magnet, then we would have 'UFO levitation'."

These slides reference:
- Barnett effect: a spinning body (especially ferromagnetic) acquires magnetization proportional to its angular velocity. For a classical body: M = (chi_m * omega) / gamma, where gamma is the gyromagnetic ratio and chi_m is the magnetic susceptibility.
- Homopolar generator (Faraday disc): a conducting disk rotating in an axial magnetic field generates a radial EMF: V = (1/2) * omega * B * (R2^2 - R1^2), where R1, R2 are inner and outer radii and omega is the angular velocity. This is the inverse of the Corbino disc propulsion setup.
- Einstein-de Haas effect: mechanical angular momentum and magnetic moment are coupled; a change in magnetization of a ferromagnet causes physical rotation. The inverse (rotation causing magnetization) is the Barnett effect.

## 6. Vortex Magnetism — Theoretical Claim vs. Classical EM

The author's slides (3_1914620463751524352.txt) state:
    "This may be why many UFOs have been seen to spin rapidly as they 'rise up'."

The argument chain:
    rapid spinning -> vortex magnetic field -> gravitational potential energy gained from vacuum/field -> apparent weight-free ascent.

Classical EM objection noted in 3_1914620624045268995.txt:
    "The claim of 'no change of weight' for vortex magnetism contradicts conventional physics — in standard electromagnetic theory, magnetic forces do no work on charged particles moving perpendicular to the field (F = qv x B), so they cannot provide net energy for levitation against gravity without an energy source."

This is the key unresolved tension: the Lorentz force on a moving charge in a magnetic field is always perpendicular to the velocity, doing no work. Sustained levitation against gravity requires work input equal to mgh per unit height gained. The author's vortex magnetism mechanism does not identify the energy source for this work.
