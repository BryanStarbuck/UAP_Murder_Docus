- Reduced ripple when rectified: useful if DC bias is needed for inner power-spin magnet drive

## 3. No Claim of Over-Unity or Free Energy in This Corpus

The author does not claim free energy or energy beyond conservation laws in this batch of tweets. The energy budget of the proposed drive is:
- Input: AC electrical power (3-phase or 5-phase)
- Output: rotational kinetic energy (inner magnet power-spin + outer flywheel counter-spin) + claimed axial δ force (lift)

The δ force is proposed to do positive work (lifting the craft), which would require energy input commensurate with the work done against gravity. The author's physics claim is about the mechanism of force generation, not about circumventing energy conservation.

However, the non-Newtonian character of δ (if real) would represent a new energy coupling pathway that is not accounted for in standard electromagnetic motor efficiency calculations. The efficiency of converting AC electrical input to Z-axis lift force via the δ mechanism is unknown and not quantified in the source materials.

## 8. External Energy Source from Continuous Rotation

No specific claim of energy beyond conservation of energy is made in this corpus. The author describes an electric-motor-like input (3-phase or 5-phase AC), with the novel output being Z-axis force rather than (or in addition to) azimuthal torque.

If the δ force is real, the energy budget would be:
  E_input (AC electrical) → E_kinetic (counter-rotation) + W_lift (δ × displacement)

This would be fully consistent with energy conservation — the δ force is doing work against gravity at the expense of electrical input energy. The "beyond Newtonian" character is in the mechanism of transducing rotational kinetic energy into axial force, not in the thermodynamic energy balance.
