## 1. Rotating Magnetic Field (Tesla-Type, 2-Phase and 3-Phase)

From tweet 1960833745004716041:
> "The slide at upper left shows Tesla's first 2-phase induction motor with a rotating magnetic field."

Tesla's 2-phase induction motor produces a rotating magnetic field by supplying two windings with currents 90 degrees out of phase. The resultant magnetic field vector rotates uniformly in the XY-plane at the supply frequency ω. For 2-phase:

  B_x(t) = B_0 cos(ωt)
  B_y(t) = B_0 sin(ωt)
  |B| = B_0 (constant magnitude, rotating direction)

The author extends this to 3-phase (120-degree phase separation):

  B_x(t) = B_0 [cos(ωt) + cos(ωt - 2π/3) + cos(ωt - 4π/3)]
  (vectorially sums to a rotating field of constant magnitude)

This 3-phase rotating field is the core driver mechanism for UFO levitation in the author's model.

From tweet 1966268286666158144:

In the context of the UFO drive hypothesis, bifilar winding could be used to tune the resonant frequency of the coil assemblies to match the desired rotation rate of the magnetic flywheel.
