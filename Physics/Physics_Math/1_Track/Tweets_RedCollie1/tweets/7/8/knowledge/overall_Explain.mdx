## Core Propulsion / Levitation Hypothesis

The central claim advanced across this tweet corpus is that UAP propulsion operates via a
combination of (1) a rapidly spinning electromagnetic disc (the Corbino disc geometry) acting
as the interior rotor, and (2) an external three-phase rotating magnetic field that provides
the outer "stator" excitation. Drew labels this "spinning three-phase magnetism outside, with
an electromagnetic Corbino disc inside." He states explicitly: "That is how UFOs fly."

The Corbino disc is a flat conducting disc with radial current flow (center to rim or rim to
center), subject to an axial magnetic field. In the standard homopolar/Corbino geometry, the
Lorentz force F = q(v x B) on radial current carriers produces a tangential (azimuthal) torque,
driving disc rotation without commutation. In Drew's proposed configuration this geometry is
nested inside a three-phase polyphase rotating magnetic field. The three-phase field pattern
rotates at angular frequency omega_s (synchronous speed), and the inner Corbino disc responds
with its own Lorentz-driven spin omega_r. Drew labels this interaction "Lorentz spin, then a
3-phase counter-spin."

The proposed beneficial output: if the Corbino disc geometry is oriented with its axis
vertical (Z-axis), and the three-phase exterior field rotates in a plane transverse to
gravity (XY-plane), the claimed result is net vertical (Z-axis) levitation of the entire
device assembly. Drew compares this to the observed behavior in his coil experiments: a
spinning permanent magnet was observed to rise up a vertical pole when driven by three-phase
power, whereas the coil assembly encasing it did not rise. This asymmetry is the central
empirical puzzle he is attempting to engineer toward.

The claimed physics enabling the benefit:
* Radial current in axial B-field produces azimuthal torque (Lorentz): tau = I * integral(r x (J_r x B_z) dV)
* Three-phase rotation of outer field induces a counter-rotating torque on the inner disc
  via induced EMF and eddy-current reaction
* Angular momentum conservation coupling: when the inner disc reverses spin direction
  (anti-clockwise to clockwise flip), the outer flywheel counter-spins faster to conserve
  total L_total = L_inner + L_outer = constant
* Claimed but not yet demonstrated: this coupled counter-spin produces a net axial (vertical)
  force component sufficient to partially or fully cancel gravitational acceleration g

The mercury-vimana hypothesis: Drew proposes that replacing the three-phase wire coils
with a spinning liquid mercury torus (driven by low-voltage DC) as the spin source, combined
with an embedded permanent magnet, might produce the same or stronger anti-gravity effect
with only two DC wire connections (eliminating complex three-phase drive electronics). This
references the ancient Indian "vimana" texts which describe mercury-vortex propulsion.

The spinning-gyroscope gravity-suppression model: Drew proposes that gravity is a "downward
pressure into any large mass (essentially a spherical volume of low vacuum-pressure)." If a
"spinning vortex through time" (a rapidly rotating electromagnetic or gyroscopic system) is
superimposed upon that downward pressure field, spinning objects are "held up at high
potential-energy while overall downward pressure remains the same." He cites spinning
gyroscopes and magnets spinning in rotating magnetic fields as first-order empirical
demonstrations of this effect.

Lenz levitation reduction result (experimental, confirmed by Drew as not-in-textbooks):
When a fast-spinning aluminum disc becomes gyroscopic, its Lenz-effect repulsion of a
nearby AC magnetic field drops to approximately 20% of the repulsion magnitude produced
by the same disc when stationary. Quantitatively: spinning disc Lenz repulsion =
0.20 x (stationary disc Lenz repulsion). This 80% reduction in downward repulsive
strength was experimentally confirmed. Drew interprets this as a non-trivial coupling
between gyroscopic angular momentum and electromagnetic induction efficiency, outside
standard textbook treatment.

Device geometry described for the UFO propulsion experiment (four sets):
* Outer ring: four sets of three-phase wire coils arranged circumferentially
* Inner rotor: four permanent magnets arranged N-S-N-S (alternating pole, quadrupole)
* The three-phase field sequence is U-V-W-U-V-W rotating at high frequency
* At low excitation frequencies: inner magnet disc spins anti-clockwise (attracted to
  outer flywheel NN-SS poles)
* At high excitation frequencies: inner disc spins clockwise (three-phase U-V-W field
  dominates over weak attraction to outer flywheel)
* Transition (spin flip): when inner magnet flips from anti-clockwise to clockwise,
  outer flywheel counter-spins faster to conserve total angular momentum L
