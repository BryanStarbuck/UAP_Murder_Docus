## 1. Lorentz Torque — Corbino / Faraday Disc

Force on radial current element dl in axial field B_z:
  dF = I (dl x B) = I * dr * (hat{r} x B_z * hat{z}) = I * B_z * dr * (-hat{phi})

Torque element about the axis:
  dtau = r * dF_phi = -I * B_z * r * dr

Integrated torque for Corbino disc (inner radius r1, outer radius r2):
  tau = I * B_z * integral_{r1}^{r2} r dr = I * B_z * (r2^2 - r1^2) / 2

This is the net azimuthal (XY-plane) torque that drives rotation. Direction: sign of
I (radial current direction) cross sign of B_z (axial field direction).

## 2. Electromagnetic Induction — Faraday's Law and Lenz's Law

Induced EMF in disc of area A from time-varying axial flux:
  epsilon = -d(Phi_B)/dt = -A * dB_z/dt

For sinusoidal driving: B_z(t) = B_0 * sin(omega * t)
  epsilon = -A * B_0 * omega * cos(omega * t)
  |epsilon_peak| = A * B_0 * omega

Skin depth in conductor (relevant to eddy current penetration):
  delta_s = sqrt(2 * rho / (mu_0 * omega))
  = sqrt(2 / (mu_0 * sigma * omega))

where rho = resistivity, sigma = electrical conductivity, mu_0 = 4*pi*10^-7 H/m.

For aluminum: sigma_Al ~ 3.5 x 10^7 S/m
  delta_s(Al) = sqrt(2 / (4*pi*10^-7 * 3.5*10^7 * omega))
  delta_s(Al) = sqrt(2 / (4.4 * omega)) [meters, omega in rad/s]

For mercury: sigma_Hg ~ 1.04 x 10^6 S/m at 20 deg C
  rho_Hg = 1 / sigma_Hg ~ 9.6 x 10^-7 Ohm*m

Lenz force scaling:
  F_Lenz ~ (sigma * omega * B_0^2 * delta_s * A) in the high-frequency (thin-skin) limit
         ~ sigma^{1/2} * omega^{1/2} * B_0^2 * A   (substituting delta_s)

## 4. Angular Momentum Conservation

Total angular momentum of closed system (no external torque, tau_ext = 0):
  L_total = L_outer + L_inner = I_outer * omega_outer + I_inner * omega_inner = constant

For the inner-magnet spin flip (anti-clockwise -> clockwise):
  delta_omega_inner = -2 * omega_inner  (sign reversal)
  delta_L_inner = I_inner * (-2 * omega_inner)
  delta_L_outer = -delta_L_inner = I_inner * 2 * omega_inner
  omega_outer_new = omega_outer_old + (2 * I_inner * omega_inner) / I_outer

Numerical example (outer flywheel, 16 magnets):
  Let I_outer = (M_outer * R_outer^2) / 2  (thin ring approximation for flywheel)
  Let I_inner = (m_inner * r_inner^2) / 2  (disc)
  When omega_inner reverses: omega_outer increases by 2 * I_inner * omega_inner / I_outer

If I_outer >> I_inner: small change in omega_outer
If I_outer ~ I_inner: large coupled response (strong counter-spin)

Drew's device design intent: balance I_outer and I_inner to maximize angular momentum
exchange and (hoped) axial force coupling.

## 5. Three-Phase Rotating Field Mathematics

Three-phase currents:
  i_U(t) = I_m * cos(omega_e * t)
  i_V(t) = I_m * cos(omega_e * t - 2*pi/3)
  i_W(t) = I_m * cos(omega_e * t - 4*pi/3)

Magnetic flux from spatially distributed coils (U at phi=0, V at phi=2*pi/3, W at phi=4*pi/3):
  B(phi, t) = (3/2) * B_m * cos(omega_e * t - phi)

This is a traveling wave in the azimuthal (phi) direction, rotating at omega_e. For
a p-pole machine (p poles), synchronous mechanical speed:
  omega_s_mech = (2 / p) * omega_e

For Drew's four-set (four-pole, p=4) configuration:
  omega_s_mech = omega_e / 2

The phase sequence U-V-W-U-V-W determines the rotation direction. Counter-phase (U-W-V)
reverses rotation direction. Drew identifies this as "3-phase counter-spin" when the
outer rotating field drives the inner disc in the opposite direction at high frequencies.

Induced voltage in inner disc from rotating field:
  V_induced = -d/dt (integral B . dA) = omega_s * Phi_max * sin(omega_s * t - delta)

where delta is a phase angle determined by disc impedance.

## 7. Homopolar Generator EMF

For a disc of radius R rotating at angular velocity omega in axial field B_z:
  EMF = (1/2) * B_z * omega * R^2

This is the Faraday disc generator formula. For mercury torus of inner radius r1,
outer radius r2, rotating at omega in field B_z:
  EMF_mercury = (1/2) * B_z * omega * (r2^2 - r1^2)

The radial current driven by this EMF through mercury's resistance:
  I_radial = EMF_mercury / R_torus
  R_torus = rho_Hg * ln(r2/r1) / (2 * pi * h)  [h = torus height]

Lorentz torque on mercury from its own induced current in B_z:
  tau_mercury = I_radial * B_z * (r2^2 - r1^2) / 2

This is the self-consistent Hartmann-flow type calculation for conducting fluid rotation
in an axial field. Whether this produces a net vertical force (anti-gravity) is the
unresolved question.
