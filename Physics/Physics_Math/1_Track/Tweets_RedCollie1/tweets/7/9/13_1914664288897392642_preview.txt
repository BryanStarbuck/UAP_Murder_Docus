PHYSICS CONTEXT:
The capacitor bank is a critical component in the experimental system. A capacitor bank can:
1. Store substantial energy: E = ½CV². Three large capacitors in parallel multiply total capacitance
2. Deliver very high instantaneous current when discharged: I = C(dV/dt)
3. Be charged slowly then discharged rapidly for pulse power applications
In rail gun physics, the capacitor bank provides the high-current pulse that drives the armature along the rails. In electromagnetic propulsion experiments, pulse power may create transient high-amplitude magnetic fields.
