## 1. Claimed Violation of Newton's Third Law in Counter-Rotating Magnetic Assembly

From tweet 2044598282689941568 (note_tweet):
"I suspect that the KEY to UFO physics will be to have a fast-spinning and upward-jumping
magnet to 'push upward' against the magnetic fields of a flywheel, which is spinning
rapidly in the opposite direction. This may perhaps cancel Newton's 3rd Law?"

From video 13_2044596310473580544:
"Normally the magnet pushing up on the flywheel would not cause any overall gravity weight
change. However, in this case it's pushing up on a flywheel being driven at about 40 Hz
in the opposite direction — relative velocity about 80 Hz — so we may see non-Newtonian
forces which change the interactions quite a lot due to the fast spin rates."

Context: Newton's Third Law (actio = reactio): for every force exerted by body A on body B,
body B exerts an equal and opposite force on body A. In a closed mechanical system, internal
forces cancel and the center of mass does not accelerate. The claim here is that at high
relative angular velocity (omega_rel ~ 80 Hz) between counter-rotating magnetic bodies,
this cancellation fails, producing a net internal upward force on the system.

Status in source material: unverified claim, proposed as a testable hypothesis (weigh the
system on a scale before and after activation). No quantitative experimental result
demonstrating the violation is present in the source files.

## 2. Claimed Absence of Back-Reaction in Galinstan Homopolar Motor

From tweet 1966452992540017086 (note_tweet):
"The spin motion of this 'galinstan' homopolar motor has inertia, but no apparent
back-reaction due to Newton's 3rd Law."

In standard homopolar motor theory:
The torque on the conducting disk/fluid: tau_fluid = integral(r x (J x B)) dV (counterclockwise)
The reaction torque on the magnet providing B: tau_magnet = -tau_fluid (clockwise)
Newton's Third Law requires tau_magnet + tau_fluid = 0.

The claim that "no apparent back-reaction" is observed suggests either:
(a) The measurement apparatus cannot detect the reaction torque on the permanent magnet
(e.g., if the magnet is fixed to a rigid mount, the reaction force is absorbed by the
mount without visible deflection), OR
(b) A genuine anomaly in which the azimuthal electromagnetic momentum is transferred
to the far field rather than locally back to the magnet.

The source does not specify which of these is the explanation, nor are quantitative
measurements provided.

## 3. "Non-Newtonian Forces" at High Relative Spin Rate

From video 13_2044596310473580544:
"We may see non-Newtonian forces which change the interactions quite a lot due to the
fast spin rates."
"I've already seen such things, say, for studying Lens levitation or spin."

"Non-Newtonian forces" is used here to mean forces that cannot be predicted by the
standard Newtonian mechanics framework applied to slow-moving bodies. Possible
non-classical force regimes at high angular velocity include:
* Relativistic corrections: For omega_rel ~ 80 Hz = 80 * 2*pi rad/s = 502 rad/s at a
  radius of r, the tangential velocity is v = r * omega. For r = 0.05 m:
  v = 0.05 * 502 = 25 m/s — entirely non-relativistic (v << c = 3x10^8 m/s).
  Relativistic corrections are of order (v/c)^2 ~ 7x10^-15 — negligible.
* Gyro-gravitational coupling: Spin-orbit-like coupling between the angular momentum of
  the rotating magnetic assembly and the local gravitational field. Not predicted by
  standard Newtonian mechanics or even GR at these energy scales.
* Mach's principle effects: Hypothesis that inertia arises from the interaction with
  distant matter; rapidly spinning bodies might interact differently with the distant
  mass distribution. (Woodward's Mach effect transients — cited in passing by the "Lens
  levitation" reference — involve transient mass fluctuations due to Machian inertia
  coupling, described by the Abraham-Lorentz force in a Machian context.)

The source provides no theoretical framework for the mechanism, only the experimental
hypothesis and the reference to "Lens levitation or spin" as a precedent.

## 4. Vortex Theory of Space as an Alternative to Standard GR/Newtonian Gravity

From video 13_1957293251477811200 (transcription):
"It's really quite easy to explain in terms of vortex theory."
"There's a vortex in space created by the spinning gyroscope."
"It's when it pulls down on one side, it's pressing against normal space and rolling
with it like that — just sideways — and that makes it go round and round at the boundary
layer."

This is a qualitative alternative theory to the Euler-Lagrange treatment of gyroscopic
precession. The claim is:
* Spinning bodies create vortices in the fabric of space.
* These vortices interact with "normal space" at a boundary layer.
* The interaction at the boundary layer causes precession (in the standard case) and
  could potentially be harnessed to produce directional thrust.

This is not equivalent to any known published physical theory (not torsion field theory,
not gravitomagnetic effects from GR, not the Lense-Thirring effect). The Lense-Thirring
effect (frame dragging) in GR does predict that rotating massive bodies drag the
surrounding spacetime metric, but the magnitude for a small laboratory gyroscope is:
  delta_phi ~ (2*G*J)/(c^2 * r^3)
where J = I * omega is the angular momentum of the gyroscope. For a kilogram-scale
gyroscope at omega = 100 rad/s at r = 0.1 m:
  J ~ 0.001 kg*m^2 * 100 rad/s = 0.1 kg*m^2/s
  delta_phi ~ (2 * 6.67e-11 * 0.1) / (9e16 * 1e-3) ~ 1.5e-26 rad/s (negligible)
So the claimed "vortex in space" is not frame-dragging at these scales.

## 5. Circular-to-Helical Trajectory Transition as Propulsion Principle

From tweet 1966452992540017086 (note_tweet):
"So if we can alter the spin motion from circular to helical, the device may 'rise up'."

Standard physics: A particle constrained to helical motion in a magnetic field (charged
particle in a uniform B field) has:
  Circular component: v_perp = omega_c * r_L (cyclotron motion, omega_c = qB/m)
  Axial component: v_parallel = constant (no force along B in uniform field)
For the transition from planar circular to helical: an axial velocity component must
be introduced, requiring either an axial magnetic field gradient (grad-B force:
F_z = -mu * dB/dz where mu = magnetic moment), or an explicit axial force component.

In the galinstan homopolar motor, the current flow is radial and the magnetic field is
axial. Introducing a helical component would require either:
* A rotating B field with a helical component (produced by, e.g., a screw-wound solenoid
  superimposed on the axial permanent magnet field), OR
* Exploiting a radial magnetic field gradient to create a net axial Lorentz force on the
  rotating conducting fluid.

The source does not specify the mechanism — it is stated as a goal ("if we can alter...").
