Source: tweet 1966452992540017086 (note_tweet), 13_2044596310473580544_video.mdx, tweet 2044598282689941568 (note_tweet)

The primary anomalous energy / force claim in these materials is not zero-point energy per se, but rather the claimed suppression or circumvention of Newton's Third Law in a counter-rotating magnetic system.

Stated explicitly: "The spin motion of this 'galinstan' homopolar motor has inertia, but no apparent back-reaction due to Newton's 3rd Law." (tweet 1966452992540017086)

"This may perhaps cancel Newton's 3rd Law?" (tweet 2044598282689941568, note_tweet)

"We may see non-Newtonian forces which change the interactions quite a lot due to the fast spin rates." (13_2044596310473580544_video)

Implications for energy conservation: If Newton's Third Law is genuinely violated in this system, then the system can exert a net force on itself (propellantless propulsion). This does not necessarily violate energy conservation — a system can do work on itself by converting internal energy to kinetic energy — but it would violate momentum conservation (which is a consequence of Newton's Third Law in mechanics, or equivalently, the homogeneity of space in the Lagrangian / Noether formalism). In standard physics:

    Newton's 3rd Law ↔ Conservation of momentum ↔ Homogeneity of space (Noether's theorem)

A violation of Newton's Third Law therefore implies either (a) violation of momentum conservation, or (b) transfer of momentum to a field or medium not accounted for (e.g., the aether, vacuum, or spacetime frame-dragging).

## 5. Vortex Energy — Liquid Metal Vortex Energy Content

Source: tweet 1966452992540017086

A rotating column of liquid metal (galinstan) in a homopolar motor stores kinetic energy:
    E_kinetic = (1/2) * I_fluid * omega^2

where I_fluid is the moment of inertia of the rotating fluid column and omega is its angular velocity.

The rotational kinetic energy is input from the external DC power supply via Lorentz force conversion:
    P_input = V * I = power delivered to the motor

Efficiency of conversion from electrical to rotational kinetic energy:
    eta = P_mechanical / P_input = P_input - P_ohmic / P_input = 1 - I^2 * R_fluid / (V * I)

The statement that the vortex "has inertia but no apparent back-reaction" suggests the kinetic energy is acquired (normal) but the reaction torque on the housing is absent or greatly reduced (anomalous). If the reaction torque were truly zero, the motor would acquire angular momentum with no equal and opposite angular momentum in the housing — violating conservation of angular momentum.
