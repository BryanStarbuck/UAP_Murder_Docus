## 1. Primary Lift Mechanism Hypothesis — Three-Phase Driven Magnet Pushing Counter-Rotating Flywheel

Source: 13_2044596310473580544_video.mdx, tweet 2044598282689941568 (note_tweet)

The stated key to UFO physics per Dr. Drew: "I suspect that the KEY to UFO physics will be to have a fast-spinning and upward-jumping magnet (shown in the first 15 seconds of this video) to 'push upward' against the magnetic fields of a flywheel, which is spinning rapidly in the opposite direction. This may perhaps cancel Newton's 3rd Law?"

Mechanism in detail:
1. Central disk magnet is driven by three-phase AC to spin at high angular velocity (clockwise, viewed from above).
2. The same three-phase drive causes the magnet to oscillate axially — jumping upward periodically.
3. An outer flywheel (ring of magnets) spins at high angular velocity in the opposite direction (anticlockwise).
4. When the upward-jumping central magnet pushes magnetically against the downward face of the flywheel:
   * At low flywheel speed: magnet and flywheel are nearly co-planar; the magnet wants to rise but the flywheel holds it down in plane. They are "very close to one another."
   * At high flywheel speed (~40 Hz): the magnet jumps upward and pushes the flywheel upward from below. "It's pushed up because this magnet is pushing on the magnetic field at its center."
5. The upward push on the flywheel lifts the entire device: "that lifts the whole UFO up."

Why this is claimed to circumvent Newton's Third Law: Normally, if the internal magnet pushes the flywheel upward, the flywheel pushes the magnet downward, and the total weight of the system (on a scale) is unchanged. The author hypothesizes that at high counter-rotation speeds (~80 Hz relative), "non-Newtonian forces" appear that break this exact cancellation, producing a measurable change in total weight on a scale.

Experimental test planned: "We have to put the whole device on a weighing scale and see how these motions affect its actual total gravity weight of those non-wire coils, 3 plus 6 plus the magnets and everything, just like for a real UFO."

## 6. XY-Plane Interactions Causing Z-Axis Change

The radially symmetric attraction of the four surrounding ring magnets (north pole up)
toward the central disk magnet (south pole up) produces a lateral (XY) restoring force
that acts as a constraint — it "holds" the central magnet at the geometric center while
allowing it to move along Z.

The XY interaction also modifies the Z-axis potential:
When the central magnet is directly centered (no XY offset), the net XY force is zero
but the axial gradient of the attraction (dF_z/dz from the ring magnets) is maximized
at the height of the ring magnets' plane. This creates a "magnetic barrier" at that
plane — the central magnet feels a sudden increase in attractive force as it approaches
the ring plane from below, acting as a ceiling on the upward travel.

At higher counter-rotation speeds, the dynamic magnetic pressure from the rotating outer
ring may modify this barrier, allowing the central magnet to pass through the ring plane
and push the flywheel further upward — directly translating XY rotational dynamics into
Z-axis displacement.
