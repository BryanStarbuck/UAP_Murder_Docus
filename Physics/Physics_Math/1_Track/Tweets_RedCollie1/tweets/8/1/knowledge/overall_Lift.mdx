The appearance of a net Z-axis force is the claimed beyond-classical effect.

the gravitational field is altered.

Direct statement (tweet 2039104083945218325, note_tweet):

engineer) UFO physics... I will get some coffee, then go into the lab again to make more
anti-gravity measurements and movies!"

The "anti-gravity measurements" refer to observation of the magnet/flywheel rising off
the platform — i.e., net upward force without conventional ground reaction coupling.

### 8b. Z-Axis Lift vs. Gravity

In all experimental configurations, the observed "lift" is the rotor magnet rising off
the stator platform against gravity. The claim is not that gravity is reduced but that
the electromagnetic configuration provides an upward force exceeding the downward
gravitational force on the rotor, and that this is achieved without an equal and opposite
downward force being applied to the Earth via the stator (the claimed Newton's Third Law
bypass).
