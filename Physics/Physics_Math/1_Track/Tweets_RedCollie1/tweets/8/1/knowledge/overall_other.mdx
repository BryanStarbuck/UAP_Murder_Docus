or a mercury compound) in a strong electromagnetic field. Reported effects include: intense
radiation, severe biological damage to nearby personnel, and possibly antigravitational effects.
Drew's invocation: the German Bell operated on the same spin-counter-spin + conducting fluid
principle he is investigating.
