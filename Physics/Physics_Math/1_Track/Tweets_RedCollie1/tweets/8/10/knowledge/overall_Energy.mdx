## 5. 3I/ATLAS: Anomalous Brightness Increase at Perihelion

Source: YAML 1984219618978984339

"3I/ATLAS just became much BRIGHTER and turned BLUE at perihelion."

Physical context: at perihelion, solar irradiance I_sun is maximum (I_sun ~ 1/r^2 where r is
heliocentric distance). A natural comet brightens at perihelion due to:
(a) Increased sublimation rate of volatiles (water ice, CO2 ice, etc.): Q ~ exp(-E_a / kT),
where Q is sublimation rate, E_a is activation energy, k is Boltzmann constant, T is surface temperature.
(b) Increased solar reflection: L_reflected = A_geometric * A_bond * I_sun.

The color change to BLUE at perihelion is anomalous for a natural comet:
Natural comets at perihelion emit CN (violet ~388 nm), C2 (green ~516 nm), and dust scattering
(neutral/yellow-white color). A BLUE color change suggests either:
(a) Emission from ionized gas (plasma) at high electron temperature (characteristic of artificial
propulsion — ion exhaust or plasma thruster), or
(b) Cerenkov radiation or synchrotron emission from relativistic electrons (highly exotic), or
(c) Artificial illumination (self-luminous craft).

The experimenter compares 3I/ATLAS to a SpaceX rocket, implying the blue color and brightness
pattern resembles rocket exhaust rather than cometary outgassing. This is an observational anomaly
claim, not a theoretical energy claim.

## 6. 3I/ATLAS: Anti-Tail as Potential Active Propulsion Signature

Source: YAML 1992104378896089549

"Long dark anti-tail at lower left, pointing TOWARDS the Sun. No natural comet in all of history
has ever shown such a feature."

An anti-tail pointing sunward would require a force directed TOWARD the Sun exceeding solar
radiation pressure and solar wind pressure (which both push material AWAY from the Sun).

For solar radiation pressure F_rad = (I_sun/c) * A * Q_pr (anti-solar direction):
To produce a sunward anti-tail, a force F_sunward > F_rad is required, i.e.:
F_propulsion_sunward > (I_sun / c) * A * Q_pr

At 1 AU: I_sun = 1361 W/m^2, c = 3e8 m/s.
For F_rad to be overcome for large (km-scale) particles: requires force density > 4.5e-6 N/m^2.

An active propulsion system (rocket, ion drive) could produce such a sunward force. Alternatively,
a charged object in the solar wind could experience an electric field force, but the required
charge density for macroscopic objects would need to be extreme.

This constitutes an observational energy anomaly: if the anti-tail is real and sustained, it implies
an active energy source aboard 3I/ATLAS producing a force of known direction and magnitude.
