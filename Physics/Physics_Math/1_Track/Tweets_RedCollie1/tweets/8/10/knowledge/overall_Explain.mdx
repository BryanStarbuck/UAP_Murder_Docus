## 1. The Central Research Claim: Propulsion Without Ground Reaction

Source: YAML 1916093709445194077 (note_tweet); MDX 13_1916093512992653312_video.mdx

The experimenter (Dr. Horace Drew / RedCollie1) has identified what he believes to be the key
non-classical finding with practical propulsion implications:

At frequencies below ~25 Hz, a permanent magnet driven by a three-phase rotating magnetic field
generates an upward force (measured: up to 300 g equivalent = 2.94 N) that is transmitted through
mechanical contact to the supporting structure (scale reads nonzero). This is Newtonian — the
force is fully explained by Newton's Third Law, and has no propulsion utility for a self-contained
craft (the reaction pushes on the ground/support, not in the opposite direction of the desired thrust).

At frequencies above ~25 Hz (non-Newtonian regime), the same system generates sufficient upward
force to levitate the magnet freely in the field, with ZERO reading on the support scale. The upward
force on the magnet is real (the magnet is suspended against gravity), but no downward reaction
appears on the scale.

If this zero-reaction observation represents a genuine decoupling of thrust from ground reaction —
rather than simply a re-routing of the reaction into the electromagnetic field (which is generated
by the coil system that IS part of the craft structure) — then:

PROPULSION BENEFIT: A craft with three-phase rotating electromagnetic drive could generate lift
without transmitting a reaction force to any external structure. This would enable levitation and
flight without aerodynamic surfaces, propellant mass ejection, or ground contact.

SPECIFIC PHYSICS ADVANCE ENABLING THIS: The transition from Newtonian screw-advance of the
rotating magnet to gyroscopic precession-and-levitation in the rotating field. The key parameter
is the ratio of gyroscopic stiffness (S_gyro = I_z * omega_spin^2) to the applied electromagnetic
torque coupling. Above the critical frequency, gyroscopic stiffness dominates, and the magnet
"escapes" from the screw-advance regime into free levitation.

The experimenter's stated goal:
"If we could just CAPTURE as much upward force from the non-Newtonian phase, as from the
Newtonian phase, then the great problem of 'UFO propulsion' would be solved."

## 3. Physics Advances Required (Summary)

This research program requires advances or applications of the following physics beyond Newtonian
mechanics:

### A. Gyromagnetic Physics
Understanding the precise conditions for transition from Newtonian screw-advance to non-Newtonian
gyroscopic levitation. This requires solving the full equations of motion for a magnetic dipole in
a rotating field, including:
- Synchronization dynamics (lock-in / escape).
- Effect of field gradient on axial force: F_z = m * dB_z/dz + (higher order terms for rotating field).
- Phase angle between magnet axis and rotating field vector at each frequency.

### B. Magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) Physics for Liquid Metal Drives
For the galinstan phase:
- J x B body force with three-phase induction drive (no DC electrodes).
- Turbulence onset in liquid metal: Reynolds number Re = rho*v*L/mu, Magnetic Reynolds number
  Rm = mu_0*sigma*v*L (relevant when Rm > 1 for field-flow coupling).
- Stability of rotating liquid metal column under J x B: Tayler instability, MHD turbulence.

### C. Multi-Body Electromagnetic Interaction (Coils + Magnet + External Field)
The system is a coupled electromagnetic-mechanical system:
- Back-EMF from rotating magnet on driving coils.
- Self-consistent field calculation (field from coils + field from magnet, with rotor position
  and velocity as dynamic variables).
- Power flow analysis: where does the reaction force actually go? Into the coils? Into the field?
  Into the support structure?

Answering this last question definitively determines whether the non-Newtonian regime represents
a genuine propulsion advance or a classical but non-intuitive force routing.
