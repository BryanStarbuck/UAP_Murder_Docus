### Core Experimental Setup (Theory)
A permanent magnet (70 mm diameter, 14 mm central hole, mounted on an 8 mm aluminum rod) is
driven by a three-phase electromagnetic field. A second magnet below holds the assembly aloft
by mutual magnetic repulsion (magnetic bearing). The assembly sits on a precision scale to measure
net downward force (weight transfer). A stainless steel plate above limits maximum rise height
and allows measurement of upward impulsive force.

The wire coil orientation defines the field geometry:
- Vertical coils produce a HORIZONTAL three-phase rotating field (field vector rotates in XY-plane).
- Horizontal coils produce a VERTICAL three-phase rotating field (field vector rotates about Z-axis).

The experiment specifically studies horizontal three-phase field (vertical wire coils, field 90 degrees
from the vertical coil orientation baseline).

### Phase 1: Newtonian Spin Regime (Low Frequency)
At frequencies f < f_transition (experimentally: f < ~25 Hz), the rotating magnet behaves as
a mechanical screw:
- The rotating magnetic field applies torque tau to the permanent magnet.
- The magnet rotates in synchrony or near-synchrony with the applied field.
- As angular velocity omega increases from zero, the magnet/rod assembly generates an upward
  axial force analogous to a right-hand screw advancing: F_z_Newton ~ f(omega, pitch_effective).
- Experimental result: at 10 Hz, the rod rises and strikes the plate above; scale reads +200 g.
  At 18 Hz: +250 g downward force transferred to scale (rod still in contact with plate, pushing up).
  At 20 Hz: +300 g.
- This regime is explicitly labeled by the experimenter as "Newtonian form of spin."
- Physical interpretation: The spinning magnet transfers linear momentum downward through
  contact with the stainless steel plate (Newton's Third Law reaction pair: magnet pushes plate up,
  plate pushes magnet down, magnet pushes rod down, rod pushes scale).

### Phase 2: Non-Newtonian Spin Transition (High Frequency Escape)
At f ~ f_c = 25 Hz (experimentally observed critical frequency), the magnet "escapes" and rises
freely into the three-phase field:
- Scale reading returns to ZERO — no force transferred to scale or plate.
- Rod is lifted; no contact with plate; rod free to move.
- At 26 Hz: scale still zero. At 27 Hz: scale still zero.
- On deceleration back through f_c: scale reading returns (+300 g at "hot RPM down").
- Physical interpretation posited by experimenter: analogous to the non-Newtonian "spinning top"
  precession-and-rise phenomenon, where gyroscopic forces partially counter gravity without
  direct mechanical reaction. The system appears to generate net upward force without mechanical
  reaction load on the support structure.

### Chain of Reasoning for UFO Propulsion Hypothesis
The experimenter (tweet note_tweet) states:
"If we could just CAPTURE as much upward force from the non-Newtonian phase, as from the
Newtonian phase, then the great problem of 'UFO propulsion' would be solved."

This constitutes a proposed mechanism:
Step 1: Three-phase rotating magnetic field applied to permanent magnet assembly.
Step 2: At sub-critical frequency, mechanical screw force generates reaction on support (Newtonian, scale reads nonzero).
Step 3: At super-critical frequency, magnet escapes into field; scale reads zero (apparent elimination of reaction force).
Step 4: Hypothesis: in the non-Newtonian phase, upward force is generated without equal-and-opposite ground reaction — violating or circumventing Newton's Third Law as applied to propulsion.
Step 5: If the non-Newtonian upward force could be engineered to equal the Newtonian upward force, net levitation without ground reaction is achievable.

Note: The physical mechanism producing the scale-zero condition may be purely Newtonian (the magnet is levitated by the field, not by a ground reaction, so no force transfers to the scale). The claim of "non-Newtonian" may refer to the dynamical behavior (precession-type, gyroscopic) rather than a literal violation of Newton's laws.

### Electromagnetic Driving Mechanism: Lorentz Force
The Lorentz force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field:
F = q(E + v x B) [per unit charge]
or in bulk conductor form:
F = J x B [force per unit volume, N/m^3]
where J is current density (A/m^2), B is applied magnetic field (T).

In the galinstan experiment: external magnetic field B applied; current J driven through the liquid
metal by applied voltage. The cross product J x B produces a body force on the conducting fluid,
driving azimuthal rotation (phi-direction torque about the Z-axis).

Experimental observation: Galinstan spins visibly under electromagnetic Lorentz force drive.
Context: experimenter states this is being studied for its relation to UFO physics.
