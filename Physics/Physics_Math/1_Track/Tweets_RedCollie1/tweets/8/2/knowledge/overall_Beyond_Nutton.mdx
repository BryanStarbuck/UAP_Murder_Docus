## 1. Newton's Third Law Violation (Claimed Experimental Result)

### Source: note_tweet 1927593867060093435

Claim: "I have done MANY experiments which show that a 'spinning magnet' will rise without causing any downward back-reaction by Newton's 3rd Law!"

This is a direct empirical claim of Newton's Third Law violation in the specific context of a spinning permanent magnet rising along a vertical pole under a 3-phase rotating magnetic field. The standard statement of Newton's Third Law: for every force F_A→B exerted by body A on body B, there is an equal and opposite force F_B→A exerted by B on A. Applied to magnet + coil system: the coil exerts an upward force F_up on the magnet; Newton's Third Law requires the magnet to exert an equal downward force F_down on the coil (and thereby on the scale and Earth).

### What Is Observed Instead

The scale does NOT show a proportional downward reaction. The net measured weight of the whole device DECREASES by 20-50 grams, rather than remaining constant or increasing.

### Proposed Non-Newtonian Explanation

"Any 3rd Law back-reaction for a spinning magnet would create a force perpendicular to the pole on which it is rising, so we would not expect to see a 3rd Law reaction downward against the ground."

The reaction force, if any, is hypothesized to be radially or azimuthally directed (perpendicular to Z), not vertically downward. This would be a form of momentum-direction re-routing by the rotating field geometry — the angular impulse from the rotating field is delivered to the magnet in a helical path, and the reaction impulse is directed tangentially (azimuthally), not axially. If true, this represents a genuine deviation from the vector addition of linear impulses as applied in Newtonian mechanics to non-rotating force systems.

### Qualification

The experimenter acknowledges this "does not explain how UFOs fly" since it leads to only 50% time-averaged weight reduction (free-fall mechanism), not full levitation. The non-Newtonian character of the force is presented as necessary but not sufficient for full levitation.

## 2. Magnets Hovering at Close Range — Claimed Contradiction of Mainstream Physics

### Source: referenced tweet in 1923299494193471680

"Mainstream physics 2022: 'Magnets should not hover when they are close together.' Yet akin to what Galileo said in 1600: 'Still they hover!'"

The classical Earnshaw theorem (1842) states: a collection of point charges cannot be held in stable equilibrium solely by electrostatic forces. Its extension to magnetostatics: a rigid magnetic dipole cannot be levitated stably by a static magnetic field in free space (only one degree of freedom is stable, the others are unstable). This underpins why simple permanent magnet levitation (without active stabilization or diamagnetism) is unstable.

### The Claimed Result

Three copper wire coils (air-core, no iron) arranged in a triangular formation produce a "focal point" — a region of enhanced field — in the center. Permanent magnets close to each other (in the vicinity of this focal point) are observed to hover, contrary to the prediction of the Earnshaw theorem for static fields.

### Why This Could Be Beyond Classical EM

The Earnshaw theorem applies to static fields and rigid dipoles. However:
* If the field contains a rotating component (which three coils in triangular arrangement can produce if driven by 3-phase AC), the effective potential is time-averaged and may create a pseudo-potential minimum on the Z-axis — similar to a Paul trap for ions or a magnetic bottle for plasma.
* If the hovering requires the rotating field (dynamic condition), it is not a violation of Earnshaw's theorem per se but an exploitation of time-averaged potentials, which lie beyond the strictly static analysis of standard magnetostatics.

The claim that mainstream physics says "they should not hover" may reflect the experimenter's assertion that the time-averaged dynamic trapping mechanism is not recognized or acknowledged in standard EM texts.

## 3. Faraday's Paradox — Unsolved Problem in Classical EM (200 Years)

### Source: tweet 1914936648145555818

"After 200 years. Go ahead, try to find this important result in your physics textbooks? 'The true solution of Faraday's Paradox for a homopolar motor/generator'"

Faraday's Paradox in the homopolar generator: when a conducting disc and the magnet providing the axial field B both rotate together (co-rotate rigidly), there is NO EMF measured in an external circuit (in one experimental interpretation) — yet the formula EMF = (1/2)ωBr² predicts nonzero EMF. Different observers (one rotating with the disc+magnet, one stationary) disagree.

The paradox touches on:
* The question of whether magnetic field lines "rotate" with their source magnet (they do not, strictly speaking — field lines are a coordinate artifact)
* The correct application of Faraday's law in rotating frames
* The relationship between the Faraday disk and special relativity (the motional EMF is a relativistic effect — electric fields appear in the rest frame of moving charges)

The experimenter claims a definitive resolution exists but has not been published in textbooks. This is non-Newtonian in the sense that the correct treatment requires relativistic mechanics (special relativity transformations of E and B fields), beyond classical Newtonian EM.

### Source: multiple (2032326803059314829, 2027624426397962585, 13_2027624017113526272)

## 6. Helical B-Field Jet Analogy — Beyond Conventional Propulsion Physics

### Source: note_tweet 1927593867060093435

"UFOs seem to fly by 'helical magnetic fields' like for an astronomical jet."

The application of astrophysical MHD jet physics (Blandford-Znajek mechanism, magnetocentrifugal acceleration, hoop stress confinement) to a terrestrial craft propulsion system is beyond conventional propulsion physics. Standard jet propulsion (chemical rockets, turbofans, electric thrusters) operates by reaction mass expulsion (Newton's Third Law). A magnetic jet analogy, where the Poynting vector of the craft's own rotating field provides directed thrust without mass expulsion, would require:
* The craft to be embedded in or coupled to a medium that can absorb the EM momentum flux (for momentum conservation)
* Or a genuine deviation from momentum conservation in the isolated system

In astrophysical jets, the "medium" is the ambient plasma/ISM — the jet pushes on the ambient medium. For a UAP craft in vacuum (or air), the analogous medium is absent, making the energy/momentum accounting non-trivial.
