## 4. Corbino Disc — Conductive Fluid / Ionic Gas as Energy Conversion Medium

From note_tweet 1992007523407466702:

"If we need spin of a magnet by 3-phase power, balanced by the counter-spin of some conductive fluid or ionic gas outside, should we use galinstan, or argon ions at high voltage?"

This poses an engineering question about the counter-rotating medium in a spin/counter-spin system:

* Galinstan: eutectic alloy of gallium (68.5%), indium (21.5%), tin (10%) — liquid metal at room temperature (melting point -19°C). High electrical conductivity (σ ≈ 3.46 × 10⁶ S/m) allows Corbino disc operation as a liquid-metal rotor. Low viscosity (η ≈ 2.4 × 10⁻³ Pa·s) reduces viscous drag losses. The Corbino disc geometry drives azimuthal current flow, which in the axial field produces azimuthal (spin) torque.

* Argon ions at high voltage: a partially ionized argon plasma or glow discharge driven by high-voltage AC. Ions are accelerated by the electric field, producing ionic current. The Hall drift of ions in the crossed E and B fields produces azimuthal flow. This is essentially a homopolar ion thruster in annular geometry.

Both options represent exotic conductive media for achieving counter-spin without mechanical bearings — relevant to UAP applications where the outer counter-rotating element cannot easily be a rigid flywheel.

The 11 kV AC Corbino disc experiment referenced in the same note_tweet demonstrates that capacitive phase-shift at high voltage can reverse the spin direction of such a device. The energy input at 11 kV AC is substantial (kV × A = kW range), suggesting the full-scale system is a high-power device.

## 6. Helical Magnetic Field — Analogy to Astrophysical Jet Energy Transport

From note_tweet 1927593867060093435:

"As for UFOs, they seem to fly by 'helical magnetic fields' like for an astronomical jet."

Astrophysical jets are highly efficient at converting magnetic energy (Poynting flux) to kinetic energy of plasma. The magnetic flux in the jet:

S = (1/μ₀) (E × B) [W/m²]

carries electromagnetic energy that is transferred to the jet plasma via MHD wave-particle interaction (Alfvén waves, magnetosonic modes). The efficiency of this energy transfer can approach the Blandford-Znajek limit for magnetically dominated jets.

The UAP analog: if the craft's propulsion draws energy from a helical rotating magnetic field (produced by its internal polyphase AC drive), the electromagnetic energy (Poynting flux) propagates along the helical field lines and is deposited as kinetic thrust on the craft body. The craft would be "riding" its own Poynting vector — extracting translational kinetic energy from the electromagnetic field that it generates.

This would not violate energy conservation (the AC power supply provides the energy), but would be an unusual topology for energy-to-thrust conversion without reaction mass.
