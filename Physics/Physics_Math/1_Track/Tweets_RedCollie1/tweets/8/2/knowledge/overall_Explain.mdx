## Central Propulsion Thesis

The core claim of this research program is that UAP (UFO) levitation and propulsion can be achieved by exploiting rotating magnetic field configurations that go beyond classical Newtonian mechanics. Specifically:

* A spinning permanent magnet driven by polyphase AC power (3-phase or 5-phase) produces a net upward axial force that cannot be fully accounted for by standard Newtonian reaction mechanics (Newton's Third Law).
* When a spinning magnet rises along a vertical pole under the action of a rotating magnetic field, the experimenter claims this rise does NOT produce a commensurate downward back-reaction on the base (i.e., the ground or the coil assembly). This is a direct empirical challenge to Newton's Third Law as applied to this geometry.
* The practical benefit would be: a device capable of lifting its own mass (and payload) without expelling reaction mass, i.e., a reactionless drive or field propulsion system.

## Specific Physics Advances Proposed as Enabling This

### 1. Polyphase AC Rotating Magnetic Fields and Levitation

Standard 3-phase AC power produces a rotating magnetic field. When a permanent magnet is placed within this field, it experiences a torque and begins to spin. As spin rate increases, the magnet also rises axially along a central pole (analogous to the physics of a jumping ring, but for a permanent magnet spinning in a 3-phase field).

Claimed advance: the upward force on the spinning magnet (measured at 500 grams of upward force on a 70 mm diameter permanent magnet at 20-30 Hz spin rate) is much larger than any classical prediction for mere inductive or purely magnetostatic levitation. A portion of this force (30-50 grams) appears as net reduction in weight on a precision scale, with no proportional downward reaction detectable.

### 2. Faraday's Paradox / Homopolar Motor-Generator

Tweet 1914936648145555818 references: "The true solution of Faraday's Paradox for a homopolar motor/generator" — claimed as a result not found in physics textbooks after 200 years. The homopolar motor (Faraday disk) produces a continuous DC current from a rotating conductor in a static magnetic field without commutation. The paradox concerns the reference frame of the magnetic field when the magnet itself rotates. Resolution of this paradox is presented as a key conceptual advance enabling new motor/generator topologies relevant to UFO propulsion.

### 3. Counter-Rotating Disk Spin / Counter-Spin Configuration

Referenced from UFO contactee Norman Paulsen (1960s): propulsion via two magnetic disks rotating in opposite directions. This is reproduced in lab experiments with:
* Inner magnet spinning clockwise driven by central 3-phase coils (UVW wiring)
* Outer flywheel (8 magnets, NSNSNSNS alternating, or 4 magnets NNNN same polarity) driven by outer non-wire coils (UVW, UVW, UVW pattern), spinning anti-clockwise

Result: the two counter-rotating fields produce independent, non-interfering rotating fields when properly isolated. This is also correlated with "spin / counter-spin" observed by UFO contactee Edwin Fuhr and researcher Tom Valone.

Claimed advance: the spin/counter-spin geometry produces a novel field configuration not described in classical electrodynamics texts, potentially generating an axial force relevant to propulsion.

### 4. Lenz's Law Repulsion of Induced Currents in Conducting Discs

When a large single-phase AC wire coil is energized below an aluminum disc (60-80 mm diameter, 6 mm thick, on a 5-6 mm pole), the induced eddy currents in the aluminum generate a magnetic moment that repels the AC field below — the disc rises by Lenz repulsion. This is standard physics. The advance claimed here is the coupling of this Lenz-repelled aluminum disc with a 3-phase rotating field: the question posed is whether the induced-current disc will spin in the same direction as the 3-phase rotating field or opposite, and whether the combination enhances the levitation force beyond what either mechanism alone provides.

### 5. Corbino Disc in High-Voltage AC

Tweet 1992007523407466702 references: "A big capacitor can change the spin-direction of a Corbino disc with 11 kV AC power." The Corbino disc is a radial current geometry (current flows radially in a conducting disc in an axial magnetic field, producing azimuthal Hall-effect forces). At 11 kV AC, the spin direction of the Corbino disc is reversed by capacitive phase-shifting. This is proposed as a method for counter-spin generation using conductive fluid (galinstan) or ionic gas (argon ions at high voltage) to balance the inner magnet's spin — achieving spin/counter-spin without mechanical bearings.

### 6. Helical Magnetic Field Propulsion (Astronomical Jet Analogy)

Note_tweet 1927593867060093435: UFOs appear to fly by "helical magnetic fields" like for an astronomical jet. Astrophysical jets (from pulsars, AGN, stellar bipolar outflows) are collimated by helical magnetic fields — the toroidal B-field component confines the jet while the poloidal component drives axial flow. The claimed analogy is that the UFO propulsion mechanism is a miniaturized version of this jet-collimation physics, where helical rotating magnetic fields drive axial thrust.

### 7. Buga Orb Sphere as Physical Evidence of 5-Phase AC Propulsion Device

A ~10 kg aluminum sphere (the "Buga orb" / "Bougar orb") found crashed near Buga/Bougar, Colombia, exhibits 5 equally-spaced arc-shaped protrusions ("moons") around its upper hemisphere. The experimenter interprets these as 5 wire-coil housings running 5-phase AC power (an extension of 3-phase to 5 phases, all equally spaced at 72° intervals around the circumference). 5-phase AC would produce a smoother rotating field than 3-phase (smaller torque ripple, higher field symmetry). The sphere demonstrates a spinning magnet / polyphase AC coil architecture in a physical artifact.

### 8. Time-Averaged Weight Reduction Mechanism

Note_tweet 1927593867060093435 provides explicit quantitative reasoning:

* A spinning magnet of weight W_0 rises periodically along its pole, then falls.
* During free fall (downward phase), the magnet is in free fall: effective weight on scale = 0.
* During the rising phase, weight on scale is approximately W_0.
* If the magnet spends fraction f of time rising and (1-f) in free fall: time-averaged weight = f * W_0.
* For f = 0.5 (equal time rising and falling): time-averaged weight = 0.5 * W_0 = 50% reduction.
* Experimentally observed: "about minus 20 to minus 30 grams" net anti-gravity weight reduction from a magnet of ~100 g.

This is a kinematic mechanism for apparent weight reduction without invoking new physics per se, but serves as a baseline. The unsolved problem is achieving continuous, sustained net upward force (not periodic oscillation) sufficient to lift the entire device.

## Stated Gap Between Current Result and Full Levitation

* Measured upward force on magnet: ~500 grams (for 70 mm NdFeB magnet at 20-30 Hz)
* Measured net anti-gravity weight reduction of whole device: 30-50 grams
* Shortfall: ~450 grams of upward force is "cancelled by a downward reaction of some kind"
* Two proposed solutions (from note_tweet 1934194761935770022):
  1. Add a second set of 3-phase coils staggered axially above or below the first set
  2. Add a wire coil below the existing assembly to modify the lower field geometry
* Additional identified issue: the stainless steel rod (304 grade) is slightly paramagnetic and couples to the magnetic field, creating an undesired force path. 316 stainless would be less magnetic. This is a materials science constraint on the experiment.
