### Physical Mechanism

converts it to rotational kinetic energy via an electric motor. This is:
- Standard photovoltaic conversion: E_photon = hν → electrical energy → mechanical energy
- No anomalous energy claim; energy budget: E_mechanical ≤ E_solar × η_pv × η_motor

   distances requires an energy source orders of magnitude beyond solar photovoltaics.

## Element 115 as Potential UAP Energy Source (Bob Lazar Claim): Skeptical Assessment

### Claim (Not Drew's Own; Drew Evaluates It Critically)
Source tweet (2032327150901276847):
Reply to "@RedCollie1 What is your take on the gravity propulsion device described by Bob Lazar?"
Drew: "Possibly true, or possibly intel disinformation. Since a stable isotope of 115 seems
now not to exist."

### Energy Mechanism in Lazar's Claim
Lazar claimed element 115 (moscovium) powered an alien reactor via:
- Alpha decay products of element 115 → element 113 (nihonium) in an excited state
- Conversion of nuclear decay energy to gravity waves via an undescribed process
- The gravity waves then amplified by "gravity amplifiers" to produce propulsion

### Physical Assessment: Stability Constraint
All known isotopes of Mc (moscovium, Z=115):
  Mc-287: t₁/₂ ≈ 37 ms (alpha decay → Nh-283)
  Mc-288: t₁/₂ ≈ 164 ms (alpha decay → Nh-284)
  Mc-289: t₁/₂ ≈ 330 ms (alpha decay → Nh-285)
  Mc-290: t₁/₂ ≈ 650 ms (alpha decay → Nh-286)

None of these half-lives permit practical reactor fuel storage or sustained power generation.
The "island of stability" (theorized stable superheavy nuclei near Z=114-126, N=184) has not
been reached by synthesis; Mc-290 is the closest approach to the island with 175 neutrons
(stability island predicted near N=184). No stable Mc isotope has been produced.

Drew's assessment "possibly intel disinformation" reflects this nuclear physics constraint:
a claim based on an element with no stable isotope is either:
(a) referring to a yet-undiscovered stable isotope (speculative), or
(b) disinformation designed to be unprovable/unfalsifiable.

## 3-Phase AC Magnetic Energy Input to Spin/Counter-Spin System

### Energy Accounting
Drew's proposed spin/counter-spin system requires:
- External electrical energy input: 3-phase (or 5-phase) AC power to drive both the inner
  spinning magnet and the outer counter-rotating flywheel.
- The claimed anomalous lift ("non-Newtonian" upward force) would represent work done against
  gravity — which must be supplied by the AC electrical input.

Drew does NOT claim energy generation in excess of input ("overunity"). He claims anomalous
force coupling — that the mechanical architecture of spin/counter-spin via rotating magnetic
fields produces more effective lift per unit energy than classical magnetic levitation schemes.
This would be an efficiency claim, not a free energy claim.

### If Non-Newtonian Force Is Real
If the spin/counter-spin system genuinely produces "non-Newtonian" upward force (force not
predicted by F = ∇(m·B) or classical Lorentz forces), the energy source for that excess force
must come from somewhere. Candidates within the theoretical framework:
1. Zero-point field (ZPF) coupling: the rotating magnetic field configuration creates
   conditions where quantum vacuum fluctuations are asymmetrically tapped.
2. Inertial frame dragging energy: coupling to the local gravitational field metric,
   extracting energy from spacetime geometry.
3. EM field angular momentum release: transient release of stored electromagnetic field
   angular momentum (L_field) as kinetic energy — consistent with classical EM.

Drew does not specify which mechanism provides the energy source for any apparent anomalous
force. His experimental program is at the stage of confirming whether the anomalous force
exists at all, not characterizing its energy source.
