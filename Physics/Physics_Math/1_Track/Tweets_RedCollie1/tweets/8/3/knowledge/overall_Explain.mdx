## Proposed Physical Mechanism Enabling Propulsion Advance

The proposed mechanism requires two distinct rotational inputs acting in spatial opposition:

1. A spinning permanent magnet (or electromagnet) driven by 3-phase AC power, producing
   gyroscopic motion and a vertically oriented rotating magnetic field.
2. A large magnetic flywheel spinning in the OPPOSITE direction around the first element
   (counter-rotation), so that the upward-spinning magnetic moment of element (1) couples
   into the counter-rotating magnetic field of element (2).

## Claimed Advance Beyond Newtonian Mechanics

Drew explicitly suspects the combined spin/counter-spin system produces a net upward force that
is "non-Newtonian" — i.e., not explainable as a reaction force against a propellant or against
the surrounding medium. The upward force would arise from internal coupling of counter-rotating
magnetic fields rather than expulsion of mass or momentum transfer to external medium.

Connection to Newton's 3rd Law anomaly: In the DC disk experiment (video 13_2007971058230644737):
"I'm going to add juice to this circuit and the wires will rotate one direction and the disc will
rotate the other direction. But the wires are not enough mass to start the disc rotating in the
opposite direction, yet the disc starts rotating in the opposite direction. Where are the torques
interacting? This is not equal in opposite torques."

This is framed as an empirical deviation from Newton's 3rd Law (equal and opposite torques).
The reference tweet for that video (2007786944215089284) explicitly states: "Bypassing Newton's
3rd Law? Love the audacity! That's the kind of thinking that creates paradigm shifts."

## Broader Claimed Benefits

- Non-reactive (reactionless) propulsion: no propellant required, enabling long-duration
  deep-space or atmospheric flight.
- Transmedium operation: the Buga orb-sphere class of craft appears to operate across
  atmospheric and possibly aquatic media without aerodynamic surfaces.
- Vertical takeoff / hovering / rapid acceleration: the 2011 Jerusalem Temple Mount orb
  (video 13_2024610343155298304) demonstrates hover at low altitude followed by near-
  instantaneous vertical acceleration "in less than a second" to exit frame — consistent
  with anomalous inertial behavior.
- Elimination of g-force loads on occupants: reactionless internal-field propulsion implies
  the craft and occupants share the same local field, eliminating differential inertial stress.

anomalous torque. Spin-magnet/flywheel lift = as-yet untested hypothesis.
