## 1. Special Relativity in 4D Euclidean Space: Geometric Parameterization

### Core Substitution
  sin(θ) = v/c

This maps relativistic velocity v (0 ≤ v < c) to a geometric angle θ ∈ [0°, 90°).

### Derived Identities
  cos(θ) = √(1 - v²/c²) = 1/γ   where γ = Lorentz factor
  tan(θ) = v/√(c² - v²) = βγ    where β = v/c
  θ → 90° as v → c               (lightspeed limit as right-angle limit)

### Lorentz Factor in Geometric Form
  γ = 1/cos(θ)

### Relativistic Energy in Geometric Form
  E = γm₀c² = m₀c²/cos(θ)

### Relativistic Momentum in Geometric Form
  p = γm₀v = m₀c × (sin(θ)/cos(θ)) = m₀c × tan(θ)

### Energy-Momentum Relation in Geometric Form
  E² - (pc)² = (m₀c²)²
  Dividing by E²:
    1 - (pc/E)² = (m₀c²/E)²
    1 - sin²(θ) = cos²(θ)  ✓ (Pythagorean identity)

This shows the relativistic energy-momentum hyperbola maps exactly to the unit circle
in the (E/m₀c², pc/m₀c²) plane under the sin(θ) = v/c parameterization.

### de Broglie Equation Connection

de Broglie wavelength: λ = h/p
Phase velocity of matter wave: v_ph = ω/k = E/p = c²/v

In geometric form:
  v_ph = c²/v = c/(v/c) = c/sin(θ)

Group velocity of matter wave: v_g = dω/dk = v (particle velocity)
  v_g = c sin(θ)

The dispersion relation:
  ω² = (ck)² + (m₀c²/ħ)²

maps in Euclidean 4D to the same geometric circle as the SR energy-momentum relation.
Drew's claim: both theories are presentations of the same Euclidean 4D rotation by angle θ,
not two independent empirical theories.

### Wick Rotation Correspondence
Under Wick rotation t → iτ (Euclidean continuation), the boost rapidity φ defined by:
  tanh(φ) = v/c

becomes:
  φ → iθ  where  sin(θ) = v/c

This is the precise mathematical content of Drew's assertion. The Lorentz group SO(1,3)
becomes the Euclidean rotation group SO(4) under Wick continuation, and the boost angle
becomes a real rotation angle.

## 5. Carbon-14 Dating of Buga Sphere Material

### Claim
Source tweet (1970079760824058234, note_tweet):
"Second, some of the material from that sphere has apparently tested by carbon-14 to 10,000 BC."

### Physical Basis of C-14 Dating
¹⁴C is produced in the atmosphere by cosmic ray neutron bombardment of ¹⁴N:
  ¹⁴N + n → ¹⁴C + p

¹⁴C decays by beta emission:
  ¹⁴C → ¹⁴N + e⁻ + ν̄ₑ
  t₁/₂ = 5730 ± 40 years (Cambridge value)

For a material dated to 10,000 BC (≈ 12,000 years before present):
  Age ≈ 2.09 half-lives
  Remaining fraction: 2^(-2.09) ≈ 0.233 = 23.3% of original ¹⁴C activity

This is within the measurable range of AMS (Accelerator Mass Spectrometry) C-14 dating.
Maximum reliable range is ~50,000 years (approximately 8.7 half-lives, ~0.3% remaining).

### Significance of the Claim
If an artifact of industrial/machined appearance (the Buga orb-sphere, which has machined
marks and encoded symbols) dates to 10,000 BC by C-14, this would imply either:
(a) the material was manufactured before the development of metallurgy (~3000 BC),
(b) the C-14 date applies to an organic component (adhesive, coating) not the primary material,
(c) the C-14 signal was artificially depleted (low C-14 = appears old), or
(d) the object is from a non-contemporary technological civilization.
Drew takes interpretation (d) as consistent with the other "ancient past" anomalies he lists.
