Ultimately, the easiest way to see how a gyroscope maintains its orientation is with a three-axis gimbal.
This gimbal allows the gyroscope to spin on all three axes.
So, if the gyroscope starts in one orientation, no matter how much we turn the rest of the gimbal, or what it's sitting on,
the gyroscope will keep pointing in the same direction.
This is the same gimbal that Feynman used when he was teaching.
