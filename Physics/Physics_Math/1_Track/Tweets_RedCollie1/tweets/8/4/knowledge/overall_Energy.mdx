## Energy Input to the Three-Phase Levitation Device

The energy source for Drew's device is explicitly conventional three-phase AC power — there is no claim in this batch of self-generation or over-unity energy production. The three-phase power drives:

1. The rotating magnetic field in the six wire coils (UVW, UVW ring)
2. The spin of the permanent magnets on the inner and outer flywheels

The anomalous energy claim in this batch is not about the source of energy but about the application of energy: the claim is that under spinning Lorentz force conditions, Newton's 3rd Law reaction forces are reduced or absent, meaning the energy input to the device produces unidirectional (Z-axis) work rather than being canceled by an equal-and-opposite reaction. This is an efficiency claim about how input electrical energy is converted to mechanical potential energy (altitude gain), not a claim about creating energy from nothing.

## Self-Exciting Homopolar Dynamo: Partial Energy Autonomy

From tweet note_tweet 1983835595828072898:
  "a self-exciting homopolar dynamo made of liquid metal (say galinstan), which spins in a direction opposite to the applied 3-phase power."

"Self-exciting" has a specific meaning: once the dynamo's angular velocity exceeds ω_c (critical velocity for self-excitation), the device generates its own sustaining magnetic field from its own induced current. Above ω_c, no external electrical energy is required to maintain the dynamo's field — it is self-sustaining.

This is analogous to a self-excited DC generator: once it builds up field above threshold, it sustains itself. For a homopolar (Faraday) disk dynamo in liquid metal:

  Self-excitation condition: ω > ω_c = 2 R_total L / (μ_0 N r²)

Above this threshold, the dynamo runs on the kinetic energy of the spinning liquid metal. The kinetic energy must be supplied initially (spin-up phase), but once self-excited, the dynamo sustains its field without additional external electrical input.

The energy balance claim: the kinetic energy stored in the spinning galinstan is converted into electromagnetic force that cancels the stator's magnetic recoil. This is not over-unity: the kinetic energy of the liquid metal was supplied during spin-up. But in steady operation, the dynamo is self-sustaining and the reaction cancellation occurs "for free" from an electrical-input perspective.

## Energy Analysis of 3I/ATLAS Jet System

From tweet note_tweet 2005590873623416947:
  "The total power of two jet-exhausts emerging from 3I/ATLAS remains conserved."

This is a conservation-of-energy statement: P_total = P_jet1 + P_jet2 = constant. For a natural comet, jet activity is driven by solar illumination — total jet power ∝ (solar flux at distance d) = L_sun / (4π d²). As 3I/ATLAS approaches the sun, P_total should increase, not remain constant.

If P_total is constant regardless of solar distance, the jet power is not driven by solar illumination but by an internal energy source of fixed total power. This would imply an on-board power source with a fixed power budget — consistent with an artificial propulsion system rather than solar-driven sublimation.

The internal energy source for such a system (if artificial) is not specified in Drew's tweets but is implied to be a non-solar source. The "total power conserved" observation is the evidence offered; the nature of the internal energy source is not claimed.

## Tidal Force / Solar Gravity Reference (Peripheral Energy Context)

Tweet 1989073384681185703:
  Reply to @mikeod88: "Or a 'tidal force' due to the Sun's gravity?"

This is a brief comment on a possible gravitational energy mechanism (tidal forces from the Sun's gravity) in the context of 3I/ATLAS trajectory anomalies. Tidal forces arise from the differential gravitational acceleration across a finite-size body:

  F_tidal ~ 2 G M_sun m r / d³

where r = radius of the body, d = distance from the Sun, m = mass of the body, G = gravitational constant, M_sun = solar mass. These forces could differentially stress an object and release internal stresses — a form of tidal heating analogous to Io's volcanic activity from Jupiter's tidal forces. However, Drew raises this only as a rhetorical question ("Or a tidal force?"), not as a committed explanation. No further development of this idea appears in this batch.

## Plasma Ionization at High Voltage: Energy Context

Source: tweet note_tweet 1915205714114687276; video transcript 13_1915205371091996672

Voltage levels used for ionizing air:
* 8 kV AC (kilovolt, alternating current)
* 8.8 kV AC

At these voltages, air breakdown (ionization) begins near the electrode surface. The dielectric strength of dry air is approximately 3 MV/m; for AC at 8-8.8 kV over a short gap (millimeters to centimeters), local ionization creates a plasma corona discharge.

Energy input: the high-voltage power supply provides energy to maintain the plasma discharge. The ionized air serves as a conducting medium in which the Lorentz force from the rotating magnetic field can act on free charge carriers. The energy deposited into the plasma is a loss term in the device's energy budget — it does not contribute to lift but makes the rotating-field effect visible through the motion of the ionized air.

This experiment is purely observational/diagnostic: the ionized air reveals the rotating-field's effect on a conducting fluid (plasma) as a proxy for understanding what happens inside the spinning permanent magnet and within the conducting liquid-metal dynamo.
