## Central Propulsion Thesis

Dr. Drew's overarching claim is that UAP/UFO levitation and propulsion is achievable through a specific electromagnetic mechanism whose core advancement is the elimination (or substantial reduction) of Newton's 3rd Law counter-reactions under conditions where a spinning Lorentz force is involved. The central statement:

  "Newton's 3rd Law does not apply to a spinning Lorentz force."
  (Source: tweet 2007971400427110683, 2026-01-05)

The implication is that a spinning magnetic flywheel subjected to 3-phase electromagnetic excitation generates a net upward force (Z-axis) without an equal-and-opposite reaction force that would cancel the lift. This is the proposed mechanism that makes electromagnetic anti-gravity possible.

The device Drew is constructing is described as a "UFO-levitation device" (tweet 2039175553719357443, 2026-04-01). Its architecture as stated:

* 8 (or 16) permanent magnets arranged on each inner flywheel
* 8 (or 16) permanent magnets arranged on each outer flywheel

* Inner flywheel rotates clockwise; outer flywheel rotates anticlockwise
* Magnet polarity arrangement: inside magnets arranged as MN, MN, MN, MN (alternating); outside magnets arranged as NS, NS and S (alternating opposite)

Specific count: "When we have six three-phase wire coils arranged as a ring, as UVW, UVW, we can put eight magnets in each flywheel. It'll go clockwise inside, anticlockwise outside."
(Source: video transcript 13_2039172561586790401)

The advancement over conventional electric motors: conventional motors convert rotational electromagnetic force into torque output — they do not attempt to convert it into net translational (Z-axis) force. Drew's device is specifically designed to produce vertical lift rather than rotational output.

## The Homopolar Dynamo Counter-Reaction Cancellation Mechanism

The most physically specific advancement described by Drew concerns cancellation of Newtonian reaction forces via a secondary self-exciting system:

  "To understand UFO levitation, we have to understand how a magnet spins and rises due to 3-phase power, and also how its 3rd Law, Newtonian counter-reactions of 'mass recoil' and 'magnetic recoil' get reduced or even cancelled, by a self-exciting homopolar dynamo made of liquid metal (say galinstan), which spins in a direction opposite to the applied 3-phase power."
  (Source: tweet note_tweet 1983835595828072898, 2025-10-30)

Two distinct Newtonian counter-reactions are explicitly identified:

1. "Mass recoil" — the conventional equal-and-opposite translational reaction to the upward thrust force, which would otherwise push the stator/frame downward.

2. "Magnetic recoil" — a magnetic reaction force generated when the spinning permanent magnets interact with the stator coils; this is separate from and in addition to the mass recoil.

The proposed solution to both: a self-exciting homopolar dynamo made of liquid metal (galinstan is specified as an example — a gallium-indium-tin alloy, eutectic, liquid at room temperature) that counter-rotates relative to the 3-phase excitation direction. The homopolar dynamo in this context is claimed to generate an opposing electromagnetic field that cancels the magnetic recoil component. The mass recoil component may be addressed by the same mechanism if the homopolar dynamo also generates a downward-directed inertial reaction in the liquid metal that cancels the upward push on the frame.

This is a non-trivial claim: a homopolar (Faraday) dynamo generating a self-sustaining current in a conducting liquid, counter-rotating to the primary magnetic excitation, would need to produce a force on the stator frame precisely equal and opposite to both recoil terms. The device would need to satisfy:

  F_thrust = F_mass_recoil + F_magnetic_recoil (for net zero reaction on frame)

where F_thrust is the Z-axis levitation force on the spinning magnet assembly.

If the gyroscope disc both spins and rises under 3-phase excitation, it would constitute evidence that the Lorentz force acting on a rapidly spinning magnetic body produces a net Z-axis translational force not canceled by an equal-and-opposite reaction.

## What Physics Advancement Is Required

The specific physics advances that would have to be validated for this propulsion mechanism to work:

1. Non-reciprocal Lorentz force under rotation: The claim that "Newton's 3rd Law does not apply to a spinning Lorentz force" requires that the Lorentz force F = q(v × B) acting on charges in the rotating magnetic body does not generate an equal-and-opposite force on the source of B (the stator coils). This would constitute a violation of Newton's 3rd Law in the electromagnetic domain under rotational conditions — a result absent from classical Maxwell-Lorentz electrodynamics.

2. Homopolar dynamo reaction cancellation: The Faraday/homopolar dynamo in liquid metal must generate sufficient back-EMF and associated force to cancel both mass recoil and magnetic recoil simultaneously. This requires careful engineering of the geometry, conductivity, and rotation rate of the galinstan layer.

3. Three-phase rotating magnetic field coupling to levitation: The 3-phase field (UVW, UVW winding at frequencies demonstrated at 40 Hz or 50 Hz) must couple to the permanent magnet assembly in such a way that net translational force exceeds frictional, gravitational, and reaction forces on the assembly.

The advancement over Newtonian/classical EM mechanics: if validated, a device of this type would produce propulsive force in the Z-axis without jet exhaust, reaction mass, or equal-opposite structural reaction on the frame — enabling silent, propellant-free flight.

## Relation to Jumping Ring and Classical Electromagnetic Induction

Drew also references the "jumping ring" experiment as a comparison point for UFO levitation (tweet 1934367052594397363). In the standard Elihu Thomson jumping ring demonstration, an aluminum ring placed on an iron core energized by AC electromagnet is repelled upward by Lenz's law induced currents opposing the changing flux. The analogy Drew draws: the spinning magnet assembly under 3-phase excitation may be experiencing an analogous but rotationally enhanced version of this phenomenon, where the rotating magnetic field continuously induces currents in the magnetic material or surrounding conductors such that the net Lenz-law reaction produces sustained upward force rather than a single transient jump.

The relationship: jumping ring is F_z ~ (dΦ/dt) at a single moment; the 3-phase rotating-field version sustains (dΦ/dt) continuously at the rotation frequency, potentially producing continuous sustained lift if the geometry is correct.
