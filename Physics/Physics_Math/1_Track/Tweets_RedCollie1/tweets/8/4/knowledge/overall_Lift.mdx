Drew mentions the "jumping ring" as a comparison suggested by crop artists:

  "It may have something to do with this, but I don't understand it yet. The crop artists compare UFO levitation to a well-known 'jumping ring.'"
  (Source: tweet 1934367052594397363, 2025-06-15)

The Elihu Thomson jumping ring experiment:
* An aluminum (conducting, non-magnetic) ring placed on an iron core
* A solenoid below the ring is energized with AC current
* Faraday's law: the changing flux Φ induces an EMF ε = -dΦ/dt in the ring
* The induced current I_ring flows in a direction (Lenz's law) to oppose the changing flux
* This induced current in the ring interacts with the magnetic field B of the solenoid: F = I L × B
* Net result: F_z is upward — the ring is repelled (jumps up)

For the ring to levitate indefinitely, the AC frequency must be high enough that the ring levitates in the time-averaged force balance between electromagnetic repulsion and gravity.

The analogy to Drew's device:
* The "ring" is replaced by the spinning permanent magnet (which has an equivalent conducting surface from eddy current induction)
* The "solenoid" is replaced by the 3-phase rotating field coils
* The "jump" is replaced by a continuous rise under the sustained rotating field
* The key difference: in the jumping ring, the field is purely oscillating (AC) and the ring experiences a continuous upward force due to inductive repulsion; in Drew's device, the field is rotating (not just oscillating), and the magnet is also a permanent magnet rather than a passive conductor

Whether Lenz-law repulsion contributes to the observed rise in Drew's experiment (in addition to or instead of the direct Lorentz force mechanism) is not resolved in this batch.

## Proposed 3-Axis Gyroscope Anti-Gravity Experiment

Source: tweet note_tweet 1942537540801814931

For Z-axis rise from a gyroscope geometry:
* A magnetized disc spinning with angular velocity ω (spin axis vertical, initially)
* Subjected to a 3-phase rotating field (rotation frequency Ω_field in horizontal plane)
* The field exerts a torque τ = m × B on the spinning magnetic disc
* For a gyroscope with large angular momentum L = Iω:
  * If τ is horizontal: Ω_precession = τ / L (Euler's precession) — gyroscope precesses, no net Z rise
  * If τ has a Z component due to the coupling of spin and rotation: F_z = dL_z/dt ≠ 0 — net Z-axis acceleration

The proposed experimental test: if the magnetized gyroscope disc rises under 3-phase excitation, that provides evidence that the Z-axis rise is a general property of rotating magnetic bodies in rotating fields, not an artifact of the specific pole geometry.

The requirement for a 3-axis (not 2-axis) gimbal:
* 3-phase field rotates the spin axis of the gyroscope; it needs full 3-axis freedom to track the rotating field vector
* 2-axis gimbal constrains the spin axis to a plane; the 3-phase field cannot effectively drive such a constrained system
* "Using a cheap toy gyroscope with just 2 axes does not work, because we cannot spin it using 3-phase power." (note_tweet 1942537540801814931)

Feynman-era Caltech 3-axis gimbal (referenced):
* Allows gyroscope to spin on all three axes simultaneously

## The Self-Exciting Homopolar Dynamo and Its Role in Enabling Net Lift

From tweet note_tweet 1983835595828072898:
  "To understand UFO levitation, we have to understand how a magnet spins and rises due to 3-phase power, and also how its 3rd Law, Newtonian counter-reactions of 'mass recoil' and 'magnetic recoil' get reduced or even cancelled, by a self-exciting homopolar dynamo made of liquid metal (say galinstan), which spins in a direction opposite to the applied 3-phase power."

The homopolar dynamo is necessary for net levitation of the entire device (frame + payload). Without it:
* The spinning magnet rises (the experimental observation)
* But the stator frame is pushed down by an equal-and-opposite force (mass recoil + magnetic recoil)
* Net Z-axis displacement of the entire system: zero (no center-of-mass motion)

With the homopolar dynamo:
* The liquid-metal disc counter-rotates
* The back-EMF and associated electromagnetic force on the stator frame is directed upward (canceling the downward reaction forces)
* Net Z-axis displacement of the entire system: the upward force on the spinning magnet is no longer opposed by a downward force on the stator — the entire assembly rises

The homopolar dynamo "spins in a direction opposite to the applied 3-phase power" — this is the key detail. The Faraday disk spinning opposite to the main field generates an EMF that drives a current in a direction that produces a force opposing the magnetic recoil on the stator. The sign of the force depends on the current direction and the field direction: by choosing the counter-rotation direction, the force can be made upward on the stator.

This is the proposed physical mechanism that elevates the entire device rather than just the internal magnet.
