## Energy Harvesting: Spinning Magnetic Flywheel + External Wire Coil

Source: 1945272410573541660.yaml (note_tweet)

"Slide 4 shows how to add a wire coil outside, to collect AC power from a spinning magnetic flywheel (like Ed did at Coral Castle). Elon Musk could use method 4 to recharge the batteries in his Model 3 electric cars, but he may not know how yet? All three crop pictures were drawn from June 6 to 13, 2009."

This describes a standard electromagnetic energy recovery method:
* A magnetic flywheel (spinning disc with permanent magnets) rotates at angular velocity omega
* An external wire coil of N turns, cross-sectional area A, is positioned near the flywheel
* The changing flux through the coil induces an EMF: EMF(t) = N * A * B_peak * omega * sin(omega*t + phi)
* The extracted power into a load resistance R_L: P = EMF^2 / (2*R_L) = (N*A*B_peak*omega)^2 / (2*R_L)

The "like Ed did at Coral Castle" reference: Edward Leedskalnin (1887-1951) built Coral Castle in Florida and claimed to have rediscovered "the secrets of the pyramids." He demonstrated small electromagnetic generators powered by rotating permanent magnets. Leedskalnin's "flywheel generator" used a bicycle wheel with magnets attached, rotating past wire coils. This IS standard AC generator physics (Faraday's law), not anomalous.

The proposed application (recharging a Tesla Model 3 while driving) would require the flywheel energy to exceed the electrical energy input powering it -- which is ruled out by energy conservation: P_output ≤ eta * P_input, where eta < 1 (always some losses). This application as described (self-recharging) would require overunity (P_out > P_in), which is not supported. The source appears to conflate the energy recovery from the primary drive system (which is powered by an external 3-phase power supply in the experiments) with a self-powered system.

No explicit claim of zero-point energy or overunity in this passage -- it is framed as "recovering power" from an already-spinning flywheel, which is legitimate energy recovery if the flywheel is externally driven.

## 3-Phase Power as Energy Input for UFO Propulsion

Source: 1945272410573541660.yaml (note_tweet), 1970471190230937728.yaml, video 13_1970464668910231552_video.mdx

All experiments described use an external 3-phase variable-frequency power supply as the energy source:
"three wire corals, one, two, three, attached to a three-phase variable frequency power supply above them"
Frequency range: 20-45 Hz
Power level: not specified in watts in the source texts

The energy budget:
* Input: 3-phase electrical power (P_input = 3 * V_rms * I_rms * cos(phi), where phi is power factor angle)
* Output: kinetic energy of disc magnet (spin + rise): KE_spin = (1/2)*I_moment*omega^2; PE_rise = m*g*h
* Losses: resistive heating in coils, eddy currents in disc, friction

For the disc-magnet rising experiment, the energy to lift the disc against gravity must come from the electrical input (3-phase supply). No anomalous energy generation is claimed for the input-to-lift energy transfer. The anomalous claim is about momentum (reactionless force), not energy generation.

## Counter-Rotating Homopolar Dynamo: Energy and Power Considerations

Source: 1983881901372010784.yaml, referenced parent tweet 1983835595828072898

"A self-exciting homopolar dynamo made of liquid [metal]"

A self-exciting homopolar dynamo amplifies its own magnetic field above a critical angular velocity omega_crit. In the subcritical regime, it is a conventional generator (energy input = mechanical rotation; energy output = electrical EMF). In the supercritical (self-exciting) regime, it is an amplifier: a small initial B field grows exponentially until limited by resistive dissipation.

Self-exciting dynamo energy balance:
* Input: kinetic energy of the rotating liquid metal
* Output: magnetic field energy + thermal dissipation (Ohmic heating in the liquid metal)
* The kinetic energy source must be maintained externally (the 3-phase drive in the experiment, or some internal power source in the UAP)

No claim of overunity is explicit for the homopolar dynamo. The claim is functional: the counter-spin of the dynamo reduces Newton's 3rd Law reaction forces (a momentum claim), not an energy-generation claim.

The energy input to maintain counter-rotation against the drag of the eddy currents and fluid viscosity in the liquid metal must come from somewhere -- the source does not specify this energy source explicitly.

## Ion Propulsion Energy

Source: 1989073759001878934.yaml

Ion propulsion thrust-to-power relationship (standard):
T = 2 * P * eta / v_exhaust

Where:
T = thrust (N)
P = electrical power input (W)
eta = efficiency (typically 0.6-0.7 for modern ion engines)
v_exhaust = exhaust velocity (30,000 - 90,000 m/s for xenon, vs. ~3,000 m/s for chemical rockets)

Ion propulsion has very high Isp (specific impulse) = v_exhaust / g_0 ≈ 3,000-10,000 s, compared to 300-450 s for chemical rockets. However, thrust levels are very low (millinewtons to a few Newtons), requiring long burn times for significant velocity changes (delta-V).

The source invokes ion propulsion as a possible natural or engineered mechanism for the non-gravitational acceleration of 3I/ATLAS, without making claims about anomalous energy generation.

## 3I/ATLAS Jet Power Conservation

Source: 2005766187758797128.yaml and referenced parent tweet 2005590873623416947

"The total power of two jet-exhausts emerging from 3I/ATLAS remains conserved. Slide 1: when the anti-tail jet (left) is weak, pointing toward the Sun, the other jet (right) is strong. Slide 2: when the anti-tail jet (left) is strong, the other jet (right) is weak."

Power conservation between two jets:
P_jet1 + P_jet2 = P_total = constant

Anticorrelation: when P_jet1 increases, P_jet2 decreases by the same amount.

This is consistent with a fixed total power budget (as would be the case for a craft with a finite power source). For a natural comet, outgassing from two vents would not necessarily show this anticorrelation -- the outgassing rate from each vent depends on local sublimation conditions (solar flux, albedo, thermal inertia), which are independent. The source argues that the anticorrelation of the two jets argues for artificial control of a fixed total power budget.

If genuine, this would be evidence of an engineered energy distribution system: a central power source with total power P_total that can direct energy between two exhaust systems to fine-tune direction. No specific energy source or generation mechanism is claimed.
