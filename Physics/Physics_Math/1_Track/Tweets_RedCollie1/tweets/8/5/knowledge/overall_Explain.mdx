## Jumping Ring Analogy

From tweet 1934367758671896655:
"The crop artists compare UFO levitation to a 'jumping ring'."

The jumping ring is a classical electromagnetic induction demonstration: an aluminum ring placed over a solenoid core is repelled upward when AC power is applied, because the induced eddy current in the ring creates a magnetic moment opposing the solenoid's field (Lenz's law). The induced current and the solenoid field interact to produce a repulsive force (F = ∇(m · B), where m is the induced magnetic moment).

The claim extends this: in the UAP case, the jump is sustained and steerable rather than transient, through the use of a spinning (rotating) rather than static driving field.

## Corbino Disc: Redirection of Force by 90 Degrees via Magnetism

From tweet 2007971953236423039:
"Newton's 3rd Law does not apply to a Corbino disc, because an inward electric force is redirected by N/S magnetism to another force-direction 90 degrees away."

The Corbino disc effect: when a radial electric current flows in a conducting disc placed in an axial magnetic field B (along Z), the Lorentz force F = qv x B redirects the radial current carriers into an azimuthal (tangential) direction. The force on the current-carrying disc is thus redirected 90 degrees from the driving electric force direction.

The claim: because the reaction force (Newton's 3rd Law pair) is redirected by 90 degrees, the standard action-reaction balance is broken for this geometry. If the input force is radially inward (along r), the output force is azimuthal (along phi), and the reaction to the azimuthal force is directed somewhere else -- the system is claimed to not satisfy Newton's 3rd Law in the conventional sense because force direction is not preserved through the magnetic redirection.

This is directly relevant to the UFO propulsion model: the Corbino disc is proposed as a component in the homopolar dynamo that cancels Newtonian reaction forces.
