## Newton's 3rd Law and Its Claimed Violations

Source: 1983881901372010784.yaml, 2007971953236423039.yaml

Newton's 3rd Law: For every force F_12 (object 1 exerts on object 2), there is an equal and opposite reaction force F_21 = -F_12.

Claim 1 (homopolar dynamo):
"If Newton's 3rd Law was strictly observed, then it would never leave the ground, so long as F-up = F-down. But that homopolar dynamo seems to reduce F-down so that it becomes less than F-up."

The argument structure:
* The 3-phase rotating field exerts F_up on the disc magnet
* By Newton's 3rd Law, the disc magnet exerts equal F_down on the field source (the coils, i.e., on the craft body)
* If F_up = F_down, net external force on the craft + disc magnet system = 0, and the craft cannot rise (in the absence of an external medium to push against)
* The counter-rotating homopolar dynamo generates its own angular momentum in the opposite sense
* The claim is that this counter-rotating angular momentum changes the momentum budget of the system such that F_down_net < F_up
* This would require generation of net external force without an external reaction body -- which violates conservation of linear momentum as standardly understood

Claim 2 (Corbino disc):
"Newton's 3rd Law does not apply to a Corbino disc, because an inward electric force is redirected by N/S magnetism to another force-direction 90 degrees away."

Physics of force redirection in the Corbino disc:
* Driving force: radial electric field E_r (from applied voltage) drives radial current J_r
* Lorentz force: F = q(E + v x B). For current carriers moving radially (v = v_r * r_hat) in axial field B = B_z * z_hat:
  * F_Lorentz = q * v_r * r_hat x B_z * z_hat = q * v_r * B_z * (r_hat x z_hat) = -q * v_r * B_z * phi_hat
* The Lorentz force is purely azimuthal (phi direction)
* The original driving force is purely radial
* The reaction to the azimuthal Lorentz force acts on the magnetic field source (the external magnet providing B_z), not on the power supply driving E_r
* The reaction to the radial electric force acts on the power supply electrodes, not on the current carriers' azimuthal momentum

The 90-degree angular separation between action (radial electric) and reaction (azimuthal Lorentz) is claimed to prevent the standard Newton's 3rd Law cancellation because the reactions go to different systems (power supply vs. magnet source) rather than to a common reaction partner.

Standard response: the total system (disc + magnet + power supply + all EM fields) does conserve linear momentum because the EM field itself carries momentum (Poynting vector: g = epsilon_0 * (E x B) = S/c^2). The crossed E_r and B_z create azimuthal Poynting vector, which carries angular momentum in the phi direction. The torque on the current carriers is exactly balanced by the rate of change of electromagnetic field angular momentum. Newton's 3rd Law applies to the total system including EM field momentum.

The source does not engage with EM field momentum arguments, which constitutes the critical gap in the reasoning chain.

## Rotating Magnetic Field and Disc Magnet Dynamics

Source: 1970471190230937728.yaml, video 13_1970464668910231552_video.mdx, video 13_2044924356170854400_video.mdx

Phase 1: Permanent disc magnet above 3-phase coils

The rotating field B(t) at angle phi = omega*t drives the disc magnet via synchronous torque:
tau = m x B(t)

where m is the permanent magnetic dipole moment of the disc magnet.

For m = m_0 * (cos(theta)*z_hat + sin(theta)*(cos(phi_m)*x_hat + sin(phi_m)*y_hat)) and B rotating in XY plane:
The torque drives phi_m to synchronize with phi = omega*t. Once synchronized (phi_m = omega*t + delta_phi for some phase lag delta_phi), the torque becomes a constant synchronizing torque.

Observed: the magnet spins in the SAME direction as the field, regardless of whether North or South pole faces up.

The "regardless of polarity" observation:
* With North pole up: m_z > 0, m_xy in XY plane
* With South pole up: m_z < 0, m_xy reversed
* In both cases, the XY component of m synchronizes to the rotating field
* The axial (Z) component m_z merely shifts the equilibrium phase angle delta_phi
* The synchronous rotation in the XY-plane occurs in both cases -- this is physically expected for a synchronous motor

The axial rise:

The field from three coils below has a spatial gradient along Z (the field weakens with distance from the coils). A magnetic dipole m in a field gradient experiences force:
F = nabla(m · B)

For a disc magnet with m pointing in Z (or partly Z): F_z = m_z * dB_z/dz + m_x * dB_x/dz + m_y * dB_y/dz

For the rotating disc magnet (synchronized to the rotating field), the time-averaged axial force:
<F_z> = <m(t) · nabla B_z(z,t)>

If the helical field has a specific phase relationship between the rotating XY component and the axial gradient dB/dz, there can be a net time-averaged upward force.

The "helical" qualifier: a helix combines rotation in XY with translation in Z. A helical magnetic field has both a rotating transverse component and a component propagating along Z, like a corkscrew. The time-averaged force on a spinning dipole aligned with this helical field is net axial (Z-direction), because the phase of the rotating field is correlated with the axial position.

Formally: if B(r,t) = B_0 * (cos(k*z - omega*t) * x_hat + sin(k*z - omega*t) * y_hat), where k is the helical wavenumber and omega is the angular frequency, then the force on a synchronously rotating dipole m = m_0*(cos(omega*t + phi_0)*x_hat + sin(omega*t+phi_0)*y_hat) is:

F_z = m · dB/dz = m_0 * B_0 * k * sin(k*z - phi_0) (approximately, time-averaged)

This gives a nonzero time-averaged axial force that depends on the phase angle phi_0 and the axial position z (via k*z). The disc magnet rises until it reaches a position where this force balances gravity or falls to zero (where the field gradient diminishes).

Phase 2: Aluminum disc induction -- size-dependent chirality

Large disc (100 mm): eddy currents induced by 3-phase field create a magnetic moment m_eddy. The induced moment lags the driving field by a phase angle determined by the skin depth and the ratio of inductive to resistive impedance (omega*L/R ratio). For the 100 mm disc:
m_eddy = -sigma * omega * ... (complex, frequency-dependent)
The torque tau = m_eddy x B_field is in the SAME direction as the field rotation (co-rotation)

Small disc (70 mm): the same analysis but with different dimension-dependent eddy current distribution. The reversal to anti-clockwise rotation for the smaller disc is presented as anomalous.

Standard skin depth argument: delta = sqrt(2*rho / (omega*mu)), where rho is resistivity and mu is permeability. For aluminum at 20 Hz:
delta ≈ sqrt(2 * 2.7e-8 / (2*pi*20 * 4*pi*1e-7)) ≈ 19 mm

For the 100 mm disc (radius 50 mm >> delta): eddy currents flow in a thin surface layer; the effective eddy current loop has a specific geometry
For the 70 mm disc (radius 35 mm ≈ 2*delta): eddy currents penetrate more deeply; the current distribution is significantly different

The claim that the 70 mm disc spins opposite is presented without a complete theoretical explanation. The source proposes "vortex theory of magnetism" as the required new theoretical framework.

## 3I/ATLAS Jet Conservation and Orbital Mechanics

Source: 2005766187758797128.yaml (text and referenced parent 2005590873623416947)

"3I/ATLAS is headed for the Jupiter-Sun 'Hill radius', as Avi Loeb published a month ago. Those two opposing jets may be used to fine-tune its course."

Parent tweet: "The total power of two jet-exhausts emerging from 3I/ATLAS remains conserved. Slide 1: when the anti-tail jet (left) is weak, pointing toward the Sun, the other jet (right) is strong. Slide 2: when the anti-tail jet (left) is strong, the other jet (right) is weak."

Conservation of jet power: P_jet1 + P_jet2 = P_total = constant. When one jet strengthens, the other weakens, conserving total power. This is claimed as evidence of controlled propulsion rather than natural cometary outgassing.

Hill radius (Hill sphere): the region around a body where its gravity dominates over the Sun's tidal force. For a planet of mass M_p orbiting the Sun (mass M_sun) at distance a:
r_Hill ≈ a * (M_p / 3*M_sun)^(1/3)

For Jupiter: r_Hill ≈ 5.2 AU * (1898e24 / (3 * 1.989e30))^(1/3) ≈ 0.35 AU

If 3I/ATLAS is directed toward Jupiter's Hill sphere, it could decelerate by interaction with Jupiter's gravity well, potentially entering a bound orbit. The claim is that two counter-directional jets are used to fine-tune the trajectory into the Hill sphere.

The argument uses standard orbital mechanics (Hill sphere radius formula) and conservation of power to argue for artificial control of 3I/ATLAS trajectory -- the "constant total power with anticorrelated jet strength" is the key observational claim.
