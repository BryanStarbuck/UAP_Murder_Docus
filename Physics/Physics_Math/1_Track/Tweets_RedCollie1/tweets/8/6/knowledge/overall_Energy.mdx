This is an extremely high power density requirement for the series resistor, suggesting the actual operating current in the larger experiment will be substantially lower (likely ~100A range for practical nichrome wire resistors).

## 4. The Self-Excited Dynamo: Energy Feedback Loop

Source (YAML 1984099193573798291 note_tweet): "We show how a spinning homopolar motor creates a new magnetic field which enhances the applied magnetic field as a 'self excited dynamo' (see Tesla: https://teslauniverse.com/nikola-tesla/articles/notes-unipolar-dynamo). This should reduce or cancel 'magnetic recoil', which creates an opposing magnetic field by Lenz's Law."

A self-excited dynamo is a generator that uses part of its own output current to excite its field magnets. In the homopolar/unipolar dynamo context (Tesla's notes on the unipolar dynamo, 1891):

- The spinning conducting disc generates EMF = ω × B_z × R²/2 (homopolar generator equation)
- In a closed circuit, this EMF drives current I_dynamo through the excitation coil
- I_dynamo creates an additional field ΔB_z = μ₀ × N × I_dynamo / L (coil contribution)
- If ΔB_z is co-aligned with B_z, the total field B_total = B_z + ΔB_z > B_z

This is a positive feedback loop: more field → more EMF → more current → more field. The self-excited condition is:

    ω × (B_z + ΔB_z) × R²/2 = I_dynamo × R_circuit

For stability, the operating point is where the generated EMF equals the resistive drop. The energy for ΔB_z comes from the DC input, not from a free-energy source.

The claimed anti-Lenz effect: the enhanced B_total makes the Lenz's Law induced current work against a larger total field, reducing the fraction of the applied field that is opposed. This does NOT violate energy conservation — it simply reduces the fraction of input energy that is wasted overcoming Lenz's Law opposition, increasing the efficiency of the vortex-lift process.

## 5. Magnetohydrodynamics (MHD) Reference

Source (YAML 1995468258527588830 note_tweet): "Slide 2 and 3 come from someone else's YT video on the 'Lorentz force and MHD'."

MHD (magnetohydrodynamics) governs the behavior of electrically conducting fluids (plasmas, liquid metals) in magnetic fields. In the galinstan homopolar motor, the relevant MHD equations are:

Momentum equation (Navier-Stokes + Lorentz body force):

    ρ(∂u/∂t + (u·∇)u) = -∇p + η∇²u + J × B

where u is fluid velocity, ρ is density, p is pressure, η is dynamic viscosity, J is current density, B is magnetic field.

Current density in the conducting liquid:

    J = σ(E + u × B)

where σ is electrical conductivity and the u × B term is the motional EMF (back-EMF from fluid motion). The Lorentz body force J × B drives azimuthal flow in the galinstan disc, creating the homopolar motor effect in the liquid metal. Energy input is from the DC electrical source; no anomalous energy source is claimed.

The reference to Maury Island 1947 (YAML 1995468258527588830 note_tweet: "Think 'Maury Island 1947'") alludes to an alleged UFO encounter in Washington state where molten slag-like material was reportedly expelled from a craft — interpreted by Drew as evidence that UAPs use liquid metal MHD propulsion systems similar to what he is testing.

Source (YAML 1992043395527971061 note_tweet): "I remain unsure whether we should use liquid galinstan and a DC 12-volt battery? Or need to use some ionizable gas such as argon or xenon with high DC 10 kV voltage for faster velocities?"

At 10 kV DC, argon or xenon gas becomes ionized (breakdown voltage in argon ≈ a few kV for centimeter-scale gaps at near-atmospheric pressure; for xenon, lower due to higher atomic weight and Penning ionization cross-section). The ionized gas forms a plasma with electrical conductivity σ_plasma ≈ 10²-10⁴ S/m (much lower than galinstan ≈ 3.5 × 10⁶ S/m), but at 10 kV the plasma velocity can be much higher:

    v_plasma ∝ E/B  (ExB drift in crossed fields)

At E = 10 kV / 0.01 m = 10⁶ V/m and B = 0.1 T:

    v = E/B = 10⁷ m/s

This is unrealistically high (approaching relativistic speeds) for the experiment geometry, suggesting much lower field strengths are intended. The purpose of high voltage with plasma is to achieve faster counter-spin velocity (ω_gas) to match the 3-phase field frequency with a lower-mass rotating element, satisfying the angular momentum balance condition I_m ω_m = I_g ω_g more easily for low-mass gas vs. dense galinstan.

## 8. Anomalous Energy Interpretation: Mass Recoil Elimination

The elimination of "mass recoil" via angular momentum cancellation can be understood as a redistribution of where the reaction force appears in the system:

Normal operation (L_total ≠ 0):
    F_up (on magnet) → F_down (on coils → on scale)
    Net scale force change = 0

Proposed operation (L_total = 0):
    F_up (on magnet) → internal angular momentum balance absorbs reaction
    Net scale force change = -F_up (scale reads lighter)

In terms of energy: the AC electrical input still provides the energy for the Z-axis displacement (ΔPE = mgΔh). The only difference is that in the L_total = 0 case, the gravitational potential energy of the magnet increases while the scale reads lighter — meaning the Earth-magnet gravitational potential energy changes without the usual Newton's Third Law coupling to the Earth through the support structure.

This is not claimed to be free energy — it is claimed to be a way to convert electrical energy into Z-axis kinetic/potential energy without a Newtonian reaction, analogous to how a rocket converts chemical energy into thrust but without the exhaust. Whether this is physically possible within standard electrodynamics is the unresolved theoretical question.
