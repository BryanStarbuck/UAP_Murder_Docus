============================

NON-NEWTONIAN RESULT #2: HOMOPOLAR MOTOR ACTION-REACTION ANOMALY
============================

* Classical prediction (Newton's 3rd law in rotational dynamics): When a motor drives a rotor clockwise, the stator experiences an equal and opposite anti-clockwise torque. Total angular momentum of the closed system is conserved.

Classical Newton's 3rd law would require the disc (rotor magnet) to rotate in the opposite direction only if the stator wires provide the reaction torque. But the wires do not have sufficient angular momentum/mass for this.
* Additional anomaly: "Where are the torques interacting? This is not equal in opposite torques." [MDX 13_1966828665024704512]
* Investigator's explanation: "Because its spin-motive energy is a sideways Lorentz force." The Lorentz force (F = IL × B) acts transversely to both the current direction and the magnetic field — it is an azimuthal force, not a direct push-pull. The reaction appears in a different spatial direction than the action, breaking the simple collinear Newton's-3rd-law accounting.
* Conventional physics counterpoint: in standard electromagnetic theory, the angular momentum carried by the electromagnetic field (via the Poynting vector stress tensor T_ij) accounts for the missing angular momentum. The investigator does not invoke this explanation.
