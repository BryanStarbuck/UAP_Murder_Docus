ANOMALOUS ENERGY CLAIMS, ENERGY SYSTEMS, HOMOPOLAR GENERATION
============================

TOTAL POWER INPUT FOR PRIMARY EXPERIMENT:
* Variable frequency drive + three-phase coils: 500 W total input power. [MDX 13_1915645568136994817]
* This produces upward forces up to ~1000 grams (9.81 N) in the Newtonian domain.
* Power density: 500 W → 9.81 N, i.e., ~51 W per Newton of force in Newtonian domain.
* In the non-Newtonian domain: upward lift claimed to be 300-500 grams from the same 500 W input (though the mechanism changes from rod-push to magnet-rise, making direct force-to-power comparison non-trivial without full force measurement on a scale).

HOMOPOLAR GENERATOR — ENERGY RECOVERY SYSTEM:
* The investigator explicitly calls the homopolar generator aspect an "energy recovery system" analogous to the alternator in a car.
* "ALL CLEAR? We are talking here about a kind of 'energy recovery system' like the 'alternator' in your car." [YAML 1923616460108333194, note_tweet.text]
* Mechanism: the AC-driven spinning of the internal magnetic element creates a rotating field in the conducting outer medium, inducing a DC current via the homopolar effect.
* This DC current is available to power the Lorentz counter-spin drive (closing the energy loop partially).
* Whether the recovered energy exceeds the energy input for the counter-spin is not quantified in the source material.

HOMOPOLAR GENERATOR MEASURED OUTPUT:
* Liquid galinstan pool with spinning magnet (3-phase driven): I ≈ 0.04 mA DC output. [MDX 13_1975492158070398976]
* Liquid galinstan with magnet spinning directly by 3-phase into galinstan: I ≈ 0.15 mA DC output. [YAML 1975495221040574744, note_tweet.text]
* These are extremely small currents — they represent proof-of-concept generation at sub-milliamp scale, not usable power for counter-spin (which requires 50 A or more).
* Voltage of homopolar generator scales as: V_EMF = omega * B_z * (r_o^2 - r_i^2) / 2. At bench scale (small r_o - r_i, modest B_z, moderate omega), V_EMF is in the microvolt to millivolt range, consistent with the measured microamp currents into the low-resistance liquid-metal load.

SCALE-UP ENERGY REQUIREMENTS FOR COUNTER-SPIN:
* Initial test: 50 A at 12 V DC = 600 W input for Lorentz counter-spin.
* Produced: possible 2-3 grams of anti-gravity (0.02-0.03 N).
* Energy efficiency: ~600 W to generate 0.02-0.03 N — very poor by any standard conversion metric.
* Proposed scale-up: 250-500 A at (presumably) 12 V = 3,000-6,000 W for 10-20 g target.
* The investigator does not claim this represents net energy gain; the proposed benefit is force production, not energy surplus.
* Source: YAML 1995658627571728665

BUGA SPHERE ENERGY SYSTEM — HOMOPOLAR GENERATOR AS ENERGY COLLECTOR:
* The 32 outer electrodes + 16 inner electrodes in the Buga sphere are interpreted as a large-scale homopolar generator.
* The internal spinning conducting fluid (galinstan proposed) generates a radial EMF between inner and outer electrodes.
* This EMF drives a circulating DC current: inner electrodes (axis) to outer electrodes (perimeter) or vice versa.
* The DC current is then routed back into the Lorentz-force drive coils or counter-spin drive.
* This constitutes a closed-loop electromechanical energy conversion: AC drive → spinning magnetic field → spinning conductor → DC homopolar EMF → DC Lorentz drive → counter-spin.
* The net energy balance of this loop is not quantified in the source material. The investigator does not claim over-unity.
* Source: YAML 1923616460108333194 (note_tweet.text)

ANTI-TAIL OF 3I/ATLAS — CLAIMED TECHNOLOGICAL ENERGY SIGNATURE:
* 3I/ATLAS (a solar system interloper object) is claimed to exhibit "a long and powerful, sunward-facing 'anti-tail' opposing the solar wind."
* "No 'natural comet' in all of recorded history has shown such a long and powerful, sunward-facing 'anti-tail'. Opposing the solar wind. Some kind of technological device is required to produce it." [YAML 1992044379775930684, text]
* This claim implies an active energy source within the object that can maintain a directed ion/plasma emission against the solar wind pressure (~1-10 nPa at 1 AU).
* The sunward anti-tail would require an EMF or electromagnetic exhaust mechanism with momentum flux exceeding solar wind dynamic pressure:
    P_solar_wind = (1/2) * rho_sw * v_sw^2 ~ 1-10 nPa at 1 AU
  To maintain a visible anti-tail, the object's emission must carry momentum flux of comparable or greater magnitude in the sunward direction.
* This is interpreted as evidence of an active technological propulsion or energy emission system aboard the interloper.
* Source: YAML 1992044379775930684, YAML 1950002936810999971, YAML 1950002936810999971 (referenced_tweets: "DARK FOREST")

ZERO-POINT ENERGY — NOT EXPLICITLY ADDRESSED:
* The source files in this directory (tweets/8/7/) do not contain explicit references to zero-point energy, vacuum energy, Casimir effects, or over-unity energy claims beyond the counter-spin anti-gravity observation.
* The investigator focuses on Lorentz-force-driven counter-spin and gyroscopic spin-rise as the energy and force mechanism.
* The phrase "against the vacuum of space" in YAML 1995658627571728665 ("fast spin of a permanent magnet upward against the vacuum of space") may allude to a vacuum-field coupling, but no quantitative vacuum-energy model is presented in these files.
