============================

MECHANISM CHAIN AS DESCRIBED:

2. At low frequencies (approximately 12 Hz), the magnet responds with Newtonian upward/downward forces on the central rod — the rod rises due to Newton's third law reaction to the magnet being pushed downward.
3. At elevated frequencies (25-30 Hz), the magnet transitions to a non-Newtonian "top-like" spin regime: it rises stably rather than exhibiting a simple Newtonian force balance. The rod no longer shows a Newtonian reaction force — it hovers freely.
4. A secondary counter-spin is induced in the surrounding medium (galinstan/gallium liquid metal, or air) by a Lorentz force from an applied DC current through the conducting medium; this counter-spin is claimed to cancel the downward Newton's-3rd-law reaction.
5. The combined upward screw-spin (3-phase motor action) plus flat-pancake counter-spin (Lorentz-induced) is theorized to yield a net upward lift force with reduced or zero equal-and-opposite downward push.
