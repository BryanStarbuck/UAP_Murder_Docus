GYROSCOPIC PHYSICS — PRECESSION, NUTATION, AND RISE:
* A spinning permanent magnet in a rotating magnetic field is governed by:
  L_dot = tau
  where L = I * omega is the angular momentum vector of the magnet (I = moment of inertia, omega = angular velocity of spin), and tau is the applied torque from the rotating magnetic field.
* At low spin rates: tau causes precession of L around the vertical axis (standard gyroscope precession Omega_prec = tau / (I * omega_spin)).
* At high spin rates (omega_spin >> omega_field such that the precession rate equals the field rotation rate): the gyroscope can enter a self-sustaining spin-up regime where precession causes the spin axis to align with the field rotation axis (Z-axis), and the magnet rises along Z.
