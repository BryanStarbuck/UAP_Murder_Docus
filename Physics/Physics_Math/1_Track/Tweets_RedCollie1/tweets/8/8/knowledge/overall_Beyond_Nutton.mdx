## 1. The Core Non-Classical Experimental Claim: Two Frequency Domains

### 1a. The Observation (Primary Non-Classical Finding)
A permanent magnet spinning within a three-phase rotating magnetic field exhibits two qualitatively distinct dynamical regimes separated by a frequency threshold:

**Domain 1 (Newtonian, ≲15 Hz):**
- Forces are consistent with classical electromagnetic coupling (dipole-field interaction).
- Upward and downward forces are approximately balanced or predictably weighted.
- Forces as large as 1000 g (9.8 N) are observed but attributable to classical eddy-current and dipole-coupling mechanisms.
- Newton's third law holds: forces on rotor and reaction forces on coil assembly are equal and opposite; net scale weight changes are predictable from classical EM.

**Domain 2 (Non-Newtonian, 25–30 Hz and above):**
- A new upward force term emerges (≈1 kg in the experimental system) that has no apparent classical origin.
- Both polarities of permanent magnet (N-S up and S-N up) produce identical upward force — inconsistent with dipole-field classical coupling (which reverses sign with pole reversal).
- Both directions of field rotation (CW and CCW) produce identical results — inconsistent with classical chirality-dependent torque coupling.
- The net scale weight change is approximately zero despite the large internal upward force — implying Newton's third law is apparently violated (the upward force has no corresponding downward reaction on the external coil assembly as measured by the scale).
- Author labels this: "Second domain of non-Newtonian forces."

### 1b. Non-Newtonian Label Justification
In Newtonian mechanics + classical electrodynamics:
- F_action = −F_reaction (Newton's third law applied to magnet-coil interaction)
- Scale should register the full reaction force to any upward push on the magnet.
- Polarity reversal reverses force direction.
- Rotation chirality reversal reverses torque direction.

All three classical predictions are apparently violated in the high-frequency domain. The author does not propose a specific non-Newtonian force law but describes the experimental phenomenology as requiring "new physics."

## 2. Gyroscopic Anti-Gravity as Non-Classical Extension

### 2a. Standard Gyroscopic Behavior (Classical)
Classical gyroscope physics is well-established: precession ω_p = τ / L, no energy extraction from the angular momentum, no permanent lift without continuous applied torque.

### 2b. Extension Claim
The author extends classical gyroscopic behavior to propose that an internally spinning body can produce sustained lift by "pushing against the vacuum of space" — i.e., the vacuum inertial field serves as the reaction medium, replacing the physical pivot of a precessing gyroscope.

This is related to the Woodward-Mach Mechanism (Woodward effect): James F. While not explicitly cited by the author, the conceptual framework is analogous: vacuum inertia as propulsion reaction medium.

### 2c. What the Author Adds

- This is presented as a measurable, testable phenomenon (the gyroscope demo shows vertical lift from horizontal touch).

## 3. Polarity and Chirality Independence: The Key Non-Classical Signature

### 3a. Classical Prediction
For a dipole **m** in field **B**: **τ** = **m** × **B**
Reversing polarity: **m** → −**m**, therefore **τ** → −**τ** (torque reverses)
Reversing field rotation direction: **B**(t) → **B**(−t), therefore **τ** → −**τ**

Both reversals should produce opposite force/torque behaviors.

### 3b. Experimental Result (Non-Classical)
Both polarity reversals and both field rotation chirality reversals produce identical Z-axis force behavior.
The author explicitly states: "Interestingly, the spinning magnet can have either dipole polarity of NS or SN, NS or SN relative to the earth's downward gravity field. It won't matter — both polarities behave identically, both directions of spin behave identically."

### 3c. Implication
The observed force is even (not odd) under polarity and chirality reversal. Classical electromagnetic forces are odd under both. This implies the force arises from a term in the interaction Hamiltonian that is proportional to m² or |m × B|² (quadratic in the dipole moment, hence even under sign reversal) — or from a genuinely new coupling to the gravitational or inertial field that classical electrodynamics does not include.

## 4. The "Second Domain" Force at Threshold Frequency

### 4a. Threshold Behavior
The non-Newtonian behavior appears at approximately 24–25 Hz and becomes fully developed at 30 Hz. Below ~15 Hz, only Newtonian behavior is observed.

This threshold suggests a resonance or critical spin rate rather than a continuous function of frequency. Possible interpretations:

## 5. Magnetic Recoil: A Partially Non-Classical Phenomenon

### 5a. Classical Component
"Magnetic recoil" in the Newtonian regime is classical: the repulsive force between the coil's induced field and the permanent magnet's field produces an equal and opposite reaction on the coil assembly — fully described by Maxwell's equations and Newton's third law.

### 5b. Non-Classical Component
In the high-frequency domain, the apparent absence of magnetic recoil on the scale (despite the measured upward force on the rotor) constitutes the non-classical result. Either:
(a) The recoil force is exactly canceled by some other force within the apparatus (self-cancellation), or
(b) The upward force is not electromagnetically coupled to the coil assembly in the classical sense (it arises from a coupling to an external field not present in the coil, such as the gravitational field or the inertial vacuum field).

The author does not resolve this ambiguity but states both "mass recoil" and "magnetic recoil" must be cancelled for free levitation.

## 6. Counter-Rotating Flywheel Geometry: Proposed New Physics

### 6a. CARET Program Reference
The author references the CARET (Completely Automated Research for Extraterrestrial Technology) program as having studied a "portable anti-gravity device" with two magnetic flywheels spinning in opposite directions. This configuration is proposed as the mechanism by which UAP achieves levitation without the recoil problem.

### 6b. Physics Beyond Newton
In classical mechanics, two counter-rotating flywheels cancel net angular momentum but not mechanical forces. If each flywheel produces a non-Newtonian upward force (from the high-frequency spinning-magnet effect), and if the magnetic recoil of each cancels the other's, the result is:
Net upward force = 2 × F_up (additive)
Net recoil = 0 (canceling)

This would constitute a genuine violation of Newton's third law as applied to the electromagnetic interaction — or a coupling to a non-electromagnetic field (gravity/vacuum) that provides the reaction partner without manifest mechanical feedback.

## 7. Gravity Lens Test: Negative Non-Classical Result

The non-Newtonian forces claimed in the spinning-magnet experiments are therefore NOT attributable to electromagnetic stress-energy contributions to the Einstein field equations (which would require energies many orders of magnitude beyond laboratory EM fields).
