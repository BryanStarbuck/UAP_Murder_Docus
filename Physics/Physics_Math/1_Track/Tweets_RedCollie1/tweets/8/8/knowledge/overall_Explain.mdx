## Central Thesis: Spinning Magnetism as the Path to Non-Newtonian Propulsion

The author (Dr. Horace Drew, Caltech / MRC LMB Cambridge) argues that "spinning magnetism" — as distinct from classical dipole magnetism — represents a domain of physics that was not developed in the public corpus after ~1950, but is allegedly encoded in crop-circle diagrams and demonstrated in laboratory experiments. The beneficial output sought is a compact propulsion/anti-gravity device capable of producing net upward force without equivalent downward reaction — i.e., a device that decouples from the ground-state gravitational potential without expelling reaction mass in the conventional Newtonian sense.

### What Physics Advance is Claimed

1. **Frequency-domain bifurcation of magnetic force behavior.** A permanent magnet spinning within a three-phase rotating magnetic field produced by UVW-phased wire coils exhibits qualitatively different force behavior in two distinct frequency regimes:
   - Low-frequency regime (≲15 Hz): Newtonian dipole-like forces dominate. Up and down forces are roughly balanced; weight on a calibrated scale shows conventional response; the system behaves as classical electromagnetic coupling predicts.
   - High-frequency regime (25–30 Hz and above): Non-Newtonian "top-like" forces emerge. The measured upward force can reach ~1 kg, but the scale weight does not register a corresponding net reduction because a simultaneous downward force (attributed to "mass recoil" and "magnetic recoil") nearly cancels it. The author labels this the "second domain of non-Newtonian forces."

2. **Gyroscopic analogy extended to vacuum coupling.** The author draws an analogy between a precessing gyroscope — where horizontal torque input is converted by angular momentum conservation into rotation about an orthogonal axis (a 90-degree spatial rotation of the torque vector), producing net vertical lift — and the proposed UFO propulsion mechanism. In both cases, a spinning body couples an externally applied horizontal force into a vertical displacement. For UFO propulsion, the vacuum of space replaces the physical fulcrum of the gyroscope's pivot; the craft is claimed to "push against the vacuum."

3. **Key engineering challenge stated explicitly:** The upward force produced by a spinning magnet in a 3-phase field is always accompanied by a nearly equal downward force ("magnetic recoil") plus a "mass recoil" term. Net levitation requires suppressing both recoil terms while preserving the upward force. The author proposes that a liquid self-excited dynamo (using conducting liquids such as mercury, galinstan, or liquid gallium) may be capable of cancelling both recoil terms simultaneously.

4. **Dual counter-rotating magnetic flywheels.** Reference is made to a "portable anti-gravity device" studied under the CARET program (alleged government research), in which two magnetic flywheels spin in opposite directions. This configuration is claimed to appear in alien-attributed crop-circle diagrams and to have been observed aboard a UAP by contactee Norman Paulsen. The opposing spin directions are proposed to cancel the "magnetic recoil" through mutual flux cancellation, while the angular momentum vectors sum to produce net vertical impulse.

5. **Five-phase AC power extension.** Beyond the standard three-phase (UVW, 120° apart) configuration, the author proposes five-phase AC power (five wire coils at 72° separation) as an alternative or enhancement for spinning magnetic levitation, referencing the "Buga orb-sphere" — a physical UAP artifact with five wire coils embedded in its aluminum shell — as evidence. Five-phase rotating magnetic fields produce a smoother, more symmetric rotating field envelope than three-phase and may reduce torque ripple in the magnetic coupling.

6. **Homopolar generator as energy recovery subsystem.** The homopolar generator (Faraday disc) — demonstrated using an inverted stainless steel bowl acting as the conducting disc — is characterized not as the anti-gravity component but as the "energy recovery system" of an orb-type UAP. The homopolar generator converts rotational kinetic energy of the spinning conducting mass into DC electrical power without commutation, enabling continuous energy extraction from the spinning flywheel to power the propulsion coils in a feedback loop.

7. **MHD / Lorentz-force fluid drive as propulsive medium.** The Lorentz force F = J × B (current density crossed with magnetic field) acting on conducting liquids (mercury, galinstan, gallium) drives them into rotational vortex motion. This fluid vortex is proposed as an intermediate step: the rotating conducting fluid generates a self-sustaining dynamo field that supplements or replaces the external 3-phase driving power, enabling a self-excited loop. At 90 A demonstrated current levels, visible vortex rotation is produced in a flat copper coil / central rod electrode geometry.

### Advancement Pathway

The chain from known physics to the claimed advance:

Classical three-phase induction motor physics (Tesla, 1888) → extension to permanent-magnet rotor in rotating field → frequency-domain emergence of non-Newtonian force term → identification of "mass recoil" and "magnetic recoil" as the barrier to net levitation → proposed cancellation via liquid conducting dynamo or counter-rotating flywheels → net upward force without reaction mass expelled → propulsion/anti-gravity device applicable to space travel.

The proposed benefit if realized: spacecraft propulsion that does not require chemical or nuclear reaction mass, enabling effective interplanetary and interstellar transit. The author explicitly states: "We HAVE to discover the physics of UFO space propulsion, in order to travel effectively to the planets and stars."
