## 1. Primary Z-Axis Lift Mechanism: Spinning Magnet in Three-Phase Field

### 1a. Experimental Setup
- Permanent magnet (70 mm diameter, 14 mm central hole) seated on a 6.3 mm aluminum rod.
- Three-phase wire coil arrays (U=0°, V=120°, W=240° AC phase) positioned above and below the rotor magnet.
- Silicone bead positioned above the magnet as a mechanical stop / force capture element.
- Rod constrained to move only vertically (up or down).
- Scale below the rod assembly measures apparent weight changes.

### 1b. Experimental Observations — Z-Axis Response
- At 3-phase power ON: magnet begins spinning (azimuthal rotation in XY-plane).
- Scale starts at zero.
- Magnet rises vertically (Z+) and contacts the silicone bead.
- After contact with bead: rod lifts slightly upward (Z+), but scale reads approximately zero net weight — implying net upward force ≈ net downward force.
- Forcibly pushing rod downward (Z−) at low speed: scale registers 600 g downward — confirming substantial upward force is being exerted by the system.
- At ~30 Hz: pressing down on the rod requires > 1 kg of force, i.e., the net upward magnetic force on the rotor exceeds 1 kg (≈ 9.8 N).
- Without pushing: scale reads approximately zero — net upward force is nearly canceled by "magnetic recoil" and "mass recoil" downward terms.

### 1c. Dual-Force Problem for Z-Axis Levitation
The Z-axis force balance in the non-Newtonian regime:
F_up(spinning magnet) + F_down(magnetic recoil) + F_down(mass recoil) ≈ 0

Both the upward force AND the opposing downward recoil forces increase together as spin frequency increases. The ratio F_up / (F_down_total) is slightly greater than 1, but insufficient to produce free levitation.

Author's statement: "The force up is only maybe a tiny bit bigger than the force down. So that's the basic trouble with the UFO levitation. We have to get the force up to be much greater than the force down for spinning magnetism."

### 1d. Required Condition for Actual Levitation
For net vertical lift to occur (vehicle leaves the ground):
F_up > Mg + F_down(magnetic recoil) + F_down(mass recoil)

where Mg = weight of craft plus payload.

This requires either:
(a) Suppression of the recoil terms while preserving the upward force — proposed via liquid self-excited dynamo.
(b) Engineering a configuration in which the recoil forces cancel internally without canceling the upward force — proposed via dual counter-rotating flywheels (CARET device geometry).

## 2. Gyroscopic Lift Mechanism

### 2a. Observed Phenomenon
- A spinning gyroscope disc, when given a horizontal nudge (horizontal torque applied), lifts upward.
- Without spin: horizontal push produces only horizontal rotation (no vertical displacement).
- With spin (disc rotating away from observer): touch from side → blue side (top) lifts up.
- With spin (disc rotating toward observer): touch from side → red side (top) lifts up.
- The vertical lift occurs at 90° from the direction of the applied horizontal force.

### 2b. Physical Explanation
- The spin angular momentum vector **L** is directed along the spin axis (vertical or near-vertical).
- Applied horizontal torque **τ**_H is perpendicular to **L**.
- By the gyroscope equation **τ** = d**L**/dt, the angular momentum vector precesses perpendicular to both **τ**_H and **L**, which is a vertical direction (Z-axis).
- This precession constitutes a rotation of the entire gyroscope assembly about the vertical axis — but if the gyroscope is constrained (e.g., the hub is fixed), the precession force is expressed as a vertical force (lift).

### 2c. Extension to UFO Propulsion
Author's claim: "Internally-spinning UFOs can lift up, by pushing against the 'empty' vacuum of space."
- The vacuum of space plays the role of the external torque source (the pivot or horizontal force) that the gyroscope requires.
- An internally spinning mass (permanent magnet flywheel) in a UFO, when its angular momentum vector is perturbed by an internal mechanism, produces precession forces oriented vertically (Z-axis).
- The "push against vacuum" is claimed to be the mechanism by which no external reaction mass is required.
- This is related to the Mach principle interpretation: the inertial reference frame of the vacuum (Machian inertia) provides the "lever" against which the spinning internal mass reacts.

### 2d. Key Requirement: External Force
Author explicitly states: "We require an outside force to make it lift up." For the gyroscope model of UFO propulsion, this external force is proposed to be:
- The rotating magnetic field (3-phase coils) applying a continuously rotating torque to the spinning permanent magnet.
- The torque rotates the angular momentum vector continuously, producing a sustained precession (and thus a sustained Z-axis force) rather than a one-time deflection.

### 5a. Force on Magnet Below the Coils
"If we have three phase wire coils in the center, our magnet coming from below will be lifted as a force up."

### 5b. Force on Magnet Above the Coils

The author's claim is not that gravity is locally shielded or reduced in the classical Biefeld-Brown sense, but that the spinning magnet in a 3-phase field produces a Z-axis force that is independent of the local gravitational field direction (i.e., it always produces an upward force in the direction opposing gravity, regardless of magnet polarity or spin chirality).

2. The polarity independence means the force is not simply a magnetic repulsion (which is polarity-dependent).
3. The frequency threshold (at ~25 Hz) suggests the effect is tied to a dynamical regime not present in static magnetism.

Negative gravity lens result: at high electromagnetic powers, no measurable gravitational lensing effect is produced, confirming that the mechanism is not GR-level spacetime curvature.
