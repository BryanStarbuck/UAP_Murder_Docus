## 2. What Physics Advances Are Claimed to Enable This

### 2.1 Frequency-Dependent Force Reversal in Rotating Magnetic Field

Source: 13_1916095047227084800_video.mdx

The demonstrated physics (confirmed experimentally, though conventionally explained):
* At low frequencies (< ~20 Hz), the 3-phase rotating field + spinning disc magnet produces a NET DOWNWARD FORCE on the magnet (Newtonian mechanical screw effect), transmitted as weight increase on scale
* At high frequencies (> ~25–30 Hz), this downward force disappears (gyroscopic decoupling)

The NOT-YET-DEMONSTRATED but claimed physics:
* At high frequencies, the gyroscopic regime produces a NET UPWARD FORCE (not merely zero force) that needs to be "captured" by a different coupling mechanism
* The transition from "pushes down" to "pushes up" is what the author is trying to capture

Physical advance required: Understanding whether and under what conditions the non-Newtonian (gyroscopic) phase of a spinning magnet in a rotating field can produce net axial thrust greater than zero. This is not established by mainstream physics.

### 2.2 Five-Phase Power for Smoother Field

Source: 1970987567299559428.yaml

Five-phase AC (72-degree phase offset) produces a more uniform rotating field than 3-phase (120-degree). The physical advance is:
* Reduced torque ripple on the spinning rotor (smoother coupling)
* Claimed: better efficiency in the non-Newtonian regime
* Practical outcome: potentially higher RPM achievable for the spinning magnet, pushing further into the gyroscopic regime

### 2.3 Counter-Rotating Geometry for Angular Momentum Cancellation

Source: 2027850978926727258.yaml

Physical design advance: Having both a central upward-moving spinning magnet AND peripheral counter-rotating magnets in the same device simultaneously:
* Central magnet: large, spins on axis (Z-axis angular momentum), driven upward by axial rotating field
* Peripheral magnets: many small, spin in horizontal plane in opposite direction (cancel Z-angular momentum of central magnet while spinning in XY plane)

If this works as claimed:
* The device has zero net angular momentum (angular momentum conservation not violated)
* The device produces net upward force (linear momentum not conserved — this is the non-classical claim)

Physics advance needed: Demonstration that the angular-momentum-cancellation configuration produces measurably different lift than a single-rotor configuration. This requires careful isolated force measurement (no mechanical contact between device and test platform during measurement).

### 2.4 Pole-Ball Spherical Architecture

Source: 1923735136211959909.yaml

Design advance: A spherical device with 5 internal coils at 72-degree spacing. This geometry has specific advantages:
* A sphere has no preferred orientation (full SO(3) symmetry) — a spherical craft can accelerate in any direction without reorienting its structure
* 5-phase internal coils generate a rotating internal magnetic field in any plane (by selecting which coils to drive)
* The rotating field drives the sphere's outer shell or an internal rotor to spin

If the "pushing against vacuum" mechanism is valid, a spherical implementation could:
* Generate thrust in any direction by selecting field orientation
* Have no directional preference (unlike a disc-based system which has an axial preference)

This would be a significant design advance for omnidirectional propulsion.

### 3.2 Scale Measurement Protocol (Woodward-Effect Analogy)

Source: 13_1916095047227084800_preview.txt, 13_1916095047227084800_video.mdx

The experimental methodology directly parallels thrust measurement in the Woodward Mach Effect Thruster (MET) program:
* Woodward's device is also measured by placing it on a precision balance and measuring net weight change during operation
* Key requirement: the device must be electrically isolated (no mechanical thrust transmission from wiring/cables)
* The author's current device has wires connected during measurement, which could transmit mechanical forces from the power supply

Physics advancement needed for valid measurement: Replace alligator clip connections with wireless power or flexible cables that cannot transmit axial force; measure on a soft-mounted pendulum balance rather than a hard-coupled scale; achieve 6-sigma statistical significance for any net weight reduction.

### 3.3 From Scale Measurement to Flight

The gap between "scale reads X grams less" and "device lifts off" is:
* Must measure: F_lift > M_device * g (gravitational force on full device assembly)
* Current best measurement: maximum weight INCREASE = 300 g during Newtonian regime; gyroscopic regime = ~0 g change
* For liftoff: need F_lift > (total device weight)/g; device appears to be several kg, so need several kg of net upward force

The specific physics advance needed (bridging claim): demonstrate that in the non-Newtonian (gyroscopic) regime, the scale reading goes BELOW tared zero (negative reading = upward force on device greater than weight). None of the tweets in this batch report achieving this. The author states it as future work: "very soon" (2045097486575161759.yaml reply about scale test results).
