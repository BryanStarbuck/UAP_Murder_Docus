The interior of the formation contains a highly complex, bilaterally symmetric design:
- Dominant feature: An hourglass or figure-8 pattern running vertically through the center of the circle
- The hourglass has concentric oval/elliptical rings within it, creating a layered depth appearance
- The top half: concentric oval arcs converging upward (like a vortex or cone seen from above)
- The bottom half: mirror-image concentric oval arcs converging downward
- Where the two halves meet at the center: a pinch point or narrow waist

- These flanking elements create a 4-fold symmetry when combined with the hourglass
- The diamond texture visible in the wings is characteristic of highly detailed crop circle formations created by precisely flattening alternating sections of crop
