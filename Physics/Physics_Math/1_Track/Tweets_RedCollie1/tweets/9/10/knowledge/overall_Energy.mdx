1. ENERGY INPUT: 3-PHASE ROTATING MAGNETIC FIELD DRIVE
============================================================================================

Source (1928409956849692903, text):
"To get the most force by spinning magnetic levitation, place a central magnet at the center
of six 3-phase wire coils."

The energy input to the system is standard 3-phase AC electrical power:
    P_in = 3 · V_phase · I_phase · cos(φ)  [W]
where:
    V_phase = per-phase RMS voltage [V]
    I_phase = per-phase RMS current [A]
    φ       = power factor angle between V and I

At the ~10 Hz drive frequency observed in experiment (video 13_1976075963135037440):
    ω_drive = 2π·10 ≈ 62.8 rad/s
This is far below conventional power line frequency (50/60 Hz), suggesting a variable
frequency drive (VFD) or function generator power supply in the lab demonstrations.

2. CLAIMED ANOMALOUS LIFT vs. CONVENTIONAL EXPECTATION
============================================================================================

Source (1946057554548912446, note_tweet):
"We should be able to get a powerful anti-gravity force during the 'transition' between
low rpm and high rpm, as the axis of precession becomes more vertical."

Drew claims the anti-gravity force generated during the rpm transition constitutes energy
output (mechanical work against gravity: W = M·g·Δz) that is not accounted for in
conventional electromagnetic motor analysis. However, he does not claim this energy is
generated from vacuum or zero-point fields — the input energy is the 3-phase electrical
power driving the coils.

The anomaly claim is not about energy creation but about the DIRECTION of force application:
conventionally, a motor spinning an internal component produces reaction torque on the frame
(which does not lift the frame). Drew claims the gyroscopic transition regime generates a
real Z-axis force that lifts the entire assembly — which, if true, would not violate energy
conservation but would represent a previously unrecognized transduction pathway from
rotational kinetic energy (about XY axes) to linear kinetic energy (along Z axis) via
angular momentum vector precession.

============================================================================================

Source (1976079428825514470, note_tweet) and video (13_1976075963135037440_video.mdx):
"No change of weight on the scale when we do that, even if we go fast to look."

Current experimental status: the scale shows ΔW = 0 when the magnet rises in the 3-phase
coil. This means all energy is circulating internally:
    W_electrical_in → W_rotational_magnet + W_rotational_coils (equal and opposite)
    → No net W_gravitational (no work done against gravity in the lab frame)

The experiment as currently configured does NOT demonstrate anomalous energy output.
The entire electrical input is consumed by:
    - Coil resistive losses (I²R)
    - Magnetic hysteresis and eddy current losses in the magnet
    - Mechanical friction (small, as coil is on a "Lazy Susan" or floating)
    - Field energy storage in the rotating magnetic field

4. PROPOSED ENERGY PATHWAY — LORENTZ LIQUID METAL COUNTER-SPIN
============================================================================================

Source (2001577665980174511, note_tweet):
"The new idea (to solve that problem) is that we might add a counter-spinning Lorentz force
in liquid metal (galinstan), so as to violate Newton's 3rd law of action-reaction for two
counter-spinning objects."

If the Lorentz liquid metal spin successfully cancels the coil reaction torque (Drew's
hypothesis), the energy budget changes:
    W_electrical_3phase → W_rotational_magnet (↑ spin energy)
    W_DC_Lorentz → W_rotational_liquid_metal (counter-spin)

Net result (if hypothesis is correct):
    - The reaction torque on coils → 0
    - Gyroscopic precession force on Z-axis becomes net external
    - W_gravitational = M·g·Δz (positive work done against gravity)
    - Energy is conserved — the electrical input (3-phase + DC Lorentz) equals
      the gravitational potential energy gained plus resistive losses

This is not a free-energy claim. It is a claim about a novel energy transduction pathway
(electrical → rotational → linear gravitational potential) via an unconventional mechanism.

5. ION THRUSTER ENERGY EFFICIENCY (CONVENTIONAL)
============================================================================================

Source (2005919502165242353, text):
"Ion thrusters"

Ion thrusters are mentioned as the hypothesized propulsion of 3I/ATLAS. Relevant energy
parameters for context:
    Specific impulse: I_sp ~ 3,000–10,000 s (vs. chemical rockets ~300–450 s)
    Thrust: F_thrust = (ṁ · v_exhaust) [N], where ṁ = mass flow rate [kg/s]
    Power: P = (1/2) · ṁ · v_exhaust² = F² / (2 · ṁ) [W]
    → At high I_sp, very low thrust per watt (mN/kW range)
    → At scale of a large spacecraft, enormous power required for meaningful thrust

Drew identifies ion thrusters (not magnetic anti-gravity) as the probable propulsion of
the interstellar object 3I/ATLAS. This is consistent with conventional high-efficiency
propulsion engineering, not with the anomalous physics he proposes for smaller UAPs.
