============================================================================================

2. GYROSCOPIC PRECESSION → ANTI-GRAVITY TRANSITION
   - A spinning top "rises up" against gravity as rpm increases. This is an observable,
     reproducible effect of gyroscopic precession: as precession-axis tilt changes with
     angular momentum L = I·ω, the effective support vector changes direction.
   - Drew proposes this same mechanism, amplified by magnetic spin rather than mechanical
     contact spin, as the foundation of UAP lift.
   - Experimental apparatus: spinning disc-magnet levitated by a 3-phase coil arrangement,
     filmed on video (tweet 1946057554548912446, videos 1–3).

3. HOMOPOLAR MOTOR CONFIGURATION
   - A disc-magnet configured as a homopolar motor (Faraday disk configuration) will be
     "lifted up" by an applied 3-phase magnetic field.
   - Source (tweet 1989474122372714627, retweeted text): "A disc-magnet which is spinning as
     a 'homopolar motor' will be 'lifted up' by an applied 3-phase magnetic field."

4. LIQUID METAL (GALINSTAN) LORENTZ SPIN TO CANCEL REACTION
   - The core engineering problem: spinning a magnet with a 3-phase coil imparts an equal
     and opposite spin to the coil assembly (conservation of angular momentum). This means
     no net change in total weight on a scale — confirmed by experiment (tweet
     1976079428825514470, note_tweet and video transcription).
   - Proposed solution: drive a counter-rotating Lorentz current in liquid metal galinstan.
     The DC Lorentz force produces rotation in a plane 90° orthogonal to the input current
     vector, so by geometric argument its angular momentum vector is orthogonal to that of
     the spinning magnet. Drew claims this breaks the action-reaction symmetry for the
     lift axis.
   - Experimental apparatus: stainless steel bowl, permanent magnet 60 mm below (fixed),
     second permanent magnet above that can rise with 3-phase power, liquid metal galinstan
     between them, 3-phase wire coils surrounding the bowl. Electrode rods: 5 mm and 6 mm
     diameter tested.
   - Source: video transcription 13_2001574820505620480_video.mdx and note_tweet in
     2001577665980174511.

5. ZERO WEIGHT-CHANGE ON SCALE AS KEY DIAGNOSTIC
   - Experimental result stated in tweet 1976079428825514470 (note_tweet) and confirmed in
     video 13_1976075963135037440_video.mdx: "No change of weight on the scale when we do
     that, even if we go fast."
   - The conservation-of-angular-momentum explanation: any spin imparted to the magnet is
     compensated by equal and opposite spin of the coil ("boat") system — shown by the
     floating-boat angular momentum conservation demonstration (video
     13_1976075981111828480_video.mdx).
   - The proposed Lorentz-counter-spin step is intended to circumvent this null result.
