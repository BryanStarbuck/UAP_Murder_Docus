2. SPINNING TOP ANALOGY — OBSERVED LIFT PHENOMENON
============================================================================================

Source (1946057554548912446, text and note_tweet):

"We can see a similar effect with a spinning disc-magnet (video 3). Yet as it lifts off the

The spinning top observation is standard precession mechanics. The upward rise occurs because:
- At low ω: precession axis is tilted, precession rate Ω_prec = M·g·r/(I·ω) is fast
- At high ω: precession axis approaches vertical, spin axis aligns with gravity vector
- The transition involves a net upward displacement of the center of mass (nutation damping)
- For the top resting on a frictionless surface: the rising is energetically driven by the
  conversion of precessional kinetic energy to vertical potential energy as ω increases

For the spinning disc-magnet:
- The magnetic coupling to the 3-phase coils provides the torque to spin up ω
- The same gyroscopic rising effect is observed
- Problem: "as it lifts off the ground, its connection to the lower device becomes unstable"
  → the magnetic coupling (needed to maintain spin) weakens as the magnet rises away from
  the coils → magnet flies off rather than producing stable controlled lift

6. WEIGHT-CHANGE NULL RESULT — PHYSICAL EXPLANATION
============================================================================================

Source (1976079428825514470, note_tweet):
"Any increase of spin angular motion (say by a small spinning magnet) creates an equal and
opposite spin in the 'boat' to which it is attached (or to those three wire coils placed
below in movie 1). If we place those three wire coils on a 'Lazy Susan' (not shown), we
can see them turning in a direction opposite to that of the fast-spinning magnet. Hence
there [is] no change of total weight on a scale, even though the spinning magnet RISES
FORCEFULLY, by pushing against a vacuum of space."

Explanation: Conservation of angular momentum means the coil assembly acquires
L_coil = −L_magnet. The net vertical force on the external scale is:
    ΔN = d²(M·z_cm)/dt² = 0 (center of mass does not accelerate)
Therefore the scale reads no change. The magnet rises WITHIN the system, but the system
center of mass remains stationary → no external lift force.
