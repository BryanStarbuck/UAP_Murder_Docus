## Claimed Newton's 3rd Law Violation as Energy Anomaly

### Tweet 1971034425413812434, note_tweet
"The fast spin of that liquid metal does not follow Newton's 3rd Law, so if we could
direct its 'Lorentz force' upward, then we would have 'anti-gravity'."

The Newton's 3rd Law violation claim implies:
* If the liquid metal exerts an upward force on the container without a downward
  reaction on the Earth/support structure, then work done lifting the container
  is not balanced by work done against a reaction.
* If force is generated without an equal reaction, the energy bookkeeping of classical
  mechanics breaks down.
* In standard MHD, the "reaction" is carried by the electromagnetic field (Maxwell
  stress tensor) and ultimately by the power supply providing J. Drew's claim implies
  the reaction is not found in the mechanical system visible to the experimenter.
* This is NOT an over-unity claim in the sense of output energy exceeding electrical
  input — it is a claim of directional momentum asymmetry in the mechanical domain.

### Tweet 1996203901125837155
"If you spin a magnet by 3-phase forces, you see both spinning and counter-spinning
forces of equal spin angular momentum. Not so for the spinning Lorentz force!"

Energy interpretation:
* For 3-phase spun magnet: energy goes into equal and opposite angular momenta (stator
  reacts against rotor). Total mechanical energy equals electrical energy input (minus
  resistive losses). Angular momentum is conserved; no anomaly.
* For Lorentz force on liquid metal: angular momentum of the fluid appears without
  equal counter-angular-momentum in an obvious mechanical component. Drew identifies
  this asymmetry as potentially exploitable for propulsion beyond Newton's 3rd Law.

## Galinstan as Energy-Conversion Medium

### Physical properties relevant to energy conversion
Galinstan (Ga-In-Sn eutectic, m.p. ≈ -19°C, b.p. >1300°C):
* Electrical conductivity sigma ≈ 3.46 × 10^6 S/m (good conductor, ~6% of copper)
* Density rho ≈ 6440 kg/m^3
* Low viscosity: eta ≈ 0.0024 Pa·s (similar to water at room temperature)
* Non-toxic unlike mercury

These properties make galinstan an efficient MHD working fluid:
* High sigma → large J × B body force for given applied current
* Low eta → fluid responds quickly to Lorentz force (low magnetic Reynolds number
  criterion not limiting)
* High density → significant angular momentum when spinning

Energy conversion pathway:
    Electrical (12V, 50A) → Ohmic heating + Lorentz body force → Kinetic energy of
    vortex flow → (if directed axially) → Upward force on container → Potential energy
    of lifted craft

The anomaly claim: this upward force does not have a Newton's 3rd Law reaction
downward → energy input does not need to overcome a reaction weight → effectively
free lift for cost of electrical input only.

## Energy Relevance to Anti-Gravity Goal

Drew's key statement (tweet 1963915629892899295):
"It is easy to get increased gravitational 'potential' energy, but not decreased total-
weight on a scale."

This means:
* Energy input → mechanical uplift of rotating component → increase in (mass × g × height).
* This is NORMAL energy conversion (electrical → gravitational PE).
* What Drew has NOT achieved: a situation where total weight on a scale decreases while
  electrical power is applied (i.e., where the propulsive force exceeds classical
  electromagnetic reaction, and net lift force reduces apparent weight).

If the proposed mechanism worked:
* Electrical energy (600W input) → anomalous upward Lorentz body force → lift
* The craft's weight W = Mg could be partially or fully cancelled without a downward
  reaction on the support structure.
* This would be an effective energy-free lift (the electrical energy would drive
  only the rotational motion, not the gravitational work — the gravitational work
  would appear "free" from the perspective of the support structure).

This is the specific over-Newton energy/momentum anomaly Drew is pursuing,
though he has not yet achieved it at lab scale.
