# Bridging: Beneficial Output / Advancement from Research Beyond Newtonian Mechanics
# Source: RedCollie1 (Dr. Horace Drew), tweets batch 9/2

## Core Claim and Research Program

Dr. Horace Drew (Red Collie, Caltech / MRC LMB Cambridge) asserts that UFO/UAP propulsion
operates through "spinning magnetic levitation" — a mechanism he has physically modeled at
small scale. The central research question: can a spinning magnetic system produce a net
upward force sufficient for levitation, and can that force be harnessed for practical
propulsion analogous to how James Watt harnessed steam?

## The James Watt Analogy (explicit in source)

Drew explicitly frames his research via the Watt steam engine analogy:
* Watt's valve reversed force halfway through each rotary cycle — functionally identical
  to an early DC motor commutation step.
* Drew argues an equivalent "valve" or control mechanism for magnetic spin-force could
  enable anti-gravity propulsion, beginning a new industrial revolution.
* The specific challenge: identifying how to harness the high-frequency upward gyroscopic
  spinning force for useful work ("levitation" or "propulsion"), exactly as Watt harnessed
  steam expansion through a reversing valve.

## Advancement Beyond Newtonian Mechanics: What Is Claimed

1. Newton's 3rd Law violation candidate: Drew states explicitly that the fast spin of liquid
   metal galinstan in the Lorentz force configuration "does not follow Newton's 3rd Law."
   If the Lorentz force on the liquid metal can be directed upward, the claim is that
   "anti-gravity" results — i.e., a net upward thrust without an equal and opposite
   downward thrust on a reaction mass.

2. Frequency-dependent force reversal: At low frequency (< 20 Hz), a 3-phase spinning
   magnetic system produces a downward or upward force on the central pole (direction
   unpredictable across specimens). At high frequency (25-30 Hz), all specimens shift
   to a strong upward gyroscopic force. This frequency-dependent transition from
   "laminar" to "chaotic" spin behavior (Drew's terminology) has no classical explanation
   within standard magnetostatics or standard gyroscope theory.

3. Gyroscopic decoupling: At high frequency (~25-30 Hz), the spinning magnet decouples
   from the central pole. A previously large force on the pole (upward or downward) goes
   to ZERO as the spinning magnet hovers like a gyroscope. Drew identifies this as a
   new kind of gyroscopic motion never previously observed using iron-free, 3-phase,
   copper electromagnets.

4. Practical propulsion architecture proposed: A disc-shaped craft (matching observed UAP
   geometry) with two sets of 3-phase wire coils (six coils each, wired U-V-W in phase),
   one above and one below a central magnetic flywheel. The flywheel spins at high speed
   between the two coil sets. The proposed benefit: achieving upward force (anti-gravity
   effect) through the spin-gyroscopic mechanism, without rocket propellant, without
   combustion, and without classical reaction mass.

5. Liquid metal / magnet combination: A permanent disc magnet embedded in liquid metal
   galinstan, driven by DC power (12 V, ~50 A), produces magnetically-driven vortex flow.
   Simultaneous application of 3-phase power to the embedded magnet and Lorentz-force DC
   power to the galinstan creates combined spin of both components. This combination is
   proposed as necessary to achieve lift.

6. Connection to astrophysical helical magnetic jets: Drew explicitly links the observed
   upward spin force in his lab to helical magnetic fields observed in astrophysical
   proto-stellar and galactic jets ("jets" in telescope imagery). He notes that helical
   magnetic fields also act on ionized air (not only solid magnets), though more weakly.
   This astrophysical analogy is treated as evidence that the underlying physics is real
   and scale-independent.

## Open Engineering Problem Stated

Drew states he can achieve increased gravitational "potential energy" from a spinning
flywheel but cannot yet achieve decreased total weight on a scale — unless the central
spinning flywheel enters "free fall." The engineering gap between observed lab effects
and practical anti-gravity levitation remains unsolved in this source corpus. He solicits
engineering ideas explicitly.

## Comparison to Laithwaite (1970s)

Eric Laithwaite's experiments with large mechanical spinning gyroscopes in the 1970s are
referenced as a related prior observation. Drew notes that Laithwaite's conclusions were
vague and not amenable to further small-scale direct experimentation, whereas Drew's
approach using iron-free 3-phase copper electromagnets is intended to be reproducible
at small scale.
