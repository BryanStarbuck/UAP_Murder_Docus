## Why the Three-Phase Motor is the Right Analogy

Source tweet: 1923903179806396615

The three-phase induction motor was rejected initially ("Nobody wanted it at the time!") but now powers virtually all industrial machinery and electric vehicles. The author positions UAP anti-gravity in the same category: an initially rejected technology that represents a genuine advance beyond its predecessor.

Specific physical analogy:
* DC motor with commutator : reaction-mass propulsion :: Three-phase motor : spinning-magnetic levitation

The commutator DC motor requires mechanical contact (brushes), physical wear, and can only reverse force at discrete intervals. Reaction-mass propulsion requires discrete fuel expenditure per increment of velocity change.

The three-phase motor eliminates the commutator by using phase relationships to produce continuous force. The proposed spinning-magnetic levitation eliminates reaction-mass by using phase relationships between counter-spinning magnetic elements to produce continuous lift force.
