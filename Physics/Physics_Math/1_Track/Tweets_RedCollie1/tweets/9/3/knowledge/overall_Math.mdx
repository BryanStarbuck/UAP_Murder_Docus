  Formation 1: count = 40 + 3 = 43; pi represented to 10 significant digits (3.141592654)

Pattern: each additional digit of precision in the pi representation increases the total count element by 4, with the "+3" circles as a constant offset. This constitutes a positional encoding where the number of geometric elements minus the 3-circle offset equals 4 × (number of pi digits).

  n=10: 40 + 3 = 43 ✓

Source tweet: 2039678934200283365 (note_tweet)

* "Energy E = mv² is always conserved" — This is proportional to kinetic energy (the actual expression for rotational kinetic energy is T = (1/2)Iω²; the author uses mv² as a proxy for kinetic energy without the ½ coefficient, consistent with colloquial physics notation in this context).

* "Spin angular momentum mvr is only conserved in the starting condition of v = 0" — Again using the linear proxy for angular momentum L = Iω = mvr (for a point mass m rotating at radius r with tangential velocity v = ωr). The claim is that L conservation is conditional.

The proposed physics problem explicitly in variables:

Case 1:
  Initial state: wheel spinning at v_wheel = +2 (vertical axis), body at v_body = 0
  Action: wheel lowered to horizontal (v_wheel_vertical → 0)
  Result: v_body = -2 (by conservation of total vertical angular momentum = 0)

  L_total = L_wheel + L_body = I_wheel × v_wheel + I_body × v_body = 0
  When v_wheel_vertical → 0: I_body × v_body = 0 → v_body = -2 (momentum transferred)

  [Author states v_body = -2 as result of lowering, which means the body rotates to conserve total = 0]

Case 2:
  Initial state: wheel spinning at v_wheel = -2 (vertical axis, anti-clockwise), body at v_body = 0
  Action: wheel raised to vertical over head (v_wheel_vertical = -2 fully expressed in vertical axis)
  Result: body moves at v_body = +2

  L_total = 0: L_body = -L_wheel = +2

Case 3 (hypothetical, key):
  Initial state: body spinning at v_body = +8 (clockwise), wheel spinning at v_wheel = -2 (anti-clockwise)
  Action: raise anti-clockwise wheel over head

  Standard conservation prediction:
  L_total_before = I_body × 8 + I_wheel × (-2) [in appropriate units]
  L_total_after = I_body × v_body_new + I_wheel × v_wheel_new
  If I_body = I_body and I_wheel = I_wheel (moments of inertia unchanged):

  Author's question: "Will the spin of his body and feet around that vertical axis increase to v = +10, or might some other value be observed?"

  The author notes that E = mv² conservation vs L = mvr conservation may give different predictions:
  * If angular momentum is strictly conserved: L_body_new + L_wheel_new = L_total_initial = I_body×8 + I_wheel×(-2)
  * If energy is separately conserved: T = (1/2)I_body×v_body² + (1/2)I_wheel×v_wheel² = conserved

## Rotating Magnetic Field Vector — Three-Phase Mathematics

Source tweet: 1923903179806396615 and experimental description (1938840881119011324)

Three-phase current system:
  I_U(t) = I₀ cos(ωt)
  I_V(t) = I₀ cos(ωt - 2π/3)
  I_W(t) = I₀ cos(ωt - 4π/3)

For three coils oriented at 0°, 120°, 240° in the XY plane, the composite field vector:
  B_x(t) = B₀ [cos(ωt)·cos(0) + cos(ωt-2π/3)·cos(2π/3) + cos(ωt-4π/3)·cos(4π/3)]
  B_y(t) = B₀ [cos(ωt)·sin(0) + cos(ωt-2π/3)·sin(2π/3) + cos(ωt-4π/3)·sin(4π/3)]

Using the identity: sum of three cosines at 120° offsets = (3/2) cos(ωt - φ):
  B_x(t) = (3/2) B₀ cos(ωt)
  B_y(t) = (3/2) B₀ sin(ωt)

  |B(t)| = (3/2) B₀ = constant (uniform rotation, no amplitude modulation)

This is the key result: three-phase drive produces a field of constant amplitude rotating at angular frequency ω. At the experiment's 1.45 Hz drive: ω = 2π × 1.45 ≈ 9.11 rad/s.

Torque on a magnetic dipole m in this field:
  τ = m × B → |τ| = |m||B|sin(δ) where δ = angular lag between m and B

For the chain-of-magnets in the experiment at 1.45 Hz: the magnets were observed to rotate synchronously → δ was small → they were within the pull-in range of the synchronous torque.

For disc magnets at higher frequency: when ω exceeds ω_pullout (defined by τ_max = |m||B|), synchronism is lost and magnets undergo pole-reversal events ("jumping from north-red to south-blue").
