## Angular Momentum Conservation and Gyroscopic Dynamics

### Spinning-Wheel Physics Problem — Conservation Laws and Their Limits

Source tweet: 2039678934200283365 (full note_tweet)

A detailed physics problem is posed using a man on a rotating platform holding a spinning wheel (gyroscope), demonstrating conservation of spin angular momentum:

Step 1: Wheel spinning on vertical axis with angular velocity v = +2 (clockwise when viewed from above).
* Man lowers the spinning wheel to horizontal orientation (times 0:12 to 0:20 in video).
* Effect: spin angular momentum around the man's vertical axis is reduced to zero (the wheel's angular momentum is now directed horizontally and no longer couples to the vertical axis of rotation).
* Reaction: the man's body and feet rotate anti-clockwise at v = -2. The system satisfies:
  L_total_vertical = L_wheel_vertical + L_body_vertical = constant = 0
  When L_wheel_vertical → 0, then L_body_vertical = -(−2) = ... [the body counter-rotates to compensate].

Step 2: Man raises the wheel back to vertical (over his head), times 0:20 to 0:31.
* Effect: L_wheel_vertical returns to v = +2 (or −2 depending on sign convention).
* Reaction: body rotates clockwise at v = +2.

Author's additional hypothetical case:
* Man is spinning rapidly clockwise at v = +8 before he raises an anti-clockwise spinning wheel at v = -2 over his head.
* Question: does the body spin increase to v = +10, or some other value?

Author's stated energy/angular momentum distinction:
"Energy E = mv² is always conserved, whereas spin angular momentum mvr is only conserved in the starting condition of v = 0 for his body and feet, not moving in either direction."

This is a non-standard claim. Standard classical mechanics states:
* Conservation of angular momentum: dL/dt = τ_external. In the absence of external torques, L = Iω = constant.
* The kinetic energy of rotation: T = (1/2)Iω².
* These two quantities are independently conserved or not, depending on whether external torques or non-conservative forces are present.

The author's claim that angular momentum conservation is conditional on starting velocity v = 0 is non-standard and constitutes a proposed modification or limitation of classical mechanics — explicitly linked to the question of UFO levitation via "spin" and "counter-spin."

### Anti-Precession Concept

Source: video 13_2039678248859422720 (transcription) and tweet context

"Okay, now we're up like this and let it fall through an arc, a process in this direction. Okay, that's normal process. If I raise it up, force it against that, I do what I call an anti-precession this way."

Standard precession: for a gyroscope with angular momentum L = Iω, subject to gravitational torque τ = r × mg (where r is the distance from the pivot to the center of mass), the precession angular velocity is:

  Ω_precession = τ / L = mgr / (Iω)

The precession vector is perpendicular to both τ and L, causing the spin axis to sweep a cone.

Anti-precession: the demonstrator applies a torque in the direction that opposes the natural precession direction, or forces the gyroscope axis upward against the arc of natural fall. This is distinguished from normal precession as:
* Normal precession: torque causes L to rotate about vertical at Ω_p.
* Anti-precession: externally imposed angular displacement in the direction opposing Ω_p, which the author claims produces a different resultant force — potentially with a vertical component that classical mechanics does not predict.

Laithwaite's claim (as referenced): a precessing gyroscope, when lifted, exhibits apparent weight reduction — i.e., the force required to lift it is less than its weight, suggesting that precessing angular momentum interacts with gravity in a way not captured by simple vector addition of the gravitational and centrifugal pseudo-forces.

## Three-Phase Electrical Physics — Historical Development

Source tweet: 1923903179806396615

* Early DC motor: uses a split-ring commutator to reverse magnetism direction every half-cycle of rotation. This is a mechanical switching approach to maintaining unidirectional electromagnetic torque.

* Nikola Tesla's three-phase power invention: three sinusoidal currents, each displaced 120° in phase and delivered to three spatially offset stator coils, produce a continuously rotating magnetic field vector. This eliminated the need for mechanical commutation.

  The three phase currents:
  I_U(t) = I_peak × cos(ωt)
  I_V(t) = I_peak × cos(ωt - 2π/3)
  I_W(t) = I_peak × cos(ωt - 4π/3)

  The resulting field B(t) at the center of an ideal stator:
  B(t) = (3/2) × B_max × [cos(ωt) x̂ + sin(ωt) ŷ]
  which describes uniform circular rotation of the field vector in the XY plane at angular frequency ω.

* Author's extension: "Now dozens of Tesla motors fill every house." The three-phase induction motor is ubiquitous in industrial and residential applications (HVAC fans, pumps, compressors, EV drivetrains). The author uses this as a stepping stone to argue that the next revolutionary step is "anti-gravity" driven by an analogous insight about rotating fields interacting with gravitational or inertial fields.

## Cosmological Physics — Evolving Universe and Redshift Reinterpretation

Source tweet: 2005878120025661624

Reference to Fred Hoyle's steady-state cosmological model: "Fred Hoyle believed that the Universe was slowly EVOLVING over time to states of higher energy."

Physical content:
* In Hoyle's Steady State Model (with Bondi, Gold, 1948), the Universe has no beginning and matter is continuously created to maintain constant density as the Universe expands.
* Hoyle's later evolving variant: the Universe moves to states of higher energy over time.
* Key observational consequence stated by Dr. Drew: "when we see light from distant galaxies, it seems to have lower energy and longer wavelengths than any light emitted 'today', because we are 'looking' into the distant past."

This statement describes cosmological redshift z:
  z = (λ_observed - λ_emitted) / λ_emitted = (a(t_obs) / a(t_emit)) - 1

In the standard ΛCDM model, this redshift is due to the expansion of spacetime (the scale factor a(t) increases). In Hoyle's interpretation, this could alternatively be interpreted as an intrinsic evolution of atomic energy levels or photon energy over cosmic time — i.e., the photon did not lose energy to expansion but was emitted at lower energy in the past because the physical constants or vacuum energy state differed.

This is an alternative to the standard expansion redshift interpretation and is cited here as evidence that there exist non-standard physics frameworks (like Hoyle's) that attribute redshift to evolving vacuum energy states rather than metric expansion — a framework relevant to the broader claim that classified physics has discovered a manipulable vacuum energy.

## 3I/ATLAS Observational Physics

Source tweets: 1975782238576074817, 1989294228556714077, 1992194646303502539, 1996319943001002064

### Anomalous Tail Structure of 3I/ATLAS

3I/ATLAS was photographed on December 1, 2025 by Sebastian Voltmer. Observed features:
* Green anti-tail: flows against the solar wind direction.
* Blue opposite tail: flows with the solar wind.

Physical explanation for the green color stated: "The green color is due to nickel ions."

Standard cometary physics:
* Normal comet tails: plasma/ion tail (Type I) points directly anti-sunward (driven by solar wind pressure on ionized gases, predominantly CO⁺ and H₂O⁺). Dust tail (Type II) follows the orbital path and curves behind. No natural comet exhibits an anti-tail flowing against the solar wind.
* Anti-tails in comets: anomalous structures can appear geometrically when Earth passes through the orbital plane of the comet, causing forward-scattering of sunlight from the dust trail. This creates an apparent "anti-tail" pointing sunward — but this is a projection effect, not a physical flow against the solar wind.
* Claim: "No natural comet in all of modern history has ever looked like this." The dual-tail structure (green anti-tail against solar wind + blue tail with solar wind) is presented as anomalous and inconsistent with known cometary physics.

Nickel ions in comets: Ni⁺ and Fe⁺ have been detected in cometary comae since 2021 (Bromley & Kenyon, Nature 2021; Manfroid et al., Nature 2021). The mechanism for their production at large heliocentric distances (sublimation temperature too low for metallic Ni) is not fully understood — a genuine open question in cometary science. However, green emission from Ni⁺ is an unusual identification.

Note: standard green cometary emission is from C₂ Swan bands (vibronic transitions of dicarbon C₂) or from O(¹D) photodissociation products. Ni⁺ emission in the green would require identification of specific Ni⁺ spectral lines.

Morphological description (Nov 21 image from "Dobsonian Power"):
* Central core: elongated like an OVAL.
* Outer gaseous coma: approximately circular.
* No identifiable ion or dust tail at small-telescope resolution.

The elongated-oval core with circular coma is anomalous for a comet nucleus (typical nuclei are irregular, potato-shaped, <20 km). An oval core visible at the distance of 3I/ATLAS from amateur telescopes implies either an extremely large nucleus or an extended emitting region of anomalous geometry — potentially consistent with an artificial structure.
