## 1. Newton's Third Law Question: Spinning Magnet + Counter-Spinning Flywheel

Source: 2045269852815016297.yaml (note_tweet), 2039838839351792056.yaml

The most direct "beyond Newtonian mechanics" claim in this corpus:

"Firstly, the rise of a spinning magnet up a pole, which then applies its upward force
to the center of a counter-spinning, magnetic flywheel (rather than say to a stationary
'bead'). Could this bypass Newton's 3rd Law?"

Newton's Third Law states: for every action force, there is an equal and opposite
reaction force acting on a different body, along the same line of action. In a closed
mechanical system, internal forces cancel and the total linear momentum of the system
is conserved.

The author's question: if a spinning magnet interacts with a counter-spinning flywheel
(rather than a stationary target), does the counter-rotation geometry create a time-
asymmetric force transfer such that the sum of all internal forces does NOT cancel over
a complete interaction cycle?

This would be a violation of Newton's Third Law for electromagnetic interactions — not
expected from Maxwell's equations (which rigorously conserve linear momentum including
EM field momentum). However, the question is framed as experimental, not theoretical:
the author intends to measure whether the combined system shows net Z-axis impulse.

Related to known "reactionless drive" claims:
  - The EmDrive: microwave radiation in a closed frustum — tested at NASA, UK, Germany;
    results inconclusive, most positive results within measurement noise
  - Woodward's Mach Effect Thruster: based on transient mass fluctuations in accelerating
    capacitors; some experimental claims of small thrust
  - The present experiment differs: it uses spinning permanent magnets + flywheel,
    not radiation pressure or capacitor transients

The experiment is described as:
  "My next experiment, I will study spin plus counter-spin, as relating to Newton's
  Third Law." (2039838839351792056, referenced_tweet)

This is explicitly framed as a test of whether classical mechanics holds in the spinning
magnet counter-spinning flywheel configuration.

Source: 1971039265112121476.yaml (note_tweet)

"It was only 2 kg apparent weight when it fell, but 10 kg now, with total loss of power.

    produces forces on the order of micronewtons to millinewtons per cm^2, not 78.5 N
    for a 10 kg object
  - The mechanism (5-phase exterior AC rotating field + interior spinning mass) is
    proposed as a fundamentally different class of electromagnetic levitation

The author states: "I could not care less WHO made it? Only that it was flying by
'anti-gravity', and now we have it for study."

The term "anti-gravity" implies a mechanism that produces upward force without
reacting against the ground — i.e., without the reaction force that classical
electromagnetic repulsion between the craft and Earth's surface would require.

## 7. 3I/ATLAS Non-Gravitational Trajectory: Violation of Pure Keplerian Orbit

"3I/ATLAS was 4 arcseconds away in Right Ascension, from where it was supposed to be

A pure Keplerian orbit is dictated solely by gravity (Newton's universal gravitation:
F = G*M*m/r^2). Any positional deviation from the predicted Keplerian trajectory is
a "non-gravitational acceleration" (NGA), which is beyond Newtonian gravitational prediction.

For natural comets, NGAs arise from cometary outgassing (Yarkovsky-type non-gravitational
forces), radiation pressure, and relativistic corrections. These are well-modeled by
the Marsden-Sekanina parameterization:
  a_NG = A1 * g(r) * r_hat + A2 * g(r) * theta_hat + A3 * g(r) * n_hat

where g(r) = alpha * (r/r_0)^(-m) * (1 + (r/r_0)^n)^(-k) is the heliocentric distance
function, and A1, A2, A3 are fitted parameters.

For 3I/ATLAS, the author claims the NGA is too large to be explained by outgassing
("should have lost at least a sixth of its mass"), implying either:
  (a) Active artificial propulsion (spacecraft engine) — beyond Newtonian natural forces
  (b) A very unusual natural outgassing event

The sunward jet observed by Avi Loeb's group (avi-loeb.medium.com, referenced in
1979334138294604099) is a directed EM emission or propulsion exhaust feature, consistent
with artificial propulsion.
