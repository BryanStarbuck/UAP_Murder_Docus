## Central Thesis: AC Electromagnetic Induction as UAP Propulsion Mechanism

The author claims that crop circle formations encode engineering schematics for UAP
(Unidentified Aerial Phenomena) propulsion systems. The proposed mechanism is a scaled
extrapolation of the classical "jumping ring" (AC levitation) experiment to macroscopic
flight-scale systems. The specific physics advance claimed is:

Conventional electromagnetic induction (AC solenoid + conductive ring) produces measurable
upward repulsive force via Lenz's law — induced eddy currents in the ring oppose the
changing primary magnetic flux, generating a repulsive magnetomotive force directed along
the solenoid axis (Z-axis). The author proposes UAP craft operate on a dramatically scaled
version of this exact mechanism, replacing the aluminum ring with a conducting disc or
ring-shaped hull, and the solenoid with a high-power resonant AC magnetic system internal
to the craft.

The engineering parameters demonstrated in personal laboratory experiments:
- Primary power source: 40 kV DC (used to drive the compact fusion chamber and the
  Farnsworth-fusor-analog device)
- AC magnetic field configuration applied surrounding a cylindrical gas chamber at 40 kV DC

## Specific Physics Advances Claimed

1. AC Induction Levitation (Jumping Ring Scaled Up)
   - Classical: An aluminum ring over an AC solenoid levitates due to induced eddy currents
     opposing the AC magnetic flux (Lenz's law). The net force is F = (mu_0 / 4*pi) * ...
     (the standard mutual inductance repulsion formula). The author claims this generalizes
     to a superconducting or high-conductivity toroidal hull at megawatt AC power levels.
   - Proposed advancement: Replace single-turn ring with multi-turn conducting structure;
     replace laboratory solenoid with multi-phase (3-phase or 5-phase) AC electromagnet
     system integrated into the craft. Net Z-axis force scales with current^2 and mutual
     inductance gradient.

## Relationship Between Mechanisms

The progression proposed by the author is:
  Classical Lenz's law (jumping ring)
    --> AC induction levitation (3-phase, multi-coil scaling)
      --> Spinning magnet + counter-spinning flywheel (novel Newton's 3rd Law question)
        --> 5-phase AC exterior field driving interior spinning mass (Buga orb model)
          --> Full UAP disc: multi-phase AC coil + spinning magnetic flywheel integrated

All mechanisms share the common feature of rotating or oscillating magnetic fields
(multi-phase AC) interacting with a conductive or magnetic secondary element to produce
net Z-axis force — the proposed UAP propulsion principle.
