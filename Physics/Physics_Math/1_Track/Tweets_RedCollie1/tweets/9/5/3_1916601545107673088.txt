PHYSICS CONTENT:
This is a professional radio astronomy figure demonstrating Faraday rotation measure analysis of AGN jet magnetic fields. The helical magnetic field of relativistic jets is the standard model where:
- Toroidal (azimuthal) field component: generated by the jet's rotation and Lorentz force
- Poloidal (axial) field component: generated by the accretion disk's magnetic field
- The resulting helical (screw-pitch) geometry wraps the field around the jet axis
- Faraday rotation of polarized radio emission reveals the line-of-sight B field component

This is highly relevant to UAP propulsion research as the helical field structure of astrophysical jets is proposed as an analog to UAP propulsion: a rotating magnetic helix that creates directed thrust along the axis.
