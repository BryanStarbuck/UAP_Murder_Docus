From the energy conservation analysis of tweet 2039847044744118505:

Classical Newtonian prediction for reaction force:
  F_reaction = F_action  (equal and opposite, Newton's 3rd)
  Δv_reaction = Δv_action = 2.0 (for v_action = 2 anticlockwise applied to disc)

  Δv_reaction_actual = sqrt(v_0^2 + Δv_0^2) - v_0

Suppression: 0.25 / 2.00 = 1/8

This is not a violation of energy conservation — it is an overlooked consequence of it.

reaction body receives velocity increment equal to the action increment (which assumes

flight that bypasses Newton's 3rd Law of action-reaction."

## 3. Non-Classical Counter-Rotation in Disc/Wire Experiment (Homopolar Motor Anomaly)

The experimental observation in video 13_2010855197346820098 constitutes a testable non-
classical claim:

Setup:
  * A conducting disc in a DC magnetic field (homopolar motor configuration)
  * A wire loop or wire mass also in the circuit
  * DC current applied

Observed: disc rotates in one direction; wires rotate in opposite direction.
Anomaly: the disc's angular momentum exceeds what the wire's mass can account for as a
Newton's 3rd reaction partner.

One possible explanation within classical electrodynamics (NOT requiring new physics):
  * The DC magnetic field source (permanent magnet or electromagnet) is itself a third
    body in the torque balance
  * The reaction to the disc's torque is shared between the wires AND the magnetic field
    source (i.e., the source magnet experiences a counter-torque)
  * If the magnet is fixed (mounted rigidly), its counter-torque is absorbed by the Earth
    (through the mounting), making it appear that the disc's rotation has no visible reaction
    in the wire/disc subsystem

This is the classical Feynman disk paradox resolution: angular momentum conservation is
maintained by including the electromagnetic field momentum in the system. The field carries
angular momentum L_field = ε_0 ∫ (E × B) d^3x, which is not zero for a charged spinning
body in a magnetic field.

The author does not invoke this explanation but instead interprets the anomaly as a genuine
departure from Newton's 3rd Law — evidence for a new physics mechanism operative at the
boundaries of electromagnetic and mechanical interaction.

## 9. Beyond Classical Electrodynamics: Field Carries Torque Without Newton's 3rd

The homopolar motor experiment (tweet 2010857577387868637) and the analysis of torque
distribution in spinning magnetic systems point toward a specific claim about classical
electrodynamics:

Standard claim: in a closed system of charges and currents, total angular momentum
(mechanical + electromagnetic) is conserved. The electromagnetic field itself can carry
angular momentum:
  L_EM = ε_0 ∫ r × (E × B) d^3x

For a static electric field + static magnetic field:
  L_EM = ε_0 ∫ r × (E × B) d^3x ≠ 0  (for non-collinear E and B)

The Feynman disk paradox demonstrates this concretely: a charged ring around a solenoid
gains angular momentum when the solenoid field is turned off, entirely due to the angular
momentum stored in the EM field.

The author's non-classical claim: in the homopolar motor + 3-phase rotating field system,
the distribution of angular momentum between the mechanical components (disc, wires) and
the EM field is not what naive Newton's 3rd Law predicts for direct mechanical interaction.
The EM field acts as an angular momentum reservoir, absorbing and releasing angular momentum
in ways that make mechanical torques appear unbalanced.

This is technically within classical electrodynamics (the full Maxwell + Lorentz theory
accounts for field angular momentum) but is effectively "beyond Newton" in the sense that
Newtonian mechanics without EM field momentum terms fails to predict the observed behavior.
