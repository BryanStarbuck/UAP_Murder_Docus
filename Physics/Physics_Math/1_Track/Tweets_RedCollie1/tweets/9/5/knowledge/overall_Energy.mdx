## 3. Three-Phase Rotating Field as Energy Input to Propulsion System

The 3-phase rotating magnetic field generated by the coil stator is the energy input mechanism
for the UFO propulsion system. Key energy relationships:

Input: 3-phase electrical power P = V × I × cos(φ) × 3  [three-phase power formula]

Coupling to spinning magnet (synchronous motor analog):
  * Torque: τ = (3/2) × (P/2π) × P_poles/2 × L_m × I_s × I_r × sin(δ)
    [simplified synchronous machine torque, δ = load angle between stator and rotor fields]
  * Power transferred to spinning magnet: P_spin = τ × ω_spin
  * Mechanical efficiency of coupling: η_mech = P_spin / P_electrical

For the UFO propulsion claim, the output is not rotational mechanical work in the conventional
sense, but rather translational kinetic energy (upward motion). The efficiency of conversion
from electrical spin energy → upward kinetic energy is the undefined quantity that the
author's experiments aim to measure.

## 6. Claimed Fuel-Free Propulsion (No Propellant Expulsion)

The proposed UFO propulsion explicitly does NOT expel propellant (unlike rockets):

Tweet 2045282927811174545:
"UFOs fly using the upward spin force from a fast-spinning magnet, in a 3-phase helical
magnetic field."

No propellant is mentioned. The energy input is purely electrical (3-phase power). The
proposed output is translational kinetic energy without mass expulsion. If correct, this
would violate the standard rocket equation (Tsiolkovsky):
  Δv = v_exhaust × ln(m_initial / m_final)

which requires mass expulsion for Δv. The author's proposed mechanism instead derives thrust
from the interaction of the spinning magnet's angular momentum with the external rotating
magnetic field — an electromagnetic traction mechanism analogous to a linear induction motor
where the "track" is the Earth's gravitational field or ambient EM environment.

Note: the exact physical mechanism by which angular momentum exchange in a rotating magnetic
system produces net translational momentum (violating or circumventing Newton's 3rd Law for
mass expulsion) is explicitly acknowledged as unresolved: "the underlying physics remains
hidden (for now)."
