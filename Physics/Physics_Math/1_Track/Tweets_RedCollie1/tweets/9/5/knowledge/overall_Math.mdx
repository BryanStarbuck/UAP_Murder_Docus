## 1. Energy-Velocity Relationship for Spinning Bodies (tweet 2039847044744118505)

The author uses the rotational kinetic energy proportionality:
  E ∝ v^2

(shorthand for E = (1/2)mv^2 or E = (1/2)Iω^2, treating m and I as constants in relative
unit comparisons)

### Energy Budget Calculation

Given initial state: body spinning at v = +8 (clockwise, positive convention)
  E_initial = (+8)^2 = 64  [relative units]

Apply counter-spin impulse to upper disc: v_disc = -2 (anticlockwise)
  E_disc = (-2)^2 = 4  [relative units, energy added to system]

Total system energy after disc is spun:
  E_total = E_initial + E_disc = 64 + 4 = 68  [relative units]

New body velocity from energy conservation:
  v_new = sqrt(E_total) = sqrt(68) ≈ 8.246...

Actual velocity increment of body:
  Δv_actual = v_new - v_initial = sqrt(68) - 8 ≈ 0.246 ≈ 0.25

### Comparison to Newton's 3rd Law Prediction

Naive Newton's 3rd Law (momentum transfer): if disc gets v = -2, body gets Δv = +2
  Δv_Newtonian = +2.0

### Suppression Factor

  Suppression = Δv_actual / Δv_Newtonian = 0.25 / 2.00 = 1/8

### Acceleration Reduction
From F = ma (equivalently F = m × dv/dt):
  a ∝ Δv (for same time interval Δt)
  F_actual ≈ (1/8) × F_Newtonian

### Generalized Formula
For a body pre-spinning at velocity v_0 and receiving a counter-spin impulse Δv_0:
  E_initial = v_0^2
  E_added = (Δv_0)^2
  v_new = sqrt(v_0^2 + (Δv_0)^2)
  Δv_actual = sqrt(v_0^2 + (Δv_0)^2) - v_0
  
  For v_0 >> Δv_0 (fast pre-spin regime):
  Δv_actual ≈ (Δv_0)^2 / (2 v_0)  [Taylor expansion: sqrt(v_0^2 + ε) ≈ v_0 + ε/(2v_0)]
  
  Suppression factor ≈ Δv_0 / (2 v_0)  [approaches zero as v_0 → ∞]

The suppression factor thus scales as Δv_0 / (2v_0), becoming negligible at very high
pre-spin velocities relative to the applied impulse. At v_0 = +8, Δv_0 = 2:
  Suppression ≈ 2/(2×8) = 1/8  ✓ (matches the author's calculation)

## 2. Faraday Rotation Measure (radio astronomy, from txt file 3_1916601545107673088.txt)

Rotation of linear polarization angle as a function of wavelength:
  Δθ = RM × λ^2

Rotation Measure definition:
  RM = (e^3 / 2π m_e^2 c^4) × ∫_source^observer n_e(l) B_∥(l) dl

where:
  e = 1.602 × 10^-19 C (electron charge)
  m_e = 9.109 × 10^-31 kg (electron mass)
  c = 2.998 × 10^8 m/s (speed of light)
  n_e = electron density (cm^-3)
  B_∥ = magnetic field component along line of sight (μG, positive = toward observer)
  l = path length (pc)

Numerical coefficient (practical units):
  RM [rad m^-2] = 0.812 × ∫ n_e [cm^-3] B_∥ [μG] dl [pc]

From the AGN jet images:
  RM range: 200 rad/m² to 500 rad/m²
  Sign convention: blue = toward observer (+), red = away from observer (-)

For alternating RM stripes of width w transverse to the jet:
  Helix pitch angle α = arctan(B_φ / B_z)
  Stripe spacing = one half-period of the helix transverse projection

## 3. Homopolar Motor Torque (tweet 2010857577387868637)

Lorentz force on current-carrying element:
  dF = I dl × B

For a radial current element dr at radius r in axial field B_z:
  dF = I dr × B_z ẑ  →  azimuthal force dF_φ = I B_z dr

Torque contribution:
  dτ = r × dF_φ = r I B_z dr

Total torque:
  τ = I B_z ∫_{R_in}^{R_out} r dr = I B_z × (R_out^2 - R_in^2) / 2

Power:
  P = τ × ω

where ω is the angular velocity of the disc.

The author observes that the *actual* counter-torque on the wire loop is less than the
torque driving the disc, suggesting the mathematical identity τ_wire = -τ_disc does not hold
— i.e., the torques do not sum to zero as required by Newton's 3rd Law in the rotational case.

## 5. Three-Phase AC Power (Rotating Field Mathematics)

For three-phase power with phases A, B, C:
  I_A(t) = I_0 cos(ωt)
  I_B(t) = I_0 cos(ωt - 2π/3)
  I_C(t) = I_0 cos(ωt - 4π/3)

where ω = 2π f (angular frequency), f = applied frequency (40 kHz or 50 kHz in experiment).

Resulting rotating magnetic field at center of a symmetric 3-coil stator:
  B_x(t) = B_0 [ cos(ωt) + cos(ωt - 2π/3)cos(0) + cos(ωt - 4π/3)cos(0) ]  ... simplified
  
For an ideal symmetric stator, the resultant field is a uniformly rotating vector:
  B(t) = (3/2) B_0 [cos(ωt) x̂ + sin(ωt) ŷ]

The field magnitude is constant = (3/2)B_0, and the field direction rotates at angular
velocity ω in the XY-plane.

For five-phase power (tweet 1935119499742822703):
  I_k(t) = I_0 cos(ωt - 2πk/5),  k = 0, 1, 2, 3, 4  (5-phase symmetry)
  
The resulting field is:
  |B_resultant| = (5/2) B_0  (larger than 3-phase case of 3/2 B_0)

Five-phase provides smoother torque ripple and higher average torque coupling to the load.

## 7. Cyclotron Radius for Deuterium in Compact Fusion Device

For a charged particle in a magnetic field:
  r_c = mv / (qB)

For deuterium nucleus (D+):
  m_D = 2 × 1.661 × 10^-27 kg = 3.322 × 10^-27 kg
  q = e = 1.602 × 10^-19 C
  v = sqrt(2eV/m) for ion accelerated through voltage V

At V = 50 kV:
  v_D = sqrt(2 × 1.602×10^-19 × 5×10^4 / 3.322×10^-27)
       = sqrt(4.818×10^12) ≈ 2.195 × 10^6 m/s

Cyclotron radius at B = 1 T:
  r_c = (3.322×10^-27 × 2.195×10^6) / (1.602×10^-19 × 1)
       = 7.293×10^-21 / 1.602×10^-19 ≈ 0.0455 m ≈ 4.6 cm

This is consistent with the "50 mm" scale mentioned for the compact fusion device (tweet
1928230874304368975 parent: "hot fusion on a very small scale (50 mm)"). A cyclotron radius
of ~5 cm at B ~ 1 T and V ~ 50 kV places the device in the correct dimensional range.

## 9. Schwarzschild Spacetime Metric (context: 3I/ATLAS GR reference)

From tweet 1950324331990700058 context ("Einstein's General Relativity"):

  ds^2 = -(1 - r_s/r) c^2 dt^2 + (1 - r_s/r)^-1 dr^2 + r^2 dΩ^2

where r_s = 2GM/c^2 (Schwarzschild radius)

For Mars:
  M_Mars = 6.417 × 10^23 kg
  r_s_Mars = 2 × 6.674×10^-11 × 6.417×10^23 / (2.998×10^8)^2 ≈ 9.53 × 10^-4 m ≈ 0.95 mm

The weak-field approximation is entirely valid for Mars (r >> r_s), and the GR corrections
to orbital mechanics are of order r_s/r << 1. The main GR effect relevant to the slingshot
is the Shapiro time delay:
  Δt_Shapiro = (2GM/c^3) × ln[(r_1 + r_2 + d) / (r_1 + r_2 - d)]
  
which is negligible for trajectory timing purposes but is interesting as a signature of
the spacetime curvature through which 3I/ATLAS passes.
