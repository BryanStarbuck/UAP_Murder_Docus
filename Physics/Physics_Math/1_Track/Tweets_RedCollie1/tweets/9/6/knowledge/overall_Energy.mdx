## Primary Energy Anomaly Claim: No Downward Reaction Energy

Source: tweets 1928347152150896786, 1964077861780017653.

The central energy anomaly claim: the spinning magnetic device produces upward mechanical work
(lifting the flywheel and rod assembly) without a corresponding downward energy expenditure
on the mechanical support.

Standard energy balance for lifting a mass m by height h:
  W_gravity = m * g * h  (work against gravity, stored as potential energy)
  W_input = W_gravity + W_losses  (input electrical energy)

Classical prediction: the scale weight should increase by the downward reaction force while the
device is accelerating upward. It does not.

From note_tweet of 1964077861780017653:
"It does this by 'pushing down against space'. However there is no change of weight on a scale,
because the total force of magnet-up and space-down remains constant."

This phrasing "pushing down against space" is the author's claim that the reaction is against
the electromagnetic field / vacuum structure, not against the mechanical support. If true:
* The electrical energy input goes into: (a) kinetic energy of the spinning magnet, (b)
  lifting potential energy of the flywheel, (c) resistive losses in the coils.
* The gravitational potential energy gained by the rising flywheel is supplied by the electrical
  input power, but the reaction force does not appear in the mechanical system -- it appears
  as momentum transferred to the electromagnetic field.

This is not zero-point energy extraction; it is a claim about WHERE the reaction momentum goes,
not about creating energy from nothing. Energy conservation is explicitly maintained by the
author ("total force of magnet-up and space-down remains constant").

## Energy Conservation in Counter-Spin System

Source: tweet 2039849887341396035, quoted tweet 2039847044744118505.

The author poses the energy-conservation question for a counter-spinning two-disc system:

"How will the spin velocities change, if total energies must remain conserved?"

Setup:
* Upper disc pre-spun to v = -2 (anticlockwise), storing E_1 = m*(−2)^2 = 4m (in author's units)
* Lower disc then spun: by Newton's 3rd Law, it would get v = +2, storing E_2 = m*(+2)^2 = 4m
* Total energy: E_total = E_1 + E_2 = 8m

But if the lower disc was ALREADY spinning at v = -1 (anticlockwise, pre-loaded) before the
upper disc acts on it:
* The lower disc's velocity changes from -1 to some final value v_f due to the action of the
  upper disc.
* Energy conservation: E_1_final + E_2_final = E_1_initial + E_2_initial
  m*(v_upper_final)^2 + m*(v_f)^2 = m*(−2)^2 + m*(−1)^2 = 4m + m = 5m

The question is whether angular momentum conservation (L_total = constant) combined with energy
conservation (E_total = constant) allows the LOWER disc to acquire a larger spin than Newton's
3rd Law classical prediction would give, when the lower disc was pre-loaded into counter-spin.

The author's claim is that action can exceed reaction (action > reaction) under these conditions.
This would represent an apparent violation of energy-momentum conservation unless the extra energy
comes from the pre-loaded counter-spin (which would decrease as the "action > reaction" effect
proceeds).

The pre-loaded counter-spin energy IS a source of extractable kinetic energy: the counter-spin
stores kinetic energy E = m*v_counter^2 that can be converted to directed translational motion.
In this interpretation, no new energy is created; the counter-spin kinetic energy is the
energy source for the excess action.

## Galinstan (Liquid Metal) as Low-Loss Electrical Conductor

Source: tweet 1971040932478001503.

Liquid galinstan (Gallium-Indium-Tin eutectic, composition approximately Ga 68.5%, In 21.5%,
Sn 10% by weight) is proposed as the contact medium for spinning the magnet without friction.

Electrical properties of galinstan:
* Electrical conductivity: sigma ~ 3.46 x 10^6 S/m (compared to copper 5.96 x 10^7 S/m,
  approximately 6x lower than copper but still an excellent conductor)
* Melting point: -19°C (liquid at room temperature)
* No vapor pressure at room temperature (unlike mercury), non-toxic relative to mercury
* Surface tension: ~ 534 mN/m (high, tends to bead rather than wet surfaces unless oxide
  layer is removed with HCl)

The contact resistance of a galinstan bath is essentially zero friction and no wear -- the magnet
spins freely inside the liquid metal. The DC current path: power supply → one galinstan pool
(e.g., bottom) → through disc magnet radially → outer edge of magnet → upper galinstan pool
→ return to power supply.

This enables:
1. Continuous DC current through the spinning magnet without brush wear or friction.
2. Very high spin rates (limited only by magnetic forces and hydrodynamic drag of galinstan).
3. Simultaneous three-phase AC spin-up alongside the Lorentz force DC spin.

## Spinning Top / Gyroscopic Energy Storage

Source: tweet 1924058591545454850.

"It works by spinning magnetic levitation, something like for the anti-gravity rise of a
spinning top."

Energy stored in a spinning top / gyroscope:
  E_spin = (1/2) * I * omega^2

For a permanent disc magnet:
  I_disc = (1/2) * m * R^2
  E_spin = (1/4) * m * R^2 * omega^2

At the claimed operating conditions (sufficient to lift 1 kg):
* If m_magnet = 70g = 0.07 kg (estimated for a 70mm diameter, 3mm thick NdFeB magnet, density
  ~7500 kg/m^3: V = pi*(0.035^2 - 0.007^2)*0.003 m^3 = pi*(0.001225 - 0.000049)*0.003
  = pi*0.001176*0.003 = 1.108e-5 m^3, m = 7500*1.108e-5 = 0.083 kg ≈ 83g, matching 70-100g)
* R = 0.035 m
* I_disc = (1/2) * 0.083 * (0.035)^2 = (1/2) * 0.083 * 0.001225 = 5.08e-5 kg*m^2
* For omega such that E_spin is sufficient to generate 1 kg*g = 9.8 N upward force...
  The spin rate required cannot be calculated from energy alone without knowing the force law.
  But for context, at omega = 10,000 RPM = 1047 rad/s:
  E_spin = (1/2) * 5.08e-5 * (1047)^2 = (1/2) * 5.08e-5 * 1.097e6 = 27.9 J

This is a moderate energy storage (comparable to a small capacitor bank), achievable with
three-phase AC drive at laboratory power levels.
