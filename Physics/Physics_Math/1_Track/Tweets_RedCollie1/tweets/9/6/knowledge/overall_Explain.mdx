  central spinning magnet, and do not see any Newtonian downward force."
* This is framed as the discovery of "transitional magnetism" -- a term given in what the author

  require a high angular velocity for whatever is spinning.
* If the claim is validated, it would require a revision to the classical treatment of rotating
  magnetized bodies: specifically, the coupling between spin angular momentum of the magnetic
  domain structure and the external rotating field must produce a translational force not accounted
  for in standard magnetohydrodynamics or Lorentz-force treatments.

  in opposite (counter-rotating) directions.

  counter-spinning flywheel from below, generating a sustained upward force.
* The crop picture diagram then shows BOTH the central blue magnet and the red flywheel moving
  UPWARD together relative to their non-spinning positions -- interpreted as the UFO rising.
* This joint rise is predicted NOT by Newton's 3rd Law of action-reaction, but by the novel
  magnetic interaction between counter-spinning magnetic assemblies.

## Relationship to Astronomical Jet Analogy

* Author compares the helical magnetic field structure driving UFO propulsion to "helical magnetic
  fields like in an astronomical jet."
* Astrophysical jets (from active galactic nuclei, microquasars, etc.) are sustained by
  magnetically dominated plasma threading a rotating accretion disc: the magnetic field winds into
  a helix, and the hoop stress of the helical field collimates and accelerates the jet along the
  rotation axis. The Blandford-Znajek mechanism (for magnetically arrested discs) and the
  Blandford-Payne mechanism (centrifugally launched magnetized wind) both rely on the coupling of
  rotation to magnetic topology.
* The author's analogy implies that the rotating three-phase magnetic field creates a helical
  field topology around the spinning permanent magnet, and this helical topology produces an
  axial (Z-direction) accelerating force on the magnet -- analogous to the axial collimation of
  a jet.
