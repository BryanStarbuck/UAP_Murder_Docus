## Spinning Top Analogy for Anti-Gravity

Source: tweet 1924058591545454850 (note_tweet).

"It works by spinning magnetic levitation, something like for the anti-gravity rise of a
spinning top. Helical magnetic fields like in an astronomical jet."

The spinning top analogy:
* A rapidly spinning gyroscope can precess without falling -- the gravitational torque (mg × r)
  causes precession rather than immediate toppling.
* Precession rate: omega_precession = (m*g*r) / (I*omega_spin)
* As omega_spin → large, omega_precession → 0 (the top becomes increasingly stable and
  "resists" gravity).
* However, a precessing top does NOT levitate -- it merely changes the direction of its angular
  momentum vector. The author's claim goes further: the spinning magnetic system actually rises.

The helical magnetic field comparison to astrophysical jets:
* In an astrophysical jet, the helical field (B_phi + B_z components, where B_phi is the toroidal
  component and B_z is the axial/poloidal component) provides both collimation and acceleration.
* The hoop stress of the wound B_phi field (tension ~B_phi^2 / (mu_0)) accelerates material
  along the jet axis.
* Analogy to the spinning magnet: the three-phase rotating field creates a helical field topology
  around the rapidly spinning magnet (the rotation of the magnet winds up the field lines into
  a helix), and the resulting hoop stress accelerates the magnet along the Z-axis.

## Silicone Bead Coupling: Rotational-Translational Decoupling

Source: tweet 1928347152150896786 (note_tweet).

"Also a 'elastic silicone bead' is needed to capture the upward force from that spinning magnet,
without causing the aluminum rod to counter-rotate by unwanted mechanical friction."

Engineering rationale:
* The silicone bead is compliant (low shear modulus): it can transmit axial (Z) compressive or
  tensile force but cannot efficiently transmit torque.
* The central magnet spins at high angular velocity; if rigidly coupled to the aluminum rod, it
  would impart equal and opposite torque to the rod and support (Newton's 3rd Law in rotation),
  which would add a confounding downward force through the support.
* With the silicone bead, the rotational momentum of the magnet is decoupled from the rod; only
  the vertical (Z) force component is transmitted upward into the rod.
* The rod rising without spinning (confirmed in the note_tweet: "the aluminum rod does NOT spin
  after it rises") confirms the decoupling is working.

## Polarity Independence of Lift Direction

Source: tweet 1928347152150896786 (note_tweet).

"We can see only an upward force, in both N or S orientations of the central spinning magnet, and
do not see any Newtonian downward force."

From note_tweet: "Those can be easily assessed by flipping the central magnet about its N-S poles,
and making sure that it behaves the same in both orientations."

Significance:
* In classical magnetostatics, the force F_z = mu_z * (dB_z/dz) on a dipole in a field gradient
  changes sign when mu_z changes sign (flipping N/S).
* If the observed lift is the same in both orientations, it CANNOT be explained by a simple
  field gradient force.
* A polarity-independent upward force implies the mechanism depends on the MAGNITUDE of the
  magnetic moment (|mu|) or on the spin angular velocity, not on the sign of the dipole.
* This would be consistent with a force law of the form: F_z = f(|mu|, omega, B_rotating)
  where f is even in mu_z (invariant under mu → -mu).

## Magnetic Flywheel Stabilization of the Rising Magnet

From the crop picture interpretation (note_tweet 2045383245861392487):

"If that (blue) magnet happens to rise above the flat plane of that (red) magnetic flywheel,
simple attraction (between red and blue) will pull it back down again."

Mathematical form of this restoring force:
  F_z(z) = -k * z  (approximately, for small displacements z from the equilibrium position)

where k is the effective magnetic spring constant:
  k = d^2 U / dz^2 |_{z=0}

This is a magnetic harmonic oscillator in the Z-direction. The natural frequency of oscillation:
  omega_n = sqrt(k / m_magnet)

For stable levitation, the three-phase driving force must overcome the weight (m*g) while the
flywheel ring provides the vertical restoring force to keep the levitated magnet centered.

This is analogous to the Earnshaw theorem problem: Earnshaw's theorem states that static magnetic
fields cannot stably levitate a permanent magnet (any equilibrium is unstable in at least one
direction). The flywheel ring + active three-phase drive is the active stabilization that
circumvents Earnshaw's theorem -- the active spin-up provides the non-equilibrium condition
necessary for stable levitation.
