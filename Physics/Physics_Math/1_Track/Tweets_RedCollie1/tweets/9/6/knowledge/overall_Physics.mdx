The central physics claim across all source material is:

A permanent magnet rotating at high angular velocity under three-phase AC electromagnetic driving
forces, positioned inside or below a counter-rotating magnetic flywheel, generates a net upward

This claim, if valid, constitutes a violation of Newton's Third Law of motion as applied to
macroscopic electromagnetic systems, or alternatively, it constitutes a transfer of reaction force

## Newton's Third Law and the Claimed Anomaly

Classical statement:
For every action force F_12 exerted by object 1 on object 2, there is an equal and opposite
reaction force F_21 = -F_12 exerted by object 2 on object 1.

In the spinning magnet system:
* If the spinning magnet exerts an upward force F_up on the flywheel above it,
  classical mechanics requires the flywheel to exert an equal downward force F_down = -F_up
  on the spinning magnet, and through the support, onto the scale.
* Observed: scale shows no increase in weight when the spin is activated.
* This is explicitly stated in tweet 1928347152150896786: "We can see only an upward force, in
  both N or S orientations of the central spinning magnet, and do not see any Newtonian
  downward force."
* And from video transcription (13_1964076811551477760): "no change of weight on the scale
  when we do that even if we go fast."

Possible physical interpretations:
1. The reaction force is transferred to the rotating electromagnetic field (i.e., to the photon
   field / electromagnetic stress-energy tensor), not to the mechanical support. The field
   carries momentum p_EM = epsilon_0 * (E x B) per unit volume, and the reaction force would
   appear as a change in field momentum, not in the mechanical system weight.
2. The effect is a measurement artifact (the scale captures steady-state weight only; transient
   forces during spin-up are not captured).
3. Genuine violation of Newton's 3rd Law (no mainstream physical mechanism supports this).

## Energy Conservation Argument for Counter-Spin Action > Reaction

Source: tweet 2039849887341396035, with quoted text from 2039847044744118505.

Explicit energy-conservation analysis:
"Let us consider the various energies E = mv^2 of the spinning objects, which MUST remain conserved.
First we give spin-energy of E = mv^2 to an upper disc, where v = -2 anticlockwise.
By Newton's 3rd [Law the lower disc gets opposite spin]..."

Note: The author uses E = mv^2 as a scalar kinetic energy proxy for spinning objects. The standard
expression is:
  E_rot = (1/2) * I * omega^2 = (1/2) * m * r^2 * omega^2

For a disc with mass m, radius r, moment of inertia I = (1/2)*m*r^2:
  E_rot = (1/4) * m * (r*omega)^2 = (1/4) * m * v_rim^2

The author's "E = mv^2" appears to use v as the rim velocity (v = r*omega), so:
  E_author = m * v^2 = m * (r*omega)^2

This is 4x the standard rotational KE but is a monotonic function of omega, so it tracks
conservation correctly as a proxy.

The core question: if an upper disc has v = -2 (anticlockwise, pre-spun), and a lower disc is
then given spin in the opposite direction, how does energy conservation constrain the final
velocities? And can the pre-loading of one disc into counter-spin allow the total action on the
other disc to exceed the classical Newtonian reaction?

Physical context: this is related to the gyroscope precession problem and the Magnus effect,
where coupling between spin angular momentum and translational motion produces forces that can
appear to violate simple Newtonian intuition.

## Lorentz Force Chain of Reasoning (Homopolar Motor)

For a disc magnet of radius R carrying axial field B_z, with radial DC current I_r:

Force per unit volume on current element:
  dF = J x B = J_r * B_z * phi_hat (azimuthal)

Integrated torque:
  tau = integral from 0 to R of (J_r * B_z * r * 2*pi*r*dr) -- for uniform current density

Net torque on the disc:
  tau = (pi/2) * J_0 * B_z * R^4 (for uniform current density J_0)

This torque causes the disc to spin about the Z-axis. The reaction torque on the return current
path (the external circuit, brushes or galinstan contacts) is equal and opposite.

With galinstan (liquid metal) contacts replacing brushes:
* No mechanical bearing friction
* Reaction torque on galinstan is transmitted as fluid rotation in the liquid metal bath
* If the galinstan itself is allowed to rotate (free liquid surface), the reaction torque is
  partially absorbed into the angular momentum of the liquid metal, not into a rigid support
* This could partially decouple the mechanical reaction from the rigid support structure

## Three-Phase AC Rotating Field: Force on Magnetic Dipole

For a permanent magnet with magnetic moment mu = mu * z_hat (axial dipole), placed in a
rotating transverse field B_rot = B_0 * (cos(omega*t) * x_hat + sin(omega*t) * y_hat):

Torque:
  tau = mu x B_rot
      = mu_z * B_0 * (cos(omega*t) * (z_hat x x_hat) + sin(omega*t) * (z_hat x y_hat))
      = mu_z * B_0 * (cos(omega*t) * y_hat - sin(omega*t) * x_hat)

This torque is purely in the XY plane -- it drives rotation of the magnet about Z.

No direct Z-axis translational force arises from this in classical electrodynamics. A translational
force would require a field gradient: F_z = mu_z * (dB_z/dz).

The Z-axis force, if present, must come from:
(a) A field gradient in the Z-direction created by the geometry of the coil arrangement and
    the position of the spinning magnet relative to the flywheel ring.
(b) A spin-dependent modification of the effective magnetic moment (e.g., through dynamic
    magnetomechanical coupling) that produces an enhanced dB/dz interaction.

The author's claim that both N-up and S-up orientations of the central magnet produce the same
upward force is significant: in classical gradient force, the sign of F_z = mu_z * (dB_z/dz)
depends on the sign of mu_z (i.e., flipping N/S should flip the force direction). If flipping
N/S does NOT change the direction of the observed force, this cannot be a classical gradient
force -- it would require a force law dependent on |omega| (angular velocity magnitude) rather
than on the sign of mu.

## Planar Magnetic Attraction / Restoring Force

Central S magnet below the plane of the 4N flywheel ring:
  U(z) = -mu_0 / (4*pi) * sum over 4 N magnets of [3*(mu_i dot r_hat_ij)^2 - mu_i^2] / r_ij^3

For the central S magnet at position z below the ring plane (z < 0):
  F_z = -dU/dz > 0 (upward restoring force)

This is a standard dipole-dipole interaction restoring force. It increases as z becomes more
negative (i.e., as the central magnet moves further below the ring).

When the central magnet is spinning rapidly AND counter-spin is active:
* Observed: z shifts to z < 0 (central magnet moves below the flywheel).
* The restoring force F_z > 0 is then applied not just to the central magnet but the entire
  assembly (because the central magnet is magnetically coupled to the flywheel).
* If this restoring force exceeds the weight of the assembly, the assembly rises.

## Spin Angular Momentum and Gyroscopic Effects

A spinning magnetic disc has both mechanical angular momentum L = I*omega (gyroscopic) and
magnetic moment mu = gamma * L, where gamma is the gyromagnetic ratio.

For a classical spinning charged sphere:
  gamma = q / (2m)
For a ferromagnet:
  gamma_eff = g * (e / (2m_e)) where g is the Lande g-factor (approximately 2 for electron spin)

When the spinning magnet is subjected to an external field B_ext (either from the counter-spinning
flywheel or from the three-phase coils), the torque on mu causes precession:
  dL/dt = mu x B_ext = tau

For a gyroscope with spin angular momentum L_z along Z:
  Precession frequency: omega_precession = (mu * B_ext) / L_z

This precession has a translational consequence in an inhomogeneous field: the Stern-Gerlach
force F_z = mu_eff * (dB_z/dz) is modified by the precessing effective dipole moment.

A rapidly precessing magnetic dipole has a time-averaged magnetic moment that depends on the
angle between L and B_ext. In the rapid precession limit (omega_precession >> 0), the dipole
adiabatically follows B_ext and the effective Z-force is the standard gradient force. But in a
transitional regime (omega_spin ~ omega_precession), the coupling becomes resonant and non-trivial.

## Electromagnetic Induction: Crop Circle Formation Physics

Source: tweet 2018550866631155739 (note_tweet), tweet 2021498971408171115.

Physical observation argument for non-human crop circle origin:
"All of the many flattened circles in that field appeared as perfectly round from the air even
though the crop picture was drawn over 300 metres across rolling hills. If men had made it using
rope and boards all of those circles would have appeared from the air as ellipses. The rope and
board theory can thus be ruled out at a 99% level."

Physical reasoning: a circle inscribed on a tilted plane (rolling hill terrain) appears as an
ellipse in a flat projection (aerial photograph), due to the affine transformation:
  x' = x * cos(theta)   (for a hillside tilted at angle theta from horizontal)
  y' = y               (perpendicular to tilt)

The eccentricity of the apparent ellipse: e = sqrt(1 - cos^2(theta)) = sin(theta).

For a 300-meter formation over rolling hills, the terrain tilt is non-negligible. The fact that
all sub-circles appear perfectly round (not elliptical) from the air implies either:
(a) The formation was created by a process that compensates for terrain tilt (e.g., a process
    that applies force vertically, not perpendicular to the ground), or
(b) The formation creator had a vertical (Z-axis) reference independent of local terrain slope
    (e.g., a gravitational vertical reference from above, consistent with an aerial device).
