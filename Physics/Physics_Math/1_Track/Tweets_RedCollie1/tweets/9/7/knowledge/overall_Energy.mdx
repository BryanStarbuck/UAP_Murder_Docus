### Gravitational Potential Energy Conversion to Kinetic Energy

From YAML 1964079462410326120 (note_tweet):
> "Thus gravitational potential energy, created by 3-phase spin, gets converted to downward kinetic energy."

This describes an energy conversion process within the classical framework:
- Three-phase spin energy drives the magnet upward (increasing gravitational potential energy U = mgh).
- When the magnet enters free-fall phase, U converts back to kinetic energy KE = mv²/2.
- No net energy is created; energy is cyclically exchanged between electrical input, kinetic rotational energy, gravitational potential energy, and kinetic translational energy.

The energy balance: electrical energy input → rotational kinetic energy of magnet → gyroscopic precession energy → axial kinetic energy (upward) and gravitational potential energy → downward kinetic energy during free-fall phase → (repeat).

No claim is made that this cycle produces more energy than is input. The 20 g weight loss observed on the scale is explicitly attributed to classical free-fall during half the cycle, not to energy generation.

### Tesla Unipolar Motor as Energy Reference

From YAML 1984788602585563171 (note_tweet):
> "Alien tech / yin-yang / Tesla unipolar motor / liquid metal."

The Tesla unipolar (homopolar) motor converts DC electrical energy to rotational kinetic energy via the Lorentz force F = J × B acting on current-carrying liquid metal in a magnetic field. Energy conservation is maintained: electrical power input P = I · V equals mechanical power output P_mech = τ · ω (minus resistive losses in the liquid metal).

The galinstan (liquid metal) configuration adds: resistive heating losses (P_Joule = I² · R_galinstan) as a significant energy cost. Galinstan has higher resistivity than solid copper, making the liquid-metal motor less efficient than solid-conductor alternatives.

No claim is made that the galinstan / 3-phase / DC configuration produces energy from an external or exotic source.

### "Vacuum Viscosity" Energy Mechanism

From YAML 1953767622644052357 (note_tweet):
> "Video 1 shows how a spinning disc can make the vacuum around it become 'viscous' like water."

The concept of vacuum "viscosity" in this context is non-technical and metaphorical. In quantum field theory, the quantum vacuum has measurable effects (Casimir force, Lamb shift, spontaneous emission) but is not literally viscous — it does not exert a drag force on a spinning macroscopic object in the manner of a viscous fluid.

The Casimir-Polder effect describes a small attractive force between neutral objects due to vacuum fluctuations, but this force is extremely weak at macroscopic scales (~10⁻¹⁵ N for macroscopic gaps) and is not directional in a manner that would produce lift.

There is no conventional physics mechanism by which a spinning disc extracts energy from the quantum vacuum to produce levitation. The "vacuum viscosity" language is the researcher's informal description of the gyroscopic precession behavior (the spinning disc "resists" being moved, giving the impression of a viscous medium).

No energy from zero-point vacuum is claimed in the experimental results or in the source descriptions. The lift experiments all use conventional electrical energy input.

### Semiconductor Technology as Reverse-Engineered UAP Energy Technology

From YAML 1992362812589621539 (note_tweet):
> "We 'discovered' how to make silicon semiconductors by studying fragments of the Roswell UFO crash. Transistors, diodes, MOSFETs, solar cells. Bell Labs patented the transistor just 6 months after that UFO went down, and the military collected crash fragments."

This is a claim that semiconductor technology (silicon transistors, diodes, MOSFETs) was reverse-engineered from recovered extraterrestrial hardware after the Roswell 1947 crash. If true, this would imply that UAP technology uses semiconductor electronics at a level significantly more advanced than 1947 human technology.

Material evidence cited:
> "The results showed 99% silicon, 1% germanium with isotopes much heavier than from our solar system."
> "He sent it to a materials scientist at UCSD for EXAFS testing."

EXAFS (Extended X-ray Absorption Fine Structure) is a legitimate spectroscopic technique for determining local atomic structure and isotope composition. The claimed finding: silicon with anomalously heavy isotopes (implying nucleosynthesis in a stellar environment different from our solar system) and a specific 99:1 Si:Ge ratio.

Standard silicon isotopes: ²⁸Si (92.2%), ²⁹Si (4.7%), ³⁰Si (3.1%). Isotopes heavier than ³⁰Si are unstable (³¹Si half-life 157 min). If the EXAFS measurement found stable silicon isotopes heavier than ³⁰Si, this would be physically anomalous and physically impossible under standard nuclear physics. The claim therefore implies either measurement error, contamination, or unknown nuclear physics.

This is presented as evidence of an energy/materials technology source beyond current human capability but is not directly connected to the electromagnetic levitation experiments in the source files.

### 3I/ATLAS Propulsion: Ion Thrusters (Six Jets)

From YAML 1996717438818570676 (note_tweet):
> "It is clearly the 'same comet/whatever' with six jets coming out behind, like six ion thrusters."

Six ion thruster jets are proposed as the propulsion mechanism for the interstellar object 3I/ATLAS. Ion thrusters are a well-understood technology:

    F_thrust = ṁ_exhaust · v_exhaust

where ṁ_exhaust is the mass flow rate of the ion beam and v_exhaust is the exhaust velocity. Ion thrusters achieve very high specific impulse (I_sp = v_exhaust / g ≈ 1,500–10,000 s for xenon ion drives) but very low thrust (typically millinewtons). For interstellar travel, very high exhaust velocity (approaching c) would be needed, requiring exotic energy sources (antimatter, nuclear fusion) per the Tsiolkovsky rocket equation:

    Δv = v_exhaust · ln(m_initial / m_final)

This is a speculative energy claim (the power source for six interstellar ion thrusters is not specified) but is categorized here as an energy observation rather than a physics mechanism claim.
