### Semiconductor Reverse Engineering (Historical)

From YAML 1992362812589621539:
Timeline:
- July 1947: Roswell crash.
- December 1947: Bell Labs patent application for the transistor (John Bardeen, Walter Brattain, William Shockley).
- Gap: approximately 6 months between crash and patent.

Claim: semiconductor transistor technology was reverse-engineered from Roswell crash fragments within 6 months, with "practically no research-trail on the subject beforehand."

Historical context: Bell Labs semiconductor research began in 1945 with a solid-state electronics group. The transistor effect was observed December 16, 1947. The patent application was filed June 1948 — approximately 18 months after the July 1947 crash, not 6 months. The discrepancy is not noted in the source material. Furthermore, Bell Labs' solid-state physics research in the 1940s built on decades of quantum mechanical theory (Bloch 1928, Wilson 1931, Shockley's own 1939 work on surface states) — there was an extensive research trail. The claim of no prior research trail is historically inaccurate.
