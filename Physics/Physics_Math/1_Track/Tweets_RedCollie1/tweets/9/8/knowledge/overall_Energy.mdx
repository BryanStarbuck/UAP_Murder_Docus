## Counter-Rotating Disc as Energy Generator

Source: video 13_2039902664704831488_video.mdx

Primary energy generation claim from the Vellone UAP contact account:
"According to Vellone, the separate halves of UFOs might rotate to create energy that
can be harnessed to power the spaceship."

The proposed mechanism:
* Two halves of a disc-shaped UAP counter-rotate relative to each other
* Permanent magnets arranged on the outer edge (rim) of the disc
* The relative counter-rotation of the magnet-bearing halves generates electromagnetic
  energy (EMF induction between the rotating magnetic poles of each half)
* This energy is then harvested and used to power the drive system

This is functionally a rotating-magnet alternator with the stator replaced by the second
counter-rotating half. The power output scales as:
P = (N * Φ_peak * ω_rel)^2 / R_load

Where:
* N = number of magnets per half
* Φ_peak = peak flux per magnet
* ω_rel = relative angular velocity between the two counter-rotating halves
* R_load = effective electrical resistance of the power extraction circuit

The energy chain: mechanical rotation energy → electromagnetic induction → electrical
power → drive current for stator coils → additional spin (closed-loop drive)

Claim: this constitutes either (a) an energy-positive loop if spin creates excess lift
energy beyond the input, or (b) a highly efficient energy conversion from an input
rotational source (initially external, then self-sustaining) to propulsion.
