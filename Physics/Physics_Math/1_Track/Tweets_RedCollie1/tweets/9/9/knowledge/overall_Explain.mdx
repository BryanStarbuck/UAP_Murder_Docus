BRIDGING: WHAT PROPULSION/ENERGY BENEFIT IS PROPOSED AND WHAT PHYSICS ADVANCES ENABLE IT
============================

The overarching claim across all source material is that UFO/UAP flight is achievable by human technology if specific electromagnetic and rotational physics are correctly understood and combined. Dr. Drew argues that the path from conventional terrestrial electromagnetics to actual levitation requires three sequential physical advances beyond classical Newtonian mechanics:

ADVANCE 1: ROTATING MAGNETIC FIELD GENERATION (3-PHASE AC)
The first enabling advance is the generation of a strong, rapidly rotating magnetic field using three-phase AC wire coils arranged in a ring topology. This is standard electrical engineering (induction motor physics), but the application here is as a primary motive field for a levitating object rather than a stator in a rotary machine. Six wire coils arranged in series as U-V-W-U-V-W create a continuously rotating magnetic field. The field rotates at the AC supply frequency (or its harmonic). A test magnet placed within this field will counter-spin at six discrete angular positions just outside the coil ring, while remaining stationary at the exact center where fields cancel.

ADVANCE 2: HOMOPOLAR MOTOR OPERATION OF THE CENTRAL DISC MAGNET (LORENTZ FORCE SPIN-UP)
The second advance is making a disc-shaped permanent magnet spin as a homopolar motor using liquid-metal electrical contacts (brushes). In a homopolar motor, a radial current flowing outward through a conducting disc in the presence of an axial magnetic field experiences a tangential Lorentz force F = qv x B. This induces continuous rotation of the disc in one direction. The disc magnet then becomes a spinning magnet, not a stationary one.

The proposed configuration:
* Disc magnet: permanent magnet disk with axial (Z-axis) magnetization
* Liquid-metal brushes: galinstan (Ga-In-Sn eutectic alloy, non-toxic substitute for mercury) making electrical contact at inner radius R1 and outer rim R2
* Current I_in enters at center, flows radially outward, I_out exits at outer radius
* Axial B field of the disc magnet provides the Lorentz force: F = IL x B, resulting in tangential torque N = I * (R2 - R1) * B * 2*pi*(mean radius)
* Disc spins continuously without commutation, since the homopolar topology produces DC torque directly

ADVANCE 3: VIOLATION OF NEWTON'S THIRD LAW FOR THE SPINNING DISC CASE (THE CRITICAL CLAIM)
This is the central non-classical claim: when the disc magnet is already spinning as a homopolar motor, and the external 3-phase rotating magnetic field is applied in the opposite rotational direction, the standard cancellation mechanism predicted by Newton's Third Law is disrupted.

Standard case (stationary disc magnet): An upward levitation force from the 3-phase field is exactly cancelled by an equal downward reaction force via Newton's Third Law. Net weight change = 0.

Proposed non-classical case (rapidly spinning disc magnet): The downward reaction force from Newton's Third Law, which would normally cancel the upward levitation force, is greatly reduced or eliminated because it is applied laterally (at 90 degrees to the spin axis) by the Lorentz force rather than axially. The 90-degree Lorentz force acting on the spinning disc converts what would have been a downward force into a sideways tangential torque, which simply increases or decreases the spin rate rather than pushing the disc down. Result: net upward levitation force is not cancelled, and the disc rises.

Experimental evidence cited: Video (13_1989465309305274368_video.mdx) shows a demonstration where current is added to a circuit, wires rotate one direction and a disc rotates the other direction. The mass of the wires is insufficient to produce the observed counter-rotation of the disc. Conclusion stated: "The torques are not equal and opposite. Newton's Third Law has been violated [for a spinning homopolar motor subject to an external rotating field]."

ADVANCE 4: THREE-DIMENSIONAL SPHERICAL EXTENSION OF THE DEVICE
For a continuously rising disc, electrical contact must be maintained as the device moves axially. Dr. Drew claims the entire device must be enclosed in a spherical shell so that liquid-metal brushes maintain contact with the spinning disc at all altitudes. Reference: "Buga orb-sphere" sighting — a luminous sphere observed in Buga, Colombia, argued to show the three-dimensional geometry required for a practical device.
