PERMANENT DISC MAGNET POLARITY CONSTRAINT
Source: tweet 1984812062972002480

* For liquid-metal (galinstan) to spin in the same rotational direction (clockwise or anti-clockwise), both magnets in a dual-magnet configuration must be oriented N/S up (rather than S/N up)
