CLAIM 2: THE LORENTZ FORCE GEOMETRY
Source: tweets 1989473708160029037, 1979664027740688527

F_Lorentz = q(v x B) = I(L x B)

In the homopolar disc:
* v = radial velocity of current carriers (outward from R1 to R2)
* B = axial magnetic field (Z-direction, from the permanent magnet of the disc)
* v x B = tangential direction (phi in cylindrical coordinates)
* F = tangential = produces azimuthal torque on the disc = spin

When the external 3-phase field applies an upward force on the spinning disc:
* Newton's Third Law predicts an equal downward force on the field source (the coil ring)
* But if the disc is spinning, the downward force on the disc is applied via the Lorentz mechanism
* The Lorentz force on a spinning body acts 90 degrees to both the velocity vector and the field
* This 90-degree rotation of the reaction force converts a downward force into a tangential (azimuthal) torque
* The tangential torque does not push the disc down; it changes the disc's spin rate
* Result: the upward levitation force is partially or fully uncompensated by a downward reaction

This chain of reasoning is the central non-standard physical claim. It is argued as an "exception" to Newton's Third Law for the specific case of a fast-spinning homopolar motor subjected to an external rotating field.

* A simplified model where E represents twice the classical kinetic energy, or

Via Newton's Third Law, it imparts E = 4 to the reaction system in the opposite spin direction.
The reaction system receives E = 4 in a +2 spin direction.
For a rapidly spinning receiving object (already at v = +2), the reaction is qualitatively different from that on a stationary receiving object (v = 0).

This is the core of the claimed Third Law exception for spinning systems.
