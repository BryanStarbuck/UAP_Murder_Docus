**Energy accounting for the propulsion claim:**

If the 9.81 N (1 kg) upward force were to accelerate the device upward at velocity v, the output power would be:

```
P_output = F · v = 9.81 N × v
```

For unity energy balance: v = P_in / F = 240 W / 9.81 N = 24.5 m/s

At any velocity below 24.5 m/s (which is all physically relevant cases for a lab-scale device starting from rest), the energy balance is subunity. No over-unity claim is made or needed.

No energy conservation violation is required. The electrical energy input from the 3-phase power supply funds the gravitational potential energy gain. The proposed anomaly is that the **momentum** of the rising craft is not balanced by any equal and opposite momentum in a physical reaction mass — i.e., the conservation of *momentum* is what is claimed to be violated (by coupling to the EM field or vacuum), not conservation of energy.

## Part B — Where Would the "Extra" Momentum Come From?

Conservation of momentum: Σ p = const requires that if the craft gains upward momentum p_craft, some other part of the system gains equal and opposite downward momentum. Three proposed sources:

### B.1 Electromagnetic Field as Momentum Reservoir

The electromagnetic field carries momentum density:

```
g_EM = ε₀ (E × B)    [kg·m⁻²·s⁻¹]
```

Total field momentum:

```
p_EM = ε₀ ∫ (E × B) dV
```

If the rotating counter-spin device continuously transfers downward momentum into the EM field (increasing p_EM downward), the craft can gain upward momentum without any material reaction mass. This is physically possible within Maxwell's equations and does not violate any conservation law.

The question is whether the field momentum flux is large enough to account for the observed forces. For laboratory field strengths (B ~ 0.1 T, E ~ 10⁴ V/m):

```
|g_EM| ~ ε₀ |E| |B| ~ (8.85×10⁻¹² F/m)(10⁴ V/m)(0.1 T) ~ 10⁻⁸ kg·m⁻²·s⁻¹
```

For a device volume of ~10⁻³ m³: p_EM ~ 10⁻¹¹ kg·m·s⁻¹. To sustain F = 9.81 N upward over time Δt = 1 s would require Δp_EM = 9.81 kg·m·s⁻¹ — nine orders of magnitude larger than available EM field momentum at laboratory field strengths. The field momentum mechanism therefore cannot explain the observed forces at laboratory field levels without a resonant enhancement of ~10⁹.

### B.2 Gravitational Field as Momentum Reservoir

Drew's most speculative claim: the counter-rotating configuration couples to the gravitational field (spacetime metric) such that the reaction force distributes into the gravitational field. The relevant GR formalism is the gravitational stress-energy pseudo-tensor T^μν_grav, which carries gravitational field energy and momentum. In this scenario:

```
d(p_mech + p_EM)/dt = F_gravity_from_field ≠ 0
```

The gravitational field supplies the "reaction" momentum. This is equivalent to claiming the craft warps local spacetime to produce a net force — the Alcubierre mechanism. As Drew notes, the Alcubierre metric requires exotic matter with negative energy density:

```
ρ = T_{μν} u^μ u^ν = −(c²/32πG) v_s² (df/dr_s)² [y²+z²]/r_s² < 0
```

No mechanism for generating negative energy density using conventional electromagnetic fields has been demonstrated.

### B.3 Quantum Vacuum as Momentum Reservoir

Drew's explicit claim:

> "They fly by 'pushing against the vacuum'... Its upward force comes from the rise of a spinning magnet under 3-phase magnetism, as it pushes against the vacuum (like a spinning gyroscope)."

The quantum vacuum carries zero-point field energy:

```
ρ_vac = (ħ/2) ∫₀^{k_max} ω(k) 4πk² dk / (2π)³ ~ 10¹¹³ J/m³ (with Planck cutoff)
```

The Casimir effect confirms that macroscopic geometry can modify local vacuum energy density and produce measurable mechanical forces:

```
F_Casimir = −(π² ħ c A) / (240 d⁴)
```

For plate separation d = 10 nm: F/A ≈ 1.3 × 10⁻³ N/m² (measurable). At d = 1 cm: negligible.

For a Dynamic Casimir Effect (DCE) from a spinning electromagnetic boundary:

```
P_DCE ∝ ħ Ω⁵ r² / c³
```

At laboratory spin rates (Ω ~ 10³ rad/s), DCE effects are approximately 10⁻³⁰ W — far below practical significance. Drew's implicit claim: the resonant rotating magnetic field topology amplifies the effective coupling to the quantum vacuum far beyond the DCE formula. No mathematical framework for this amplification is provided.

**Energy conservation with vacuum as reservoir:** If the vacuum genuinely provides momentum to the craft, the vacuum itself must decrease in momentum. This does not violate energy conservation — it is equivalent to extracting momentum from the zero-point field. However, this would constitute permanently lowering the local vacuum energy state, which has profound implications for cosmology and vacuum stability that are not addressed in Drew's framework.

## Part F — Verdict

**For the propulsion device:** Drew does **not** claim energy creation. The electrical energy input is acknowledged as the energy source. Conservation of energy holds in his framework. The claimed anomaly is momentum conservation (Newton's Third Law bypass), not energy conservation. The claimed mechanism for momentum transfer — electromagnetic field momentum or quantum vacuum coupling — is speculative, and at laboratory field strengths the available EM field momentum is ~9 orders of magnitude below what would be needed to account for the observed forces without resonant amplification.

**For the compact nuclear fusion device:** This is a legitimate energy source claim (nuclear binding energy extraction), not an over-unity claim. Partial experimental confirmation of the head-on collision mechanism (argon plasma) is cited. No fusion yield data has been published. If demonstrated, this would constitute a new compact energy generation technology, not a violation of conservation of energy.

**For the self-excited homopolar dynamo:** The conventional analysis (without vacuum coupling) is not over-unity. With vacuum coupling, the question is open and speculative. No experimental data conclusively showing excess energy output is available.
