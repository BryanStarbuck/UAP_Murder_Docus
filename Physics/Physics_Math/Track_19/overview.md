## Overview

While not a patent, this declassified CIA document from 1984 is the most theoretically significant item in this archive for understanding the physics underlying claimed Soviet scalar weapons programs. The document's opening section explains the Bohm-Aharonov (BA) effect: Aharonov and Bohm demonstrated in their 1959 paper that quantum mechanical charged particles are affected by electromagnetic potentials even in regions where the fields themselves are zero. This overturns the classical assumption that only E and B fields (derived from potentials A and φ) have physical consequences — the potentials themselves are primary physical entities.

## The Bohm-Aharonov Effect: Physics Foundation

In standard quantum electrodynamics, the electromagnetic potentials A (vector potential) and φ (scalar potential) are related to the physical fields by:

B = ∇ × A
E = −∇φ − ∂A/∂t

In classical electrodynamics, only E and B are considered physically observable; A and φ are treated as mathematical conveniences with gauge freedom — they can be changed by a gauge transformation without affecting the observables E and B.

The Aharonov-Bohm paper (Physical Review, 1959) demonstrated that even when B = 0 in the region where a charged quantum particle travels, the vector potential A is nonzero and produces a measurable phase shift in the particle's wave function. This was experimentally confirmed. The implication: the potentials, not just the fields, are the fundamental physical entities in quantum mechanics.

## Bearden's Thesis: Soviet Scalar Interferometry

The document states: "the overwhelming importance of the scalar electromagnetics indicated by Bohm and Aharonov has been noted by this analyst, and work to investigate and apply [it follows]." Bearden argues that Western physics relegated potentials to mathematical conveniences while Soviet scientists took the BA effect seriously as a basis for a new class of weapons.

The key claim is that scalar potentials can be engineered to produce interference patterns (scalar interferometry) in regions distant from the source apparatus, creating energy deposition, force fields, or other physical effects at a target without detectable electromagnetic field propagation along the beam path — because the effect propagates through the potential (non-observable) rather than the field (observable).

## Implications for UAP Propulsion

The physics implications for UAP propulsion: if scalar potentials can produce macroscopic forces at remote locations through potential interference rather than field propagation, then a craft equipped with a scalar interferometer could generate thrust or lift at any point in space by controlling the potential landscape around itself, without emitting detectable electromagnetic radiation or producing a conventional propellant plume.

This is the physical mechanism most consistent with reports of UAP objects that appear to "warp" or "compress" spacetime locally — the potential landscape distortion produces a local effective modification of inertial mass or gravitational coupling.

The scalar interferometry thesis connects to the Pais Effect (US10144532B2, Track_20) via the mechanism of vacuum polarization: both propose that manipulating the electromagnetic vacuum state (through potential engineering or resonant cavity excitation) produces macroscopic kinematic effects. They differ in the specific implementation — Pais uses a resonant cavity and microwave emitters; Bearden's scalar interferometry uses phased potential transmitters at macroscopic separation.
