**Valerijs Černohajev (1958–2019) – Expanded Background and Full Technical Corpus**  
Černohajev was a Soviet aerospace engineer who worked in public-works/official capacities in the USSR (including Kazakhstan assignments tied to space/defense programs). After the USSR collapse, he struggled privately with the implications of his knowledge until sending the sealed manuscripts to his daughter shortly before his death. The 12–14 handwritten/typed works (1980–2007) are framed as private reverse-engineering notes from a recovered “UFO” (disc-shaped craft) under Soviet programs. They are not peer-reviewed but presented as engineering blueprints with schematics, parameters, and derivations.  

The full collection (publicly digitized at cernohajev.omeka.net) includes high-resolution scans, English translations, and metadata. It was compiled into the 2024 book *Engineering Infinity: Earth’s First Interstellar Blueprint*. Key works relevant to the original post’s propulsion claims:  
- **Work No. 1 (VC-002)**: Foundational *Reverse Engineering Gravitational-Charge Dualism* (33 pages). Proposes gravity as a dual-charge structure analogous to electromagnetism; time flow is proportional to opposing charge/gravity energy-field magnitudes. Massive objects carry excess negative charge. Refutes Einstein’s relativity via “instantaneous” propagation in an ether containing massive photons, neutrinos, and charges. Introduces three propagation modes: (1) oscillating charge at *c* (no mass), (2) photon flux, (3) neutrino-magnetic flow (ν_m) with minimal mass/charge tied to local time.  
- **Work No. 2 (VC-003)**: *Reverse Engineering UFO Propulsion Systems* (15 pages) – exactly the pages shown in the original X post. Details solenoid arrays, plasma reactors, thrust via ν_m flux (instantaneous, B ~ {ν_μ} + {ν_{e-e+}}), hydrogen annihilation + oppositely spinning e⁻/e⁺ or e⁻/p⁺ particles, and thermonuclear power plant. No rotating parts; uses piston/MHD generators. Specific testable parameters: reactor pressure >2,000 atm, magnetic induction B = 16.65 T, 32 solenoids in spherical array, operating current I = 1.65 × 10³ A, electron density at ignition >0.12 × 10¹³ m⁻³, charge density 0.2 × 10⁻⁶ C/m³. Hull: monocrystalline silicon-aluminum paramagnetic material for thrust generation.  
- Supporting works expand on MHD power conversion (Work No. 2²/2³), unified current/mass principles (Work No. 3), monocrystalline thrust (Work No. 4), thermonuclear synthesis characteristics (Work No. 8), and galactic dynamics under GCD (Works No. 5–7, 9, 12). Work No. 10 touches consciousness-field navigation and civilizational cycles (predicting a 2024–2042 “window” for disclosure/tech release).  

Additional physics claims (from archive summaries): structured-vacuum propulsion, gravitomagnetic field control, resonant energy transfer, and non-inertial drives. Reactor designs use ν_m fluxes to “slow time flow” in the plasma core, creating non-uniform negative-charge fields for controlled fusion without harmful neutron/beta output. Diagrams show solenoid cross-sections, particle spin directions, flux paths, and Laval-nozzle/MHD/piston systems. The framework is claimed to explain Doppler redshift, lightning, and Earth’s magnetic field via ether non-uniformity.
