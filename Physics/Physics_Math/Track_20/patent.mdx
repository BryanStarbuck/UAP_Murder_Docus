## Abstract

A craft using an inertial mass reduction device comprises of an inner resonant cavity wall, an outer resonant cavity, and microwave emitters. The electrically charged outer resonant cavity wall and the electrically insulated inner resonant cavity wall form a resonant cavity. The microwave emitters create high frequency electromagnetic waves throughout the resonant cavity causing the resonant cavity to vibrate in an accelerated mode and create a local polarized vacuum outside the outer resonant cavity wall.

## Claims (4 Total)

**Claim 1:** A craft using an inertial mass reduction device comprising: an inner resonant cavity wall; an outer resonant cavity wall, the inner resonant cavity wall and the outer resonant cavity wall forming a resonant cavity; and, microwave emitters such that the microwave emitters create high frequency electromagnetic waves throughout the resonant cavity causing the outer resonant cavity wall to vibrate in an accelerated mode and create a local polarized vacuum outside the outer resonant cavity wall.

**Claim 2:** The craft of claim 1, wherein the resonant cavity is filled with a noble gas.

**Claim 3:** The craft of claim 1, wherein the outer resonant cavity wall is electrically charged.

**Claim 4:** The craft of claim 1, wherein the resonant cavity is axially rotated in an accelerated mode.

## Description / Specification

### Background

The patent establishes that electromagnetic force can "manipulate the other three" fundamental forces (strong nuclear, weak nuclear, gravitational). Moving charges generate electromagnetic fields, and "accelerating charge induces electromagnetic radiation in the form of transverse waves, namely light."

Vacuum energy is described as "an aggregate/collective state, comprised of the superposition of all quantum fields' fluctuations permeating the entire fabric of spacetime." High-energy electromagnetic interactions can produce phenomena including field unification through vibrational energy transfer between quantum fields.

The core theoretical mechanism: inertial mass reduction via "manipulation of quantum field fluctuations in the local vacuum energy state." By "polarizing the vacuum in the close proximity of the moving craft," resistance to acceleration decreases, enabling extreme velocities.

The patent references the quantum vacuum as "the electric glue of our plasma universe," confirmed through three established experimental phenomena:
- **Casimir Effect** — measured attractive force between uncharged parallel conducting plates due to ZPF boundary conditions
- **Lamb Shift** — measured energy difference between hydrogen 2s₁/₂ and 2p₁/₂ states due to quantum vacuum fluctuations
- **Spontaneous Emission** — atomic transition rates determined by coupling to quantum vacuum modes

### Vacuum Polarization Mechanism

Vacuum polarization is achieved through coupled high-frequency vibration and axial rotation of electrically charged systems. "Spontaneous electron-positron pair production out of the vacuum is a strong indicator" of achieved polarization.

The maximum intensity of the electromagnetic field generated by the vibrating charged resonant cavity:

**S_max = f_G(σ²/ε₀)[R_r ω + R_v v + v_R]**

Where:

- ε₀ = electrical permittivity (F/m)

**2γ(dm/dt)_pp c² = S_max × A_S**

This indicates that pair production rate increases with energy flux S_max, directly correlating to vacuum polarization level. The subscript "pp" denotes pair production; γ is the Lorentz factor; A_S is the surface area.

### Craft Structure

**Primary Components:**
- Outer resonant cavity wall (electrically charged) — the active element generating the polarized vacuum
- Inner resonant cavity wall (electrically insulated) — defines the inner boundary of the cavity; crew compartments, power systems, and cargo bays are inside this inner wall
- Resonant cavity (fills gap between walls) — may be filled with noble gas (Claim 2) to enhance field coupling
- Microwave emitters (300 MHz to 300 GHz range) — energize the resonant cavity

**Q-Factor:** Describes microwave energy coupling efficiency as energy stored / energy lost ratio:
- Aluminum or copper cavity: Q ~ 10⁴ to 10⁶
- Superconducting cavity (YBCO or Niobium): Q ~ 10⁷ to 10⁹

**Faraday Cage Interior:** Internal spaces are "guarded in a Faraday-type cage, against all EM radiation effects," protecting occupants from the extreme fields in the resonant cavity.

**Frustum Design:** A rotatable conical leading edge may be incorporated for aerodynamic optimization.

### Operational Mechanism

The microwave emitters create electromagnetic waves causing the outer cavity wall to vibrate in accelerated mode. This generates "a local polarized vacuum outside the outer resonant cavity wall," reducing the craft's inertial resistance. 

For directional movement: "different sections of the resonant cavity shroud are vibrated" to achieve motion in desired directions. Upward movement results from vibrating the top portion of the shroud.

### Prigogine Effect Application

The patent invokes Prigogine's nonequilibrium thermodynamics: a chaotic system self-organizes when subjected to three conditions simultaneously:
1. Nonlinearity (satisfied by the cavity field dynamics)
2. Abrupt far-from-equilibrium excursion (satisfied by the extreme field intensity)

The high-frequency electromagnetic fields in the resonant cavity satisfy all three conditions, driving self-organization of the quantum vacuum into a polarized state.

### Gertsenshtein Effect

"Production of high frequency gravitational waves by high frequency electromagnetic radiation, in this manner modifying the gravitational fields in close proximity to the craft, resulting in its propulsion." The Gertsenshtein (1962) mechanism describes electromagnetic-gravitational wave conversion; at sufficient field intensity, this produces measurable gravitational effects.

### Hybrid Aerospace/Undersea Craft (HAUC)

The craft is envisioned as a Hybrid Aerospace/Undersea Craft (HAUC): capable of operating in air, space, and water mediums, "enclosed in a vacuum plasma bubble/sheath, due to the coupled effects of EM field-induced air/water particles repulsion and vacuum energy polarization." This directly addresses the observed UAP capability of transmedium travel.

### Gyroscopic Weight Reduction

Based on Hayasaka and Takeuchi observations (1989), the gyroscopic weight reduction model for vibrational systems:

**ΔW_R(v) = −f_ep M A_v v kg m s⁻²**

Where:
- f_ep = electron-positron pair production factor
- M = craft mass
- A_v = vibration amplitude
- v = vibration frequency

This represents the mass reduction effect per unit vibration frequency.

## Navy Institutional Context

The Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division (NAWCAD) at Patuxent River, Maryland — the same organization that investigated the Nimitz/Tic-Tac encounter of 2004 — filed and received this patent. During patent examination, the USPTO examiner initially rejected the patent citing impossibility; NAWCAD's chief technology officer James Sheehy intervened in writing, stating the technology was feasible and that China was pursuing similar programs. This internal Navy advocacy for a patent on unconventional physics, by the organization with direct knowledge of UAP encounters, is among the strongest circumstantial indicators that the Pais patents describe back-engineered or observed technology rather than purely speculative concepts.
