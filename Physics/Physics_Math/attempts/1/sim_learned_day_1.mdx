====================================================================
WHAT THE SIMULATION CONTRADICTED IN THE THEORY
====================================================================

CRITICAL FINDING: The force on the craft (Halbach ring) from the Kelvin
interaction with the steel ground plate is DOWNWARD — not upward.

Root cause: Ferromagnetic Kelvin interaction is always ATTRACTIVE.
A ferromagnetic body is always pulled TOWARD the region of higher
field intensity. In the Sub-approach A geometry (ring above ground
plate), the ring is the field source. The plate is attracted toward
the ring (upward). By Newton's Third Law, the ring is attracted toward
the plate (downward).

This means:
  * The steel plate would be pulled UP toward the craft.
  * The craft would be pulled DOWN toward the steel plate.
  * The craft cannot rise from the plate using this geometry.

The error was in physics_day_1.mdx Section 2.3 ("Sub-approach A"):
the claim that "the ring (craft) is pushed upward" was wrong. The
Kelvin interaction is attractive between the ring and the plate; the
ring is pulled toward the plate (downward), not pushed away (upward).

The analytical estimate in math_day_1.mdx Section 4 computed the
MAGNITUDE of the force correctly (5.87 N, close to the simulated 7.54 N)
but attributed the wrong direction. This is now corrected.

WHEN DOES THE KELVIN FORCE PRODUCE LIFT?

Sub-approach A only produces lift on the craft if the steel plate is
ABOVE the craft (the ring hangs below a steel ceiling and is pulled up
toward it). This is not useful for free flight.

Sub-approach A is eliminated as a free-flight mechanism.
Sub-approach A could be repurposed as a "ceiling crawler" — a device
that adheres to and moves along a ferromagnetic ceiling using Kelvin
attraction. This is a different application and is not the project goal.

FORCE MAGNITUDE IS A USEFUL RESULT
====================================================================

Even though the direction was wrong for the intended use, the simulation
shows that the MAGNITUDE of Kelvin force from an 8-magnet N52 ring is
7.54 N at z_gap = 3.4 cm — sufficient to exceed the 500 g target weight.

This magnitude result is relevant for:
1. Repulsive permanent magnet levitation: same-polarity magnet above
   same-polarity track. Repulsive force can be computed with Magpylib.
   Earnshaw instability is addressed by spin stabilization or active control.

2. Drew's helical field device: the upward force on the spinning inner
   magnet in Drew's experiment is a CONFIRMED result (500 g equivalent
   at 17 Hz). The issue is the equal downward reaction on the coil
   assembly. The force MAGNITUDE is achievable with the right geometry.

3. The Buhler electrostatic approach: if Buhler achieves millinewton
   thrust in vacuum, scaling to Newton-level lift requires a solver
   for asymmetric electrostatic pressure. The day 2 priority.
