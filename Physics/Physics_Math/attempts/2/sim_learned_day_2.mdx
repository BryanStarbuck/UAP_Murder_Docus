This is because the rapid field variation near the equator (due to
the discontinuity at the ground plane edge) generates large-amplitude
higher-order multipoles, all of which contribute positively to F_z.

2.2 REQUIRED APEX E-FIELD IS BELOW VACUUM FIELD EMISSION LIMITS

At V_lift = 227 kV, R = 10 cm:

  E_apex = 3 * V_lift / R ≈ 3 * 227,200 / 0.10 = 6.82 MV/m

CONCLUSION: The required apex field for lift (6.82 MV/m) is BELOW the vacuum

This contradicts the informal assessment in physics_day_2.mdx that the

2.3 THE ANOMALOUS E-FIELD AT THETA = 10 DEGREES REQUIRES INVESTIGATION

The E-field profile from the simulation shows an unexpected sign reversal:

  theta = 0 degrees:   E_r = +528,627 V/m  (positive, outward)
  theta = 10 degrees:  E_r = -15,796 V/m   (negative, inward!)
  theta = 20 degrees:  E_r = +229,786 V/m  (positive again)

A negative outward E_r means the E-field points INWARD at theta = 10 degrees
(toward the conductor). This physically means the conductor surface charge
is NEGATIVE at that angular position, while positive at theta = 0 and theta = 20.
This is an unexpected charge distribution.

Physical explanation candidate: The Legendre series at L_MAX = 21 is not
fully converged. The l=21 term still has amplitude 3,444 V. The alternating
sign of the A_l coefficients (l=1 positive, l=3 negative, l=5 positive, ...)
creates ringing (Gibbs-like oscillation) in the truncated series near the
theta = 0 region. The actual physical solution is smooth there.

Test to confirm convergence issue: increase L_MAX to 51 or 101 and check
if the theta = 10 sign reversal disappears.

IMPORTANT: The sign reversal in E_r near theta = 10 degrees means the
total force integral includes a small downward contribution from that ring,
but the net integral is still strongly upward (9.5 mN). The sign reversal
does NOT change the direction of the net force, but it may slightly
underestimate the true force magnitude (since the true apex field is higher
than the series estimates at its current convergence level).

A 5 m radius hemisphere at 565 kV (implied by Cernohajev charge density)

This calculation is rough but motivates investigation of the Cernohajev

4. ANOMALOUS RESULTS THAT DO NOT MATCH STANDARD PHYSICS
====================================================================

4.1 THE RESULT IS STANDARD PHYSICS — NO ANOMALY IN THE SIMULATION

The simulation result (net upward force from asymmetric electrostatic
pressure on hemisphere surface) is fully explainable by classical
electrostatics and the Maxwell stress tensor. There is NO departure from
standard physics here.

The interesting question is whether the Buhler result (in hard vacuum,
no ground plane, propellantless thrust) represents the same mechanism
or something additional. For the hemisphere-on-grounded-plane case
simulated today, the force is a reaction against the grounded plate —
classical Newton's third law applies, with the equal and opposite force
on the ground plane.

For a FREE-FLYING device (no external ground plane), the physics is
different. The device would need to carry its own internal ground reference
(e.g., a capacitor-type pair where one conductor is the asymmetric hemisphere
and the other is a flat plate internal to the craft). In that case, the
net force on the whole system (both conductors) would be zero by Newton's
third law — UNLESS there is a mechanism by which the electromagnetic field
carries net momentum into the far field.

This is the key open question for Day 3: does the ISOLATED asymmetric
charged conductor system (no external ground plane) produce a measurable
net force on the complete isolated system, or only on the individual
conductors relative to each other?
