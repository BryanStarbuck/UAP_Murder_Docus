4. WHAT IS BEING COMPUTED
====================================================================

Primary computation:
  * E_r(R, theta) = sum_{l odd} A_l * (l+1)/R * P_l(cos theta)
    — normal E-field at the hemisphere surface

  * P_e(theta) = (eps_0/2) * E_r(R, theta)^2
    — electrostatic pressure at each surface point

  * F_z = 2*pi*R^2 * integral_0^{pi/2} P_e(theta) * cos(theta) * sin(theta) dtheta
    — net vertical force from Maxwell stress tensor surface integral
