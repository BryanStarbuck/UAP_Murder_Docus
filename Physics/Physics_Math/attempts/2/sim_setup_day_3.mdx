KEY FINDING: The Legendre series is NOT converged at L_MAX=21.

(theta = pi/2). The singularity causes slow convergence of the Legendre series.
The force integral is finite (cos(theta) → 0 at the rim), but the series
converges logarithmically, not to a fixed value — suggesting the actual
integral has a weak logarithmic divergence from the corner.

from the ringing are at the surface near theta=90 degrees.

SIMULATION 3C: Isolated Conductor Force Analysis
====================================================================

FILE: simulation/scipy_numpy/software/run_2_3_isolated_conductor.py

GEOMETRY: Hemisphere in free space (no ground plane). Various cases.
WHAT IT TESTS: Does the Maxwell stress on an isolated conductor give F_z = 0?
EXPECTED: F_z = 0 for any closed isolated conductor.
            F_z ≠ 0 only when external ground plane is present.
INPUTS: R = 0.10 m, V_0 = 10 kV. Three test cases (sphere, isolated cap, Newton's 3rd law).

--- ACTUAL RESULTS ---

TEST 1 (Full sphere validation): F_z = -2.17e-19 N ≈ 0 exactly. PASS.
TEST 2 (Isolated hemisphere cap, uniform charge): F_z_cap = 1.39 mN (upward).
  This is the force on the CAP ALONE when the base disk is the reaction body.
  For a CLOSED conductor: F_z_total = 0. Green's theorem confirmed.
TEST 3 (Newton's 3rd law check):
  F_z on hemisphere = +9.499 mN (upward)
  F_z on ground plate = -9.499 mN (downward)
  System total = 0 N (Newton's 3rd law holds exactly)

KEY FINDING: The Buhler mechanism is an EXTERNAL FORCE between the electrode
(hemisphere) and the external ground plane. The force on the ISOLATED CONDUCTOR
alone is zero. For flight, an external ground plane (Earth or tether) is required.
Free-space propulsion from electrostatic pressure alone is not possible with a
closed conductor — requires either a nearby external conductor or ion emission
(which is mass-consuming propulsion).
