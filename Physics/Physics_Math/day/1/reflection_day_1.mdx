Q6: SHOULD AN OLD ATTEMPT BE REVISITED?
====================================================================

Attempt 1 Sub-approach B (rotating field time-asymmetric force):
Revisit on Day 3 after Attempt 2 has been started. Requires ElmerFEM
or openEMS. Not urgent — Sub-approach A is ruled out, B is unproven.

Specifically: the rotating Halbach array may create eddy currents in
the steel plate below that produce a REPULSIVE secondary field. Eddy
currents in a conducting plate from a time-varying magnetic source
create fields that oppose the change (Lenz's law) — this can produce
repulsive force between the rotating array and the plate. This is
exactly how maglev induction repulsion works (EDS: electrodynamic
suspension). If this is the mechanism, the lift force scales with
(rotation speed)² and becomes significant above a critical RPM.

This is worth a dedicated Day 3 investigation. ElmerFEM handles
rotating fields and eddy currents in 3D.

"9.7 KELVIN FORCE DIRECTION RULE
The Kelvin body force on a ferromagnetic material (χ > 0) always points
TOWARD higher |B|. The reaction force on the field source always points
TOWARD the ferromagnetic target. Therefore, in any geometry where the
field source is above the target: source is pulled DOWN, target is pulled
UP. To use Kelvin force to lift the source, the target must be ABOVE
the source."
