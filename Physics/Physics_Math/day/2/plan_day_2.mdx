PHYSICS PLAN (Section 4.3 Buhler + Manual 3.4)
====================================================================

The Buhler mechanism:
* A conductor with asymmetric geometry is charged to high voltage.
* The electrostatic pressure on the conductor surface is P = eps_0 E^2 / 2.
* On a curved asymmetric surface, the normal to the surface varies.
* The net integrated electrostatic pressure force F = integral over S of
  P * n_hat dA is nonzero when the conductor geometry is asymmetric.
* The force is in the direction of the apex of the asymmetric conductor
  (the end with highest E-field concentration due to curvature).

For a hemisphere on a flat ground plane:
* Top surface: curved, E-field concentrated at apex.
* Bottom surface: flat, E-field uniform.
* Net force: upward (toward the apex of the hemisphere).

This is what Buhler measured in hard vacuum. Ion wind was ruled out by
vacuum testing. The mechanism is purely electrostatic pressure on the
conductor surface, transmitted to the conductor as a body force.

SIMULATION PLAN
====================================================================

Tool: Python scipy/numpy (electrostatic analytical model)
  * No FEM needed for hemisphere geometry — analytical solution exists.
  * Use Method of Images for a hemispherical conductor on a grounded plane.
  * Compute E-field on the hemisphere surface.
  * Compute Maxwell stress tensor T_ij = eps_0 (E_i E_j - delta_ij |E|^2/2).
  * Integrate T_ij * n_j over the surface to get net force.

Validation check: For a spherically symmetric conductor (full sphere),
net force must be zero (symmetry). Test this first. If non-zero, the
code has a sign or integration error.

Main test: Hemisphere on ground plane. Net force must be nonzero and
upward. Record magnitude for comparison to Buhler result.

Extension test: Vary the conductor geometry (cone, prolate spheroid)
and record how force varies with asymmetry ratio.
