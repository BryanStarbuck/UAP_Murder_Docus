WHAT PHYSICS MECHANISM IS ANALYZED TODAY
====================================================================

Two physics themes for Day 3:

THEME 1 (Tasks 3A, 3B): Accuracy and geometry optimization of the
Maxwell stress tensor integration for asymmetric electrostatic thrust.
  * Legendre series convergence: quantify Gibbs ringing error in L_MAX=21
  * Cone geometry: find optimal half-angle for maximum geometry factor C

THEME 2 (Task 3C): Reaction force accounting for an isolated conductor system.
  * When hemisphere and plate are both part of the same device (not one
    grounded externally), Newton's third law requires F_total = 0 on the system.
  * The question: is the Buhler mechanism an external-reaction-force effect
    (the craft pushes off the external ground plane — not useful for free flight)?
  * Or: is there a self-contained geometry where net force on the total
    system is nonzero in vacuum (which would violate classical EM...)?
  * Resolution via image charge method: the image charge in the ground plane
    represents the charge distribution on the external conductor. For a truly
    isolated craft, the image charge does not exist — the only conductor is
    the craft itself.
  * The correct simulation for an isolated craft: asymmetric surface charge on
    a single conductor (no ground plane). Compute E externally. Integrate Maxwell
    stress over the full conductor surface.
  * If F_total = 0 for any isolated conductor regardless of shape (which
    classical EM demands for a charge-neutral conductor), then the Buhler
    mechanism requires an external reaction surface and is NOT a free-flight
    propulsion system.
  * If F_total > 0 (possible only for a conductor with net charge), then the
    system requires net charge, and thrust comes from Coulomb interaction with
    the charged environment (ion wind in air, or field interaction in vacuum).
