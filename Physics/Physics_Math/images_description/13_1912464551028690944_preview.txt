PHYSICS/UAP CONTEXT:
This low-angle view provides a different perspective on the device's structural architecture:

1. Shaft and coupling geometry: The visible brass coupling/collar at mid-shaft height suggests the shaft is assembled from two sections that join at this point — the lower section connects to the platform/base, and the upper section extends toward the canopy or upper assembly. This modular construction allows the shaft height and configuration to be modified.

2. Assembly mounting height: From this eye-level perspective, the disc assemblies are seen to be mounted at approximately the same height as the brass coupling — suggesting they are clamped or threaded onto the shaft at or near this joint position. The three-fold arrangement around the shaft creates the three-phase stator geometry.

3. Material deterioration analysis: The low-angle view confirms the full extent of the foil deterioration across all assemblies. The dark gray epoxy compound is now the primary structural element — the foil was a secondary wrapping. This suggests the core magnetic/electrical function is in the potted epoxy element, not the foil alone.

4. Wooden frame scale: The four-post wooden frame is the outer housing/structural frame that will eventually support additional components (the starburst panels, toroidal ring) in later construction stages. The posts are spaced approximately 30-40cm apart — setting the outer scale of the device.

5. Device evolution timeline: This image dates to the same construction phase as 13_1912105612931932160 (deteriorated foil, pre-gray-epoxy recoating), providing additional documentation of the intermediate stage. The transition from this foil-deterioration stage to the fully gray-epoxy-recoated assemblies (13_1964079071182409728) represents a significant material upgrade.
