PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This is a linear electromagnetic rail gun or railgun experiment in its elementary form. In a railgun:
  - Two parallel conducting rails carry current from a power source.
  - A conducting projectile (the small metallic block at the right) sits across the rails, completing the circuit.
  - Current flows through the circuit: source → rail 1 → projectile → rail 2 → source.
  - The magnetic field B generated by current in the rails interacts with the current in the projectile via the Lorentz force F = IL × B, propelling the projectile along the rail.
  - The bright glowing spot at the left contact area indicates a high-current arc or contact point — classic railgun behavior where the contact point generates intense heat and plasma.
* The force on the projectile in a railgun is: F = (mu_0 * I^2 / pi) * ln(d/r), where I is current, d is rail separation, and r is rail radius.
* This is a fundamentally important electromagnetic propulsion concept — scaled versions of railguns are used in military applications (Navy electromagnetic railgun program) and are also discussed in advanced propulsion physics.
