PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This is a schematic/diagram of a Watt or Newcomen-style reciprocating steam engine — the thermodynamic heat engine that was central to the Industrial Revolution.
* The cycle depicted is: Heat → Boiler generates steam → Steam enters cylinder and pushes piston → Connecting rod converts reciprocating motion to rotational motion via crank/flywheel → Flywheel smooths rotational output.
* Thermodynamically, this is a Rankine cycle: water → high-pressure steam (boiler at high T) → expansion through piston (work output) → exhaust (heat rejection at low T).
* The Carnot efficiency bound: eta_Carnot = 1 - T_cold/T_hot.
