PHYSICS CONTEXT:
This frame shows the gyroscope in a more inclined precessing state. In gyroscopic precession:
- A spinning top tilted from vertical experiences a gravitational torque τ = r × mg (where r is the distance from the contact point to the center of mass)
- This torque causes the spin axis to precess around the vertical at angular frequency Ω_precession = τ/L = Mgr/(Iω), where I is the moment of inertia and ω is the spin angular velocity
- As the top loses rotational speed (due to friction at the tip), the precession rate increases and the tilt angle increases
- Eventually the top falls (nutation becomes dominant, then full topple)
- This frame may show the top approaching its final precession before stopping, consistent with the tilted posture
