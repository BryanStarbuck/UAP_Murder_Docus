PHYSICS CONTEXT:
This configuration represents a major design evolution:
- The two-tier acrylic disc sandwich structure creates a well-defined axial separation between components, allowing the disc coil assemblies to be positioned between two precisely parallel planes
- The aluminum foil shielding on individual coil assemblies is consistent with electromagnetic interference (EMI) shielding — preventing cross-coupling between adjacent coil assemblies (a known problem in multi-coil motor configurations where mutual inductance between stator coils wastes energy)
- Alternatively, the foil may be part of a capacitive electrode configuration — each foil-covered coil assembly acts as both an inductor (the coil winding) and a capacitor (the foil surface) simultaneously, creating an LC resonant structure
- This two-tier design also allows the use of both faces of each disc coil assembly (top face interacts with upper tier rotor, bottom face interacts with lower tier), effectively doubling the active surface area
