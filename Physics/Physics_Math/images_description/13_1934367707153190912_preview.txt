PHYSICS CONTEXT:
This setup is characteristic of a small-scale Tesla coil demonstration:

- The Tesla coil operates on the principle of coupled LC resonators: the primary drives the secondary magnetically (transformer coupling), but when both are tuned to the same resonant frequency, energy transfer is extremely efficient through resonant coupling
