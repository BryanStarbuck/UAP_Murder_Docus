PHYSICS/UAP CONTEXT:
Ground-level photography of crop circle interiors is important for research because it allows direct examination of the physical characteristics of the bent stems:
- Stem node elongation and explosion: some researchers (notably W.C. Levengood and the BLT Research Team) have documented that crop stems in some circles show elongated plant nodes and exploded node tissue, which they attribute to rapid internal heating from microwave-frequency radiation (consistent with a focused beam from a UAP or electromagnetic source)

- The central standing tuft (if present) is consistent with the geometry of a vortex with a low-velocity center (eye of a vortex) surrounded by a high-velocity rotating annular region
