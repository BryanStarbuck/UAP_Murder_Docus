PHYSICS/UAP CONTEXT:
This motorized gyroscope has several important features relative to the research collection:
- The large black circular base suggests an electric motor — likely providing continuous spin to the conical disc element (unlike the pull-string or hand-launched gyroscopes in other frames)
- A motor-driven gyroscope maintains constant spin speed (no friction decay), enabling sustained gyroscopic stability for extended periods
- The conical/funnel shape of the spinning mass is distinct from the flat disc gyroscope shapes — a cone has a different moment of inertia tensor (I_xx ≠ I_zz ≠ I_yy) creating more complex precession behavior than a flat disc
- The conical shape is particularly relevant to UAP craft geometry: some UAP witnesses report cone or trumpet-shaped craft (the inverse of the classic disc shape)
- Motor-driven gyroscopes are used in spacecraft attitude control (control moment gyroscopes) and ship stabilizers
