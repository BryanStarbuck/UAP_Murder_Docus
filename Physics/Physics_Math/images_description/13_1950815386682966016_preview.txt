PHYSICS/UAP CONTEXT:
This is the fourth instance of the 3I/ATLAS inner solar system diagram (see 13_1948914967396450309 for full diagram, 13_1949622406672515072 and 13_1949982280119668736 for near-duplicates of the full frame, and 13_1950320524095258624 for the companion inner solar system crop). The planet labels in this version are the most explicit, making this the most pedagogically complete version of the inner solar system crop — a viewer can clearly identify which orbit belongs to which planet and understand the relative scale of 3I/ATLAS's hyperbolic approach path relative to the habitable zone (Earth orbit). The labeled Mars position at the left side of the diagram shows that 3I/ATLAS's trajectory line enters from the left side near the Mars orbit level, sweeps inward past Earth and Venus toward perihelion, and exits to the lower-right — indicating a perihelion distance likely inside the Earth orbit (< 1 AU from the Sun). At 60.6 km/s approaching velocity (as shown in companion full-frame diagrams), this represents an object with hyperbolic excess velocity v_∞ ≈ 55-58 km/s relative to the solar system — consistent with but faster than the anomalous 'Oumuamua (v_∞ ≈ 26 km/s) and I/Borisov (v_∞ ≈ 32 km/s).
