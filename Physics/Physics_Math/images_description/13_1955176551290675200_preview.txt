For full physics context, see 13_1940173495037485056_preview.txt. This is another image in the spinning top demonstration series. The stable upright position of the top while spinning demonstrates the conservation of angular momentum (L = Iω pointing vertically upward) and gyroscopic rigidity — the top's angular momentum vector resists gravity-induced torque that would otherwise cause it to simply fall over.
