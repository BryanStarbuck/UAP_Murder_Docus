PHYSICS/UAP CONTEXT:
The larger black instrument being held by the demonstrator suggests a more dramatic demonstration is underway or about to begin. In Royal Institution Christmas Lectures on gyroscopes, one of the most famous demonstrations involves a large bicycle wheel or heavy spinning wheel that, when spun rapidly, dramatically demonstrates gyroscopic precession when tilted and gyroscopic rigidity when the holder tries to change its spin axis orientation. The person holding the heavy spinning wheel can feel the strong gyroscopic force resisting any attempt to change the spin axis — making the invisible angular momentum vector physically tangible and memorable for the audience. This demonstration is the most viscerally effective way to demonstrate L = Iω as a real physical quantity. The blackboard diagram above provides the mathematical framework for what the audience is feeling physically.
