PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This is a small tabletop homopolar motor experiment using an AA battery (1.5V Energizer), a brass screw, and a conducting disc. This is the simplest form of homopolar motor:
  - A battery provides EMF.
  - Current flows through the brass screw (axle).
  - A conducting disc (gold-colored disc at the base) sits on a permanent magnet.
  - The Lorentz force on the current-carrying disc in the magnet's field causes rotation.
* The design sketches on the background papers show the circular Lemniscate or figure-eight patterns — these may represent the planned current flow paths, magnetic field topologies, or the intended rotor shape for a more complex design.
* This is the classic DIY homopolar motor demonstration that can be replicated with a battery, a strong magnet, and a piece of wire — demonstrating Faraday's principle at minimal cost.
