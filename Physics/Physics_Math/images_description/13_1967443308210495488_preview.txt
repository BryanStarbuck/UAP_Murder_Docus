PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* Mercury (Hg) is a liquid metal at room temperature (melting point -38.83°C). It has electrical conductivity of approximately 1.04 × 10^6 S/m (much lower than copper but sufficient for low-resistance contact in rotating devices).
* In homopolar motor experiments, mercury serves as the liquid electrical contact that maintains conduction between a stationary circuit and the rotating conducting disc or bowl.
* The glass bowl filled with mercury is the conducting vessel for the experiment — when current flows from the center contact (black clip) radially through the mercury to the rim (red clip) in the presence of an axial magnetic field from a permanent magnet below the bowl, the Lorentz force drives circular rotation of the mercury (and any floating conductor in it).
* This is Faraday's original homopolar motor configuration using liquid metal as the active conducting medium.
* Safety note: mercury is highly toxic — the use of mercury in a home workshop (on a concrete floor) represents significant environmental hazard.
