The disc/magnet ring assembly: The configuration of multiple small disc magnets arranged in a ring pattern is consistent with a Halbach array — a magnet arrangement in which the magnetic field is concentrated on one side and nearly cancelled on the other. Halbach arrays are used in linear motors, magnetic levitation systems (maglev trains), and some experimental propulsion concepts.

2. Halbach array physics: In a Halbach array, the magnetization vectors of adjacent magnets rotate by 90° around the array. For a one-sided flux Halbach array:
   B_peak = B_r × (1 - e^(-2πl/λ)) × (sin(π/M)/(π/M))
   where B_r is remanence flux density, l is magnet thickness, λ is spatial period, and M is the number of magnets per wavelength. This concentrates the magnetic field to one face of the array, achieving ~1.4× the field strength of a conventional arrangement.
