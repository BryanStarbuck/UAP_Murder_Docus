PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* Homopolar motor — simplest self-contained electromagnetic motor: current from battery flows through axle screw → conducting disc → external wire → battery base, in presence of the battery's own magnetic field (from internal chemistry, which is weak, but supplemented here by the neodymium magnet on top of the battery).
* Demonstrates Faraday's original 1821 homopolar motor principle.
* This is the minimal-component demonstration version for teaching the concept before scaling up to the mercury-contact large disc versions shown elsewhere in this series.
