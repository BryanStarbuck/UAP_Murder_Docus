PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* The orange sphere on top of the axle is a notable addition — it serves as a visual rotation indicator:
  - When the conducting disc in the mercury bowl rotates, the axle rotates with it, and the orange sphere at the top would be visually obvious when spinning (the uneven surface/color of a real fruit would show rotation clearly even at low RPM).
  - This is a clever low-cost diagnostic for proving axle rotation in the homopolar motor.
* Alternatively, the sphere's mass provides a known load for torque measurement (F = mr ω² for the centripetal force at known radius and known mass).
* The wider-flanged lower-profile bowl may be a new design iteration for improved mercury contact area.
* The plaster/putty securing the bowl is visible — this prevents the bowl from being ejected or tilting when the disc spins at high speed.
