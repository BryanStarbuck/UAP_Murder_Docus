PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This is an orbital tracking diagram for **3I/ATLAS** — a newly discovered interstellar object (the third interstellar object ever identified, after 1I/'Oumuamua and 2I/Borisov) that was detected passing through the solar system:
  - 3I/ATLAS was discovered in 2025 — its "3I" designation indicates it is the third interstellar object.
  - At the time shown (2025-05-26), 3I/ATLAS was at 4.9787 au from Earth and 5.7950 au from the Sun, traveling at 60.6 km/s (135,600 mph) — well above the solar escape velocity at that distance (~13 km/s at 5 au), confirming its hyperbolic (interstellar) trajectory.
  - The close approach occurred 2025-05-26 at 05:30 UTC.
* In UAP physics context: interstellar objects like 3I/ATLAS receive significant attention in the UAP research community as potential artificial craft or probes:
  - 'Oumuamua (1I) exhibited anomalous non-gravitational acceleration that Harvard astronomer Avi Loeb (among others) argued was inconsistent with natural explanations.
  - The extreme speed of 3I/ATLAS and its interstellar trajectory place it in the category of objects that UAP researchers monitor for anomalous behavior.
  - The speed of 60.6 km/s suggests a hyperbolic excess velocity of ~45-50 km/s relative to the solar system — at the high end for natural interstellar objects.
