PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This is the post-run state documentation of the central mercury bowl — showing the electrochemical and thermal degradation after sustained high-current operation:
  1. Mercury oxidation products: when high current passes through mercury in the presence of air, mercury reacts with oxygen to form mercury oxide (HgO) and possibly mercury sulfide (HgS) if sulfur contaminants are present. The dark brown-black layer is predominantly HgO, a solid that forms at the mercury surface and inhibits further current flow.
  2. The extent of oxidation suggests the bowl operated at very high current densities for extended periods — consistent with the UAP propulsion thesis requiring hundreds to thousands of amperes through a small mercury contact area.
  3. The four radial paper strips serve as angular reference markers — they allow measurement of any rotational displacement of the bowl contents or reference objects after an experimental run.
  4. The two crocodile clips on the central axle are the high-current contact points — the clip geometry and axle rod form the center electrode of the Corbino disc circuit (current in at center, out at rim through mercury, or vice versa).
