PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This is a significant post-catastrophic-run documentation — showing extensive mercury spillage from the disc assemblies:
  1. The mercury has not merely oxidized in place (as in the normal post-run state) but has physically spilled out of the bowl containment — this indicates either an extremely high-current run that generated sufficient disc rotation and centrifugal force to eject mercury from the bowl, or a physical upset of the apparatus during the run.
  2. The centrifugal ejection of mercury during high-speed disc rotation is a known risk in homopolar motor experiments: when the conducting disc spins at high rpm, the mercury interface at the rim experiences centrifugal acceleration a = ω²r, and if ω is large enough, the mercury can overcome surface tension and be flung outward.
  3. The ball-joint sphere visible at the center-top of the central axle is the "ball magnet" contact element — this is a key component of the UAP ball magnet thesis, where a spherical magnet on a pivot can orient in any direction.
  4. The red magnet blocks are the neodymium or ferrite permanent magnets providing the axial magnetic field for each disc position.
  5. Mercury spills in a laboratory context are a serious health hazard (Hg vapor at room temperature, Hg skin absorption) — the white paper and safety materials visible suggest the experimenter was using some protective materials.
