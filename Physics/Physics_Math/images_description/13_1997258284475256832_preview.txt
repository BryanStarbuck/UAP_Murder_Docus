PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* Close-up of the capacitor-discharge homopolar motor bowl assembly from 13_1997258262782357504, showing the central electrode termination in detail:
  1. The central axle post with ring nut is the zero-radius current contact for the Corbino disc — current injected at the center flows radially outward through the conducting disc (or mercury pool) to the outer rim electrode (the bowl rim).
  2. The mercury pooled around the bowl base has spilled from the interior — either from centrifugal ejection during high-rpm operation, from physical upset, or from the capacitor discharge impulse physically disturbing the bowl.
  3. The heavy clamps on the center axle are designed to handle high-current pulses from the capacitor — standard crocodile clips would overheat or arc at the discharge current levels used.
  4. This close-up confirms the bowl assembly design: upward-facing conducting disc inside the bowl, with mercury as the liquid-metal interface between the disc and the bowl rim, and the center axle providing the inner electrode contact.
