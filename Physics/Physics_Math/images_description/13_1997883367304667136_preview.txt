* This linear electromagnet array with active plasma arc discharge is a significant new component in the UAP physics experimental series:
  1. **Linear solenoid/electromagnet array**: The white rectangular blocks each contain coil windings energized by the red/black leads. When energized sequentially or simultaneously, the coils produce a linear magnetic field array. This is analogous to a linear motor or stator winding configuration.
  2. **Plasma arc discharge**: The blue-violet arc between two adjacent blocks indicates a high-voltage differential — either from a short circuit between adjacent coil elements or from intentional high-voltage generation between electrode gaps in the array. The nitrogen plasma emission (blue-violet at ~337-391 nm) is characteristic of a high-voltage air gap breakdown.
  3. **Possible function**: This linear array may be a high-voltage primary coil array (similar to a linear induction motor or a Tesla coil secondary exciter array) designed to create a traveling electromagnetic wave that interacts with the main three-disc apparatus. Alternatively, it could be a coil array for an electromagnetic cannon/rail gun type experiment.
