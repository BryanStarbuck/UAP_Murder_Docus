PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This is a water/electrolyte Corbino disc experiment — a safer and more accessible analog to the mercury Corbino disc:
  1. **Electrolytic Corbino disc**: Replacing mercury with a conducting electrolyte (saline water, copper sulfate solution, or sodium chloride solution) creates an electrolytic version of the Corbino disc motor. The electrolyte has much lower conductivity than mercury (~5-50 S/m for saline vs. ~10⁶ S/m for mercury), so it requires much lower currents for observable rotation, but the physics is identical.
  2. **Electrohydrodynamic rotation**: Current flows radially through the electrolyte (center electrode to rim), and in the presence of an axial magnetic field (from a magnet below the bowl), the Lorentz body force F_φ = J_r × B_z drives tangential rotation of the conducting fluid.
  3. **"N2" marking**: The "N2" inscribed in the bowl may indicate this is the second experimental run of a nitrogen-related experiment — or it may simply be a run number or reference mark for angular position tracking.
  4. **Yellow tabs**: The four yellow tabs at 90° intervals serve as angular reference markers to visually track rotation — if the fluid rotates, the tabs (attached to the bowl or floating) would allow measurement of angular displacement.
  5. **Safety advantage**: Water/electrolyte instead of mercury dramatically reduces hazard — mercury vapor is toxic (Hg PEL = 0.1 mg/m³), while saline water is benign. This makes the experiment accessible for demonstration and replication.
* This may be the "Roobertgg YT" apparatus or a different experimenter replicating the Corbino disc physics with safer materials.
