PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This image is particularly important for revealing the gold/brass Faraday conducting disc of the central assembly:
  1. **Gold/brass Faraday disc**: The bright gold-colored flat disc is the primary conducting element of the Corbino disc motor. Its relatively high conductivity (brass ~15×10⁶ S/m) and its flat disc geometry allow radial current flow from the center axle to the rim mercury contact. The gold color indicates brass or possibly gold-plated copper — both are used in precision electromagnetic experiments for their corrosion resistance.
  2. **Post-catastrophic run state**: The heavy oxidation and physical debris on all three disc housings confirms this is after the catastrophic high-current run that caused mercury ejection (as documented in 13_1994256631992135680). The gold Faraday disc appears to have survived the run relatively cleanly compared to the surrounding mercury oxide.
  3. **Central axle bolt**: The threaded bolt below the gold disc on the central axle is a securing nut that prevents the disc from sliding off during high-speed rotation — the centrifugal force on the disc during spinning would otherwise cause it to fly off the axle.
  4. **Hand repositioning**: The hand holding the axle rod suggests active disassembly or inspection of the apparatus following the experimental run.
