PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This is an electrolytic Corbino disc (also called an electrolytic homopolar motor) — a liquid-conductor analog of the classical Corbino disc:
  1. **Electrolytic Corbino disc principle**: Instead of mercury or a solid conducting disc, the conducting medium is an electrolytic solution (saline water or other ionic solution). Current flows radially from the central copper rod (inner electrode) to the outer copper ring (outer electrode), or vice versa depending on polarity. The applied axial magnetic field (not visible in frame — presumably from magnets above or below the tank) produces a Lorentz force F = J × B on the radially-flowing ionic current, driving azimuthal fluid rotation — the electromagnetic equivalent of a homopolar motor.
  2. **EMF analysis**: For a Corbino disc with inner radius r₁ and outer radius r₂ in axial field B, the induced EMF is ε = ½ω B(r₂² - r₁²). The spiral wave pattern confirms azimuthal velocity ω > 0, establishing rotation.
  3. **Ekman spiral / inertial wave pattern**: The visible concentric arc pattern in the rotating fluid is characteristic of Ekman spiral dynamics — the boundary layer solution to the Navier-Stokes equations in a rotating frame under a magnetic body force. The spiral arm curvature indicates the ratio of electromagnetic driving force to viscous drag.
  4. **Comparison to mercury Corbino disc**: The electrolytic analog operates at lower current density and is safer (no mercury toxicity hazard). However, electrolysis at the electrodes (copper oxidation, hydrogen/oxygen evolution) introduces complications absent in mercury systems. The copper ring electrodes will develop copper oxide patina over time.
  5. **Current level**: The heavy cable gauge suggests currents in the 50-100A range — comparable to the 90A mercury Corbino disc runs documented elsewhere in this series (see 13_1995467474893262848).
  6. **Azimuthal rotation confirmation**: The spiral vortex wave pattern is the definitive visual proof of azimuthal fluid motion — static fluid under radial current flow without magnetic field would show no such pattern.
