PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This shows a different construction iteration of the SEG roller assemblies — the red disc-shaped rollers sitting on a wire ring (the ring magnet track/raceway). In the SEG design, the rollers ride in the groove of the ring magnet, not sitting on top of it. This construction may represent a simplified test configuration where flat disc magnets (wrapped in red tape to mark polarity or protect the surface) are positioned on a wire guide ring to test their tendency to rotate. The wire ring serves as the circular track/guide.
