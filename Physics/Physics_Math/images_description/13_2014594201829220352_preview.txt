PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* The upgrade from three to six rollers represents a more advanced SEG configuration. John Searl's original design specifies multiple concentric rings, each with increasing numbers of rollers. The innermost ring (closest to center) traditionally has the fewest rollers; outer rings have more. Moving from 3 to 6 rollers may represent adding a second ring tier or simply increasing the roller count for the single ring. In electromagnetic terms, 6 equally-spaced rollers around a ring magnet creates a hexapole magnetic field configuration, which may produce more symmetric force distribution on the rotating assembly. The six-roller arrangement also has implications for rotational modes: with 6-fold symmetry, the system has degenerate rotational eigenmodes at each (60-degree) equivalent position.

This image shows an updated SEG (Searl Effect Generator) replica configuration with six roller assemblies (increased from three in earlier images), arranged in a hexagonal ring pattern on the white disc platform.
