PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* The left reference diagram explicitly labels "3-phase magnetism" — this is key to the SEG theory. The SEG ring magnet is designed with alternating north-south magnetic segments arranged so that, as the rollers rotate around it, each roller experiences a 3-phase magnetic excitation (analogous to how a 3-phase AC motor stator creates a rotating magnetic field). This 3-phase interaction is claimed to drive spontaneous roller acceleration. The right diagram shows the resulting magnetic field topology — a complex rotating pattern of magnetic field lines created by the orbiting rollers.
