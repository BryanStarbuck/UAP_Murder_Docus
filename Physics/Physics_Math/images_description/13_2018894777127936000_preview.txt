PHYSICS / ENGINEERING CONCEPTS:
* 6-COIL RADIAL-FLUX STATOR ASSEMBLY:
  * This is the stator component of a radial-flux permanent-magnet generator (PMG) or motor
  * The 6 equally spaced radial coils create a stator field that can rotate when energized sequentially with phase-shifted currents
  * For a 3-phase supply at 120° phase separation: the net B-field vector rotates continuously → creates a rotating magnetic field

* RELEVANCE TO UAP DISC PROPULSION:
  * A rotating magnetic field in the plane of the disc is precisely the physics proposed for UAP disc propulsion:
    * The rotating B-field acts on charge carriers in the surrounding medium (ionized plasma sheath or inertial mass of the craft)
    * The gradient of the rotating field creates a net force in the direction of field rotation (momentum exchange)
    * This 6-coil prototype is a scaled-down physical test of that principle
  * Compare with the 3-arm triskelion and 6-arm crop formations: these encode 3-phase (3-arm) or 6-phase (6-arm) winding topologies

* INDUCTION MOTOR PRINCIPLE:
  * If a conducting rotor (disc, ring, or squirrel-cage) is placed at the center of this stator:
    * The rotating stator field induces eddy currents in the rotor
    * The eddy currents interact with the rotating field → Lorentz force → rotor torque
    * This is the standard squirrel-cage induction motor principle at prototype scale
  * Efficiency η = (1 - s) where s = slip = (n_s - n_r)/n_s; for disc topology, slip minimization requires very low rotor resistance
