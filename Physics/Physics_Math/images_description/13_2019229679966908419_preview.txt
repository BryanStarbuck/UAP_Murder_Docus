PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This is a 6-roller SEG configuration using large flat disc magnets rather than the cylindrical rollers seen in earlier assembly stages. The flat disc form factor may represent an attempt to maximize the contact arc with the ring stator, increasing the overlap between roller magnet flux and stator ring flux gradient. In Searl's design, the rollers must maintain both rotational freedom (spinning on their own axis) and orbital freedom (orbiting the stator ring), and the flat disc form creates a different flux coupling geometry than cylindrical rollers. The alligator clip wiring suggests measurement of output EMF per roller or between roller pairs — standard characterization of an electromagnetic generator design.
