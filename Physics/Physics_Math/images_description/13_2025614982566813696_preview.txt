PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This is a homopolar generator / Faraday disc voltage measurement experiment. The 55 mV measured "between the axles" is the open-circuit EMF generated by two coaxial conducting discs rotating (or being tested statically for residual magnetic flux-induced EMF). This measurement is significant because:
* A homopolar generator (Faraday disc) generates EMF via the equation E = (1/2)ωBr², where ω is angular velocity, B is axial magnetic field strength, and r is disc radius. For a stationary disc, this EMF is zero unless a permanent magnetic field is present and there is mechanical rotation.
* If the 55 mV is measured with the discs stationary, it may indicate a residual Hall voltage or contact potential difference — potentially an unexpected electrostatic or galvanic effect.

* The phrase "the surprise" implies the result was unexpected — 55 mV from a stationary or slowly rotating simple disc pair would be anomalous under standard electromagnetic theory.
