PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This is a top-view schematic of the Searl Effect Generator showing the canonical roller-ring arrangement. The 14-16 rollers arranged around the inner stator ring correspond to Searl's documented design, where rollers travel in the space between the stator ring and the outer case. Each roller (small circle with black center) represents a layered permanent magnet cylinder: the white outer ring is the outer magnetic layer and the black center is the inner magnetic core, both with specific magnetization patterns (alternating N-S zones around the circumference, plus axial bias). The toothed outer ring may represent either the outer race of the bearing housing or the outer stator ring in a multi-ring SEG design (Searl designed SEGs with up to 3 concentric rings, each with its own set of rollers). The bullseye center (white ring within black within white) represents the central hole and bearing of the assembly.

Explicit black-and-white 2D SEG roller arrangement schematic showing 14-16 roller elements (white discs with black centers) orbiting a black stator ring, all within a toothed outer gear ring — with a bullseye pattern at the center. This is the clearest top-view SEG layout diagram in the series. The 14-16 roller count corresponds to more advanced multi-roller SEG designs.
