PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* Same precision-machined acrylic SEG stator disc as documented in 13_2028932949316698112. The double concentric grooved race tracks are for guiding the toroidal roller magnets in two concentric rings. The notched profile of the groove walls provides the mechanical constraint (roller race) while allowing the rollers to precess around the stator. This disc represents a significant engineering advancement over earlier wooden-frame constructions — the precision of the CNC-machined acrylic allows consistent roller-to-stator gap geometry across all azimuthal positions, which is critical for achieving the claimed symmetric rotating magnetic field.
