PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* Classical angular momentum demonstration: a spinning flywheel (disc) with angular momentum vector L = Iω (where I is moment of inertia and ω is angular velocity) can transfer angular momentum to a person standing on a low-friction rotating platform. When the disc is tilted or its angular momentum vector is changed, the platform rotates to conserve total angular momentum. This is a standard physics lecture demonstration, often called the "bicycle wheel on turntable" or "spinning disc gyroscope" experiment. The white tire likely serves as a low-friction bearing to allow the platform to rotate freely. Relevance to UAP physics: gyroscopic effects and angular momentum coupling are fundamental to rotating magnetic field systems like the SEG — the Searl Effect Generator's claimed behavior (spontaneous rotation, angular momentum generation) is proposed to arise from similar angular momentum considerations in the magnetic field domain.
