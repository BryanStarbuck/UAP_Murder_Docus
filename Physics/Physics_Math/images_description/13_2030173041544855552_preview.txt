PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This is a critical assembly step: the toroidal roller magnets (3-phase wrapped) are being positioned in the precision-machined annular race grooves of the acrylic stator disc. The blue tape indicates these are the phase-U or phase-V wound rollers. The double concentric groove design allows two rings of rollers to precess around the stator — the inner and outer rings create superimposed rotating magnetic field patterns. The precise groove geometry (notched profile) maintains constant gap between roller outer surface and groove wall, which is critical for maintaining consistent magnetic coupling geometry as the rollers orbit.
