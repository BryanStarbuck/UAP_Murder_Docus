PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This is an explicit 3-phase toroidal magnet roller ring schematic for the SEG. The three colors (blue/red/yellow = U/V/W or equivalent) show the phase assignment of each roller in the ring. For a 9-roller ring with 3-phase symmetry, rollers are grouped as 3 sets of 3, with each set wound at a 120° phase offset. This creates a rotating magnetic field (RMF) analogous to a 3-phase AC motor's stator field, but here generated by permanent magnets with specific magnetization patterns rather than current-carrying windings.
* The internal black-gray banding of each roller represents the multi-layer axial magnetization of the Searl-type roller: each roller is magnetized with multiple concentric rings of alternating North/South polarity across its cross-section. This creates a complex radial and axial field profile that differs from a simple dipole magnet.
* The 9-roller configuration (as opposed to the 6-roller, 10-12 roller, or 14-16 roller configurations seen elsewhere in this series) is an intermediate design step. The 3-fold symmetry is preserved (9 = 3×3), ensuring the RMF has no preferred azimuthal bias.
* Key physics: The rotating magnetic field generated by this roller arrangement, combined with the interaction between roller magnetic moments and the stator ring's magnetization, is proposed in Searl's theory to create a net electromagnetic force accelerating the roller ring into self-sustained rotation and generating an EMF across the stator.
