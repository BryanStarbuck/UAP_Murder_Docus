This creates a symmetric 3-phase configuration in the toroidal roller ring.

The roller ring or an inner element appears to be in motion. This is the first clear evidence of the SEG assembly actually operating (rotating).

The spinning chrome collar visible here may represent either the inner stator ring spinning (with the outer roller ring stationary) or the outer roller assembly in motion relative to a fixed inner element.
