PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This direct axial view provides the clearest documentation of the 6-roller 3-phase configuration: all 6 rollers and their U/V/W assignments are visible simultaneously. The "1" suffix distinction (U1/W1) confirms the two rollers per phase are individually labeled, useful for independent measurement or differential sensing of each phase's individual roller.
* The chrome collar is in rapid rotation — this is another dynamic operating frame. The tapered/cone shape at center combined with the orange bearing ball creates a geometrically significant focal point. In SEG theory, the bearing ball serves as the pivot/contact point for the rotating system, and the chrome collar's rapid spin is consistent with the initial drive phase of an electromagnetic test.
* The direct axial view shows the 6-fold rotational symmetry of the roller ring explicitly — the rollers are equidistantly spaced at 60° intervals, and the U/V/W phases are evenly distributed at 120° intervals.
