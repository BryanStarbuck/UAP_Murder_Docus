PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This is an early-stage assembly of the precision-machined acrylic SEG stator disc with the inner ball bearing race installed. The 8 small disc magnets placed around the bearing outer race are positioned as the initial prototype of the inner roller ring — the same concept as the chrome toroidal washer ring seen in 13_2029029446406291461 but here using individual disc magnets placed on an acrylic disc with the bearing race providing the running track.
* The acrylic disc serves as both the structural substrate and the transparent housing for the assembly, allowing visual observation of the bearing and magnet positions during testing.
* The 8 magnets at 45° spacing represent a different count from the 3-phase (6-roller) series — this sub-assembly may be a preliminary prototype before the full 6-roller system was developed, or it represents an inner ring with different count (8) nested inside an outer ring with a different count (as in a multi-ring Searl configuration with coprime or harmonically related roller counts).
* The ball bearing race provides the low-friction orbital track for the disc magnets to roll around. In Searl's design, the rollers orbit the stator ring on a magnetic bearing — here the experimenter is using a conventional ball bearing as the mechanical track.
