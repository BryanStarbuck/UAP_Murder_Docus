PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This close-up reveals important construction detail: the bronze/brass hub element at the spindle base serves as the lower bearing seat for the spindle ball. The brass material is soft enough to conform to the ball bearing contact geometry over time, providing a self-centering support for the rotating ball. This is an intentional design choice — the harder chrome ball rolling in a softer brass seat creates a near-frictionless contact with good conformance.
* The dried white material on the chrome flange suggests the experimenter has used adhesive, RTV silicone, or wax as a sealant or securing compound between the flange and adjacent components — not unusual for a prototype build where parts need to be temporarily fixed for testing.
