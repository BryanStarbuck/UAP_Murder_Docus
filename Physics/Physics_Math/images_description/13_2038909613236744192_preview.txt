PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This is a significantly more refined and larger version of the roller ring sub-assembly first seen in 13_2033809814065975296. The 14-15 disc magnets arranged around the bearing represent the outer roller ring. The bearing serves as the low-friction rolling track — as the magnets orbit the bearing, they roll on the bearing's outer race surface.
* The "40" marking may indicate a test number (test #40), a magnet count parameter, a measurement value (40 mV, 40 Hz), or a diameter specification (40 cm — which may have been the originally planned disc diameter).
* With 14-15 magnets around the ring, the count is close to the 14-16 roller count shown in the primary SEG schematic (13_2028704773655207938). If these are 15 magnets, 3-phase symmetry requires a multiple of 3 — 15 = 5×3, giving 5 magnets per phase with 72° separation per phase sequence. This is a valid 3-phase symmetric configuration.
* The larger scale (35-40 cm diameter) versus the earlier 20-25 cm acrylic disc (13_2033809814065975296) represents a direct scale-up — larger disc diameter increases the magnetic flux linkage per unit rotation and the potential output power.
