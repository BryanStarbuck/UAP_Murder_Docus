PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This is the precision-machined acrylic SEG stator disc — the central static structural element in the Searl Effect Generator assembly. The two concentric notched ring races serve as the mechanical running tracks for the disc magnet rollers:
  - Outer notched race: engages the outer diameter of the large outer-ring rollers
  - Inner notched race: engages the inner roller ring (if a two-ring configuration) or serves as a secondary alignment feature
* The notched/serrated profile of the race grooves serves multiple functions: (1) it constrains the roller magnets laterally, preventing axial wandering; (2) it can create periodic magnetic coupling pulses as the roller disc teeth engage the race notches — analogous to a magnetic gearwheel interaction; (3) the notch periodicity sets a characteristic frequency for the EMF generated as rollers traverse the race.
* The central aluminum spindle serves as the rotation axis — the rollers orbit around this spindle. The spindle also functions as the current collection point in the homopolar generator geometry (center electrode).
* The drilled hole grid in the disc interior reduces mass (for dynamic balance at high RPM) and may serve as a dielectric pattern affecting the local electric field distribution.
