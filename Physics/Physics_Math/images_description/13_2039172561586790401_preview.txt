PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This frame shows the most heavily instrumented state of the SEG roller ring assembly yet documented — with all phase-labeled rollers connected by color-coded leads and a Faraday pickup coil encircling the assembly.
* The 3-phase winding designation (U/V/W) on roller labels follows IEC 60034 convention. With 9 rollers at 3 per phase, each phase group subtends 3 × 40° = 120° of arc, producing a geometrically symmetric 3-phase rotating magnetic field as the rollers orbit the stator.
* The Faraday pickup coil (circular wire loop around the full assembly) measures the total time-varying magnetic flux through the enclosed area: EMF = -dΦ/dt. As the roller ring rotates, the net magnetic flux through the loop changes periodically, producing an induced EMF proportional to rotation rate and total magnetic flux amplitude.
* The per-roller output leads (red/blue/white) suggest each roller also has direct electrical connections — these could be for:
  (1) Externally driving the rollers with AC current to produce motor torque (motoring mode)
  (2) Collecting EMF generated inductively in each roller's winding as it passes through the stator field (generator mode)
  (3) Measuring the local magnetic field at each roller position via Hall-effect sensors embedded in the rollers
* The ATX PSU at upper left continues to provide the primary DC power rail for instrumentation and any electronic drive circuitry.
* The orange bearing ball at the spindle base is the center-tap electrode contact point for the homopolar generator measurement geometry.
