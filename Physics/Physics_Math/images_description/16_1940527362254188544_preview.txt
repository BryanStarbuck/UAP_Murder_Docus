PHYSICS / PHENOMENON:
* This is a standard gimbal-mounted gyroscope visualization. A gyroscope with two or more gimbals has freedom to precess in any direction while maintaining the angular momentum direction of the spinning flywheel. Key physics:
  - Conservation of angular momentum: L = Iω (I = moment of inertia, ω = angular velocity). The spinning flywheel maintains its orientation in space regardless of how the outer frame rotates.
  - Gyroscopic stability and precession: τ = dL/dt; when a torque is applied, the gyroscope precesses perpendicular to the applied force.
  - Applications: inertial navigation, stabilization platforms, attitude control in spacecraft.
* In UAP physics research, gyroscopes are central to: Eric Laithwaite's claimed gyroscopic weight reduction experiments, Woodward/Mach Effect transient mass fluctuation in rotating masses, and gyroscopic inertial propulsion concepts.
* The gold flywheel disk matches the high-density flywheel design needed for maximum angular momentum storage.
