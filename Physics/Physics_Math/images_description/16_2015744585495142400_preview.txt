PHYSICS / PHENOMENON:
* This is a cross-sectional schematic of a three-phase AC induction motor or synchronous motor. The diagram shows:
  - Stator: the outer ring of colored segments with ⊗/⊙ current direction indicators, representing three phases of windings (3 colors = 3 phases). The stator creates a rotating magnetic field when fed with 3-phase AC current.
  - Rotating magnetic field: represented by the red ring and red dots (poles of the rotating field).
  - Rotor: the inner disk that rotates due to the electromagnetic torque from the stator's rotating field.
  - Three-phase coil paths: the three overlapping sinusoidal loops on the diagram trace each phase's winding path through the stator slots.
* This diagram is the theoretical basis for the experimental device in companion images: the 8-element polyphase electromagnet ring driven by VFD controllers IS a 3-phase motor stator — the same topology shown here but in a custom geometry.

A cross-sectional educational diagram of a three-phase AC induction/synchronous motor showing stator (3-phase windings in color-coded segments with current direction indicators ⊗/⊙), the rotating magnetic field region (red ring with pole markers), the rotor disk, and three overlaid sinusoidal coil paths.
