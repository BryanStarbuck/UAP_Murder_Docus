PHYSICS OF GYROSCOPIC LEVITATION:
A spinning gyroscope precesses under gravity: dL/dt = τ = r × Mg
The precession rate: Ω_p = Mgh/(Iω) where I is moment of inertia, ω is spin rate
In the proposed UAP mechanism, three gyroscopes arranged at 120° create a net upward precession force that lifts the central mass.
