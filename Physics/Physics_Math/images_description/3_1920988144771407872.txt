PHYSICS INTERPRETATION:
1. VESICA PISCIS / MAGNETIC DIPOLE: The horizontal eye shape is the vesica piscis — the intersection region of two circles whose centers are each on the other's perimeter. This encodes the geometry of a magnetic dipole field at the equatorial plane: the "pinch point" between two opposing poles
2. OBLATE SPHEROID / DISC: The horizontal ellipse represents the cross-sectional profile of an oblate spheroid (flattened sphere) — the shape of a spinning disc under centrifugal force. UAP discs are typically oblate spheroids
3. TOROIDAL CROSS-SECTION: The eye shape in vertical cross-section is the cross-section of a torus at the equatorial plane — the "hole" of the donut appears as a vesica piscis when the torus is sliced
4. SEGMENTED ELECTRODE DISC (BILATERAL): The two large solid petal regions (top and bottom) with the central eye resemble a Corbino disc with only two electrode segments — a bipolar (DC) rather than multipolar (AC) configuration
5. FISH BLADDER / VESICA PISCIS SACRED GEOMETRY: The vesica piscis has irrational ratio √3:1 (height:width of the lens shape) — related to the hexagonal close-packing constant. Its area is (π/2 - √3/2)r² for unit circles
6. CROP FORMATION ENCODING: This design, if laid in a field, would appear as a large horizontal oval element flanked by crescent forms within a scalloped outer ring — a distinctly different visual signature from the radially symmetric companion design
