PHYSICS RELEVANCE:
A homopolar (Faraday) generator is a DC electrical generator that operates through continuous rotation of a conducting disc in a uniform magnetic field aligned with the rotation axis. Key physics:
- The EMF generated is: ε = ½ωBr² (where ω is angular velocity, B is field strength, r is disc radius)
- No commutation needed — produces true DC with no ripple
- First demonstrated by Michael Faraday in 1831
In UAP physics research, homopolar generators are highly significant because:
1. They are central to many free energy device claims (some researchers claim correctly-configured homopolar generators can exceed conventional energy conservation predictions)
2. They produce unidirectional current without slip-ring losses in principle
3. The rotating magnetic field topology is connected to inertial mass reduction claims (spinning strong magnetic fields may affect local spacetime curvature according to some researchers)
4. Norman Paulsen's claimed UAP propulsion description (seen in companion text images) specifically mentions "two magnetic discs rotating in opposite directions" — matching a counter-rotating homopolar generator configuration
