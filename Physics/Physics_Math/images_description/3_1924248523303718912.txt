PHYSICS/SYMBOLIC INTERPRETATION:
This is a more complex variant of the "central bullseye + outer satellite ring + downward tail" crop circle design seen in 3_1924246862464172032.jpg. Here the outer ring uses 8 groups of two circles (paired satellites) rather than individual elements, and the tail uses a large outline circle as the first element. The 8-fold outer symmetry (rather than 12-fold) is significant: 8-fold symmetry relates to octagonal geometry, and in the context of UAP disc propulsion with 12 magnets, the 8-fold here may represent a different configuration. The paired satellites (one inside, one outside the ring) may represent the bipolar nature of each magnet position (north and south poles).
