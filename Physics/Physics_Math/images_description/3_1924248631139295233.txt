PHYSICS/UAP RELEVANCE:
Ground-level documentation of crop circle interiors is used by researchers to document the physical characteristics of the flattened crop. Key research findings from such documentation include: elongated plant nodes (nodes on stems), crystallized plant material at nodes, changes in germination rates of seeds from affected plants, and in some formations, the absence of soil displacement that would occur if humans with boards flattened the crop. The central mound feature is particularly noted, as mechanical flattening (boards walked over crop) would not produce this. This type of ground photography complements aerial photography in the evidentiary record.
