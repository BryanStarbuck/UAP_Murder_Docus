PHYSICS/UAP RELEVANCE:
Edward Leedskalnin (1887-1951) was the mysterious builder of Coral Castle in Florida who claimed to have rediscovered the secrets of how the ancient Egyptians built the pyramids using a device he called a "magnetic flywheel." He built his Coral Castle alone, moving enormous coral blocks, and left behind cryptic writings on "Magnetic Current" and "The Sweet 16" describing a rotating magnetic device. His "magnetic flywheel" concept involves counter-rotating magnets producing directional magnetic forces — directly connecting to the Norman Paulsen "two magnetic discs rotating in opposite directions" UAP propulsion description. The appearance of this design in a crop circle near Honey Street, Wiltshire (2011) is cited as evidence that the crop circle intelligence was encoding Leedskalnin's suppressed physics knowledge.
