STATISTICAL INTERPRETATION:
The Kaplan-Meier survival estimator S(t) is a non-parametric statistic estimating the survival function from lifetime data. Each downward step in the curve represents one or more deaths/events at that time point. The area between the two curves represents the cumulative difference in survival probability over time. The separation between the red (treatment) and blue (placebo) curves, maintained over the full ~6000-day (~16-year) follow-up period, indicates a sustained mortality benefit of the intervention.

The data depicted corresponds to the KiSel-10 study or its long-term follow-up — a Swedish randomized controlled trial of selenium (200 μg/day) plus CoQ10 (200 mg/day) supplementation in elderly subjects (mean age ~77-78 years at baseline). The study demonstrated statistically significant cardiovascular mortality reduction. The 6000-day follow-up corresponds to approximately 16 years total observation period, consistent with published KiSel-10 extension studies.
