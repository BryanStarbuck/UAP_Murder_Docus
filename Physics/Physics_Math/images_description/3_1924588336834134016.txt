PHYSICS NOTES ON DOUBLE RAINBOW:
The secondary rainbow forms from light undergoing two internal reflections within each water droplet (rather than one reflection for the primary). This reverses the color order and places the arc at approximately 51° from the antisolar point (vs. 42° for the primary red). The two-reflection path also attenuates more light, making the secondary rainbow dimmer. Alexander's dark band (the darker region between the arcs) results from the absence of reflected light in the angular range between the two arcs.
