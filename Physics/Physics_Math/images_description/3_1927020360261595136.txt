PHYSICS/UAP RELEVANCE:
The wormhole-as-communication-channel is a genuine topic in theoretical physics. Morris and Thorne (1988) proposed traversable wormholes as potential faster-than-light communication channels. Maldacena and Susskind's ER=EPR conjecture (2013) proposes that Einstein-Rosen bridges and quantum entanglement (Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen correlations) are the same phenomenon at different scales — suggesting that wormholes may be the physical substrate of quantum entanglement. This has profound implications for communication and information theory in quantum gravity.
