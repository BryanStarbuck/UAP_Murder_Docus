PHYSICS/UAP RELEVANCE:
This extreme close-up provides the highest detail view of the circuit-board motif and script characters. The precision of the concentric IC chip squares, the regularity of the radiating traces, and the neat termination in copper/bronze pins are inconsistent with purely decorative/artistic production — they suggest functional intent in the design. The unknown script surrounding the IC motif has not been identified with any known human writing system (neither ancient nor modern alphabets, Phoenician, proto-Sinaitic, Sanskrit, Arabic, Hebrew, nor any other catalogued script). The combination of functional-looking electronic circuit symbolism with an unknown script on a metallic sphere is one of the most anomalous physical artifact features documented in this UAP physics research series.
