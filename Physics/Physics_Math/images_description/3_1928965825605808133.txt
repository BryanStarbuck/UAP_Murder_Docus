HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The Barbury Castle formation of July 17, 1991 (near Barbury Castle Iron Age hillfort, Wiltshire) is one of the most historically significant crop formations in the research literature:
1. It appeared overnight on July 16-17, 1991
2. The triangular arrangement with three distinctive satellite symbols (plain disc, pinwheel/propeller, yin-yang) was interpreted by multiple researchers as encoding physical constants and geometric relationships
3. The formation was one of the most complex and geometrically precise formations documented up to that point in time
4. Various researchers have decoded the triangle dimensions as encoding π, the golden ratio φ, and other mathematical constants
