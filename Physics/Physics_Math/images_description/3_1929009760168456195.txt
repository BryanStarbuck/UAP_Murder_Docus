INTERPRETIVE PHYSICS:
The systematic RM gradient across the jet width is the observational signature of a helical magnetic field in the jet:
- A helical B field has a toroidal (azimuthal) component that changes sign across the jet diameter
- This creates a systematic variation in the line-of-sight B component (which drives Faraday rotation)
- The reversal of the RM sign between jet and counterjet is a relativistic effect: the jet moves toward the observer (Doppler boosted) while the counterjet moves away (Doppler deboosted), but the observed RM sign depends on the direction of v × B relative to the line of sight
