ARCHAEOLOGICAL SIGNIFICANCE OF FALKNER'S CIRCLE:
Falkner's Circle is one of many megalithic stone circles in the Wiltshire region that have been largely or entirely destroyed through agriculture, construction, and stone-robbing over the centuries. Its original diameter and stone count (10 or 12) are uncertain due to the extensive destruction. The June 8, 2025 crop formation near its ruins may be interpreted (by the researcher) as a symbolic comment on the destruction of ancient megalithic knowledge sites by modern civilization.
