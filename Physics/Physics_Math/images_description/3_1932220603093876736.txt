The 11-year solar cycle (Schwabe cycle) is a fundamental periodicity in the Sun's magnetic field activity — it represents the time for the Sun's global magnetic field to complete one polarity reversal cycle (a full 22-year Hale cycle = two consecutive 11-year cycles with opposite magnetic polarity)

The underlying mechanism: the Sun's differential rotation (equator rotates faster than poles — period ~25 days at equator vs. ~35 days at poles) progressively wraps and amplifies the global dipole field into a toroidal field configuration (ω-effect), which eventually becomes unstable (Parker instability / magnetic buoyancy) and erupts as active regions and sunspots. This is the solar αΩ dynamo model
