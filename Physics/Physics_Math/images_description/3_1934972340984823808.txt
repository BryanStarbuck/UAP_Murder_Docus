PHYSICS CONTENT — FARADAY'S LAW:
The diagram illustrates Faraday's law of electromagnetic induction in integral form:
∮_C E · dl = -d/dt ∫∫_S B · dA = -dΦ_B/dt

Where:
- Φ_B = ∫∫_S B · n̂ dA is the magnetic flux through surface S
- The left side is the EMF (electromotive force) around the closed loop C
- The right side is the negative time derivative of the magnetic flux
- A time-varying B(t) inside the solenoid induces a circulating electric field E outside (and inside)
- The induced EMF drives current in any conductor placed along loop C
