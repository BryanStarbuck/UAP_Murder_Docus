PHYSICS/UAP RELEVANCE — PhD PHYSICIST LEVEL:
Gyroscopic precession equation: dL/dt = τ, where L = Iω (angular momentum), τ = r × F (torque). Precession angular velocity Ω_p = τ/|L|sin(θ). For a uniform disc: I = MR²/2. The claim that gyroscopic action creates net upward force violates Newton's third law in the classical case — the gyroscope precesses but does not generate net lift in Newtonian mechanics. However, Hayasaka and Takeuchi (1989) reported anomalous weight reduction for spinning gyroscopes, and Podkletnov (1992) reported weight reduction above spinning superconductors. These experimental claims (disputed but never fully refuted) form the basis for the gyroscopic UAP propulsion hypothesis. The 6-blade turbine matches the optimal blade count for minimum hydrodynamic drag in rotating disc flow (related to Ekman layer pumping in rotating fluid dynamics).
