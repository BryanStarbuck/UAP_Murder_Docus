PHYSICS/UAP RELEVANCE — PhD PHYSICIST LEVEL:
The Nimitz FLIR1 event (November 14, 2004) is the most rigorously documented UAP case:
1. The object demonstrated NO conventional propulsion signatures (no heat plume, no turbulent wake, no acoustic signature)
2. The object performed instantaneous acceleration from hover to Mach 1+ (estimated by pilot Lt. Cdr. David Fravor and corroborated by radar operators)
3. The object showed transmedium capability (confirmed by radar tracking to have descended from 80,000 ft to 50 ft above ocean surface in under 1 second — implied hypersonic deceleration)
4. The object was simultaneously tracked by: AN/SPY-1 shipboard radar, AN/APG-73 aircraft radar, AN/ASQ-228 ATFLIR targeting pod
5. The Aegis combat system's radar cross section (RCS) data showed an object 20-40 feet in length with zero radar-reflective structure
