PHYSICS INTERPRETATION:
1. LENZ EFFECT LEVITATION: The Lenz effect is electromagnetic induction opposing the cause that produces it (Faraday/Lenz law: V = -dΦ/dt). If a conducting disc is placed in a rapidly changing magnetic field, eddy currents are induced in the disc, and the interaction between the induced currents and the applied field creates a REPULSIVE FORCE (Lenz levitation). The formation encodes this principle: the 6-pole 3-phase stator creates a rotating magnetic field; the disc (rotor) is levitated and spun by the Lenz effect
2. THREE-PHASE ROTATING MAGNETIC FIELD: Six coils at 60° spacing, connected in three pairs (A, B, C phases at 120° phase shifts) create a ROTATING MAGNETIC FIELD that sweeps continuously around the stator. The rotor spins synchronously with this rotating field — the operating principle of a 3-phase induction motor
3. SPINNING CORBINO DISC AS UAP DRIVE: The Corbino disc at the center (a flat disc geometry where radial current flow in an axial magnetic field creates azimuthal force) is spun by the 3-phase rotating field. At high RPM (10,000+ rev/s), the spinning disc + magnetic field creates enough gyroscopic stability and electromagnetic thrust for levitation
4. FLAT COIL + ROTATING FIELD = MAGLEV: The combination of a flat 1-phase coil (upper-right schematic) + 3-phase rotating field (6-coil stator) creates the exact electromagnetic configuration used in MAGLEV trains — the flat coil provides lift force, the 3-phase field provides propulsion and guidance
