PHYSICS/UAP RELEVANCE:
The location significance: crop formations near white horse geoglyphs in Wiltshire are a recurring pattern. The white horses are ancient or semi-ancient landscape art, cut into chalk hills — the UAP/crop circle community has noted that formations frequently appear in fields adjacent to or visible from white horse sites (Milk Hill, Alton Barnes, Pewsey Valley, Hackpen Hill). Whether this proximity is accidental, culturally motivated by human makers choosing visually impressive locations, or intentionally chosen by UAP-connected creators as a reference to ancient landscape art traditions is debated.
