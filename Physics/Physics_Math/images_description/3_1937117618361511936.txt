BOOK CONTENT SUMMARY:
"Hair of the Alien" documents a 1988 case in which an Australian man claimed a UAP encounter. A hair sample was recovered and subjected to DNA analysis by molecular biologist Dr. Kary Mullis (Nobel laureate for PCR invention) and others. The DNA analysis reportedly showed anomalous sequences — specifically a hybrid of two different DNA types that had never been seen together in any known human population. The book documents: the encounter details, chain of custody for the hair sample, laboratory DNA analysis methodology, and the reported anomalous findings.

PHYSICS/SCIENCE SIGNIFICANCE:
1. FORENSIC UAP EVIDENCE: This book represents one of the most scientifically rigorous attempts to analyze alleged physical evidence from a UAP encounter using standard forensic molecular biology
2. PCR ANALYSIS METHODOLOGY: The hair sample was analyzed using Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) — the same technique used in criminal forensics, disease diagnosis, and population genetics. If the methodology was sound, anomalous results would represent genuine scientific evidence
3. MITOCHONDRIAL DNA ANOMALY: The reported finding was an unusual human mtDNA sequence (HVR1 region) that appeared to combine two normally mutually exclusive haplogroup lineages — suggesting either: laboratory contamination, a previously unknown human population, or non-standard biological origin
4. HAIR AS BIOLOGICAL SAMPLE: Hair shafts contain: nuclear DNA in follicle cells (if recently extracted), mitochondrial DNA in all cells, and protein sequences in the hair shaft itself. Each layer can be analyzed independently — the book documents multi-level analysis
