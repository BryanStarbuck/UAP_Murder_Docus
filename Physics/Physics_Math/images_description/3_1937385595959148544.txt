PHYSICS INTERPRETATION:
1. PRECESSION OF THE EQUINOXES: The "turning wheel of the night sky" refers to Earth's axial precession (period 25,772 years) — the slow wobble that shifts the celestial pole and moves the vernal equinox through the zodiac. The overlapping circles in the disc encode the ECLIPTIC CIRCLE, the CELESTIAL EQUATOR, and the intersection of these great circles
2. CANOPUS ENCODING: Canopus (α Carinae) was the "star of Ēa" in Babylonian astronomy. It is the second brightest star in the sky (−0.74 magnitude), located 310 light-years away. Ancient navigators used Canopus as a southern celestial pole marker (stars of Ēa = stars of the southern sky)
3. 14-YEAR CROP CIRCLE PAIR: If the 2011 and 2025 formations are deliberately related, 14 years = approximately 1 full cycle of Jupiter's orbital period (11.86 years × 1.18 ≈ 14) or Saturn's half-period (29.46/2 ≈ 14.7)
