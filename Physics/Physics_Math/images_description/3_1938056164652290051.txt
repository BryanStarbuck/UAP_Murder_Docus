PHYSICS / PHENOMENON:
* This is the decoded interpretation of the crop formation near Torino Airport (Italy, June 23, 2015) — a formation nearly identical to the large complex one in 3_1938000267783639043. The researcher RC (with thanks to Silvio and Valeria) has decoded the binary-ASCII encoding in the formation's chevron/triangle ring and obtained the Latin phrase "timeo ET ferentes" — a modification of the famous Latin warning "timeo Danaos et dona ferentes" ("beware of Greeks bearing gifts," from Virgil's Aeneid).
* The decoded message "timeo ET ferentes" translates as "beware of extraterrestrials bearing gifts" — with "ET" replacing "Danaos" (Greeks). If this decoding is accurate, it represents a deliberate warning message encoded in a crop formation using ASCII binary code, directed at all humans on Earth.
* This claim is highly significant in the UAP research context:
  (1) It would confirm that at least some crop circles are not made by humans — no human artist would encode a Latin pun about extraterrestrials in ASCII binary, then leave it undisclosed for years.
  (2) It represents a form of first contact communication — an entity capable of creating geometrically precise formations at night also encoding warning messages in human-readable ASCII.
  (3) The specific message echoes the 2002 Crabwood formation decoded message: "Beware the bearers of FALSE gifts & their BROKEN PROMISES" — suggesting a consistent non-human source with a consistent warning theme.
* From a physics standpoint, the ability to lay down an ASCII-encoded binary pattern in a crop field at night — with character-level precision across a 100-meter diameter — would require either: (a) sophisticated automated physical manipulation equipment (robotic planking system with GPS precision), or (b) a directed energy system capable of selective crop flattening at centimeter precision over large areas.
* The Torino Airport proximity is noted — crop formations near airports have occasionally been documented, raising questions about whether proximity to electromagnetic navigation systems is coincidental or causal.
* Disclaimer: the ASCII decoding methodology used here has not been independently peer-reviewed and should be evaluated with appropriate skepticism. The encoding interpretation depends on which segments of the formation are treated as "1" vs. "0," and the boundaries of each "character" in the ring.
