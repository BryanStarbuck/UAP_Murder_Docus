PHYSICS / PHENOMENON:
* This line drawing depicts the Sumerian god Ea (also called Enki), the deity of water, wisdom, magic, crafts, and creation. Ea/Enki is a central figure in the "stars of Ea" archaeoastronomical research documented in companion images.
* The globe-like object held by the deity is particularly significant in UAP physics research contexts:
  (1) Some researchers interpret the sphere with internal divisions as a representation of a planet (Earth) or celestial body — arguing that Ea is depicted as a "creator" holding the Earth.
  (2) Others interpret the sphere as a navigational device, a "cosmic map," or a technological artifact — arguing it represents advanced knowledge of spherical geometry that early humans would not have possessed without outside (extraterrestrial) assistance.
  (3) The spherical object with internal cross/division lines could also represent a gyroscope, an armillary sphere (celestial sphere with coordinate rings), or an orrery (mechanical planetary model) — all technologies that would have been anachronistic for 2500 BCE if this is indeed what is depicted.
* The connection to the "stars of Ea" research (3_1937646604514918400 et al.) is that Ea/Enki was the patron deity of astronomers and mathematical knowledge in Sumerian culture, and that the specific stars attributed to Ea in cuneiform astronomical texts (particularly the Mul.Apin series) are what the Italian crop formations are claimed to encode.
* Ea/Enki's association with the "Abzu" (primordial freshwater ocean beneath the Earth) has also been connected by some UAP researchers to the "water" or "fluid" propulsion medium proposed for MHD (magnetohydrodynamic) drives — an ancient mythological encoding, they argue, of a real physics concept.
