* The Chilbolton Observatory is particularly significant in crop circle research because, in August 2001 (one year after this 2000 formation), two more formations appeared in the adjacent field — the "Chilbolton face" and the "Arecibo reply" (the latter purportedly encoding a response to the 1974 Arecibo message sent by Carl Sagan's team). The 2000 formation documented here predates those famous 2001 formations.
* The title calling it an E.T. "antenna" references the proximity of the formation to the radio telescope — suggesting the crop formation researchers interpret it as:
  (1) A message directed at the radio observatory — non-human intelligence choosing to place its "messages" adjacent to humanity's most prominent SETI (Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence) listening station.
  (2) A representation of an extraterrestrial communication device (an "antenna" schematic) as a crop formation.
* From a physics perspective, placing crop formations adjacent to a radio observatory is significant because:
  (a) Radio telescopes operate at very specific frequencies and orientations — if the formation placement is intentional, it suggests awareness of the telescope's capabilities.
  (b) The August 13, 2000 date is within the Perseid meteor shower peak — a time when radio telescopes may be observing increased ionospheric activity from meteoric ionization.
  (c) The proximity raises the question of whether the formation was created by a plasma-based phenomenon (e.g., ionospheric discharge, ball lightning) that may also interact with the radio telescope's signals.
* The Chilbolton 2001 "Arecibo reply" formation encoded (according to researchers): a response to the binary-encoded Arecibo message, showing changes to the DNA encoding (silicon added to the carbon/hydrogen/oxygen/nitrogen/phosphorus bases), different human figure dimensions, a different population number, and a different solar system configuration. If genuine, this would represent the most scientifically significant crop formation evidence.
