PHYSICS / PHENOMENON:
* This is the "lunar phases" or "crescent moon" crop circle — a well-known formation type in the UK crop circle record. The ring of crescent shapes encodes the phases of the Moon:
  (1) If there are 28-30 crescents, they represent 28-30 days of the lunar month — one crescent per day.
  (2) The crescent shape itself is the iconic representation of the Moon's horns (crescent phase).
  (3) The central concentric bull's-eye represents either the Full Moon (the disk at its brightest) or the Sun (the Earth-Moon-Sun system center).
* In UAP physics and archaeoastronomy contexts, lunar phase encoding in crop formations is interpreted as: (a) a calendrical reference — the formation was created on a specific lunar phase date and encodes that phase, (b) a reference to lunar gravitational cycles — the Moon's gravity creates a well-documented time-varying gravitational field on Earth, and some UAP propulsion theories involve coupling to lunar gravitomagnetic effects, or (c) symbolic reference to the rotating magnetic disc (the crescent shapes representing the "phase" position of rotating elements).
* The 18-20 crescent count is significant: 18 lunations = 18 months ≈ 1.5 years; 19 lunations = the Metonic cycle (19 years' repetition of lunar/solar calendar), a number known to ancient astronomers including the Babylonians who used it for calendar synchronization.
* The crop formation's compact size and clear aerial shape make it one of the more photographically striking in the series.
