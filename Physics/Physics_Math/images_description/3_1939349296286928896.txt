PHYSICS / PHENOMENON:
* The regular octahedron is one of the five Platonic solids (the others being tetrahedron, cube, dodecahedron, icosahedron). A Platonic solid has all faces as congruent regular polygons and all vertex angles equal. The octahedron has: 8 equilateral triangular faces, 12 edges, 6 vertices. It is dual to the cube (each vertex of the cube corresponds to a face of the octahedron and vice versa).
* Octahedral geometry in physics:
  (1) Crystal structure: the octahedral coordination site is the fundamental building block of close-packed face-centered cubic (FCC) lattices. In FCC metals and ionic crystals, atoms occupy octahedral holes between close-packed layers. The octahedron represents the local coordination environment of many transition metal complexes (ligand field theory: octahedral crystal field splitting Δ_oct).
  (2) Electromagnetic field topology: the electric field of a 3D dipole has octahedral symmetry elements. Three mutually orthogonal dipoles (along x, y, z axes) create a field pattern with full octahedral symmetry Oh.
  (3) Rotation group: the rotation group of the octahedron is the same as that of the cube — the octahedral group O, of order 24 (or Oh with inversion, order 48). This group is isomorphic to S4 (the symmetric group on 4 elements). In quantum mechanics, octahedral symmetry appears in the crystal field Hamiltonian for d-orbital splitting.
  (4) UAP propulsion relevance: the octahedron's 6-vertex structure maps to a 6-axis electromagnetic field control system — three orthogonal axes, each with +/− poles, giving 6 independent force vector directions. A craft with octahedral field geometry could generate propulsive force in any direction by modulating the 6 vertex field amplitudes. This is more symmetric than the 3-generator (120° spacing) Bob Lazar Sport Model but represents a different design philosophy.
  (5) Net projection: the net of a 3D solid (its unfolded 2D surface) encodes the complete topological information about the solid. Different nets can fold to the same solid. The octahedron has 11 distinct nets. The crop formation's researcher RC argues that the West Overton 1999 formation specifically encodes the standard "strip + 2 tabs" net layout, implying detailed knowledge of polyhedral combinatorics by the formation creators.

The regular octahedron can be constructed by placing two square pyramids base-to-base — a morphology directly analogous to the double-disc UAP propulsion configuration (Norman Paulsen: two counter-rotating discs).
