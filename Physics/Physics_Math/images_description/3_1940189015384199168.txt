PHYSICS / PHENOMENON:
* The Barbury Castle 1991 crop formation is one of the most historically significant and widely analyzed formations in the crop circle record. It appeared on July 17, 1991 near Barbury Castle (an Iron Age hillfort north of Swindon, Wiltshire). The formation's 3-fold symmetric design with central hub + three satellite circles at 120° has been interpreted by multiple researchers as:
  (1) Bob Lazar Sport Model propulsion: the three satellite circles (at 120°, connected by thin radials to the central hub) directly map to the three gravity wave generators (GWGs) at 120° spacing around the central reactor hub — the propulsion configuration described by Bob Lazar for the S-4 Sport Model disc. See 3_1939105715932532736 for the technical schematic.
  (2) Three-phase rotating magnetic field: three generators at 120° is the configuration of a 3-phase AC motor stator — the three satellite circles represent the three phase windings (A, B, C) and the central concentric ring system represents the rotating magnetic field they collectively generate in the central rotor region.
  (3) Pinwheel satellite encoding: the three satellite circles have different internal geometries:
    - Upper: double-circle / yin-yang (representing counter-rotation or dual-polarity).
    - Lower-left: quartered circle with open section (Pac-Man geometry, representing 270° field arc or 3/4 duty cycle).
    - Right: 3-blade pinwheel/triskele (representing 3-phase field rotation).
    These three different internal designs may encode different operational parameters of the three generators (phase offset, power level, or field geometry).
  (4) Alchemical interpretation: the formation's 3 elements with different geometries (sun circle, moon circle, and ratchet/pinwheel) have been connected to the alchemical triad of Mercury, Sulfur, and Salt — the three principles of alchemical transformation. The central concentric rings represent the alchemical fire or the philosopher's stone.
  (5) Sacred geometry: the 3-fold triangular arrangement with circles at vertices encodes the equilateral triangle inscribed in the outer boundary circle, the Sierpinski triangle at the first iteration, and the Borromean ring topology.
* Historical significance: the Barbury Castle formation appeared in the same summer (1991) that saw an unprecedented wave of complex UK crop formations. Its 3-fold design was extensively analyzed by researchers including John Martineau and Michael Glickman, who identified connections to the tetrahedral number 3, the threefold nature of many spiritual traditions, and the geometry of 3-phase electrical systems.

The formation shows a large central concentric ring system (~4 rings + filled central disc) with three radial lines at 120° spacing connecting to three satellite circles: an upper double/yin-yang circle, a lower-left quartered Pac-Man circle, and a right 3-blade pinwheel circle. This 3-fold symmetric configuration (central hub + three satellites at 120°) directly maps to the Bob Lazar Sport Model 3-gravity-wave-generator propulsion topology, the 3-phase AC rotating magnetic field stator geometry, and the Borromean ring threefold coupling topology.
