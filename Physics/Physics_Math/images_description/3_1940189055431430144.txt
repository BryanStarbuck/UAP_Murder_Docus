PHYSICS / PHENOMENON:
* This slide connects the Barbury Castle formation to gyroscope physics — specifically the precession behavior of a spinning disc under applied torque. The key physics:
  (1) Gyroscope precession: a spinning disc with angular momentum L = Iω (I = moment of inertia, ω = angular velocity vector) experiences precession when a torque τ is applied perpendicular to L. The precession angular velocity is Ω_p = τ/L = τ/(Iω). The precession direction is perpendicular to both L and τ (Ω_p = τ × L / |L|²).
  (2) Three satellite elements = three gyroscope states: RC's interpretation of the Barbury Castle formation through gyroscope physics:
    - Upper satellite (pinwheel/triskele): represents the spinning gyroscope with torque applied — the 3-blade pinwheel represents the three-phase rotation of the precessing system.
    - Lower-left satellite (large circle with dot): represents the gyroscope at rest (vertical, no precession) — the large circle is the disc, the central dot is the spin axis.
    - Lower-right satellite (partial circle/Pac-Man): represents the gyroscope at an intermediate tilt angle — the partial-arc element represents the portion of the full rotation cycle that is active.
  (3) Counter-rotating disc propulsion / gyroscopic stabilization: in UAP propulsion systems (Norman Paulsen: "two magnetic discs rotating in opposite directions"), the gyroscopic effect of two counter-rotating discs is crucial for:
    - Stability: counter-rotation cancels net angular momentum (L_total = L₁ + L₂ = 0 if |L₁| = |L₂| and they oppose), eliminating gyroscopic precession torques during maneuvers.
    - Directional control: by changing the ratio of the two discs' rotation speeds, a net angular momentum is introduced, enabling gyroscopic precession-based steering — the craft "precesses" in the direction of the applied torque.
  (4) The gyroscope as UAP propulsion analogy: gyroscopic precession is sometimes cited as a mechanical analog of the Lense-Thirring gravitomagnetic effect — a spinning mass (gyroscope) resists torque (frame-dragging), and the UAP craft's rotating mass creates a gravitomagnetic field that interacts with the background spacetime (analogous to torque producing precession).
  (5) The equilateral triangle inscribed in the formation: the thin-line equilateral triangle connecting the three satellite circles is explicitly drawn in the schematic — this triangle represents the 3-phase configuration geometry, where the three generators (at triangle vertices) are equidistant. The triangle's centroid is at the central reactor hub.
