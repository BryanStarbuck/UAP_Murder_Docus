PHYSICS / PHENOMENON:
* This formation encodes Hopi iconography — specifically the Blue Kachina and/or Man in the Maze motifs documented in 3_1938590402736803840.txt (the Uffcott Down Eagle Dancer formation). The Hopi Blue Kachina (Saquasohuh Kachina) is the star in the Hopi prophecy that marks the end of the Fourth World and the beginning of the Fifth World — often interpreted as an ET or astronomical object. In some interpretations, the Blue Kachina = Planet X / Nibiru / a UAP appearing in the sky.
* The Greek key/meander border: in Hopi pottery and ceremonial design, the Greek-key (stepped fret / terraced spiral) pattern is a water/rain symbol. Its appearance in the crop formation's decorative ring connects to: Ea/Enki (lord of the waters), rain/fertility symbolism, and the mathematical spiral of the meander pattern (which encodes a discrete approximation of a logarithmic spiral).
* The wing/feather sunburst: eagles and eagle feathers are sacred in many Native American traditions, including Hopi. The Eagle Dancer kachina represents messages between Earth and the Sky (i.e., communication with ET/spiritual beings). The formation at Uffcott Down 2015 (3_1938590402736803840) was identified by RC as encoding the Hopi Eagle Dancer — this Hopi kachina formation appears to be a related or companion formation.
