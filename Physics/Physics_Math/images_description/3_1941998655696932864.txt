PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* GRAVITOMAGNETIC / GYROSCOPIC DEFLECTION OF GRAVITY:
  * The central claim of this diagram: a spinning disc can redirect or "transfer" gravitational force from one direction (downward) to another direction (lateral/diagonal)
  * This is presented as a proposed mechanism for UAP propulsion or anti-gravity
  * The CONVENTIONAL physics analog: a gyroscope under gravity precesses (its spin axis rotates about the vertical axis) — the gravitational torque is converted into precession, not into levitation
    * Precession angular velocity: ω_p = Mgr/(Iω) where M = mass, g = gravity, r = center of mass offset, I = moment of inertia, ω = spin rate
    * BUT: precession does not redirect gravity — the gyroscope still falls if unsupported
  * The CLAIMED physics: spinning at sufficient speed redirects gravity — the disc "steers" gravity from downward to lateral, effectively cancelling vertical acceleration
  * This is NOT supported by conventional physics but IS the core claim of several alternative physics/UAP propulsion proposals:
    1. SEARL EFFECT GENERATOR: John Searl's spinning ring system claims to produce anti-gravity
    2. PODKLETNOV EFFECT: Eugene Podkletnov's (1992) claim that a rotating superconducting disc reduces the weight of objects above it by ~0.3-2%
    3. TAJMAR EFFECT: M. Tajmar (2006) claimed anomalous gravitomagnetic fields from spinning superconductors
    4. WOODWARD MACH EFFECT: fluctuating inertia from mass-energy fluctuations (different mechanism)
* GRAVITOELECTROMAGNETISM (GEM):
  * In GR linearized theory (weak field, slow motion): there is a gravitomagnetic field Bg analogous to the magnetic field
  * A rotating mass generates Bg = (4G/c²) × M × ω/r³ (frame-dragging)
  * For the Earth: Bg ≈ 10⁻¹⁴ rad/s (the Lense-Thirring effect — too small to produce UAP levitation)
  * The claimed "Podkletnov effect" would require Bg at a macroscopic laboratory scale — orders of magnitude larger than GR predicts for normal matter
* GYROSCOPIC GRAVITY INTERPRETATION:
  * The diagram presents the concept that spin couples to gravity in a way that deflects the gravitational vector
  * This is inspired by gyroscopic precession: torque = dL/dt where L = angular momentum
  * The green arrow (gravity redirected) could represent: precession, frame dragging, or a claimed "gravitomagnetic coupling" mechanism
  * If the craft spins rapidly enough, the coupling between spin angular momentum (L) and gravitational field (g) produces a reaction force orthogonal to both — which is the green arrow direction
