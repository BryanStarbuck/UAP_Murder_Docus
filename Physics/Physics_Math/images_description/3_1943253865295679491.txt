PHYSICS / CONCEPTS:
* Complex 3-arm triskelion / Celtic knotwork interior:
  1. The purple-highlighted triple-lobe or triskelion structure encodes a 3-phase rotating field — the same physics as the simpler triskelion formations, but rendered in more complex interlocking Celtic knotwork geometry. The three interleaved lobes represent three phases A, B, C of the rotating magnetic field, 120° apart.
  2. The Celtic knotwork interlacing of the three lobes encodes mutual inductance: the lobes overlap and interweave rather than simply touching — this is the topological representation of M₁₂, M₁₃, M₂₃ (the three mutual inductance coupling terms between pairs of coils in a 3-phase winding). When two coil phases overlap geometrically, they are magnetically coupled; the degree of geometric overlap encodes the coupling coefficient k = M₁₂/√(L₁L₂).
  3. The double elliptical outer ring encodes the stator boundary — the outer housing of the motor. An elliptical rather than circular stator cross-section is used in some motor designs to reduce cogging torque (the reluctance torque caused by magnetic pole alignment with stator teeth).
