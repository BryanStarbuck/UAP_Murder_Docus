PHYSICS / INFORMATION CONCEPTS:
* COMET 17P/HOLMES — 2007 OUTBURST:
  * Comet 17P/Holmes (orbital period P ≈ 6.88 years, discovered 1892 by Edwin Holmes during a similar outburst) underwent one of the most dramatic outbursts ever observed on October 23-24, 2007
  * Pre-outburst magnitude: V ≈ 17 (invisible to naked eye, requiring large telescope)
  * Post-outburst magnitude: V ≈ 2.8 (easily naked-eye visible in Perseus)
  * Brightness increase: factor of ~500,000 (≈ 14 magnitudes in ~24 hours)
  * This is one of the largest known single cometary outbursts in recorded history
* COMA EXPANSION:
  * By early November 2007, Holmes's coma had expanded to a diameter of approximately 1.4 million km (approximately the sun's photosphere diameter)
  * Angular diameter as seen from Earth: ~1.4° (November 2007) — visible as a distinct disc to the naked eye
  * By November 19 (this image date): the coma was still large but beginning to fade
  * Coma expansion velocity: v_exp ≈ 0.5 km/s (derived from diameter increase rate)
* OUTBURST MECHANISM (proposed):
  * Several hypotheses for Holmes 2007 outburst:
    1. CRYSTALLINE ICE PHASE TRANSITION: Amorphous water ice → crystalline ice transition releases latent heat (~9 kJ/mol), causing an eruption; occurs at T ≈ 130-140 K (at ~2.4 AU from sun for Holmes at outburst)
    2. IMPACT EVENT: a small meteoroid impact excavating fresh volatile material from the nucleus interior
    3. OUTGASSING FEEDBACK: CO or CO₂ outgassing creating a positive thermal feedback loop
  * Most supported: the amorphous-to-crystalline ice phase transition (the Haruyama et al. model)
* MIRPHAK (ALPHA PERSEI) CONTEXT:
  * Mirphak (Alpha Persei): distance 592 ly, spectral type F5Ib (yellow supergiant), absolute magnitude M_v = -4.5, luminosity ~5,000 L_sun
  * Part of the Alpha Persei Cluster (Melotte 20): a moving group of young stars ~52 Myr old, distance ~180 pc
  * The visual conjunction of 17P/Holmes with Mirphak made for a dramatically memorable sky scene in late 2007
* CONNECTION TO 3I/ATLAS:
  * Both 17P/Holmes and 3I/ATLAS (see 3_2006249444371771392) are cometary outburst events of interest in the UAP context
  * The collection appears to document multiple instances of unusual cometary activity as potential ETI signals
  * The Bracewell probe concept: an ETI civilization might use a directed cometary impact or outburst as a signaling event — the sudden, dramatic brightening of 17P/Holmes would be observable across many light years
* OBSERVATION PARAMETERS:

  * 15×30s stacked exposures: sidereal tracking, sum = 7.5 min; enough to reveal the diffuse outer coma structure

HIGH-LEVEL SUMMARY:
Astronomical photograph of Comet 17P/Holmes in its famous 2007 outburst, imaged November 19, 2007 by observer T. Scarmato at Calabria, Italy (Canon 10D, 8cm refractor f/5, 15×30s exposures). The comet — which brightened by a factor of ~500,000 (14 magnitudes) on October 23-24, 2007, expanding its coma to >1.4 million km diameter (larger than the sun) — is visible as a large diffuse yellow-golden circular glow surrounding a bright condensed nucleus, with the F5Ib supergiant Mirphak (Alpha Persei) visible within/adjacent to the upper coma. Outburst mechanism: amorphous-to-crystalline water ice phase transition at ~130-140 K releasing ~9 kJ/mol latent heat. This image documents one of the most dramatic single cometary outbursts in recorded history and appears in this collection alongside 3_1943447864916525056 (unlabeled version) and 3_2006249444371771392 (3I/ATLAS false-color image) as examples of extraordinary cometary/interstellar object activity.
