OVERALL SUMMARY: Composite image documenting the Milk Hill crop circle formation of June 30, 2009, which is described as showing "astronomical hieroglyphs" in a long linear sequence. The formation is one of the longest on record, spanning hundreds of meters, and its linear sequence of rectangular and circular symbols has been interpreted as astronomical/hieroglyphic writing. The claim is that the formation encodes a message too complex to have been created by human circle-makers in a single night.
