CONTENT TYPE: Astrophotograph — long-exposure photograph of Comet 17P/Holmes showing its spectacular 2007 outburst coma.

SUBJECT: A high-quality long-exposure astrophotograph of Comet 17P/Holmes during its unprecedented brightness outburst in late 2007, showing the comet's dramatic coma and tail structure.

COMET STRUCTURE:
- NUCLEUS/CORE: A brilliant, near-saturated white/light blue spherical glow at the upper-center of the image (~70px diameter). This represents the highly compressed innermost coma and possibly the nucleus location, though the actual nucleus is far too small to resolve.
- INNER COMA: A large, roughly spherical bright region (~200px diameter) surrounding the central bright point, transitioning from bright blue-white at center to cyan-green at the edges
- OUTER COMA: A very large, diffuse fan-shaped or elongated cloud extending from the comet's head — this is the extended coma that was visible as a large pale disc to the naked eye during the outburst. In this image, it extends downward and to the sides from the main nucleus area.
- TAIL: A faint, diffuse tail extends to the lower-left of the image — lighter than the surrounding sky, shown as a wedge or fan shape pointing away from the sun direction
- The overall comet appears enormous relative to the star field

STAR FIELD:
- Hundreds of background stars visible throughout the image — the comet was positioned in the Perseus constellation during its 2007 outburst
- Stars appear as small white/yellow pinpoints at various brightnesses against the dark sky background
- The comet's coma and extended cloud cover a large fraction of the image, making many stars appear embedded in or just beyond the comet's outer envelope

COLORS: The comet shows a blue-white inner coma, cyan-green middle coma, fading to a warm beige-gray outer coma. The background sky is very dark navy. Stars are white-yellow points.

TEXT (visible in image corners):

TECHNICAL DETAILS FROM CAPTION:
- Date: November 5, 2007 at 22:00 local time
- Photographer: Paulo Candy
- Telescope: Zen Baker-Schmidt 8" f/2 (a fast Newtonian/Baker-Schmidt configuration)
- Camera: SBIG STL-6303E (large-format CCD camera)
- Filter composite: LRGB (Luminance, Red, Green, Blue) with exposures 5:3:3.5 minutes

HISTORICAL CONTEXT: Comet 17P/Holmes underwent a spectacular outburst on October 23-24, 2007, brightening by approximately 15 magnitudes (~500,000× in brightness) in under 48 hours — one of the most dramatic cometary outbursts in recorded history. At peak, it briefly became one of the largest objects in the solar system by apparent coma diameter, appearing larger than the sun when measured in linear size.

OVERALL SUMMARY: A professional astrophotograph of Comet 17P/Holmes (November 5, 2007, 22:00 LT) taken by Paulo Candy using a Zen Baker-Schmidt 8" telescope with STL-6303E CCD camera. The image shows the comet's dramatically oversized coma — a bright blue-white nuclear region surrounded by a vast diffuse cyan-green cloud with a faint tail extending toward the lower left, set against the star-rich Perseus constellation field. This image is used in the crop circle research context to illustrate the comet that the Wayland's Smithy 2005 crop circle allegedly predicted.
