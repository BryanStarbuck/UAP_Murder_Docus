SUBJECT: A black-and-white binary pixel grid image representing the data content of the Chilbolton crop circle formation that appeared near the Chilbolton radio telescope in Hampshire, England in 2001 — presented as an alleged extraterrestrial reply to the 1974 Arecibo message broadcast by NASA.

IMAGE CHARACTERISTICS:
- Approximately 150px wide × 450px tall (very tall, narrow aspect ratio matching the 73×23 binary grid structure of the Arecibo-style message)
- Composed entirely of small black filled squares (pixels "on" = 1) and white empty spaces (pixels "off" = 0)
- The overall pixel density is medium — approximately 30-40% black pixels
- A thin black border frames the entire grid
- The content reads from top to bottom, left to right

DECIPHERED CONTENT (row by row from top):
The message appears to be structured similarly to the original Arecibo 1974 message, encoding:

TOP SECTION (approximately top 15% of grid):
- Binary number representations — the number/counting system key
- Several small pixel clusters arranged in a row — these are the binary-coded numbers from 1-10

UPPER-MIDDLE SECTION (~15-35% from top):
- A larger pixel cluster — representing atomic number data for the elements of life
- The "elements" section, but with Silicon (atomic number 14) replacing Carbon (atomic number 6) — indicating a silicon-based sender

MIDDLE SECTION (~35-55% from top):
- A long vertical bar or column of pixels — representing the binary for DNA strand information
- DNA nucleotide count data appears here in the original Arecibo structure

CENTRAL SECTION (~55-65% from top):
- A humanoid/alien figure profile — a recognizable pixel-art figure that differs from the original Arecibo human figure:
  - Appears shorter/wider or has a different head proportion than the human silhouette
  - Some researchers interpret this as depicting a "Grey alien" body type

LOWER-MIDDLE SECTION (~65-80% from top):
- Numerical data — population figure, etc.

BOTTOM SECTION (~80-100% from top):
- A pixelated representation of a solar system diagram
- And/or a representation of the transmission apparatus (the sender's equivalent of a radio telescope)

COLORS: Pure black pixel blocks on white background.

TEXT: None.

CONTEXT: The Chilbolton 2001 crop formations appeared adjacent to the Chilbolton radio telescope on August 14, 2001. Two formations appeared: one (the "Arecibo Reply") containing a pixel grid similar to this image, and one (a "Face") — later that year the Crabwood 2002 formation also appeared. The decoded content of the "Arecibo Reply" formation differed from the 1974 Arecibo message in key ways: Silicon substituted for Carbon in the elements of life, the humanoid figure appeared shorter and larger-headed, the DNA strand count differed, and the solar system diagram showed a different arrangement.

OVERALL SUMMARY: A black-on-white binary pixel grid image representing the alleged Chilbolton 2001 "Arecibo Reply" crop circle message. The grid encodes information structured analogously to the 1974 Arecibo message but with modifications suggesting the sender is silicon-based, has a different body form, and resides in a different solar system configuration. This is one of the most significant and complex crop circle formations ever documented in terms of its claimed information content.
