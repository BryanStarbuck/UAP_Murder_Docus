HISTORICAL CONTEXT — THE 1952 WASHINGTON FLAP:
- The Washington DC UFO Flap occurred over two consecutive weekends: July 19-20 and July 26-27, 1952
- Objects were detected on radar simultaneously by:
  - Washington National Airport (now Reagan National Airport) — primary tracking
  - Andrews Air Force Base — confirmed targets
  - Bolling Air Force Base — additional tracking
- F-94 interceptors were scrambled but the objects disappeared when aircraft approached, only to reappear after the fighters left
- Visual observations were made by airline pilots (Capital Airlines Flight 807 crew, and others) and multiple control tower personnel
- The incident prompted a press conference — the largest Pentagon press conference since WWII — and led to the CIA Robertson Panel (1953)
- President Truman personally called the head of Air Force Intelligence to demand an explanation
- Project Blue Book's eventual (contested) explanation: temperature inversions causing radar anomalies — widely disputed by radar operators and pilots who were present
