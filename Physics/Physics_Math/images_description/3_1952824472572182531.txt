SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION:
- 3I/ATLAS is an interstellar object (third known, following 1I/'Oumuamua and 2I/Borisov) entering the solar system on a hyperbolic trajectory
- The object has an active coma (outgassing), which distinguishes it from 1I/'Oumuamua (no coma detected) — making it more similar to 2I/Borisov
- Over the 25 days between July 4 and July 29, 2025:
  - Heliocentric distance decreased: 4.386 → 3.564 au (moved ~0.8 au closer to Sun)
  - Geocentric distance decreased: 3.392 → 2.844 au (moved ~0.5 au closer to Earth)
  - Coma brightness increased: from ~420 to ~800-1000 counts (factor ~2 brightening)
  - Phase angle increased from 3.13° to 12.91° (object moved off opposition)
- The directional compass (+N, E, -V, Θ) shows the antisolar direction (Θ) and negative velocity vector (-V), which are used to determine if the coma is sunward-enhanced (radiation pressure tail) or velocity-vector-aligned (activity or outgassing patterns)
- The very small phase angle on July 4 (3.13°) indicates the object was near opposition (Earth between Sun and 3I/ATLAS) at that time
