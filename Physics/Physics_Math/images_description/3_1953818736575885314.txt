- This is a form of pareidolia analysis — the human visual system's tendency to find faces in ambiguous patterns — applied systematically to crop circle data

- This type of analysis (crop circle → pixel reconstruction → face recognition) has been performed by several researchers, most notably by researchers associated with crop circle information sites
- The argument that a crop circle encodes a recognizable human face is used to support claims of non-human intelligence behind crop formation design
