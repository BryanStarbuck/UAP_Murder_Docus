PHYSICS / INFORMATION CONCEPTS:
* LITHIUM SALT CONDUCTIVITY IN SOLUTION:
  * This chart presents the ionic conductivity of various lithium salts dissolved in an electrolyte solvent (likely a polar organic solvent such as acetonitrile, dimethylformamide, or water)
  * Ionic conductivity σ (μS cm⁻¹): a measure of how well dissolved ions carry electrical current; σ = n q μ where n = ion number density, q = ion charge, μ = ion mobility
  * Ion mobility μ = q/(6πηr) where η = solvent viscosity, r = hydrodynamic ion radius (Stokes-Einstein relation)
  * For Li⁺: the bare Li⁺ ion is small (r_ion = 76 pm) but highly solvated in polar solvents — the solvation shell adds significant hydrodynamic radius, reducing mobility
* ELECTROLYTE CHEMISTRY FOR BATTERY / SUPERCONDUCTOR APPLICATIONS:
  * Lithium salts are critical electrolyte components in:
    * Lithium-ion batteries (LIBs): Li⁺ shuttles between anode and cathode; LiPF₆, LiClO₄, LiTFSI are common
    * Lithium-sulfur batteries
    * Superconductor doping: LiCl, Li₂O, LiOH doping of oxide superconductors (YBCO, BSCCO)
    * Biological lithium (Li⁺ ion therapy): LiC = lithium carbonate (Li₂CO₃), LiO = lithium orotate (LiC₅H₃N₂O₄)
  * The chart's comparison of inorganic vs. organic Li salts: organic anion Li salts generally have lower conductivity (lower mobility of larger organic anions) — consistent with the data shown
* LITHIUM OROTATE (LOWEST CONDUCTIVITY):
  * LiO (lithium orotate) shows the lowest conductivity (~29 μS cm⁻¹)
  * Orotate anion: a pyrimidine carboxylate (the potassium salt of orotic acid, C₅H₄N₂O₄)
  * Lithium orotate is notable because orotate is a natural mineral transporter — it facilitates lithium penetration across the blood-brain barrier more efficiently than inorganic salts (LiC)
  * Lithium orotate dosing: effective at much lower doses than lithium carbonate, with potentially fewer side effects (still under research)
* RELEVANCE TO NEUROSCIENCE / UAP BRAIN STUDIES:
  * Lithium (Li⁺) therapy is used clinically for: bipolar disorder, major depression, suicidal ideation, and recently investigated for Alzheimer's prevention
  * Mechanism: Li⁺ inhibits GSK-3β (glycogen synthase kinase 3 beta) — a key enzyme in tau hyperphosphorylation (Alzheimer's pathology) and in Wnt signaling
  * See 3_1953971040654438401 for GSK3β fluorescence microscopy images (directly related to this chart)
  * The lithium orotate vs. lithium carbonate comparison in this chart is the same comparison made in the companion microscopy image: LiO (orotate) vs. LiC (carbonate)
* IONIC CONDUCTIVITY EQUATION:
  * Kohlrausch's law (strong electrolytes): Λ_m = Λ_m° - K√c where Λ_m = molar conductivity, Λ_m° = limiting molar conductivity (infinite dilution), c = concentration, K = empirical constant
  * Onsager equation: K = (A + B Λ_m°)/(η_solvent)
  * The data at a fixed concentration shows the trend: inorganic anions (smaller, less charge delocalized) → higher mobility → higher conductivity; organic anions (larger, charge delocalized over aromatic/alkyl structure) → lower mobility → lower conductivity
