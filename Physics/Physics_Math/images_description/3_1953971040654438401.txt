PHYSICS / INFORMATION CONCEPTS:
* GSK-3β (GLYCOGEN SYNTHASE KINASE 3 BETA):
  * GSK-3β is a serine/threonine protein kinase, one of the most important enzymes in cellular signaling
  * Key roles:
    * Tau phosphorylation: GSK-3β hyperphosphorylates tau protein → tau aggregates into neurofibrillary tangles (NFTs) — the hallmark of Alzheimer's disease (AD)
    * Wnt signaling: GSK-3β phosphorylates β-catenin, targeting it for proteasomal degradation (inhibiting Wnt target gene expression)
    * Glycogen metabolism: inhibition of glycogen synthase → reduced glycogen storage
  * GSK-3β inhibition is a major therapeutic target for: Alzheimer's disease, bipolar disorder, type 2 diabetes, certain cancers
  * Li⁺ inhibits GSK-3β by competing with Mg²⁺ at the enzyme active site (uncompetitive inhibition with respect to ATP; IC₅₀ ≈ 2 mM for lithium carbonate)
* MAP2 (MICROTUBULE-ASSOCIATED PROTEIN 2):
  * MAP2 is a dendritic marker — it is expressed in neuronal dendrites and cell bodies, not in axons
  * MAP2 binds to microtubules, stabilizing them against depolymerization
  * MAP2 expression level / organization is a proxy for: dendritic branching and density, neuronal health and maturation, synaptic connectivity
  * A more organized MAP2 pattern (bottom row, LiO) suggests: better dendritic organization, improved neuronal structural integrity, or increased dendritic branching
* LITHIUM OROTATE (LiO) vs. LITHIUM CARBONATE (LiC) COMPARISON:
  * The microscopy shows:
    * LiO (bottom row): dramatically reduced GSK3β signal (left panel, much dimmer green) + more organized MAP2 (right panel, more prominent filamentous magenta structure)
    * LiC (middle row): similar GSK3β to water control, less MAP2 change than LiO
    * Water (top row): high GSK3β, normal MAP2 pattern
  * Interpretation: LiO more effectively inhibits GSK-3β (as an active form) compared to LiC
  * Why? Orotate acts as a natural intracellular mineral carrier, facilitating Li⁺ entry across the blood-brain barrier:
    * LiO crosses the BBB more efficiently → higher intracellular Li⁺ → stronger GSK-3β inhibition
    * LiC: higher dose needed to achieve equivalent intracellular Li⁺ → more systemic side effects (tremor, polyuria, thyroid effects)
  * Lithium orotate for neurological conditions: proposed as a superior alternative to LiC for:
    * Alzheimer's disease prevention (tau reduction via GSK-3β inhibition)
    * Bipolar disorder (mood stabilization)
    * Neuroprotection after traumatic brain injury (TBI)
