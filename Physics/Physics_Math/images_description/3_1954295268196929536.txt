MOLECULAR STRUCTURE — LITHIUM OROTATE:

SYSTEMATIC NAME: Lithium 2,6-dioxo-1,2,3,6-tetrahydropyrimidine-4-carboxylate (or lithium orotate)
MOLECULAR FORMULA: C₅H₃LiN₂O₄
PARENT ACID: Orotic acid (6-carboxyuracil, also called uracil-6-carboxylic acid, vitamin B13)
MOLECULAR WEIGHT (lithium salt): approximately 162 g/mol

- Six-membered pyrimidine (diazine) ring

PHARMACOLOGICAL/BIOLOGICAL SIGNIFICANCE:
- Lithium orotate is a form of lithium supplement popular in alternative medicine and biohacking communities
- Claimed advantages over lithium carbonate (the pharmaceutical form): better cellular penetration due to the orotate anion carrier, lower effective doses required
- The orotate anion may act as a carrier, facilitating lithium transport across cell membranes and the blood-brain barrier
- Orotic acid itself is involved in the pyrimidine biosynthesis pathway (de novo synthesis of uracil and cytosine)
- Lithium has established effects in: bipolar disorder treatment, neuroprotection (inhibition of GSK-3β), potential Alzheimer's disease prevention, telomere length preservation
