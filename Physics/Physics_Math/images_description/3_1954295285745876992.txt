COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS:
The four lithium compounds represent the spectrum of lithium delivery forms:
1. Lithium carbonate — inorganic salt, most commonly prescribed pharmaceutical form, gastric irritant
2. Lithium citrate — organic salt, liquid formulation, faster onset
3. Lithium orotate — organic salt with pyrimidine carrier, biohacking/supplement community preferred form
4. Lithium aspartate — organic amino acid salt, another supplement form with claimed superior bioavailability

All four deliver the Li⁺ cation, which is the pharmacologically active species. The anion determines: absorption rate, GI tolerance, cellular penetration, and tissue distribution. The orotate anion (from orotic acid) has particular relevance as a pyrimidine biosynthesis intermediate, potentially facilitating incorporation into DNA/RNA synthesis pathways.
