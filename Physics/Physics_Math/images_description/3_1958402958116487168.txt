THE CMS DETECTOR IN CONTEXT:
- The CMS detector: 15 meters in diameter, 21 meters long, weighs 14,000 tons
- It surrounds one collision point of the LHC (the underground ring at CERN, 27km circumference, beneath Switzerland and France)
- Its superconducting solenoid generates a 3.8 Tesla magnetic field (140,000 times Earth's field)
- The detector design is based on: tracking charged particles (silicon detectors), measuring electron and photon energies (ECAL), measuring hadron energies (HCAL), and detecting muons (muon chambers)
- Key discovery: the Higgs boson was confirmed in 2012 by both CMS and ATLAS detectors
