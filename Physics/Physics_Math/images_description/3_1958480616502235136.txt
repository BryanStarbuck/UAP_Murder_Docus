THE ZETA RETICULI BINARY SYSTEM:
- Zeta 1 Reticuli (ζ1 Ret): G3V spectral type, approximately 39.4 light-years from Sol, visual magnitude 5.52
- Zeta 2 Reticuli (ζ2 Ret): G1V spectral type, approximately 39.5 light-years from Sol, visual magnitude 5.22
- The two stars are separated by approximately 0.5 light-years and orbit each other very slowly
- They are both solar-type stars (G-type, similar to our Sun) and are considered candidate stars for harboring habitable planets
