PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* SPIRAL PLASMA MODES:
  * A two-arm spiral plasma discharge is a classic mode of rotating plasma in a cylindrical geometry with axial magnetic field B_z
  * The spiral mode number m=2 arises when the plasma is driven at a frequency ω = ω_ce (electron cyclotron frequency, ω_ce = eB/m_e) that excites the m=2 azimuthal eigenmode
  * In a cylindrical plasma column of radius R with axial field B_z: the dispersion relation for electrostatic spiral modes is ω = mω_E × B (E×B rotation frequency), where ω_E×B = E_r/(r·B_z)
  * The Archimedean spiral pattern: r = a + bθ — this would appear if the plasma rotates at ω while the discharge travels radially outward at constant radial velocity v_r = bω
* BIRKELAND CURRENT SPIRALS:
  * Kristian Birkeland (1867-1917) created laboratory experiments (terrella experiments) showing that charged particles flowing along magnetic field lines in a dipole field create spiral current patterns analogous to the spiral arms of galaxies and auroral spirals
  * The two-arm spiral in this image is a direct laboratory analog of a two-arm spiral galaxy
  * Birkeland's experiments demonstrated that electromagnetic forces (not purely gravitational) can create large-scale spiral structures — the basis of plasma cosmology (Hannes Alfvén)
* SPIRAL VORTEX AND UAP:
  * One hypothesis for UAP propulsion (particularly for the plasma vortex mechanism) involves creating a helical electromagnetic field structure inside a circular cavity — the two-arm spiral would represent the m=2 mode of such a cavity
  * The helical field structure could reduce drag by creating an axially extended plasma sheath around the craft (Magnus effect analog for plasma)
  * Alternatively: the spiral mode represents a specific winding of toroidal magnetic field lines (magnetic helicity = ∫A·B dV) that stabilizes a compact toroid plasma configuration
* IEC / FUSOR STAR MODE:
  * In a Farnsworth fusor at low pressure: ions travel inward along straight radial paths, creating a "star mode" with multiple bright filaments
  * At higher pressures: the star mode transitions to a spiral mode as ion-neutral collisions introduce angular momentum, creating spiral current patterns — consistent with this image showing a transition-to-spiral-mode fusor
* ROTATING E×B DRIFT:
  * In the presence of crossed electric (radial, E_r) and magnetic (axial, B_z) fields: electrons and ions undergo the E×B drift in the azimuthal direction with velocity v_E×B = E_r×B_z/B_z² = E_r/B_z
  * This drift is purely azimuthal (no radial component) — so the radial expansion of the discharge comes from another mechanism (ionization front propagation or secondary emission)
  * The spiral shape arises from the combination of: azimuthal E×B rotation + radial ionization front propagation — analogous to the Archimedean spiral formation in a Penning discharge
