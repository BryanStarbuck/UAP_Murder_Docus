PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* MID-INFRARED SPECTROPHOTOMETRY:
  * Mid-infrared (MIR) astronomy covers 2-20 μm; atmospheric windows: L-band (2.9-4.1 μm), M-band (4.4-5.0 μm), N-band (7.5-14 μm)
  * The 4.16-4.37 μm window specifically targets: CO₂ ice absorption (ν₃ asymmetric stretch mode at 4.27 μm, band strength A = 7.6 × 10⁻¹⁷ cm molecule⁻¹), and CO₂ gas emission (ν₃ fundamental, same frequency)
  * The log₁₀ scale colorbar in the middle panel indicates the source is many orders of magnitude brighter in the CO₂ band than in the adjacent bands — consistent with a cometary CO₂ outgassing spike
* COMET 17P/HOLMES OUTBURST MIR SPECTROSCOPY:
  * Post-outburst observations of Holmes showed: elevated CO₂, CO, and H₂O production rates; the CO₂/H₂O ratio was anomalously high compared to typical comets; this suggested the outburst originated from a CO₂ ice-rich sublayer exposed by a cliff collapse or impact event on the nucleus
  * Published observations: Reach et al. (2010), Spitzer Space Telescope IRAC observations of Holmes post-outburst, found strong 4.5 μm emission (CO₂ + CO blend in Spitzer's Channel 2 band 4.0-5.0 μm)
  * The pixel scale and field of view are consistent with Spitzer IRAC: pixel scale = 1.22 arcsec/pixel, field 5'×5' (but this image shows 50"×50" subset)
* SPECIFIC INTENSITY I_ν (MJy/sr):
  * I_ν is the specific intensity per unit frequency interval per steradian
  * 1 MJy sr⁻¹ = 10⁶ Jy sr⁻¹ = 10⁶ × 10⁻²⁶ W Hz⁻¹ m⁻² sr⁻¹ = 10⁻²⁰ W Hz⁻¹ m⁻² sr⁻¹
  * The spatial map of I_ν traces the surface brightness distribution of the cometary coma — where dust and gas are densest, I_ν is highest
  * The radiative transfer equation in the coma: dI_ν/ds = -κ_ν ρ I_ν + j_ν, where κ_ν is the absorption coefficient, j_ν the emission coefficient; for a tenuous coma (κ_ν ρ ≪ 1): I_ν ≈ ∫j_ν ds (the line-of-sight emission integral)
* ANGULAR RESOLUTION AND INSTRUMENT INFERENCE:
  * At Comet Holmes distance ~1.6 AU during the 2007 outburst: 1 AU ≈ 1.5 × 10¹¹ m; at 1.6 AU distance, 1 arcsecond ≈ 1.5 × 10¹¹ / 206265 × 1.6 ≈ 1163 km on the sky
  * The 50 arcsecond field of view = 50 × 1163 km ≈ 58,000 km — capturing the inner 58,000 km of the coma
  * Spitzer IRAC pixel = 1.22 arcsec ≈ 1419 km/pixel at this distance
