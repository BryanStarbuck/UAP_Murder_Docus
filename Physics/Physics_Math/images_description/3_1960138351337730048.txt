PHYSICS / INFORMATION CONCEPTS:
* EAGLE NEBULA PILLARS OF CREATION — M16:
  * Location: 6,500-7,000 light-years from Earth in the constellation Serpens
  * The pillars: remnant structures from a molecular cloud being actively photo-evaporated by UV radiation from the nearby stellar cluster NGC 6611 (which contains several hot O-type stars)
  * Pillar dimensions: each ~4-5 light-years tall, ~0.5 light-year wide at the base
  * Age: the pillars will be completely destroyed in approximately 3 million years (already may be destroyed — the light showing them now left 6,500 years ago; if destroyed by a nearby supernova ~6,000 years ago, we would not see the supernova light for another ~500 years)
* EVAPORATING GASEOUS GLOBULES (EGGs):
  * First identified in the 1995 Hubble Space Telescope image by Jeff Hester and Paul Scowen
  * EGGs: dense spherical or prolate-spheroidal knots at pillar tips, ~100 AU diameter, mass ~10⁻² - 1 M_sun
  * EGG density: n_H ≈ 10⁵-10⁶ cm⁻³ (much denser than surrounding pillar material at ~10³-10⁴ cm⁻³)
  * The photo-evaporation rate: dM/dt = -Φ_UV × m_H / α_B × (1/(n_H × A_EGG)) where Φ_UV = UV photon flux, α_B = Case B recombination coefficient
  * Each EGG is expected to form a low-mass star (0.5-2 M_sun) in ~10⁵ years before photo-evaporation destroys it
* JWST vs. HUBBLE COMPARISON:
  * The 1995 Hubble (HST) image: visible light (R,G,B = Hα, [OIII], [SII]); the pillars appeared dark against bright emission nebula
  * The 2022 JWST image: near-infrared (1-5 μm); the pillars glow orange-tan (warm dust emission) against a darker background; the infrared penetrates dust, revealing star formation activity INSIDE the pillars
  * JWST's improved resolution at these wavelengths: resolves smaller structures (EGGs, density gradients) than Hubble
* STAR FORMATION INSIDE THE PILLARS:
  * Protostellar jets visible in the JWST image: orange-red streaks extending from pillar tips (HH jets = Herbig-Haro objects, shocked outflows from protostars)
  * HH jet physics: bipolar outflow from an accreting protostar; velocity v_jet ≈ 100-500 km/s; emission from: [SII], Hα, [OI] (shock excitation)
  * The jet structures in the JWST image: small horizontal protrusions visible at several points on the pillar surfaces (these are the jets)
* MOLECULAR CLOUD PHYSICS:
  * Pillar interior: dark, cold (T ≈ 10-30 K), dense (n_H ≈ 10⁴-10⁶ cm⁻³) molecular gas
  * Composition: H₂ (dominant), CO, OH, H₂O ice, NH₃, HCN, and complex organic molecules
  * The interface (pillar surface): photodissociation region at T ≈ 100-8000 K, where UV photons break H₂ → 2H and ionize C → C⁺
  * Jeans mass: M_J = (5kT/GμmH)^(3/2) (4π n μ mH)^(-1/2) ≈ 1-5 M_sun for T=30 K, n=10⁵ cm⁻³
