THE INSET SPECTRA:
* Upper spectrum (4.1-4.4 μm): The sharp spike at 4.27 μm = CO₂ ν₃ asymmetric stretch fundamental; this emission confirms active CO₂ outgassing; line width consistent with unresolved gas-phase emission from the coma
* Lower spectrum (2.4-3.0 μm): broader feature centered at ~2.7-3.0 μm; in comets this region contains: H₂O ice absorption (3.1 μm), the 3.0 μm O-H stretch in water ice, and various organic molecular bands; the feature in this band confirms water ice or organics on the dust grains
* These two spectra together confirm: Comet Holmes was actively outgassing CO₂ and had water ice/organic-covered dust grains in its expanding coma — consistent with a CO₂ ice-driven outburst from a sub-surface layer

PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* CO₂ EMISSION BAND (4.27 μm):
  * The ν₃ asymmetric stretch mode of CO₂ at 4.26 μm (gas phase) / 4.27 μm (ice phase) — strong vibrational transition
  * Band strength A = 7.6 × 10⁻¹⁷ cm molecule⁻¹ (one of the strongest IR-active vibrational modes)
  * In a cometary coma: CO₂ is photolyzed by solar UV (CO₂ + hν → CO + O); the lifetime of CO₂ at 1 AU is ~1 year (longer than water)
  * CO₂ production rate from the flux: Q(CO₂) = 4πr²·F_CO₂ / (hν_CO₂ · g_CO₂) where g_CO₂ is the fluorescence efficiency (g-factor)
* H₂O ICE FEATURE (3.0 μm):
  * The O-H stretch fundamental of water ice at 3.0-3.1 μm is a diagnostic feature for icy grain mantles
  * In interstellar grain analogues and cometary dust: water ice is the dominant volatile, with mixing ratios H₂O:CO₂:CO ≈ 100:20:5 (by number)
  * The 3.0 μm band optical depth τ₃.₀ is used to estimate the column density of water ice: N_ice = τ₃.₀ / A₃.₀ where A₃.₀ ≈ 2×10⁻¹⁶ cm/molecule
* MULTI-EPOCH OBSERVATIONS:
  * The two right panels (compact source) vs. the left panel (extended coma) suggest different epochs: left = near outburst maximum (extended coma), right = later (fading, more compact)
  * Or: the right panels show a different spatial scale (zoomed in to the nucleus region)
  * Tracking the evolution of the CO₂ feature over time constrains: the duration of active CO₂ sublimation, the depth of the CO₂ ice layer exposed by the outburst, and the total CO₂ inventory released
