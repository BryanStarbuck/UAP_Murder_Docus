PHYSICS / INFORMATION CONCEPTS:
* THERMAL/HOT COLORMAP COMA IMAGE:
  * This is the same hot colormap series as 3_1959780062506717184 — a heavily pixelated low-resolution thermal false-color image of a cometary coma
  * The coarse pixelation (large square pixels visible) indicates extreme spatial rebinning (2×2, 4×4, or 8×8 pixel binning) to increase signal-to-noise at the cost of spatial resolution
  * This technique is used for faint objects: sum N×N pixels → increase flux per "super-pixel" by factor N², reduce read noise by factor N
* ASYMMETRIC COMA — RADIATION PRESSURE:
  * The asymmetric isophotes (extending further toward upper-right) are a key physical diagnostic:
    * Radiation pressure acceleration: a_rad = (L_sun/4πr²c) × Q_pr/m (where Q_pr = radiation pressure efficiency, m = grain mass, r = heliocentric distance)
    * Syndyne/synchrone analysis: dust grains of different sizes and different ejection times follow different syndyne and synchrone curves
    * The extended direction toward upper-right indicates the anti-sunward direction (tail direction) — grains pushed by solar radiation
  * For an interstellar object (3I/ATLAS): the radiation pressure effect is the same as for solar-system comets (since radiation pressure comes from the Sun at the current heliocentric position), but the dust-to-gas ratio and grain properties may differ
