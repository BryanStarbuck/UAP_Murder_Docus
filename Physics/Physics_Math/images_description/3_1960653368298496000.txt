PHYSICS / INFORMATION CONCEPTS:
* FULL-FRAME COMA IMAGE (NO CHIP GAP):
  * This image differs from 3_1959780062506717184, 3_1960388851488509952, 3_1960517124507725824, 3_1960571758018498560 — those all have the L-shaped black chip gap in the lower corners
  * This full-frame image suggests: either a different instrument/detector with full coverage, OR the object was positioned away from the chip gap, OR this is a different observation epoch
  * The absence of chip gap makes it easier to characterize the full coma extent and asymmetry
* PLUS-PATTERN SATURATION ARTIFACT:
  * The white nucleus shows a slight cross/plus (+) pattern — this is characteristic of:
    * Pixel saturation bleeding (charge bleeding along columns in CCD detectors — saturation in one pixel bleeds into adjacent pixels along the column direction)
    * PSF diffraction spike (for telescopes with a secondary mirror support structure — a 4-legged spider creates 4 diffraction spikes; some telescopes have 3-legged or 2-legged spiders)
  * For a compact unresolved nucleus, the plus-pattern indicates the nucleus is at or near saturation
  * Saturation means the absolute flux of the nucleus is a lower limit only — the true nucleus magnitude cannot be measured from this pixel
* SYMMETRIC COMA VS. ASYMMETRIC (RADIATION PRESSURE):
  * The isophotes here are closer to circular/symmetric than in the chip-gap images
  * Possible explanations: (1) earlier observation epoch before asymmetry developed; (2) different viewing geometry (anti-sunward direction projected nearly behind the observer); (3) higher spatial resolution making the tail/coma asymmetry less obvious; (4) different wavelength filter (gas emission vs. dust continuum — dust shows stronger radiation pressure asymmetry)
* HASER MODEL AND SURFACE BRIGHTNESS:
  * The smooth radial falloff (white → blue) follows the Haser model I(r) ∝ r^{-n}
  * The absence of visible structure (no jets, fans, or spiraling shells visible at this resolution) suggests either: azimuthally symmetric outgassing, or the resolution is insufficient to resolve outgassing features
