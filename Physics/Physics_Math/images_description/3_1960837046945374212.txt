PHYSICS / INFORMATION CONCEPTS:
* WIDE RECTANGULAR COMA IMAGE — NEW ASPECT RATIO AND COLORMAP:
  * This image has a different aspect ratio from companion images (wider than tall, ~2:1 or 3:2 width:height ratio)
  * The black background (rather than blue or green) and the purely red-orange-yellow-white colormap suggest a different display configuration
  * This may be: (1) a different telescope/instrument, (2) a different processing pipeline, or (3) a zoomed/cropped version of a wider field
* IRREGULAR COMA STRUCTURE:
  * The bright "blobs" in the left-side red region are unusual — in a smooth symmetric coma these would not exist
  * Possible causes:
    * Outgassing jets: discrete regions of enhanced gas/dust production from localized active areas on the nucleus surface
    * Radiation pressure structures: dust grains of specific size ranges (near the radiation pressure turnover) can produce ring-like or blob structures in the coma
    * Star/galaxy contamination: background objects embedded in the coma (misidentified as coma structure)
    * Image stacking artifacts: if multiple exposures were stacked with imperfect alignment, ghost images of the nucleus can appear
  * For a genuine interstellar comet: discrete jets would indicate a rotating nucleus with localized active regions (same as solar-system comets)
* JET PHYSICS:
  * Cometary jets form where sunlight directly heats localized active areas (sublimation of ices: H₂O, CO, CO₂)
  * Jet direction: initially radial from the nucleus, then curved by:
    * Solar radiation pressure (grains): produces anti-sunward curved tail
    * Coriolis force (rotating nucleus): produces spiral jet patterns
    * Nuclear rotation: jets precess with the nucleus rotation period
  * Measuring jet directions and intensities constrains: nuclear rotation period, active area locations, dust grain size distribution
* PHOTODISSOCIATION SCALE LENGTHS:
  * Different gas species in jets have different photodissociation scale lengths r_γ = v_mol/β_γ where v_mol is the molecular outflow velocity and β_γ is the photodissociation rate
  * CN: r_γ ≈ 10⁵ km at 1 AU
  * C₂: r_γ ≈ 5×10⁴ km
  * OH: r_γ ≈ 2×10⁵ km
  * Different species produce coma structures at different spatial scales
