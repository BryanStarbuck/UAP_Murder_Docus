* TAIL MORPHOLOGY AND RADIATION PRESSURE PARAMETER:
  * If the cyan emission extends further to one side (tail direction), this constrains:

    * Typical β values: 0.001 (large grains) to 10 (sub-micron grains)
  * The syndyne envelope at a given β constrains the grain size; the synchrone at a given ejection time constrains when the grains were ejected
