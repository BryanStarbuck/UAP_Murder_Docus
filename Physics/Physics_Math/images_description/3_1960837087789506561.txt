PHYSICS / INFORMATION CONCEPTS:
* DIFFERENT CHIP GAP ORIENTATION:
  * In companion images (3_1959780062506717184, 3_1960388851488509952, 3_1960517124507725824, 3_1960571758018498560), the chip gap was in the LOWER-LEFT corner
  * In this image, the chip gap runs along the LEFT side — a staircase from upper-left to lower-left
  * This could indicate: (1) a different pointing/dithering position (the telescope was offset so the object fell closer to the left chip boundary), or (2) a different observation epoch when the object had moved relative to the chip
  * The purple-violet background (rather than blue or blue-violet in companion images) suggests a different stretch/colormap or a different filter/wavelength
* HORIZONTAL COMA ELONGATION:
  * The coma is visibly elongated in the horizontal direction
  * For a comet: horizontal elongation indicates the tail direction is close to horizontal in this image orientation
  * The elongation direction plus knowledge of the solar position angle gives the dust/gas tail orientation, which constrains the object's orbit
* PURPLE BACKGROUND VS. BLUE/VIOLET IN COMPANIONS:
  * The more purple (less blue) background suggests a different filter or a redder overall color of the emission
  * Alternatively: a different stretch/gamma applied to the same data
  * Physically: if this is a redder emission band (e.g., OH red-doublet, or R-band dust continuum), the background would appear more red-dominated in the colormap
