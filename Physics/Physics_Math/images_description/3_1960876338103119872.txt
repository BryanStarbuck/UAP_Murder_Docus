PHYSICS / INFORMATION CONCEPTS:
* MARS RECONNAISSANCE ORBITER (MRO):
  * MRO is a NASA Mars orbiter launched August 12, 2005, Mars orbit insertion March 10, 2006
  * Instruments: HiRISE (High Resolution Imaging Science Experiment): 50 cm resolution, resolution capability to image meter-scale objects on Martian surface; CRISM (Compact Reconnaissance Imaging Spectrometer for Mars); CTX (Context Camera); MARCI (Mars Color Imager); SHARAD (Shallow Radar) subsurface sounder
  * HiRISE has been used to image UAP-related structures on Mars (claimed "anomalies"):
    * The "Face on Mars" (Cydonia region) was reimaged at high resolution, revealing a mesa/butte
    * Proposed artificial structures, crash sites, and "monolith" objects have been claimed in HiRISE imagery
    * HiRISE has imaged the Curiosity rover, Opportunity rover, InSight lander from orbit
  * SHARAD: subsurface radar sounding to ~1 km depth; has detected underground ice and layered deposits
* ORBITAL MECHANICS AND AEROBRAKING:
  * MRO used aerobraking to reduce fuel consumption: 6-month aerobraking campaign, using atmospheric drag in 100-km-altitude periapsis passes to circularize the orbit from highly elliptical insertion orbit to the science orbit (~300 km circular, 112-minute period)
  * Aerobraking: F_drag = ½ρv²C_D A; each periapsis pass removes orbital energy
  * Science orbit: 300 km altitude, 12 km/s orbital velocity, 112-minute period
* SOLAR PANELS:
  * MRO power: 2 × 10-panel solar arrays totaling ~2000 W at Mars (1.52 AU from Sun, solar flux = 590 W/m² vs. 1361 W/m² at Earth)
  * Solar flux scales as 1/r² (r = heliocentric distance in AU): P_Mars = P_Earth × (1 AU/1.52 AU)² ≈ 0.43 × P_Earth
  * The dark grey solar cells are GaAs/Ge triple-junction cells: ~28-30% efficiency
* COMMUNICATIONS:
  * High-Gain Antenna (HGA): the large parabolic dish, 3.0m diameter
  * Frequency: X-band (8.4 GHz) and Ka-band (32 GHz)
  * Data rate: up to 6 Mbps downlink using Ka-band (record for Mars missions)
  * Light travel time: Earth-Mars: 3-22 minutes depending on orbital geometry
* UAP PHYSICS CONTEXT:
  * In the context of this collection: MRO is relevant because:

    * MRO subsurface radar (SHARAD) could theoretically detect buried structures if they exist
