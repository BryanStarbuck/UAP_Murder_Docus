PHYSICS / INFORMATION CONCEPTS:
* 3I/ATLAS — THIRD INTERSTELLAR OBJECT:
  * This plot documents the NICKEL (Ni) production rate Q(Ni) of the newly discovered interstellar object "3I/ATLAS"
  * 3I/ATLAS is the third known interstellar object (following 1I/ʻOumuamua and 2I/Borisov) — it was discovered by the ATLAS survey at heliocentric distances of ~3-4 AU in 2024-2025
  * "3I" designation = third interstellar object confirmed by the IAU
  * The nickel (Ni) spectral feature was discovered in solar system comets by Manfroid et al. 2021 (Nature) — atomic nickel emission lines in the visible (Ni I lines at 336, 338, 341, 346, 352 nm) were detected even at temperatures well above nickel's condensation temperature, suggesting a photodissociation pathway from an unknown Ni-bearing precursor molecule
  * Q(Ni) = nickel production rate in atoms s⁻¹; the log scale spans ~20.5 to 24.0 = 3.5 orders of magnitude
  * The 3I/ATLAS data cluster (blue oval) is at ~3-4 AU — the object was already producing nickel at large heliocentric distances, unlike typical solar system comets
  * The power-law fit Q ∝ r⁻⁰·⁴³ is much shallower than the expected Q ∝ r⁻² from pure solar radiation-driven sublimation — suggesting: (a) an unusually large nucleus, (b) a different composition, or (c) an active energy source independent of solar radiation
  * The 2I/Borisov comparison (green squares at ~2 AU) shows similar Ni production rates to solar system comets at that distance
  * 3I/ATLAS Ni production at 3-4 AU appears to match or exceed solar system comets at 1-2 AU — anomalously high activity for a distant object
* UAP PHYSICS CONTEXT:
  * The discovery of 3I/ATLAS renewed discussion of ʻOumuamua's anomalous acceleration and the "interstellar artifact" hypothesis (Loeb, Avi et al. 2021, 2023)
  * If 3I/ATLAS shows anomalous Ni production at large heliocentric distances, it could indicate a non-standard energy source (e.g., internal radioactive heating, or active energy generation)
  * Interstellar objects with anomalous properties are directly relevant to the UAP physics thesis that non-human intelligence may deploy "probes" in the solar system
