* RADIAL INTENSITY PROFILE ANALYSIS:
  * The concentric ring structure visualizes the coma surface brightness profile I(ρ):
    * For steady-state coma: I(ρ) = Q/(4π v_gas ρ) × g(r) where Q = production rate [molecules/s], v_gas = gas velocity, g(r) = fluorescence efficiency at heliocentric distance r
    * Deviations from pure ρ^(-1) profile (seen in the asymmetric orange-left enhancement) indicate: non-uniform gas release, anisotropic outgassing from active regions, or dust shell structure from outburst (Holmes was NOT steady-state — massive single outburst)
  * Holmes outburst (Oct 23-24, 2007): released ~10^17 kg of material; coma expanded from ~10km nucleus to ~1.4 million km in weeks
* SHELL STRUCTURE FROM OUTBURST:
  * The concentric color rings may represent actual physical shells of ejecta from the outburst:
    * First shell: highest-velocity material, now at largest radius
    * Inner shells: lower-velocity material still close to nucleus
    * Shell spacing encodes velocity dispersion: Δρ = Δv × t_outburst
  * Each shell in the ejecta: Δρ = Δv × t where t ≈ days-weeks post-outburst
* LEFT-RIGHT (W-E) ASYMMETRY:
  * Orange-amber enhancement on the LEFT (west/anti-solar): dust tail pushed sunward by radiation pressure; β-parameter sorting, larger grains lag behind smaller grains

* RAYLEIGH-TAYLOR INSTABILITY:
  * The ring-shaped brightness enhancement at fixed radius from nucleus: the Rayleigh-Taylor instability occurs when a lighter gas (expanding ejecta) pushes into a heavier ambient gas
  * Growth rate: γ_RT = √(A_t × k × g_eff) where A_t = Atwood number, k = wavenumber, g_eff = effective deceleration of the expanding shell
