PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* JUPITER-FAMILY COMETS (JFCs):
  * Jupiter-family comets are short-period comets with orbital periods P < 20 years and Tisserand parameter 2 < T_J < 3
  * The red ellipse represents a JFC orbit: high eccentricity (e ≈ 0.5-0.9), aphelia near Jupiter's orbit (Q ≈ 5.2 AU), perihelia near or inside Earth's orbit (q ≈ 1-3 AU)
  * Comet 17P/Holmes IS a Jupiter-family comet: T_J ≈ 2.94, period P ≈ 6.88 years, q ≈ 2.05 AU, Q ≈ 5.18 AU
* TISSERAND PARAMETER:
  * T_J = a_J/a + 2√(a/a_J × (1-e²)) × cos(i)
  * Where a = semi-major axis, a_J = Jupiter's semi-major axis = 5.20 AU, e = eccentricity, i = inclination
  * JFCs: 2 < T_J < 3; Long-period comets: T_J < 2; Asteroids: T_J > 3
  * T_J is a conserved quantity in the restricted three-body problem (Sun + Jupiter + comet)
* ORBITAL MECHANICS — KEPLER'S LAWS:
  * Period: T² = a³ (in AU and years), so Holmes with a ≈ 3.6 AU has T = 3.6^(3/2) ≈ 6.8 years
  * Vis-viva equation: v² = GM(2/r - 1/a) — velocity at any point in orbit
  * At perihelion (r=q): v_perihelion = √(GM(2/q - 1/a)) — maximum orbital velocity
  * At aphelion (r=Q): v_aphelion = √(GM(2/Q - 1/a)) — minimum orbital velocity
* JUPITER'S ROLE IN JFC DYNAMICS:
  * Jupiter acts as a gravitational shepherd: strong perturbations from Jupiter scatter Kuiper Belt objects and outer solar system bodies into JFC orbits
  * Close approaches to Jupiter ("Jupiter encounters") can change orbital elements dramatically: Δa/a ~ ΔE/E where ΔE = energy kick from Jupiter fly-by
  * Comet Shoemaker-Levy 9: tidal disruption during Jupiter encounter (1992), impact chain (1994) = extreme example of Jupiter's gravitational influence
