PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* BROADBAND UGRI PHOTOMETRY OF COMET HOLMES:
  * The ugri filter system (SDSS/Sloan Digital Sky Survey photometric system) provides broadband photometric coverage:
    * u-band: central wavelength λ_eff ≈ 354nm (ultraviolet); width Δλ ≈ 60nm
    * g-band: λ_eff ≈ 477nm (blue-green); Δλ ≈ 140nm
    * r-band: λ_eff ≈ 623nm (red); Δλ ≈ 140nm
    * i-band: λ_eff ≈ 763nm (near-IR); Δλ ≈ 150nm
  * Cometary dust has a reddening slope S' = [r-band flux / g-band flux] normalized at a reference wavelength
  * Dust color from ugri: S' ≈ 10-15%/1000Å for typical fresh cometary dust (neutral to slightly red)
  * Gas contribution in g-band: CN (0,0) band at 388nm, C₂ Swan bands (Δv=0 at 516nm) can enhance g-band flux above pure continuum
* COMA MORPHOLOGY AND ISOPHOTES:
  * Isophotes = contours of constant surface brightness I(ρ,θ) = const
  * For an isotropic, steady-state coma: isophotes are perfect circles; for Holmes (outburst-driven non-steady-state): they are elliptical
  * The anti-solar elongation of isophotes: due to radiation pressure acting on dust grains (F_rad ∝ β × GM_sun/r²)
  * The asymmetry parameter A_p(θ) = F_observed(θ)/F_isotropic(θ) quantifies the non-radial outgassing
* u-BAND BEHAVIOR:
  * The u-band shows a nearly stellar comet (no resolved coma) despite the coma being visible in g,r,i
  * This is because: cometary dust has very low u-band albedo (carbonaceous grains absorb UV strongly)
  * Cometary dust geometric albedo: p_u < 0.03; p_g ≈ 0.04-0.06; p_r ≈ 0.05-0.08
  * Also: no known cometary gas species has strong emission in the u-band (most gas fluorescence is in the optical/UV vacuum UV < 300nm, outside u-band)
* SUN AND VELOCITY ARROWS:
  * The "Sun" direction indicates the anti-solar direction (where the dust tail points): r-hat_sun (unit vector from comet to Sun)
  * The "V" vector indicates the heliocentric velocity direction (V_comet in its orbit)
  * The angle between V and -r-hat_sun gives the angle at which the ion tail deflects from the anti-solar direction (solar wind aberration): tan(α_aberration) = V_solar_wind / V_comet
* ISOPHOTE FITTING:
  * Fitting ellipses to isophotes: I = I₀ × exp(-ρ/ρ_0) (exponential profile) or I = I₀ × (ρ/ρ_0)^(-k) (power law)
  * For Holmes: deviation from power-law (k≠1) indicates the outburst shell expansion is NOT in steady state
  * The isophote elongation axis angle gives the position angle of maximum outgassing (connected to the active nucleus rotation)
