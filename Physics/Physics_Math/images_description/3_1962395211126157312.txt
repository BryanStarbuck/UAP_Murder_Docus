* CROP CIRCLE STEM NODE BENDING — BLT RESEARCH DOCUMENTATION:
  * This close-up photograph documents the INTERIOR of a crop circle formation at stem level
  * BLT Research Team (Burke, Levengood, Talbott; 1990-2010): the most systematic scientific investigation of crop circle physical effects
  * The key finding: in genuine formations (as opposed to board-and-rope hoaxes), the stems are BENT at the nodes (intercalary meristem regions) but NOT BROKEN
  * Mechanism: the intercalary meristem (growth zone at each node) retains turgor pressure and growth capacity; when rapidly heated (or exposed to microwave radiation), steam pressure builds inside the node → the node expands and bends, but the cellulose cell walls are NOT ruptured
  * In mechanical (human) flattening: the stems BREAK at the nodes or crimp without bending (cell walls rupture irreversibly)
  * Heat required for node bending: ΔT ~ 50-100°C above ambient for 10-100ms → power density P/A ~ 10⁵-10⁶ W/m²
