SHAPES AND ELEMENTS:
* Black-and-white graphic design/logo on a white background
* 3-fold rotational symmetry (C₃ point group)
* Complex interlocking ring/arc design

PRIMARY STRUCTURE:
* THREE OUTER LOBE CIRCLES: at the upper, lower-left, and lower-right positions, three large circle outlines (rings) form the outer extremities of the design
* Each outer lobe circle consists of: a large outer ring + two concentric inner rings (forming a three-ring target/bullseye structure at each lobe)
* The three lobe circles are interconnected — they overlap with each other and with the central element via curved connecting bands

CENTRAL ELEMENT:
* At the geometric center: a BULLSEYE target pattern — a small filled disc (the innermost circle) surrounded by a white ring (gap) surrounded by a black ring
* The central bullseye is connected to all three lobe circles via curved flow elements

CONNECTING BANDS:
* Between each pair of adjacent lobe circles: wide curved black bands flowing from one lobe into the next, creating an interlocked "knot" appearance
* The bands cross over and under each other in the center, creating a Celtic knotwork topology
* The overall topology: the three bands form a trefoil knot (or a simplified version thereof) with the three outer lobes

MORPHOLOGY:
* The design has the visual topology of a THREE-PETAL KNOT or CELTIC TRISKELION KNOT
* It is also strongly similar to the BIOHAZARD SYMBOL (see 3_1964087117203861504) but with concentric rings at each lobe rather than the crescent shape

COLORS:
* Black: all filled elements
* White: background and the ring gaps

PHYSICS / INFORMATION CONCEPTS:
* 3-FOLD ROTATING FIELD TOPOLOGY:
  * The 3-fold interlocking ring design encodes: 3-phase rotating electromagnetic field geometry with the three outer lobes = the three phase poles (at 120° angular spacing)
  * The interlocking bands (connecting each lobe to the adjacent two) = the mutual coupling between the three phases of the rotating field: each phase couples to the other two in a balanced fashion
  * The central bullseye = the axis of the rotating field (the center where the three-phase fields superpose to create a uniformly rotating field)
  * The concentric rings at each lobe = the radial field profile at each pole (Bessel function: J₁(kr) at the pole maximum)
* COMPARISON TO BIOHAZARD SYMBOL:
  * The biohazard symbol (ISO 7010:2011) was designed in 1966 by Charles Baldwin of Dow Chemical to be: memorable, unique, meaningful to the uninformed, and learnable
  * Both this design and the biohazard symbol share the same 3-fold lobe + central hub + interlocking band topology
  * The primary difference: biohazard symbol has crescent-shaped outer lobes; this design has full concentric-ring outer lobes
* CROP FORMATION ANALOGY:
  * This design is similar to the abstract schematic of a triple-triskelion crop formation: three large outer circles (the "shoulder" circles of the formation) connected by the flowing bands of flattened crop
