BIOHAZARD SYMBOL STRUCTURE:
* OUTER ELEMENT: three large crescent shapes at the top, lower-left, and lower-right positions (at 120° spacings); each crescent curves away from the center
* MIDDLE RING: a circular ring centered on the main axis (shared central hub)
* INNER CONNECTING ELEMENT: three curved flow bands connecting the central ring to each of the three outer crescents — each band curves outward from the center and then curves back inward to the crescent lobe
* The three flow bands create an overlapping appearance where they cross each other near the center
* CENTRAL CIRCLE: a small circle (bullseye center) at the exact geometric center
* The complete symbol: crescents + central ring + connecting bands + central circle, all in black

MATHEMATICAL TOPOLOGY:
* The biohazard symbol is topologically a trefoil-like knot-adjacent structure (not a true knot but a 2D representation with 3-fold symmetry and overlapping paths)
* The crescent lobes: each crescent is a lune — the intersection of two circles of different radii, or a circular arc subtending approximately 240° with the ends curving away
