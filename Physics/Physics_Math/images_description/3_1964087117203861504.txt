OVERALL DESCRIPTION:
A clean, high-resolution rendering of the standard international biohazard symbol on a white background. This is the universally recognized symbol designed in 1966 to identify biological hazard warning. It is presented here as a comparison/reference alongside crop circle formations that have similar three-fold symmetric patterns.

IMAGE DIMENSIONS: Approximately 960 x 960 pixels
BACKGROUND: Pure white

SYMBOL DESCRIPTION:

The biohazard symbol consists of:

Three outer "bident" or "crescent" shapes:
- Three identical curved crescent/blade shapes arranged at 120-degree intervals with three-fold rotational symmetry
- Each shape sweeps in a clockwise curve from its base to its pointed tip
- The shapes are positioned: one pointing upward (12 o'clock), one pointing to the lower-left (approximately 8 o'clock), and one pointing to the lower-right (approximately 4 o'clock)
- Each crescent is solid black and relatively thin-to-medium width
- The outer tips of each crescent curve inward/backward

Central concentric ring system:
- At the center of the symbol: a set of concentric circular elements
- Outermost: A large arc at the top connecting to the three outer crescents (this creates the upper portion of the symbol)
- Inner arcs: Two (or more) concentric arcs creating a segmented ring
- The inner concentric elements create the distinctive "three-armed ring" at the center
- This inner ring has three "windows" or open areas at 120-degree intervals where the crescents attach

Central circle (innermost):
- At the very center: a small white circle (approximately 70 pixels diameter) — the open center
- This white circle is the innermost element, surrounded by the concentric ring system

Overall:
- The symbol has perfectly defined mathematical curves
- The three outer crescents + the central concentric rings together create the full biohazard symbol
- The symbol has six-fold apparent symmetry in appearance despite three-fold actual rotational symmetry (because each element has a 180-degree flip symmetry)

PHYSICS/SCIENCE RELEVANCE: The biohazard symbol appears in this UAP physics research collection for comparison with crop circle formations that have nearly identical three-fold symmetric patterns. This is significant because:
1. Multiple crop circles have appeared that closely resemble the biohazard symbol (or its geometric parent structure)
2. The appearance of biohazard-like symbols in crop formations is interpreted as a warning about biological contamination or modification
3. The standard biohazard symbol was designed specifically to be: non-directional, non-culturally specific, unfamiliar to avoid confusion with existing symbols, and easily recognized
4. Some researchers interpret crop circle biohazard-like patterns as warnings about genetic modification, viral contamination, or biological weapons
5. The geometric parent of the biohazard symbol (three circles arranged at 120 degrees around a central ring) appears frequently in electromagnetic field diagrams, making it a candidate for both "three-phase field emitter" AND "biological warning" interpretations
