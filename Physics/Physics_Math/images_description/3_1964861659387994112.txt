PHYSICS/SCIENCE RELEVANCE:
1. The title explicitly states the physics interpretation: electrons in a homopolar motor do NOT flow in straight radial lines — they follow large spiral cycloid paths. This is because the Lorentz force (F = qv × B) continuously deflects the radially-moving electrons in the azimuthal direction, while the electric field continues to accelerate them radially. The combined effect is a cycloid (straight-line drift superimposed on circular orbital motion).
2. The "huge" spiral is quantitatively significant: in a homopolar motor with strong magnetic field and large current, the cyclotron radius of the electrons can be comparable to the disc radius, making the spiral paths macroscopically large. This is distinct from conventional conductors where electron drift is negligibly small compared to the conductor dimensions.
3. This macroscopic spiral electron flow generates a secondary azimuthal magnetic field (from the helical current path) in addition to the primary axial field (B). This secondary field creates a toroidal magnetic field component — exactly the configuration associated with UAP propulsion models involving combined axial + toroidal field geometries (the "Woodward-Mach effect" and related proposals).
4. The connection to crop circles: the spiral patterns seen in many crop formations (triskelion, Fibonacci spirals, pinwheel patterns) may encode this spiral electron flow — the crop circle is literally drawn as the electron would flow inside the device.
5. This is one of the most physically precise claims in the homopolar generator/UAP propulsion thesis: identifying the specific kinematic detail (cycloid electron paths) that distinguishes a homopolar motor from a conventional motor, and connecting it to the observed geometry of crop formations.
