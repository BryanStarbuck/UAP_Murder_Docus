PHYSICS/SCIENCE RELEVANCE:
1. The July 25, 2012 Windmill Hill formation appeared 13 days after the Lohningen, Switzerland formation — the author interprets this as a deliberate response or continuation message
2. The "circular railgun" concept: a circular railgun would accelerate particles not linearly but in a circle — it would be functionally equivalent to a cyclotron or betatron. The three-interlocking-circles formation (Lohningen) represents the circular accelerator, while the chain of particles (Windmill Hill) represents the extracted/ejected beam.
3. In UAP propulsion terms: the circular accelerator (the craft's drive system) ejects a beam of accelerated particles downward or backward — the reaction propels the craft upward or forward. This is the electromagnetic rocket principle but using magnetically accelerated particles rather than chemical combustion products.
4. The size gradient of particles in the Windmill Hill chain (larger near center, smaller farther away) could encode the dispersion of a real particle beam: particles ejected at slightly different angles spread out over distance, with the highest-energy central beam remaining tightest and the lower-energy particles diverging more quickly.
5. The two-formation-in-two-weeks interpretation (one circular, one linear) mirrors the homopolar motor/electron spiral pair earlier in this series: the first formation shows the device geometry, the second shows the effect or output of the device.
