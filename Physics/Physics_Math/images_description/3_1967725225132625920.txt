CADUCEUS vs. ROD OF ASCLEPIUS:
The caduceus (TWO serpents + wings + globe) shown here is distinct from the Rod of Asclepius (one serpent, no wings), which is the true medical symbol. The caduceus is associated with Hermes/Mercury (messenger, commerce, travel, thieves). This distinction is often confused:
- Caduceus (two serpents + wings): Hermes/Mercury, used by US Army Medical Corps by historical error since 1902
- Rod of Asclepius (one serpent, no wings): Asclepius god of medicine, used by WHO, AMA, most medical associations
The image here (two serpents + wings) is the caduceus/Hermes staff
