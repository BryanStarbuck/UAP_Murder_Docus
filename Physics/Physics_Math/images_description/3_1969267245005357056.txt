PHYSICS / INFORMATION CONCEPTS:
* LOGARITHMIC SPIRAL + DISC SYSTEM — ANALOGOUS TO 3_1963004586245160962:
  * The combination of a CW logarithmic spiral element (upper) connected to a large flattened disc (lower) matches the earlier Steve Alexander formation analyzed in 3_1963004586245160962 (spiral + concentric ring target)
  * A logarithmic spiral r = r₀ e^{aθ} is self-similar under scaling: magnification by factor e^{2πa} maps the spiral to itself
  * This self-similarity encodes scale invariance of the electromagnetic field: B(r) ∝ r^{-1} (the field of a line current) or B(r) ∝ r (uniform rotating field in the interior of a solenoid)

* SPIRAL AS FARADAY DISC EVOLUTION:
  * The tight multi-turn spiral at the top of the formation = the Faraday/Archimedean spiral coil of the homopolar generator (see 3_1964659271616102401 — 19th-century Faraday disc engraving showing Archimedean coil)
  * The large circle at the lower-right = the conductive rotating disc (the Faraday disc element)
  * The curved connecting arcs = the current flow path from the spiral generator coil to the disc rotor
  * Together: the formation encodes the complete homopolar motor/generator circuit: spiral coil (stator winding) → arc connecting path → disc (rotor)

* SMALL SATELLITES AT SPIRAL HEAD — QUANTIZED FIELD MODES:
  * The 3-4 small circles at the spiral tip correspond to the spiral waveguide's evanescent near-field modes: at the termination of a tapered spiral, higher-order modes are excited that decay exponentially (evanescent waves with k_z imaginary)
  * The size of each satellite circle = the amplitude of one mode: solid circles = propagating modes, small/faint circles = evanescent modes
  * The arrangement in an arc matches the known form factor of a tapered spiral antenna's radiation pattern

* CONNECTING ARC PATHS — TRAJECTORY OF CHARGED CARRIERS:
  * The curved arcs connecting the spiral head to the discs follow a cycloid-like path: the path of a charged particle in combined electric (radial) and magnetic (axial) fields
  * In a homopolar disc: E_r (radial electric field due to charge separation) + B_z (axial magnetic field from the stator) → particle trajectory follows a curved arc = E×B drift: v_drift = (E×B)/B² (perpendicular to both E and B)
  * The arc paths encode the E×B drift velocity field of the rotating charge carriers in the disc
