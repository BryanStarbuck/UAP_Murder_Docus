PHYSICS/SCIENCE RELEVANCE:
1. DNA DOUBLE HELIX CROSS-SECTION: The formation depicts the X-shape and ring row that appears in a CROSS-SECTIONAL view of a DNA double helix: the two crossing diagonal bands = the two sugar-phosphate backbone strands crossing each other in perspective view; the row of ring-outline circles = the nitrogenous base pairs connecting the two strands at the crossing point; the upper and lower eye shapes = the major and minor groove regions. This X-shaped cross-section view of DNA is the visual signature that was used by Rosalind Franklin's X-ray diffraction photograph (Photo 51, 1952) to determine the helical structure of DNA
2. PHOTO 51 REFERENCE: Rosalind Franklin's X-ray crystallography photograph of B-form DNA (Photo 51) shows an X-shaped diffraction pattern with a central cross and reflections at the crossing arms — exactly the pattern seen in this crop formation. The horizontal row of circles = the layer line reflections in the X-ray diffraction pattern, and the diagonal X arms = the main cross pattern. This formation may be an explicit encoding of the Photo 51 X-ray crystallography pattern that revealed DNA's double helix structure
3. VESICA PISCIS MAJOR/MINOR GROVE: The two vesica piscis eye shapes (upper and lower) represent the major and minor grooves of the DNA double helix when viewed in cross-section or projection: the wider major groove (upper eye) and narrower minor groove (lower eye) are the two groove regions in the DNA helix where proteins bind. The eye shapes are the classic Vesica Piscis (intersection of two equal circles), which encodes √3 as the ratio of height to width
4. X-SHAPE AS LORENTZ FORCE DIAGRAM: In an alternative physics interpretation, the X-shape with horizontal brush contacts represents: two counter-rotating discs (left arm going up-left and right arm going up-right after crossing below) with the horizontal ring row = the brush contact ring between them. The upper diverging arms = the incoming electrical leads, the lower tentacles = the outgoing current paths. The crossing X = the point where the current direction reverses between the two counter-rotating disc stages
5. JELLYFISH / DRAGONFLY UAP MORPHOLOGY: The formation's overall shape (X-body with four trailing tentacles below) matches the "jellyfish" or "dragonfly" UAP morphology reported by some witnesses — UAPs that appear as a central body with trailing plasma appendages. The ring row at the center = the central disc/body, the four trailing tentacles = the plasma discharge trails
