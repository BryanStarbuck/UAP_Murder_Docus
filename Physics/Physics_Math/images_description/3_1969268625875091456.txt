PHYSICS / INFORMATION CONCEPTS:
* NAZCA MUMMY SPECIMENS — MORPHOLOGICAL ANALYSIS:
  * This photograph almost certainly shows one of the "Nazca mummy" hand specimens (also called "Inkari" specimens) publicized by the "Comunicaciones Orion" organization and examined by researchers including Thierry Jamin and Jaime Maussan in 2017-2023
  * The Nazca specimens were allegedly discovered in the Peruvian Atacama region near the Nazca Lines

* THREE-FINGERED MORPHOLOGY:
  * The three-fingered specimen contrasts with the five-fingered human reference hand
  * Three-fingered anatomy is non-mammalian: most terrestrial mammals (including humans) have five digits (pentadactyly) as a conserved vertebrate feature
  * Three-fingered hands occur naturally in: some dinosaur species (Iguanodon had 5 fingers but 3 functional), some lizards, and birds (avian dinosaur descendants)
  * If authentic: the three-fingered anatomy encodes a fundamentally different evolutionary lineage from the pentadactyl vertebrate tree
  * The elongated finger aspect ratio (length/width >> human norm) suggests adaptation for: precision grasping at small scale, or arboreal/climbing grip

* DIATOMITE PRESERVATION:
  * The chalk-white coating of the specimen is consistent with preservation in diatomaceous earth (diatomite): amorphous silica from fossilized diatom shells
  * Diatomite is hydrophilic (large surface area ~20-200 m²/g, pore size 10-100 nm) and absorbs body fluids during mummification
  * This type of preservation is distinct from Andean salt/cold mummification — suggesting deliberate entombment in silica-rich material

* SOCKET FEATURES AT WRIST:
  * The multiple socket/pit features at the base of the specimen are consistent with carpal bone articulation surfaces (wrist joint sockets)
  * If the specimen has more carpal bones than the human 8 (scaphoid, lunate, triquetrum, pisiform, trapezium, trapezoid, capitate, hamate), the wrist morphology is non-human
  * The visible features could represent: a different number of carpal bones, larger metacarpal-carpal joints, or additional metacarpal bones for the missing 4th and 5th fingers

* SCALE COMPARISON:

  * The elongated three-finger arrangement suggests a hand overall length of approximately 15-20cm (similar to human) but with fingers comprising a larger fraction of total length
