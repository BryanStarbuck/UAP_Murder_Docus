PHYSICS / INFORMATION CONCEPTS:
* MULTI-TAP FARADAY DISC — VOLTAGE PROFILE MEASUREMENT:
  * This diagram shows a Faraday homopolar disc with 5 radially distributed voltage taps (brush contacts at 5 radial positions)
  * The disc rotates CCW at angular velocity ω in an axial magnetic field B_z (directed into or out of the page — not shown explicitly, but implied by the rotation and voltage generation)
  * The EMF generated between a tap at radius r and the central axle: V(r) = ½ω B_z r²
  * The EMF between two taps at radii r₁ and r₂: ΔV = ½ω B_z (r₂² - r₁²)
  * For 5 taps at evenly spaced radii r_n = n × (R_outer - R_inner)/5 + R_inner (n = 1, 2, 3, 4, 5):
    - The voltage increments increase with r: ΔV_n+1 - ΔV_n = ½ω B_z (r_{n+1}² - r_n²) > ΔV_n - ΔV_{n-1} (the increments are larger at larger radii, since r² grows faster than r)

* CCW ROTATION AND FIELD POLARITY:
  * The CCW rotation (as viewed from above) with axial B_z upward: the Lorentz force on positive charge carriers in the disc is F = qv × B = q(rω θ̂) × (B_z ẑ) = -qrω B_z r̂ (inward radial direction)
  * This means positive charges accumulate at the inner axle, making it positive; the outer rim becomes negative
  * The voltage taps at larger radii are at more negative potential relative to the axle
  * If B_z is downward (into page): the force reverses, outer rim becomes positive

* MULTI-TAP APPLICATION:
  * 5 taps generating incremental voltages: V₁ < V₂ < V₃ < V₄ < V₅ (from inner to outer)
  * These can power 5 circuits simultaneously at different voltages from a single rotating disc
  * In a UAP propulsion context: the multi-tap disc powers 5 phase coils of a 5-phase stator, each coil receiving the appropriate voltage for its position in the rotating field sequence
  * 5-phase systems are used for fault tolerance (if one phase fails, the system continues with reduced but non-zero output) and for smoother torque ripple (compared to 3-phase)
