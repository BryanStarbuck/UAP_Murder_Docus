PHYSICS CONCEPT:
This aerial photograph documents a crop circle formation with the geometric structure of a DNA double helix. The double-helix crop circle is one of the most famous formations — the Crabwood DNA formation (2002, near Alton Barnes/East Field, Wiltshire). In UAP research this formation is interpreted as a communication from non-human intelligence encoding biological information (DNA), potentially indicating that the makers share or know human genetic structure. In physics terms, the double-helix geometry is also associated with helical toroidal field lines in plasma physics, magnetic flux tube topology, and the proposed helical structure of certain field propulsion plasma discharge patterns.
