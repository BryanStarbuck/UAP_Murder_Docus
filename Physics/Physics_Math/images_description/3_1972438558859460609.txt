PHYSICS CONCEPT:
This collage makes the explicit claim that crop circle formations are recreations of Ed Leedskalnin's magnetic inventions at Coral Castle (Homestead, Florida). Leedskalnin (1887-1951) was a Latvian-American who claimed to have discovered the secrets of ancient Egyptian construction and magnetism, building Coral Castle single-handedly using methods he never fully disclosed. He built a "spinning magnetic flywheel" — a large stone/concrete disk with gear teeth and a magnetic rotor — which he claimed demonstrated his understanding of "magnetic current." The collage argues that the gear-tooth crop circle and H-shaped crop circle are direct reproductions of his devices, implying non-human intelligence was aware of and endorsing Leedskalnin's magnetic discoveries. This is highly relevant to UAP physics because Leedskalnin's theories about "individual magnets," magnetic currents, and antigravity are often cited in discussions of alternative electromagnetic propulsion systems.
