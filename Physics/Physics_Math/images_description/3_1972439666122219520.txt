PHYSICS CONCEPT:
This is a close-up overhead photograph of Ed Leedskalnin's "spinning magnetic flywheel" at Coral Castle, Homestead, Florida. Leedskalnin built this large stone/concrete disk with a gear-toothed outer edge and a central rotor mechanism. He claimed it demonstrated principles of "magnetic current" and that understanding the geometry and rotation of individual magnets (which he believed were bar-shaped particles, not dipoles) allowed manipulation of electromagnetic fields in ways not recognized by mainstream physics. The flywheel is specifically associated with claims that Leedskalnin discovered the same physics principles used to build ancient megalithic structures, and that this knowledge overlaps with UAP propulsion principles involving rotating magnetic fields. The gear-tooth outer edge creates a specific pulsed magnetic field pattern during rotation that Leedskalnin believed was fundamental.
