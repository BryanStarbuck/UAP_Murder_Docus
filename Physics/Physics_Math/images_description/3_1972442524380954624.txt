PHYSICS CONCEPT:
The portrait appears to be of Jean-François Champollion (1790-1832), the French philologist who deciphered Egyptian hieroglyphics using the Rosetta Stone. The right panel shows an Egyptian hieroglyphic cartouche — the oval frame used to enclose royal names in ancient Egyptian script. In UAP physics research this combination is used in discussions connecting ancient Egyptian knowledge to advanced physics or non-human intelligence. Arguments include: (1) Champollion's decipherment revealed that Egyptian texts may contain encoded technical knowledge; (2) Egyptian symbolic systems (ankh, cartouche, eye of Horus) are reinterpreted as diagrams of electromagnetic or field physics principles; (3) the architectural and mathematical achievements of ancient Egypt are cited as evidence of access to physics beyond what the civilization should have had. The ankh in particular is frequently reinterpreted as a diagram of toroidal energy flow.
