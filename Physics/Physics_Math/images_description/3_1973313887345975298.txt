PHYSICS CONCEPT:
This is a digital/print representation of the binary dot-matrix from the Chilbolton Arecibo Response crop formation (or the Crabwood 2002 formation). The binary pattern encodes information about: (1) the numbers 1-10 in binary; (2) atomic numbers of key elements (hydrogen, carbon, nitrogen, oxygen, phosphorus); (3) nucleotide structure of DNA; (4) a humanoid figure with height indicator; (5) world population; (6) solar system diagram; (7) a schematic of the Arecibo dish (or in the crop version, a different transmitter geometry). The blurry rendering is a scan or low-resolution photograph of a printout used for analysis purposes.

HIGH-LEVEL SUMMARY:
Blurry black-and-white dot-matrix representation of the Chilbolton Arecibo Response binary encoding. Low-resolution scan or printout. Used in UAP research for close analysis of the binary encoding structure.
