PHYSICS CONCEPT:
This satellite image documents a "crop circle ghost" — the visible soil marks remaining in a plowed field where a crop circle existed in the previous growing season. The presence of ghost marks demonstrates that crop formations affect the soil below the crop — the growth pattern, moisture retention, or soil chemistry is altered in the formation footprint, making it visible years later even after plowing. This is used in UAP research as physical evidence that the formation mechanism interacts with the soil and subsoil, not merely the crop surface — inconsistent with simple board-and-plank human creation but consistent with a field (electromagnetic, acoustic, or microwave) that affects the ground. Cross/plus-shaped ghost marks are interesting because they form specific angles that can be measured to verify geometric precision.
