PHYSICS CONCEPT:
This ground-level photograph documents the boundary between standing and flattened crop within a crop circle formation. Two key physical observations: (1) the color difference between standing (green) and flattened (silver-white) crop — in genuine formations the color change is often attributed to a rapid desiccation or crystallographic change in the stalk cells caused by high-energy field exposure; (2) the stalks appear bent at the node rather than broken, which requires a brief high-temperature or high-humidity condition to soften the stalk cells, then they cool and harden in the bent position. This is physical evidence consistent with a rapid thermal/microwave pulse event rather than mechanical stomping. This type of documentation is used in UAP research to establish the physical mechanism of formation creation.
