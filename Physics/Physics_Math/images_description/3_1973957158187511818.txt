PHYSICS CONCEPT:
This ground-level photograph documents the interior structure of a crop formation, showing the complex multi-directional layering of flattened crop. The "woven" appearance of multiple directional layers (stalks in one section go left-to-right while adjacent section goes toward viewer, creating an interlocked pattern) is a key physical characteristic of genuine formations. This multi-directional layering creates mechanical interlocking that prevents wind from re-raising the flattened crop. In UAP research this complex layering is cited as evidence that the formation mechanism creates a rotating or patterned field that lays different sections of crop in specifically calculated directions — not achievable by simple stomping boards which would create unidirectional flattening.
