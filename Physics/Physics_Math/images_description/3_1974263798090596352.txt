PHYSICS CONCEPT:
This is the famous Nuremberg 1561 broadsheet — a contemporary illustrated news report describing a mass UFO sighting over Nuremberg, Germany on April 14, 1561. The original German text describes numerous objects in the sky including globes, cylinders, discs, and crosses, engaged in an apparent aerial battle. The objects then fell to the Earth, appeared to burst into flames, and vanished into smoke. This is one of the earliest and most detailed historical UAP records, predating the modern UFO era by nearly 400 years. In UAP physics it is cited as: (1) historical evidence of UAP activity across centuries, not a modern phenomenon; (2) early documentation of UAP varieties (spherical "plasma orbs," cylindrical craft, disc/cross shapes); (3) evidence that these phenomena were witnessed by large numbers of people (broadsheets were printed to report significant public events). The Hans Glaser broadsheet is considered one of the best-documented historical UAP events.
