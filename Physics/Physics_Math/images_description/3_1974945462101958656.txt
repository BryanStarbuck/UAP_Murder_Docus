PHYSICS CONCEPT:
This image documents a complex ornate crop circle formation interpreted as encoding the Latin phrase "timeo ET ferentes" — a play on the Trojan War phrase "timeo Danaos et dona ferentes" (I fear the Greeks bearing gifts, from Virgil's Aeneid), substituting "ET" (extraterrestrials) for "Danaos" (Greeks). The individual letters annotated around the outer ring of petals are identified as spelling this Latin message. This is one of the more linguistically sophisticated crop circle interpretations in UAP research — arguing that a formation's geometric elements encode a literal text message. The "I fear extraterrestrials bearing gifts" interpretation expresses a cautionary message about the nature of non-human contact.
