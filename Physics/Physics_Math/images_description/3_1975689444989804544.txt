PHYSICS CONCEPT:
This is the complete Crabwood 2002 crop formation showing both components: (1) the alien face binary grid (tall rectangle encoding a binary pixel image of a Grey alien face); (2) the spiral disc (a binary encoding along a spiral track, like a vinyl record or DVD). The disc's spiral binary encoding, when decoded from outside to inside (or inside to outside), yields the ASCII message beginning "Beware the bearers of FALSE gifts..." The formation is of historic importance in UAP research as one of the most complex and informative formations ever recorded. The combination of a visual image (the face) with an encoded text message on a separate disc element demonstrates a sophisticated communication design.
