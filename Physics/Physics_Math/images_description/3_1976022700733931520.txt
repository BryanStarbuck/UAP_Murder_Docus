PHYSICS CONCEPT:
This extremely close-up aerial photograph of a large complex crop formation reveals the micro-structure and panel-level detail of a mandala-type formation. The concentric ring structure with evenly spaced triangular notches and radially arranged outer panels demonstrates sophisticated geometric planning requiring precise angular and radial measurements. The scale (estimated 80-150m diameter) and geometric precision make plank-board human construction in darkness exceedingly difficult. In UAP crop circle research, such formations are studied for: (1) precision of angular spacing and radial symmetry; (2) crop lay patterns and node bending evidence (Levengood/BLT Research methodology); (3) the possible use of electromagnetic or acoustic standing waves (Chladni patterns) as the formation mechanism.
