PHYSICS CONCEPT:
The "Face of the crop artist" at Newton Barrow (June 15, 2015) is a notable crop formation because its structure, when viewed from aerial perspective, resembles a stylized humanoid face — interpreted by some researchers as the crop formation creators leaving a self-portrait or signature. This face-in-crop format has precedent in the Crabwood 2002 Grey alien face formation. The hourglass/bowtie central element is a recurring geometric motif in crop formations (related to the Vesica Piscis in sacred geometry — the intersection of two equal circles). The bright-white flattened rapeseed against dark green standing crop creates unusually high contrast. Lucy Pringle is a noted crop circle aerial photographer whose photographs are widely cited in formation research.
