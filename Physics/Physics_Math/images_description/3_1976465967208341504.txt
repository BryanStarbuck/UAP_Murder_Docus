Headline: "Flying Saucer Sought in Death of Horse"

Article text:
"ALAMOSA, Colo., Oct. 5 — (UPI) — Authorities and residents near here are trying to find out who or what killed Snippy and there is a growing body of opinion that the culprits were traveling in a flying saucer.

Snippy was a 3-year-old Appaloosa horse whose carcass was found Sept. 9 with the flesh from his neck and head neatly stripped away.

Two weeks after the animal was found, Duane E. Martin, a forestry aide with the U.S. Forestry Service who is qualified in radiological monitoring, brought a radiation measuring device to the scene and 'got a high reading in a couple of spots near where the horse was killed.'

Readings were registered on black spots on the ground nearby and on the boots of Mrs. Berle Lewis of Alamosa, who was along.

The mystery, coupled with reports of strange aircraft recently, have led to the opinion by many that the death of the horse could be connected with the sightings.

Alamosa County Sheriff Ben Phillips disagree Thursday. 'I don't believe in flying saucers,' he said. 'I believe the horse was killed by lightning.'

But he said there were no signs of the horse being burned as is nearly always the case in lightning deaths.

Phillips said there had been at least four or five reports of flying aircraft in the area in the past year."
