PHYSICS CONCEPT:
This solar system orbital diagram shows the hyperbolic trajectory of 3I/Atlas — the third confirmed interstellar object detected passing through our solar system (after 1I/Oumuamua in 2017 and 2I/Borisov in 2019). The trajectory line enters from outside the solar system, passes through the inner solar system (inside Mars orbit, potentially inside Earth orbit), and exits on the other side. The bright cyan trajectory line contrasts with the colored planetary orbits for easy visualization of the approach geometry. In UAP research, 3I/Atlas is being analyzed as a potential interstellar probe (cf. Avi Loeb's Galileo Project / Project Lyra research), following the probe hypothesis proposed for 1I/Oumuamua. The trajectory parameters (hyperbolic excess velocity, perihelion distance, inclination) are being compared against natural comet/asteroid populations to identify anomalous characteristics.
