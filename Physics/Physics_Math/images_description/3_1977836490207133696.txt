This extreme close-up reveals the micro-structure of a crop formation's binary pixel grid. The key observations for crop formation mechanism research: (1) The individual "pixel" elements are clusters of standing crop — typically 3-5 stalks standing together as a unit, surrounded by flattened stalks; (2) The boundary between standing and flattened clusters is sharp (not gradual) — suggesting the formation mechanism acts at a precise spatial scale; (3) The lattice orientation (diagonal rather than aligned with field rows) indicates the formation was not created by walking along field rows.
