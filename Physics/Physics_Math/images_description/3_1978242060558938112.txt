PHYSICS CONCEPT:
The Pleiades (M45) is the open star cluster most commonly referenced in UAP contact narratives — the alleged home star system of the "Pleiadians" or "Nordics," a specific extraterrestrial group described by numerous contact experiencers including Billy Meier (Semjase), Barbara Marciniak, and others. The Pleiades is approximately 440 light-years from Earth in the constellation Taurus. In UAP physics, the Pleiades connection is significant because the Pleiadians are described as the source of much of the UAP physics technology described in contact accounts (including free energy, anti-gravity, and faster-than-light travel). Alex Collier, the EMERTHER contact literature, and numerous other sources reference the Pleiades as a key origin system for ET visitors to Earth. The labeled individual star names (Alcyone, Merope, Maia, etc.) are used in specific location references in contact literature.
