PHYSICS CONCEPT:
This composite presents RC's "circular railgun" interpretation of two 2012 crop formations. The Lorentz force law (F = IL × B, or for a conducting medium F = J × B per unit volume) describes the electromagnetic force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field. In a railgun, current flows through a conducting projectile bridging two parallel rails; the magnetic field created by the rail currents (and any external B-field) exerts a Lorentz force that accelerates the projectile along the rails. RC's thesis: the July 12, 2012 Lohningen formation (trefoil = the 3-pole rotating "circular rail" system) and the July 25, 2012 Windmill Hill formation (horizontal beaded chain = the projectile + ejection track) together encode a circular railgun mechanism — where a rotating electromagnetic "track" (trefoil of 3-lobe plasma arcs) accelerates particles along a spiral or circular path, then ejects them radially. In MHD and plasma propulsion physics, this corresponds to a Hall-effect thruster operating in circular geometry, or to a magnetoplasmadynamic (MPD) accelerator where the combination of azimuthal current and axial B-field creates a J×B force that accelerates plasma radially outward. The two formations appearing two weeks apart and near each other are interpreted as a two-part message: "circular railgun geometry (trefoil)" + "particle ejection trajectory (horizontal chain)."
