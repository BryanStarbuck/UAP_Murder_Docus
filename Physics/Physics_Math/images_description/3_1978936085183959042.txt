PHYSICS CONCEPT:
RC's analysis of the August 3, 2016 crop formation near "Sparticles Wood" (the name is the pun: the formation appeared near a location called "Sparticles Wood," and "sparticles" is the colloquial term for supersymmetric particles). RC's thesis: the formation's visual illusion pattern — rotating nested squares creating a 3D corridor depth illusion — encodes physics information about supersymmetry (SUSY). Supersymmetry proposes that each Standard Model particle (quark, lepton, boson) has a supersymmetric partner ("sparticle") of opposite spin statistics (fermion ↔ boson). SUSY partners have not been detected at the LHC up to ~1.5 TeV mass range (as of 2024), leading to significant doubt about SUSY as a complete theory. However, in higher-dimensional SUSY models (particularly M-theory and brane-world scenarios), SUSY partners might exist in "shadow brane" or extra-dimensional sectors accessible only through gravitational interactions — exactly the "shadow particles" interpretation. The bottom-left diagram illustrates this precisely: Standard Model particles (colored spheres) on our brane, and SUSY shadow partners on an adjacent brane separated by an extra dimension. RC's interpretation of the formation: the crop artists (from an extra-dimensional or "shadow universe") are encoding a visual illusion to represent visual/dimensional paradoxes — the formation LOOKS like a 3D object from above (a depth illusion) but IS flat, just as SUSY shadow particles SEEM to be non-existent from our perspective but exist in an adjacent dimensional sector.
