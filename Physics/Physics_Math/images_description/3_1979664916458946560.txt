PHYSICS CONCEPT:
RC's analysis connects the August 13, 2013 Hackpen Hill formation (Wiltshire, England — one day before the nova event "Nova Delphini 2013" which appeared on August 14, 2013) to the famous Hubble Space Telescope ring nebula image of Supernova 1987A (the circumstellar ring around the 1987 supernova in the Large Magellanic Cloud). The visual similarity is striking: both show a ring of ~20-25 discrete circular bright nodes arranged in a ring. The Supernova 1987A ring is formed by the interaction of the supernova's shock wave with a pre-existing circumstellar equatorial ring (ejected by the progenitor star ~20,000 years before the explosion); each bright knot marks a Rayleigh-Taylor instability finger where the blast wave has compressed a cloud of denser circumstellar material. RC's argument: the formation appeared exactly one day before a nova event (Nova Delphini 2013) — a near-simultaneous astrophysical correlation that he interprets as evidence that the crop artists were predicting or referencing stellar explosion phenomena. The timing (formation on Aug 13, nova observed Aug 14) combined with the visual match to SN1987A suggests: the crop artists encoded a nova/supernova ring structure, and the timing was chosen to coincide with the actual nova event. In astrophysical terms, the ring of ~20-23 nodes with internal concentric structure corresponds directly to the Rayleigh-Taylor instability mode number m ≈ 20-25, which can be computed from the circumstellar ring's density contrast and the blast wave Atwood number A = (ρ₂-ρ₁)/(ρ₂+ρ₁).
