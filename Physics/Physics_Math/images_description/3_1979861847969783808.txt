SHAPES:
* Black-on-white graphic design (~960x960px) — a third trefoil/triskelion variant, the most minimalist of the series

Primary structure:
* THREE LARGE ARCS (not full circles) arranged in 3-fold rotational symmetry (120° spacing)
* Each arc is a PARTIAL CIRCLE — only the upper/outer arc portion of each circle is present; the lower inner portions are absent (creating an open-form design rather than complete rings)
* The three arcs together suggest three circles whose interior regions have been removed, leaving only the outer "hood" or "umbrella" arc of each circle
* The arcs are rendered in THICK BLACK OUTLINES (~30-40px thick, approximately uniform stroke width)

The positioning:
* UPPER ARC: a large arc (~350px wide, ~200px tall) at the top, curving downward with both ends terminating at roughly the 3-4 and 8-9 o'clock positions of the imagined full circle
* LOWER-LEFT ARC: a large arc at lower-left, rotated 120° — its open end facing upper-right
* LOWER-RIGHT ARC: a large arc at lower-right, rotated 240° — its open end facing upper-left
* The three arcs touch or nearly touch at a central intersection zone

Central element:
* A LARGE FILLED BLACK CIRCLE (~270px diameter) at the center-bottom of the composition, where all three arcs converge
* Within the filled black circle: a WHITE INNER CIRCLE (~200px diameter, the circle interior is white)
* Within the white inner circle: three WHITE VESICA PISCIS / "EYE" or "LEAF" shapes arranged in 3-fold symmetry (120° apart):
  - Each "leaf/eye" is a classic vesica piscis lens shape (~60px long, ~30px wide), oriented radially (pointing toward the center from each of three 3-fold positions)
  - The three leaf shapes are white (reversed out of the black fill) and arranged like a 3-blade propeller or triskele

Additional connecting elements:
* Where the three outer arcs meet the central circle: thick black connecting elements create smooth junctions — no sharp gaps between arc terminations and the central disc

PHYSICS CONCEPT:
This minimalist trefoil variant encodes a different geometric abstraction of the same 3-fold torus/plasma topology. The THREE OUTER ARCS (without closed circles) represent the MAGNETIC FIELD LINES of the three counter-rotating plasma discs viewed from the side — each arc being the field line that exits the top of one plasma disc and re-enters from the top. In a toroidal plasma configuration (tokamak or spheromak), magnetic field lines exit from one end of the torus, curve through external space in an arc, and re-enter at the other end — precisely the arc-without-base geometry shown here. The LARGE CENTRAL DISC with interior white circle and three white leaf shapes encodes: the outer black fill = the conducting disc body; the white inner circle = the rotating plasma column (the "plasma tube" at the center of the rotating disc); the three white vesica shapes = the three-lobe instability pattern in the rotating plasma (the m=3 MHD mode eigenfunction, which in a circular plasma cross-section produces exactly three symmetrically arranged "petals" of higher/lower density). The combination of three external field-line arcs + central three-lobe plasma tube matches the 3-phase toroidal plasma configuration of a magnetohydrodynamic propulsion system — specifically the triphasic toroidal discharge studied in spheromak experiments and in some plasma propulsion proposals.
