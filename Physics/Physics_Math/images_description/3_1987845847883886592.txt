* The 4 protrusions are at approximately 90° intervals.
* The overall outer envelope is roughly square with triangular protrusions at each side midpoint (or alternatively, a rounded square with point-extensions).

Inner Concentric Ring System:
* Just inside the outer star boundary: a clear circular ring or buffer zone of standing crop.
* This standing ring separates the outer star perimeter from the inner design.

Middle Annular Rings:
* Inside the standing ring: multiple concentric rings of alternating flattened/standing crop bands.
* These concentric rings create a classic "ringed" appearance similar to many English formations.
* The alternating standing/flattened bands are clearly visible due to the close-up framing.

Central Spiral Hub:
* At the very center of the formation: a small compact swirl or "nest" of tightly flattened crop.
* This is the characteristic clockwise/counter-clockwise lay center found in many authentic English crop formations.
