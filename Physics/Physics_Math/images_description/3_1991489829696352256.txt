Summary: Inverted-color processed color astronomical photograph of a comet: pink-red circular coma (concentric shell structure, dark nucleus at center visible in inversion) on the right side, with a narrow yellow-tan dust tail extending nearly horizontally to the left edge of the frame. White background with ~30-35 dark inverted star points. The inverted rendering maps the normal green C₂ coma emission to pink-red tones and the normal grey-white dust tail to yellow-tan. RC uses this in the 3I/ATLAS series to demonstrate image-processing effects on cometary color perception and to contrast dust tail vs. ion tail morphology.
