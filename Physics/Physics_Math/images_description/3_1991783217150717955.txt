Summary: Aerial photograph of the Crabwood 2002 crop formation (Winchester, Hampshire, August 15, 2002): a large rectangular area (left, ~60% of frame) containing a pixelated rasterized Grey alien face (large dark almond eyes, domed cranium, thin mouth) created by alternating standing/laid crop rows in a binary pixel grid; adjacent to a large circle (lower-right, ~35% of frame) containing ~20-30 concentric binary rings encoding an ASCII message ("Beware the bearers of FALSE gifts & their BROKEN PROMISES..."). Golden grain field background with parallel harvest lines. The Crabwood formation is one of the most famous and complex crop formations ever documented, containing the first known binary ASCII text message encoded in a crop formation. RC uses this as primary evidence that crop formations contain authenticated binary-encoded messages from non-human intelligences.
