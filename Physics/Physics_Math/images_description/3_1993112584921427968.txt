Physical Interpretation:
* The concentric color rings are isophotes — lines of equal surface brightness in the coma. The posterization maps each brightness isophote to a different false color.
* The presence of distinct isophotes (rather than a smooth gradient) indicates that the radial brightness profile of 3I/ATLAS's coma has a non-monotonic structure — there are distinct shell-like features at specific radii.
* The innermost pink zone corresponds to the region of maximum dust continuum emission (closest to the nucleus, highest dust column density).
* The magenta/purple middle zone corresponds to a transition region.
* The teal outer zone corresponds to the peak molecular emission (C₂ at ~516nm + CO⁺ at ~420nm).
* The blue outer boundary represents the outer coma where CO⁺ emission dominates (the blue shifts the composite color toward the blue end of the teal spectrum).
* The concentric shell structure in the posterized image provides quantitative data on the photodissociation lengthscales: the radius of each shell relative to the nucleus condensation constrains the production rates of C₂ and CO⁺ precursor molecules.
