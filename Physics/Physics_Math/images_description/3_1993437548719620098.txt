RC's Contextual Use:
* The Chilbolton 2001 formations (the "Arecibo reply" face formation and the data formation) are among the most famous crop circle events — they appeared adjacent to the Chilbolton radio telescope in Hampshire, England, and appeared to be a response to the 1974 Arecibo message.
* This slide analyzes the ASCII binary content of the crop formation's encoded data as spelling out "People of Earth." — a direct address to humanity.
* The "triple binary" / Texpix encoding scheme is RC's broader argument: that crop formations and associated messages use not just simple binary (0/1) but more complex multi-level encoding schemes that embed both textual and visual information simultaneously.
* The @RodneysGraphics attribution indicates this analysis was community-developed, not solely RC's work — RC is aggregating community decoding efforts.
