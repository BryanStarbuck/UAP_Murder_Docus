Physical Interpretation:
* At this extreme zoom, the comet nucleus itself is not resolved (it is point-like compared to the optical PSF). The white blob is the PSF of the 305mm telescope at this focal length, smeared by the comet's motion during exposure.
* The yellow-green inner zone around the white nucleus at this scale may represent: the innermost C₂ emission shell (photodissociation scale ~ 10,000-30,000 km from nucleus), but at this Earth distance (2.02 AU), this corresponds to an angular scale of ~0.03-0.1 arcsec — below the resolution limit of the telescope. The yellow-green zone is therefore likely the wing of the PSF rather than a resolved emission shell.
* The isophote contour lines at this scale are likely processing artifacts from sharpening or unsharp masking.
* The red-orange fringing is chromatic aberration — the Schmidt-Cassegrain at f/6.3 shows significant chromatic aberration in broadband white light, causing red and blue channels to focus at slightly different positions (axial chromatic aberration).
