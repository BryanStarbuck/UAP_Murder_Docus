Physical Interpretation:
* The extreme length of the ion tail (relative to the nucleus-to-Earth distance) is a key diagnostic: a long, straight, narrow ion tail indicates strong solar wind coupling to the ionized gas (CO⁺) escaping from the nucleus at high velocity.
* The color transition from orange-yellow (inner dust coma) to green (C₂ molecular coma) to cyan-blue (CO⁺ ion tail) traces the full photodissociation and ionization cascade: dust (nearest nucleus) → C₂ molecular species (intermediate) → CO⁺ ions (farthest, ion tail).
* The near-horizontal alignment of the tail (in this image) provides an orientation reference for the Sun-comet-observer geometry.
