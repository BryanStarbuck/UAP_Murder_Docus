PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* LEFT PANEL: This is a false-color (pseudocolor) enhanced astronomical image of a comet's inner coma, showing brightness distribution as a thermal-like color map (red = highest surface brightness, green = lowest within the mapped region)
  * The technique used is called "adaptive Laplace" or "digital enhancement" to reveal inner coma structure
  * The small pink core may represent a rotating nucleus or jet sources
* RIGHT PANEL: This shows resolved coma structure with three distinct elements:
  * Two symmetric green dust/gas fans (jets) diverging from the nucleus — characteristic of a rotating bimodal active nucleus
  * One blue secondary component (possibly CO or CN emission at a different wavelength than C2)
  * The fan geometry indicates two dominant active regions on the nucleus rotating to produce symmetric fan patterns
* The combination of fan geometry and wavelength-separated imaging is a standard technique for mapping cometary nucleus rotation and outgassing patterns
* Relevant to UAP research: the highly structured, directional emission from cometary nuclei creates organized field patterns that can resemble manufactured systems
