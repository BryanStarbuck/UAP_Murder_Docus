HIGH-LEVEL SUMMARY:
Aerial photograph of the Chilbolton 2001 "Arecibo Reply" crop formation — a large rectangular pixel-array formation that modifies the original 1974 Arecibo binary message. The modifications include: a non-human figure (shorter body, larger head), silicon added to the DNA strand, different population number, a different solar system, and a disc-shaped craft.
