HIGH-LEVEL SUMMARY:
A cylinder seal impression (Sumerian/Akkadian, circa 3000-500 BCE) depicting two long-robed figures flanking a central altar or device, with a winged bird deity above. An omega-like symbol or structured object appears on the central table. This iconographic type is extensively cited in ancient astronaut / UAP archaeology research as possible evidence of advanced technology depicted in ancient art — specifically, interpretations range from depictions of Anunnaki interactions with divine devices to possible representations of plasma generators, communication equipment, or energy devices.
