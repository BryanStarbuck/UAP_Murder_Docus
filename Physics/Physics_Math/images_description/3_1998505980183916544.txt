PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* The vivid green color of the coma is definitive C2 Swan band emission (d³Πg → a³Πu transitions of diatomic carbon C₂ at 516nm wavelength) — excited by solar UV photolysis of cometary volatiles (primarily C₂H₂, C₂H₆, and other carbon compounds)
* The blue-white ion tail is caused by CO⁺ and H₂O⁺ ion fluorescence, dragged anti-sunward by the solar wind
* The parallel streaks within the ion tail indicate disconnection events or plasma ray structures — instabilities in the cometary magnetopause where solar wind field lines reconnect with cometary ion tail field lines
* The tail morphology (fan-shaped, predominantly ion tail with ray structures) is typical of comets at perihelion with high solar wind interaction
