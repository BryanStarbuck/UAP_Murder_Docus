PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* Bubble rings (toroidal vortex rings in water) are a direct real-world demonstration of vortex ring dynamics:
  * A vortex ring is a torus-shaped region of rotating fluid (here: air bubbles in water)
  * The ring is self-propelling: the rotating toroidal motion creates a net forward thrust (similar to smoke rings in air)
  * The physics of vortex rings is described by the Biot-Savart law for vortex filaments: the velocity field induced by the ring vorticity causes it to self-propel
  * Vortex ring stability is related to the ratio of core radius to ring radius, and the Reynolds number
* The device generates toroidal bubble vortex rings — a controlled vortex ring generator
