PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This comet photograph shows typical two-component tail morphology:
  * Ion/plasma tail (Type I): blue, straight (approximately anti-sunward), narrow — CO⁺ and H₂O⁺ fluorescence at 420nm and 619nm respectively; dragged anti-sunward by solar wind at solar wind speed (~400 km/s)
  * Dust tail (Type II): if present, would appear as a broader, slightly curved, yellowish-white structure — dust particles driven anti-sunward by radiation pressure, but with different acceleration than ions, causing the curvature (syndyne/synchrone analysis)
* The bright background star (upper-right, blue-white with diffraction spikes) appears to be a B-type or A-type star — the blue color indicates high surface temperature (T > 10,000 K)
* The comet's trajectory (moving from lower-left to upper-right through the star field) is inferrable from the nucleus position
