PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This is a duplicate/alternate version of the previously described comet photograph (3_1999229815564697601)
* The differences from the previous version: the comet has moved (relative to the same background stars), and the tail appears broader and more violet/purple in this image
* The broader, more violet/purple tail compared to the previous blue tail may indicate:
