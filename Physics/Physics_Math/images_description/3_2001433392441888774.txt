* TRIQUETRA PHYSICS:
  * The triquetra (three interlocking circles, forming a triangle of vesica piscis shapes) has three-fold (D3) rotational symmetry
  * In physics: D3 symmetry appears in three-phase rotating magnetic fields, equilateral triangle plasma geometries, and the three-body problem
  * The triquetra is topologically equivalent to the Borromean rings in 2D projection
  * The three lobes of the triquetra represent: three phases (0°, 120°, 240°), three spatial dimensions, three color charges in QCD (quantum chromodynamics)
