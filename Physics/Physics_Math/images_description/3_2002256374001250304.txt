PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* COMET IMAGING TECHNIQUES:
  * This appears to be a DIGITALLY INVERTED astrophotograph: conventional comet images (dark sky, bright comet) are sometimes inverted to enhance coma detail — the darkest features in the inverted image correspond to the brightest in the original
  * In an inverted image: bright white nucleus → black oval; bright green C2 coma → magenta-pink zone; bright blue ion tail → yellow-orange; black sky → pale background
  * This color inversion technique is used in professional cometary research to better resolve surface brightness gradients in the coma that would otherwise be lost in high-dynamic-range imaging
* SURFACE BRIGHTNESS MAPPING:

* NUCLEUS MORPHOLOGY:
