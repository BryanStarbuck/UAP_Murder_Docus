PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* SURFACE TENSION AND WATER STRIDER LOCOMOTION:
  * The water strider (Gerridae) uses surface tension to walk on water — a beautiful demonstration of the capillary length and surface tension physics:
    * Surface tension γ ≈ 72.8 mN/m for water at 20°C
    * The insect's leg contact creates a meniscus deformation; the restoring capillary force supports the insect's weight
    * Capillary length: λ_c = √(γ/ρg) ≈ 2.7mm for water — the relevant length scale for floating objects
    * The insect's total weight is distributed across six legs, each creating a contact perimeter; the upward component of surface tension times contact perimeter = weight
  * LOCOMOTION: The water strider propels itself by surface waves (capillary waves) generated by the middle legs acting as oars; these waves are a mix of capillary and gravity waves
* CROP FORMATION INTERPRETATION:
  * The crop formation's water strider shape is interpreted as: a reference to surface tension locomotion (walking on water = non-contact propulsion); or a reference to Christ's "walking on water" (Matthew 14:25-29)
  * The formation's six arms correspond to the insect's six legs (all Insecta are hexapods)
  * The outer ring = the "pond" surface on which the insect sits
* WALKING ON WATER — CHRISTIAN SYMBOLISM:
  * Jesus walking on the Sea of Galilee (Matthew 14, Mark 6, John 6) is the implied link
  * "Be of good cheer. It is I, do not be afraid!" — the quote in the companion image (3_2004690632116473856) is the direct quote from Matthew 14:27 when Jesus walks on water
