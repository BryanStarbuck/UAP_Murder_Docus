* CHI-RHO / WATER STRIDER GEOMETRIC CORRESPONDENCE:
  * The key insight in this composite: the Chi-Rho symbol (☧) has a geometric structure that resembles a water strider when viewed appropriately

  * "IC XC NIKA" (Ἰησοῦς Χριστὸς νικᾷ = "Jesus Christ conquers") is a traditional Byzantine Christian inscription
