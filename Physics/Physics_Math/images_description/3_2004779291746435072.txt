PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* HOMOPOLAR GENERATOR (FARADAY DISC):
  * The homopolar generator was invented by Michael Faraday in 1831 — one of the first electrical generators
  * Operation: a conducting disc rotates in a uniform magnetic field B (perpendicular to the disc, into the page as shown by the × symbols in the "voltage taps" region)
  * The radial EMF is generated by: ε = ∫(R₁ to R₂) (v × B) · dr = ½ ω B (R₂² - R₁²)
    where ω = angular velocity, B = magnetic field, R₁ = inner radius, R₂ = outer radius
  * The current flows radially from center (brushes at R₁) to rim (brushes at R₂), or vice versa
  * The "voltage taps" represent sliding brush contacts that extract the radially-generated EMF
* CURRENT FLOW PATHS:
  * The concentric arc lines represent current streamlines in the rotating disc
  * In a real homopolar generator, the current flows purely radially (Ohm's law in rotating frame: J = σ(E + v×B)); the curved appearance may represent the azimuthal component induced by the current's own magnetic field (back-reaction)
* HOMOPOLAR GENERATOR AND UAP CONNECTION:
  * Homopolar generators produce very high current (kA range) at low voltage from simple rotating disc geometry
  * Bruce DePalma (N-Machine), Edwin Gray, and others claimed over-unity performance from homopolar designs — connected to the broader zero-point energy and free energy research in this collection
  * The Searl Effect Generator (SEG) is based on rollers rotating on a ring magnet — conceptually related to the homopolar principle
  * NASA has studied homopolar generators for electromagnetic railgun energy storage (MJ-class pulsed power)
* MATHEMATICAL STRUCTURE:
  * The EMF equation ε = ½ ωBR² is derived directly from the Lorentz force on charge carriers in the rotating disc
  * In the rotating frame: the fictitious "centrifugal force" plays a role; the charges in the rotating disc experience both E and v×B
