PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* LUNAR ILLUMINATION:
  * The Moon reflects approximately 12% of incident sunlight (geometric albedo = 0.12)
  * The mare regions (dark basalts) have albedo ≈ 0.06-0.08; the highland terrae have albedo ≈ 0.11-0.18
  * The visible crater ray systems (Tycho, Copernicus) are high-albedo ejecta blankets composed of fresh, unweathered material
* CLOUD SCATTERING AND COLORING:
  * The orange cloud at upper-left demonstrates atmospheric scattering of moonlight through a cloud layer:
    * Rayleigh scattering by gas molecules preferentially scatters shorter (blue/green) wavelengths, leaving the transmitted light richer in red/orange
    * Mie scattering by cloud droplets is more wavelength-independent (white light) but with some preferential forward scattering
    * The orange-tinted cloud is at low altitude (warm atmospheric layer) where the moonlight has traversed more atmosphere
  * The pale blue-white lower streak: thin ice cloud (cirrus) at high altitude, where Mie scattering dominates — more wavelength-neutral
