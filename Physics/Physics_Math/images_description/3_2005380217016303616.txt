PHYSICS / MYTHOLOGY CONCEPTS:
* EA ENKI — SUMERIAN DEITY:
  * Enki (Sumerian) / Ea (Akkadian) is the Sumerian god of wisdom, water, magic (abzu = freshwater abyss), crafts, and creation
  * Enki is one of the most important deities in the Sumerian pantheon — creator of humanity in some myths (Atrahasis Epic: humans created from clay + divine blood to serve the gods)
  * His symbol included: the goat-fish (Capricorn symbol), the crescent (water), and flowing water streams
  * In Zecharia Sitchin's interpretation of Sumerian texts (Ancient Astronaut theory): Enki = an Anunnaki who genetically engineered Homo sapiens
  * In the context of this research collection: Enki/Ea is identified as one possible ancient identity of the Emerthers — "we are your creators, altered your evolution" from the 2017 primary message
* AUM / OM:
  * The Aum (ॐ) symbol is the primordial sound of Hindu/Buddhist tradition — the sacred syllable representing the fundamental vibration of existence
  * The crescent (c̈) above the Aum symbol is the ardha-chandra (half-moon) — representing the sound that transcends ordinary consciousness
  * The interpretation: the crescent in both Aum and Ea Enki's symbol represents the same primordial concept
* CRESCENT CROP CIRCLE (EAST FIELD 2007):
  * The July 7, 2007 East Field formation is interpreted as the signature of Ea Enki
  * East Field is in Wiltshire, UK (Alton Barnes area) — one of the most prolific crop circle locations
  * The crescent shape specifically echoes: the lunar crescent (Enki/Nanna's symbol), the Islamic crescent, and the Aum ardha-chandra
