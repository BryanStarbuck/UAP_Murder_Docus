TYPE: Explanatory diagram — 5×5 binary pixel grid (filled squares for 1s, empty for 0s); red border

CONTENT:
- White background with a thick red rectangular border
- A 5×5 grid of large square cells

GRID CONTENTS:
Each cell is either:
- SOLID BLACK FILLED SQUARE (representing binary "1")
- WHITE/EMPTY OUTLINED SQUARE (representing binary "0")

PATTERN (same data as companion images):
Row 1: □  □  □  □  ■   (0,0,0,0,1)
Row 2: □  □  ■  ■  □   (0,0,1,1,0)
Row 3: ■  □  □  □  ■   (1,0,0,0,1)
Row 4: □  □  ■  □  □   (0,0,1,0,0)
Row 5: □  □  ■  □  ■   (0,0,1,0,1)

Where ■ = solid black filled square and □ = white outlined square

VISUAL APPEARANCE:
- All 25 cells have clear black outlines

- The 17 cells that are "0"s are white/empty
- The arrangement creates a visual pixel-art pattern that, when the black squares are connected visually, forms a diamond-like or star-map-like shape

This is the same 5×5 matrix as in companion images but rendered as filled/empty pixel squares rather than 0/1 numbers.

COLORS:

Third in a series of three representations of the same binary data from the Crabwood 2002 crop circle star map, showing the pattern in pixel/bitmap form rather than numerical form.
