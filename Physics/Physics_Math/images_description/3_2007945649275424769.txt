PHYSICS CONTEXT — Corbino Disc:
The Corbino disc is a disc-shaped conductor (named after Orso Mario Corbino, 1876-1937) with:
- Current flowing radially between a central terminal (+) and an outer ring terminal (-)
- When placed in a uniform axial magnetic field (B perpendicular to disc), the Hall effect causes electrons to follow circular paths
- This creates a net azimuthal (circumferential) current component in addition to the radial current
- The device is of interest in studies of the Hall effect, magnetoresistance, and electron transport in 2D materials

The Corbino disc is a specific case of the homopolar/Faraday disc generator but in a different geometry:
- Faraday disc: rotating disc in B field → radial EMF
- Corbino disc: stationary disc with radial current in B field → azimuthal current
