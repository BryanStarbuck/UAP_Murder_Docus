PHYSICS DESCRIPTION:
The Corbino disc geometry:
- Current enters at the inner conductor (radius R₁) or outer ring (radius R₂)
- In the presence of an axial magnetic field B (perpendicular to the disc plane), the Lorentz force F = qv × B deflects the radially flowing charge carriers
- This deflection creates an azimuthal component, causing the current path to spiral rather than flow purely radially
- The Hall voltage is measured between the voltage taps at different radial positions
- The magnetoresistance of a Corbino disc is particularly large compared to other geometries because there are no free edges to allow Hall field buildup

The concentric arcs in the upper portion represent the spiral current paths: J_r (radial) + J_φ (azimuthal) = J_total (spiral)
