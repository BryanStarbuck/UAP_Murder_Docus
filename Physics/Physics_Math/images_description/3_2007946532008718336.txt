PHYSICS DESCRIPTION:
This is a Faraday disc (homopolar generator) in plan view:
- The disc rotates with angular velocity ω (clockwise as shown)
- At each point on the disc, velocity v = ω × r (tangential, circumferential)
- Magnetic field B is axial (into page)
- Lorentz force on positive charge carriers: F = qv × B = q(ωr θ̂) × (-Bẑ) = qωrB r̂ (radially outward)
- This creates an EMF from center to rim: ε = ∫₀ᴿ (ωrB) dr = ½ωBR²
- Current flows radially outward through the disc and returns via the external circuit
- The "wire slice" concept divides the disc into radial spoke elements, each cutting magnetic flux lines as the disc rotates

The EMF generated per revolution is: ε = ½ωBR² = πnBR² (where n = rotations per second)

CONTEXT:
This colored diagram pairs with the monochrome Corbino/Faraday disc schematics in companion images (3_2004779291746435072.txt, 3_2004779613759954944.txt, 3_2007946448701394944.txt). The explicit labeling of "wire slice cutting the magnetic flux" is a pedagogical framing — each spoke element of the rotating disc cuts through magnetic flux lines, generating an EMF by Faraday's law: ε = -dΦ/dt. This is the fundamental mechanism of the Faraday disc, which is the simplest homopolar generator and generates DC voltage without commutation.
