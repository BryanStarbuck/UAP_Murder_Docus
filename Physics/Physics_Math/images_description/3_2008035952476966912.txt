BIOLOGICAL PROCESS DEPICTED:
This diagram illustrates the Central Dogma of Molecular Biology (Francis Crick, 1958/1970):
1. Transcription: DNA is transcribed into messenger RNA (mRNA) by RNA polymerase
   - Template strand of DNA is read 3'→5'
   - mRNA is synthesized 5'→3' with complementary bases (A→U, T→A, G→C, C→G)
2. Translation: mRNA is translated into protein by ribosomes
   - tRNA molecules carry specific amino acids to the ribosome
   - Codons (3-nucleotide sequences) are matched to anticodons on tRNA
   - Amino acids are linked by peptide bonds to form the polypeptide chain
   - The polypeptide folds into its functional 3D structure (the protein)
