CONTEXT:
The combination of Greek key meander border and 8-pointed star interior is a specific and recurring pattern combination in UK crop formations. The 8-pointed star of Ishtar (Venus) is the oldest star symbol in the Mesopotamian tradition, appearing on artifacts as early as 3000 BCE. Its appearance in crop formations is interpreted by researchers as a reference to Venus/Ishtar/Inanna — or as a star map. The Greek meander border (also called the eternal labyrinth or key of infinity) connects to classical Greek tradition. The combination of Mesopotamian and Greek symbolic vocabulary in a single formation is characteristic of the more sophisticated post-2000 formations.
