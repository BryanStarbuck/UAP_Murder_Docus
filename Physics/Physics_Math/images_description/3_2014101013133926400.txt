PHYSICS / CONCEPTS:
* This image presents Ed Leedskalnin's own description of his magnetic generator, combined with a photograph of his actual device preserved at Coral Castle. Key physics points:
  1. Leedskalnin's quote describes a rotating armature with coils passing through a magnetic field — the standard Faraday induction generator principle
  2. The left diagram shows a classic magneto configuration — a rotating armature winding between permanent magnet pole pieces (N and S), producing AC or DC depending on commutator configuration
  3. The right photograph shows Leedskalnin's actual flywheel: a multi-pole device with pairs of magnets labeled NN and NN (like poles facing each other) or SS-SS, suggesting a specific non-standard magnetic configuration. The NN-NN and SS-SS labeling (same poles facing) is unusual — standard generators use N-S facing pairs. Leedskalnin's theory held that "individual North and South pole magnets" behave differently from conventional dipole magnets, and his machine was designed accordingly.
  4. The degree markings (0° through 345° in 15° increments) confirm 24 equally spaced pole positions (360°/15° = 24), consistent with a 24-pole multi-phase generator. A 24-pole machine at 60 RPM produces 720 Hz output, or at lower speed, subharmonics of high-frequency AC.
  5. Leedskalnin claimed his machine produced "magnetic currents" distinct from conventional electric current — a claim related to his unconventional theory of magnetism as composed of independent North and South pole particles.
