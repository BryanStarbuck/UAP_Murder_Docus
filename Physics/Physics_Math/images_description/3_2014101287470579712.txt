PHYSICS / CONCEPTS:
* This image presents the interpretation that a crop circle near Santa Catarina, Brazil (October 12, 2012) depicts a "Muller dynamo" — a specific magnetic generator design attributed to Wilhelm Muller (a German-Canadian inventor who claimed an over-unity permanent magnet motor). The Muller motor/dynamo features N-S-N-S alternating permanent magnets arranged in a ring around a central rotor, with pickup coils outside the rotor ring.
* The crop formation's 30-bead outer ring (30 elements) is interpreted as representing 30 N-S-N-S magnet pairs (15 complete N-S-N-S cycles = 15 magnet pairs in opposing polarities = 30 magnetic pole faces)
* The smaller adjacent formation (the concentric circles) represents the pickup wire coil
* N = "swirled clockwise" and S = "swirled anti-clockwise" refers to the direction of crop flattening at each bead position, which the researcher argues encodes the magnetic polarity
* This connects to: over-unity permanent magnet motor theory, Muller motor replication attempts, and the broader claim that crop circles encode instructions for alternative energy generators. The 16-magnet original vs. 30-magnet crop version is noted as a scale-up iteration.
