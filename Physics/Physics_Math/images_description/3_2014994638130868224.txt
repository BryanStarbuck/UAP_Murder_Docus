PHYSICS / CONCEPTS:
* This is a standard educational cross-section diagram of a three-phase induction motor:
  - The stator is the outer stationary ring containing the three-phase AC winding coils (represented by the red "O" symbols, which are cross-sections of the current-carrying conductors in the stator slots)
  - The rotor is the inner rotating element (blue disc) driven by the rotating magnetic field generated by the three-phase stator windings
  - The three-phase winding creates a rotating magnetic field at synchronous speed (n_s = 120f/p, where f is AC frequency and p is the number of poles)
  - In an induction motor, the rotor follows the rotating stator field at slightly below synchronous speed (slip ≠ 0); in a synchronous motor/generator, the rotor rotates at exactly synchronous speed
  - The 24 red coil symbols suggest a 24-slot stator, consistent with a three-phase winding (slots = 3 × 8, divisible by 3 for balanced three-phase)
* This diagram is included in the collection to explain the three-phase rotating magnetic field principle that crop circle researchers argue is encoded in the Milk Hill and similar crop formations
