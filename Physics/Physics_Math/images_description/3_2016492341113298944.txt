  1. LEFT: the stator — 12 toroidal inductors wired in three-phase (blue=phase A, yellow-green=phase B, red=phase C) in a ring

* Key technical observations:

  - The O/X labeling of magnets is standard electromagnetic convention: O = current/field coming out of the page, X = going in

  - The central shaft on the right device is where the two halves would be assembled: left disc (stator) stationary, right disc (rotor) rotating
