PHYSICS / CONCEPTS:
* This is the most technically explicit variant of the vertical disc diagram — it removes the uncertainty markers and replaces them with definitive technical specifications:
  1. "15 wire coils" — definitively states there are 15 coils in the beaded ring (not pairs, but individual coils; or 15 pairs for 30 total — ambiguous). This is consistent with:
     - The Muller dynamo: 15 coil pairs = 30 slots
     - The "(15)" annotation in 3_2015744548140720128.jpg
     - Note: 15 individual coils = 5 coils per phase in a three-phase system (15/3 = 5) — giving a 5-pole-pair three-phase configuration
  2. "3-phase rotating magnetic field" — definitively states the physical principle: a rotating magnetic field produced by three-phase excitation of the coil ring. This is the fundamental operating principle of the induction motor:
     - When three-phase current is applied to three sets of coils at 120-degree angular intervals, the resulting superposition of three sinusoidal fields produces a field that rotates uniformly in space — the "rotating magnetic field"
     - Rotating magnetic field frequency = electrical supply frequency (synchronous speed = f/p where f = frequency, p = pole pairs)
  3. "3-phase magnetism" (upper) + "3-phase rotating magnetic field" (lower): both labels emphasize three-phase — the upper describing the input field from the magnets, the lower describing the output of the coil ring
* This variant is the clearest unambiguous statement of the physical principle being encoded: a 15-coil three-phase rotating magnetic field induction device
