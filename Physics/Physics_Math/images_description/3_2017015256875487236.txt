PHYSICS / CONCEPTS:
* The Rendlesham Forest incident (December 26-28, 1980) is one of the most documented military UFO encounters:
  - Location: RAF Woodbridge/RAF Bentwaters dual-base near Rendlesham Forest, Suffolk, England (NATO base)
  - Multiple USAF personnel witnessed a landed craft with subsequent radiation readings at the landing site
  - Lieutenant Colonel Charles Halt filed an official USAF memo (the "Halt Memo") documenting the incident
* Physics observations from the witness sketches:
  1. The craft in top view is triangular — a triangle in plan view. This is consistent with B-2 Spirit stealth bomber geometry but also with the AATIP/UAP triangular craft reports (TR-3B allegations)
  2. "Ionophore?" label: ionophores are ion-channel molecules in biology, but in physics context, "ion" + "phore" (bearer) may refer to an ion propulsion element — a charged particle emitter. This would be consistent with electrohydrodynamic (EHD) or ionic wind propulsion claims for UAPs
  3. The triangle-within-circle symbol in the top-right drawing: identical to the alchemical "fire" element symbol AND to the standard three-phase motor symbol used in circuit diagrams. The solid black disc at the upper triangle vertex may represent the N-pole magnet position
  4. The front-view sketch shows a lens-shaped cross-section with internal elements visible — consistent with a disc craft with internal machinery visible through a transparent dome or detected via sensor
  5. "Pitchings": refers to the pitching motion (nose up/down rotation) of the craft — relevant to flight dynamics and attitude control
