PHYSICS / CONCEPTS:
* This formation is one of the clearest examples of a three-phase rotating magnetic field diagram in crop circle form:
  1. Triskelion / three-arm pinwheel: three arms at 120-degree intervals rotating in the same direction = the fundamental geometry of a three-phase rotating magnetic field. When three sinusoidal currents at 120-degree phase offsets are applied to three sets of coils, the resultant field traces a triskelion rotating in time
  2. Recycling symbol shape: the "recycling" international symbol is itself a triskelion — the three arrows represent the cycle of material transformation. Here it represents the cycling of electromagnetic energy between three phases
  3. Satellite circles (approximately 24-30): these satellite elements may encode:
     - The number of coil slots in the stator ring (24 = standard for many induction motors, 30 = Muller dynamo spec)
     - Binary information in their spatial arrangement
     - The 12 positions of 30-element rings (30/12 = 2.5 — not clean), or 24 positions (24-slot motor standard)
  4. Three-arm curved shape: the curvature of the arms encodes the direction and sense of rotation (clockwise arms = clockwise rotating field)
  5. The uniformly distributed satellite circles with the three-arm center = a complete motor specification: inner stator (triskelion = rotating field) + outer coil positions (satellite circles = coil slots)
