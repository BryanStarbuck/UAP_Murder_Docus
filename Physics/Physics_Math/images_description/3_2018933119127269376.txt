PHYSICS / CONCEPTS:
* 5-fold symmetric crop formation with concentric bullseye hub:
  1. 5-fold (pentagonal) symmetry: unusual for crop circles which overwhelmingly favor 3-fold (C3v), 4-fold, 6-fold, and 8-fold symmetry. Five-fold symmetry does NOT correspond to standard 3-phase motor topology (which requires divisors of 3 or multiples of 2). Five-fold symmetry is however:
     - Related to golden ratio / Fibonacci geometry (pentagon diagonals divide in the golden ratio φ ≈ 1.618)
     - Related to quasicrystal diffraction patterns (Penrose tiling; icosahedral quasicrystals discovered by Shechtman, Nobel Prize 2011)
     - Related to 5-pole magnetic geometry (possible in permanent magnet configurations but unusual in industrial motors)
  2. The 5 kidney-shaped petals: in motor topology, could represent 5 D-shaped pole pieces of a 5-pole permanent magnet motor. However, 5-pole motors produce inherent torque ripple at the fundamental frequency (no cancellation) — they are not used in high-performance propulsion
  3. More likely physics encoding: the 5-fold geometry encodes the Fibonacci/golden-ratio propulsion thesis — that UAP propulsion relates to the golden ratio spiral geometry of space (as in 3_2018620652065046528.jpg analysis)
  4. Concentric bullseye hub (3-4 rings): the alternating standing/flattened rings at the center = a multi-ring toroidal coil cross-section viewed axially. Multiple concentric current-carrying rings produce a superposition of magnetic fields — used in high-field magnet design (e.g., NMR/MRI solenoid windings)
  5. The overall plan: outer annular stator ring (flattened ring) + 5 pole-shaped regions + concentric multi-ring hub = a 5-pole axial-flux motor cross-section
