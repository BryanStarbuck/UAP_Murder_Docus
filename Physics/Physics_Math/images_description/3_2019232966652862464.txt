PHYSICS / CONCEPTS:
* This is a "6 wire coils" variant of the standard disc motor schematic (same type as seen in other labeled schematics throughout this collection):
  1. "6 wire coils": the 6 bead clusters around the central disc ring = 6 coil positions. These are 6 coils wired as 3 pairs (pair A at 0°/180°, pair B at 120°/300°, pair C at 240°/60°) to create a 3-phase rotating magnetic field. A 6-coil 3-phase motor is a 2-pole-pair (4-pole) machine: 3 phases × 2 coils per phase = 6 coils
  2. Blue outer ring on the central disc: blue = coil/stator element (same color convention used throughout: blue = electromagnetic coil winding)
  3. Red inner circle: red = magnet/rotor element (red = permanent magnet N/S ring). The label "magnet N/S" placed adjacent to the red ring confirms this
  4. "3-phase rotating magnetic field" + "3-phase magnetism": two labels for the same concept — the stator coils (blue) energized by three-phase AC current produce a rotating magnetic field that drives the rotor magnet ring (red)
  5. This 6-coil design differs from the 15-coil Muller dynamo variants — it is a simpler 2-pole-pair topology. 6-coil 3-phase motors are standard in small appliance and HVAC applications
  6. The white annular ring between black ring and red circle: represents the air gap between stator and rotor in the motor cross-section
  7. The cluster sizes (large + medium + small dots): within each bead cluster, the decreasing bead sizes represent the wire turn cross-sections within a single coil slot — the conductor bundle in the slot, with fewer but larger conductors at the center and smaller ones at the edges (shaped winding)
