PHYSICS / CONCEPTS:
* This is an annotated vertical (axial / side-view) assembly diagram of the counter-rotating magnetic disc propulsion system — the most completely labeled version in this collection. It shows the full device stack from top to bottom:
  1. Top magnet disc ("magnet N/S"): a permanent magnet disc at the top of the assembly. The ∧ caret = this disc generates upward thrust. "3-phase magnetism" label = this disc also participates in the rotating field system
  2. Y-shaped yoke connector: the mechanical coupling between the top magnet and the main coil assembly — transfers torque and holds the assembly aligned. The Y-shape is a standard mechanical coupling hub (Oldham coupling or similar)
  3. Main disc (center): the 15-wire-coil stator ring:
     - "15 wire coils": 15 coils arranged in the bead ring = the Muller dynamo specification (15-coil ring). Wired as 5 groups of 3 (three-phase, 5 poles per phase)
     - Each bead with curly hook = one toroidal coil winding (the hook represents the coil wound around a core bead)
     - "Stainless-steel tube": the structural containment tube that holds the coil ring — Galinstan-filled stainless-steel tube as the conducting medium and structural ring
     - "Ball bearings": the bearing system at the hub that allows the magnet rotor to spin relative to the stator coil ring
  4. Spring/flexible coupling (bottom of main disc): a flexible mechanical link that accommodates thermal expansion and vibration between the main disc and the bottom magnet
  5. Bottom magnet disc (medium): a second permanent magnet, with the ∧ thrust arrow confirming upward force contribution from this magnet as well
  6. Lower connector (spring-like): second flexible coupling
  7. Smallest bottom disc (outline only, not filled): possibly the rotor disc of the bottom magnet assembly (the rotating part, shown as hollow/ring, contrasting with the filled solid-rotor parts above)
