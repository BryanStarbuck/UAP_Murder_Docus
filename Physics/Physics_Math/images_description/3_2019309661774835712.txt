PHYSICS / CONCEPTS:
* This is a highly simplified variant of the disc assembly side view — showing just the essential minimal elements without the 15-coil bead ring:
  1. Top hollow ring ("magnet N/S"): one ring magnet (hollow = ring geometry, not solid disc). The ring magnet orientation is shown as an open circle — this is the external "field magnet" that provides the bias field for the homopolar generator portion
  2. Large solid black disc: this is the conducting/rotating disc element — the Faraday homopolar disc. The solid fill indicates it is a dense conducting disc (e.g., galinstan-filled)
  3. Magenta tube ring + black half-disc ("stainless-steel tube," "3-phase magnetism"): the central assembly where the rotating disc sits within the stainless-steel containment tube. The asymmetric positioning of the black half-disc inside the magenta ring represents the disc in motion — offset from center due to rotation (or represents the two counter-rotating disc components at a moment when they are at different angular positions)
  4. Smaller ring-magnet below: the second magnet element below the main assembly — the "counter" magnet that provides the return path for the magnetic circuit
  5. Bottom hollow ring ("magnet N/S"): the bottom ring magnet, completing the magnetic circuit from top to bottom
  6. Two ∧ carets: two upward thrust arrows = upward force generated by both the upper and lower magnet/disc interactions

* This simplified view strips the design to: two ring magnets (top and bottom) + one or two conducting discs + the stainless-steel tube containment. This is the minimum viable homopolar generator/motor configuration with the Paulsen "twelve magnets" specification reduced to its essential 2-magnet structure.
