* Greek meander ring + 8-pointed star compass rose:
  1. Greek meander ring: the meander pattern has appeared in multiple formations in this collection (East Field 2007, Quetzalcoatl formation, others). In the electromagnetic motor encoding, the meander ring represents the rotor path or the stator winding path — the conductor that follows a back-and-forth right-angle path (like the winding of a meander antenna or a rectangular loop coil). The Greek key is topologically equivalent to a series of right-angle turns that maximize conductor length within a confined ring width
  2. 8-pointed star (octagram): an 8-fold symmetric pattern encodes an 8-pole motor topology. An 8-pole motor has 4 pole pairs; running at grid frequency (50 Hz or 60 Hz) it produces: 50 Hz / 4 pairs = 750 RPM or 60 Hz / 4 pairs = 900 RPM. This is a common topology in low-speed, high-torque applications (pumps, compressors)

The two squares encode the two counter-rotating field components
  4. The meander ring + star combination: the meander outer ring = the current-carrying path (stator winding), the 8-point star = the 8-pole rotor magnetic geometry. Together they form the complete motor topology: winding path (meander) → rotating field (8-point star)
