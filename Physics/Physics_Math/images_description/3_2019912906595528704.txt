PHYSICS / CONCEPTS:
* The Crabwood 2002 formation is one of the most complex and most analyzed crop formations in the historical record:
  1. The grey alien face: the pixelated portrait is interpretable as a grey alien — the most commonly reported non-human intelligence morphotype in abduction accounts. The face in the crop formation is itself a communication: "we (the entities who made this) look like this"
  2. The binary disc: the spiral disc encodes a binary ASCII message — a deliberate choice of information encoding:
     - Binary (base-2) encoding: the most universal number system, independent of language or culture
     - ASCII mapping: the specific ASCII character set is a 1963 US telecommunications standard. The formation's creators chose ASCII, implying either knowledge of human computer standards or independent invention of the same encoding
     - Spiral reading direction: the spiral format requires the reader to identify the spiral pitch and reading direction (inward or outward) before decoding — an additional layer of puzzle that filters for sufficiently sophisticated decoders
  3. The decoded message: "Beware the bearers of false gifts and their broken promises. Much pain but still time. Believe there is good out there. We oppose deception. Conduit closing [bell character]." The final "bell character" (ASCII 7 = auditory alert tone) is interpreted as a closing signal or warning tone
  4. "Conduit closing": interpreted as a warning that the communication channel between this ET entity and humanity is about to close — consistent with the 2002 timing (the Crabwood formation appeared just before the major 2003-2004 wave of Congressional interest in UAP topics ended)
