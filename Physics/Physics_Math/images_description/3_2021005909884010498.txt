This type of bearing is used specifically when the bearing must surround a large-diameter hollow space (to allow a shaft, winding, or fluid to pass through the center) while still providing full-circumference rotational support. This is the correct bearing type for a ring generator / disc propulsion system where the rotor hub must be accessible for current extraction (Faraday homopolar configuration) or for the Galinstan fluid channel.

  3. Full-circumference bearing mounting on the tube OD: the bearing is mounted on the outer diameter of the stainless-steel tube (rather than on a shaft through the tube center). In the Muller dynamo context: the stainless-steel tube is the fixed stator housing; the bearing race mounted on the tube OD provides the rotating interface for the magnets or the disc to spin around the tube. The rotor (disc + magnets) mounts to the inner race; the stator tube is the outer race.
