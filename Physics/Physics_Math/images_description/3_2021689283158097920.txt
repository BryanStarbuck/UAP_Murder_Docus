HIGH-LEVEL SUMMARY:
Aerial photograph of the famous Crabwood Farm crop formation (Winchester, Hampshire, 2002), showing two elements: a pixelated humanoid "Grey alien" face within a rectangular frame on the left, and a large circular disc on the right containing a dense spiral/concentric pattern of dots encoding a binary message. When decoded, the disc's dot pattern yields an ASCII text message in binary. This formation is among the most analyzed and debated in UAP/crop circle research — researchers who accept its non-human origin present the binary-encoded message as direct communication from an extraterrestrial or non-human intelligence. Skeptics argue it was human-made but acknowledge the technical achievement of creating a readable binary encoding at scale.
