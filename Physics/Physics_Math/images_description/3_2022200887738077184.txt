PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This is a black-and-white diagram of the Barbury Castle 2008 crop formation (the pi-encoding formation discussed in image 3_2021966483321237507.jpg)
* The three circles at the top: small (1.0 = digit 1), medium (0.0 = digit 0), large filled (3.0 = digit 3) — representing the digit 3 of pi = 3.14159...
* The spiral track with notch positions: each arc segment of the spiral encodes one decimal digit of pi through the angular fraction of the circle it occupies — this is the binary/angular encoding of pi discovered by astrophysicist Mike Reed in 2008
* The small dot may represent the decimal point (the .1415... part)
* The three-circle sequence at top encodes: small = digit 1 unit (tiny), medium = digit 2 units, large filled = digit 3 — or alternatively, they are circles of diameter 1, 2, 3 representing a ratio

HIGH-LEVEL SUMMARY:
Black-and-white diagram of the Barbury Castle 2008 crop formation, which is widely analyzed as encoding the mathematical constant pi (π = 3.14159265358979...) in a novel encoding scheme. Three circles of different sizes at the top encode the leading digit "3." The spiral track within the disc is divided by notch positions into arc segments, where each segment's angular fraction relative to a full circle encodes successive decimal digits of pi in a base-10 positional notation. This formation was analyzed by astrophysicist Mike Reed who decoded the pi encoding, and is presented in UAP physics research as an example of a crop formation containing mathematical content of extraordinary sophistication, given that pi encoding in angular arc-length notation represents a non-trivial mathematical concept that was not widely known in this visual form.
