Aerial photograph of the famous Chilbolton crop formation (August 2001, Hampshire, UK) — a 73x23 pixel binary-encoded rectangle that represents an apparent response to the 1974 Arecibo radio message sent into space. The formation modifies the original Arecibo message in several key ways: changing the atomic element list (adding silicon), changing the humanoid figure proportions (non-human), changing the population number, and modifying the solar system and antenna diagrams.
