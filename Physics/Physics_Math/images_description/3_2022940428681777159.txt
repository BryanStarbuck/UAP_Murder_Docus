* This close-up botanical photograph documents "elongated nodes" in crop circle stems — one of the key physical anomalies cited by researcher W.C. Levengood and later the BLT Research Team

Close-up documentary photograph of green wheat stems from a crop circle formation, held in a human hand to show clearly the characteristic "elongated nodes" anomaly. The nodes (stem internodes/joints) appear swollen and elongated compared to normal wheat, with small adventitious buds emerging. This physical evidence — rigorously studied by W.C.
