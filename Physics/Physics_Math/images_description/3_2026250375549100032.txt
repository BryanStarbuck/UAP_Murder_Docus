- Color: Indigo/deep blue.
- Sanskrit: Ajna (meaning "command" or "perceive").

CHAKRA 5 — THROAT (VISHUDDHA):
- Color: Light blue/turquoise.
- Sanskrit: Vishuddha (meaning "especially pure").

- Domain: Communication, self-expression, truth, creativity, sound.
- Symbol: Sixteen-petaled lotus with a downward triangle inside a circle.

CHAKRA 4 — HEART (ANAHATA):
- Color: Green (sometimes pink).
- Sanskrit: Anahata (meaning "unstruck" or "unhurt").
- Location: Center of chest.
- Domain: Love, compassion, healing, balance, relationships.
- Symbol: Twelve-petaled lotus with two interlocking triangles (Star of David / hexagram).

CHAKRA 3 — SOLAR PLEXUS (MANIPURA):
- Color: Yellow.
- Sanskrit: Manipura (meaning "city of jewels").
- Location: Upper abdomen/solar plexus.

- Symbol: Ten-petaled lotus with a downward triangle.

CHAKRA 2 — SACRAL (SVADHISTHANA):
- Color: Orange.
- Sanskrit: Svadhisthana (meaning "one's own place").
- Location: Lower abdomen, below navel.
- Domain: Creativity, sexuality, pleasure, emotions, flow.
- Symbol: Six-petaled lotus with a crescent moon.

CHAKRA 1 — ROOT (MULADHARA):
- Color: Red.
- Sanskrit: Muladhara (meaning "root support").
- Location: Base of spine/perineum.
- Domain: Survival, grounding, security, physical existence, earth connection.
- Symbol: Four-petaled lotus with a downward triangle and square.
