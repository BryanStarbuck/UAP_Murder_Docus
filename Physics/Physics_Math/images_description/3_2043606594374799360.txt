PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This annotated composite explores how the relative vertical position of the magnet ring affects thrust generation:
  1. THE QUESTION ("MIGHT EACH OPEN-CLOSED CYCLE CREATE A SLIGHT UPWARD THRUST?"):
     * "Open-closed cycle" refers to the electromagnetic flux cycle of the rotating disc:
       * OPEN PHASE: As a magnet sweeps past a stator coil gap, the flux path is open (lower permeance) → lower field energy stored
       * CLOSED PHASE: As a magnet aligns with a stator coil, the flux path is closed (higher permeance) → higher field energy stored
       * The transition from open to closed creates an attractive force between magnet and stator iron
     * If the magnet ring is positioned BELOW the midplane of the flywheel (left diagram: "magnet below flywheel"), the attractive force during flux closure has an upward vertical component → net upward thrust per open-closed cycle
     * If the magnet ring is LEVEL with the flywheel (right diagram: "magnet level with flywheel"), the attractive force is purely radial → no upward thrust from this mechanism
  2. LEFT DIAGRAM ("MAGNET BELOW FLYWHEEL"):
     * The green Δ arrows between the red top disc and the blue center element indicate where thrust is generated: at the interface between the upper magnet ring (red) and the lower coupling region (blue) — the attractive force points upward toward the magnet (which is below the stator mid-plane)
     * The red "V" and blue "∧" markings confirm the counter-rotation: the red outer ring spins clockwise (V = downward in the cross-product sense), the blue inner element spins counterclockwise (∧ = upward), producing the net lift
  3. RIGHT DIAGRAM ("MAGNET LEVEL WITH FLYWHEEL"):
     * The green horizontal line marks the disc equatorial plane
     * The red "V V" and blue "∧ ∧" markings confirm the same counter-rotation, but now the magnetic forces are symmetric about the equatorial plane → no net upward or downward force from the open-closed cycle alone
     * Thrust in this configuration must come from a different mechanism (e.g., J × B Lorentz force on the radial current, or gravitomagnetic Lense-Thirring effect)
  4. THRUST OPTIMIZATION: The diagram argues that optimal thrust is generated when the magnets are positioned BELOW the flywheel midplane (or above, by symmetry) — a design parameter for the physical construction of the disc system
