  2. "STAINLESS-STEEL TUBE" (purple circle): The purple/magenta outlined circle represents a non-magnetic stainless steel tube (austenitic stainless steel is non-magnetic) that:

     * Is made of non-magnetic material to avoid perturbing the magnetic field of the central magnet
     * The overlap of the stainless-steel tube circle with the black filled circles shows that the tube encloses the spinning magnet (the magnet rotates inside the tube, with the tube providing the structural support and sliding contact surface)

     * A spinning permanent magnet (North/South axially oriented) rotating inside a stainless steel tube produces an alternating B-field
