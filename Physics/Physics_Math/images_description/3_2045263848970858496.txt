PHYSICS CONCEPTS:
* This schematic introduces the explicit magnetic pole labeling of the disc system components:
  1. FOUR "N" (NORTH) LABELS ON THE OUTER RED RING: The four red N labels at the outer quadrant positions indicate that the outer red flywheel has NORTH polarity facing outward at four positions (N, E, S, W). This encodes:
     * A 4-pole outer rotor (4 north poles at the outer perimeter, with alternating south poles between them — but the south poles may be facing inward, not outward)
     * Or: the outer flywheel generates a magnetic field that is North-outward in all four quadrant positions simultaneously (a more unusual topology, suggesting a monopole-like or quadrupole-symmetric radial field)
     * The green dashed X-quadrant lines define the 4 symmetry axes
  2. "S" (SOUTH) LABEL ON THE INNER BLUE DISC: The large cyan "S" label on the inner blue disc indicates that the inner spinning magnet has SOUTH polarity facing outward (toward the outer N-labeled flywheel)
     * This creates the attractive interaction: inner South (blue) attracts outer North (red) — consistent with "The blue magnet and red flywheel attract" from companion image 3_2044596462236090368
     * The magnetic attraction between inner S and outer N is what keeps the two counter-rotating elements coupled electromagnetically (they can't fly apart despite counter-rotation because opposite poles attract across the gap)
  3. GREEN Δ LIFT INDICATORS (RIGHT DIAGRAM): The three green Δ symbols in the right plan-view diagram (two above and one below the axis) indicate that lift is generated both above and below the disc (in both axial directions from the disc midplane), consistent with the upward push being a volumetric effect throughout the magnetized disc region, not just at one surface
  4. THE MAGNETIC TOPOLOGY: Inner S surrounded by outer N (with gap between them) is a specific magnetic topology:
     * Configurations where a South magnetic object is surrounded by North-facing surfaces at 4 positions with a gap creates a "magnetic bottle" or "magnetic mirror" topology — analogous to the fusion plasma confinement configurations
     * The gap between inner S and outer N is the active region where the magnetic flux gradient (∂B/∂z ≠ 0) exists — this gradient is what creates the axial force (F_z = μ ∂B/∂z) on a magnetic dipole
