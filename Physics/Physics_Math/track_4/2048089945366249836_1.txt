UPPER DIAGRAM: TOP-DOWN (PLAN) VIEW OF THE DISC
============================================================
A circular top-down engineering diagram of the entire saucer. The disc is shown
as a wheel-spoke structure radiating outward from a central hub.

CENTER CIRCLE — labeled (Russian):
  "кабина экипажа и реактор 7Яе"
  Translation: "crew cabin and reactor 7Ya" (7Ya = likely 7th element or reactor type)
  This is the central hub from which all radial stringers extend.

RADIAL STRINGERS — labeled:
  8 radial lines extend from the central cabin to the outer rim.
  Each line represents a structural stringer made of titanium (Ti).
  They are labeled l1, l2, l3 with the inequality: l1 > l2 > l3
  This suggests the stringers are not uniform — they taper or have
  hierarchical lengths, possibly indicating different structural zones
  or electromagnetic gradient zones.

OUTER RIM:
  The outermost circle represents the outer edge (skirt) of the disc.
  Short arrows point outward from the rim — these appear to represent
  flux emission or thrust vectors emanating from the solenoid skirt.

ANNOTATION TEXT (alongside upper diagram, Russian):
  "щелевое пространство между пластинами стрингерами и шангоутами заполняется
  сварку и снизу пластиной изсвертироборошка, с соленондами, тщательно заполнять
  кремнием (Si) расплавленным Si-Ti 1400°C соленондали крепнент"
  Translation (approximate): "The slot space between the stringer plates and
  shanghouts (frame ribs) is filled — welded and from below with a plate of
  swirled material, with solenoids, carefully filled with silicon (Si), melted
  Si-Ti alloy at 1400°C solenoids attached"

  Additional annotation:
  "на свертироборошковые пластины (через стрингеры и шангоуты) подается к
  разности потенциалов (т.е. сила тока) на соленоидах, питается от реактора 7Яе"
  Translation: "To the swirled-powder plates (through stringers and shanghouts)
  is applied a potential difference (i.e., current force) to the solenoids,
  powered by reactor 7Ya"

ENGINEERING INTERPRETATION
============================================================
This is page 13 of a technical manufacturing specification for a disc-shaped craft.
The key engineering choices described are:

1. STRUCTURE: 8 radial solid-cast titanium stringers extending from a central
   reactor/cabin to the outer rim. The stringers are the primary load-bearing
   spokes of the disc.

2. MATERIAL: Silicon-titanium alloy (Si-Ti) at 1400°C fill between the stringer
   plates. Silicon (Si) selected as a structural and likely dielectric/semiconductor
   material for the disc skin.

3. PROPULSION INTERFACE: Solenoids are embedded in the skirt structure. Current
   from the reactor (7Ya) is applied via the stringers to the solenoid array.
   The potential difference (voltage) across the solenoids drives the propulsion.

4. MANUFACTURE: The central cabin, bottom plate, and all 8 stringers are cast as
   a single integrated titanium piece. The upper plate is bolted on separately.

5. The disc morphology (flat top, central post/cabin, flat skirt with striations)
   matches the Los Alamos/Corbell observational descriptions of the same disc.
