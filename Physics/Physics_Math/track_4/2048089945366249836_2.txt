MASS CALCULATION
============================================================
"На базе объемного аналога 'тарелки', рассчитаем её внутренний объём"
Translation: "Based on the volumetric analogue of the 'saucer', we calculate its volume"

VOLUME:
  V_тарелки = π·r²·h = 3.14·(24/2)²·3 = 452 m²·3 m = 1356 m³
  — Volume of the disc (excluding cabin) ≈ 1356 cubic meters

MASS/WEIGHT:
  G = ρ_Si · V = 2330 [кг/м³] · 1356 m³ = 3,159,480 кг
  — Using silicon density ρ = 2330 kg/m³
  — Calculated mass ≈ 3.16 million kilograms = 3,160 metric tons

  NOTE: This is a massive craft. 3160 tonnes is roughly 3x the mass of a fully
  loaded Boeing 747. The silicon density assumption drives this number.

SOLENOID AREA CALCULATION:
  "Площадь 'тарелки' под соленоидами (т.е. без площади кабины НЛО) определим

  — Solenoid coverage area (annular ring) ≈ 678 square meters
  — Outer radius = 15m (total disc radius), inner radius = 3m (cabin radius)

============================================================
ENGINEERING INTERPRETATION
============================================================
Page 4 establishes the core geometry and mass budget for the disc craft:

1. TOTAL DIAMETER: 30 meters. This matches the tweet's claim exactly.

2. MASS: ~3160 metric tonnes, calculated from silicon alloy density. This is
   consistent with a craft that has solenoid arrays capable of 10× thrust-to-weight.

3. ELECTRON ASYMMETRY: The upper and lower plates are maintained at opposite
   electron charge states (deficit vs. surplus). This asymmetry is fundamental
   to how the solenoid array generates directional force — it is not a
   conventional electromagnetic motor but a system exploiting charge differential
   across the disc sandwich.

4. MATERIAL SELECTION: Aluminum high-temperature alloys for the solenoids and
   plates. Silicon structural alloys for the disc body. Titanium for stringers.
   Three distinct materials, each chosen for specific functional roles.

5. SOLENOID RING: 678 m² of solenoid coverage provides an enormous thrust
   surface area — this is the "engine" of the craft distributed across the
   full annular skirt, not a point-source propulsion system.
