* Harvard-educated, taught physics at MIT for 15 years, worked at Polaroid under Edwin Land -- then DIED in New Zealand in October 1997, weeks before a scheduled test of his free energy device.
* His spinning ball experiment broke Newtonian mechanics: a ball spinning at 27,000 RPM traveled HIGHER and FELL FASTER than an identical non-spinning ball.
* Built the N-Machine -- a generator he claimed produced FIVE TIMES the energy needed to run it, pulling power from the vacuum of space.
* Left the United States and fled to New Zealand in the early 1990s. His final device test in New Zealand failed after his death.
* The Indian government reportedly tested his design and got promising results -- but never published them.
