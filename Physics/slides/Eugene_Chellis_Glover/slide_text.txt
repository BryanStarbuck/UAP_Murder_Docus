* DEAD at 29 from CYANIDE POISONING at Harvard's Thorndike Memorial Laboratory in 1932.
* Young cancer researcher at Boston City Hospital who believed he was on the "threshold of a major DISCOVERY."
* Official story: cyanide solution dripped on his hand, he wiped his mouth. Found UNCONSCIOUS in a corridor.
* New York Times headline: "Young Cancer Expert Victim of Science; KILLED by Poison With Big Discovery Near."
* Twenty-nine years old. Harvard-trained. One of the earliest cases of a promising researcher dying before a BREAKTHROUGH.
