* DEAD of a heart attack at age 58 on the opening night of a major UFO convention -- after someone reportedly WARNED researcher Gray Barker that "a well-known radio commentator would die during the convention."
* He was one of the most prominent UFO advocates in America -- his book Flying Saucers Serious Business was an international bestseller reaching audiences far beyond the UFO community.
* FIRED from the Mutual Broadcasting System in 1954 after refusing to stop discussing UFOs on his nationally syndicated program. Thousands of listeners wrote protest letters.
* He was inducted into the Indiana Broadcasters Hall of Fame. His death fell within hours of the 20th anniversary of Kenneth Arnold's sighting that launched the modern UFO era.
* No known history of heart disease. The timing -- maximum impact on the UFO research community gathered in one place -- was devastating.
