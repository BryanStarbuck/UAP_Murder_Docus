* FELL from a 16th-floor window at Bethesda Naval Hospital on May 22, 1949. The FIRST Secretary of Defense.
* Allegedly a founding member of MAJESTIC 12 — the top-secret committee managing recovered ALIEN craft.
* Forced to resign by Truman, then immediately committed to a military hospital under 24-hour GUARD.
* His personal diary was SEIZED and held by the government for a year before being returned — with pages MISSING.
* Age 57. Princeton man, Wall Street titan, champion of military integration. A bathrobe cord was found near his body.
