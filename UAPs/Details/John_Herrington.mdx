## Background

John Stewart Herrington was born in Los Angeles, California, on May 31, 1939. He received a B.A. from Stanford University in 1961 and a J.D. from the University of California, Hastings College of the Law, in 1964.

After law school, Herrington served as a First Lieutenant in the United States Marine Corps and subsequently maintained his connection to the Marine Corps through the Retired Reserves. He practiced private law in San Francisco and served as a deputy district attorney in Ventura County, California, from approximately 1965 to 1981.

**Government Career:**

- **Assistant Secretary of the Navy (Manpower and Reserve Affairs)**: 1981–1983. This was his first senior federal position, placing him inside the Navy's administrative structure and exposing him to the classified national security apparatus.
- **Deputy Assistant for Presidential Personnel**: 1983–1985. Senior White House position responsible for managing personnel across the executive branch.
- **Secretary of Energy**: February 6, 1985 – January 20, 1989. Nominated by President Reagan, Herrington became the fifth U.S. Secretary of Energy, overseeing the Department of Energy through Reagan's entire second term. The DOE's portfolio includes the National Laboratories (Los Alamos, Livermore, Sandia, Oak Ridge, Argonne, and others), nuclear weapons development, classified energy research, and some of the nation's most sensitive scientific programs.

After leaving government, Herrington became Chairman of Harcourt, Brace, Jovanovich, Inc., and later served as Chairman of the California Republican Party.

The reference to West Virginia is notable: West Virginia is home to the National Radio Quiet Zone and is adjacent to classified facilities associated with signals intelligence and deep-state continuity-of-government infrastructure. **The emotional response:** The alleged claim that Herrington cried himself to sleep nightly is significant. ## The Underground Facility in West Virginia

The reference to West Virginia in the alleged briefing location is consistent with documented geography of classified U.S. government programs:

- **The Greenbrier bunker** (known as "Project Greek Island"): A classified Cold War emergency government facility built under the Greenbrier resort in White Sulphur Springs, WV. Kept secret for decades, it was designed to shelter Congress in the event of nuclear war. Declassified in 1992 by the *Washington Post*. Its existence demonstrates that the U.S. government maintained operational, staffed underground facilities in West Virginia.
- **Sugar Grove, WV**: Home to the former Naval Security Station / National Security Agency signals intelligence facility, now closed. Operated classified communications interception programs for decades.
- **Site R (Raven Rock Mountain Complex)**: While officially in Pennsylvania, the complex extends to the Maryland-Pennsylvania-WV border region and serves as an alternate command facility for classified national security functions.

The pattern of classified underground facilities in the WV/MD/PA corridor makes the geographical claim plausible, though not verifiable.

## The Counterargument

Several important caveats must be noted:

1. **No direct statement from Herrington**: As of May 2026, John Herrington has made no public statements about UFOs, UAPs, or classified programs. The account exists entirely through Brent Friedman's claim about a private conversation, relayed secondhand through Bryce Zabel.

2. **Unverified secondhand account**: Researcher Dennis Koch and others have explicitly noted that no corroborating sources have emerged. A single secondhand chain is insufficient for evidentiary conclusions.

3. **Memory reliability over decades**: If the alleged conversation occurred in 1981, Friedman's recollection of specific quotes 40+ years later may be imperfect regardless of good faith.

4. **Political and intelligence context**: Herrington's entry into the Reagan administration coincided with his role as Assistant Secretary of the Navy, a position with classified access — but not necessarily access to the deepest UAP Special Access Programs. Whether the Secretary of Energy would be briefed into UAP legacy SAPs (as opposed to nuclear/energy programs) is itself unclear.

5. **No response from Herrington**: No journalist or researcher appears to have directly contacted Herrington to confirm or deny the claim. His response, if sought, has not been reported.
