In November 1985, Jonathan Walsh, a 29-year-old digital communications expert, fell to his death from a hotel window in Abidjan, the commercial capital of Ivory Coast. The circumstances of the fall were not clearly established, and the coroner returned an open verdict — neither confirming suicide nor ruling out foul play.

Walsh had reportedly expressed fears for his life before traveling to West Africa. The overseas location of his death made a thorough British police investigation difficult.

## Background

Jonathan Walsh was a 29-year-old specialist in digital communications who worked for GEC and British Telecom. His expertise in digital communications technology was relevant to both civilian telecommunications and classified defense applications. GEC was one of Britain's largest defense contractors, with extensive involvement in projects connected to the U.S. Strategic Defense Initiative and other classified programs.
