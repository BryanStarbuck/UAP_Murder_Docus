## Background

Ken Wheeler operates the YouTube channel "Theoria Apophasis" (300,000+ subscribers) and authored "Uncovering the Missing Secrets of Magnetism" (multiple editions). He is a professional photographer and self-taught physicist who has built a significant following within the alternative physics community.

His theoretical framework — which he calls "Ether mechanics" or "counter-space physics" — draws heavily on pre-relativistic physics (Faraday, Maxwell, Tesla), rejects Einsteinian relativity and quantum mechanics, and posits that:
- Magnetism, dielectricity, and gravity are three aspects of a single underlying field
- Gravity is not a force but a "conjugate" phenomenon arising from the dielectric field
- The "ether" or "counterspace" is the medium through which these fields propagate
- Conventional physics has suppressed or ignored the correct interpretation of electromagnetic phenomena

He argues this framework explains UAP propulsion: if gravity is a dielectric phenomenon, then controlling the dielectric field allows gravity manipulation without exotic matter or energy.

## The Counterargument

- Ken Wheeler has no formal physics credentials; his theoretical framework is not peer-reviewed
- Mainstream physicists categorize his work as pseudoscience, citing misuse of terminology and mathematical frameworks
- There is no documented government suppression of his work
- RationalWiki describes his framework as "technobabble"
- Unlike Tesla, Townsend Brown, or Podkletnov, Wheeler has produced no experimental device demonstrating gravity reduction
- He is alive and operates freely — inconsistent with the suppression pattern Amy described for others on the list
