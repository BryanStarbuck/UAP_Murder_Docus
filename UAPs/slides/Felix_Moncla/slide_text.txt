* USAF pilot who VANISHED at age 27 while scrambled to intercept an unknown radar target over Lake Superior.
* His F-89 Scorpion appeared to MERGE with the unidentified object on radar — then both blips disappeared.
* Neither the aircraft, the crew, nor any wreckage was EVER recovered from the lake.
* His radar operator, Robert Wilson, also vanished without a trace.
* The Air Force initially blamed a Canadian aircraft — Canada DENIED any aircraft was in the area.
* One of the earliest and most dramatic cases of a military pilot lost while pursuing a UFO.
