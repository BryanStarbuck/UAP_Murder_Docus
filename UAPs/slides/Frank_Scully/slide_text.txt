* DEAD of a heart attack on June 23, 1964 -- one day before the anniversary of Kenneth Arnold's landmark UFO sighting. Age 72.
* Author of Behind the Flying Saucers (1950), the FIRST major book claiming a UFO crash recovery. Sold over 10 million copies worldwide.
* Described the Aztec, New Mexico crash of 1948: a recovered craft using magnetic propulsion with small humanoid occupants found dead inside.
* His main sources were exposed as con artists in 1952 -- yet Scully maintained until his death that the underlying crash story was REAL.
* Frank Edwards, another UFO author, died on the SAME DATE three years later. Otto Binder noted the pattern of June 24 deaths.
