* Author of "Behind the Flying Saucers" (1950) — one of the first books to claim the U.S. government recovered crashed UFOs.
* DIED of a heart attack at age 72 in Palm Springs, California.
* His book reported two crashed saucer recoveries with alien bodies — predating the Roswell narrative by decades.
* His reputation was DESTROYED when his main source was convicted of fraud — but some claims later gained credibility.
* Died on June 23, 1964 — the same date Frank Edwards would die three years later.
