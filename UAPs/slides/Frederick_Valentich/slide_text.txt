* VANISHED at age 20 after radioing Melbourne air traffic control: "It is not an aircraft." His last words were cut off by METALLIC SCRAPING SOUNDS.
* Described a craft with a shiny metal surface and green light ORBITING above his Cessna 182 over Bass Strait, Australia. His engine began running rough.
* NEITHER he NOR his aircraft were EVER found -- despite a search covering over 1,000 square miles.
* Multiple witnesses on the ground reported "an erratically moving green light in the sky" at the same time.
* The Australian Department of Transport concluded: "The reason for the disappearance of the aircraft has NOT BEEN DETERMINED." October 21, 1978.
