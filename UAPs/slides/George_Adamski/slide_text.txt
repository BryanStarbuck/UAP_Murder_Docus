* DEAD of a heart attack at 74. The most famous UFO contactee of the 1950s. Died shortly after giving a lecture in Washington, D.C.
* Claimed meetings with Nordic alien "Space Brothers." Co-authored Flying Saucers Have Landed (1953). His books were translated worldwide.
* Most UFO researchers considered him a fraud -- his famous photographs were identified as chicken feeders and light fixtures.
* Despite the controversy, he had a devoted following and shaped the entire contactee movement of the 1950s and 1960s.
* Buried in Arlington National Cemetery due to his U.S. Army service. His name appears on Otto Binder's list of UFO researcher deaths.
