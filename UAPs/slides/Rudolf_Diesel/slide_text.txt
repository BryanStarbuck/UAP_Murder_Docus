* Inventor of the DIESEL ENGINE who VANISHED from a ship crossing the English Channel in 1913.
* His body was found floating in the sea days later — but was too decomposed for autopsy.
* Was on his way to England to discuss licensing his engine to the British Navy.
* Multiple parties had motive: oil companies, coal interests, and rival nations.
* The most famous inventor disappearance in history — and the first to demonstrate the inventor-death pattern.
