* Kentucky Air National Guard pilot who became the FIRST person to DIE pursuing a UFO — age 25.
* His P-51 Mustang crashed near Franklin, Kentucky on January 7, 1948 while chasing an unknown object.
* Described the object as "metallic and of tremendous size" before his radio went silent.
* The Air Force blamed oxygen deprivation — but his pursuit of an enormous craft at altitude demands explanation.
* His death made national headlines and established that UFO encounters could be FATAL.
