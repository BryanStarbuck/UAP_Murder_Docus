# UAP Murder Docus

Three investigations into suspicious deaths, disappearances, and suppression connected to UAP programs, exotic energy technology, and classified physics breakthroughs.

## [UAPs Murders (General)](/uaps/)

**Researchers, Whistleblowers, and Scientists Silenced (1949-Present)**

In July 2023, former intelligence officer David Grusch told Congress under oath that people had been harmed or killed to conceal the U.S. government's possession of non-human technology. He is not the first to make this claim — as early as 1971, researcher Otto Binder documented 137 UFO researchers who had died under mysterious circumstances.

The killings follow a pattern: researchers or insiders who threaten to go public about UAP technology die shortly before testimony, publication, or disclosure. The methods mirror intelligence-service tradecraft: staged suicides, car accidents, sudden illnesses, and professional destruction preceding death.

**Key cases:**
- [James Forrestal](/uaps/Details/James_Forrestal) (1949) — First U.S. Secretary of Defense, fell from hospital window
- [Phil Schneider](/uaps/Details/Phil_Schneider) (1996) — Found dead with catheter wrapped around neck after public lectures
- [Mark McCandlish](/uaps/Details/Mark_McCandlish) (2021) — Shotgun blast to head, ruled suicide, scheduled for Senate testimony
- [Maj. Gen. William McCasland](/uaps/Details/William_McCasland) (2026) — Former AFRL commander, vanished without trace
- **25+ GEC-Marconi scientists** (1982-1990) — Defense scientists working on SDI/"Star Wars" died in a cluster of bizarre "suicides"

**Sections:** Government Officials, UFO Researchers, Aerospace Whistleblowers, Energy Scientists, Disappeared Persons

## [UAP Energy Systems Murders](/energy/)

**Suspicious Deaths of Inventors, Physicists, and Investors in Suppressed Energy**

For over a century, inventors who claimed breakthroughs in zero-point energy, cold fusion, water fuel cells, and other systems beyond conventional physics have died under suspicious circumstances. Labs have been raided. Patents seized under national security orders. Funding cut overnight. Careers destroyed.

The pattern is consistent: an inventor demonstrates a device that allegedly produces energy beyond what conventional physics allows. Then government agencies issue gag orders, the inventor's lab is ransacked, the inventor dies suddenly — often just before securing funding or going public — and the research disappears.

**Key cases:**
- [Stanley Meyer](/energy/Details/Stanley_Meyer) (1998) — Water fuel cell inventor, last words: "They poisoned me"
- [Eugene Mallove](/energy/Details/Eugene_Mallove) (2004) — Cold fusion advocate, beaten to death with 32 lacerations
- [Nikola Tesla](/energy/Details/Nikola_Tesla) (1943) — FBI seized 80 trunks of papers within hours of death; 20 still missing
- [Arie DeGeus](/energy/Details/Arie_DeGeus) (2007) — ZPE battery inventor, found dead in car en route to secure funding
- [Wilhelm Reich](/energy/Details/Wilhelm_Reich) (1957) — Died in federal prison; 6 tons of his books burned by court order

**Sections:** Cold Fusion/LENR, Water Fuel Cells, Zero-Point Energy, Breakthrough Devices, GEC-Marconi Deaths (25 scientists)

## [UAP Physics Murders](/physics/)

**Classified Breakthroughs and The Stagnation of Public Science**

This investigation examines the claim that public physics effectively stopped advancing around 1950 — that when the US military recovered UAPs, they discovered physics so fundamentally different from mainstream science that breakthroughs were classified and public physics was allowed to stagnate.

A top general disclosed to venture capitalist Marc Andreessen that during the Cold War, "entire branches of physics were classified and taken out of the research community." The public was left with string theory and the Standard Model while classified programs allegedly leapt decades ahead.

**Physics theses investigated:**
- **Gravity Manipulation** — UAPs warp spacetime around themselves rather than flying through air
- **Zero-Point Energy** — Extracting limitless energy from the quantum vacuum
- **Alcubierre Warp Drive** — Contracting spacetime ahead, expanding behind for FTL travel
- **Exotic Metamaterials** — Recovered materials with non-terrestrial isotopic ratios analyzed at Stanford
- **Electrogravitics** — Demonstrated in labs in the 1950s, then classified

**Key people:** David Grusch, Luis Elizondo, Hal Puthoff, Bob Lazar, Garry Nolan, Eric Davis, Salvatore Pais
