# Murdering UAP Experts

Three investigations into suspicious deaths, disappearances, and suppression connected to UAP programs, exotic energy technology, and classified physics breakthroughs.

## UAPs Murders (General)

**152+ profiles** | <Link to="/uaps/">View Full Investigation</Link>

Researchers, whistleblowers, and scientists silenced from 1949 to the present. In July 2023, former intelligence officer David Grusch told Congress under oath that people had been harmed or killed to conceal the U.S. government's possession of non-human technology. The killings follow a pattern: insiders who threaten to go public die shortly before testimony, publication, or disclosure.

**Notable cases:**

- <Link to="/uaps/Details/James_Forrestal">James Forrestal</Link> — First U.S. Secretary of Defense, fell from hospital window (1949)
- <Link to="/uaps/Details/Phil_Schneider">Phil Schneider</Link> — Found dead with catheter wrapped around neck after public lectures (1996)
- <Link to="/uaps/Details/Mark_McCandlish">Mark McCandlish</Link> — Shotgun blast to head, ruled suicide, scheduled for Senate testimony (2021)
- <Link to="/uaps/Details/William_McCasland">Maj. Gen. William McCasland</Link> — Former AFRL commander, vanished without trace (2026)
- **25+ GEC-Marconi scientists** (1982-1990) — Defense scientists working on SDI/"Star Wars" died in a cluster of bizarre "suicides"

## UAP Energy Systems Murders

**93+ profiles** | <Link to="/energy/">View Full Investigation</Link>

Suspicious deaths of inventors, physicists, and investors in suppressed energy technology. For over a century, inventors who claimed breakthroughs in zero-point energy, cold fusion, water fuel cells, and other systems beyond conventional physics have died under suspicious circumstances. Labs raided. Patents seized under national security orders. Research disappears.

**Notable cases:**

- <Link to="/energy/Details/Stanley_Meyer">Stanley Meyer</Link> — Water fuel cell inventor, last words: "They poisoned me" (1998)
- <Link to="/energy/Details/Eugene_Mallove">Eugene Mallove</Link> — Cold fusion advocate, beaten to death with 32 lacerations (2004)
- <Link to="/energy/Details/Nikola_Tesla">Nikola Tesla</Link> — FBI seized 80 trunks of papers within hours of death; 20 still missing (1943)
- <Link to="/energy/Details/Arie_DeGeus">Arie DeGeus</Link> — ZPE battery inventor, found dead in car en route to secure funding (2007)
- <Link to="/energy/Details/Wilhelm_Reich">Wilhelm Reich</Link> — Died in federal prison; 6 tons of his books burned by court order (1957)

## UAP Physics Murders

**44+ profiles** | <Link to="/physics/">View Full Investigation</Link>

Classified breakthroughs and the stagnation of public science. This investigation examines the claim that public physics effectively stopped advancing around 1950 — that when the US military recovered UAPs, they discovered physics so fundamentally different that breakthroughs were classified and public physics was allowed to stagnate. A top general disclosed that during the Cold War, "entire branches of physics were classified and taken out of the research community."
